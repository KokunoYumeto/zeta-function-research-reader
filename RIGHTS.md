# Rights and source boundaries

This is a user-authorized public mirror of authored research readers, TeX,
calculations and permitted certificates. It does not copy protected source
books, primary-literature shelves, private conversations or credentials.
References to external literature preserve their authors' attribution and
do not imply permission to redistribute those external works.

No blanket license is asserted over third-party material or source results.
Any existing explicit per-file license or notice remains applicable. Public
availability and user authorization to mirror are not a claim of new licensing
rights over every intellectual contribution.

PUBLIC_FILE_MANIFEST.json identifies each file's source class, byte hash and
mechanical privacy transformations. Source proof/sketch status is preserved;
this publication process does not certify mathematical correctness.
