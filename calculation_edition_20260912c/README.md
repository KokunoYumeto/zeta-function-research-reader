# Theta measures, Stieltjes branches and arithmetic control

[Published DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22729194](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22729194) · [Current research guide](../CURRENT_RESEARCH.md)

This edition follows the original theta-function representatives through their
finite spectral packets, full relative tensor directions and arithmetic inner
products. It calculates both positive-half-line Stieltjes branches, exact
gamma-reference norms, arithmetic Gram determinants, spectral-sum connections,
conormal derivative responses and residue maps. The original measures, local
units, Jacobians and multiplicities remain in the proofs.

## What these calculations establish

The complete written arguments give exact maps and identities on their stated
analytic and algebraic domains. Finite regression tests have their stated
finite scope. The merged frontier formalization has five modules and 40
selected reports, checked with the preserved 68/38/87/73/28 target groups under
the pinned toolchain. Those formal checks do not certify every analytic theta
estimate. PR20's unchanged 20-method checker and its empty-packet clarification
have separate written-source and exact finite checks, not a new Lean claim.

The original PR20 source appendix remains a historical delivered witness; its
later short-note clarification at `600ab7a527f7a626c162bf0199d093d454b86c14`
is separately retained. No source receipt is silently relabelled. The uniform
asymptotic arithmetic-control estimate motivating this programme remains
unfinished; this edition is not presented as an RH proof.

Follow [what was attempted and why](../CURRENT_RESEARCH.md#what-was-tried-why-and-what-it-produced)
for concise motivation, outcomes and remaining directions. Source citations,
existing attribution and rights remain with each work; private conversations
and reference-library archives are excluded.
