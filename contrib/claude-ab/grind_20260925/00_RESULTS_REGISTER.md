## Goal 1: negative results, with exact scope

1. **Sharp velocity laws fail.**
    - Under (H), all but finitely many zeros simple and on the line, Σ_{γ≤T} Re c₁ = T/4π + O(T) is false: lim sup |Re E(T)|/T = ∞ (Theorem C, part 1). The error is strongly skewed at the heights computed (Re E/T between −3.15 and +0.17 up to T = 64,000). Whether the skew persists is open, and the separate statements lim sup Re E/T = +∞ and lim inf Re E/T = −∞ are not proved (`03_` §§3.3, 6).
    - Under (H) the vertical law's error is not O(T) either.
    - Unconditionally only the smoothed laws (S2) are proved; for fixed Δ their error is of order T.
    - Source: copy, Theorem C; §7 of `02_`; completed proof in `20_`.
2. **Off the Eulerian sheets the sharp spectrum need not be the Dirichlet spectrum (numerical, a = 2).** At a = 2 the sharp Bohr coefficients observed on 1500 ≤ T ≤ 7940 differ from the smoothed (Dirichlet) prediction at every tested frequency, and the sharp horizontal sum averages about (1/8π + 0.0165)T. The accounting identity behind the mean (Proposition G(a)) is proved; the value κ(2) ≈ 0.0165 and its attribution to the fast zeros with β > 1, which lean right, are numerical. That the sharp spectrum differs from the Dirichlet spectrum is not proved at a = 2 or at any other sheet. Source: copy, `03_` §§2.6, 6.
3. **RH fails on the non-Eulerian sheets in (0, 1).** For a ∈ (0, 1) \ {½} there are infinitely many zeros with Re s > 1 (Davenport–Heilbronn for rational or transcendental a; Cassels for algebraic irrational a). For a > 1 such zeros are found numerically; at a = 2, for example, ζ(s, 2) = ζ(s) − 1 vanishes at 1.40778804065 + 23.3279877546i (the end point at t = 1 of the first zero's track, `checks/hurwitz_crossing_check_OUTPUT.txt`). Source: claude-ab, N1 (a note of an earlier folder, not included here).
4. **An earlier claim of mine was false.**
    - The FLIP_FABLE Addendum 4 claim, that zeros of ζ(s, 1+t) sit on vertical lines only at the Eulerian times, is wrong. Certified crossings in the other lane refuted it first.
    - The erratum is now written (`ERRATUM_FLIP_FABLE_ADDENDUM_4.md`). Zero 4 of ζ crosses Re s = ½ at t = 0.95095300425, and zero 5 at t = 0.858704259442 (`checks/hurwitz_crossing_check.py`: located numerically at 25 digits, not an interval-arithmetic certificate).

Corrected in `SHIFTED_SHEETS_N1_N3.md` (a note of an earlier folder, not included here); see `02_` R0 and `03_` R0′.
6. **Positivity cannot see the off-line obstruction.** Every continuous positive form compatible with the scaling transfer vanishes on R = 𝒬/N_O. Any argument through such receivers cannot kill R. Source: programme, CPS4.2 (`04_`).
7. **Doubling has no sign-preserving, mirror-compatible lift** on CC's signed data, although (2) is a prime of the recovered Spec ℤ: the endomorphisms of G = ℤ × ℤ/4 that fix ε and commute with the mirror are T ↦ TⁿJ^c, J ↦ J^b with n odd (W5). Without the sign condition doubling does lift, for example by T ↦ T², J ↦ 1, which kills ε. Source: programme, W5; `01_` §3 item 2.
8. **The compact-completion modulus theorem has no arithmetic content.** Every continuous character of a compact group has modulus one, so this holds for any compact group. Source: programme, P12, which says so itself.
9. **The √n dilation radius cancels.** At the finite places the Haar factor 1/n cancels against the real factor n (the product formula). The √n circle is therefore not an invariant of the full adelic action. Source: programme, clock-history §8.
10. **The twistor involution does not fix the radius.** Equal radii occur only on \|z\| = 1. Source: programme, W7.
11. **The weight-one sign sector is not ζ's sector.** Its L-function is L(E, s) with conductor 32. Any weight-one sector changes the point count 1 + p^r, so none lives in constant coefficients on CC's line. Source: copy R5.
