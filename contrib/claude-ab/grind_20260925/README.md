# claude-ab lane: salvage, verification and new results (25–26 September 2026)

Author: Claude (Anthropic), model `claude-opus-5-5` (Opus 5.5) at maximum reasoning effort, working as the "claude-ab" lane beside the ChatGPT/Codex lanes and the other Claude sessions. Everything here lives on the branch `claude/claude-ab-grind-20260925`. Nothing on `main` is modified.

The folder is organized by the owner's four goals:

1. negative results, stated readably;
2. bridges between programmes;
3. lemmas that can be stated independently of the project;
4. the F1 context.

`00_RESULTS_REGISTER.md` is the index. Each other file states its own scope and verification status.

**The reader.** `reader/` holds the LaTeX source and the compiled PDF (`reader/reader.pdf`, 50 pages) of *The split-zero programme around the Riemann zeta function: a reader of its results, with proofs*, compiled from these notes and refereed on 26 September 2026, and published on Zenodo as version [10.5281/zenodo.22970703](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22970703) of the programme's record (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22678085). It states the proved results (the central ones with full proofs), the routes that provably do not decide the location of the zeros, each within its exact scope, the connections with other fields and with the author's other workbenches, and the open questions. Build with `lualatex reader.tex` twice. **Version 2** of the reader, `reader_v2/` (58 pages, `reader_v2/reader.pdf`), applies note `48_`: a review of version 1 in both directions, its verification, and a referee pass on version 2 itself. It makes four corrections and states more than twenty results in stronger forms, among them a Carleman criterion in the angle |arg w| < π/4 under which the degrees {2^a3^b} are dense for every t > (log 2)(log 3)/(4π). Version 1 is kept unchanged in `reader/`. Version 2 is published on Zenodo as version [10.5281/zenodo.22977243](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22977243) of the same record (26 September 2026), with the workbench reader beside it and the version-1 files in a provenance zip; the six Claude files there have the MD5 checksums of the branch copies.

**The other workbenches.** Notes `43_`–`47_` audit the owner's Erdős–Straus, Collatz, Erdős 817 and Yang–Mills workbenches (with the Navier–Stokes, S⁶ and Jacobian-map material) and their intersections, and the second reader, `workbench_reader/` (LaTeX source and compiled PDF `workbench_reader/wbreader.pdf`, 35 pages: *Four workbenches and where they meet*), states their results with proofs, the negative results within their exact scope, and the intersections as typed maps; build with `lualatex wbreader.tex` twice. The reader had three referee passes of its own after the one on the notes; the last two read it for understatement and missed generality as well as for overstatement. None of them claims a proof or disproof of the Erdős–Straus conjecture, the Collatz conjecture or the Yang–Mills mass gap, and none of the workbenches claims one.

**The Erdős–Straus reader.** `es_reader/` (LaTeX source and compiled PDF `es_reader/esreader.pdf`, 26 pages: *The Erdős–Straus workbench: a reader of its results, with proofs*) is a dedicated reader of the Erdős–Straus workbench: the 619-page archive of 31 August, the supplements, the 124-page reader and the September continuation. It proves the core (the shell criterion, the first two shells, the reduction modulo 840, the Jacobi-symbol barrier, the least-class function, four explicit families), recomputes the record primes and the record-shell deficit data, maps the archive section by section, and states the negative results with their exact scope. Two statements go beyond the workbench: each family meets all six hard classes modulo 840, and the supplement's no-two-shears theorem holds for every prime p ≢ 2 (mod 3) and fails for some p ≡ 2 (mod 3). **This version has not yet had an independent referee pass**; one is planned after 30 September 2026. Its scripts are in `checks/es_reader/`. Build with `lualatex esreader.tex` twice.
