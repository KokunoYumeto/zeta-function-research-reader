\section{How the audit was verified}\label{app:verification}

\subsection{What was read}

The audit read the programme's files at the commits recorded in the notes, and in particular: the second attempt's fixed-point, clock, positive-quotient, integrality, weight-separation and character-lifting blocks (content maps \note{01\_}, \note{04\_}, \note{05\_}, \note{07\_}); the continuations of 24--25 September (\lbl{OZD}, \lbl{SPF}, \lbl{GSP}, \lbl{CTS}, \lbl{FGR}, \lbl{WHR}, \lbl{HCS}, \lbl{GMC}, \lbl{VWR}, \lbl{ATG}, \lbl{PMS}, \lbl{NCI}, \lbl{RSS}, \lbl{NHJ}, \lbl{SMC}, \lbl{GAP}, \lbl{ADM}, \lbl{FTD}, \lbl{PRS}, \lbl{DER}, \lbl{FSC}, \lbl{FEM}); the three-lane edition's analytic core (\lbl{GSL}, \lbl{GMS}, \lbl{AST}, \lbl{GDC}, \lbl{PTQ}, \lbl{SSI}, \lbl{ECR}, \lbl{ECI}, \lbl{GZR}, \lbl{OMS}, \lbl{CGS}, \lbl{DCP}, \lbl{RTT}, \lbl{RZ}, \lbl{SDT}, \lbl{FOD}, \lbl{NEA}); the bulletin of 25 September (\lbl{ETR}, \lbl{CEI}, \lbl{GDE}, \lbl{ABH}, \lbl{NJS}, \lbl{SSR}, \lbl{NPE0--NPE4}, \lbl{NHI}, \lbl{HSR}, \lbl{HBW}, \lbl{BML}, \lbl{BRC}, \lbl{GJN}, \lbl{BNS}); the programme's reconstruction of Weil~II (\lbl{DW}) and its full Deligne reader (\lbl{DP}, \lbl{DMG}, \lbl{DLM}, \lbl{DB}, \lbl{DBC}, \lbl{DW0--DW4}, \lbl{MDB}); the copy's two rounds of results; the cross-programme material of the Erdős--Straus, Yang--Mills, Navier--Stokes and $S^6$ workbenches used in Sections~\ref{sec:other} and~\ref{sec:bridges}, including the proof of the Yang--Mills workbench's Theorem~14.2; and, in the light form that the session's rules allowed, the final messages of the 94 Codex mathematics sessions of 24--25 September. What was not read is listed in Section~\ref{sec:open}.
Printed sources were checked where the notes say so: Weil~II on the numdam scan (pp.~150--203), Titchmarsh's Theorems 9.2, 9.6(A) and 9.7 (by a referee, on page images), and the published pages of the cited theorems of Berenstein--Taylor and Abanin--Le Hai Khoi--Nalbandyan.

\subsection{Referee passes}

The audit notes \note{08\_}--\note{42\_} were each refereed by a Claude instance that had not written them; the referee re-ran the note's check script, wrote checks of its own, and reported findings as major (a statement false or overstated as written) or minor. The content maps \note{01\_}, \note{04\_}, \note{05\_}, \note{07\_} and the copy's reports \note{02\_}, \note{03\_} had no separate pass (their summaries in the register were checked in final pass A, and the copy's Theorem~E had a full referee pass, \note{06\_}). All findings were checked by the author of the note against the sources before they were applied.

\subsection{Checks}

Every numerical or symbolic claim in the notes has a script with recorded output: 48 scripts in \note{checks/} (47 of them with a recorded \texttt{\_OUTPUT.txt}), four further scripts of the first referee pass in \note{checks/referee08/} with their argument-principle outputs, five figure scripts in \note{figures/}, and the copy's code in \note{copy\_round2/code/} (which reads its zero data from the directory named by the environment variable \texttt{ZERO\_DATA\_DIR}). They use \texttt{mpmath}, \texttt{sympy} and, for the argument-principle counts, Arb ball arithmetic; runtimes are seconds to minutes. Checks marked ``expected fail'' in a referee's script document a finding (for example the counterexample of Lemma~\ref{lem:concentration}) and are not failures of the note. Every check is a check of a stated identity or bound; no statement of this reader rests on a numerical check alone unless it is labelled as numerical.
