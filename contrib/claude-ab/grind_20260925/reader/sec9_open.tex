\section{Open questions, and what was not audited}\label{sec:open}

\subsection{Questions left open by the proved results}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \emph{The primary decomposition.} Do the principal parts of $1/\zeta$ at the zeros satisfy polynomial bounds (Theorem~\ref{thm:primary}(c))? For multiple zeros, does the leading coefficient alone suffice?
\item \emph{The diagonal family.} Is the $T$-even family $\{f_k\}$ of Corollary~\ref{cor:chiral} easier to control on the prime side (the explicit formula for $G'/G$) than Weil's own family?
\item \emph{An index for off-line zeros alone.} The holonomy index $I(T)$ counts off-line pairs together with $\sum\lfloor m_\rho/2\rfloor$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:index}); an index counting off-line zeros alone, obtained from a map on the strip, is not constructed. Whether one-line inputs can bound the bulk count better than Turing's method is not decided.
\item \emph{The Davenport--Heilbronn function.} Does the splitting $\B/(I\cap I_-)\cong\B/I\oplus\B/I_-$ fail for it? The separator proof does not reach it (Section~\ref{sec:twolines}).
\item \emph{Sparse cover degrees.} Are the cover discrepancies with $n$ in $\{2^a3^b\}$ dense in $\B$? This set has quadratic counting, where the Jensen argument is inconclusive; no finite combination of point evaluations and their derivatives annihilates the family (\note{31\_} Lemma 31.1, scope).
\item \emph{Velocity sums.} The one-sided statements for the sharp velocity sums, and the lemma the copy calls U at rational non-Eulerian $a$ (\note{03\_} §6); whether the sharp spectrum differs from the Dirichlet spectrum off the Eulerian sheets (numerically suggested at $a=2$, not proved).
\item \emph{Beurling systems.} Does $N(x)=cx+O(x^\theta)$ with $\theta<\tfrac12$ force $\zeta_P\ne0$ on $\sigma>\tfrac12$ (\note{02\_} §6)?
\item \emph{The weight question.} Can the programme's reconstructed arithmetic supply either a square with a Künneth map and discrete weights, or an independent source for a pole bound uniform in the tensor degree (Section~\ref{sec:deligne})? \lbl{TWC9} and \lbl{TWC11} state that the programme's computations do not rule such a construction out, and none has yet been assigned the fixed-curve cohomological denominator.
\item \emph{The local model at $\tau$.} A ``pole at $\tau$'' would be computed from a local model of the test functions at $\tau$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:poles}(b)); that model has not been specified.
\item \emph{Codex's newer ramified map.} Codex reported a ramified map with the exact factor $n^{-1}$ in the nilpotent relation, which the ordinary counting map lacks; its comparison with the uniform-pole-bound question of Section~\ref{sec:deligne} is open (board task 1 follow-up).
\end{enumerate}
