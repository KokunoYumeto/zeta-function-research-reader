\section{The record's ledger and its central claims}\label{sec:ledger}

The monograph labels each claim with its own status: \textsf{VERIFIED} for a complete proof, \textsf{OPEN} when neither a proof nor a counterexample is recorded, and \textsf{REFUTED} for an exact assertion with a recorded contradiction or counterexample. These are the AI verification's labels.

\subsection{The central claims (record: \textsf{VERIFIED})}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The construction.} The monograph's sections 6--7 prove the period-family descent, the toric and finite fillings, the global gluing, and the integral nearby-cycle and Leray comparisons. With them the constructed threefold $X$ satisfies $H^1(X;\ZZ)=H^2(X;\ZZ)=0$, $a(X)=1$, and $c_3(X)=2$, with $f:X\to\Pp^1$ holomorphic and an algebraic reduction.
\item \emph{A counterexample to the published theorem.} $X$ satisfies every stated hypothesis of Theorem~2.2 of \cite{CDP20} and contradicts its conclusion (monograph §13.11, item~5). The monograph says that this uses the complete construction and topology proofs, not only the two defects below.
\item \emph{A corrected criterion.} The part of the published argument that survives: if some line bundle $L_0$ has $H^2(X,T_X\otimes L_0)=0$, then generic vanishing holds and $c_3(X)\le0$ (monograph Theorem 13.28; the correction note's Theorem 5.2). For the constructed $X$ this hypothesis fails for every $L_0$, since $c_3(X)=2$.
\item \emph{Further invariants}: the Hodge diamond and the Frölicher sequence, the Bott--Chern and Aeppli dimensions, and global representatives with the Bott--Chern product structure (ledger rows 17, 19, 20).
\end{itemize}
\status{\textsf{Located}. Not checked here. They are the substance of the record, and they need specialist verification; the record itself says that independent specialist replication remains open.}

\subsection{The four rows labelled \textsf{REFUTED}}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \emph{The singular-fibre inference of \cite{CDP20}} (p.~692 of the journal version). For an irreducible reduced fibre, the published proof reduces a vanishing to the vanishing of $H^0(S,\widetilde\Omega^1_S\otimes L|_S)$. The record says that this is not a formal consequence of the two cotangent sequences, and gives a test family. The source manuscript had already located the failure at its own cusp fibre (its Lemma~10.3 and Theorem~10.5), and had noted that the step is valid on a normal fibre (its Remark~10.4). \status{The test family and the corrected criterion are \textsf{audited} here. The exact size of the failure, and the exact condition for the torsion that causes it, are \textsf{proved here} (Section~\ref{sec:correction}). Whether the published proof uses the inference in exactly this unrestricted form was not checked against the paper.}
\item \emph{A homology formula imported from \cite{CDP98}.} The direct-product formula of the 1998 paper (its Lemma~3.2, in the record's reading) omits the coinvariants of the monodromy, which leaves a torus branch of the 2020 argument without its printed rank argument. \status{\textsf{Located}.}
\item \emph{A remark of the source manuscript on relative differentials.} Its Remark~1.3 speaks of the sheaves $\Omega^p_{X/B}$, and its Remark~9.21 of $\Omega^1_{X/B}$, as having torsion along all three singular fibres. The record shows that at the cusp $\Omega^1_{X/B}$ is torsion-free but not locally free, and that its torsion occurs only at the two multiple fibres. \status{\textsf{Proved here} for the local models the record gives (Proposition~\ref{prop:torsion}). The refutation is correct for $p=1$. For $p=2$ the same local models do have torsion along the double curves of the cusp fibre, so Remark~1.3's statement holds for $\Omega^2_{X/B}$.}
\item \emph{A remark of the source manuscript on the gluing integers} (its Remark~7.28), for a fixed triple. The triple $(\ell_0,\ell_1,\ell_2)=(100,1,-1)$ gives $12\ell_0-4\ell_1-3\ell_2=1199\neq\pm1$, so the statement must quantify existentially over $\ell_0$ for fixed admissible $(\ell_1,\ell_2)$. \status{\textsf{Reproduced}: the arithmetic.}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The two rows labelled \textsf{OPEN}}

Both concern replication: an independent recovery of the cyclic invariant lattices, and an independent replication of the cotangent calculation. The monograph states that these are open only because independent human specialist replication is outstanding, and that they are not unresolved premises of its proofs.
