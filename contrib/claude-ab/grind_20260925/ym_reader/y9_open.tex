\section{What remains open, and what was not checked}\label{sec:open}

\subsection{Open questions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \emph{The continuum.} Every result of the record holds at a fixed lattice spacing and at strong bare coupling (Proposition~\ref{prop:path}). A continuum theory, and its mass gap, are outside what the record addresses; the spatial line's conclusion names the interacting local continuum and a nonzero low-energy spectral weight as its unresolved target (§28).
\item \emph{Lower thresholds.} The workbench records that re-centring its norm inequality cannot move the source domain past $\alpha_5$. It names the next candidate, the same-support linearized action $J_5$ with a preconditioned residual, and records that the sixth-source catalogue is missing from the delivered sources (H7).
\item \emph{Independent confirmation of the inputs.} The threshold $g^2\ge3.6836$ rests on computed coefficient bounds of orders 3 to 5. An implementation independent of the workbench's programs would remove the condition; with the inputs checked here the threshold is $4.0187$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:ym-orderN}(b)).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What this reader did not check}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The computed inputs.} The coefficient logic of orders 3 to 5 (F14--F25) was not re-implemented. The first-pass audit re-ran the workbench's producer and auditor, and their output was byte-identical to the published rows (Appendix~\ref{app:verification}).
\item \emph{The inherited heat data.} The matrices $C_0,C_2,C_4$, the degree-2 and degree-4 row bounds, and the kinetic-row constants $30$ and $48$ were not re-derived (Proposition~\ref{prop:bench}).
\item \emph{The spatial limit.} The domain argument (H25) was read. The spatial limit (H34) is an outline and is located.
\item \emph{The earlier editions.} The 8--9 September lines were read only in the passages cited, and the 14--19 September continuations were not read beyond their statements (Section~\ref{sec:earlier}).
\item \emph{The Overleaf archive.} The 87~MB archive \texttt{08\_} was not downloaded.
\item \emph{The side branches.} The Navier--Stokes theorem imported from \cite{OpenAINS}, and the global statement of \cite{AlpogeS6}, were not checked; neither is used by the results of Sections~\ref{sec:gap}--\ref{sec:heat}.
\item \emph{The literature} cited above (Osterwalder--Seiler, Schütte--Zheng--Hamer, Eymard, Lumer--Phillips) was not re-read. No literature search for novelty was made.
\end{itemize}
