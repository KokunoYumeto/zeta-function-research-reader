\section{What remains open, and what was not checked}\label{sec:open}

\subsection{Open questions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item \emph{The continuum.} Every result of the record holds at a fixed lattice spacing and at strong bare coupling (Proposition~\ref{prop:path}). A continuum theory, and its mass gap, are outside what the record addresses; the spatial line's conclusion names the interacting local continuum and a nonzero low-energy spectral weight as its unresolved target (§28).
\item \emph{Lower thresholds.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{What the workbench shows.} Re-centring its elementary certificate, of order 1 and in a single norm, returns that certificate's endpoint $3/256=\alpha_1$ (\texttt{ROUTE\_ASSESSMENT.md}; §H7).
\item \emph{The next candidate.} The workbench names the same-support linearized action $J_5$ with a preconditioned residual. It records that the sixth-source catalogue is missing from the delivered sources (§H7).
\item \emph{Open.} Can a re-centred certificate of order $N$ reach past $\alpha_N$ if it uses data beyond the positive majorants, such as signed or same-support information? For certificates built only from majorant series with nonnegative coefficients, Pringsheim's theorem suggests not; the workbench's calculation shows this for $N=1$.
\end{itemize}
\item \emph{Independent confirmation of the inputs.} The threshold $g^2\ge3.6836$ rests on computed coefficient bounds of orders 3 to 5. An implementation independent of the workbench's programs would remove the condition; with the inputs checked here the threshold is $3.9781$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:ym-orderN}(b)).
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Order 3, computed by the referee.} The referee pass of this reader computed the order-3 norms numerically, with its own implementation (Clebsch--Gordan contraction over the $612$ anchored labels, in $22$ symmetry classes). It found $m(v_3)=1203.1424\ldots$ and $t(v_3)=127.2886\ldots$, below the workbench's $1611.59$ and $180.35$. Its checks include the record's energy coefficient $e_4=5M/216-2J/1053$, reproduced exactly.
\item \emph{What it would give.} If confirmed, preferably in exact arithmetic, the inputs of orders 1--3 give $\Delta_L\ge\kappa d_3(\xi)$ for $g^2\ge3.7154$ (the threshold is $3.71533\ldots$), with $d_3(1/64)=1.902$.
\item \emph{Status.} The computation of the norms is reported by the referee and was not repeated here. The threshold and $d_3$ were recomputed here from its values, rounded up to $(m_3,t_3)=(1203.1425,127.2887)$.
\item \emph{Order 4.} An independent computation of order 4 (8{,}621 anchored multisets) would need a spin-network implementation.
\end{itemize}
\item \emph{The first-order loss.} Is there $c>0$ with $\Delta_L\ge\kappa(3-c\xi^2)$ for all $L$ and all small $\xi>0$? The true gap has no first-order term (Proposition~\ref{prop:gapupper}(c)). The lower bounds lose one because the spectral return bounds $2\Gamma(v,h')$ by $4t(v)\|Kh'\|_X$.
\item \emph{Clustering.} Suppose, as §H34 asserts, that the coefficient of $\xi^n$ in $\widehat C_{pq}$ vanishes unless the face distance of $p$ and $q$ is at most $n$. Then the coefficient bounds of Section~\ref{sec:heat} give decay $|\widehat C_{pq}(\tau;\xi)|\le\frac{2829}{13}e^{-13\tau/8}(55|\xi|)^{2\lceil d(p,q)/2\rceil}/(1-(55|\xi|)^2)$, uniform in $L$. A written proof of that locality statement is open.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What this reader did not check}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The computed inputs.} The coefficient logic of orders 3 to 5 (F14--F25) was not re-implemented. The first-pass audit re-ran the workbench's producer and auditor, and their output was byte-identical to the published rows (Appendix~\ref{app:verification}).
\item \emph{The inherited heat data.} The matrices $C_0,C_2,C_4$ and the degree-2 and degree-4 row bounds were not re-derived (Proposition~\ref{prop:bench}). The kinetic-row constants are now derived (Proposition~\ref{prop:kinetic}).
\item \emph{The spatial limit.} The domain argument (H25) was read. The spatial limit (H34) is an outline and is located.
\item \emph{The earlier editions.} The 8--9 September lines were read only in the passages cited, and the 14--19 September continuations were not read beyond their statements (Section~\ref{sec:earlier}).
\item \emph{The Overleaf archive.} The 87~MB archive \texttt{08\_} was not downloaded.
\item \emph{The side branches.} The Navier--Stokes theorem imported from \cite{OpenAINS}, and the global statement of \cite{AlpogeS6}, were not checked; neither is used by the results of Sections~\ref{sec:gap}--\ref{sec:heat}.
\item \emph{The literature} cited above (Osterwalder--Seiler, Schütte--Zheng--Hamer, Eymard, Lumer--Phillips, Yarotsky, Baez, Kato) was not re-read. No literature search for novelty was made. The results marked as found by the referee pass are short consequences of the record's material and of standard tools, and no novelty is claimed for them.
\end{itemize}
