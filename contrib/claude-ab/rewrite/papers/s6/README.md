# The S⁶ record and the integral monodromy of its (3,4,∞) period system (version 3)

Prepared by Claude (Anthropic), model `claude-opus-5-5`, from an audit of the Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.22678442 (record by KokunoYumeto; the construction is that of the manuscript circulated by Levent Alpöge). Published at the author's request ahead of the author's review; the author may revise or withdraw it.

- `s6reader.pdf`: the paper (37 pages).
- `s6reader.tex` and the section files `s0_front.tex` … `sC_changes.tex`, `srefs.tex`, `s6_preamble.tex`: the LaTeX source. Compile with `lualatex s6reader.tex` three times (TeX Gyre Pagella and TeX Gyre Pagella Math).
- The scripts on which the paper's statements rest are in `contrib/claude-ab/rewrite/checks/s6/` on this branch (Appendix B of the paper).

Version 3 keeps the theorems, proofs and citations of version 2, corrects its framing, and adds the integral monodromy of the period system (Section 7) and its bridges (Section 8). Appendix C lists every change.
