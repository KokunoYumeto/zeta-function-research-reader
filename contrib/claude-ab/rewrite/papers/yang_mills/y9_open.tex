\section{Questions, and what was not checked}\label{sec:open}

\subsection{Questions}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,ref=\arabic*]
\item\label{q:multiscale} Can the vacuum expansion of Section~\ref{sec:gap} be replaced by a multiscale control that persists along a path to weak coupling?
\item\label{q:recentre} Can a re-centred certificate of order $N$ pass $\alpha_N$ if it uses signed or same-support information? The workbench shows that re-centring its elementary certificate, of order $1$ and in a single norm, returns that certificate's endpoint $3/256=\alpha_1$ (\texttt{ROUTE\_ASSESSMENT.md}; §H7), and it records that the sixth-source catalogue is missing from the delivered sources.
\item\label{q:inputs} Confirm the coefficient bounds of orders 3--5 by an independent, preferably exact, implementation. An independent computation of order 4 ($8{,}621$ anchored multisets) would need a spin-network implementation.
\item\label{q:secondorder} Is there $c>0$ with $\Delta_L\ge\kappa(3-c\xi^2)$ for all $L$ and all small $\xi>0$?
\item\label{q:locality} Prove the locality statement of §H34 for the heat coefficients.
\item\label{q:jacwindow} For the Jacobian branch, which properties of the map move the energy quotients of the trial states outside the window around the free two-gluon threshold?
\item\label{q:axioms} Can a sequence of the spatial line's kind, in which finite gaps close while spectral weight escapes to high energy, be realised along a path whose limit satisfies the reconstruction axioms of the continuum problem?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What this paper did not check}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The computed inputs.} The coefficient logic of orders 3 to 5 (F14--F25) was not re-implemented. The first-pass audit re-ran the workbench's producer and auditor, and their output was byte-identical to the published rows (Appendix~\ref{app:verification}).
\item \emph{The inherited heat data.} The matrices $C_0,C_2,C_4$ and the degree-2 and degree-4 row bounds were not re-derived (Proposition~\ref{prop:bench}). The kinetic-row constants are now derived (Proposition~\ref{prop:kinetic}).
\item \emph{The spatial limit.} The domain argument (H25) was read. The spatial limit (H34) is an outline and is located.
\item \emph{The earlier editions.} The 8--9 September lines were read only in the passages cited, and the 14--19 September continuations were not read beyond their statements (Section~\ref{sec:earlier}).
\item \emph{The Overleaf archive.} The 87~MB archive \texttt{08\_} was not downloaded.
\item \emph{The side branches.} The Navier--Stokes theorem imported from \cite{OpenAINS}, and the global statement of \cite{AlpogeS6}, were not checked; neither is used by the results of Sections~\ref{sec:gap}--\ref{sec:heat}.
\item \emph{The literature} cited above (Osterwalder--Seiler, Schütte--Zheng--Hamer, Eymard, Lumer--Phillips, Yarotsky, Baez, Kato) was not re-read. No literature search for novelty was made. The results marked as found by the referee pass are new relative to the record as it was read; whether they are in the literature was not checked, and no novelty beyond that is claimed.
\end{itemize}
