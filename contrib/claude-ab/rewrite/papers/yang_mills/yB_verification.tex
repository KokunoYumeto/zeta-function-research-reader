\section{How this paper was checked}\label{app:verification}

\paragraph{Sources.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The repository \texttt{KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench} at commit \texttt{fa79faf}, read-only.
\item The record 10.5281/zenodo.22883643. Its file list and MD5 checksums, and the files added by each of its five versions, were read from the record's public metadata on 26 September 2026. Twenty-three of its 43 files are byte-identical to files of the repository at \texttt{fa79faf}. Nineteen of the other twenty were downloaded from the record, with the owner's permission, and their MD5 checksums equal the record's. The 87~MB Overleaf archive was not downloaded. The script \note{ymr\_record\_inventory.py} reproduces this inventory.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What was refereed earlier.}
Section~\ref{sec:gap} and Proposition~\ref{prop:neumann} come from the Yang--Mills section of the reader of four workbenches \cite{WorkbenchReader}, in which they went through five stages:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item a first-pass audit by a separate Claude instance (\note{46a\_}). It recomputed the closed-form arithmetic of the theorem from the ten rationals of (F26), recomputed the order-2 inputs from explicit $SU(2)$ coefficient tensors, and checked order 1 by hand. It also re-ran the workbench's order-5 producer (662 rows, 51 seconds) and its polynomial and dual-certificate auditor (20 seconds): both outputs were byte-identical to the published ones. The workbench's execution record reports these runs; its publication intake matched their hashes but did not repeat them;
\item a referee pass on the notes \note{43\_}--\note{47\_}, by an instance that had written none of them;
\item a referee pass on that reader;
\item two further passes that read it for understatement and missed generality as well as for overstatement. They produced the girth form of Lemma~\ref{lem:casimir}, Corollary~\ref{cor:ym-orderN} and the physical-space part of Proposition~\ref{prop:neumann}, and corrected the credit for the order-1 and order-2 theorems of the 14--16 September edition.
\end{enumerate}
The checks are in \note{checks/\allowbreak workbenches/}: the first pass in \note{audit46\_ym/}, then \note{workbench\_reader\_claims\_checks.py} and \note{generality\_checks.py}.

\paragraph{The referee pass of version~1.}
An independent Claude instance (\texttt{claude-opus-5-5}), which wrote none of the text, refereed version~1 of this paper on 27 September 2026. A first attempt stopped before it wrote a report; its programs are kept. The second attempt read the whole of version~1, both proof manuscripts in full and every record passage cited, in both directions, as the author asked. It reran the two scripts of version~1, whose outputs were byte-identical, and wrote its own checks. It found no error that invalidates a stated result. It reported two major and fourteen minor findings, and results A--K. Every finding was verified here before it was applied: by reading the record at the cited place, or by an independent script (\note{ymr\_referee\_checks.py}, \note{ym\_order2\_threshold.py}).
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Understatements.}
\begin{itemize}
\item With the first pass's order-2 values the audited threshold is $3.9781$, not $4.0187$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:ym-orderN}(b)). The rational rounding had to be made with $\sqrt3<1.7320509$; the referee's $\sqrt3<1.7321$ was not tight enough for the stated bounds.
\item The heat prefactor improves from $67896/169$ to $2829/13$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:heatconst}).
\item The fraction bound gets a second form (Proposition~\ref{prop:relax}(a)).
\item The kinetic-row constants are derived and sharpened (Proposition~\ref{prop:kinetic}).
\item The benchmark table is sharpened accordingly.
\end{itemize}
\item \emph{Attributions and descriptions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The factor $255$, the constant $32$ and the rule $\frac18$ are in the earlier heat reader.
\item That reader's second bound U29b is described.
\item The order-5 replay was reported by the workbench, and its intake did not repeat it.
\item The attribution file's statement concerns the 21 September heat and volume proofs.
\item Yarotsky and Baez are credited.
\item Five of the twenty Zenodo-only files are inside committed archives.
\item The re-centring result is the order-1 certificate's.
\item The status labels are made consistent, and the scripts are described as they are.
\end{itemize}
\item \emph{Results adopted.} After verification: Remark~\ref{rem:perlink}, Corollary~\ref{cor:fullspace}, Propositions~\ref{prop:complexgap}, \ref{prop:kinetic} and~\ref{prop:gapupper}, the count in Proposition~\ref{prop:path}(b), the remarks after the Cauchy step, and the record's arithmetic erratum in C21.
\item \emph{Reported, not repeated.} The referee's numerical computation of the order-3 norms is reported in Section~\ref{sec:directions} and was not repeated here. Its one-loop reading of the path coefficient $\beta$ was not adopted.
\end{itemize}
The referee's report and its programs are in \note{checks/ym\_reader/referee/}.
