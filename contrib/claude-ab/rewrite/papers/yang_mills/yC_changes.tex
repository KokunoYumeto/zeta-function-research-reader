\section{Changes in version 3}\label{app:changes}

Version~2 of this paper was withdrawn on 27 September 2026, together with the audit notes, because of its framing. Version~3 keeps every theorem, proof, table and citation of version~2 and corrects its framing. The changes are the following.

\paragraph{Framing.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The title, abstract and introduction are rewritten. The introduction states the main results as Theorems~I--IV, explains what they mean, describes the typed maps of the side branches, credits the workbench's contributors and sources, and sets out the four statuses and how the status labels map onto them.
\item Statements that the results, the side branches or the negative results have no bearing on the mass gap are replaced. The introduction and Section~\ref{sec:gap} now say what the results are about, a fixed spacing and strong bare coupling, and what would connect them to the continuum regime; Section~\ref{sec:side} states what each typed map carries, and the table of Section~\ref{sec:negative} states the scope of each negative result precisely.
\item Section~\ref{sec:directions} is new. It collects, from Sections~\ref{sec:open} and~\ref{sec:side} of version~2 and from the spatial line, the open directions of the workbench, with what is established and my assessment of each; the list of questions is shortened accordingly.
\item The edge between the Erdős--Straus and Yang--Mills workbenches is described as a shared term with no typed map written, instead of as having no mathematical content.
\item Quotation marks around the terms of the record are replaced by italics; one attributed quotation of the record is kept.
\item The companion papers on Navier--Stokes, $S^6$ and the Jacobian map are cited where version~2 cited the reader of four workbenches or a separate reader for that material.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Content.}
No theorem, proof or number changed. The status line of the Navier--Stokes branch now records that the rate bounds it rests on are derived, conditionally on four named statements of the imported manuscript, in the companion Navier--Stokes paper.
