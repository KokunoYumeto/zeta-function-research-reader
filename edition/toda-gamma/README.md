# Arithmetic Toda volumes and Gamma convolution descent

This source continuation accompanies the 482-page integrated reader and the
424-page cumulative split-zero companion. The integrated reader retains all
earlier chapters and adds complete Toda/CV and Gamma-descent proofs. The companion
retains the full cumulative proof and source appendices, including fixed-order
theta-tail enclosures and the unchanged-mass Gaussian calibration.

The 482-page integrated reader is the default readable preview for this edition,
because it includes both the Toda and the later Gamma work. The 424-page
companion remains a separate human-readable PDF; neither source ZIP is a preview.

A packet is a selected finite collection of zeta zeros with every multiplicity
retained. Its theta-function source has Mellin transform `2 xi / h`, and tensor
products represent sums of the selected zero jets. The purpose is to calculate
their cost in that original norm, not to choose a favorable metric or assert the
existence of an off-critical-line zero. The fuller public introduction is
`edition/toda-gamma/PUBLIC_READING_GUIDE.md`.

The purpose is to make the arithmetic quotient's original least-norm metric
explicit enough to isolate the analytic estimate still needed. The proved
results express its volume using the source and original-relation Gram
determinants; retain the phase and four nonnegative losses; and express the
actual arithmetic convolution through a mass-preserving Gamma reference and
its exact multiplier. The analytic generating-map join and its finite metric
jets are included in the integrated chapter, not silently attributed to the
earlier delivered note.

No Riemann-hypothesis proof or uniform growing-degree estimate is asserted. In
particular, separate upper bounds do not control consecutive determinant
correction ratios. The remaining arithmetic uniform estimate is unresolved.
Finite symbolic fixtures and saved interval checks have their stated bounded
scope; they are not all-degree certification.

The endpoint degree-selection work uses a finite written Jensen argument with
four endpoint quotient volumes and two endpoint polynomial norms. Its Gamma
coordinate form keeps the exact reference and arithmetic correction factors.
The five-module Toda formal continuation is finite strict-kernel checked;
the Jensen/root endpoint assembly remains a written proof, not an additional
claimed Lean theorem. Exact source and terminal checking status are recorded
separately from the earlier four-module exterior-trace formalization.
