\section{Arithmetic source and relation volumes: exact Toda dynamics}
\label{sec:arithmetic-toda-volumes}

\textbf{Purpose and result.}
The previous exterior-trace chapter gives a lower bound for the arithmetic
control from the total displacement of a finite zero packet. This chapter
calculates that same control from two determinant sequences: the moments
of its original theta source and the moments of its original polynomial
relations. Their quotient is the determinant of the actual least-norm
metric, not the determinant of a newly selected metric.

Both determinants satisfy the classical Hankel--Toda equation. The proof
below calculates the individual outgoing and incoming source maps as well
as their difference. Their norms share a term which must be retained;
the two Toda recurrence coefficients are not separately those two norms.
The derivative of the determinant quotient recovers the imaginary cross
term. Exact equality retains the volume-imbalance and phase squares, then
joins them to the earlier spectral-coupling and compression remainders.
The result is a finite upper estimate and an explicit necessary contraction
of the original quotient volumes. It supplies no growing-degree arithmetic
estimate or conclusion about the location of all zeta zeros.

The complete mathematical body of the standalone continuation
\emph{The exact bridge from cyclic interpolation volumes to the two Toda
flows} (13 September 2026), including all TVB.1--44 and lettered identities,
is retained below. Only the heading level and locally scoped presentation
macros are changed. Its complete source and exact hash accompany the
edition. The supplied Toda note and the earlier exterior equality remain
