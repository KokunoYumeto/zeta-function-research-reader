\begin{abstract}
This research collection joins source-attributed zeta-function literature
to complete calculations on arithmetic packets, their original theta
sources, and canonical cohomological quotient metrics. Exact coordinate
and operator maps retain the original scales, multiplicities and source
relations. The current continuation calculates full-jet determinant
transfer, arithmetic monic-norm bounds and phase-retaining degree-window
products, culminating in a necessary four-volume growth estimate. Written
proofs, finite regression checks and formal kernel checks have separately
stated scopes. The remaining arithmetic upper-volume estimate is not proved.
\end{abstract}
