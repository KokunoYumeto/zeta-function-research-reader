\begin{abstract}
This is a living research synthesis, not a claim of a completed paper or endpoint theorem.
It consolidates the relevant literature and a modern local corpus at claim level, preserves
exact hypotheses and coordinates, audits proofs, searches for explicit morphisms, and records
derived results only after literature, novelty, and verification gates are satisfied.
\end{abstract}
