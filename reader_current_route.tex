\section*{Current arithmetic continuation: a reading route}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Current arithmetic continuation: a reading route}

A \emph{packet} here is a finite selection of zeros of the completed zeta
function $g=2\xi$, with every selected multiplicity retained. Its monic
polynomial is $h$. The original theta-function source has Mellin transform
$g/h$. On the critical line its density and its $k$-fold convolution are
\[
w_h(t)=\frac{|(g/h)(1/2+it)|^2}{2\pi},\qquad
m_{h,k}=w_h^{*k},\qquad \int m_{h,k}=\mu_h^k.
\]
The coordinate remains $S=k/2+iu$. The original cyclic relation polynomial
$\chi$ defines the finite quotient $\mathbb C[S]/(\chi)$, of dimension $q$.
Among admitted source polynomials of degree at most $N$, each quotient
class has a unique least-norm representative. Its Gram matrix is $G_N$,
and $V_N=\det G_N$ is its quotient volume. The same source norm defines
$\omega_{h,k,n}$, the squared least norm of a monic degree-$n$ polynomial.
The units, total mass, repeated-root orders and all quotient maps remain
those of the displayed construction.

The purpose is to compute the cost of a spectral configuration in this
specified arithmetic source. Source relations become zero in the quotient,
but their original norms affect the cost of representatives. The earlier
Toda and gamma chapters calculate this through source and relation
determinants. The new complete proof chapters make the next steps explicit:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Full-jet transfer.} A fixed-width confluent matrix computes
the relation determinant and its phase derivative. The proof includes
the actual maps between the auxiliary squared relation and the original
depth-two conormal quotient. Fixed width alone is not a conditioning bound.
\item \textbf{Arithmetic endpoint norms.} Local zeta mass and the gamma
factor give a lower envelope for the actual convolution. Exponential
moments and the exact Legendre monic minimum bound the norm ratio by
$C_hn$ for $n\ge k\ge3$. The following balanced-window proof covers
$n/2\le r\le n$ without changing the measure.
\item \textbf{Exact window products.} Multiplying the original local
radius identities retains the phase and two occurrences of every interior
volume contraction. This gives a stronger endpoint estimate than bounding
each degree separately.
\item \textbf{Four-volume consequence.} For a packet consisting exactly of
a full off-line quartet $1/2\pm\delta\pm i\gamma$, with common order $m$,
the cyclic degree is $q_k=[1+k(m-1)](k+1)^2$. The balanced window
$n=q_k,r=q_k-1$ proves
\[
\liminf_{k\to\infty}
\frac{\log(V_{q_k-1}V_{q_k}/(V_{2q_k-1}V_{2q_k}))}{q_k\log k}\ge4.
\]
This is necessary growth for that exact configuration, not an assertion
that off-line zeros exist. The opposing arithmetic upper-volume estimate
has not been proved.
\end{enumerate}

These are complete written arguments with separately identified exact
finite checks. The finite Lean developments have their own pinned checking
records; no new Lean certificate for the analytic norm estimate is
inferred from those records. Numerical examples retain their actual input
mass and are not presented as interval-certified zeta-zero packets.
Each imported source's earlier inspection and publication statements are
historical. The new reader contains those full sources with current scope
qualifications placed before them, rather than replacing their proofs by
handoff summaries. Earlier chapters and contributing-model provenance are
retained from the corpus history. No proof or disproof of RH is claimed.
