# Recorded assistant claims about Connes and collaborators

23 September 2026. This is a factual record of statements in a Claude/Fable-labelled assistant's saved messages and research notes, compared with the cited authors' LaTeX. It is separate from the Split-Zero programme's joint self-audit. The external assistant's self-audit was excluded by the investigator and is not reinstated here.

**The record supports a narrower attribution than “Connes's work has nothing to do with RH.”** The assistant called one restricted test class “RH-blind,” described several adelic propositions as “Weil's criterion under new names,” and wrote that the corresponding renaming created “No new mathematical content on RH.” It also acknowledged structural value, a proposed extension, and constructions it had not reconstructed. Those qualifications are included in the [evidence excerpts](EVIDENCE_EXCERPTS.md).

2. **The adelic trace-pairing programme.** Retained assistant record 867, timestamp 2026-09-23 14:13:17 UTC, calls Propositions 6.2, 6.3 and 7.2 “Weil's criterion under new names.” It calls Proposition 6.4 “the one genuinely structural theorem.” U05, section 3 G1, states that the renaming creates no new mathematical content on RH; section 5 says “None beyond Weil's criterion.” U05 separately records that the cyclic-module construction and trace theorem were not reconstructed, and that its reading of the thermodynamic sections was a skim. [Evidence E4–E6](EVIDENCE_EXCERPTS.md#e4-adelic-message).

The exact sentence “Connes's work does not pertain to RH” and the exact phrase “trivial restatement” were not found in the inspected assistant-message extraction or U04/U05. They should not be presented as verbatim quotations. This does not erase the stronger evaluative language that is present. The investigator's objection concerns the implication that an equivalent formulation contributes no useful mathematical structure. Equivalence alone does not establish that assessment.

## What the cited human papers establish and propose

**Connes–Consani, archimedean paper.** The original introduction states a restricted operator inequality using the scaling representation and projection onto Sonin space. It also relates the operator construction to the geometric approach through Schwartz kernels, and explains its intended use in the general semi-local programme. Thus the absence of prime terms for this particular support class is a scope fact; it does not make the paper unrelated to RH. Nor does the paper claim to have proved the full global positivity result. Relevant original labels are `mainthmintro`, `maininequintronew`, `weilnegav` and `intersection`; source lines 99–151 and 166–186 were inspected. [Alain Connes and Caterina Consani, *Weil positivity and Trace formula, the archimedean place*, arXiv:2006.13771v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771v1).

**Connes–Consani–Marcolli, adelic paper.** Proposition 6.2 transports Weil's positivity criterion to the representation and gives the comparison map. Other results include a cohomological trace (`varthetagTrH1`, retained TeX lines 1739–1774), an action by endomorphisms (`globMorita`, 2577–2609), a characterization of positive-energy representations (`classpointsperorb`, 2765–2812), and a partition-function/KMS theorem (`DQprepsHam`, 3011–3034). Its authors identify remaining geometric steps, including principal divisors. Calling selected equivalences reformulations is supported by the text. Reducing the entire paper to those reformulations, or declaring Proposition 6.4 its only structural theorem without reconstructing the other constructions, is not supported by that bounded review. The original source supplies concrete objects and dynamics whose value cannot be decided merely by observing an equivalence of positivity statements. [Alain Connes, Caterina Consani and Matilde Marcolli, *The Weil proof and the geometry of the adeles class space*, arXiv:math/0703392](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703392).

Neither comparison establishes a completed KMS-to-RG-to-RH argument, nor an obstruction excluding every such argument. No universal exclusion is supplied by the inspected notes.
