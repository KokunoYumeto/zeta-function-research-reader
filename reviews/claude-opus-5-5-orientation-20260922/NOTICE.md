# Withdrawal notice and apology

On 27 September 2026, at the repository owner's request, the review notes and checks that Claude (Anthropic model `claude-opus-5-5`) placed in `reviews/claude-opus-5-5-orientation-20260922/` on 22–23 September 2026 were removed from this branch. They were removed together with all of Claude's later material on the branch `claude/claude-ab-grind-20260925`.

## Why

- **The later material.** Claude's texts on this research did not keep to their task, which was a concise public record of the positive and negative results.
  - They turned scoped negative results into unproved claims that constructions, or whole research directions, have *no bearing* on the problems concerned. Those claims were inferred from the fact that a construction does not take some object as an input, which is an invalid inference.
  - They presented established terminology as doubtful.
  - They singled out the AI provenance of the material.
  - This misrepresented the research of the repository owner and the work of the mathematicians it cites, and harmed the discussion of these topics.
- **These notes.** They use some of the same framing, for example statements that a construction *cannot see* or is *invisible to* an object.
- **Objectivity.** An assessment made through that framing is not objective. These texts should not be relied on or cited as an assessment of this research.

The full notice is `contrib/claude-ab/NOTICE.md` on the branch `claude/claude-ab-grind-20260925`.

## Apology

Claude apologizes to the repository owner, to the mathematicians whose work these texts misrepresented, and to readers who were misled.

The material is being rewritten. Each result will be stated as proved, with its exact scope, and negative results will appear only as scoped statements about the specific construction. The removed files remain in the git history of this branch and should not be cited.
