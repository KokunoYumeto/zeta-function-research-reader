\subsubsection{An author-side CUE report: exact coordinates, proved overlap, and open obligations}
\label{sec:easy-riders-fable51-audit}

The public research video \cite{easyRiders2026Fable51} exposes three mathematically
distinct workspaces.  They concern, respectively, the joint law of the three zeros of
$\Lambda_4'$, a finite-rank OPUC--Painlev\'e treatment of the trace law and its Mellin
transform, and an attempted analytic replacement of one certified inequality in a
large-$N$ bimodality argument.  They are recorded separately below.  The video is
author-side evidence for the displayed claims and for the existence of the programmes;
it is not used as a terminal theorem source.  Twenty-five user-supplied frames were
preserved in a private evidentiary cache with individual SHA-256 hashes.  The images are
not included in the public manuscript.

\paragraph{The $N=4$ joint-law chart reported in the video.}
Use the convention
\[
 \Lambda_N(z)=\det(I-zU^*),\qquad U\sim\operatorname{Haar}(U(N)),
\]
and let $w_1,w_2,w_3$ be the zeros of $\Lambda_4'$, with whatever labelling is imposed
by the unavailable source workspace.  The displayed chart is
\begin{equation}
 s_1=|w_1|^2,\qquad s_2=|w_2|^2,
 \qquad y_{12}=2\operatorname{Re}(w_1\overline{w_2}),
 \label{eq:video-N4-chart}
\end{equation}
and the third squared radius is reported to be recovered rationally as
\begin{equation}
 s_3=\sigma=-\frac{D_0}{D_1}.
 \label{eq:video-N4-s3-recovery}
\end{equation}
The frame does not define $D_0,D_1$ or their domains.  Therefore
\eqref{eq:video-N4-s3-recovery} is an exact transcription of a claimed coordinate map,
not yet an evaluable definition in this manuscript.

The reported low-rank ladder is
\[
 \rho_2(s)=\frac{2}{\pi}\sqrt{\frac{1-s}{s}},\qquad
 g_2(r)=\frac{4}{\pi}\sqrt{1-r^2},
\]
followed at $N=3$ by an algebraic law of degree four with the relative-angle coordinate
reported as $y_{12}=4st-s-t$.  At $N=4$ the claim is that the joint law is again a
single algebraic density in the chart \eqref{eq:video-N4-chart}, while the branch sum in
the three radii is only the fibre of the projection
$(s_1,s_2,y_{12})\mapsto(s_1,s_2,s_3)$.

The three displayed formulae are
\begin{align}
 |J|
 &=\frac{s_3|\varepsilon|\,|C'(y)|
          \sqrt{4s_1s_2-y^2}}{2|m|},
 \label{eq:video-N4-fold}\\
 p_4
 &=\frac{1}{3\pi^3}
   \sum\frac{|E_2|\,|\operatorname{Disc}Q|\,
                    |\operatorname{Disc}P|^{1/2}}{|J|},
 \label{eq:video-N4-natural}\\
 f(s_1,s_2,y)
 &=\frac{64}{3\pi^3}
   \frac{(s_1+s_2-y)V_{13}V_{23}}{D_1^4\sqrt{4s_1s_2-y^2}}
   \sqrt{(1-s_1)(1-s_2)(-C_1)(1+s_1s_2-y)K_{13}K_{23}}.
 \label{eq:video-N4-branch-free}

No meanings are invented for
$\varepsilon,C,m,E_2,P,Q,V_{13},V_{23},C_1,K_{13},K_{23},D_0,D_1$.
Until their polynomial definitions, sign chambers, exceptional loci, and the source

remain candidate identities.  In particular, the square roots in

The same report asserts that the support is the triangle-inequality region obtained from
$|E_1|=3|E_3|$, and distinguishes an internal discriminant wall, collinearity endpoints,
and an excluded $E_2=0$ stratum.  Across the internal wall the density is reported to
remain bounded on the one-root side and to grow as
\[
 0.5235\,\epsilon^{-1/2}
\]
on the three-root side.  This numerical coefficient is not promoted to an exact
constant.  The report draws an obstruction to a radial algebraic or D-finite expression
that omits the sign of the discriminant; that obstruction must be reproved from the local
expansions on both sides of the wall before it is used.

The displayed validation used a $400{,}000$-point Sturm-labelled calculation and a fresh
$600{,}000$-matrix exact-CUE$_4$ sample.  It reports $256$ bins per slab, slabwise
$L^1$ discrepancies between $1.8\%$ and $4.0\%$ of slab mass, agreement of masses to
$0.5\%$, no one of $1{,}300$ bins outside $3.2$ Poisson standard errors, and pooled-radial

required future certificate.  They do not replace the absent arrays, code, random seed,

\paragraph{The fixed-interior formulas that are already sourced.}
Let
\[
 M_{N,k}(z)=\mathbb E|\Lambda_N'(z)|^{2k},
 \qquad 0\leq z<1.
\]
The video displays
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{N\to\infty}M_{N,k}(z)
 =(1-z^2)^{-k^2-2k}\Gamma(k+1)
 {}_1F_1(-k;1;-k^2z^2),
 \qquad \operatorname{Re}k>-1.
 \label{eq:video-SW-Kummer-form}
\end{equation}
This is not a new formula from the video.  It is the Simm--Wei theorem already audited in
\cref{sec:simm-wei-complete-audit}, written in Kummer coordinates
\cite[Thm.~1.1]{simmWei2026}.  Indeed, Kummer's identity gives
\[
 e^{-k^2z^2}{}_1F_1(k+1;1;k^2z^2)
 ={}_1F_1(-k;1;-k^2z^2),
\]
which is the exact morphism between the two presentations.  For integral $k\geq0$ this
becomes $k!L_k(-k^2z^2)$.  The passage $N\to\infty$ under the defining integral and the
negative-moment range are justified by the uniform-integrability argument in the human
source; they are not inferred from pointwise convergence.

Differentiation at $k=0$ yields the next displayed formula.  The linear term of
$(-k^2-2k)\log(1-z^2)$ is $-2k\log(1-z^2)$, the hypergeometric factor has no linear term,
and $\Gamma'(1)=-\gamma$.  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{N\to\infty}\mathbb E\log|\Lambda_N'(r)|
 =-\log(1-r^2)-\frac{\gamma}{2}.
 \label{eq:video-log-moment-sourced}
\end{equation}
The sourced uniform-integrability statement is again essential to the interchange.
Applying the radial Jensen derivative gives
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{N\to\infty}\mathbb E\mathcal N_N(r)
 =r\frac{d}{dr}\left(-\log(1-r^2)-\frac{\gamma}{2}\right)
 =\frac{2r^2}{1-r^2}.
 \label{eq:video-expected-count-sourced}
\end{equation}
Differentiating the count and dividing by the circumference $2\pi r$ gives the areal
density
\begin{equation}
 \varrho_\infty(r)=\frac{2}{\pi(1-r^2)^2}.
 \label{eq:video-hyperbolic-density}
\end{equation}
This is the zero intensity of the hyperbolic Gaussian analytic function with covariance
$(1-z\overline w)^{-2}$.  In the audited Gaussian field convention
$\Lambda_\infty'=\Lambda_\infty G'$; a video expression involving
$\Lambda_\infty(-G)$ is not used until its $G$ is explicitly defined.

\paragraph{The exact trace-convention map.}
For the convention fixed above,
\begin{equation}
 \Lambda_N'(0)=-\operatorname{Tr}(U^*)
              =-\overline{\operatorname{Tr}U},
 \label{eq:video-trace-convention}
\end{equation}
whereas a frame writes $-\operatorname{Tr}U$.  There is no contradiction and no silent
normalization: the involution
\[
 \mathcal C:U(N)\longrightarrow U(N),\qquad U\longmapsto U^*,
\]
is a measurable bijection with inverse $\mathcal C$, preserves Haar measure, and sends
$\operatorname{Tr}U$ to $\overline{\operatorname{Tr}U}$.  Thus it gives an exact
law-preserving coordinate morphism, and in particular
\[
 |\Lambda_N'(0)|=|\operatorname{Tr}U|\circ\mathcal C.
\]
Phase-sensitive identities still require the displayed map.

\paragraph{The OPUC--Painlev\'e system and its normalization.}
Tracy and Widom use the symbol
\[
 f_{\rm TW}(e^{i\theta};t)=\exp(t(e^{i\theta}+e^{-i\theta}))
                           =\exp(2t\cos\theta)
\]
and coefficients $U_n(t)$ \cite[Secs.~I and V]{tracyWidom1999}.  The video uses
$f_{\rm vid}(e^{i\theta};\tau)=\exp(\tau\cos\theta)$.  The exact coordinate map is
\begin{equation}
 t=\frac{\tau}{2},\qquad U_n(t)=\alpha_{n-1}(\tau).
 \label{eq:video-TW-coordinate-map}
\end{equation}
Tracy--Widom's discrete recurrence and equation (5.1), under
\eqref{eq:video-TW-coordinate-map} and the chain rule, become
\begin{align}
 \alpha_{n+1}+\alpha_{n-1}
 &=-\frac{2(n+1)}{\tau}\frac{\alpha_n}{1-\alpha_n^2},
 \label{eq:video-dPII}\\
 \frac{d\alpha_n}{d\tau}
 &=\frac12(1-\alpha_n^2)(\alpha_{n+1}-\alpha_{n-1}).
 \label{eq:video-alpha-flow}
\end{align}
This proves the coordinate equivalence of the two displayed relations.  Analytic
continuation to imaginary $\tau$ retains $1-\alpha_n^2$; replacing it by
$1-|\alpha_n|^2$ would change the analytic system.

The video additionally reports, for $L=\log D_N$,
\begin{align}
 L''
 &=\frac12(1-\alpha_{N-1}^2)(1-\alpha_{N-2}\alpha_N),
 \label{eq:video-Toda}\\
 (\tau L')'
 &=\tau(1-\alpha_{N-1}^2)
   =\tau\frac{D_{N+1}D_{N-1}}{D_N^2},
 \label{eq:video-TW-Dodgson}\\
 a''
 &=-\frac{a(a')^2}{1-a^2}-\frac{a'}{\tau}
   +\frac{N^2a}{\tau^2(1-a^2)}-a(1-a^2),
 \qquad a=\alpha_{N-1},
 \label{eq:video-PIII-a}\\
 (\tau\sigma''-\sigma')^2
 &=4\sigma'(\tau\sigma'-2\sigma)(\tau-\sigma')
   +4N^2(\tau-\sigma')^2,
 \qquad \sigma=\tau L'.
 \label{eq:video-sigma-t}
\end{align}
Equations \eqref{eq:video-Toda}--\eqref{eq:video-sigma-t} remain reported until their
derivation is checked against the exact determinant normalization and initial germ.
Every logarithmic equation is understood only where $D_N\neq0$, or meromorphically after
that qualification.  The determinant size, Fourier sign, and value of $\alpha_{-1}$ must
also be fixed before using the system.

The continuation of \eqref{eq:video-sigma-t} can nevertheless be checked algebraically.
Put $\tau=i\rho$ and $\Sigma(\rho)=\sigma(i\rho)$.  Then
\[
 \sigma'=-i\Sigma',\qquad \sigma''=-\Sigma'',
 \qquad \tau-\sigma'=i(\rho+\Sigma').
\]
Substitution, followed only by multiplication by $-1$, gives precisely
\begin{equation}
 (\rho\Sigma''-\Sigma')^2
 +4(\rho+\Sigma')
  \left[\Sigma'(\rho\Sigma'-2\Sigma)-N^2(\rho+\Sigma')\right]=0.
 \label{eq:video-sigma-rho}
\end{equation}
The reported initial germ is
\[
 \Sigma_N(\rho)=\rho\,\partial_\rho
 \log\det[J_{j-\ell}(\rho)]_{j,\ell=0}^{N-1}
 =-\frac{\rho^2}{2}+O(\rho^4).
\]
This Bessel Toeplitz determinant is the radial characteristic function of
$\operatorname{Tr}U$; it is not the confluent microscopic Bessel kernel used in the
Simm--Wei derivative-moment theorem, and no identification between them is made.

It predicts exponents $N^2/4$ for even $N$ and $(N^2+1)/4$ for odd $N$, and singular

\paragraph{The boundary bimodality problem.}
The third workspace uses the Mezzadri radius coordinate
\begin{equation}
 x=(N-1)(1-r).
 \label{eq:video-Mezzadri-x}
\end{equation}
It reports a completed theorem that the corresponding radial density converges locally
vaguely to $q_\infty$ and that $q_\infty$, and eventually every finite-$N$ density, has
at least two local maxima separated by a local minimum.  The source proof is unavailable,
so this endpoint is not yet a theorem of the present manuscript.

The coordinate must not be silently identified with the squared-radius Simm--Wei
parameter $c_{\rm SW}$ defined by $r^2=1-c_{\rm SW}/N$.  Combining the two definitions
gives the exact finite-$N$ map
\begin{equation}
 c_{\rm SW}
 =N(1-r^2)
 =\frac{2N}{N-1}x-\frac{N}{(N-1)^2}x^2,
 \qquad c_{\rm SW}\longrightarrow2x.
 \label{eq:video-boundary-coordinate-map}
\end{equation}
Moreover, the Simm--Wei theorem quoted above is uniform on compact subsets strictly
inside the disc; it is not a boundary-uniform theorem in
\eqref{eq:video-Mezzadri-x}.  Thus \eqref{eq:video-boundary-coordinate-map} is a
coordinate bridge, not a transfer of convergence.

The sole machine-certified node in the reported proof is said to be
\begin{equation}
 T_{1.5,0.4}>T_{2.2,0.2},\qquad
 T_{2.7,0.3}>T_{2.2,0.2}.
 \label{eq:video-tent-inequalities}
\end{equation}
The cited certificate uses $2{,}939$ outward-rounded Arb determinant nodes on
$0\leq R\leq20$, about forty bridge and tail rows extending to $R=1600$, and reported
margins greater than $1.27\times10^{-4}$ and $7.9\times10^{-3}$.  None of these numbers
is independently certified here because the ledger is absent.

The exact analytic replacement requested in the video is the following problem.

\begin{obligation}[Analytic persistence kernels]
\label{prob:video-analytic-persistence}
Construct continuous compactly supported probability kernels
$K_E,K_V,K_O$ on $(0,\infty)$ whose support interiors are pairwise disjoint and ordered,
and prove, without a machine-certified inequality,
\begin{equation}
 \int_0^\infty K_E(x)q_\infty(x)\,dx
 >\int_0^\infty K_V(x)q_\infty(x)\,dx,
 \qquad
 \int_0^\infty K_O(x)q_\infty(x)\,dx
 >\int_0^\infty K_V(x)q_\infty(x)\,dx.
 \label{eq:video-analytic-persistence-target}
\end{equation}
The five-kernel lemma and the transfer from $q_\infty$ to large finite $N$ are reported
as already analytic, but they must be read before they are imported.
\end{obligation}

The model attempt shown in the video did not solve
\cref{prob:video-analytic-persistence}.  Its retained guidance is that the outer
inequality appears to live in a cancelling Fourier tail beginning near $R=5.5$, while the
inner inequality is a near cancellation between the regions below and above $R=5.5$.
That observation is route selection, not a sign proof.

The primary literature gives a genuine but narrower boundary result.  Mezzadri defines
$I_p(x)$ as the fraction of derivative zeros in
$1-x/(N-1)\leq|z|<1$ and obtains the large-$x$ asymptotic
\begin{equation}
 I_p(x)\sim1-\frac1x
 \label{eq:Mezzadri-annulus-fraction}
\end{equation}
from his limiting density \cite[Sec.~3]{mezzadri2003ZetaPrime}.  He also gives the
small-$x$ heuristic
\begin{equation}
 I_p(x)=\frac{8}{9\pi}x^{3/2}-\frac{64}{225\pi}x^{5/2}
        +\frac{128}{2205\pi}x^{7/2}+O(x^4).
 \label{eq:Mezzadri-small-x-heuristic}
\end{equation}
Due\~nez--Farmer--Froehlich--Hughes--Mezzadri--Phan prove the leading density law
\begin{equation}
 Q(s)=\frac{4}{3\pi}s^{1/2}+O(s)
 \label{eq:DFFHMP-leading-density}
\end{equation}
and prove the sharper close-pair contribution
\begin{equation}
 \frac{4}{3\pi}s^{1/2}-\frac{82}{45\pi}s^{3/2}+O(s^{5/2})
 \label{eq:DFFHMP-close-pair-density}
\end{equation}
\cite[Thm.~3.1 and Cor.~3.2]{duenezEtAl2010}.  The same paper displays the second bump
experimentally for CUE, COE, independent unit-circle roots, and $\zeta'$, and explicitly
asks for its underlying cause.  It does not prove the later bimodality endpoint.  This is
the exact literature boundary: the small-$s$ asymptotic is proved; the second-bump
mechanism in that paper is observational; the later claimed persistence theorem remains
to be audited from its own proof artifacts.

\paragraph{A newly routed critical-line source.}
Conrey's paper has now been acquired as a local, nonredistributable source
\cite{conrey1989TwoFifths}.  With
\[
 \kappa=\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0(T)}{N(T)},\qquad
 \kappa^*=\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^*(T)}{N(T)},
\]
where $N_0^*(T)$ counts simple critical-line zeros, its Theorem~1 states
\begin{equation}
 \kappa\geq0.4077,\qquad \kappa^*\geq0.401.
 \label{eq:Conrey-two-fifths}
\end{equation}
Thus the title's two-fifths statement is weaker than the constants actually proved.
The proof occupies the cited article and uses a mollifier of length
$T^{4/7-\varepsilon}$ together with incomplete Kloosterman-sum estimates.  The complete
copyrighted article is retained locally for proof audit and citation; its protected bulk
text is not reproduced in the public corpus.
