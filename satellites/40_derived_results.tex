\section{Derived results and global propagation}

This satellite is for results derived during the consolidation rather than merely transcribed.
Each entry must identify:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
  \item the exact literature and local inputs;
  \item the new statement and proof;
  \item novelty-search coverage and the correct attribution tier;
  \item every affected definition, theorem, programme, comparison, and obligation;
  \item the Lean theorem or executable certificate status;
  \item limitations and larger implications not proved.
\end{enumerate}

Refinements of published results are recorded even when they are not novel user results, with
the original authors retained as the primary source and the refinement separately attributed.

The first admitted tranche consists of exact coordinate results already isolated during the
literature audit.  Their complete statements and proofs are in
\cref{sec:complete-coordinate-proofs}; the corresponding machine records are
\texttt{RESULT-20260824-0001} through \texttt{RESULT-20260824-0007}.  They comprise:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the exponential/logarithm Haar-coordinate isomorphism, the vertical-line
  Mellin--Fourier identity, and the multiplicative/additive convolution transport;
  \item the continuous Keating--Snaith density coordinate and its explicitly lossy integer
  projections;
  \item the exact transition between Montgomery's finite-height scale and the local-density
  scale, including collapse and orientation loci;
  \item the Fourier-sign involution between Montgomery and Goldston conventions;
  \item the weighted pair measure, its complete Fourier--Stieltjes transform, and an explicit
  signed-measure kernel for restriction to $[-1,1]$; and
  \item the exact affine re-expression of the Keating--Snaith moment parameter.
\end{enumerate}
These are admitted as proved coordinate derivations and literature refinements, not as
novelty claims.  No larger zeta, random-matrix, spectral, or endpoint implication is inferred.
