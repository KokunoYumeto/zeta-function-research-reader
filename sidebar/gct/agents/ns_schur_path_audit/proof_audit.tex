\section*{Independent audit of the concentration path through the Schur endpoint}

This audit concerns the specified based endpoint diagram and its Schur
functors. No identification of a tableau vector with a physical velocity,
Hamiltonian state or generic quantum-group source vector is used.
The root proof hash and all audit artifacts are recorded in
\texttt{final\_audit.json}. The independently enumerated tableaux are
retained in \texttt{tableau\_audit/}; neither audit checker imports the
root checker.

\subsection*{The exact descent required for the formal valuation}

Let $R=\mathbb Z[1/2][q,q^{-1}]$, and retain the original based maps
$E_C\xrightarrow{H}B_R\xrightarrow{V}N_R$ with fourth diagonal
entries $[7]_q[3]_q$ and $(q-q^{-1})^2$. They descend as ordinary
based module maps to three distinguished free $\mathbb Z[m]$-modules:
\[
 h_m=(m+3)\bigl((m+4)^3-5(m+4)^2+6(m+4)-1\bigr),
 \qquad C_m=mh_m.
\]
Indeed the map $\mathbb Z[m]\to R$, $m\mapsto(q-q^{-1})^2$,
sends $m+4$ to $(q+q^{-1})^2$. The complete finite Laurent sums give
$[3]_q=(q+q^{-1})^2-1$ and
$[7]_q=(q+q^{-1})^6-5(q+q^{-1})^4+6(q+q^{-1})^2-1$.
Thus the three base-changed matrices equal the original matrices.
The map of each corresponding free basis symbol to its original
labelled basis vector, with inverse the same coordinate tuple,
proves the stated isomorphisms of diagrams. This is a statement
after base change to $R$, retaining its inversion of $2$.

The distinct ring map $\mathbb Z[m]\to\mathbb R[[\tau]]$,
$m\mapsto-2\tau$, gives exactly
\[
 h_\tau=21-98\tau+140\tau^2-80\tau^3+16\tau^4,
 \quad B(\tau)=-2h_\tau,
 \quad C_\tau=\tau B(\tau).
\]
It does not send the Laurent variable $q$ to an ordinary real power
series. More explicitly, after $\tau=v^2$ the chosen complex branch is
\[
 q(v)=\sqrt{1-v^2/2}+iv/\sqrt2,
 \qquad q(v)^{-1}=\sqrt{1-v^2/2}-iv/\sqrt2.
\]
Their product is one and their difference squared is $-2v^2$.
The coefficient of $v$ is nonzero, so this branch is not a series
in $v^2$. This explains why the diagram descent, rather than an
asserted map of the full Laurent ring to $\mathbb R[[\tau]]$, is
the correct source for the length calculation. Over the ramified
ring $\mathbb C[[v]]$ the corresponding orders are doubled.

\subsection*{Tableau eigenvalues, exact orders and inertia}

The integral column-exterior to row-symmetric Schur construction is
homogeneous in the content of each tableau: every tensor summand
has the same number of each alphabet letter. A diagonal matrix
therefore multiplies a tableau containing $k$ letters $4$ by its
fourth diagonal entry to power $k$ when the other entries are one.
In a nested Schur functor this count sums the contents of the inner
tableaux with their outer multiplicities. This proves the claimed
diagonal entries and functorial factorization directly over the
coefficient rings, without dividing by a factorial.

For $\nu=(7,5,3)$, the independent checker fills its fifteen cells
in row-major order. At each cell it tries exactly the values in
$\{1,2,3,4\}$ which are at least the value to its left and strictly
larger than the value above. These comparisons are precisely the
semistandard conditions. Every semistandard tableau has one unique
sequence of choices in this recursion, so the enumeration is
exhaustive and without repetition. The resulting content profile is
\[
 (m_0,m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4,m_5,m_6,m_7)
 =(27,81,162,270,300,252,126,42).
\]
It has total $1260$, weighted total $\sum k m_k=4725$, even total
$615$ and odd total $645$. These counts agree with the root's
independent interlacing-shape formula.

The constant term $B(0)=-42$ is a unit over $\mathbb R$. Therefore
each coordinate map $C_\tau^k=\tau^kB(\tau)^k$ has image exactly
$\tau^k\mathbb R[[\tau]]$, zero kernel and cokernel
$\mathbb R[[\tau]]/(\tau^k)$. Its length is $k$ by its basis
$1,\tau,\ldots,\tau^{k-1}$ for $k>0$. The eight multiplicities
give length and determinant order $4725$, specialized rank $27$
and specialized kernel and cokernel dimensions $1233$. This
valuation calculation takes place over the stated real series ring,
not over an integral ring in which the constant $21$ is a nonunit.

On $I=(0,2\sin^2(\pi/7))$, put
$\theta=\arcsin\sqrt{\tau/2}\in(0,\pi/7)$. The previously proved
exact identity is
\[
 C_\tau=-4\sin(7\theta)\sin(3\theta)<0.
\]
Both factors are positive before the leading minus sign, because
$0<7\theta<\pi$ and $0<3\theta<3\pi/7<\pi$.
Also $-2\tau<0$, so $h_\tau=C_\tau/(-2\tau)>0$.
After the explicitly matching source-to-target tableau identification,
the diagonal endomorphism is selfadjoint for the fixed orthonormal
tableau metric. Even and odd contents give orthogonal positive and
negative spaces of dimensions $615$ and $645$. A larger positive
space must intersect the negative space by the dimension formula,
and conversely. Thus the inertia is exactly $(615,645,0)$ on all
of $I$ for the unscaled endpoint and its node factor. This metric
statement makes no assertion of equivariance for the generic full
quantum source.

\subsection*{The complete bounded-error scalar asymptotics}

Let $0<h<1/100$, $A=1/2+h$, $e_0>0$ and let $\varepsilon$
be a bounded real function near $0$ on the positive real axis.
The explicitly defined map $L_\tau=g(\tau)D_\tau$ has
\[
 g(\tau)=\tau^{-A}(e_0+\tau^{2h}\varepsilon(\tau)).
\]
The Schur construction of degree $d$ multiplies every tableau
monomial by $g^d$; for a nested shape the degree is the product of
the two box counts. Consequently its exact entries are
\[
 \lambda_{k,j}(\tau)=\tau^{k-dA}B(\tau)^k
                 (e_0+\tau^{2h}\varepsilon(\tau))^d.
\]
This uses the new explicitly chosen scalar multiple of the endpoint
map, not an inferred identification of any endpoint vector with a
fluid velocity.

For completeness, the exact polynomial remainder can be written
using an independent variable $u$:
\begin{align*}
 B(\tau)^k(e_0+u)^d-(-42)^ke_0^d
 &=\tau\left(\frac{B(\tau)^k-(-42)^k}{\tau}\right)(e_0+u)^d\\
 &\quad+u(-42)^k\left(\frac{(e_0+u)^d-e_0^d}{u}\right).
\end{align*}
Both quotients are polynomials, including the zero first quotient
when $k=0$. Substitute $u=\tau^{2h}\varepsilon(\tau)$.
Boundedness of $\varepsilon$ and continuity of the displayed
polynomials on a fixed compact neighborhood make the first term
$O(\tau)$ and the second $O(\tau^{2h})$. Since $0<2h<1$,
the sum is $O(\tau^{2h})$. Thus
\[
 \lambda_{k,j}(\tau)=(-42)^ke_0^d\tau^{k-dA}
                 +O(\tau^{k-dA+2h}).
\]
This proof requires no differentiability or continuity of the
arbitrary bounded $\varepsilon$. The nonzero leading coefficient
justifies each of the three root classifications according to
the sign of $k-dA$.

For $d=15$ all occurring $k$ satisfy
$k-15A\leq-1/2-15h<0$. A uniform lower bound verifies the
simultaneous divergence of all $1260$ entries: choose sufficiently
small $\tau$ so that $\tau<1$, $|B(\tau)|\geq21$ and
$e_0+\tau^{2h}\varepsilon(\tau)\geq e_0/2$. Then, for every
$0\leq k\leq7$,
\[
 |\lambda_{k,j}(\tau)|
 \geq(e_0/2)^{15}\tau^{k-15A}
 \geq(e_0/2)^{15}\tau^{7-15A}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
The same eventual positivity of the scalar factor proves that
the inertia of this rescaled family is $(615,645,0)$ sufficiently
near zero. It is not asserted on all of $I$ for arbitrary bounded
error: where $g<0$ the odd total degree reverses the two indices,
and where $g=0$ the entire positive-degree Schur map is zero.

Multiplying all $1260$ entries gives the exact determinant
\[
 \det S_\nu(L_\tau)
 =\tau^{4725-18900A}B(\tau)^{4725}
       (e_0+\tau^{2h}\varepsilon(\tau))^{18900}.
\]
Its leading exponent is $-4725-18900h$, because
$18900=15\cdot1260$. In comparison the scalar product
$C_\tau g(\tau)$ has exponent $1-A=1/2-h>0$ and tends to zero.
These two conclusions are compatible: the fifteen-box functor
contributes fifteen copies of the scalar exponent, whereas its
fourth-coordinate content is at most seven.
