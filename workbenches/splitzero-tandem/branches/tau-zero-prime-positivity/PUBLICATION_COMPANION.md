# Supported zero, endpoint heat and the complete signed fibre

This companion integrates a completed part of the Split-Zero research programme. It studies how support-preserving arithmetic coefficients, the original escaping inverse map, theta and Weil observations, and the ES quarter geometry connect through explicit source maps. The supported integer zero `e` and the unsupported element `tau` remain distinct.

The collection contains **fourteen complete proofs in a 106-page reader**, all individual and cumulative LaTeX sources, ten finite checkers, seven reproducible figures, and a human-source account. The proofs are retained byte-for-byte from the completed research edition, not replaced by this guide.

## Verification and scope

Finite symbolic checks are supplementary evidence at their recorded scope; the analytic and infinite-dimensional claims have written arguments in the papers. The publication checks exact file identities, preserved source provenance and navigability. It does not claim an independent re-proof of the entire collection. Positivity of a selected endpoint or shell form is not positivity of the full classical Weil form, and the shifted-divisor countertest is not a classical RH counterexample.

The machine-readable index links every displayed equation tag to its exact line in the pinned public proof. The complete original source guide retains Connes and Consani, the original ES work, the supplied split-zero manuscripts, and the other human foundations with their actual reading coverage.

The newer supported-zero Weil distribution, eight-state holonomy, holonomy average and integral collision calculations belong to subsequent work. They are not silently counted among these fourteen completed papers.
