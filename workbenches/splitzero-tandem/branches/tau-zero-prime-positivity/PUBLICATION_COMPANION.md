# Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research collection

The [235-page reader](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf) contains 34 complete main derivations, with [full cumulative LaTeX](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex), individual proof texts, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded checks. It develops escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons, full-support reconstruction, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field maps, supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension classes, collision prisms, Cauchy kernels and reflection indices.

Four additions calculate the von Mangoldt operator from divisibility projections with both support corrections, short-support primitive positivity with its explicit 55/192 bound, a single fixed test detecting every possible off-critical zero, and its exact prime-window correlation with the positive Archimedean remainder. [The prime-window status note](RESEARCH_STATUS_PRIME_WINDOW.md) and the complete proof files state their scope. The final PW20 bound is equivalent to RH and remains unproved; neither the construction nor its finite checks decides RH.

The fixed bump and product zero multiplicities are classical. Point-of-use attribution and the exact coordinate map to Juan Arias de Reyna's author-translated 1982 paper, [arXiv:1702.05442v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.05442v1), are included beside UP1 and UP11. [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) identifies the human literature. The new citation revision preserves every earlier displayed equation and complete proof; its precise differences from the sealed owner source are recorded in [CITATION_REVISION.json](CITATION_REVISION.json).

[Additional integral-collision calculations](research_calculations/README.md) retain their own full sources and review status. [PUBLICATION_INDEX.json](PUBLICATION_INDEX.json) gives exact source locations, rather than a claim of independent proof certification. The [previous 210-page reader](https://zenodo.org/records/22909361/files/164-splitzero-heat-cauchy-arithmetic-reader.pdf?download=1) remains a separate immutable download.

The larger [Split-Zero and zeta research programme](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/README.md) includes the [208-page cohomology paper](https://zenodo.org/records/22909361/files/154-splitzero-full-support-graph-pulses.pdf?download=1), the arithmetic theta and quotient-volume calculations, geometry and spectral comparisons, and the separately retained heat-monodromy, fixed-test and boundary/residue papers. This collection extends that project; it does not replace its other papers or sources.
