It retains the collection's escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons, full-support and zero-label constructions, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field maps, supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension classes, collision prisms, Cauchy kernels, reflection indices, fixed compact tests and prime-window calculations.

## Original zeta: twelve complete receiving calculations

The working meromorphic function is the original Riemann zeta function. Its complete Gamma and endpoint multiplier, trivial zeros, pole, local modules and support labels remain explicit. These twelve full proofs reconstruct the affected receiving maps:

- [TF1–42: original theta source and its return](FAITHFUL_THETA_COMPLETION_RETURN.tex), including the two-moment prime-boundary map and actual ambient heat obstruction.
- [UZ1–53: original meromorphic heat](FAITHFUL_UNCOMPLETED_ZETA_HEAT.tex), including exceptional fibres, jets, reflection orientation and every derivative of the multiplier.
- [OZC1–48: compact Weil receiver](ORIGINAL_ZETA_COMPACT_WEIL_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), including the original contour, Gamma boundary and the exact compensated pairing to which the bounds apply.
- [OZH1–49: heat/contact and causal arithmetic variation](ORIGINAL_ZETA_HEAT_CONTACT_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), retaining the raw signed divisor and its full correction.
- [OZK1–38 and OZK33a: Cauchy trace and local jets](ORIGINAL_ZETA_CAUCHY_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), with resonant finite parts, finite matrices, paired stalks and the invertible map retaining both trace and correction.

- [OZG1–55 and OZG2a: Gaussian translation](ORIGINAL_ZETA_GAUSSIAN_TRANSLATION_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), with the original divergent trivial-zero series and its common-cutoff Gamma compensation.
- [OZR1–36: regular affine heat](ORIGINAL_ZETA_REGULAR_HEAT_REMAP.tex), retaining the full multiplier ratio, local jets and signed divisor.
- [SER1–27: sectorial endpoint return](SECTORIAL_ENDPOINT_ZERO_TRACE_RETURN.tex), retaining the two continuations and their original zero-divisor receiver.
- [SFT1–48: augmented inverse and divisor trace](SECTORIAL_FILTER_TRACE_DERIVATION.tex), with both endpoint modes, exceptional orders and the infinity contribution.
- [GAB1–49: two-moment arithmetic boundary](GAUSSIAN_ARITHMETIC_BOUNDARY_RETURN.tex), with its full singular sector, discrete and continuous measures and exact projections.
- [ERD1–50: the resonant test detects every nontrivial zeta zero](ENDPOINT_RESONANT_ZERO_DETECTION.tex). At the actual time 1/32, its transform is nonzero throughout the closed critical strip. Its original small-time factor is retained, including the nonzero smooth germ whose every time derivative vanishes at zero.
- [EPM1–47: the cosine–sine pair and its Weil matrix](ENDPOINT_PAIR_TRANSPORT_AND_WEIL_MATRIX.tex), with exact time transport, original arithmetic terms, nonvanishing residue matrices and a proved global-translation criterion. The global bound in that criterion has not been established.

The fixed-test trivial-zero sum equals the earlier remainder at translations at least 1/32; at zero translation the proved cutoff compensation is required. The raw signed pairing is not silently identified with the compensated Weil pairing. The finite translation bound through log(256) and the compact bounds retain their stated scope. RH remains unresolved.
