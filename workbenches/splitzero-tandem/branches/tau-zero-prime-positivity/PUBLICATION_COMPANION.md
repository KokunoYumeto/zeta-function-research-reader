# Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research collection

The [210-page reader](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf) contains 30 complete main derivations, with [full cumulative LaTeX](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex), individual proof texts, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded checks. The collection retains the escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons, full-support reconstruction, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field maps, original supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension classes and collision prisms.

Six new derivations calculate the original heat zero-distribution, its complete Cauchy and Gamma/prime arithmetic evaluation, every mixed-prime heat derivative, the exact Cauchy–Weil positive-matrix criterion, the full reflection index of that form, and its exact finite-jet/exterior decomposition. [The heat-Cauchy status note](RESEARCH_STATUS_HEAT_CAUCHY.md) and [reflection-index status note](RESEARCH_STATUS_REFLECTION_INDEX.md) record their scope. [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) and the citations beside the proofs identify the human literature. The classical positive-kernel and Schur transfer construction in CK4–CK8 is credited to Ball, Biswas, Fang and ter Horst's primary treatment, with the exact coordinate map; its full direct proof remains included.

The index theorem counts distinct exchanged pairs of actual zeros; it does not assert that any such pair exists. The finite-jet proof retains the whole exterior form and computes the higher heat coefficient that compensates the negative direction of a first Taylor truncation. Endpoint-annihilating tests still detect the whole zero set. These are exact maps and sign-count formulas, not an RH decision.

The Cauchy criterion is an equivalence with RH, not a proof that its complete matrix family is positive. The heat identities and finite checks do not decide RH. All multiplicities, support labels and endpoint terms are retained.

[Additional integral-collision calculations](research_calculations/README.md) keep their complete sources and individual review status. [PUBLICATION_INDEX.json](PUBLICATION_INDEX.json) gives exact equation locations. The owner manifest binds the sealed proof files; this publication guide and index are separate additions. The [previous 177-page reader](https://zenodo.org/records/22908744/files/159-splitzero-collision-prism-and-extension.pdf?download=1) remains an immutable separate download.

The separate [five-page fixed arithmetic test paper](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-fixed-arithmetic-heat-flags/FIXED_ARITHMETIC_HEAT_FLAGS.pdf) calculates the first and next original heat-trace coefficients for fixed vanishing arithmetic tests, their Cauchy frame and its inverse, and their full endpoint-jet range. [Its complete sources](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-fixed-arithmetic-heat-flags/README.md) retain the 60-page heat-collision and 15-page monodromy predecessor TeX. These additions belong to the larger Split-Zero and zeta research project, whose [208-page cohomology paper](https://zenodo.org/records/22908744/files/154-splitzero-full-support-graph-pulses.pdf?download=1) remains the principal cohomology entry point.
