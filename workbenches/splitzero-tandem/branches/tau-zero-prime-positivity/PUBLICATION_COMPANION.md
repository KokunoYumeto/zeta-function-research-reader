The [385-page reader](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf) contains 52 complete main derivations, with [full cumulative LaTeX](TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex), individual proof sources, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded finite checks. It retains the collection's escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons, full-support and zero-label constructions, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field maps, supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension classes, collision prisms, Cauchy kernels, reflection indices, fixed compact tests and prime-window calculations.

## Original zeta: ten complete receiving calculations

The working meromorphic function is the original Riemann zeta function. Its complete Gamma and endpoint multiplier, trivial zeros, pole, local modules and support labels remain explicit. These ten full proofs reconstruct the affected receiving maps:

- [TF1–42: original theta source and its return](FAITHFUL_THETA_COMPLETION_RETURN.tex), including the two-moment prime-boundary map and actual ambient heat obstruction.
- [UZ1–53: original meromorphic heat](FAITHFUL_UNCOMPLETED_ZETA_HEAT.tex), including exceptional fibres, jets, reflection orientation and every derivative of the multiplier.
- [OZC1–48: compact Weil receiver](ORIGINAL_ZETA_COMPACT_WEIL_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), including the original contour, Gamma boundary and the exact compensated pairing to which the bounds apply.
- [OZH1–49: heat/contact and causal arithmetic variation](ORIGINAL_ZETA_HEAT_CONTACT_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), retaining the raw signed divisor and its full correction.
- [OZK1–38 and OZK33a: Cauchy trace and local jets](ORIGINAL_ZETA_CAUCHY_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), with resonant finite parts, finite matrices, paired stalks and the invertible map retaining both trace and correction.

- [OZG1–55 and OZG2a: Gaussian translation](ORIGINAL_ZETA_GAUSSIAN_TRANSLATION_RECONSTRUCTION.tex), retaining the divergent raw trivial-zero series at positive Gaussian-test time and its common-cutoff Gamma compensation.
- [OZR1–36: regular affine heat](ORIGINAL_ZETA_REGULAR_HEAT_REMAP.tex), with the full multiplier ratio, jets, marked unit, signed trace and residue at infinity.
- [SER1–27: original-zeta sectorial endpoint return](SECTORIAL_ENDPOINT_ZERO_TRACE_RETURN.tex), retaining both continuations and the actual zero-divisor receiver.
- [SFT1–48: augmented inverse and divisor trace](SECTORIAL_FILTER_TRACE_DERIVATION.tex), with the original endpoint modes, heat compatibility, exceptional orders and infinity contribution.
- [GAB1–49: Gaussian two-moment arithmetic boundary](GAUSSIAN_ARITHMETIC_BOUNDARY_RETURN.tex), retaining the full singular sector, discrete and continuous measures and invertible projections. Its calculated small-parameter residual detects the original nontrivial zeros; it does not establish where those zeros lie.

The fixed-test trivial-zero sum equals the earlier remainder at translations at least 1/32; at zero translation the proved cutoff compensation is required. The raw signed pairing is not silently identified with the compensated Weil pairing. The finite translation bound through log(256) and the compact bounds retain their stated scope. RH remains unresolved.
