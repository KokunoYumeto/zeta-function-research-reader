\section{Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research
collection}\label{supported-zero-prime-heat-and-residue-research-collection}

The \href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf}{210-page reader} contains 30
complete main derivations, with
\href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex}{full cumulative LaTeX}, individual
proof texts, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded
checks. The collection retains the escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons,
full-support reconstruction, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat,
eight-state holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field
maps, original supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension
classes and collision prisms.

Six new derivations calculate the original heat zero-distribution, its
complete Cauchy and Gamma/prime arithmetic evaluation, every mixed-prime
heat derivative, the exact Cauchy--Weil positive-matrix criterion, the
full reflection index of that form, and its exact finite-jet/exterior
decomposition. \href{RESEARCH_STATUS_HEAT_CAUCHY.md}{The heat-Cauchy
status note} and
\href{RESEARCH_STATUS_REFLECTION_INDEX.md}{reflection-index status note}
record their scope. \url{SOURCES.md} and the citations beside the proofs
identify the human literature. The classical positive-kernel and Schur
transfer construction in CK4--CK8 is credited to Ball, Biswas, Fang and
ter Horst's primary treatment, with the exact coordinate map; its full
direct proof remains included.

The index theorem counts distinct exchanged pairs of actual zeros; it
does not assert that any such pair exists. The finite-jet proof retains
the whole exterior form and computes the higher heat coefficient that
compensates the negative direction of a first Taylor truncation.
Endpoint-annihilating tests still detect the whole zero set. These are
exact maps and sign-count formulas, not an RH decision.

The Cauchy criterion is an equivalence with RH, not a proof that its
complete matrix family is positive. The heat identities and finite
checks do not decide RH. All multiplicities, support labels and endpoint
terms are retained.

\href{research_calculations/README.md}{Additional integral-collision
calculations} keep their complete sources and individual review status.
\url{PUBLICATION_INDEX.json} gives exact equation locations. The owner
manifest binds the sealed proof files; this publication guide and index
are separate additions. The
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22908744/files/159-splitzero-collision-prism-and-extension.pdf?download=1}{previous
177-page reader} remains an immutable separate download.

The separate
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-fixed-arithmetic-heat-flags/FIXED_ARITHMETIC_HEAT_FLAGS.pdf}{five-page
fixed arithmetic test paper} calculates the first and next original
heat-trace coefficients for fixed vanishing arithmetic tests, their
Cauchy frame and its inverse, and their full endpoint-jet range.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-fixed-arithmetic-heat-flags/README.md}{Its
complete sources} retain the 60-page heat-collision and 15-page
monodromy predecessor TeX. These additions belong to the larger
Split-Zero and zeta research project, whose
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22908744/files/154-splitzero-full-support-graph-pulses.pdf?download=1}{208-page
cohomology paper} remains the principal cohomology entry point.
