\section{Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research
collection}\label{supported-zero-prime-heat-and-residue-research-collection}

The \href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf}{385-page reader} contains 52
complete main derivations, with
\href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex}{full cumulative LaTeX}, individual
proof sources, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded
finite checks. It retains the collection's escaping-fibre and Weil
comparisons, full-support and zero-label constructions, polynomial
packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported prime
traces, character/class-field maps, supported-zero heat, residue
duality, integral extension classes, collision prisms, Cauchy kernels,
reflection indices, fixed compact tests and prime-window calculations.

\subsection{Original zeta: ten complete receiving
calculations}\label{original-zeta-ten-complete-receiving-calculations}

The working meromorphic function is the original Riemann zeta function.
Its complete Gamma and endpoint multiplier, trivial zeros, pole, local
modules and support labels remain explicit. These ten full proofs
reconstruct the affected receiving maps:

\begin{itemize}
\item
  \href{FAITHFUL_THETA_COMPLETION_RETURN.tex}{TF1--42: original theta
  source and its return}, including the two-moment prime-boundary map
  and actual ambient heat obstruction.
\item
  \href{FAITHFUL_UNCOMPLETED_ZETA_HEAT.tex}{UZ1--53: original
  meromorphic heat}, including exceptional fibres, jets, reflection
  orientation and every derivative of the multiplier.
\item
  \href{ORIGINAL_ZETA_COMPACT_WEIL_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{OZC1--48: compact
  Weil receiver}, including the original contour, Gamma boundary and the
  exact compensated pairing to which the bounds apply.
\item
  \href{ORIGINAL_ZETA_HEAT_CONTACT_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{OZH1--49:
  heat/contact and causal arithmetic variation}, retaining the raw
  signed divisor and its full correction.
\item
  \href{ORIGINAL_ZETA_CAUCHY_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{OZK1--38 and OZK33a:
  Cauchy trace and local jets}, with resonant finite parts, finite
  matrices, paired stalks and the invertible map retaining both trace
  and correction.
\item
  \href{ORIGINAL_ZETA_GAUSSIAN_TRANSLATION_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{OZG1--55
  and OZG2a: Gaussian translation}, retaining the divergent raw
  trivial-zero series at positive Gaussian-test time and its
  common-cutoff Gamma compensation.
\item
  \href{ORIGINAL_ZETA_REGULAR_HEAT_REMAP.tex}{OZR1--36: regular affine
  heat}, with the full multiplier ratio, jets, marked unit, signed trace
  and residue at infinity.
\item
  \href{SECTORIAL_ENDPOINT_ZERO_TRACE_RETURN.tex}{SER1--27:
  original-zeta sectorial endpoint return}, retaining both continuations
  and the actual zero-divisor receiver.
\item
  \href{SECTORIAL_FILTER_TRACE_DERIVATION.tex}{SFT1--48: augmented
  inverse and divisor trace}, with the original endpoint modes, heat
  compatibility, exceptional orders and infinity contribution.
\item
  \href{GAUSSIAN_ARITHMETIC_BOUNDARY_RETURN.tex}{GAB1--49: Gaussian
  two-moment arithmetic boundary}, retaining the full singular sector,
  discrete and continuous measures and invertible projections. Its
  calculated small-parameter residual detects the original nontrivial
  zeros; it does not establish where those zeros lie.
\end{itemize}

The fixed-test trivial-zero sum equals the earlier remainder at
translations at least 1/32; at zero translation the proved cutoff
compensation is required. The raw signed pairing is not silently
identified with the compensated Weil pairing. The finite translation
bound through log(256) and the compact bounds retain their stated scope.
RH remains unresolved.

\subsection{Sources, verification and retained
work}\label{sources-verification-and-retained-work}

\url{SOURCES.md} gives the human foundations. Arias de Reyna's classical
bump and product zero multiplicities retain their point-of-use
attribution and exact coordinate comparison.
\url{SECTORIAL_BOUNDARY_VALIDATION.json} binds the three new full
proofs, independently reviewed scopes, 37 exact algebra checks, 17
bounded numerical checks and inspected figures and pages. Earlier
\url{TRANSLATION_RETURN_VALIDATION.json} records the translation/affine
return. \url{ORIGINAL_ZETA_VALIDATION.json} records the owner's complete
mathematical reading, six independent reviews with their bounded scopes,
finite checks and render inspection. Publication adopts those sealed
proofs; it does not claim that the publisher independently re-proved all
385 pages. \url{PUBLICATION_INDEX.json} supplies actual source locators.

The owner source is retained byte-for-byte except for the documented
reading-link repair in README.md. The cumulative PDF and LaTeX are
unchanged. \url{PUBLICATION_SOURCE_IDENTITY.json} records the exact
manifest and the distinction between original files and publisher-added
navigation. Existing \href{research_calculations/README.md}{additional
integral-collision calculations} remain at their individual review
scopes; historical source editions are not deleted.

This is one collection within the larger
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader}{Split-Zero
and zeta research programme}. The cohomology paper, arithmetic theta and
quotient-volume calculations, geometric and spectral comparisons,
original heat-monodromy work, and the other separately retained papers
and sources remain part of that project. This edition does not replace
that whole-project identity.
