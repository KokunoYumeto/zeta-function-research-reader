\section{Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research
collection}\label{supported-zero-prime-heat-and-residue-research-collection}

The \href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf}{235-page reader} contains 34
complete main derivations, with
\href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex}{full cumulative LaTeX}, individual
proof texts, human-source citations, reproducible figures and recorded
checks. It develops escaping-fibre and Weil comparisons, full-support
reconstruction, polynomial packets, quarter/shell heat, eight-state
holonomy, supported prime traces, character/class-field maps,
supported-zero heat, residue duality, integral extension classes,
collision prisms, Cauchy kernels and reflection indices.

Four additions calculate the von Mangoldt operator from divisibility
projections with both support corrections, short-support primitive
positivity with its explicit 55/192 bound, a single fixed test detecting
every possible off-critical zero, and its exact prime-window correlation
with the positive Archimedean remainder.
\href{RESEARCH_STATUS_PRIME_WINDOW.md}{The prime-window status note} and
the complete proof files state their scope. The final PW20 bound is
equivalent to RH and remains unproved; neither the construction nor its
finite checks decides RH.

The fixed bump and product zero multiplicities are classical.
Point-of-use attribution and the exact coordinate map to Juan Arias de
Reyna's author-translated 1982 paper,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.05442v1}{arXiv:1702.05442v1}, are
included beside UP1 and UP11. \url{SOURCES.md} identifies the human
literature. The new citation revision preserves every earlier displayed
equation and complete proof; its precise differences from the sealed
owner source are recorded in \url{CITATION_REVISION.json}.

\href{research_calculations/README.md}{Additional integral-collision
calculations} retain their own full sources and review status.
\url{PUBLICATION_INDEX.json} gives exact source locations, rather than a
claim of independent proof certification. The
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22909361/files/164-splitzero-heat-cauchy-arithmetic-reader.pdf?download=1}{previous
210-page reader} remains a separate immutable download.

The larger
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/README.md}{Split-Zero
and zeta research programme} includes the
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22909361/files/154-splitzero-full-support-graph-pulses.pdf?download=1}{208-page
cohomology paper}, the arithmetic theta and quotient-volume
calculations, geometry and spectral comparisons, and the separately
retained heat-monodromy, fixed-test and boundary/residue papers. This
collection extends that project; it does not replace its other papers or
sources.
