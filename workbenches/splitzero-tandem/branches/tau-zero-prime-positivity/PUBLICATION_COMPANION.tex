\section{Supported-zero, prime, heat and residue research
collection}\label{supported-zero-prime-heat-and-residue-research-collection}

The \href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf}{177-page reader} contains24
complete main derivations, with
\href{TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.tex}{full cumulative LaTeX}, individual
proof texts, cited human sources, reproducible figures and exact
auxiliary checks. The collection retains escaping-fibre and Weil
comparisons, full-support reconstruction, polynomial packets,
quarter/shell heat, eight-state holonomy, supported-prime traces,
character/class-field maps, intrinsic supported-zero heat and residue
duality.

The three added proofs calculate the original collision's integral
extension module, its two connecting maps, the bounded prism and its
exact supported prime spectrum.
\href{RESEARCH_STATUS_PRISM_EXTENSION.md}{The mathematical status note}
states their scope and review coverage. \url{SOURCES.md} records the
original literature and its actual use, including Bhatt and Scholze's
prism definitions. The previous157-page reader remains a
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22908213/files/157-splitzero-supported-residue-reader.pdf?download=1}{separate
immutable DOI download}.

\href{research_calculations/README.md}{Additional integral-collision
calculations} retain their complete Markdown/LaTeX and individual
status. They are not silently counted as new independently verified
chapters. \url{PUBLICATION_INDEX.json} gives source-line equation links.
The owner manifest binds the unchanged sealed sources; publication
companions are identified separately.

The separate
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-supported-zero-collision-holonomy/SUPPORTED_ZERO_COLLISION_HOLONOMY.pdf}{60-page
collision and heat-holonomy continuation} carries the original heat

fixed-support calculations. The
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/tree/5a89872598df0902b7c1393cf8e4692ca3010a95/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-cohomology-heat-residue}{208/103/40-page

These publications preserve the mathematical results and their precise
scope. No proof or disproof of RH is asserted.
