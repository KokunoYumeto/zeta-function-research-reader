\section{Primitive representatives of the complete arithmetic jet packets}


\subsection{The two endpoint maps and their exact theta splitting}
Keep the original objects
\[
 V=\{\phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R):\phi\text{ even},\
       \phi(0)=0,\ \int_{\mathbb R}\phi(x)\,dx=0\},\qquad D=-x\partial_x,
\]
\[
 \begin{gathered}\mathscr B=\{F\in C^\infty(\mathbb R_{>0}):
       \sup_{x>0}|x^bD^kF(x)|<\infty\ (b\in\mathbb Z,\ k\ge0)\},\\
 \quad \Theta\phi(x)=2\sum_{n\ge1}\phi(nx),\quad Q=\mathscr B/\Theta V.
 \end{gathered}\tag{PL1}\label{pl:pl:spaces}
\]
Mellin transforms use \(dx/x\): \(M_+\phi(s)=\int_0^\infty
\phi(x)x^s\,dx/x\) and \(\mathcal M F(s)=\int_0^\infty F(x)x^s\,dx/x\).
The Fourier transform on \(\mathbb R\) has kernel \(e^{-2\pi ix\xi}\).
Poisson summation, with both moments in \(V\) zero, shows that
\(\Theta V\subset\mathscr B\): the sum decays with all Euler derivatives at
infinity, while
\(\Theta\phi(x)=x^{-1}\Theta\widehat\phi(1/x)\) gives the same property
at zero. The two source conventions and their exact scale action are
retained from \cite{FJtau,PLoperator}; Meyer's original treatment
\cite{Meyer}, Theorem \texttt{the:Lap\_range}, provides the human
source context for the full Mellin quotient. Here no closure is added
to the algebraic relation space \(\Theta V\).

Use the actual completion and seed:
\[
 a(s)=s(s-1),\quad
 g(s)=2\xi(s)=a(s)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),\quad g(0)=g(1)=1,
\]
\[
 \phi_*(x)=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2},\qquad
 A_*(s)=M_+\phi_*(s)=\tfrac12a(s)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2).
 \tag{PL2}\label{pl:pl:seed}
\]
Gaussian integration gives \(\phi_*(0)=0\) and
\(\int_{\mathbb R}\phi_*=4\pi^2(3/(4\pi^2))-6\pi(1/(2\pi))=0\).
The substitution \(t=\pi x^2\) gives the Mellin identity in PL2:
its two terms are \(2\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2+2)\) and
\(-3\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2+1)\). The gamma recurrence gives exactly
the stated factor. No seed coefficient is changed.
For \(\Re s>1\), absolute summation and substitution \(t=nx\) give
\[
 \mathcal M\Theta\phi(s)=2\zeta(s)M_+\phi(s),\qquad
 \mathcal M\Theta\phi_*=g,\qquad \mathcal M DF=s\mathcal M F.
 \tag{PL3}\label{pl:pl:theta-mellin}
\]
The first identity continues across the critical strip and at its
endpoints: \(M_+\phi\) is holomorphic for \(\Re s>-2\), by evenness
and \(\phi(0)=0\), and \(M_+\phi(1)=0\).
Using \(\zeta(0)=-1/2\) and the residue one at \(s=1\) gives
\[
 \ell_0(\phi):=\mathcal M\Theta\phi(0)=-M_+\phi(0),\qquad
 \ell_1(\phi):=\mathcal M\Theta\phi(1)=2M_+'\phi(1).
 \tag{PL4}\label{pl:pl:logmoments}
\]
In particular both endpoint values of \(\Theta\phi_*\) are one.
The operator \(D\) preserves \(V\): it preserves evenness and the
Schwartz conditions, its value at zero is zero, and integration by
parts gives \(\int D\phi=\int\phi=0\). Thus every polynomial in \(D\)
preserves \(V\), and commutes with \(\Theta\).

\begin{theorem}[A primitive representative for every original class]
\label{pl:thm:universal}
Set
\[
 \alpha_0=(1-D)\phi_*,\quad\alpha_1=D\phi_*,
 \quad E_i=\Theta\alpha_i,\quad
 \mathscr B_{\rm pr}=\ker(\mathcal M(\,\cdot\,)(0),\mathcal M(\,\cdot\,)(1)).
 \tag{PL5}
\]
The exact continuous projections
\[
 \mathsf P F=F-\mathcal M F(0)E_0-\mathcal M F(1)E_1,\qquad
 \mathsf P_V\phi=\phi-\ell_0(\phi)\alpha_0-\ell_1(\phi)\alpha_1
 \tag{PL6}\label{pl:pl:projection}
\]
have images \(\mathscr B_{\rm pr}\) and
\[
 V_{\rm pr}=\{\phi\in V:M_+\phi(0)=0,\ M_+'\phi(1)=0\},
 \tag{PL7}
\]
respectively. They satisfy \(\mathsf P\Theta=\Theta\mathsf P_V\) and
give the exact decompositions and quotient isomorphism
\[
 \mathscr B=\mathscr B_{\rm pr}\oplus\C E_0\oplus\C E_1,\qquad
 \Theta V=\Theta V_{\rm pr}\oplus\C E_0\oplus\C E_1,\qquad
 Q\cong\mathscr B_{\rm pr}/\Theta V_{\rm pr}.
 \tag{PL8}\label{pl:pl:quotient}
\]
The last isomorphism is induced by inclusion, with inverse
\([F]\mapsto[\mathsf P F]\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
PL3 gives \(\mathcal M E_0=(1-s)g(s)\) and \(\mathcal M E_1=sg(s)\).
Their endpoint pairs are \((1,0)\) and \((0,1)\), since \(g(0)=g(1)=1\).
This proves idempotence, the asserted images and direct sums.
PL4 gives \(\ell_i(\alpha_j)=\delta_{ij}\), hence the same calculation
on \(V\) proves its projection. Direct substitution proves
\(\mathsf P\Theta=\Theta\mathsf P_V\).
Furthermore \(\Theta V\cap\mathscr B_{\rm pr}=\Theta V_{\rm pr}\), by PL4.
Subtracting the two theta terms in PL6 preserves every original
cohomology class, proving surjectivity and the explicit inverse in
PL8. Its kernel is exactly the stated intersection.
All endpoint functionals are continuous: split their defining
integrals at one and bound by two suitable seminorms from PL1.
All other operators used in PL6 are fixed continuous linear maps.
\end{proof}

The source conditions \(\phi(0)=\int\phi=0\) and the logarithmic
conditions in PL7 have now been joined by actual maps, without
identifying them. Also \(\Theta\) is injective: in \(\Re s>1\) the
Euler product is nonzero, so \(\Theta\phi=0\) forces \(M_+\phi=0\).
Fourier uniqueness in \(\log x\) on any line \(\Re s=c>1\) then
forces \(\phi=0\).

For \(p>1\), write \(U_pF(x)=F(x/p)\), also on \(V\).
It preserves the two primitive spaces, because its endpoint action is
\((b_0,b_1)\mapsto(b_0,pb_1)\). The full projection defect is
\[
 (\mathsf P U_p-U_p\mathsf P)F
 =\Theta\!\left[
 \mathcal M F(0)(U_p\alpha_0-\alpha_0)
 +\mathcal M F(1)(U_p\alpha_1-p\alpha_1)\right].
 \tag{PL9}\label{pl:pl:projection-defect}
\]
Both bracketed differences belong to \(V_{\rm pr}\): their two
endpoint values vanish by the same scaling calculation. Thus the
projection is prime-equivariant on \(Q\), with its precise
representative defect retained.

\subsection{Every multiplicity and every exact packet relation}
Let \(Z\) be a finite nonempty set of actual nontrivial zeros.
Keep every full multiplicity \(m_\rho\), and fix the exact polynomial
\[
 h(s)=c_h\prod_{\rho\in Z}(s-\rho)^{m_\rho},\quad c_h\ne0,\quad
 d=\deg h,\quad U_h(s)=g(s)/h(s),\quad A_h=\C[s]/(h).
 \tag{PL10}\label{pl:pl:packet}
\]
The chosen \(c_h\) is retained throughout. At \(\rho\), \(U_h\) is
a holomorphic unit. Write \(u_h=[U_h]\in A_h^\times\).
For \(u\in A_h\), define the unique polynomial of degree less than \(d\)
\[
 P_u=\rem_h(u_h^{-1}u),\qquad
 R_u=\rem_h([a]^{-1}P_u).
 \tag{PL11}\label{pl:pl:remainders}
\]
The inverse \([a]^{-1}\) exists because no selected zero is \(0\)
or \(1\). Both inverses are full inverses in the nonreduced algebra.

\begin{lemma}[The exact inverse Euler operators]\label{pl:lem:euler}
If \(H\in\mathscr B\) and \(\mathcal M H(\rho)=0\), then
\[
 S_\rho H(x)=x^{-\rho}\int_x^\infty H(t)t^{\rho-1}\,dt
 \tag{PL12}\label{pl:pl:euler}
\]
belongs to \(\mathscr B\), satisfies \((D-\rho)S_\rho H=H\), and has
Mellin transform \(\mathcal M H(s)/(s-\rho)\). Consequently
\[
 S_h=c_h^{-1}\prod_{\rho\in Z}S_\rho^{m_\rho}:
       \ker(j_h\mathcal M)\longrightarrow\mathscr B,\qquad h(D)S_h=1
 \tag{PL13}\label{pl:pl:inverse-h}
\]
is well defined, independent of the order of the factors, and
continuous. Here \(j_h\) denotes all Taylor coefficients modulo \(h\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Differentiation of the tail gives the displayed differential equation.
Put \(x=e^y\), \(\sigma=\Re\rho\), and \(H(e^y)=b(y)\).
For \(y\ge0\), \(|b(y)|\le C_Ne^{-Ny}\) gives
\(|S_\rho H(e^y)|\le C_Ne^{-Ny}/(N-\sigma)\) whenever \(N>\sigma\).
The zero moment changes the same tail at \(y\le0\) into minus the
integral from \(-\infty\) to \(y\). The bound
\(|b(y)|\le C'_Ne^{Ny}\) then gives
\(|S_\rho H(e^y)|\le C'_Ne^{Ny}/(N+\sigma)\) for \(N+\sigma>0\).
The differential equation bounds every derivative in the same way.
These are exactly the seminorm estimates in PL1, and give continuity.
Integration by parts gives the Mellin quotient identity, with its
removable value at \(\rho\).
After one factor is removed, the remaining zero orders of the
transform allow the next factor to be removed; thus PL13 is defined
with the entire multiplicity retained. Each \(D-\rho\) is injective
on \(\mathscr B\), since a homogeneous solution \(C x^{-\rho}\) fails rapid
decrease at at least one end unless \(C=0\). Their product is
injective. Every order of PL13 solves the same equation, so
uniqueness proves order independence.
\end{proof}

Define the original section and its primitive lift by actual elements
of \(\mathscr B\):
\[
 s_hu=S_h\Theta(P_u(D)\phi_*),\qquad
 \operatorname{Prim}_h(u)=a(D)S_h\Theta(R_u(D)\phi_*).
 \tag{PL14}\label{pl:pl:lift}
\]
Their Mellin transforms are \(U_hP_u\) and \(aU_hR_u\).
The domain conditions of PL13 hold because the theta transforms are
\(gP_u\) and \(gR_u\), both divisible by the unaltered \(h\).

\begin{theorem}[Full-jet same-class lift and its exact affine relation]
\label{pl:thm:primitive}
For every \(u\in A_h\),
\[
 j_h\mathcal M s_hu=j_h\mathcal M\operatorname{Prim}_h(u)=u,\qquad
 \operatorname{Prim}_h=\mathsf P s_h,\qquad
 [\operatorname{Prim}_h(u)]=[s_hu]\ \text{in }Q.
 \tag{PL15}\label{pl:pl:sameclass}
\]
More explicitly, the exact division identity and its quotient are
\[
 a(s)R_u(s)-P_u(s)=h(s)T_u(s),\qquad
 T_u(s)=-(1-s)\frac{P_u(0)}{h(0)}-s\frac{P_u(1)}{h(1)},
 \tag{PL16}\label{pl:pl:affine}
\]
and
\[
 \operatorname{Prim}_h(u)-s_hu=\Theta(T_u(D)\phi_*).
 \tag{PL17}\label{pl:pl:exactrelation}
\]
For every polynomial \(P\), the whole polynomial packet family has
the exact relation calculation
\[
 [S_h\Theta(P(D)\phi_*)]=0\text{ in }Q
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h\mid P.
 \tag{PL18}\label{pl:pl:allrelations}
\]
For the primitive family \(a(D)S_h\Theta(P(D)\phi_*)\), the same
condition is necessary and sufficient; if \(P=hT\), its theta
primitive is exactly \(a(D)T(D)\phi_*\in V_{\rm pr}\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The full unit inverse in PL11 proves the first jet identity.
The second follows because \(aR_u-P_u\) is divisible by \(h\).
That difference has degree at most \(d+1\); hence \(T_u\) has degree
at most one. At \(s=0,1\), it has the two values
\(-P_u(0)/h(0)\) and \(-P_u(1)/h(1)\); these uniquely determine
the affine polynomial displayed in PL16, including \(c_h\).
Multiplication of PL16 by \(U_h\) gives \(gT_u\), whose inverse
Mellin transform is \(\Theta(T_u(D)\phi_*)\) by PL3. Mellin
injectivity proves PL17.
The endpoint values of \(s_hu\) are \(P_u(0)/h(0)\) and
\(P_u(1)/h(1)\), since \(g(0)=g(1)=1\).
PL6 and PL16 therefore show that its primitive lift is exactly
\(\mathsf P s_hu\). This proves all of PL15.

If the element in PL18 is \(\Theta\phi\), its full jets at the
selected zeros vanish, by PL3. Its jets are \(u_h[P]\);
the unit \(u_h\) is invertible, so \([P]=0\) in \(A_h\), which is
exactly \(h\mid P\). Conversely, for \(P=hT\), PL13 and injectivity
give \(S_h\Theta(P(D)\phi_*)=\Theta(T(D)\phi_*)\).
Inserting \(a(D)\) proves sufficiency in the primitive case, and
necessity follows because \(a\) is another unit modulo \(h\).
Its primitive source belongs to \(V_{\rm pr}\) because its theta
transform is \(a(s)g(s)T(s)\), with both endpoint values zero.
\end{proof}

In particular the class of \(S_h\Theta\phi_*\) has annihilator
exactly \((h)\) under \(\C[D]\), and its cyclic subspace is
\(\C[s]/(h)\) with its entire nilpotent structure.
Neither PL15 nor PL17 asserts an isometry of a previously chosen
finite source metric or preservation of its polynomial cutoff.
The primitive transform numerator has degree at most \(d+1\),
while \(P_u,R_u\) have degree at most \(d-1\).

\subsection{The exact primitive theta relation for every prime action}
Let \(M_s:A_h\to A_h\) be multiplication by \(s\), and
\(\Pi_p=M_{p^s}\), with its full finite Taylor expansion at every zero.
For \(v\in A_h\), define the scalar
\[
 \kappa_h(v)=\frac{sR_v(s)-R_{M_sv}(s)}{h(s)}.
 \tag{PL19}\label{pl:pl:kappa}
\]
This quotient is constant: its numerator is divisible by \(h\)
and has degree at most \(d\). It retains the factor \(c_h^{-1}\)
in polynomial division. Both remainder operations in PL11 commute
with multiplication modulo \(h\), so
\[
 D\operatorname{Prim}_h(v)-\operatorname{Prim}_h(M_sv)
          =\Theta\bigl(\kappa_h(v)a(D)\phi_*\bigr).
 \tag{PL20}\label{pl:pl:D-defect}
\]
This follows by taking Mellin transforms: the difference is
\(aU_h(sR_v-R_{M_sv})=a g\kappa_h(v)\).

\begin{theorem}[The full prime defect is a primitive source relation]
\label{pl:thm:prime}
With \(L_p=\log p\), define the actual Schwartz source
\[
 \psi_{p,u}=
 \int_0^{L_p}\kappa_h(e^{tM_s}u)\,
       U_{e^{L_p-t}}\bigl(a(D)\phi_*\bigr)\,dt.
 \tag{PL21}\label{pl:pl:duhamel}
\]
Then \(\psi_{p,u}\in V_{\rm pr}\) and
\[
 U_p\operatorname{Prim}_h(u)-\operatorname{Prim}_h(\Pi_pu)
                =\Theta\psi_{p,u}.
 \tag{PL22}\label{pl:pl:prime-defect}
\]
Thus the lifted packet action is exactly prime-equivariant in the
primitive quotient of PL8, with a specified relation before taking
that quotient. The defect is zero at all actual full zero jets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The integrand is a smooth family in \(V\) on a compact interval,
so its integral is in \(V\), with every Schwartz seminorm finite.
The seed \(a(D)\phi_*\) has theta transform \(a g\); its endpoint
values vanish. Every positive dilation preserves those zero endpoint
values, proving that the integral lies in \(V_{\rm pr}\).
Differentiate
\(U_{e^{L_p-t}}\operatorname{Prim}_h(e^{tM_s}u)\)
with respect to \(t\). By PL20 its derivative is
\(-\Theta[\kappa_h(e^{tM_s}u)U_{e^{L_p-t}}a(D)\phi_*]\).
Integration between \(0\) and \(L_p\) proves PL22 with the
displayed sign. The relation space has zero full jets at every
actual zero, by PL3; this proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}

Apply every map in PL6--22 at each nonempty original tau support label,
with zero map on the empty label. The transports at degree one are
identities, so all these maps commute with them. Their kernels remain
labelled internal zeros. Thus PL8, PL15 and PL22 also hold in the full
degree-one support diagram and its reconstruction over \(G_L(\C)\).
No numerical coefficient-support observation is used in this passage.

\subsection{The original two-leg complex and its endpoint quotient}
Here is the exact chain calculation, including its degree-zero term.
Write \(\mathcal F\) for the original Fourier transform,
\(\ell=(\ell_0,\ell_1)\), \(b(F)=(\mathcal M F(0),\mathcal M F(1))\),
\(S(b_0,b_1)=(b_1,b_0)\), and
\(A(b_0,b_1)=b_0\alpha_0+b_1\alpha_1\).
The functional equation \(g(1-s)=g(s)\) and Poisson identity give
\(\Theta\mathcal F\phi_* =J\Theta\phi_*\), with Mellin transform
\(g(1-s)=g(s)\). Injectivity of theta gives
\(\mathcal F\phi_*=\phi_*\). Fourier differentiation gives
\(\mathcal F D=(1-D)\mathcal F\); therefore
\[
 \mathcal F A=AS,\quad \ell\mathcal F=S\ell,\quad
 \mathcal F\mathsf P_V=\mathsf P_V\mathcal F,\quad
 \mathcal F V_{\rm pr}=V_{\rm pr}.
 \tag{PL45}\label{pl:pl:Fourier-projection}
\]
The identity for \(\ell\) also follows directly from
\(\mathcal M JF(s)=\mathcal M F(1-s)\). These formulas prove the asserted
commutation by substitution into \(\mathsf P_V=1-A\ell\).

For each original mask \(I\subseteq\{+,-\}\), let
\[
 K_I=[\,V^{I}\xrightarrow{\,d_I\,}\mathscr B\,],\qquad
 d_I(\phi,\psi)=\Theta(\phi-\mathcal F\psi)
 \tag{PL46}
\]
when \(I\ne\varnothing\), with a missing summand interpreted as
the zero coefficient at that retained mask. Let \(K_\varnothing=0\).
Define \(K_{I,\rm pr}\) by replacing the present source copies by
\(V_{\rm pr}\) and the target by \(\mathscr B_{\rm pr}\).
PL45 proves that this is a subcomplex and that the original
projection is the chain map
\((\mathsf P_V^{I},\mathsf P)\).
Its full quotient complex is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 E_I=[\,(\C^2)^{I}\longrightarrow\C^2\,],\qquad
 (b_+,b_-)\longmapsto b_+-Sb_-,\qquad E_\varnothing=0,\\
 0\longrightarrow K_{I,\rm pr}\longrightarrow K_I
       \xrightarrow{\,(\ell^{I},b)\,}E_I\longrightarrow0 .
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PL47}\label{pl:pl:endpoint-complex}
\]
The identity \(b\,d_I=\ell\phi-S\ell\psi\) proves that the
displayed surjection is a chain map. Its kernels are exactly
the indicated primitive spaces. A chain section is
\((A^{I},\Theta A)\): its compatibility follows from
\(\mathcal F A=AS\), and its composite with the quotient is the
identity by PL4--6. Thus the sequence splits as complexes.
The quotient, section, and projection all commute with the mask
inclusions, which are coordinate inclusions in degree zero and
identities in degree one.

For a singleton mask the differential of \(E_I\) is either
\(1\) or \(-S\), hence is invertible. For the joint mask its kernel
is \(\{(b,Sb):b\in\C^2\}\), and its differential is onto. Consequently
\[
 H^1(E_I)=0,\qquad
 H^0(E_I)=
 \begin{cases}\C^2,& I=\{+,-\},\\0,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
 \qquad
 H^1(K_{I,\rm pr})\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }H^1(K_I).
 \tag{PL48}\label{pl:pl:endpoint-cohomology}
\]
The last map is induced by inclusion. Its inverse is the original
primitive projection on degree-one classes. This proves exactly
which degree-zero directions are removed; it does not identify the
entire two complexes.

Finally retain the source action
\((U_p,pU_{1/p})\) and target action \(U_p\) from the original
tau complex. Put \(D_p=\operatorname{diag}(1,p)\).
On \(E_I\) the respective actions are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (D_p,SD_pS)\text{ on the present source legs},\qquad
 D_p\text{ on the target},\\
 D_p(b_+-Sb_-)=D_pb_+-S(SD_pS)b_-.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PL49}
\]
Thus the short exact sequence PL47 and the isomorphism in PL48
are prime-equivariant. On its joint-mask \(H^0=\C^2\), use
\(b\mapsto(b,Sb)\); the induced action is exactly \(D_p\).
The displayed section is not asserted to be prime-equivariant.
Its target defect is \(U_p\Theta A-\Theta A D_p\), and the two
source defects are \(U_pA-AD_p\) and
\(pU_{1/p}A-ASD_pS\), respectively. Each lands in its primitive
space because its two endpoint values are zero. These are the
chain components of the exact representative discrepancy PL9,
with the Fourier swap and both prime factors retained.

\subsection{The raw pairing and the whole Weil form of this lift}
Assume now that \(Z\) is invariant under
\(\iota\rho=1-\bar\rho\). For the actual local coordinates
\(t_\rho=s-\rho\), retain
\[
 g(\rho+t_\rho)=u_\rho(t_\rho)t_\rho^{m_\rho},
 \qquad u_\rho(0)=2\lambda_\rho,\qquad
 f^\dagger(s)=\overline{f(1-\bar s)}.
 \tag{PL23}
\]
On \(A_h\cong\prod_{\rho\in Z}\mathcal O_\rho/(g)\), use
\[
 R_h(u,v)=\sum_{\rho\in Z}\Res_\rho
       \frac{u^\dagger(s)v(s)}{g(s)}\,ds,\qquad J_g=M_{g'}.
 \tag{PL24}\label{pl:pl:raw}
\]
The germs in this equation may be any representatives.
Divisibility by \(g\) makes a change of representative holomorphic.
The local coefficient matrix of the residue is
\((-1)^j[t^{m_\rho-1-j-k}]u_\rho(t)^{-1}\);
its antidiagonal entries are \((-1)^j/(2\lambda_\rho)\ne0\).
It is therefore perfect, without replacing the unit.
Since \(g'/g=m_\rho/t_\rho+u_\rho'/u_\rho\),
\[
 R_h(u,J_gv)=T_h(u,v)
 =\sum_{\rho\in Z}m_\rho\overline{u(\iota\rho)}v(\rho).
 \tag{PL25}\label{pl:pl:Jacobian}
\]
The full kernel of \(J_g\), and radical of \(T_h\), is
\(\mathfrak n_h=\prod_{\rho\in Z}(t_\rho)\). Indeed locally
\(J_gv=2m_\rho\lambda_\rho v(\rho)t_\rho^{m_\rho-1}\).
The raw pairing detects the complementary higher jets and stays
perfect on those directions.

For \(F,H\in\mathscr B\), define the full reflected zero form
\[
 \mathcal W_g(F,H)=
 \sum_{\rho\in\mathscr Z}m_\rho
    \overline{\mathcal M F(1-\bar\rho)}\mathcal M H(\rho).
 \tag{PL26}\label{pl:pl:Weil}
\]
The sum is absolutely convergent. Repeated integration by parts in
\(\log x\) gives arbitrary inverse powers of \(|\Im\rho|\), uniformly
on \(0\le\Re\rho\le1\). The required zero-count bound also follows
directly from the retained seed. For \(x\ge1\), its Gaussian series
satisfies \(|\Theta\phi_*(x)|\le Cx^4e^{-\pi x^2}\): factor the first
exponential out of each summand and bound the remaining series by
\(\sum_{n\ge1}n^4e^{-\pi(n^2-1)}<\infty\).
The symmetry \(J\Theta\phi_*=\Theta\phi_*\), proved in PL45, gives
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 g(s)=\int_1^\infty\Theta\phi_*(x)(x^s+x^{1-s})\,\frac{dx}{x},
 \\
 \max_{|s|\le R}|g(s)|
 \le C\pi^{-(R+5)/2}\Gamma((R+5)/2)
 \\
 \le \exp(C'(R+1)\log(R+2))\quad(R\ge1).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{PL50}\label{pl:pl:g-growth}
\]
The gamma bound follows by splitting its integral at one and
bounding its tail by \(n!\), with \(n=\lceil(R+5)/2\rceil\);
\(n!\le n^n\). To count zeros, choose a radius \(r\) between
\(2R\) and \(3R\) whose circle has no zeros. Factor the finitely
many zeros inside that circle out of \(g\). The logarithm of the
modulus of the remaining nonvanishing factor is harmonic.
Its circle average equals its central value; the average of
\(\log|re^{i\theta}-\rho|\) is \(\log r\) for \(|\rho|<r\).
Since \(g(0)=1\), subtraction yields
\(\sum_{|\rho|<r}m_\rho\log(r/|\rho|)
=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}\log|g(re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta\).
Each zero with \(|\rho|\le R\) contributes at least \(\log2\).
PL50 therefore bounds their total multiplicity by
\(C''(R+1)\log(R+2)\).
Dyadic summation now proves
\(\sum_\rho m_\rho(1+|\Im\rho|)^{-2N}<\infty\) for \(N\ge2\),
because the zeros lie in the original critical strip.
This proves the absolute convergence asserted in PL26.
The human explicit formula with this transform is recalled below
from \cite{PLConnes}.

\begin{theorem}[Complete packet pullback, with its whole radical]
\label{pl:thm:pairing}
For all full jets \(u,v\in A_h\),
\[
 \mathcal W_g(\operatorname{Prim}_h(u),\operatorname{Prim}_h(v))
 =T_h(u,v)=R_h(u,J_gv).
 \tag{PL27}\label{pl:pl:pullback}
\]
The same full Weil form is obtained using \(s_h\) in either entry.
No zero outside \(Z\) is omitted: its summand is exactly zero.
The primitive family \(\operatorname{Prim}_h(A_h)\) has trace
radical \(\operatorname{Prim}_h(\mathfrak n_h)\), whose elements
are nonzero cohomology classes whenever their jets are nonzero.
The raw residue form transported through the inverse jet map on
this family is perfect.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At selected zeros, both representative maps have precisely the full
jets \(u,v\), by PL15. At every other actual zero, the transforms
\(U_hP_u\) and \(aU_hR_u\) have the full zero order of \(g\), because
\(h\) does not vanish there. Thus PL26 reduces exactly to PL25.
This proves all equalities with both representatives.
PL15 makes each representative map injective, also after passage to
\(Q\), since theta relations have zero actual jets. The radical is
therefore the exact inverse image of \(\mathfrak n_h\).
Perfectness of the transported raw form follows from PL24.
\end{proof}

A fixed reflected zero contributes \(m_\rho|u(\rho)|^2\).
A nonfixed reflection pair contributes the matrix
\(m_\rho\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\)
on its values. In particular the exact primitive lift transports
both signs of this matrix when such an actual pair is present.
It does not create positivity. The prime identity
\[
 \mathcal W_g(U_pF,U_pH)=p\,\mathcal W_g(F,H)
 \tag{PL28}
\]
follows term by term from
\(\overline{p^{1-\bar\rho}}p^\rho=p\).
PL22 shows that the same identity is induced by \(\Pi_p\) on
the complete primitive packet classes.

\subsection{The same primitive functions for the actual and control zeros}
Keep the supplied exact control constants
\(\delta_0=1/1000,\ \gamma_0=3\). Let \(\gamma_1,\gamma_2\)
be the actual first two positive critical ordinates, and put
\[
 Q_1(s)=((s-\tfrac12)^2+\gamma_1^2)
        ((s-\tfrac12)^2+\gamma_2^2),\qquad U(s)=g(s)/Q_1(s),
\]
\[
 Q_0(s)=((s-\tfrac12)^2-(\delta_0+i\gamma_0)^2)
        ((s-\tfrac12)^2-(\delta_0-i\gamma_0)^2),\qquad
 \widetilde g(s)=U(s)Q_0(s).
 \tag{PL29}\label{pl:pl:control}
\]
The selected actual zeros are simple. The low-height verified theorem
and exhaustive sign-change method in the original author source
\cite{PlattTrudgian}, Theorem \texttt{bank} and Section 2, supply these input facts:
the zero count counts multiplicities, and matching it by distinct
sign changes forces each counted zero to be simple. The same
low-height theorem excludes the off-critical points of \(Q_0\)
from the actual zero set. Thus \(U\) is a unit at every root of
\(Q_0Q_1\). No rounded ordinate is used.

For \(P\in\C[y]_{\le3}\), put \(y(s)=(s-\tfrac12)/i\) and retain
the same entire transform in both forms:
\[
 G_P(s)=a(s)P(y(s))U(s),\qquad F_P=\mathcal M^{-1}G_P.
 \tag{PL30}\label{pl:pl:same-tests}
\]
Its element \(F_P\in\mathscr B_{\rm pr}\) is constructed by PL12--14,
using \(Q_1\) and polynomial \(P(y(s))\); no unproved inverse-transform
membership is required.
Let \(Z_-\) and \(Z_+\) be the roots of \(Q_1,Q_0\), respectively.
On coefficient vectors \(p=(p_0,p_1,p_2,p_3)\), the two evaluation maps are
\[
 (E_\pm p)_z=w_z\sum_{k=0}^3p_k y(z)^k,\qquad
 w_z=a(z)U(z),\quad z\in Z_\pm.
 \tag{PL31}\label{pl:pl:Emaps}
\]
Both maps are invertible: their diagonal factors \(w_z\) are nonzero
and their Vandermonde nodes are distinct.
At \(z=\tau\in Z_-\), the factor is exactly
\(w_\tau=\tau(\tau-1)g'(\tau)/Q_1'(\tau)\).

\begin{theorem}[Actual/control full-Weil contrast and its paired defect]
\label{pl:thm:contrast}
For the same functions \(F_P,F_Q\),
\[
 \mathcal W_g(F_P,F_Q)=p^*E_-^*E_-q,\qquad
 \mathcal W_{\widetilde g}(F_P,F_Q)=p^*E_+^*R_{\rm ref}E_+q,
 \tag{PL32}\label{pl:pl:contrast}
\]
where \(R_{\rm ref}\) exchanges the two members of each of the two
reflected pairs in \(Z_+\). The forms have inertia \((4,0)\) and
\((2,2)\), respectively. These are the entire zero sums.
The exact connecting map and signed defect space are
\[
 C=E_+E_-^{-1}:\C^{Z_-}\longrightarrow\C^{Z_+},\qquad
 \Gamma_C=\{(u,Cu):u\in\C^{Z_-}\},
 \tag{PL33}
\]
\[
 \mathfrak d((u,Cu),(v,Cv))
       =T_{\widetilde g,Z_+}(Cu,Cv)-T_{g,Z_-}(u,v).
 \tag{PL34}\label{pl:pl:paired-defect}
\]
This is the restriction of the two actual Jacobian pairings with
their indicated signs; it is not declared to be an isometry.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every actual zero outside \(Z_-\), the transform \(G_P\)
vanishes, so the actual sum is exactly over \(Z_-\).
Those four zeros are critical and simple, giving the first matrix.
For the control, the only remaining nonzero values occur at \(Z_+\):
the retained zeros of \(U\) still annihilate \(G_P\).
The four inserted zeros are simple and form two nonfixed reflected
pairs, giving the second matrix. A Vandermonde matrix is invertible
because a cubic polynomial vanishing at four distinct nodes is zero.
Congruence by each \(E_\pm\) therefore preserves the inertia.
The two reflection blocks have eigenvectors \((1,1),(1,-1)\)
with eigenvalues \(1,-1\), proving the stated counts.
Eliminating the common polynomial coordinates gives exactly \(C\);
subtraction of the two forms gives PL34.
\end{proof}

More explicitly, order one reflected pair as \(\rho_1,\rho_2=\iota\rho_1\)
and put \(\omega_j=y(\rho_j)\) for all four inserted roots.
For the full Lagrange polynomials
\(L_j(y)=\prod_{k\ne j}(y-\omega_k)/(\omega_j-\omega_k)\), take
\[
 P(y)=\frac{L_1(y)}{a(\rho_1)g(\rho_1)/Q_1(\rho_1)}
      -\frac{L_2(y)}{a(\rho_2)g(\rho_2)/Q_1(\rho_2)}.
 \tag{PL35}\label{pl:pl:negative}
\]
Its inserted values are exactly \((1,-1,0,0)\), so
\(\mathcal W_{\widetilde g}(F_P,F_P)=-2\).
It is a nonzero polynomial; the first form in PL32 is positive
definite, so \(\mathcal W_g(F_P,F_P)>0\).
Every actual unit value and phase remains in PL35.

The graph also has an exact prime-action defect. Retain the actual
diagonal actions \((\Pi_{p,-}u)_\tau=p^\tau u_\tau\) and
\((\Pi_{p,+}v)_z=p^zv_z\), and define
\[
 \Delta_{p,n}=C\Pi_{p,-}^{\,n}-\Pi_{p,+}^{\,n}C,\qquad
 \mathfrak D_p(u)=(\Delta_{p,1}u,\ldots,\Delta_{p,4}u)
          \in(\C^{Z_+})^4 .
 \tag{PL51}\label{pl:pl:prime-graph-defect}
\]
For every \(p>1\), this stacked defect map is injective.
To prove it, put \(q_p(X)=\prod_{\tau\in Z_-}(X-p^\tau)\).
Then \(q_p(\Pi_{p,-})=0\). If \(\mathfrak D_p(u)=0\),
the four power identities together with the automatic zeroth
identity give
\(q_p(\Pi_{p,+})Cu=Cq_p(\Pi_{p,-})u=0\).
Every \(p^\tau\) has modulus \(p^{1/2}\), whereas the inserted
eigenvalues have moduli \(p^{1/2+\delta_0}\) or
\(p^{1/2-\delta_0}\). These are different, so
\(q_p(\Pi_{p,+})\) is invertible. Since \(C\) is invertible,
\(u=0\), proving injectivity.
The image of \(\mathfrak D_p\), with its displayed inclusion
in \((\C^{Z_+})^4\), is the exact four-step defect space.
In particular \(\Gamma_C\) contains no nonzero subspace invariant
under the ambient action \((\Pi_{p,-},\Pi_{p,+})\): iteration on
such a subspace would force all four defects to vanish.
This does not erase the connecting map \(C\) or its paired defect.

There is a corresponding exact formula for that pairing.
Set \(\Delta_p=\Delta_{p,1}\), \(T_-=T_{g,Z_-}\), and
\(T_+=T_{\widetilde g,Z_+}\). The individual prime similitudes
on \(T_-\) and \(T_+\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\mathfrak d((\Pi_{p,-}u,C\Pi_{p,-}u),
             (\Pi_{p,-}v,C\Pi_{p,-}v))
              -p\,\mathfrak d((u,Cu),(v,Cv))\\
 &\quad=T_+(\Pi_{p,+}Cu,\Delta_pv)
        +T_+(\Delta_pu,\Pi_{p,+}Cv)
        +T_+(\Delta_pu,\Delta_pv).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PL52}\label{pl:pl:paired-prime-defect}
\]
Indeed substitute \(C\Pi_{p,-}=\Pi_{p,+}C+\Delta_p\) into
the two slots of PL34 and expand its sesquilinear form.
The two terms without a \(\Delta_p\) scale by \(p\); their
subtraction leaves precisely the three terms displayed.
All phases and unit factors in \(C=E_+E_-^{-1}\) are retained.

For the full-multiplicity extension, take \(Q_-\) to contain the full
actual multiplicities of a reflection-stable packet and let \(Q_+\)
have a disjoint reflection-stable set of nontrivial-strip roots,
none an actual zero. Use equal total degree and the same chosen
leading coefficient in \(Q_+\) and \(Q_-\).
Reflection then multiplies both polynomials by the same factor,
so \((Q_+/Q_-)^\dagger=Q_+/Q_-\) and
\(\widetilde g=gQ_+/Q_-\) retains the reflection involution.
Replace each evaluation matrix by its full Hermite matrix
\(P\mapsto j_{Q_\pm}(aUP)\), where \(U=g/Q_-\).
Multiplication by the two full unit jets and Hermite interpolation
are invertible. The maps in PL33--34 then use the raw residue forms
with denominators \(g=UQ_-\) and \(\widetilde g=UQ_+\), and their
respective Jacobians. The local antidiagonal and contraction
calculation in PL24--25 proves the statement for every jet order;
in particular no higher-jet coefficient is replaced by its value.
The definition of \(\Delta_{p,n}\) and identity PL52 apply unchanged
with these full jet actions. For full packets whose source roots
are critical and target roots are off-critical, the same proof of injectivity
uses \(q_p(X)=\prod_{\tau}(X-p^\tau)^{m_\tau}\) and the first
\(\deg Q_-\) defects. This statement defines and computes the
defect on precisely those full packets; it makes no claim that
all actual roots are critical.

\subsection{Compact primitive representatives and the prime discrepancy}
Define the exact logarithmic change of variables
\[
 (\mathscr LF)(u)=e^{-u/2}F(e^{-u}),\qquad
 \widehat f(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(u)e^{-izu}\,du.
 \tag{PL36}
\]
Substitution gives
\(\widehat{\mathscr LF}(z)=\mathcal M F(\tfrac12+iz)\) and
\(\mathscr La(D)=(\partial_u^2-\tfrac14)\mathscr L\).
In particular the two Fourier conditions
\(\widehat f(\pm i/2)=0\) are precisely the two Mellin endpoint
conditions, with their signs kept.

Write \(F_P=a(D)H_P\), where
\(H_P=S_{Q_1}\Theta(P(y(D))\phi_*)\in\mathscr B\), and set
\(h_P=\mathscr LH_P\).
Choose a fixed smooth compactly supported \(\chi\) equal to one
on \([-1,1]\), and put \(\chi_R(u)=\chi(u/R)\). Then
\[
 f_{P,R}=(\partial_u^2-\tfrac14)(\chi_Rh_P)
             \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R),\qquad
 \widehat f_{P,R}(\pm i/2)=0.
 \tag{PL37}\label{pl:pl:compact}
\]
The last equalities follow by integration by parts against
\(e^{\pm u/2}\), with no boundary term.
Since \(H_P\in\mathscr B\), all derivatives of \(h_P\) decay faster than
every exponential at both ends. Thus \(f_{P,R}\to\mathscr LF_P\)
in every exponentially weighted smooth seminorm.
In particular, for any integer \(N\), integration by parts gives
a sequence \(\epsilon_R\to0\) such that, uniformly for
\(0\le\sigma\le1\),
\[
 |\widehat f_{P,R}((\sigma-\tfrac12)/i+t)
          -G_P(\sigma+it)|\le\epsilon_R(1+|t|)^{-N}.
 \tag{PL38}\label{pl:pl:approx}
\]
Here the Fourier argument is \(t+i(1/2-\sigma)\); hence
\(1/2+i[t+i(1/2-\sigma)]=\sigma+it\), fixing its sign.

For either zero set, let \(C_N^2=\sum_\rho
m_\rho(1+|\Im\rho|)^{-2N}\), finite for \(N\ge2\), and use its
weighted value norm \(\|v\|^2=\sum m_\rho|v_\rho|^2\).
PL38 bounds the norm difference by \(C_N\epsilon_R\).
The reflection permutation has norm one, so
\[
 |\mathcal W(F_{P,R},F_{P,R})-\mathcal W(F_P,F_P)|
 \le C_N\epsilon_R(2\|v(F_P)\|+C_N\epsilon_R).
 \tag{PL39}\label{pl:pl:error}
\]
This is the entire zero sum, including every zero outside the
selected packets. Apply it to both the actual and control zero sets.
For the nonzero polynomial PL35, the actual value is positive
and the control value is \(-2\). Taking the right side of PL39
less than half the positive actual value and less than one,
respectively, proves that both opposite signs persist for all
sufficiently large \(R\) within the compact primitive class.
These approximants need not retain the original theta class;
PL15 gives the exact same-class lift, while PL37 gives the
separate compact approximation with its proved topology and error.

For completeness, the rational-prime arithmetic distinguishes the
two forms exactly. Set
\[
 H(s)=\overline{G_P(1-\bar s)}G_Q(s),\qquad
 k(x)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\Re s=c}H(s)x^{-s}\,ds\quad(c>1).
 \tag{PL40}\label{pl:pl:correlation}
\]
Products of Mellin transforms from \(\mathscr B\) are transforms of their
multiplicative convolutions, so \(k\in\mathscr B\) and \(\mathcal M k=H\).
The classical explicit formula of Guinand and Weil, in the
convention written in the original author source \cite{PLConnes},
equations \texttt{bombieriexplicit}--\texttt{bombieriexplicit2}, is
\[
 \mathcal W_g(F_P,F_Q)
 =H(0)+H(1)
 -\sum_p(\log p)\sum_{r\ge1}\bigl(k(p^r)+p^{-r}k(p^{-r})\bigr)
 -\mathcal W_\infty(k),
 \tag{PL41}\label{pl:pl:explicit}
\]
\[
 \mathcal W_\infty(k)=(\log(4\pi)+\gamma_E)k(1)
 +\int_1^\infty
 \left(k(x)+x^{-1}k(x^{-1})-\frac2x k(1)\right)
                    \frac{dx}{x-x^{-1}}.
 \tag{PL42}
\]
The numerator vanishes at \(x=1\); all infinite-endpoint terms
converge by \(k\in\mathscr B\). Both pole terms in PL41 vanish for these
primitive tests. This invocation is a cited human explicit-formula
theorem, not a newly assumed positivity assertion.

The control has the additional exact rational term
\[
 d(s)=Q_0'(s)/Q_0(s)-Q_1'(s)/Q_1(s),
 \qquad
 \mathcal W_{\widetilde g}(F_P,F_Q)-\mathcal W_g(F_P,F_Q)
       =\sum_{z\in Z_+}H(z)-\sum_{\tau\in Z_-}H(\tau).
 \tag{PL43}\label{pl:pl:rational}
\]
The second equality follows directly by subtracting the two full
zero multisets; every retained zero and multiplicity cancels.
It is also exactly
\[
 \frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\Re s=c}
                    d(s)\bigl(H(s)+H(1-s)\bigr)\,ds.
 \tag{PL44}
\]
Indeed \(d(1-s)=-d(s)\). Integrate \(dH\) around a rectangle with
vertical sides \(c\) and \(1-c\), both outside the finite roots.
The horizontal integrals tend to zero because \(d\) is rational
and \(H\) decays rapidly in this fixed strip. Substitution
\(s\mapsto1-s\) in the left integral produces PL44. The residues
are \(+H(z)\) at each inserted root and \(-H(\tau)\) at each
removed root, proving equality with PL43.
Thus using the actual prime expression PL41 for the control
without PL43 would change the arithmetic form. The paired defect
PL34 records precisely this difference on the common primitive
family. No universal positivity or RH conclusion follows.
