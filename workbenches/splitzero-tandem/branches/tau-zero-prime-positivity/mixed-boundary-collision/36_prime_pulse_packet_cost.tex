\section{The evaluated cost of retaining all packet jets in shrinking prime pulses}
\label{sec:pulse-packet-cost}

The preceding full-jet observation gives a concrete way to
transport the positive pulse calculation into a complete finite
packet. This section evaluates the original source cost of that
transport, including its first matrix correction. The pulse,
Haar measure and coefficient amplitude are precisely FP4--6
\cite{FinitePrimePulse}; the conversion to the primitive Mellin
coordinate is the one used in PL1--10. The prime set and all
jet coordinates remain fixed where a matrix limit is taken.

Fix a finite reflected packet $Z$, with its actual multiplicities,
and its algebra $A=\prod_\rho\C[t_\rho]/t_\rho^{m_\rho}$,
$t_\rho=s-\rho$, $d=\sum_\rho m_\rho$. Fix a finite set $J$
of prime centres, allowing the original centre $1$, for which
the complete exponential-column map is onto:
\[
 P_J:\C^J\longrightarrow A,\qquad
 (c_r)\longmapsto\sum_{r\in J}c_r[r^{s-1/2}].
 \tag{PCO1}\label{pco:prime-columns}
\]
The preceding prime-column spanning proof constructs such a set
for every finite packet. This surjectivity is used only to
invert its exact positive row Gram, not to remove any Taylor
coefficient. Extend the ambient prime set $I$ containing the
prime centres in $J$, and retain its prescribed FP width
$\varepsilon=\frac1{16}\log(1+1/\prod_{p\in I}p)$.
All coefficients outside $J$ are zero. Enlarging $I$ gives
$\varepsilon\to0$ on this same coefficient face. No width or
amplitude in FP is replaced by an independently chosen one.

Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 w_0(v)=\begin{cases}e^{-1/(1-v^2)}&|v|<1,\\0&|v|\ge1,
             \end{cases}\qquad
 A_w=\int_{-1}^1(w_0'')^2\,dv,\quad
 B_w=\int_{-1}^1(w_0')^2\,dv,\quad
 C_w=\int_{-1}^1w_0^2\,dv,\\
 \mu_0=\int_{-1}^1w_0\,dv>0,\qquad
 \mu_2=\int_{-1}^1v^2w_0\,dv,\qquad
 \kappa_w=\frac{\mu_2}{2\mu_0},\qquad 0<\kappa_w<\frac12.
\end{gathered}
 \tag{PCO2}\label{pco:constants}
\]
Every constant is the indicated integral of the original bump.
In particular $A_w>0$: a smooth compactly supported function
with zero second derivative would be affine and hence zero,
whereas $w_0(0)=e^{-1}$. The literal FP source norm is
\[
 N_\varepsilon
 =A_w\varepsilon^{-3}+\frac{B_w}{2}\varepsilon^{-1}
                         +\frac{C_w}{16}\varepsilon.
 \tag{PCO3}\label{pco:mass}
\]
For proof, substitute $v=t/\varepsilon$ into the square of
$z(t)=\varepsilon^{-2}w_0''(t/\varepsilon)
                  -\frac14w_0(t/\varepsilon)$.
Its cross term is $-\frac12\varepsilon^{-1}\int w_0''w_0$.
Integration by parts, with both endpoint values and derivatives
zero, turns that integral into $-B_w$. This proves all three
terms and their signs.

Let $M$ be multiplication by $s-\frac12$ in the original jet
basis, and $M_a$ multiplication by $a(s)=s(s-1)$. The latter
is invertible since the selected nontrivial zeros avoid $0,1$.
Define the complete, commuting matrix
\[
 B_\varepsilon=\mu_0^{-1}\int_{-1}^1
                 w_0(v)e^{\varepsilon vM}\,dv.
 \tag{PCO4}\label{pco:unit}
\]
The integral is entrywise in the finite original coordinates;
the matrix exponential retains every nilpotent power. For the
literal pulse $g_c$ set $F_c(x)=x^{-1/2}g_c(x)$. The two norms
agree, since $\int|F_c|^2dx=\int|g_c|^2dx/x$. Its full Mellin
jet matrix is exactly
\[
 E_\varepsilon=\varepsilon\mu_0 M_a B_\varepsilon P_J.
 \tag{PCO5}\label{pco:observation}
\]
Indeed two integrations by parts give
$\int z(t)e^{\beta t}dt=(\beta^2-\frac14)
\varepsilon\int w_0(v)e^{\varepsilon\beta v}dv$.
The centre $\log r$ contributes $r^\beta$, with
$\beta=s-\frac12$, so $\beta^2-\frac14=a(s)$.
Applying the full Taylor map to this entire identity gives
PCO5, including every derivative of both factors.

The inverse and its error can be controlled in these same
coordinates. Write $m=\|M\|$ for the Euclidean operator norm
and $\varepsilon_0=(2\max(1,m))^{-1}$. Evenness of $w_0$ and
the matrix exponential series give, for $0<\varepsilon\le
\varepsilon_0$,
\[
 B_\varepsilon=I+\kappa_w\varepsilon^2M^2+R_\varepsilon,
 \qquad
 \|R_\varepsilon\|\le
       \frac{\varepsilon^4m^4e^{1/2}}{24}.
 \tag{PCO6}\label{pco:remainder}
\]
To prove the bound, retain terms through degree three in the
exponential series. The integrated odd terms vanish and the
remaining tail is bounded by
$|\varepsilon v|^4m^4e^{|\varepsilon v|m}/24$.
Use $|v|\le1$ and divide the integral by its mass $\mu_0$.
In particular $\|B_\varepsilon-I\|<1/2$, since the quadratic
bound is at most $1/8$ and the fourth-order bound is at most
$e^{1/2}/384<1/128$ (use $e^{1/2}<3$).
The convergent geometric series proves invertibility, and
\[
 B_\varepsilon^{-1}=I-\kappa_w\varepsilon^2M^2
                              +O_{Z}(\varepsilon^4).
 \tag{PCO7}\label{pco:inverse}
\]
Here the error is effective in the displayed coordinates. For
example set $r=m^4e^{1/2}/24$ and
$b=\kappa_wm^2+\varepsilon_0^2r$; its norm is at most
$\varepsilon^4(r+2b^2)$, by
$(I+Y)^{-1}=I-Y+Y^2(I+Y)^{-1}$ and
$\|(I+Y)^{-1}\|\le2$.

Define the fixed full packet metric
\[
 K_J=(M_aP_JP_J^*M_a^*)^{-1}.
 \tag{PCO8}\label{pco:fixed-metric}
\]
All cross entries and every confluent row are included. The
minimum of the actual Haar norm over the fibre
$E_\varepsilon c=u$ has Gram
\[
 H_\varepsilon
  =N_\varepsilon(E_\varepsilon E_\varepsilon^*)^{-1}
  =\frac{N_\varepsilon}{\varepsilon^2\mu_0^2}
                B_\varepsilon^{-*}K_JB_\varepsilon^{-1}.
 \tag{PCO9}\label{pco:attained}
\]
The first equality follows by writing the minimum solution as
$c=E_\varepsilon^*(E_\varepsilon E_\varepsilon^*)^{-1}u$:
all other solutions differ by an orthogonal kernel vector.
For the second equality, use PCO5 and the fact that $M_a$
commutes with $B_\varepsilon$, then invert by congruence.

Combining PCO3, PCO7 and PCO9 evaluates both leading orders:
\[
\begin{split}
 H_\varepsilon
 ={}&\frac{A_w}{\mu_0^2\varepsilon^5}K_J\\
 &+\frac1{\mu_0^2\varepsilon^3}
   \left[\frac{B_w}{2}K_J
     -A_w\kappa_w\big((M^2)^*K_J+K_JM^2\big)\right]
       +O_{Z,J}(\varepsilon^{-1}).
\end{split}
 \tag{PCO10}\label{pco:matrix-expansion}
\]
This is a matrix-norm expansion in the original jets. Products
of the explicit PCO7 error bounds with the three terms of PCO3
give its stated remainder. It retains the off-diagonal
confluent correction, which is lost by keeping only root values.

Its full determinant, including the multiplicities, is
\[
\begin{split}
 \log\det H_\varepsilon
 ={}&-5d\log\varepsilon+d\log(A_w/\mu_0^2)+\log\det K_J\\
 &+\varepsilon^2\left[
       \frac{dB_w}{2A_w}-2\kappa_w\operatorname{Re}\tr(M^2)
                        \right]+O_{Z,J}(\varepsilon^4),\\
 \tr(M^2)={}&\sum_{\rho\in Z}m_\rho(\rho-\tfrac12)^2.
\end{split}
 \tag{PCO11}\label{pco:determinant}
\]
Indeed PCO9 gives
$\det H_\varepsilon=(N_\varepsilon/(\varepsilon^2\mu_0^2))^d
\det K_J/|\det B_\varepsilon|^2$.
For a matrix $Y$ of norm below one,
$\log\det(I+Y)=\tr(Y)+O(\|Y\|^2)$ follows by taking traces
of the absolutely convergent logarithm series. Apply this to
PCO6, taking twice the real part for the squared modulus.
The scalar logarithm of PCO3 supplies the other quadratic term.
The displayed trace follows directly from each triangular
local block of $M$, with all $m_\rho$ diagonal entries retained.

Finally let $W_I$ be the complete positive FP matrix on the
fixed coefficient face $J$, at its ambient width. Its minimum
under the same observation is
$H_\varepsilon^{\rm Weil}
 =(E_\varepsilon W_I^{-1}E_\varepsilon^*)^{-1}$.
FP37 gives, with its proved original constant
$\mu_*=6600077509/144027072000$,
\[
 H_\varepsilon^{\rm Weil}\succeq\mu_*H_\varepsilon.
 \tag{PCO12}\label{pco:weil-cost}
\]
This follows by minimizing
$c^*W_Ic\ge\mu_*N_\varepsilon\|c\|^2$ on the same fibre,
so no change of section or observation is hidden. Let $W_Z$
be the reflected full-packet value form and use the preceding
exact primitive splitting of the pulse. The complementary
whole-zero contribution for its minimum-Weil lift is therefore
\[
 u^*(H_\varepsilon^{\rm Weil}-W_Z)u
 \ge\mu_*u^*H_\varepsilon u-u^*W_Zu.
 \tag{PCO13}\label{pco:complement}
\]
For every fixed nonzero $u$, PCO10 makes its lower bound
grow as a positive constant times $\varepsilon^{-5}$.
For a reflected nonfixed pair of multiplicity $m_\rho$ and
the value vector $(1,-1)$ with all higher jets and other
factors zero, $u^*W_Zu=-2m_\rho$. Its complete complementary
contribution is at least
$\mu_*u^*H_\varepsilon u+2m_\rho$.
This is the actual cost and complementary term of the
surjective positive pulse receiver. Every factor belongs to
the specified original test; no sign of an omitted root
packet is inferred from finite-pulse positivity alone.
