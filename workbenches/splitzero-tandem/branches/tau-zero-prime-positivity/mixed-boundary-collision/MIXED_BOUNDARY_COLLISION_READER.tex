\begin{abstract}
The existing all-prime boundary character and collision Frobenius
define a two-term comparison over the original integral coefficient
lattice. Its full tensor cokernels and torsion pairings are calculated.
The order-nine cotangent extension has zero connecting map in count
one and a nonzero order-three image in every count at least two.
An explicit torsion character detects the image, while the retained
cotangent injection has a precisely determined chain-lifting
obstruction and, wherever the lift exists, kills that image.
We also compute the actual Breuil--Kisin twist at the collision
prism with ideal \((3+\epsilon)\), its local divided-Frobenius
domain, and the exact map from the earlier comparison complex.
The former connecting class is a boundary there; different
constant and \(T\)-coordinate filtration defects survive.
Their exact return through local completion is also computed:
the transported filtration preserves them, while reconstructing
the filtration from the quotient ring loses precisely these groups.
All support labels remain. These are coefficient and local
prism calculations, not a computation of absolute syntomic
cohomology or a Weil positivity theorem.
\end{abstract}
