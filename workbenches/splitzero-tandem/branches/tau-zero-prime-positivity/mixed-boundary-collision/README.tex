\section{Collision coefficients, mixed boundaries and their exact
completion
return}\label{collision-coefficients-mixed-boundaries-and-their-exact-completion-return}

This edition publishes the complete local collision calculation and
integrates it into the programme's cumulative \textbf{Split Zero and the
Arithmetic Trace} source. The cumulative manuscript develops the
support-sensitive coefficient geometry, prime spectra and boundary
cohomology, the original-zeta Mellin and trace maps, marked metrics and
jets, prime-word actions, and the heat/residue constructions. This
addition supplies the actual adelic receiving proofs, collision-prism
coefficients, tensor filtrations and local completion maps. It does not
replace the programme by this latest calculation.

Start with the \href{MIXED_BOUNDARY_COLLISION_READER.pdf}{13-page
research reader}, with
\href{MIXED_BOUNDARY_COLLISION_READER.tex}{editable LaTeX},
\href{MIXED_BOUNDARY_COLLISION_EXTENSION.tex}{complete BCE1--45 proof},
and \href{MIXED_BOUNDARY_COLLISION_FIGURE.tex}{reproducible diagrams}.
The \href{MAIN.tex}{cumulative entry point} includes the full receiving
chapters and bibliography; its complete local input dependencies
accompany it. Compile that entry point from this directory. The earlier
collection's compiled reader is retained separately; this edition does
not describe it as containing the new chapter.

\subsection{What the new calculation
establishes}\label{what-the-new-calculation-establishes}

The coefficient algebra is the original
\texttt{C\ =\ Z\_3{[}epsilon,T{]}/(epsilon\^{}2,\ T\^{}2-6\ epsilon)},
with \texttt{z=epsilon\ T} and \texttt{F(T)=9cz}. The existing adelic
boundary characters and the specified collision Frobenius are compared
by explicit maps; they are not declared identical.

\subsection{Complete proofs and human
sources}\label{complete-proofs-and-human-sources}

The \href{collision_boundary/original_sources/}{source bank} preserves
the sealed reader, original proof files and independent derivations.
Every Markdown derivation has a complete editable LaTeX counterpart.
\href{LATEX_CONVERSION.json}{Conversion checks} record all retained
mathematical expressions and equation tags; these are source-conversion
checks, not new kernel certification.

The cumulative receiving chapter contains the full
\href{collision_boundary/ABR.tex}{ABR},
\href{collision_boundary/GBC.tex}{GBC} and
\href{collision_boundary/GSB.tex}{GSB} arguments, the
\href{collision_boundary/DP.tex}{DP1--43 prism derivation},
\href{collision_boundary/CFF.tex}{CFF},
\href{collision_boundary/CF.tex}{CF} and
\href{collision_boundary/BCE.tex}{BCE}, alongside the existing
\href{45_boundary_tensor_weights.tex}{all-prime boundary calculation}
and \href{15_full_finite_support_recollement.tex}{full finite-support
receiver}. Readers do not have to reconstruct these inputs from a result
code alone.

Human foundations include
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9811068v1}{Connes's trace-formula
paper},
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703392v1}{Connes--Consani--Marcolli on
the adelic Weil proof},
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08229v4}{Bhatt--Scholze on prisms}, and
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06120v1}{Bhatt--Lurie on absolute
prismatic cohomology}. Exact source labels and the scope of their use
remain in the reader and
\href{collision_boundary/bibliography.tex}{bibliography}. The programme
calculations are derived comparisons using those human sources, not
replacements for or claims of authorship of their theorems.

\subsection{Verification and
reproducibility}\label{verification-and-reproducibility}

\href{SOURCE_INTEGRATION.json}{Source integration} records the original
and integrated manuscript hashes, complete input dependencies, ordered
equation tags and the mathematical owner's separately attributed
validation. The owner compiled the complete temporary manuscript to 457
pages with no unresolved references or duplicate labels; the added
chapters had no overfull boxes. Twelve existing overfull boxes elsewhere
were retained, not silently repaired. That temporary validation PDF is
not included as a publication reader.

The publisher rebuilt the standalone reader solely to make its two
programme-source links work online; \href{READER_BUILD.json}{the build
record} describes that repair. The original sealed PDF and TeX remain in
the source bank. No new Lean execution or independent reproof of every
earlier chapter is claimed. \href{RESULT_INDEX.json}{The
machine-readable result index} and \href{SOURCE_MANIFEST.json}{source
manifest} identify this edition's actual files and scope.
