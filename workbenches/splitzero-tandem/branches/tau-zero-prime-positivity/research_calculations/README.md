# Integral collision and residue calculations

These complete source calculations supplement the30-proof supported-zero reader. [STATUS.json](STATUS.json) records each source's actual review status. The accompanying LaTeX is generated from each complete Markdown source, with equation bodies preserved. These separate research files are not represented as additional chapters already reviewed in the210-page reader.

The calculations study the integral collision algebra, its prime-three fibre, residue/Frobenius maps, cotangent groups and nilpotent directions. The complete mathematical statements, coefficient rings and maps remain in the sources. They do not assert an RH decision. Human and programme sources remain credited at their points of use.
