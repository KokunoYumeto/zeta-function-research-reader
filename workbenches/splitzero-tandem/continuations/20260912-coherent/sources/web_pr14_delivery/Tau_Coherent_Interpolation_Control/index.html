5. A global density result, with its precise cohomological consequence

The single derivative family {Dnf0:n≥0}\{D^nf_0:n\ge0\} is already dense in ℋ\mathcal H.

Proof. Put dν(t)=|g(1/2+it)|2dt/(2π)d\nu(t)=|g(1/2+it)|^2dt/(2\pi). It has an exponential moment of every order c<π/2c<\pi/2. To verify this, the alternating-series formula and partial summation give

|ζ(12+it)|≤2|12+it|2−1.|\zeta(\tfrac12+it)|\le\frac{2|\tfrac12+it|}{\sqrt2-1}.

|g(12+it)|2≤C(1+|t|)11/2e−π|t|/2.|g(\tfrac12+it)|^2\le C(1+|t|)^{11/2}e^{-\pi|t|/2}.

The constant CC is a fixed bound, not a numerical enclosure claimed here. The gamma fact used is NIST DLMF 5.11.9. The same exponential-moment property holds for νh\nu_h, since vhv_h is entire at the finitely many possible denominator zeros and has polynomially modified tails.

Eh(k)=Eh⊗k,Ah(k)=∑j=1kI⊗(j−1)⊗Ah⊗I⊗(k−j).E_h^{(k)}=E_h^{\otimes k},\quad A_h^{(k)}=\sum_{j=1}^k I^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes A_h\otimes I^{\otimes(k-j)}.

The existing product complex over 𝔟τ\mathfrak b_\tau has differential

d(x1⊗⋯⊗xk)=∑j(−1)∑i<j|xi|x1⊗⋯⊗dxj⊗⋯⊗xk.d(x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k)=\sum_j(-1)^{\sum_{i<j}|x_i|}x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes dx_j\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k.

Define 𝒫M(k)=ℂ[t1,…,tk]total degree≤M\mathcal P_M^{(k)}=\mathbb C[t_1,\ldots,t_k]_{\text{total degree}\le M} for M≥k(d−1)M\ge k(d-1) and

𝒯h(k)P=P(D1,…,Dk)(Fh⊗k).\mathcal T_h^{(k)}P=P(D_1,\ldots,D_k)(F_h^{\otimes k}).\tag{7.1}
(7.1)

Its Mellin transform is P(s1,…,sk)∏jg(sj)/h(sj)P(s_1,\ldots,s_k)\prod_j g(s_j)/h(s_j). Its full jet map Jk,MJ_{k,M} is reduction modulo each h(tj)h(t_j) followed by multiplication by υh⊗k\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}. The target is Eh(k)E_h^{(k)} and this map is surjective. The tensor remainder basis has total degree at most k(d−1)k(d-1).

The exact relation space is

ℐk,M=𝒫M(k)∩(h(t1),…,h(tk)).\mathcal I_{k,M}=\mathcal P_M^{(k)}\cap(h(t_1),\ldots,h(t_k)).\tag{7.2}
(7.2)

Polynomial division in the fixed order t1,…,tkt_1,\ldots,t_k represents each member as ∑jh(tj)Pj\sum_j h(t_j)P_j, with degPj≤M−d\deg P_j\le M-d. It terminates because every hh is monic and replacing a leading power lowers total degree. Under (7.1), each term is an actual top cochain boundary: in the jj-th source factor use ϕ*\phi_* in place of FhF_h and give its primitive the factor (−1)j−1(-1)^{j-1}. The differential contributes the second (−1)j−1(-1)^{j-1}. The resulting boundary is +h(tj)Pj+h(t_j)P_j with no removed sign.

Thus these joint corrections are maps in the original product complex, not freely chosen positive tensor metrics. Set ℬk,M=𝒯h(k)ℐk,M\mathcal B_{k,M}=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}\mathcal I_{k,M}.

The moment matrix on the monomials of total degree at most MM is

(Mk,M)αβ=∏j=1k∫stαj¯stβjdνh(t).\boxed{(M_{k,M})_{\alpha\beta}=\prod_{j=1}^k\int\overline{s_t^{\alpha_j}}s_t^{\beta_j}\,d\nu_h(t).}\tag{7.3}
(7.3)

Its positive definiteness follows because a polynomial cannot vanish almost everywhere on the full real product unless it is zero. It is a principal submatrix of the stated product moment matrix, not a declaration that the total-degree space is a tensor product of smaller univariate spaces.

The actual joint least-norm representative and its metric are

Rk,M=𝒯h(k)Mk,M−1Jk,M*(Jk,MMk,M−1Jk,M*)−1,\boxed{R_{k,M}=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}M_{k,M}^{-1}J_{k,M}^* (J_{k,M}M_{k,M}^{-1}J_{k,M}^*)^{-1},}
Gk,M=(Jk,MMk,M−1Jk,M*)−1.\boxed{G_{k,M}=(J_{k,M}M_{k,M}^{-1}J_{k,M}^*)^{-1}.}\tag{7.4}
(7.4)

The proof is the same explicit constrained-minimum calculation as (3.6), now with the relation ideal (7.2). In particular its arithmetic class is exactly σh⊗k\sigma_h^{\otimes k} and every jet is fixed.

For a rectangular degree budget NN one has the actual inclusion

𝒫N⊗k↪𝒫kN(k).\mathcal P_N^{\otimes k}\hookrightarrow\mathcal P_{kN}^{(k)}.

It carries the old product representative into the feasible set of the new problem. Therefore

Gk,kN≼Gh,N−d⊗k(N≥d).G_{k,kN}\preceq G_{h,N-d}^{\otimes k}\qquad(N\ge d).\tag{7.5}
(7.5)

This is an inequality from an inclusion of admissible original boundaries. It is not asserted to imply the same ordering of control forms, whose dependence on AA is retained separately.

8. The product error is one new total-degree layer

Let ℋk=L2(ℝ>0k,dkx)\mathcal H_k=L^2(\mathbb R_{>0}^k,d^kx), and let Pℬk,MP_{\mathcal B_{k,M}} be its orthogonal projection onto the finite-dimensional actual boundary space. With the fixed low-degree section sh⊗ks_h^{\otimes k},

Rk,M=(1−Pℬk,M)sh⊗k.R_{k,M}=(1-P_{\mathcal B_{k,M}})s_h^{\otimes k}.

Put

ℰk,M=(1−Pℬk,M)ℬk,M+1.\mathcal E_{k,M}=(1-P_{\mathcal B_{k,M}})\mathcal B_{k,M+1}.

Because the jet map is already onto at both degrees,

dimℰk,M=(M+kk−1).\boxed{\dim\mathcal E_{k,M}=\binom{M+k}{k-1}.}\tag{8.1}
(8.1)

This is dim𝒫M+1(k)−dim𝒫M(k)\dim\mathcal P_{M+1}^{(k)}-\dim\mathcal P_M^{(k)}; the dkd^k jet constraints cancel from the dimension difference.

Let D(k)=∑jDjD^{(k)}=\sum_jD_j. Its induced action on jets is Ah(k)A_h^{(k)}. The actual derivative defect lies in ℬk,M+1\mathcal B_{k,M+1}:

Bk,M=D(k)Rk,M−Rk,MAh(k).B_{k,M}=D^{(k)}R_{k,M}-R_{k,M}A_h^{(k)}.

Define the two explicitly typed maps into the same new relation layer

Yk,M=Pℰk,MRk,M:Eh(k)→ℰk,M,Y_{k,M}=P_{\mathcal E_{k,M}}R_{k,M}:E_h^{(k)}\to\mathcal E_{k,M},
Ck,M=(1−Pℬk,M)Bk,M:Eh(k)→ℰk,M.C_{k,M}=(1-P_{\mathcal B_{k,M}})B_{k,M}:E_h^{(k)}\to\mathcal E_{k,M}.

Integration by parts gives (D(k))*+D(k)=kI(D^{(k)})^*+D^{(k)}=kI on these functions. Orthogonality then proves

Wk,M:=(Ah(k))*Gk,M+Gk,MAh(k)−kGk,M=−(Yk,M*Ck,M+Ck,M*Yk,M),\boxed{W_{k,M}:=(A_h^{(k)})^*G_{k,M}+G_{k,M}A_h^{(k)}-kG_{k,M} =-(Y_{k,M}^*C_{k,M}+C_{k,M}^*Y_{k,M}),}\tag{8.2}
(8.2)
Gk,M−Gk,M+1=Yk,M*Yk,M,rankWk,M≤2(M+kk−1).\boxed{G_{k,M}-G_{k,M+1}=Y_{k,M}^*Y_{k,M},\qquad \operatorname{rank}W_{k,M}\le2\binom{M+k}{k-1}.}\tag{8.3}
(8.3)

For (8.3), use the orthogonal decomposition ℬk,M+1=ℬk,M⊕ℰk,M\mathcal B_{k,M+1}=\mathcal B_{k,M}\oplus\mathcal E_{k,M}. Its projection sends Rk,MR_{k,M} to Yk,MY_{k,M} and the new representative is Rk,M−Yk,MR_{k,M}-Y_{k,M}.

The source quotient map is the same degree transport as before:

ℬk,M+1/ℬk,M→∼ℰk,M,[z]↦(1−Pℬk,M)z.\mathcal B_{k,M+1}/\mathcal B_{k,M}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal E_{k,M},\quad[z]\mapsto(1-P_{\mathcal B_{k,M}})z.

A derivative class in this kernel becomes the supported zero only at the next total-degree label. The primitive retains which tensor factor supplied the theta boundary and its cochain sign. Synchronization by (1,e)(1,e) in each required two-leg factor supplies the common joint support, while the original mask remains recorded.

Thus the product estimate need not simply copy kk identical one-factor errors: (8.2) gives a new jointly optimized boundary-layer expression with completely specified moment data and maps. Its rank bound alone does not bound its generalized eigenvalues.

9. Same-metric reflection and residue duality on the product

For a reflection-stable packet, jh⊗kj_h^{\otimes k} is the antilinear involution on Eh(k)E_h^{(k)}, defined on pure tensors by applying jhj_h to every factor. The corresponding function reflection is F(x1,…,xk)↦∏jxj−1F(x1−1,…,xk−1)¯F(x_1,\ldots,x_k)\mapsto\prod_jx_j^{-1}\overline{F(x_1^{-1},\ldots,x_k^{-1})}.

It preserves both the total-degree numerator space and its relation ideal. Since (g/h)†=(−1)d(g/h)(g/h)^\dagger=(-1)^d(g/h), its action on a numerator is (−1)kdP†(-1)^{kd}P^\dagger; this factor is retained. Uniqueness of the minimum in (7.4) proves reflection compatibility of Rk,MR_{k,M}. Therefore the analogue of (6.4) gives symmetry of the generalized spectrum of (Wk,M,Gk,M)(W_{k,M},G_{k,M}) on the same metric.

Residue duality remains the explicitly specified map with matrix Sh⊗kS_h^{\otimes k} and target line ℒk\mathcal L_k, on which dilation acts by aka^k. It satisfies

(Ah(k))*Sh⊗k+Sh⊗kAh(k)=kSh⊗k.(A_h^{(k)})^*S_h^{\otimes k}+S_h^{\otimes k}A_h^{(k)}=kS_h^{\otimes k}.

Any fixed degree-orientation scalar in the cochain evaluation is retained separately; the ordered product of the scalar residue pairings here has matrix exactly Sh⊗kS_h^{\otimes k}.

Its dual metric and control are

Gk,MD=(Sh⊗k)*Gk,M−1Sh⊗k,G_{k,M}^{\mathrm D}=(S_h^{\otimes k})^*G_{k,M}^{-1}S_h^{\otimes k},
Wk,MD=−(Sh⊗k)*Gk,M−1Wk,MGk,M−1Sh⊗k.W_{k,M}^{\mathrm D}=-(S_h^{\otimes k})^*G_{k,M}^{-1}W_{k,M}G_{k,M}^{-1}S_h^{\otimes k}.\tag{9.1}
(9.1)

The Jacobian contraction is still the original multiplication trace, with every multiplicity, and is unchanged by the source boundaries used to construct (7.4). Neither (9.1) nor reflection alone establishes an arithmetic upper bound.

10. Quantitative certificate and the Deligne comparison

For one or several variables write MM for the positive moment matrix, JJ for its fixed full-jet map, and 𝒞=JM−1J*\mathcal C=JM^{-1}J^*. These actual matrices now replace an unrestricted metric search.

A useful deterministic enclosure is as follows. Suppose a Hermitian approximation M̂\widehat M has a verified bound ∥M−M̂∥≤η\|M-\widehat M\|\le\eta and M̂≽μI\widehat M\succeq\mu I for a retained μ>η\mu>\eta. Then

∥M−1−M̂−1∥≤ημ(μ−η),∥𝒞−JM̂−1J*∥≤∥J∥2ημ(μ−η).\|M^{-1}-\widehat M^{-1}\|\le\frac{\eta}{\mu(\mu-\eta)},\quad \|\mathcal C-J\widehat M^{-1}J^*\|\le\|J\|^2\frac{\eta}{\mu(\mu-\eta)}.\tag{10.1}
(10.1)

This is the resolvent identity for inverses. The positive lower bound on 𝒞\mathcal C must also be retained before a second inversion. With Ĝ\widehat G and Ŵ\widehat W and verified errors ηG,ηW\eta_G,\eta_W, the sufficient matrix certificate is

Ĝ−ηGI≻0,ϵĜ±Ŵ−(ϵηG+ηW)I≽0.\widehat G-\eta_GI\succ0,\qquad \epsilon\widehat G\pm\widehat W-(\epsilon\eta_G+\eta_W)I\succeq0.\tag{10.2}
(10.2)

No numerical interval enclosure for the actual infinite arithmetic moments is claimed merely from a floating-point calculation. The previous exterior-theta sums give another exact representation of their one-variable inputs; all tails and roundoff must be supplied to (10.1).

Deligne’s retained Corollaries 3.3.4–3.3.6 place upper and dual lower bounds on the same compact-to-ordinary image; the tensor step in 3.2.13 improves an estimate only after additional geometric control. This note supplies a canonical coherent family, its exact source relation ideal on products, and the same-metric reflection/dual operations with which such an upper estimate can now be tested. It does not import finite-field hypotheses into the tau chart.

For an actual eigenvector Ahv=ρvA_hv=\rho v, every constructed tensor metric still has the exact diagnostic

(v⊗k)*Wk,Mv⊗k(v⊗k)*Gk,Mv⊗k=k(2ℜρ−1).\frac{(v^{\otimes k})^*W_{k,M}v^{\otimes k}} {(v^{\otimes k})^*G_{k,M}v^{\otimes k}} =k(2\Re\rho-1).\tag{10.3}
(10.3)

The quantity to bound is therefore the generalized boundary form (8.2) relative to (7.4). A dimension/rank estimate, a decrease of a Gram matrix, or unrestricted metric realizability is not substituted for that relative estimate. The new constructive route is the exact finite full-jet moment problem together with its next-layer flux, not another homotopy choice.
