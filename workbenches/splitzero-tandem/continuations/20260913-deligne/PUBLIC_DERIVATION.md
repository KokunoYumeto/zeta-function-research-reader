# Public derivative of the completed 715-page source cut

The readable PDF, all TeX/Lean/HTML mathematics, all images, and all ten nested
delivery archives retain their exact owner bytes. The current `scripts/` build
entrypoints are unchanged. This is not a new mathematical or Lean test run.

Private workstation prefixes in historical receipts and six historical helper
scripts are replaced by explicit `package:`, `workspace:`, `corpus:`,
`runtime:` and `local:user-profile` aliases. These aliases identify provenance
roles, not executable local paths. The six historical helpers remain evidence
of past workflows, not a portable replay interface. Their original hashes,
execution claims, and input pins are retained as historical evidence; an
aliased receipt is not claimed to be its original bytes. The separate
[change ledger](PUBLIC_DERIVATION.json) identifies every original/derived hash.

One reference-only cache containing three full French pages from Deligne's
Weil II is omitted. [Bibliographic/page/hash provenance](REFERENCE_CACHE_PROVENANCE.json)
is retained instead. The original build does not read that cache; references
to its original hash in historical manifests describe the private owner cut.
All complete authored mathematical source witnesses remain. No new blanket
licence is asserted over third-party materials.

`CURRENT_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` inventories this public derivative, excluding
itself. The original manifest and README are retained under
`history/public_derivative_20260913/`; inherited manifests elsewhere keep their
original historical scope, and may identify pre-derivative metadata hashes.
