\[
 \begin{gathered}

 \ker J=K_l,\qquad
 \dim W_\vee=\deg l=\deg a+\deg b-\deg d .
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{DC12}
\]
Well-definedness follows from $a,b\mid l$.  If both components vanish,
$a$ and $b$ divide $P$; their prime-power factorizations show that $l$
divides $P$.  This proves injectivity.  To prove surjectivity with an
explicit inverse, write $a=da'$, $b=db'$ and compute polynomials
$u,v$ with $ua'+vb'=1$.  Given compatible representatives
$X$ modulo $a$ and $Y$ modulo $b$, $Y-X=dT$ for some $T\in R$.
Then
\[
 P=X+auT,\qquad
 P-X=auT,\qquad P-Y=-bvT.
 \tag{DC13}
\]
It has the required two residues.  A different choice of
representatives, Bezout coefficients, or $T$ changes $P$ by a
multiple of both $a$ and $b$, hence of $l$.  This proves the
well-defined inverse and the entire isomorphism.  The Euclidean
algorithm terminates because its successive nonzero remainders have
strictly decreasing degrees, and its back substitution gives $u,v$.
The same proof covers $a=1$, $b=1$, or $d=1$.

\[
 0\longrightarrow E_l

 \tag{DC14}
\]
The difference map is onto because its first component is onto;

In parallel, the kernel diamond has
\[

 \end{gathered}
 \tag{DC15}

The intersection follows from the same divisibility argument.
Bezout applied to $a,b$ gives $d=ua+vb$, which proves the equality
of the sum with $K_d$.  The displayed signs then prove exactness.
Every map in (DC14)--(DC15) commutes with multiplication by $S$.

\subsubsection{The exact extra circle summand}

Squarefreeness of the actual circle polynomial gives a more explicit
joint-image formula.  Put $b_{\rm new}=b/d$.  Its roots are exactly
the sampled roots absent from $a$, so
$\gcd(a,b_{\rm new})=1$ and $l=ab_{\rm new}$.  Denote by
$W_{\rm new}$ the actual circle coordinate subspace on these
roots, with its inherited metric
$\sum_{S_n:\,b_{\rm new}(S_n)=0}\nu_n|v_n|^2$ and diagonal
action $S_n$.  There is an exact isomorphism
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 W_\vee\xrightarrow{\sim}W_a\oplus W_{\rm new},\qquad
 (x,y)\longmapsto(x,y_{\rm new}),\\
 (x,z)\longmapsto(x,y),\qquad
 y_n=
 \begin{cases}
       (j_a^{-1}x)(S_n),&d(S_n)=0,\\
       z_n,&b_{\rm new}(S_n)=0 .
 \end{cases}
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{DC28}
\]
Evaluation in the first case is independent of a polynomial
representative because $a(S_n)=0$.  It gives exactly the shared
reduction in (DC10); the two cases exhaust the sampled roots.
Thus the displayed maps are mutually inverse.  They intertwine the
original actions, since evaluation of $SP$ gives $S_nP(S_n)$.
This is the actual-image transport of the CRT isomorphism
$R/(l)\simeq R/(a)\oplus R/(b_{\rm new})$.
Its first factor retains the original unit-weighted critical map
and all of its nilpotent jets; its second factor retains the actual
new circle amplitudes and weights.  It asserts an isomorphism of
finite-dimensional $R$-modules.  If one assigns the actual circle
norm to the second component of $W_\vee\subset W_a\oplus W_b$,
that norm under (DC28) is exactly
$\sum_{d(S_n)=0}\nu_n|(j_a^{-1}x)(S_n)|^2+
\sum_{b_{\rm new}(S_n)=0}\nu_n|z_n|^2$.
The shared first sum remains present, so the module isomorphism
has not silently been asserted to preserve a chosen direct-sum
metric on $W_a\oplus W_{\rm new}$.

The new circle coordinates have an explicit original polynomial
source lift.  For a prescribed vector
$z=(z_n)_{b_{\rm new}(S_n)=0}$, set
\[
 P_z(S)=a(S)\sum_{b_{\rm new}(S_n)=0}
 \frac{z_n}{a(S_n)}
 \prod_{\substack{b_{\rm new}(S_m)=0\\m\ne n}}
              \frac{S-S_m}{S_n-S_m}.
 \tag{DC29}
\]
Each $a(S_n)$ and each $S_n-S_m$ in a denominator is nonzero,
by the proved coprimality and distinct sampled frequencies.
The factor $a$ puts $[P_z]_\chi$ in $K_a$.
At a new sample the interpolation product gives exactly $z_n$;
at a shared sample the factor $a$ gives zero.  Consequently
$C[P_z]_\chi=0$ and $\theta[P_z]_\chi=(0,z)$, with squared
circle norm $\sum\nu_n|z_n|^2$ on precisely the new samples.
At source level the lift is the unchanged
$\mathcal V_{h,k}P_z$, whose cohomology class is
$\sigma_h^{\widehat\otimes k}\eta_k[P_z]_\chi$.
Thus the circle summand in (DC28) has an actual unit-weighted
arithmetic source, including every coefficient $a(S_n)^{-1}$.
When there are no new samples, the sum and its lift are zero.
For $k=1$, every sampled root of $\chi=h$ is itself critical,
so $b\mid a$ and this extra circle summand is always zero.
