# Public derivative of the 765-page R57 source cut

The reader presents Split-Zero arithmetic source and quotient maps, original
theta metrics, finite determinant and volume comparisons, SGA residue/trace
maps and period curvature, and the Hochschild critical-comparison kernel with
its original-metric Laplacian control. The source concludes at R57. Later
R58-R62, finite-circle and holonomy research are outside this particular cut.

The PDF, all TeX/Lean/HTML mathematics, images and all eleven nested delivery
archives retain their exact owner bytes. Current `scripts/` entrypoints are
unchanged. This is not a new mathematical proof, numerical test or Lean run.

Exactly 143 historical receipt/helper files have narrow workstation prefixes
replaced by `package:`, `workspace:`, `corpus:`, `runtime:` or
`local:user-profile` aliases. Seven of these are historical Python helpers;
their non-string syntax trees are unchanged and they were not executed.
These aliases describe source roles rather than portable executable paths.
Historical input hashes and execution outcomes keep their original scope:
aliased receipts are not claimed to retain their original byte identity.
[The complete change ledger](PUBLIC_DERIVATION.json) records both identities.

One inherited reference-only cache containing three full French pages of
Deligne's Weil II is omitted. [Bibliographic/page/hash provenance](REFERENCE_CACHE_PROVENANCE.json)
is retained, byte-identically to the preceding public derivative's notice.
The original build does not read that cache. No authored mathematics or build
dependency is removed. No blanket licence over third-party material is added.

`CURRENT_SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` inventories this entire public derivative except
itself. The original manifest and README remain exact under
`history/public_derivative_20260913_sga_connes/`. Other historical manifests may
refer to private originals or former generated-byte identities; the current
public inventory and the change ledger distinguish these scopes.

The published 715-page edition remains unchanged. Relative to its raw source
cut, this separate owner 765 cut keeps 3,051 same-path files exact, changes 883,
omits 16 old paths and adds 463. These counts are source-cut comparisons, not
deletions from a prior published edition. The public locator aliases and cache
omission are further, separately disclosed transformations of this 765 cut.

The appendix deliberately retains the historically damaged metric paragraph
after SGA50; its source-role notice points to complete correction SGAC7.
Original missing relative links also remain historical. No PDF bytes are
silently rewritten by this distribution process.
