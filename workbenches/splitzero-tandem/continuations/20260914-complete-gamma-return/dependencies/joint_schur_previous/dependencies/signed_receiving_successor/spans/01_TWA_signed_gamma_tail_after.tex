For completeness, retain the original full-vector products
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \mathscr U_{l,N}=\prod_{j=0}^{l-1}[4(N+j+1)^2+t_j^2],\\
 D_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}=\prod_{a=0}^{N}\prod_{r=0}^{2l-1}
                              (a+r+\tfrac12),\\
 X_{l,N}=(N+1)\log\mathscr U_{l,N}-\log D_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}\geq0,
 \qquad Z_k=q\sum_{a=0}^3\epsilon_a\log\mathscr U_{l,N_a},\\
 Z_k-X_{l,q-1}-X_{l,q}\leq\mathfrak T_k
       \leq Z_k+X_{l,2q-1}+X_{l,2q}.
\end{gathered}\tag{TWA31}
\]
The Jacobi coefficients of the original Gamma source give
$\|yP\|_\sigma\leq2(d+1)\|P\|_\sigma$ at degree $d$.
The identity $\|(y+it)P\|_\sigma^2
=\|yP\|_\sigma^2+t^2\|P\|_\sigma^2$ proves the upper
factor $\mathscr U_{l,N}$ by successive multiplication.
The exact monic ratios are
$\gamma_{k,a}/(\beta_k\gamma_a)=\prod_{r=0}^{2l-1}(a+r+1/2)$,
so their product is $D_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}$ on source dimension
$N+1$. The full relation-kernel Schur complement used in TWA15
bounds the quotient deficit between zero and $X_{l,N}$.
Keeping the four signs proves TWA31.

The complete signed relation calculation GSR1--33 now sharpens
this enclosure at its actual two low and two high endpoints.
For $l\geq1$, $N\geq q-1$, and $r_N=N+1-q$, put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 R_N^{\rm Chr}
 =\frac{\det\operatorname{Gram}_\sigma
         (f_l\chi,f_l\chi S,\ldots,f_l\chi S^{r_N-1})}
        {\det\operatorname{Gram}_\sigma
         (\chi,\chi S,\ldots,\chi S^{r_N-1})},\\
 \mathfrak r_\chi
 =\frac{\displaystyle\int|f_l(c+iy)\chi(c+iy)|^2\,d\sigma(y)}
        {\displaystyle\int|\chi(c+iy)|^2\,d\sigma(y)},\\
 C_k^{\rm Chr}=\log D_{l,q-1}^{\rm Chr}+\log D_{l,q}^{\rm Chr}
 -\log\mathfrak r_\chi
 -\log D_{l,2q-1}^{\rm Chr}-\log D_{l,2q}^{\rm Chr},\\
 \mathfrak T_k=C_k^{\rm Chr}
                 +\log R_{2q-1}^{\rm Chr}+\log R_{2q}^{\rm Chr}.
\end{gathered}\tag{TWA31a}
\]
Both empty relation determinants are one at $N=q-1$.
At $N=q$ their ratio is precisely $\mathfrak r_\chi$.
To prove the displayed return, the monic coefficient frame
$(1,\ldots,S^{q-1},\chi,\ldots,\chi S^{N-q})$
has determinant one. Its block determinant gives
$\det G_N^f=\det H_N^f/\det H_{N-q}^{|f_l\chi|^2}$,
and the same identity holds for the original $\sigma$ source.
Their difference is
$\log\det G_N^f-\log\det G_N^\sigma
=\log D_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}-\log R_N^{\rm Chr}$.
Insert the two exact low endpoints and retain the four signs.
The four original $q\log\beta_k$ terms cancel, proving TWA31a.
The original moment has the finite bounds
$\prod_jt_j^2\leq\mathfrak r_\chi\leq\mathscr U_{l,q}$,
from the pointwise lower factor and the full-vector upper
bound applied to the actual polynomial $\chi$.

For the finite spectral inputs, let $D=N+l+1$ and let
$\mathcal J_D$ have zero diagonal and adjacent entries
$\sqrt{a(a-1/2)}$ at coordinates $a-1,a$, $1\leq a<D$.
Set $\Phi_l(x)=\prod_{j=0}^{l-1}(x^2+t_j^2)$ and order the
positive eigenvalues of $\Phi_l(\mathcal J_D)$ as
$\pi_{D,1}\leq\cdots\leq\pi_{D,D}$, with multiplicity.
Define, with zero sums when $r_N=0$,
\[
\begin{gathered}
 s^-_{l,N}=\sum_{i=1}^{r_N}\log\pi_{D,i},\qquad
 s^+_{l,N}=\sum_{i=D-r_N+1}^{D}\log\pi_{D,i},\\
 L_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}=\max\left\{s^-_{l,N},
    \log D_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}-q\log\mathscr U_{l,N},
    r_N\sum_{j=0}^{l-1}\log t_j^2\right\},\\
 U_{l,N}^{\rm Chr}=\min\left\{s^+_{l,N},
                                    r_N\log\mathscr U_{l,N}\right\},\\
 \underline{\mathfrak T}_k=C_k^{\rm Chr}
               +L_{l,2q-1}^{\rm Chr}+L_{l,2q}^{\rm Chr},\\
 \overline{\mathfrak T}_k=C_k^{\rm Chr}
               +U_{l,2q-1}^{\rm Chr}+U_{l,2q}^{\rm Chr},\\
 \boxed{\quad\underline{\mathfrak T}_k\leq\mathfrak T_k
                  \leq\overline{\mathfrak T}_k.\quad}
\end{gathered}\tag{TWA31b}
\]
Here is the exact relation-space map underlying these bounds.
Write $P(c+iy)=\sum_{n=0}^{D-1}z_nb_n(y)/\sqrt{\gamma_n}$
and let $\mathcal C_D(P)=(z_n)_n$; its inverse sends $z$ to
$\sum_nz_nb_n((S-c)/i)/\sqrt{\gamma_n}$.
This is an isometry with the original mass $\gamma_0=\sqrt{2\pi}$.
Let $Z_N$ have columns $\mathcal C_D(\chi S^a)$,
$0\leq a<r_N$, and put $B_N=Z_N(Z_N^*Z_N)^{-1/2}$.
Multiplication by $f_l$ is exactly
$\mathcal C_D(f_lP)=i^l\prod_j(\mathcal J_D+it_jI)\mathcal C_D(P)$
on degree at most $N$: every intermediate degree is at most
$N+l=D-1$, so no final source coefficient is truncated.
Consequently
$R_N^{\rm Chr}=\det(B_N^*\Phi_l(\mathcal J_D)B_N)$.
In an orthonormal eigenbasis its Cauchy--Binet expansion is
$\sum_{|I|=r_N}|\det(B_N)_I|^2\prod_{i\in I}\pi_{D,i}$.
The coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one because
$B_N^*B_N=I$. This proves the spectral bounds in TWA31b.
The remaining entries in its maximum and minimum follow
from the same full-vector upper and pointwise lower bounds
on the original relation space and from the quotient Schur
bound in the preceding paragraph. This proves every finite
bound in TWA31b on the same actual determinant.
