### Exact proof of rank two and its relation-layer map

\boxed{W_n=\mathfrak h_{n+1}(r_{n+1}^*r_n+r_n^*r_{n+1}).}
\]

The exact metric decrement is

\]

This is the source proof at 4688–4734. Its rank bound concerns the control matrix on this actual finite arithmetic packet. It is not a statement that the packet has dimension at most two, that \(A\) has at most two eigenvalues, or that the tensor boundary has the same rank bound.

It is well-defined because \(P_n\) is identity on \(L_n\), injective because a projected-zero vector of \(L_{n+1}\) is in \(L_n\), and surjective by taking a vector in \(L_{n+1}\cap L_n^\perp\) itself. ## New exact deductions from these displayed arithmetic matrices

The following are newly proved consequences in this audit, not quotations or historical claims. For each actual finite packet \(Z\), use the source's displayed section \(s_Z\) and its finite relation-filtration representatives \(R_n\), for every \(n\ge0\). All packet multiplicities, \(\varepsilon_Z\), \(\ell_Z\), \(\mathfrak h_n\), and the measure \(dx\) remain unchanged. The analytic existence of the section is the earlier construction; these deductions do not assume an estimate, purity, a positive Weil form, or semisimplicity.

### 1. Invariant trace defect and the exact trace constraint on the two rows

Write \(d=\deg h_Z=\sum_\rho m_\rho\) and \(A=M_t\). The operator whose trace and spectrum are computed below is the original \(G_n^{-1}W_n\); the source notation \(L_n=\Theta V_n\) remains reserved for its theta relation space.

Cyclicity of the finite matrix trace gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{tr}(G_n^{-1}W_n)
&=\operatorname{tr}(G_n^{-1}A^*G_n)+\operatorname{tr}A-d\\
&=2\Re\operatorname{tr}A-d
=\sum_{\rho\in Z}m_\rho(2\Re\rho-1).
\end{aligned}
\]

The equality \(\operatorname{tr}A=\sum m_\rho\rho\) retains full multiplicity because on the length-\(m_\rho\) local algebra multiplication by \(t\) is \(\rho I+M_z\), with \(M_z\) nilpotent and trace zero. Combining this invariant trace with the rank-two factorization gives the exact row constraint

\[
\boxed{2\mathfrak h_{n+1}\Re\mathsf c_n
=\sum_{\rho\in Z}m_\rho(2\Re\rho-1).}
\]

This is independent of the chosen level or representative metric. For a reflection-stable packet, the involution \(\rho\mapsto1-\bar\rho\) preserves full multiplicities; its paired contributions cancel and its fixed points have real part (1/2). Hence

\[
\Re\mathsf c_n=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Spec}_{\ne0}(G_n^{-1}W_n)\subseteq\{-\epsilon_n,+\epsilon_n\},
\]

\[
\epsilon_n=\mathfrak h_{n+1}\sqrt{\mathsf a_n\mathsf b_n-(\Im\mathsf c_n)^2}.
\]

If \(W_n\ne0\), both signs occur once. If \(\epsilon_n=0\), the operator is zero, because it is self-adjoint for the \(G_n\) inner product. This is a trace cancellation, not cancellation of \(W_n\).

### 2. General invariant-subspace bound; multiplicities strengthen the obstruction

Let \(Z_+=\{\rho\in Z:\Re\rho>1/2\}\) and \(Z_-=\{\rho\in Z:\Re\rho<1/2\}\). The Chinese remainder decomposition of the **original** algebra supplies invariant subspaces

\[
E_+=\bigoplus_{\rho\in Z_+}\mathbb C[z]/(z^{m_\rho}),\qquad
E_-=\bigoplus_{\rho\in Z_-}\mathbb C[z]/(z^{m_\rho}),
\]

and canonical inclusions \(I_\pm:E_\pm\to E_Z\) via the corresponding CRT idempotents. This keeps generalized eigenvectors and their full nilpotent blocks. Choose any coordinates on these finite subspaces solely to express the actual injection matrices. Let \(A_\pm\) denote the restrictions, so \(AI_\pm=I_\pm A_\pm\). The induced Gram matrix and exact \(G_n\)-orthogonal projection are

\[
G_\pm=I_\pm^*G_nI_\pm\succ0,\qquad
P_\pm=I_\pm G_\pm^{-1}I_\pm^*G_n.
\]

These maps preserve the original coordinates on \(E_Z\); there is no replacement by a normalized model. If a subspace is zero, its projection and all its traces below are zero. The restriction of the defect is

\[
I_\pm^*W_nI_\pm=A_\pm^*G_\pm+G_\pm A_\pm-G_\pm.
\]

Therefore

\[
\operatorname{tr}(P_\pm G_n^{-1}W_n)
=\operatorname{tr}(G_\pm^{-1}I_\pm^*W_nI_\pm)
=2\sum_{\rho\in Z_\pm}m_\rho(\Re\rho-1/2).
\tag{*}
\]

The rank-two factorization also proves that \(G_n^{-1}W_n\) has at most one strictly positive and at most one strictly negative eigenvalue in the \(G_n\) metric: its Hermitian form is the pullback of

\[
\mathfrak h_{n+1}\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\]

under \(v\mapsto(r_n(v),r_{n+1}(v))\). If a positive definite subspace had dimension two, its image in \(\mathbb C^2\) would be injective and would be a two-dimensional positive subspace of this form, contrary to its one positive direction; the same argument applies to the negative direction.

Let \(\lambda_{n,+}\ge0\ge\lambda_{n,-}\) be its possible positive and negative eigenvalues, using zero when one is absent. By the finite-dimensional spectral theorem in the positive inner product \(\langle u,v\rangle_{G_n}=u^*G_nv\),

\[
G_n^{-1}W_n=\lambda_{n,+}\Pi_++\lambda_{n,-}\Pi_-,
\]

where \(\Pi_\pm\) are \(G_n\)-orthogonal spectral projections of rank at most one. For every \(G_n\)-orthogonal projection \(P\),

\[
0\le\operatorname{tr}(P\Pi_\pm)\le1.
\]

To verify the last statement without changing the original arithmetic objects, restrict the positive contraction \(\Pi_\pm P\Pi_\pm\) to the at-most-one-dimensional image of \(\Pi_\pm\); its eigenvalue belongs to ([0,1]), and cyclicity gives the stated trace. Thus

\[
\lambda_{n,-}\le\operatorname{tr}(P G_n^{-1}W_n)\le\lambda_{n,+}.
\]

Apply this to \(P_+\) and \(P_-\) in (*). The result is the unconditional pair of exact packet bounds

\[
\boxed{\lambda_{n,+}\ge2\sum_{\rho\in Z_+}m_\rho(\Re\rho-1/2),}
\]

\[
\boxed{-\lambda_{n,-}\ge2\sum_{\rho\in Z_-}m_\rho(1/2-\Re\rho).}
\]

For a reflection-stable packet, \(\lambda_{n,+}=\epsilon_n=-\lambda_{n,-}\) by the trace identity, and the positive and negative distance sums are equal. Therefore the strongest direct scalar restriction obtained here is

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak h_{n+1}\sqrt{\mathsf a_n\mathsf b_n-(\Im\mathsf c_n)^2}
=\epsilon_n
\ \ge\ \sum_{\rho\in Z}m_\rho\,|\Re\rho-1/2|
\quad\text{for every }n\ge0.
}
\]

This bounds the **total off-line mass with multiplicity**, not merely the largest distance. No nilpotent component was removed. The proof uses invariant subspaces with the exact restricted metrics, so it does not assume that \(A\) is normal for \(G_n\).

### 3. Exact counterfactual consequences and their limit

For a single actual eigenvector \(v\ne0\), \(Av=\rho v\), the control identity gives

\[
v^*W_nv=(2\Re\rho-1)v^*G_nv.
\]

Thus every metric in this construction obeys \(\epsilon_n\ge|2\Re\rho-1|\). The packet-mass result is stronger when several zeros or multiplicities are retained.

For a reflected off-line pair \(\rho,1-\bar\rho\), each of complete multiplicity \(m\), and \(\delta=\Re\rho-1/2\ne0\), the packet bound is

\[
\epsilon_n\ge2m|\delta|=m|2\Re\rho-1|\qquad\text{for every }n.
\]

For four distinct zeros \(\rho,\bar\rho,1-\rho,1-\bar\rho\) with the common complete multiplicity \(m\), it becomes

\[
\epsilon_n\ge4m|\delta|=2m|2\Re\rho-1|\qquad\text{for every }n.
\]

The four-distinct condition is explicit: a collapsed orbit must be counted by its actual distinct points and their actual multiplicities, not by artificially duplicating points. The conjugation and functional-equation multiplicity symmetries used in this quartet statement are properties of the original xi-function; the main theorem only needs the specified packet's reflection stability.

Consequently a reflection-stable actual packet containing an off-line root forces, at every finite level, a nonzero indefinite rank-two matrix \(W_n\), exact generalized eigenvalues \(\pm\epsilon_n\), and the stated positive lower bound on the **relative** row expression involving \(G_n^{-1}\). It cannot be made small by changing the homotopy primitive, synchronizing supports, dropping \(\varepsilon_Z\), dropping a nilpotent jet, or observing that \(G_n-G_{n+1}\succeq0\). Those operations respectively leave the boundary fixed, retain its support history, change the original problem if the unit/jets are dropped, or provide only an absolute Gram decrement.

For completeness, the tensor obstruction follows independently of rank two: on a \(k\)-fold tensor of an actual eigenline, \(A_kv^{\otimes k}=k\rho v^{\otimes k}\). Every positive tensor-image metric \(H\succ0\) therefore has

\[
\frac{(v^{\otimes k})^*(A_k^*H+HA_k-kH)v^{\otimes k}}
{(v^{\otimes k})^*H v^{\otimes k}}
=k(2\Re\rho-1).
\]

In particular the orbit metric in A1721 cannot suppress this linear spectral defect by changing its time window. This is an exact restriction on a counterexample, not a proof that the counterexample exists or that the required sublinear arithmetic upper bound has been established.
