\section{The off-line counterfactual inside the original holonomy construction}

This calculation uses the original theta source in the final analytic
continuation of the programme.  In particular, the scalar measure below is
computed from $g/h$ with $g=2\xi$; it is not a Gamma reference measure.
The source equations being continued are (H5)--(H17), (H24)--(H28), and
(H53)--(H55) of \emph{All-holonomy descent of the original theta quotient}.
The complete source record and locators are recorded in the accompanying
audit.  The conclusions concern the stated counterfactual and do not assert
the existence of an off-line zero.

\subsection{The retained arithmetic objects and every phase}

Suppose $1/2+\delta+i\gamma$ is a zero of $g$ of its full order $m$, where
$0<\delta<1/2$ and $\gamma>0$.  Reflection and conjugation give the complete
quartet, with the same order.  Retain
\[
 h(s)=\prod_{\epsilon,\eta\in\{-1,1\}}
 (s-1/2-\epsilon\delta-i\eta\gamma)^m,
 \qquad v_h=g/h,
 \qquad w_h(u)=\frac{|v_h(1/2+iu)|^2}{2\pi}.
 \tag{HT1}
\]
The division is entire and its full jet $\upsilon_h=j_h(v_h)$ is a unit.
For an integer $k\geq2$, put
\[
 c=k/2,\quad \ell_k=1+k(m-1),\quad q=\ell_k(k+1)^2,
 \quad m_{h,k}=w_h^{*k},
\]
\[
 \chi(S)=\prod_{a,b=0}^{k}
 \bigl(S-c-(2a-k)\delta-i(2b-k)\gamma\bigr)^{\ell_k},
 \quad E=\mathbb C[S]/(\chi),\quad A=M_S.
 \tag{HT2}
\]
These are the original cyclic sum quotient, its full multiplicities, and
its multiplication operator.  For completeness, in each tensor local
factor the nilpotent sum is $n_1+\cdots+n_k$ with $n_i^m=0$.
Its power $k(m-1)$ has nonzero coefficient
$(k(m-1))!/((m-1)!)^k$ on $n_1^{m-1}\cdots n_k^{m-1}$, whereas the next
power vanishes.  Thus its nilpotency index is exactly $\ell_k$.
Every pair $(a,b)$ is realized by choosing independently the real and
imaginary signs in the $k$ tensor factors.  Distinct pairs give distinct
complex sums, so the annihilator in the sum variable is exactly $\chi$.

The original analytic construction provides $F_h$ with Mellin transform
$v_h$, and the original source and jet maps are
\[
 \mathcal VP=P(D_1+\cdots+D_k)F_h^{\otimes k},\qquad
 J^{(k)}\mathcal V=\eta\pi_\chi,
 \quad \eta[P]=\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}P(A_k)1.
 \tag{HT3}
\]
Here $\eta$ is an injective complex linear module map.  Its unit factor is
retained.  The norm of this source is exactly
$\int_{\mathbb R}|P(c+iu)|^2m_{h,k}(u)\,du$.
The half-density map is
\[
 (\mathcal U_k F)(r,z)=e^{(kr+\sum_{i<k}z_i)/2}
 F(e^{r+z_1},\ldots,e^{r+z_{k-1}},e^r),
 \quad r=\log x_k,\quad z_i=\log x_i-\log x_k.
 \tag{HT4}
\]
It maps into $L^2(\mathbb R,dr;\mathcal K)$ with
$\mathcal K=L^2(\mathbb R^{k-1},dz)$ and intertwines $D_1+\cdots+D_k$
with $-\partial_r+c$.  No relative variable is removed.

Fix any $L>0$ and $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$.  The source of phase $\theta$ is
\[
 (\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\psi)(r)=
 \sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}e^{ij\theta}\psi(r+jL),\qquad
 \psi(r+L)=e^{-i\theta}\psi(r)
 \quad\hbox{in the target domain}.
 \tag{HT5}
\]
On the original finite source, its Gram form is exactly the polynomial
norm for the measure
\[
 \nu_{L,\theta}=
 \frac{2\pi}{L}\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}m_{h,k}(u_n)\,\delta_{u_n},
 \qquad u_n=\frac{2\pi n+\theta}{L},\qquad S=c+iu.
 \tag{HT6}
\]
The factor $2\pi/L$ is unchanged.  The measure has positive mass at every
$u_n$.  Indeed the nonzero entire function $v_h$ has only isolated zeros
on the line; hence $w_h>0$ almost everywhere.  For every $u$, the product
$w_h(v)w_h(u-v)$ is positive almost everywhere in $v$, so its integral is
positive.  Iterated convolution preserves strict positivity.  The
original weighted Schwartz estimates give convergence and all moments
in (HT6).  A nonzero polynomial cannot vanish at every lattice point;
consequently every finite polynomial Gram matrix is positive definite.

For $N\geq q-1$, let $P_N=\mathbb C[S]_{\leq N}$ and
$J_N=\pi_\chi|_{P_N}$.  With the fixed coefficient bases define
\[
 M_{N,\theta}=(\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\mathcal U_k\mathcal V|_{P_N})^*
 (\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\mathcal U_k\mathcal V|_{P_N}),\quad
 K=J_NM_{N,\theta}^{-1}J_N^*,\quad G=K^{-1},
\]
\[
 C_{N,\theta}=M_{N,\theta}^{-1}J_N^*G,\qquad
 R=\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\mathcal U_k\mathcal V C_{N,\theta}.
 \tag{HT7}
\]
Thus $J_NC_{N,\theta}=I_E$ and $R^*R=G$.  All inverses in (HT7)
exist: $M_{N,\theta}$ is positive definite and $J_N$ is surjective.
No inverse at degree $q-2$ is used.

\subsection{A uniform phasewise lower bound for the original control}

Define
\[
 H=G^{-1/2}(A^*G+GA-kG)G^{-1/2},\qquad
 \mathcal L_{h,k}=2\delta\ell_k(k+1)
 \left\lfloor\frac{(k+1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor.
 \tag{HT8}
\]
For every $k\geq2$, $L>0$, $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$, and $N\geq q-1$,
the nonzero eigenvalues of $H$ are $\varepsilon,-\varepsilon$, and
\[
 \boxed{\varepsilon\geq\mathcal L_{h,k}>0,
 \qquad \frac{\mathcal L_{h,k}}q\geq\frac{\delta k}{2}.}
 \tag{HT9}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Let $p_j(S)$ be the monic orthogonal polynomials for (HT6), with squared
norms $\omega_j>0$, and let $b_j=[p_j]\in E$.  In the real coordinate $u$,
write $p_j(c+iu)=i^j r_j(u)$, where $r_j$ is real monic.  Orthogonality
and the symmetry of multiplication by $u$ show
\[
 ur_j=r_{j+1}+a_jr_j+\frac{\omega_j}{\omega_{j-1}}r_{j-1},
 \quad a_j\in\mathbb R,
\]
with the last term omitted at $j=0$.  To verify that no earlier term
occurs, pair $ur_j$ with $r_i$, $i\leq j-2$, and move $u$ to $r_i$;
its degree is at most $j-1$.  Pairing with $r_{j-1}$ gives precisely
$\omega_j/\omega_{j-1}$.  In the $S$ coordinate this is
\[
 Sp_j=p_{j+1}+(c+ia_j)p_j
 -\frac{\omega_j}{\omega_{j-1}}p_{j-1}.
 \tag{HT10}
\]
Since the source Gram is diagonal in this basis,
$K=\sum_{j=0}^N b_jb_j^*/\omega_j$.  Apply $A$ on the left and $A^*$
on the right and use (HT10).  The imaginary diagonal terms cancel,
and each adjacent term except the last cancels its predecessor.  This
gives, including $N=q-1$,
\[
 AK+KA^*-kK=
 \frac{b_{N+1}b_N^*+b_Nb_{N+1}^*}{\omega_N}.
 \tag{HT11}
\]
Congruence by $G^{1/2}$ identifies the left side with $H$.  Thus its
rank is at most two.  Also
$\operatorname{Tr}H=2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}A-kq=0$
by the complete grid (HT2).

Let $E_+$ be the direct sum of the complete primary spaces with $a>k/2$,
and let $P$ be the orthogonal projection onto $G^{1/2}E_+$ in the standard
Hermitian space.  The latter subspace is invariant under
$G^{1/2}AG^{-1/2}$.  Taking traces in an orthonormal basis of this
subspace gives
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(PH)
 =2\sum_{a>k/2}\sum_{b=0}^{k}\ell_k(2a-k)\delta
 =2\delta\ell_k(k+1)
 \left\lfloor\frac{(k+1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor
 =\mathcal L_{h,k}.
 \tag{HT12}
\]
The finite sum is $r^2$ if $k=2r-1$, and $r(r+1)$ if $k=2r$.
A Hermitian rank-at-most-two matrix of trace zero has eigenvalues
$\varepsilon,-\varepsilon$ and zeroes.  For every orthogonal projection,
$\operatorname{Tr}(PH)\leq\varepsilon$: in an eigenbasis the positive
contribution is at most $\varepsilon$ and the negative one is nonpositive.
Equation (HT12) proves the first inequality of (HT9), and
$4\lfloor(k+1)^2/4\rfloor\geq k(k+1)$ proves the second.\hfill$\square$

\subsection{The exact energy forced into the retained theta boundary}

In $\mathcal H_{L,\theta}=L^2([0,L],dr;\mathcal K)$ put
$D_{L,\theta}=-\partial_r+c$, with domain
$\{f\in H^1([0,L];\mathcal K):f(L)=e^{-i\theta}f(0)\}$.
The domain of its square is
$\{f\in H^2([0,L];\mathcal K):f(L)=e^{-i\theta}f(0),\quad
f'(L)=e^{-i\theta}f'(0)\}$.
The original smooth periodized source columns belong to these domains.
Monic division in the original coefficient
source gives the unique polynomial map $Q_{N,\theta}$ with
\[
 SC_{N,\theta}-C_{N,\theta}A=\chi Q_{N,\theta}.
\]
Indeed applying $J_{N+1}$ gives zero.  Define the actual boundary
\[
 \mathscr B=\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\mathcal U_k
 \mathcal V(\chi Q_{N,\theta})
 =D_{L,\theta}R-RA.
 \tag{HT13}
\]
This retains its theta primitive.  Explicitly, tensor monic division gives
\[
 \chi(S)Q(S)=\sum_i h(s_i)Q_i(\mathbf s),
\]
because its image in the full tensor jet algebra vanishes.  In the original
theta complex, $\phi_h$ has degree zero, $F_h$ has degree one, and
$h(D)F_h=\Theta\phi_h$.  The degree-$(k-1)$ tensor primitive is exactly
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{i-1}Q_i(D_1,\ldots,D_k)
 (F_h^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes\phi_h\otimes F_h^{\otimes(k-i)}).
\]
The tensor differential contributes a further sign $(-1)^{i-1}$ when
it acts on $\phi_h$, because the preceding $i-1$ factors have degree one.
The two signs cancel, so its differential is precisely
$\mathcal V(\chi Q)$.  Applying the same half-density
and phase maps gives precisely (HT13).

Set
\[
 U=RG^{-1/2},\quad T=\mathscr BG^{-1/2},\quad Z=U^*T,
 \quad T_\perp=(I-UU^*)T.
\]
Then the following exact energy identity, with no omitted source term,
holds:
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Tr}(G^{-1}\mathscr B^*\mathscr B)
 =\frac{\varepsilon^2}{2}
 +\left\|\frac{Z-Z^*}{2}\right\|_{\rm HS}^{2}
 +\|T_\perp\|_{\rm HS}^{2}
 \ \geq\ \frac{\mathcal L_{h,k}^2}{2}
 \ \geq\ \frac{\delta^2k^2q^2}{8}.}
 \tag{HT14}
\]
All quantities on the left are calculated from $g/h$ and its original
theta representatives.  In particular the same estimate holds after
integration with the original Fourier inversion measure:
\[
 \int_0^{2\pi}\operatorname{Tr}
 (G_{N,\theta}^{-1}\mathscr B_{N,\theta}^*
 \mathscr B_{N,\theta})\,\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}
 \geq\frac{\mathcal L_{h,k}^2}{2}.
 \tag{HT15}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The two arguments in integration by parts have the same boundary
multiplier $e^{-i\theta}$, so the endpoint products cancel.  Hence
$D_{L,\theta}^*+D_{L,\theta}=2cI$ on the stated domain.  Inserting
$D_{L,\theta}R=RA+\mathscr B$ and $R^*R=G$ gives
\[
 A^*G+GA-kG=-(R^*\mathscr B+\mathscr B^*R),\qquad H=-Z-Z^*.
\]
Since $U^*U=I$, the decomposition $T=UZ+T_\perp$ is orthogonal in
Hilbert--Schmidt norm.  The Hermitian and skew-Hermitian parts of $Z$
are orthogonal for the real Hilbert--Schmidt scalar product.  Therefore
\[
 \|T\|_{\rm HS}^2
 =\frac14\|H\|_{\rm HS}^2
 +\left\|\frac{Z-Z^*}{2}\right\|_{\rm HS}^2
 +\|T_\perp\|_{\rm HS}^2.
\]
By (HT9), $\|H\|_{\rm HS}^2=2\varepsilon^2$, which proves (HT14).
For fixed $k,L,N$ the weighted source sums and their derivatives converge
uniformly in $\theta$.  Thus $M_{N,\theta}$ is continuous.  Its smallest
eigenvalue is positive on the compact phase circle, so its inverse,
$G_{N,\theta}$, and the boundary columns are continuous there.
Integration of (HT14) proves (HT15).\hfill$\square$

\subsection{The full lattice test for the original Laplacian boundary}

Put $\mathscr L_{L,\theta}=D_{L,\theta}^2-kD_{L,\theta}
=\partial_r^2-c^2$.  Direct substitution of (HT13) gives the exact map
\[
 \mathscr L_{L,\theta}R-R(A^2-kA)
 =(D_{L,\theta}-k)\mathscr B+\mathscr BA.
 \tag{HT16}
\]
If $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, write $\lambda=c+x+iy$.  Then
\[
 \frac{\|((D_{L,\theta}-k)\mathscr B+\mathscr BA)v\|}
 {\|Rv\|}
 \geq
 \min_{n\in\mathbb Z}
 \sqrt{(u_n^2+x^2-y^2)^2+4x^2y^2}.
 \tag{HT17}
\]
In particular every phase and every admitted degree satisfy, on an
eigenvector for $\lambda=k(1/2+\delta+i\gamma)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \frac{\|((D_{L,\theta}-k)\mathscr B+\mathscr BA)v\|}
 {\|Rv\|}\geq 2k^2\delta\gamma.}
 \tag{HT18}
\]
For an eigenvalue with $x\ne0$ and $y=0$, (HT17) also gives the positive
lower bound $x^2+\min_nu_n^2$, even though its imaginary part is zero.

\paragraph{Proof.}
The Fourier basis $L^{-1/2}e^{-iu_nr}$ diagonalizes the quasi-periodic
Laplacian with eigenvalues $-u_n^2-c^2$, each acting identically on
$\mathcal K$.  The source vectors lie in its domain by the original
weighted differentiability estimates.  Writing $Rv$ in that basis and
using Parseval gives
\[
 \|(\mathscr L_{L,\theta}-(\lambda^2-k\lambda))Rv\|^2
 =\sum_n\bigl((u_n^2+x^2-y^2)^2+4x^2y^2\bigr)
 \|\widehat{Rv}(n)\|_{\mathcal K}^2.
\]
The lattice is discrete and the expression tends to infinity as
$|n|\to\infty$, so its infimum is a minimum.  Also $R$ is injective
because $R^*R=G\succ0$.  Divide the displayed identity by $\|Rv\|^2$;
(HT16) proves (HT17).  The extreme primary factor in (HT2) is nonzero
and has an eigenvector, so substituting $x=k\delta,y=k\gamma$ proves
(HT18).  If $y=0$, the square root in (HT17) is $u_n^2+x^2$.
\hfill$\square$

\subsection{What these computations decide}

The original off-line counterfactual survives passage to every finite
holonomy quotient only with the explicit boundary energy (HT14) and
Laplacian residual (HT17).  These quantities belong to the original
arithmetic source.  The statement is compatible with the exact
all-phase identity $\int M_{N,\theta}\,d\theta/(2\pi)=M_N$ and with
the original small gluing-norm estimate.  That estimate concerns
$C_N-C_{N,\theta}$; (HT13) applies $S$ and subtracts right multiplication
by $A$, preserving a different, explicitly displayed source quantity.
There is no claim here that averaging the $G_{N,\theta}$ preserves the
rank-two identity (HT11): the lower bound (HT15) integrates the proved
phasewise energies themselves.

The transcript's threshold-four volume lower bound was already present
before its final Deligne comparison.  Its last counterexamples retain
many generic identities but do not satisfy the actual divisor condition
$h\mid 2\xi$.  Consequently they disprove the reduced generic inference
stated there; they do not disprove the entire arithmetic programme or
provide an RH counterexample.  Equations (HT9), (HT14), and (HT17) give
further quantified consequences within the original programme without
replacing its theta metric or its represented relations.
