\section{Primary-source correction to the Deligne comparison}
\label{sec:deligne-primary-source-correction}

This dated correction supersedes the earlier reported internal numbering
mismatch in the final citation of Weil II, Section~3.7.4. The original
delivered note, S20 page records, historical typed witnesses, and sealed
intake remain preserved. The correction is based on the Zeta literature
task's complete retained primary-source review of 13 September 2026.

The final citation on printed page~216 is \textbf{(I.8.11), with Roman I}.
It refers to \textbf{Weil I, Section~8.11, printed page~306}, rather than
to Weil II, Corollary~1.8.11. Weil I, Section~8.10, reduces to one variable
and the monomial $Q=x^d$; Section~8.11 handles that case, and
Sections~8.12--8.13 continue the Euler-characteristic calculation.
The citation is resolved. This does not assert that Section~8.11 alone
states the entire general purity theorem used in Weil II.

The printed page~216 itself uses $S'_0$ in the connectedness sentence.
The preceding parameter sheaves are defined on the good open $S_0$.
That open is connected: it is a nonempty open subset of an irreducible
affine parameter space, hence is irreducible. The printed prime remains
part of the literal source typography while the application retains
the explicitly defined good open.

The high-resolution printed display has the three terms
\[
\begin{aligned}
 R^if_!\mathcal F_0(\Psi Q_S)
 &=R^i\bar f_*j_!\mathcal F_0(\Psi Q_S)\\
 &=R^i\bar f_*j_!\mathcal F_0(\Psi Q_S).
\end{aligned}
\]
The two final terms both have $\bar f_*$. The identity
$\bar f_!=\bar f_*$ for the proper compactification explains a
possible intermediate step, but replacing one of the printed stars
by an exclamation mark would reconstruct that step. It would not
transcribe a visible distinction. The repeated display and the prime
are already present in the printed source.

The same primary review confirms the strict weight inequality $<2$
in Lemma~3.2.10 on printed page~203. Section~3.2.13 explicitly uses
$w_q(\alpha^2)$ and then divides the resulting bound by two.
Both the strict inequality and the tensor square of the same
eigenvalue remain essential to those deductions; losses in older
typed witnesses must not enter the current proof.

On printed page~178, Corollary~1.8.12 requires a lisse
$\iota$-mixed sheaf on a connected scheme of finite type over the
finite field, pure at one point, and concludes pointwise purity.
Normality is not a hypothesis of that corollary; mixedness is.
Constant rank alone does not supply its hypotheses.

The verified exponential theorem~(3.7.2.3), printed pages~215--216,
applies to the actual specialized potential $(\Phi-tS)/u$.
Its degree is $d=q+1$, its leading coefficient is the unit $1/(du)$
on $u\ne0$, and the specified characteristic $p>d$ makes $d$
prime to $p$. The leading projective zero locus in $\mathbb P^0$
is empty and smooth. Thus compact-support cohomology is concentrated
in degree one, has dimension $q$, and is pure of weight one.
Repeated critical roots do not obstruct these hypotheses.
Lemma~3.7.3 supplies the lisse family through the actual compactification
and its local-acyclicity calculation, including infinity.
These are applications of the stated historical theorem. The exact
source and period comparisons in the adjoining proofs retain their
own operators and original theta metrics.

The retained primary review records visual reading of complete
Weil II printed pages~178, 202, 203, 206, 215 and~216, including
a higher-resolution check of page~216. Its bounded collaborator
checked complete Weil I printed pages~305--306 and the cited case.
This correction records their actual inspection scope; it does not
claim a new audit of every foundational theorem in either paper.
The evidence includes the complete written review, source URLs and
exact source hashes, with no reproduced scan or rendered page.
