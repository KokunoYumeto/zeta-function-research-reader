## 1. One-variable divisor morphisms and their exact units

Let h and H be nonempty monic finite packets of actual zeros of g=2ξ, with
the complete order of each selected zero, and suppose h divides H. Set
d=deg h, D=deg H, b=H/h. Because all old zeros already occur with their
complete orders, h and b are coprime. Define E_h=C[s]/(h), E_H=C[s]/(H).
The restriction π:E_H→E_h is [P]_H↦[P]_h. The module injection

\[
\mu_b:E_h\longrightarrow E_H,\qquad[P]_h\longmapsto[bP]_H
\]

is well-defined because bh=H, and injective because H dividing bP implies
h dividing P by cancellation in C[s]. Its image is the ideal generated by b.
It is generally not an algebra map: μ_b(1)=b and μ_b(P)μ_b(Q)=b²PQ.

The unit b_h=[b]_h has the entire finite Taylor inverse. Choose its unique
polynomial representative c of degree below d and set e=[bc]_H. On every
old local factor e is 1, and on every additional local factor e is 0.
Consequently e²=e. The exact CRT insertion is

\[
\iota=\mu_bM_{b_h^{-1}},\qquad
\mu_b=\iota M_{b_h},\qquad
\pi\iota=1,\qquad \iota\pi=M_e.
\]

The last equality is an equality of maps on E_H. The map ι preserves
multiplication but is not unital when the packet inclusion is proper.
Both π and ι commute with multiplication by the original variable s.
Their descriptions at every local factor prove compatibility under any
further inclusion H|K without a basis change.

Write v_h=g/h, v_H=g/H, υ_h=j_hv_h, υ_H=j_Hv_H, and ε_h=υ_h^{-1},
ε_H=υ_H^{-1}. Entire-function division gives v_h=bv_H before passage to any
quotient. Therefore

\[
\upsilon_h=b_h\pi(\upsilon_H),\qquad
\varepsilon_h=b_h^{-1}\pi(\varepsilon_H),\qquad
\pi(\varepsilon_H)=b_h\varepsilon_h.
\]

These are identities of full local Taylor series modulo their exact orders,
not merely values at the zero centres. At a centre ρ, the coefficient of
z^n in a product is the sum over i+j=n of the two Taylor coefficients; an
inverse of u_0+n with u_0≠0 and nilpotent n is
u_0^{-1}Σ_{j=0}^{L-1}(-n/u_0)^j when n^L=0. This specifies all coefficients
of every inverse used here.

## 2. Actual source function, section, and metric morphisms

The source object F_h is the actual function in the original space B with
h(D)F_h=f_0 and Mellin transform g/h. It is not an unspecified entire
functional-calculus operator (g/h)(D). Define T_hP=P(D)F_h for a polynomial P.
The original inverse construction and injectivity of the Mellin transform
give, as equalities of actual functions,

\[
b(D)F_H=F_h,\qquad
\mathcal T_H(bP)=\mathcal T_hP,\qquad
\mathcal T_h(hP)=\Theta(P(D)\phi_*).
\]

Indeed every displayed equality becomes v_Hb=v_h or v_hh=g under Mellin,
with the original D=-x∂_x and f_0=Θφ_* retained.

For the original arithmetic jet maps J_hF=j_h MF and J_HF=j_H MF, one has
J_HT_hP=ιJ_hT_hP. At old centres this is equality of the same entire
Mellin function. At every added centre, g/h has the full zero order of g,
so all additional jets vanish. This is why the target map is ι rather than
μ_b.

Let R_h(u)=rem_h(ε_hu), and let s_h=T_hR_h be the original uncorrected
section. The numerator bR_h(u) has degree below D. Its product with v_H
has the jets ιu, so uniqueness of the degree-below-D numerator with these
jets proves

\[
R_H(\iota u)=bR_h(u),\qquad s_H\iota=s_h.
\]

Since the one-variable correction space
L_n=Θ span(φ_*,Dφ_*,…,D^nφ_*) is independent of the packet, the actual
orthogonal representatives R_{h,n}=(1-P_{L_n})s_h satisfy

\[
R_{H,n}\iota=R_{h,n},\qquad
\iota^*G_{H,n}\iota=G_{h,n},\qquad
\iota^*W_{H,n}\iota=W_{h,n}.
\]

The last formula follows by substituting A_Hι=ιA_h into
W=A^*G+GA-G. These formulas are congruences, not identities obtained by
commuting inverse with compression. The map P↦bP between the declared
polynomial images IS exactly isometric for the two actual measures:

\[
\int |b(1/2+it)P(1/2+it)|^2
       |v_H(1/2+it)|^2\,\frac{dt}{2\pi}
=\int|P(1/2+it)|^2|v_h(1/2+it)|^2\,\frac{dt}{2\pi}.
\]

This does not assert that μ_b or ι is isometric for arbitrarily assigned
Euclidean coefficient metrics. The displayed actual source and Gram maps
are the exact metric statement. For k-variable joint total-degree
corrections, CT.18 retains an additional projection Z and gives
ι_k^*G_{H,k,N}ι_k=G_{h,k,M}-Z^*Z at N=M+k(D-d); equality of joint metrics
cannot be inferred from the one-variable equality.

## 3. Every conormal depth: the exact divisor bridge

Fix k≥1 and r≥1. Put P=C[s_1,…,s_k], h_i=h(s_i), H_i=H(s_i),
b_i=b(s_i), b_Π=∏_{i=1}^k b_i, I_h=(h_i)_i, I_H=(H_i)_i, and
B_{h,k,r}=P/I_h^r, B_{H,k,r}=P/I_H^r. Since I_H⊂I_h, restriction gives
a surjective algebra map π_r:B_{H,k,r}→B_{h,k,r}.

For an old ordered root tuple, write s_i=ρ_i+z_i. The germ h_i is a
unit times z_i^{m_i}; H_i is another unit times that same power.
Multiplication of an ideal generator by a unit does not change its ideal.
Thus the two old local ideals and every rth power coincide exactly with
(z_i^{m_i})_i^r. Distinct root tuples give comaximal ideals and hence
the product decomposition proved in CC.4. Let e_{old,r} be its idempotent
which is 1 at each all-old tuple and 0 at every tuple with an added root.
The resulting CRT insertion ι_r inserts zeros at precisely those latter
factors; π_rι_r=1 and ι_rπ_r=M_{e_{old,r}}. These maps commute with
each s_i, with permutations of variables, and with depth reduction.

The polynomial multiplication map valid at this depth is

\[
\mu_r:B_{h,k,r}\longrightarrow B_{H,k,r},\qquad
[P]\longmapsto[b_\Pi^{\,r}P].
\]

To prove descent, a generator of I_h^r has the form ∏h_i^{α_i} with
Σα_i=r. Then

\[
b_\Pi^r\prod_i h_i^{\alpha_i}
=\prod_iH_i^{\alpha_i}\prod_i b_i^{r-\alpha_i}\in I_H^r,
\]

since 0≤α_i≤r. At an old tuple b_Π is a unit. At any additional tuple
some b_i is a unit times the required local power z_i^{m_i}; hence
b_Π^r belongs to that local generator ideal to the rth power and is zero
in the local quotient. It follows simultaneously that μ_r is injective,
has image e_{old,r}B_{H,k,r}, and satisfies

\[
\mu_r=\iota_rM_{[b_\Pi^r]_{I_h^r}},\qquad
\iota_r=\mu_rM_{[b_\Pi^r]_{I_h^r}^{-1}}.
\]

Write U_{h,k,r}=[∏v_h(s_i)]_{I_h^r} and analogously U_{H,k,r}.
Entire multiplication and the CRT factors give

\[
\iota_r(U_{h,k,r})
=e_{old,r}\,b_\Pi U_{H,k,r},
\]

and, for every P in B_{h,k,r},

\[
U_{H,k,r}\mu_r(P)
=\iota_r\bigl([b_\Pi^{r-1}]\,U_{h,k,r}P\bigr).
\]

For the following actual-function identity, choose a polynomial
representative P∈C[s_1,…,s_k]. It is an identity on polynomial numerators,
not a map from B_{h,k,r} directly to actual functions: changing the
representative changes the function by the specified tensor theta boundary.
The physical numerator map has the corresponding exact formula

\[
\mathcal T_H^{(k)}(b_\Pi^rP)
=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}(b_\Pi^{r-1}P).
\]

There is no omission of the factor b_Π^{r-1}. Ordinary physical
multiplication by b_Π does not generally descend at depth r>1. Already
k=1, h=s-ρ, H=(s-ρ)(s-σ), ρ≠σ, gives
b h^r=(s-σ)(s-ρ)^r, whose vanishing order at σ is only 1 and so is not
divisible by H^r for r>1.

Writing ρ_h,ρ_H for the reductions r+1→r, the μ maps satisfy the exact
noncommuting square correction

\[
\rho_H\mu_{r+1}=\mu_rM_{[b_\Pi]_{I_h^r}}\rho_h.
\]

In contrast, the CRT maps ι_r commute with reduction without correction.
The unit-adjusted numerator map
β_r=M_{U_{H,k,r}}^{-1}ι_rM_{U_{h,k,r}} equals
ι_rM_{[b_Π]_{I_h^r}} and also commutes with reduction. Its polynomial
realization in B_H is e_{old,r}b_Π, not bare b_Π. Choosing any polynomial
representative E_r of e_{old,r} gives the actual lift
T_H^{(k)}(E_rb_ΠP)=T_h^{(k)}(E_rP). Its difference from T_h^{(k)}P
has numerator (E_r-1)P∈I_h^r⊂I_h and is therefore an actual tensor theta
boundary, with the signed primitive of CT.11. This is an equality modulo
that specified boundary, not equality of the two actual functions.

## 4. Exact two-block cyclic map at depth one

For a nonempty packet h=∏_ν(s-ρ_ν)^{m_ν}, retain for a k-tuple t
λ_t=Σρ_{t_i}, D_t=Σ(m_{t_i}-1), M_t=max_i m_{t_i}. Define

\[
\ell_{k,\lambda,r}
=\max_{\lambda_t=\lambda}\{1+D_t+(r-1)M_t\},\qquad
\chi_{h,k,r}(T)=\prod_\lambda(T-\lambda)^{\ell_{k,\lambda,r}},\quad
C_{h,k,r}=\mathbb C[T]/(\chi_{h,k,r}).
\]

The exact sum annihilator in C_{h,k,1}⊗C_{h,l,1} is χ_{h,k+l,1}.
Indeed the local factor indexed by (λ,μ) is C[x,y]/(x^a,y^b), where
a=ell_{k,λ,1}, b=ell_{l,μ,1}, and S_1+S_2-(λ+μ)=x+y. Its power
a+b-1 vanishes by total degree. Its power a+b-2 contains the nonzero
term binom(a+b-2,a-1)x^{a-1}y^{b-1}, while all other monomials of
that total degree vanish. Hence its nilpotency order is exactly a+b-1.
Grouping the pairs with ν=λ+μ gives exponent

\[
\max_{\lambda+\mu=\nu}
  (\ell_{k,\lambda,1}+\ell_{l,\mu,1}-1)
=\max_{\lambda_t+\lambda_u=\nu}(1+D_t+D_u)
=\ell_{k+l,\nu,1}.
\]

The middle equality is valid because the two finite maxima are independent
once λ and μ are fixed. Thus

\[
\delta_{k,l}:C_{h,k+l,1}\hookrightarrow
 C_{h,k,1}\otimes C_{h,l,1},\qquad
[F]\longmapsto[F(S_1+S_2)]
\]

is an injective unital algebra map, not merely a linear injection. Under
the original grouping E_h^{⊗(k+l)}=E_h^{⊗k}⊗E_h^{⊗l}, arithmetic
coefficient maps η_{h,k,1}=M_{U_{h,k,1}}α_{h,k,1} satisfy

\[
(\eta_{h,k,1}\otimes\eta_{h,l,1})\delta_{k,l}
=\eta_{h,k+l,1}.
\]

This follows from equality ∏_{i=1}^{k+l}v_h(s_i)
=(∏_{i=1}^{k}v_h(s_i))(∏_{i=k+1}^{k+l}v_h(s_i)), before taking
any jets. The η maps are module maps weighted by their original units;
they are not asserted to be unital algebra maps. The δ maps are
associative under tensor regrouping because either composite evaluates
F at S_1+S_2+S_3. Ordinary coefficient transposition introduces no sign;
the original graded cochain interchange does introduce (-1)^{kl} on
homogeneous top-degree k and l elements, and that graded sign is retained
when implementing the same grouping at cochain level.

## 5. All-depth cyclic gluing: exact product over distributed depth

Using equal depth r in both target blocks generally fails. For a single
simple root ρ, k=l=1 and r=2, the total cyclic algebra is
C[T]/(T-2ρ)^2. In the target C[x,y]/(x²,y²), however, (x+y)²=2xy≠0.
Thus even well-definedness fails for that proposed equal-depth map.

For r_1,r_2≥1, let R=r_1+r_2-1. In disjoint variable blocks with
ideals I_k and I_l, (I_k+I_l)^R⊂I_k^{r_1}+I_l^{r_2}: each product
of R ideal factors uses at least r_1 from the first block or at least
r_2 from the second, since otherwise it uses at most R-1 factors.
This gives the original quotient map

\[
B_{h,k+l,R}\longrightarrow B_{h,k,r_1}\otimes B_{h,l,r_2}.
\]

The exact sum-annihilator exponent in the corresponding scalar target is
max_{λ+μ=ν}(ell_{k,λ,r_1}+ell_{l,μ,r_2}-1). This follows from exactly
the same local two-variable proof as in Section 4. In particular it is
at most ell_{k+l,ν,R}, since
(r_1-1)M_t+(r_2-1)M_u≤(R-1)max(M_t,M_u).

The fully injective replacement at every original total depth r is

\[
\boxed{\quad
\Delta_{k,l,r}:C_{h,k+l,r}\hookrightarrow
\prod_{a=0}^{r-1}
\left(C_{h,k,a+1}\otimes C_{h,l,r-a}\right),\qquad
[F]\longmapsto\bigl([F(S_1+S_2)]\bigr)_{a=0}^{r-1}.
\quad}
\]

Every component is well-defined by the previous inclusion. Its kernel
before dividing C[T] is the ideal generated by the least common multiple
of the exact sum annihilators. At ν its exponent is

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\max_{0\le a\le r-1}\max_{\lambda_t+\lambda_u=\nu}
 \{1+D_t+D_u+aM_t+(r-1-a)M_u\}\\
&\hspace{8mm}=\max_{\lambda_t+\lambda_u=\nu}
 \{1+D_t+D_u+(r-1)\max(M_t,M_u)\}
=\ell_{k+l,\nu,r}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The first equality maximizes a linear function of a on the entire integer
interval 0,…,r-1: a=r-1 attains it when M_t≥M_u, and a=0 does when
M_u≥M_t. The finite maxima can be interchanged. The final equality
is the exact tuple formula for the concatenated packet, including every
collision. The kernel is therefore precisely (χ_{h,k+l,r}), proving
injectivity with all local orders preserved.

In component a, the original arithmetic unit maps exactly to
U_{h,k,a+1}⊗U_{h,l,r-a}. Consequently the square with Δ, the original
cyclic η maps, and the indicated quotient of the full tensor algebras
commutes component by component. For three blocks, either grouping has
one component for every triple a,b,c≥0 with a+b+c=r-1 and evaluates F
at S_1+S_2+S_3 in depths a+1,b+1,c+1. Reindexing by these triples
proves associativity of the complete distributed-depth construction.

## 6. Reflection, Taylor signs, and split zero

Assume here that h and H are stable under ρ↦ρ#=1-conjugate(ρ), preserving
full orders. Put P†(s)=conjugate(P(1-conjugate(s))). Direct substitution
in each monic factor gives h†=(-1)^d h, H†=(-1)^D H, and
b†=(-1)^{D-d}b. A Taylor coefficient p_{ρ,j} at an old centre maps to
(-1)^j conjugate(p_{ρ,j}) at ρ#, since 1-conjugate(ρ#+z)=ρ-conjugate(z).
Thus every nilpotent order and factorial Taylor convention remains fixed.

The induced conjugate-linear maps j_h,j_H satisfy
j_H μ_b=(-1)^{D-d}μ_b j_h, while j_Hι=ιj_h. For the latter assertion,
CRT insertion retains the old full jets and inserts zero new full jets,
and both collections are reflection stable. Equivalently the two signs
from b and its inverse cancel. At depth r on k variables,

\[
\mathfrak j_{H,k,r}\mu_r
=(-1)^{kr(D-d)}\mu_r\mathfrak j_{h,k,r},\qquad
\mathfrak j_{H,k,r}\iota_r
=\iota_r\mathfrak j_{h,k,r}.
\]

The corrected numerator map β_r=ι_rM_[b_Π] has the reflection law
j_{H,k,r}β_r=(-1)^{k(D-d)}β_rj_{h,k,r}, independent of r, since
ι_r commutes with reflection and b_Π has that exact degree-parity sign.
The idempotent e_{old,r} is reflection fixed. The uncorrected arithmetic
units satisfy U_{h,k,r}†=(-1)^{kd}U_{h,k,r}. The physical reflection
j_BF(x)=x^{-1}conjugate(F(1/x)) has Mellin transform (MF)†, by changing
x to 1/x in the integral. Hence

\[
\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}F_h=(-1)^dF_h,\qquad
\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}\mathcal T_hP
=(-1)^d\mathcal T_h(P^\dagger).
\]

The section s_h is nevertheless exactly equivariant, because
ε_h†=(-1)^d ε_h cancels this sign. Tensor powers have the corresponding
sign (-1)^{kd}. On the cyclic scalar variable, reflection is
F(T)↦conjugate(F(k-conjugate(T))) in a k-block. Substitution proves
that δ and every component of Δ intertwine block reflection with
(k+l)-block reflection, since (k-conjugate(S_1))+(l-conjugate(S_2))
=k+l-conjugate(S_1+S_2). No sign or full arithmetic Taylor unit is dropped.

For any supported linear map L:X→Y above, its original split lift is
(λ,x)↦(λ,Lx), with external τ mapped to τ. It sends (λ,0_X) to
(λ,0_Y), including at every inserted zero jet. Since L(0)=0, it commutes
with the scalar e that sends a supported vector to its supported zero;
since both compositions send every input to external τ, it also commutes
with scalar τ. No quotient map here identifies supported zero with τ.

## 7. Divisor restriction on cyclic sources and its complete insertion image

Each old tuple also occurs in the larger packet with unchanged original
orders. Consequently ell_{h,k,λ,r}≤ell_{H,k,λ,r} at every old sum λ,
so χ_{h,k,r} divides χ_{H,k,r}. There is a canonical scalar restriction
π_C:C_{H,k,r}→C_{h,k,r}. Direct evaluation gives

\[
\pi_r\alpha_{H,k,r}=\alpha_{h,k,r}\pi_C,\qquad
M_{[b_\Pi]_{I_h^r}}\pi_r\eta_{H,k,r}
=\eta_{h,k,r}\pi_C.
\]

The second formula follows from π_rU_H=U_h/[b_Π]. It retains the
complete additional multiplier and does not confuse the scalar and
arithmetic coefficient maps.

In the opposite direction the CRT insertion need not preserve the scalar
cyclic subalgebra. Here is an algebraic counterexample to such a universal
claim, with no assertion that the displayed centres are actual zeros of g.
Let k=2,r=1, let ρ=1/2, σ=1/2+i, τ=1/2+2i, and put
h=(s-ρ)(s-τ), H=(s-ρ)(s-σ)(s-τ). The all-old tuple (ρ,τ) and the
added tuple (σ,σ) have the same sum 1+2i. The element ι(1)=e_old
has values 1 and 0 at these respective local factors. Every F(S) has equal
values there, so e_old is not in the scalar cyclic image. This establishes
failure of the universal insertion claim; it does not establish anything
about existence of these specific arithmetic zeros.

The exact general image map, including the entire complement, follows
from the explicit C[T]-linear full-unit retraction Π_{H,k,r} constructed
in CC.13. Let K_{H,k,r}=ker Π_{H,k,r} and
Q_{H,k,r}=1-η_{H,k,r}Π_{H,k,r}. Since ι_r commutes with permutations,
it maps the original invariant algebra A_{h,k,r} into A_{H,k,r}. Define

\[
X=\Pi_{H,k,r}\iota_r\eta_{h,k,r},\qquad
Y=Q_{H,k,r}\iota_r\eta_{h,k,r}.
\]

Both maps are C[T]-linear. Their exact target and reconstruction are

\[
(X,Y):C_{h,k,r}\hookrightarrow C_{H,k,r}\oplus K_{H,k,r},\qquad
\iota_r\eta_{h,k,r}=\eta_{H,k,r}X+Y.
\]

The equation follows by substituting Q=1-ηΠ. If Xc=Yc=0, the equation
gives ι_rη_hc=0. Both ι_r and η_h are injective, hence c=0. This proves
the displayed injection. The insertion lies in the larger arithmetic
cyclic image exactly when Y=0, and in that case X is injective. No
vanishing of Y has been assumed. This gives the complete exact morphism
even when collisions prevent a scalar-only insertion.
