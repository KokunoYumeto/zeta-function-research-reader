Here is one finite cover that kills all geometric inertia in
either case and keeps track of the additive factor:
\[
 u=v^n,\qquad z^p-z=\frac{bc}{v^n}.
 \tag{SPF.26}\label{eq:SPF26}
\]
Both maps are finite etale on the punctured \(u\)-line or its
pullback: their derivatives there are \(nv^{n-1}\ne0\) and
\(-1\ne0\). The pullback of \(\mathcal K_\chi(u)\) is
\(\mathcal K_{\chi^n}(v)\), hence constant. The pullback of
the Artin--Schreier factor is trivialized by the displayed
torsor itself. For \(c=0\), the first map alone suffices and
the second is a disjoint trivial torsor. For \(c\ne0\), the
second map remains a nontrivial degree-\(p\) extension
after the first: its right side has pole order \(n\), and
\(p\nmid n\), so the preceding pole-order argument applies.
The composite has degree \(np\). Constants
\(\mathcal A_{-\chi(n)G_k}\) can retain arithmetic
Frobenius action; they have no geometric inertia action.

On the resulting finite-index subgroup of \(I_0\), every
one-factor monodromy matrix is the identity. Its logarithm is
\[
 N=N_{\mathrm{mon},1}=\log(1)=0.
 \tag{SPF.27}\label{eq:SPF27}
\]
The original one-factor invariant space is zero by
\eqref{eq:SPF24}--\eqref{eq:SPF25}; after the cover it is the
whole rank-\(m\) space. The same computation on the actual
ordered tensor has the following complete form.

The finite cover
\[
 u=v^n,\qquad z_K^p-z_K=\frac{bkc}{v^n}
 \tag{SPF.T23}\label{eq:SPFT23}
\]
trivializes all geometric inertia of \(\mathcal T_K\).
The first map kills every product Kummer character.
The second trivializes the displayed additive factor
by its own torsor. If \(w_K=0\), it is a trivial
disjoint torsor and the first map alone suffices.
If \(w_K\ne0\), its degree is \(p\), as its pole order
\(n\) is prime to \(p\). When \(c\ne0\) and
\(p\nmid K\), it is explicitly the original cover
\eqref{eq:SPFT13} with \(z_K=kz\); this equality follows
from \(K^p=K\) in \(\mathbf F_p\).
When \(p\mid K\), the original cover still kills the
representation, and its wild deck subgroup already
acts trivially by \eqref{eq:SPFT15}.

On the subgroup defined by this finite cover every
monodromy matrix is the identity. Hence its unipotent
logarithm is exactly
\[
 N_{\mathrm{mon},K}=0 .
 \tag{SPF.T24}\label{eq:SPFT24}
\]
There is no conflict with any of the invariant
dimensions in \eqref{eq:SPFT19}. Those concern the
original inertia group; after the displayed cover,
the restricted representation has the full
\(r_K\)-dimensional invariant space.
