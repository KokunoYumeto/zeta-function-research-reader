 {V^{\rm ar}_{2q-1}V^{\rm ar}_{2q}},\qquad
 B_\Gamma=\log\frac{V^\Gamma_{q-1}V^\Gamma_q}
 {V^\Gamma_{2q-1}V^\Gamma_{2q}},\qquad
 \Delta=B_{\rm ar}-B_\Gamma.                         \tag{CR1}
\]

$D_*=M_{2q}(0)^{-1}M_{2q}(1)$. This symbol is not the Euler differential.
Let $b_1\le\cdots\le b_{2q+1}$ be all its eigenvalues with their full

\[
 L_q=\log\frac{b_{q+2}}{b_q}
 +2\sum_{j=1}^{q-1}\log\frac{b_{2q+2-j}}{b_j}.          \tag{CR2}
\]

\ \le\ B_{\rm ar}\ \le\
\min\{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k,\mathcal U_{\nu,k},B_\Gamma+\mathfrak C_*\}.
\end{split}                                                   \tag{CR3}

For the signed arithmetic correction itself this is

\begin{split}

\ \le\ \Delta\ \le\
\min\{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k-B_\Gamma,
       \mathcal U_{\nu,k}-B_\Gamma,\mathfrak C_*\}.
\end{split}                                                   \tag{CR4}
\]

Polynomial-degree inclusion decreases the minimum quotient Gram in

The actual remainder-map estimates prove
$B_{\rm ar}\le\widehat{\mathcal U}_k$ in (HC8), while comparison
on the identical affine remainder fibres proves
$B_{\rm ar}\le\mathcal U_{\nu,k}$ in (HC15).
The computed full window spectrum and the actual relative moment
eigenvalues and retained trace/overlap give

equivalently the revised (AW14) and (ACM11).
Substituting (CR1) into this last inequality gives the remaining lower
and upper entries of (CR3). Taking the maximum of the three proved
lower bounds and minimum of the three proved upper bounds proves
(CR3), with no independent choice of their source data. Subtracting
the same literal $B_\Gamma$ everywhere gives (CR4).
\hfill$\square$

 &=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}((U(t)-W(t))C(t))\,dt\\
 &=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}

\end{split}                                                   \tag{CR5}
\]

Thus (CR5) has not silently replaced the arithmetic map by an isometry

In particular every putative actual quartet must obey the additional

Indeed the first two nonzero entries follow by combining
