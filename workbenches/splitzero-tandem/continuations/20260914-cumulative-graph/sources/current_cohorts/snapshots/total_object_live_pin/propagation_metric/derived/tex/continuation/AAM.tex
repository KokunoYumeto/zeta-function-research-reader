\section{The actual arithmetic amplification and its proved error scales}
\label{sec:arithmetic-amplification-match}

This calculation uses one fixed original counterfactual anchor
$\rho=1/2+\delta+i\gamma$, of full order $m$ in $g=2\xi$, with
$0<\delta<1/2$ and $\gamma>0$. Its entire quotient and full Taylor
unit are retained throughout:
\[
 h(s)=\prod_{\epsilon,\eta\in\{-1,1\}}
 (s-1/2-\epsilon\delta-i\eta\gamma)^m,
 \quad v_h=g/h,\quad \upsilon_h=j_h(v_h),\quad
 w_h(u)=\frac{|v_h(1/2+iu)|^2}{2\pi}.
 \tag{AAM.1}
\]
The original tensor source, its arithmetic injection and the finite
source degrees provide the following simultaneous data for every
integer $k\geq3$:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 c=k/2,\quad \ell_k=1+k(m-1),\quad q_k=\ell_k(k+1)^2,\quad
 f_k=\left\lfloor\frac{(k+1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor,\\
 \lambda_{a,b}=c+(2a-k)\delta+i(2b-k)\gamma\quad(0\leq a,b\leq k),\\
 \chi_k(S)=\prod_{a,b=0}^k(S-\lambda_{a,b})^{\ell_k},\quad
 E_k=\mathbb C[S]/(\chi_k),\quad A_k^{\rm cyc}=M_S,\\
 m_{h,k}=w_h^{*k},\quad
 \mathcal I_k=\sigma_h^{\otimes k}\eta_{h,k}:E_k\hookrightarrow Q^{\otimes k},
 \qquad\eta_{h,k}[P]=\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}P(A_h^{(k)})1.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.2}
\]
The superscript on $A_k^{\rm cyc}$ distinguishes this operator from
the scalar upper-envelope constant $A_k$ used in (TW8). No coordinate
or operator has been multiplied by this scalar.
The full local nilpotent sum has exact index $\ell_k$: its power
$k(m-1)$ has coefficient $(k(m-1))!/((m-1)!)^k\ne0$ on the product
of the $k$ top local monomials, whereas the next power is zero.
Every real and imaginary sign count occurs in the ordered tensor
product, and $\delta\gamma\ne0$ makes the displayed sums distinct.
Thus the stated complete cyclic annihilator and its dimension are
exactly those in (HT2), with no local directions omitted.

For $N\geq q_k-1$, the original exact sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal P_{N-q_k}
 \xrightarrow{\ \times\chi_k\ }\mathcal P_N
 \xrightarrow{\ J_{k,N}\ }E_k\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad \mathcal P_N=\mathbb C[S]_{\leq N}.
 \tag{AAM.3}
\]
The source norm is the integral of $|P(c+iu)|^2m_{h,k}(u)$.
The relation source is zero when $N=q_k-1$. The unique canonical
minimum lift $R_{k,N}:E_k\to\mathcal P_N$ satisfies
$J_{k,N}R_{k,N}=I$ and $R_{k,N}^*H_{k,N}R_{k,N}=G_{k,N}>0$,
in the original remainder frame, and $V_{k,N}=\det G_{k,N}$.
These facts follow by orthogonal projection onto the kernel in the
finite positive-definite source form, as proved in (CA6).

\subsection{The invariant that the tensor construction amplifies}

A nonzero eigenvector $v$ of $A_h$ at $\rho$ has nonzero ordered
tensor power: a linear functional taking $v$ to one takes
$v^{\otimes k}$ to one. The sum generator acts on that tensor by
$k\rho$, whose real displacement from $c$ is exactly $k\delta$.
The complete positive-half-plane trace uses all primary spaces,
not only this vector. Its original value is
\[
 \mathcal L_k=2\delta\ell_k(k+1)f_k
 =2\sum_{a>k/2}\sum_{b=0}^k\ell_k(2a-k)\delta.
 \tag{AAM.4}
\]
To evaluate the sum, for $k=2r$ its positive real coefficients sum
to $2(1+\cdots+r)=r(r+1)$; for $k=2r-1$ they sum to
$1+3+\cdots+(2r-1)=r^2$. These are exactly $f_k$.
In particular the following are exact finite equalities:
\[
 \frac{\mathcal L_k}{kq_k}
 =\begin{cases}\displaystyle\frac\delta2(1+1/k),&k\text{ odd},\\[2pt]
 \displaystyle\frac\delta2(1+1/(k+1)),&k\text{ even},\end{cases}
 \qquad
 \mathcal L_k\geq\frac{\delta kq_k}{2},\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal L_k}{kq_k}\longrightarrow\frac\delta2.
 \tag{AAM.5}
\]

Here is the exact finite control to which that trace applies. Suppress
$k$ in the subscripts while keeping it fixed, and put $A=A_k^{\rm cyc}$.
The Hermitian matrix in the original quotient metric is
\[
 \mathsf H_N=G_N^{-1/2}(A^*G_N+G_NA-kG_N)G_N^{-1/2}.
 \tag{AAM.6}
\]
Write $p_n$ for the original monic orthogonal polynomials and
$\omega_n$ for their squared norms, and $b_n=[p_n]_{\chi_k}$.
The monic recurrence in the exact sum coordinate is
$Sp_n=p_{n+1}+(c+i\beta_n)p_n
 -(\omega_n/\omega_{n-1})p_{n-1}$ with real $\beta_n$.
Inserting it in $K_N=\sum_{n=0}^Nb_nb_n^*/\omega_n$ cancels
every internal adjacent term and gives
\[
 AK_N+K_NA^*-kK_N
   =\frac{b_{N+1}b_N^*+b_Nb_{N+1}^*}{\omega_N},
 \qquad K_N=G_N^{-1}.
 \tag{AAM.7}
\]
Congruence by $G_N^{1/2}$ identifies its left side with (AAM.6).
It has rank at most two. Its trace is
$2\Re\operatorname{Tr}A-kq_k=0$ by the complete root grid.
It therefore has eigenvalues $\varepsilon_{k,N},-\varepsilon_{k,N}$
and zeros, with $\varepsilon_{k,N}\geq0$.

Let $E_+$ be the direct sum of the complete primary spaces with
$a>k/2$, and let $P_+$ be the orthogonal projection onto
$G_N^{1/2}E_+$ in the displayed Euclidean coordinates.
This subspace is invariant under $G_N^{1/2}AG_N^{-1/2}$.
Taking the trace in an orthonormal basis of that invariant subspace
gives $\operatorname{Tr}(P_+\mathsf H_N)=\mathcal L_k$.
For a Hermitian matrix with spectrum
$\varepsilon,-\varepsilon,0,\ldots,0$, any orthogonal projection
has trace pairing at most $\varepsilon$: its diagonal coefficients
in an eigenbasis lie between zero and one. Thus
\[
 \boxed{\varepsilon_{k,N}=\|\mathsf H_N\|\geq\mathcal L_k,
 \qquad \frac{\varepsilon_{k,N}}{kq_k}\geq
 \frac{\mathcal L_k}{kq_k}\geq\frac\delta2.}
 \tag{AAM.8}
\]
This is the common normalized invariant at every admitted source
degree. It is also the phasewise invariant in (HT9), with the phase
metric retained there. Neither a source norm nor an unnormalized
matrix can be substituted into the numerator of (AAM.8).

\subsection{The exact original window map into that control}

In this subsection $q=q_k$, and every measure is the unperiodized
original $m_{h,k}$. Define the original four-volume and norm term
\[
 \mathcal B_k=\log\frac{V_{q-1}V_q}{V_{2q-1}V_{2q}},\qquad
 \Omega_k=\log\frac{\omega_{2q-1}\omega_{2q}}
                         {\omega_{q-1}\omega_q}.
 \tag{AAM.9}
\]
Retain $d_j=V_j/V_{j-1}$ and the complete contraction penalties
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_-&=-\log(1-d_{2q-1})-2\sum_{j=q}^{2q-2}\log(1-d_j),\\
 P_+&=-\log(1-d_q)-\log(1-d_{2q})
                  -2\sum_{j=q+1}^{2q-1}\log(1-d_j),
 \qquad P_k=P_-+P_+.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{AAM.10}
\]
Here $0<d_j<1$ on these degrees by the original consecutive identities
and the positive trace floor. The phase is the original real number
$\phi_N$ specified by $b_N^*G_Nb_{N+1}=i\omega_N\phi_N$.
Let $w_{q-1}=w_{2q-1}=1$ and $w_N=2$ for the intervening degrees;
their sum is $2q$. The original first-step and regular products
(CJ.9)--(CJ.13) prove exactly
\[
 \mathcal B_k=\sum_{N=q-1}^{2q-1}w_N
                  \log(\varepsilon_{k,N}^2+\phi_N^2)
                         -\Omega_k+P_k.
 \tag{AAM.11}
\]
There is no inverse at degree $q-2$: the first product has one terminal
contraction and the squared interior contractions displayed in $P_-$.

The following quantities extract the same control from that identity,
retaining its entire phase and contraction data:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \Phi_k=\sum_Nw_N\log(1+\phi_N^2/\varepsilon_{k,N}^2)\geq0,
 \qquad
 \overline\varepsilon_k=\prod_N\varepsilon_{k,N}^{w_N/(2q)},\\
 \mathfrak n_k=e^{\Omega_k/(4q)},\qquad
 \mathfrak c_k=e^{(\mathcal B_k-P_k-\Phi_k)/(4q)}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.12}
\]
Every denominator is positive by (AAM.8). Splitting each logarithm
in (AAM.11) proves the exact multiplicative correspondence
\[
 \boxed{\overline\varepsilon_k=\mathfrak n_k\mathfrak c_k,
 \qquad \mathcal L_k\leq\min_{q-1\leq N\leq2q-1}
        \varepsilon_{k,N}\leq\overline\varepsilon_k.}
 \tag{AAM.13}
\]
In particular the exact scalar exclusion threshold for the given
window is
\[
 \mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}=4q\log\mathcal L_k-\Omega_k,\qquad
 \mathcal B_k-\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}
 =P_k+\sum_Nw_N\log
       \frac{\varepsilon_{k,N}^2+\phi_N^2}{\mathcal L_k^2}\geq0.
 \tag{AAM.14}
\]
More precisely, $\mathcal B_k-P_k-\Phi_k\geq
\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}$. This follows from (AAM.13), so it
retains rather than deletes the actual phases.

\subsection{Uniform norm errors and their precise amplified rates}

We use the literal constants already proved in (TW2), (TW7)--(TW8):
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \alpha=\pi/2,\quad p=8m-9/2,\quad B=42+8m,\quad M=B/2,\\
 \vartheta_h=\int_{-1}^{1}w_h(u)\,du,\qquad
 M_\alpha=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{\alpha u}w_h(u)\,du,\\
 a_k=\frac{c_h\vartheta_h^{k-3}e^{-\alpha(k-3)}(k-2)^{-B}}
                 {C_\Gamma2^M},\qquad
 A_k=\frac{C_h}{c_\Gamma}k^{p+1}M_\alpha^{k-1},\\
 D_N=[1+4(N+M)^2]^M,\quad
 c_\Gamma=\sqrt2e^{-7/6},\quad C_\Gamma=\sqrt{2\sqrt5}\,e^{2/3}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.15}
\]
Here $C_h$ is the full boundary-tilt constant of (BT12), and $c_h$
is the original positive convolution lower-envelope constant of
(AU.10). Their original constructions, compact contributions and
full masses remain in the retained chapters. No new zeta bound is
assumed in using them. The proved all-polynomial estimate is
$a_kH_N^\sigma/D_N\preceq H_{k,N}\preceq A_kH_N^\sigma$,
where $\sigma(u)=|\Gamma(1/4+iu/2)|^2/(2\pi)$ has its original
mass $\sqrt{2\pi}$. Minimizing on monic affine fibres gives
$a_k\gamma_n/D_n\leq\omega_n\leq A_k\gamma_n$, with
$\gamma_n=\sqrt{2\pi}(2n)!/4^n$.

Define the exact nonnegative norm-window error and common-source
comparison error by
\[
 \mathcal E_k=2\log(A_k/a_k)+\log D_{q-1}+\log D_q,
 \qquad \Lambda_k=\log(A_k/a_k)+\log D_{2q}.
 \tag{AAM.16}
\]
The symbol $\mathcal E_k$ here denotes exactly $E_k$ in (TW20),
and is distinct from the quotient vector space $E_k$ in (AAM.2).
The two original norm windows (TW16)--(TW19) imply
\[
 \mathfrak n_k/q\longrightarrow4/e,
 \qquad \Omega_k\leq4q\log(4q/e)+\mathcal E_k,
 \qquad \mathcal E_k/q\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{AAM.17}
\]
For completeness the first claim uses the separate limits
$q^{-1}(\omega_{2q}/\omega_q)^{1/(2q)}\to4/e$ and
$q^{-1}(\omega_{2q-1}/\omega_{q-1})^{1/(2q)}\to4/e$;
their positive geometric mean is exactly $\mathfrak n_k/q$.
The upper logarithmic estimate is the sum of the two original
upper norm comparisons and the exact factorial inequality (TW18).

The exponential dependence of the scalar constants can be calculated
more precisely than a big-O estimate. Put
\[
 d_h=\alpha+\log(M_\alpha/\vartheta_h).
 \tag{AAM.18}
\]
The actual $w_h$ is even and positive almost everywhere, so
$M_\alpha=\int\cosh(\alpha u)w_h(u)\,du>\vartheta_h$:
the integral outside $[-1,1]$ is strictly positive and on that interval
$\cosh(\alpha u)\geq1$. Hence $d_h>\alpha>0$.
Taking logarithms of (AAM.15) gives the exact identity
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \log(A_k/a_k)={}&(k-1)\log M_\alpha-(k-3)\log\vartheta_h
       +\alpha(k-3)+(p+1)\log k+B\log(k-2)\\
 &+\log\frac{C_hC_\Gamma2^M}{c_\Gamma c_h}
 =k d_h+(p+1+B)\log k+O_h(1).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{AAM.19}
\]
The last remainder is bounded because
$\log(k-2)-\log k=\log(1-2/k)$ is bounded for $k\geq3$.
Since $(k+1)^2\leq q_k\leq mk(k+1)^2$ and
$\log D_{2q}=O_h(\log q)$, these equalities prove
\[
 \frac{\Lambda_k}{k}\longrightarrow d_h,\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal E_k}{k}\longrightarrow2d_h,\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal E_k}{\mathcal L_k}\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \frac{(2q_k-1)\Lambda_k}{\mathcal L_k}
                 \longrightarrow\frac{4d_h}{\delta}>0.
 \tag{AAM.20}
\]
Thus the small norm error and the four-volume comparison error have
different rates on the original amplified scale, with their exact
dimension factors displayed.

Equations (AAM.5), (AAM.13), and (AAM.17) now give the particularly
direct quantitative correspondence
\[
 \frac{\mathfrak n_k}{\mathcal L_k}
      \sim\frac{8}{e\delta k}\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak c_k\geq\frac{\mathcal L_k}{\mathfrak n_k},
 \qquad
 \liminf_{k\to\infty}\frac{\mathfrak c_k}{k}\geq\frac{e\delta}{8}.
 \tag{AAM.21}
\]
The vanishing first ratio is therefore not an upper estimate on
$\overline\varepsilon_k/\mathcal L_k$. The exact second factor
in the original source identity has at least the stated compensating
growth. This proves the relationship between the small norm-window
factor and the retained arithmetic control, instead of merely declaring
them to be different quantities.

The exact threshold itself has the computed asymptotic
\[
 \frac{\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}}{q_k}-4\log k
          \longrightarrow4\log(e\delta/8),\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}}{\mathcal L_k}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{AAM.22}
\]
Indeed $\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}/(4q)=\log(\mathcal L_k/
\mathfrak n_k)$ and (AAM.21) evaluates this logarithm.
The actual finite lower estimate in (CA20) follows as well:
\[
 \mathcal B_k\geq4q_k\log(\delta e k/8)-\mathcal E_k.
 \tag{AAM.23}
\]
All further contraction, phase, control and trace savings in (TW20)
remain nonnegative. Its final trace ratio is exactly
$2\mathcal L_k/(\delta kq_k)=1+1/k$ for odd $k$ and
$1+1/(k+1)$ for even $k$, by (AAM.5).

\subsection{The available upper functions on the identical window}

The full finite reference number $\mathcal B_k^\sigma$ is evaluated
with the original $\chi_k$ by (CA18)--(CA19); in particular it is
nonnegative. On the common degree-$2q$ source let
$D_*=(H_{2q}^\sigma)^{-1}H_{k,2q}$ have ordered positive
eigenvalues $b_1\leq\cdots\leq b_{2q+1}$ and put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 L_q(D_*)=\log\frac{b_{q+2}}{b_q}
           +2\sum_{j=1}^{q-1}\log\frac{b_{2q+2-j}}{b_j},\\
 \widehat\Phi_q(B)=2\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{B/(4q-2)}),\qquad
 \widehat\Psi_q(t)=(4q-2)\log\cosh(t/2),\\
 \widehat A_\sigma=\widehat\Phi_q(\mathcal B_k^\sigma),\qquad
 U_k^{\rm spec}=\mathcal B_k^\sigma+L_q(D_*),\qquad
 U_k^{\rm joint}=\mathcal B_k^\sigma+\mathfrak C_\sigma,\\
 U_k^{\rm ang}=\widehat\Psi_q(\widehat A_\sigma+\Lambda_k).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.24}
\]
Here $\mathfrak C_\sigma$ is the integral in (CA14) on the
explicit coefficient interpolation (CA14a), whose initial density
is the original fixed $\sigma$, of mass $\sqrt{2\pi}$.
The full-spectrum, exact trace-norm, overlap and angle proofs give
$\mathcal B_k\le\min(U_k^{\rm joint},U_k^{\rm ang})$ with
$U_k^{\rm joint}\le U_k^{\rm spec}$ and
$\mathfrak C_\sigma\le\mathfrak C_\sigma^{\rm ov}
\le L_q(D_*)\le(2q-1)\Lambda_k$.
The tensor Gamma source is a separate comparison; no equality of
its Gram with $H^\sigma$ is used. The current minimum keeps the
actual interpolation and all its overlaps before integration.

The precise asymptotic cost of the displayed angle upper function
can be calculated even without an asymptotic for
$\mathcal B_k^\sigma$ at the moving endpoints. For $t,x\geq0$,
\[
 \frac{e^x}{2}\leq\frac{\cosh(t+x)}{\cosh t}\leq e^x.
\]
The upper inequality follows by expanding both hyperbolic cosines into
exponentials; the lower follows from $\cosh(t+x)\geq e^{t+x}/2$
and $\cosh t\leq e^t$. Use $t=\widehat A_\sigma/2$ and
$x=\Lambda_k/2$, and use the inverse relation between
$\widehat\Phi_q$ and $\widehat\Psi_q$. It gives
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (2q-1)\Lambda_k-(4q-2)\log2
 \leq U_k^{\rm ang}-\mathcal B_k^\sigma
 \leq(2q-1)\Lambda_k,\\
 \frac{U_k^{\rm ang}-\mathcal B_k^\sigma}{\mathcal L_k}
       \longrightarrow\frac{4d_h}{\delta}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.25}
\]
The discarded constant term divided by $\mathcal L_k$ tends to zero
by (AAM.5). This is a statement about the proved upper function;
it is not an asymptotic assertion about the signed actual difference.

For an independent upper function retain exactly the original (HC8)
constants. Set $R_0=\sqrt{\delta^2+\gamma^2}$, choose the original
$0<b<\pi/2$, and put
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 D=\max(1,2/b,2R_0),\quad a=\alpha D+\log256,\quad
 X=\max(1,M_h(b),M_h(-b)),\quad
 K=\max(1,\vartheta_h^{-1}),\\
 C_{\rm env}=\max(1,2/(3c_hD)),\quad
 M_h(t)=\int e^{tu}w_h(u)\,du,\\
 \widehat{\mathcal U}_k=2a q^2+
 2\left(\log(XK)+\alpha+\frac{16R_0}{3D}\right)kq
 +2Bq\log(1+(D+1)q)+2q\log C_{\rm env}+q\log20.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.26}
\]
All these constants are fixed by the original anchor, and the retained
proof gives $\mathcal B_k\leq\widehat{\mathcal U}_k$.
Since $k/q\to0$ and $\log q/q\to0$, direct division yields
\[
 \frac{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k}{q_k^2}\longrightarrow2a,
 \qquad
 \frac{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k}{\mathcal L_k}
          \sim\frac{4a}{\delta}\frac{q_k}{k}\longrightarrow\infty.
 \tag{AAM.27}
\]

Combine these proved bounds on the identical actual scalar by defining
\[
 U_k^{\rm all}=\min\{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k,
                                  U_k^{\rm joint},U_k^{\rm ang}\}.
 \tag{AAM.28}
\]
This definition propagates the sharper common-source estimate
through the control calculation: because $U_k^{\rm joint}\le
U_k^{\rm spec}$, its value is no larger than the preceding
three-term upper function. The following finite comparisons use
this revised $U_k^{\rm all}$ in (AAM.12)--(AAM.14):
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 1\leq\frac{\overline\varepsilon_k}{\mathcal L_k}
 &\leq\exp\!\left(
 \frac{U_k^{\rm all}-\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}-P_k-\Phi_k}{4q_k}
                    \right)\\
 &\leq\frac{4q_k}{e\mathcal L_k}
       \exp\!\left(\frac{U_k^{\rm all}+\mathcal E_k}{4q_k}\right),\\
 1\leq\frac{\varepsilon_{k,N}}{\mathcal L_k}
 &\leq\exp\!\left(
 \frac{U_k^{\rm all}-\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}-P_k}{2w_N}
                    \right)
       \qquad(q_k-1\leq N\leq2q_k-1).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{AAM.29}
\]
For the individual bound, isolate its one term in the nonnegative sum
in (AAM.14), and use
$\log((\varepsilon_{k,N}^2+\phi_N^2)/\mathcal L_k^2)
\geq2\log(\varepsilon_{k,N}/\mathcal L_k)$.
These formulas retain every known finite input. They do not supply
an upper ratio below one: their exponent with the actual data is
nonnegative by (AAM.14). The coarser scalar function
$\widehat{\mathcal U}_k$ gives
\[
 \frac{\widehat{\mathcal U}_k-\mathcal B_k^{\rm crit}}{4q_k}
          =\frac a2q_k+O_h(k+\log q_k),
 \tag{AAM.30}
\]
which follows from (AAM.22) and (AAM.26), and grows without bound.
The angle upper function has the strictly positive
$\mathcal L_k$-scale overhead (AAM.25). The full-spectrum expression
$U_k^{\rm spec}$ and the sharper $U_k^{\rm joint}$ remain the
explicitly defined original finite matrix calculations in (AAM.24)
and (CA14a). No shrinking moving-$k$ bound for the joint spectral,
trace-norm and overlap integrand is inferred from its finite value. In particular the
proved subquadratic comparison
$(\mathcal B_k-\mathcal B_k^\sigma)/q_k^2\to0$ does not give a
sublinear upper bound for the normalized control (AAM.8).

\subsection{What the fixed-packet degree limit preserves}

For each fixed $k$, the full-jet theorem (FPK.23)--(FPK.29) and
the actual comparison (LC.26)--(LC.28) give the exact leading
coefficient
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\log V_{k,N}^{\rm ar}}{\log N}
       =-\mathcal L_k,\qquad
 \log V_{k,N}^\sigma=-\mathcal L_k\log N
                      -\mathcal B_{\chi_k}\log\log N+C_{\sigma,k}+o(1),\\
 \log V_{k,N}^{\Gamma}=-\mathcal L_k\log N
                      -\mathcal B_{\chi_k}\log\log N+C_{\Gamma,k}+o(1),\\
 \mathcal B_{\chi_k}=
 \begin{cases}(k+1)\ell_k^2,&k\text{ even},\\0,&k\text{ odd}.
 \end{cases}
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AAM.31}
\]
The original tensor-Gamma reference in the third line has mass
$(2\pi)^{k/2}$; its full constant $C_{\Gamma,k}$ is the one in
(FPK.29). The fixed comparison in the second line has mass
$\sqrt{2\pi}$. The original remainder and full unit maps are the
same for these statements. Thus, for $r_{k,N}=V_{k,N}^{\rm ar}/
V_{k,N}^{\Gamma}$,
\[
 \frac{\log r_{k,N}}{\log N}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{AAM.32}
\]
Equivalently, for every positive $\epsilon$ and this fixed $k$, all
sufficiently large $N$ satisfy $N^{-\epsilon}\leq r_{k,N}\leq
N^\epsilon$. The threshold here depends on the complete fixed
$k$-packet, and is not specified by the moving endpoints $q_k,2q_k$.

There are useful exact consequences without exchanging limits.
For fixed $t>1$, (AAM.31) proves
\[
 \frac{V_{k,N}^\sigma}{V_{k,\lfloor tN\rfloor}^\sigma}
       \longrightarrow t^{\mathcal L_k},\qquad
 \frac{V_{k,N}^{\Gamma}}{V_{k,\lfloor tN\rfloor}^{\Gamma}}
       \longrightarrow t^{\mathcal L_k},\qquad
 \frac{\log(V_{k,N}^{\rm ar}/V_{k,\lfloor tN\rfloor}^{\rm ar})}
          {\log N}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{AAM.33}
\]
The first two assertions follow by subtraction of their complete
asymptotics: the constants cancel, the logarithmic ratios tend to
$\log t$, the $\log\log$ differences tend to zero, and both
remainders tend to zero. For the third, divide each of the two
actual logarithms by its own degree logarithm and use
$\log\lfloor tN\rfloor/\log N\to1$. It does not prove that
the actual ratio tends to $t^{\mathcal L_k}$.
For a fixed power $t>1$ the same argument with $\lfloor N^t\rfloor$
instead gives the nonzero actual exponent
\[
 \frac{\log(V_{k,N}^{\rm ar}/V_{k,\lfloor N^t\rfloor}^{\rm ar})}
          {\log N}\longrightarrow(t-1)\mathcal L_k.
 \tag{AAM.34}
\]
These are degree limits on a fixed original quotient; neither proof
substitutes a growing $k$ into a fixed-$k$ error term.

Finally the full operator result of the Hilbert-completion chapter
is $\|G_{k,N}^{\rm ar}\|\to0$ for each fixed $k$, while
$\mathcal O_{k,N}R_{k,N}=\mathcal I_k\ne0$. Its exact effect
on the control matrix can be computed. Put
$W_{k,N}=A^*G_{k,N}+G_{k,N}A-kG_{k,N}$. Then
\[
 \|W_{k,N}\|\leq2\|A-cI\|\,\|G_{k,N}\|\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 G_{k,N}^{-1/2}W_{k,N}G_{k,N}^{-1/2}=\mathsf H_N,
 \quad\|\mathsf H_N\|\geq\mathcal L_k.
 \tag{AAM.35}
\]
The first inequality expands $W=(A-cI)^*G+G(A-cI)$ and applies
submultiplicativity in the original fixed frame. The second is its
literal congruence (AAM.6). There is no bounded inverse factor in
