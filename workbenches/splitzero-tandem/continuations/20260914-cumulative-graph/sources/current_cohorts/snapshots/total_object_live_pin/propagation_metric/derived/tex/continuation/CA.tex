\section{The actual arithmetic form passes to every original quotient}

Retain $g=2\xi$, the full actual hypothetical quartet polynomial $h$,
its complete common order $m$, and $v_h=g/h$.  For $k\ge3$ put
$c=k/2$, $\ell=1+k(m-1)$, and $q=\ell(k+1)^2$.  The original
cyclic polynomial is
\[
 \chi(S)=\prod_{a,b=0}^{k}
 \bigl(S-c-(2a-k)\delta-i(2b-k)\gamma\bigr)^\ell.
 \tag{CA1}
\]
Thus $E=\mathbb C[S]/(\chi)$ has the increasing-power frame of length
$q$.  No distinct factors or nilpotent orders are merged.  The original
observation is $\mathcal O_N=\sigma_h^{\otimes k}\eta J_N:
\mathcal P_N\to Q_{\rm ar}^{\otimes k}$, with
$Q_{\rm ar}=\mathscr B/\Theta V$, where
$J_N$ is monic remainder and $\eta:E\hookrightarrow E_h^{\otimes k}$,
$\eta[P]=\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}
P(A_h^{(k)})1$ retains the full Taylor unit
$\upsilon_h=j_h(g/h)$.  For $N\ge q-1$ its original source and relation
sequence is
\[
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal P_{N-q}\xrightarrow{B_N=\times\chi}
 \mathcal P_N\xrightarrow{J_N}E\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{CA2}
\]
The empty relation space at $N=q-1$ is zero.  All comparison maps below
are the identity on $\mathcal P_N$ and $E$ in this sequence; they
therefore commute with $\mathcal O_N$ and retain its supported label.

Let $m_{h,k}=w_h^{*k}$, with
$w_h(u)=|v_h(1/2+iu)|^2/(2\pi)$, and let
\[
 \sigma(u)=\frac{|\Gamma(1/4+iu/2)|^2}{2\pi},\qquad
 H_N^\sigma=\int v_N(u)^*v_N(u)\sigma(u)\,du,
 \quad v_N(u)=(1,c+iu,\ldots,(c+iu)^N).
 \tag{CA3}
\]
The masses are respectively $\mu_h^k$ and $\sqrt{2\pi}$.
In particular this fixed one-factor comparison is different from the
inherited $k$-fold Gamma convolution, whose mass is $(2\pi)^{k/2}$.
Write $H_N^{\rm ar}$ for the same moment matrix with density $m_{h,k}$.
The preceding boundary-tilt calculation proves positive explicit
$a_k,A_k$ and a fixed integer $M$ depending on $h$, with
\[
 \frac{a_k\sigma(u)}{(1+u^2)^M}\le m_{h,k}(u)\le A_k\sigma(u),
 \quad D_N=[1+4(N+M)^2]^M,
 \quad c_{k,N}=a_k/D_N,
 \tag{CA4}
\]
and proves, by its finite Gamma recurrence and weighted Cauchy argument,
\[
 c_{k,N}H_N^\sigma\preceq H_N^{\rm ar}\preceq A_kH_N^\sigma.
 \tag{CA5}
\]
The complete definitions and proofs of these constants and estimates
are in the preceding chapter.  Equations (CA4)--(CA5) invoke those
proved arithmetic estimates; they add no assumption about a zeta
lower bound or about the sign of a determinant correction.

For $\star\in\{\mathrm{ar},\sigma\}$ retain the exact matrices
\[
 K_N^\star=J_N(H_N^\star)^{-1}J_N^*,\qquad
 G_N^\star=(K_N^\star)^{-1},\qquad
 R_N^\star=(H_N^\star)^{-1}J_N^*G_N^\star,
 \qquad V_N^\star=\det G_N^\star.
 \tag{CA6}
\]
Positivity and surjectivity give positive invertible matrices
$H_N^\star,K_N^\star,G_N^\star$ of their displayed square dimensions.
The map $R_N^\star:E\to\mathcal P_N$ is the displayed right inverse,
which is rectangular when $N>q-1$. Direct multiplication gives
$J_NR_N^\star=I$ and $B_N^*H_N^\star R_N^\star=0$.  Every lift of
$x\in E$ is uniquely $R_N^\star x+B_Ny$, with squared norm
$x^*G_N^\star x+y^*B_N^*H_N^\star B_Ny$.  Thus $G_N^\star$ is
the minimum on the same original affine remainder fibre.
Applying (CA5) to each point of that fibre and taking its infimum proves
\[
 \boxed{c_{k,N}G_N^\sigma\preceq G_N^{\rm ar}
          \preceq A_kG_N^\sigma,\qquad
 c_{k,N}^{q}V_N^\sigma\le V_N^{\rm ar}\le A_k^qV_N^\sigma.}
 \tag{CA7}
\]
For the determinant claim, conjugate a positive comparison by the
inverse square root of its smaller form; all relative eigenvalues are
at least one, so their product is at least one.  This also proves the
claim when the forms do not commute.

There is an exact original cochain connecting the two representatives.
Use the original two-term complex $[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B]$
in degrees zero and one and the polynomial source map
$\mathcal V_{h,k}:\mathbb C[S]\to\mathscr B^{\otimes k}$,
$P\mapsto P(\sum_jD_j)F_h^{\otimes k}$.
Since $J_N(R_N^{\rm ar}-R_N^\sigma)x=0$, monic division gives the
unique $\kappa_x\in\mathcal P_{N-q}$ with
\[
 (R_N^{\rm ar}-R_N^\sigma)x=\chi\kappa_x.
 \tag{CA8}
\]
Its coefficients depend linearly on $x$.  Use the original polynomials
$Q_i$ from successive tensor division, with
$\chi(\sum s_j)=\sum_i h(s_i)Q_i(\mathbf s)$, and the original
$F_h$ satisfying $\mathcal MF_h=v_h$ and $h(D)F_h=\Theta\phi_*$. Then
\[
 \mathfrak p_x=\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{i-1}Q_i(\mathbf D)
 \kappa_x(\textstyle\sum_jD_j)
 \bigl(F_h^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes\phi_*\otimes
 F_h^{\otimes(k-i)}\bigr)
 \tag{CA9}
\]
has degree $k-1$ in the original tensor theta complex and satisfies
\[
 d_{\rm tot}\mathfrak p_x=
 \mathcal V_{h,k}(R_N^{\rm ar}-R_N^\sigma)x.
 \tag{CA10}
\]
Indeed the $i-1$ preceding factors have degree one, so the differential
contributes $(-1)^{i-1}$, cancelling the written sign.  Its remaining
factor is $\Theta\phi_*=h(D_i)F_h$; the displayed polynomial identity
then proves (CA10) term by term.  This is the same explicit primitive
as inherited EW.46--48 applied to the new representative difference.
It maps to the supported zero in the original labelled fibre; external
$\tau$ is unchanged.

\section{A finite computable comparison for the four arithmetic volumes}

Define on the four original endpoints
\[
 B_{\rm ar}=\log\frac{V_{q-1}^{\rm ar}V_q^{\rm ar}}
 {V_{2q-1}^{\rm ar}V_{2q}^{\rm ar}},\quad
 B_\sigma=\log\frac{V_{q-1}^\sigma V_q^\sigma}
 {V_{2q-1}^\sigma V_{2q}^\sigma},\quad
 \Delta_\sigma=B_{\rm ar}-B_\sigma.
 \tag{CA11}
\]
All are in the original quotient frame.  Quotient minima decrease with
source degree, hence both $B$'s are nonnegative.  Applying (CA7) at the
four endpoints gives the asymmetric exact scalar enclosures
\[
 \begin{split}
 -q\log\frac{A_k^2}{c_{k,q-1}c_{k,q}}
 &\le\Delta_\sigma\le
 q\log\frac{A_k^2}{c_{k,2q-1}c_{k,2q}}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CA12}
\]
For example the upper numerator is at most
$A_k^{2q}V_{q-1}^\sigma V_q^\sigma$, and the lower denominator is at
least $(c_{k,2q-1}c_{k,2q})^qV_{2q-1}^\sigma V_{2q}^\sigma$.
The opposite choices prove the lower bound without imposing a sign on
$\Delta_\sigma$.

For the stronger common-source comparison let
\[
 D_*=(H_{2q}^\sigma)^{-1}H_{2q}^{\rm ar},\quad
 b_1\le\cdots\le b_{2q+1},\quad
 \Lambda_k=\log(A_kD_{2q}/a_k).
 \tag{CA13}
\]
The $b_j$ are all positive eigenvalues of this specified relative
operator, including every multiplicity.  The Rayleigh quotient in
(CA5) gives $c_{k,2q}\le b_j\le A_k$ for each $j$; in particular
$\log(b_{2q+1}/b_1)\le\Lambda_k$ and $\Lambda_k\ge0$.
On this particular interpolation put $H(t)=(1-t)H_{2q}^\sigma
+tH_{2q}^{\rm ar}$ and $C^\sigma(t)=H(t)^{-1}(H_{2q}^{\rm ar}-H_{2q}^\sigma)$.
The identity coefficient maps (CA2) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_N^\sigma(t)&=I_N(I_N^*H(t)I_N)^{-1}I_N^*H(t),\\
Q_N^\sigma(t)&=I_NB_N(B_N^*I_N^*H(t)I_NB_N)^{-1}B_N^*I_N^*H(t),\\
U^\sigma&=Q_{2q-1}^\sigma+Q_{2q}^\sigma-Q_q^\sigma,\qquad
W^\sigma=P_{2q-1}^\sigma-P_{q-1}^\sigma+P_{2q}^\sigma-P_q^\sigma.
\end{aligned}\tag{CA14a}
\]
Here $Q_{q-1}^\sigma=0$. Each inverse is on the original nonzero
source or relation domain. Direct multiplication proves the same
orthogonal projector identities as (AT11); the nested spaces give
the full spectrum $0^q,1^2,2^{q-1}$ for both window operators.
Block determinants for (CA2), followed by differentiation, give
$\Delta_\sigma=\int_0^1\Tr((U^\sigma-W^\sigma)C^\sigma)\,dt$.
Thus the exact proof (AT18a)--(AT21) applies to these displayed
matrices, with no identification of $H^\sigma$ with the tensor
Gamma Gram. Define $S_{\rm spec}^\sigma,S_{\rm tr}^\sigma,
S_{\rm ov}^\sigma$ by (AT18a) using precisely
$(U^\sigma,W^\sigma,C^\sigma,H(t))$, and set
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak C_\sigma&=\int_0^1\min\{S_{\rm spec}^\sigma,S_{\rm tr}^\sigma\}\,dt,
&\mathfrak C_\sigma^{\rm ov}&=\int_0^1\min\{S_{\rm spec}^\sigma,S_{\rm ov}^\sigma\}\,dt,\\
L_q(D_*)&=\log\frac{b_{q+2}}{b_q}
 +2\sum_{j=1}^{q-1}\log\frac{b_{2q+2-j}}{b_j}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The resulting full comparison at this earlier theorem site is
\[
\boxed{|\Delta_\sigma|\le\mathfrak C_\sigma
 \le\mathfrak C_\sigma^{\rm ov}\le L_q(D_*)
 \le(2q-1)\Lambda_k.}\tag{CA14}
\]
Every step of the transferred proof holds: positivity follows from
(CA3)--(CA6), the source dimension is $2q+1$, and the same nested
flags give all trace and overlap bounds. Eigenvalues of $C^\sigma$
are $(b_j-1)/(1-t+tb_j)$ in the actual interpolated metric, so
integration gives the displayed logarithms. Both original masses
and the coefficient map commuting with $\mathcal O_N$ remain fixed.

The stronger nonlinear angle estimate is already proved in the original
source EW.25--35c for every packet dimension and every window length.
For these endpoints its $s=\min(q,q)=q$.  To retain its attribution and
its exact effect, define
\[
 \Phi_q(B)=2\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{B/(4q)}),\qquad
 \Psi_q(t)=4q\log\cosh(t/2)\quad(t\ge0),
 \quad A_\sigma=\Phi_q(B_\sigma).
 \tag{CA15}
\]
They are mutually inverse increasing continuous functions on
$[0,\infty)$.  That inherited theorem gives
\[
 |\Phi_q(B_{\rm ar})-\Phi_q(B_\sigma)|
 \le\log(b_{2q+1}/b_1)\le\Lambda_k.
 \tag{CA16}
\]
Equivalently it supplies
\[
 \Psi_q\bigl(\max(0,A_\sigma-\Lambda_k)\bigr)
 \le B_{\rm ar}\le\Psi_q(A_\sigma+\Lambda_k).
 \tag{CA17}
\]
For clarity its exact derivative mechanism is as follows.  The two pairs
of original canonical-lift projections have at most $2q$ nonzero angle
losses $x_i=-\log\sigma_i^2$, with $\sum_i x_i=B(t)$.
Their trace norms and concavity of $\sqrt{1-e^{-x}}$ give
$|B'(t)|\le2q\,o(t)\sqrt{1-e^{-B(t)/(2q)}}$, where
$o(t)=\lambda_{\max}(H(t)^{-1}H')-
\lambda_{\min}(H(t)^{-1}H')$.  At $B>0$, division is differentiation
of $\Phi_q$, and replacing $B$ by $B+\varepsilon$ justifies integration
through $B=0$.  The exact eigenvalues
$(b_j-1)/(1-t+tb_j)$ integrate to $\log b_j$, proving (CA16).
This recalls the proved inherited argument; it does not claim it as a
new generalization of the one-dimensional AW angle result.

The accompanying calculation AGT, from the coordinating programme,
sharpens this inherited method by pairing the same endpoints as
$(q-1,2q)$ and $(q,2q-1)$.  The second pair lies in the actual
$(2q-1)$-dimensional space
$\mathcal P_{2q-1}\cap(\chi\mathcal P_0)^\perp$; its two

One of its angles is exactly zero.  There are consequently at most
$2q-1$ nonzero angle contributions in the same four-volume, rather

included in this reader.  Put
\[
 \widehat\Phi_q(B)=2\operatorname{arcosh}(e^{B/(4q-2)}),\qquad
 \widehat\Psi_q(t)=(4q-2)\log\cosh(t/2),\qquad
 \widehat A_\sigma=\widehat\Phi_q(B_\sigma).
 \tag{CA17a}
\]
Apply the same finite angle sum to (CA3)--(CA6), whose positive moment
form and relation flags meet all the proved hypotheses of AGT.  The
concavity argument just displayed now has $2q-1$ terms.  Its derivative
bound is $(2q-1)o(t)\sqrt{1-e^{-B(t)/(2q-1)}}$; its factor multiplying
$o(t)$ is exactly $1/\widehat\Phi_q'(B(t))$. Integration therefore proves
\[
 \widehat\Psi_q(\max(0,\widehat A_\sigma-\Lambda_k))
 \le B_{\rm ar}\le\widehat\Psi_q(\widehat A_\sigma+\Lambda_k).
 \tag{CA17b}
\]
The strict positive moment $\int|\chi|^2m_t>0$ used in AGT holds for
this interpolation as well, so its differentiation is valid throughout
the compact interval.  This states the precise improvement over EW;
the general angle mechanism and its original theta primitive remain
attributed to their earlier source.

The reference number $B_\sigma$ itself is a fully specified finite

\[
 b_0=1,\quad b_1=u,\quad
 b_{n+1}=ub_n-n(n-1/2)b_{n-1},\quad
 \gamma_n=\sqrt{2\pi}(2n)!/4^n,
 \quad p_n(S)=i^n b_n^{(1/4)}((S-c)/i).
 \tag{CA18}

The fixed Gamma integral proves that $p_n$ is monic in the original

\[
 K_N^\sigma=\sum_{n=0}^N\frac{z_nz_n^*}{\gamma_n},\qquad
 B_\sigma=\log\frac{\det K_{2q-1}^\sigma\det K_{2q}^\sigma}
 {\det K_{q-1}^\sigma\det K_q^\sigma}.
 \tag{CA19}
\]
To prove the kernel formula, the monic polynomial columns form an

Thus $(H_N^\sigma)^{-1}=Z\operatorname{diag}(\gamma_n^{-1})Z^*$.
Multiplying by $J_N$ and $J_N^*$ gives the displayed sum, and the

This retains the original phases, centre, degree, unit, and mass.

\section{The strengthened restriction and its exact reach}

The preceding actual-tail chapter proves the original arithmetic lower
bound
\[
 B_{\rm ar}\ge4q\log(\delta e k/8)-E_k,\qquad
 E_k=2\log(A_k/a_k)+\log D_{q-1}+\log D_q\ge0.
 \tag{CA20}
\]
It is obtained from the inherited CJ identity, the full-multiplicity
trace floor, and the newly proved norm bounds.  Its exact nonnegative
contraction, phase, control and trace residuals are retained in that
chapter.  Equations (CA14), (CA17b), and (CA20) give simultaneously
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\max\{0,\ 4q\log(\delta e k/8)-E_k,\ B_\sigma-\mathfrak C_\sigma,\\
 &\qquad\widehat\Psi_q(\max(0,\widehat A_\sigma-\Lambda_k))\}
 \\&\qquad\le B_{\rm ar}\le
 \min\{B_\sigma+\mathfrak C_\sigma,\ \widehat\Psi_q(\widehat A_\sigma+\Lambda_k)\}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{CA21}
\]
The two spectral entries of the preceding edition are now obtained
as corollaries from $\mathfrak C_\sigma\le L_q(D_*)$; the current
interval retains the pointwise minimum before integration. Each
entry bounds the same original scalar (CA11); taking the indicated
maximum and minimum therefore proves (CA21).  The earlier original
upper bounds and full-spectrum old-Gamma bounds remain simultaneous
bounds and may also be inserted in this minimum.  They are not discarded.

For the fixed hypothetical actual packet the proved constants give
$\log A_k-\log a_k=O_h(k+\log k)$ and hence
\[
 E_k=O_h(k+\log q)=o(q),\quad
 \Lambda_k=O_h(k+\log q),\quad
 |B_{\rm ar}-B_\sigma|=O_h(qk+q\log q)=o(q^2).
 \tag{CA22}
\]
Indeed $q=\ell(k+1)^2\ge(k+1)^2$, while $\ell\le mk$ for $k\ge1$,
so $\log q=O_h(\log k)$, $k/q\to0$, and $\log q/q\to0$.
Also $\log D_{2q}=M\log[1+4(2q+M)^2]=O_h(\log q)$.
Substitution in (CA14) and (CA20) proves every assertion in (CA22).
In particular the actual arithmetic four-volume loss and the explicitly
specified fixed-Gamma four-volume loss have the same leading quadratic
scale:
\[
 \frac{B_{\rm ar}-B_\sigma}{q^2}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{CA23}
\]
This statement specifies their difference; it asserts neither separate
limit exists without its calculation.

The improved exact lower restriction is
\[
 \liminf_{k\to\infty}\left(\frac{B_{\rm ar}}q-4\log k\right)
 \ge4\log(\delta e/8).
 \tag{CA24}
\]
It follows by dividing (CA20) by $q$ and using $E_k/q\to0$.
The coefficient four was already present in CJ; the literal constant
and vanishing per-dimension error here follow from the new arithmetic
tail estimate.  Since the error bound in (CA14) has order $qk$, it is
larger than the $q\log k$ lower scale in (CA20).  Thus (CA23) alone
does not establish an opposing estimate on that smaller scale.
No actual off-critical zero is constructed by any comparison here,
and no contradiction to its counterfactual is asserted.  What is proved
is the sharper arithmetic restriction (CA21), the exact cochain map
(CA8)--(CA10), and the subquadratic comparison (CA23) on the unchanged
original source.

\section{The original convolution reference has a compulsory spectral spread}

The old $k$-fold Gamma comparison remains part of the programme and has
the explicit density and monic squared norms
\[
 m_k^\Gamma(u)=\frac{(\sqrt{2\pi})^k}{c_{k/4}}
 \frac{|\Gamma(k/4+iu/2)|^2}{2\pi},\quad
 c_a=2^{1-2a}\Gamma(2a),\qquad
 \gamma_{k,n}^{\Gamma}=(2\pi)^{k/2}
 n!\frac{\Gamma(n+k/2)}{\Gamma(k/2)}.
 \tag{CA25}
\]
These are the inherited generating-integral identities with their
literal mass.  On its original degree-$2q$ space put
$D_{\rm old}=(H_{2q}^{\Gamma})^{-1}H_{2q}^{\rm ar}$ and let
$\kappa_{\rm old}$ be the ratio of its largest to smallest eigenvalues.
The preceding actual estimate forces
\[
 \boxed{\kappa_{\rm old}\ge
 \max\left\{1,
 \frac{\mu_h^k}{\sqrt2 A_k\Gamma(k/2)}
 \frac{\Gamma(2q+k/2)}{\Gamma(2q+1/2)}\right\}.}
 \tag{CA26}
\]
To prove it without changing a source frame, let $b_-,b_+$ be these two
extreme eigenvalues.  The constant polynomial gives
$b_+\ge\mu_h^k/(2\pi)^{k/2}$.  The full form comparison defining
$b_-$ gives $\omega_{h,k,n}\ge b_-\gamma_{k,n}^{\Gamma}$ on every
monic affine fibre with $n\le2q$; take its infimum to see this directly.
Thus $b_-\le\omega_{h,k,2q}/\gamma_{k,2q}^{\Gamma}$.
The proved TW.14 inequality further gives
$\omega_{h,k,2q}\le A_k\gamma_{2q}$.  Combining these inequalities
and substituting (CA18), (CA25) proves (CA26), including the factor
$\Gamma(1/2)/\sqrt{2\pi}=1/\sqrt2$.  The independent lower bound one
is the ordered positive-spectrum inequality.

Here the size of the compulsory spread can also be proved explicitly.
For integers $n\ge1,k\ge1$ one has
\[
 n^{(k-1)/2}\le
 \frac{\Gamma(n+k/2)}{\Gamma(n+1/2)}
 \le(n+k/2)^{(k-1)/2}.
 \tag{CA27}
\]
For odd $k=2r+1$, the ratio is the exact product
$\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}(n+1/2+j)$, and bounding each factor proves it.
For even $k=2r$, separate the product
$\prod_{j=1}^{r-1}(n+j)$ and the factor
$\Gamma(n+1)/\Gamma(n+1/2)$.  The Gamma integral and Cauchy--Schwarz
give $\Gamma((x+y)/2)^2\le\Gamma(x)\Gamma(y)$ for $x,y>0$:
apply that inequality to the functions
$t^{(x-1)/2}e^{-t/2}$ and $t^{(y-1)/2}e^{-t/2}$.
Using $(x,y)=(n,n+1)$ and $(n+1/2,n+3/2)$, and using the Gamma
recurrence in both cases, yields
\[
 \sqrt n\le\frac{\Gamma(n+1)}{\Gamma(n+1/2)}
 \le\sqrt{n+1/2}.
\]
Every factor is positive, so multiplication proves (CA27) also for
even $k$.  Empty products in the cases $k=1,2$ are one.  In particular
the error in replacing the logarithm of its ratio by
$((k-1)/2)\log n$ lies between zero and
$((k-1)/2)\log(1+k/(2n))\le k^2/(4n)$.

The actual $A_k$ in TW.8 satisfies $\log A_k=O_h(k+\log k)$.
Also $\log\Gamma(k/2)=(k/2)\log k+O(k)$ for integer $k\to\infty$.
For the latter claim no complex asymptotic is needed: bounding the
sum $\log(r!)$ between its two adjacent integrals of $\log x$ gives
$\log(r!)=r\log r-r+O(\log(r+1))$.  For even $k$ apply this to
$\Gamma(k/2)=(k/2-1)!$; for odd $k=2r+1$ use the exact identity
$\Gamma(r+1/2)=\sqrt\pi(2r)!/(4^r r!)$ and the same two bounds.
Both substitutions give the stated $O(k)$ form.
Taking logarithms in (CA26), using the lower side of (CA27) at $n=2q$,
and retaining the fixed original mass, proves
\[
 \log\kappa_{\rm old}\ge
 \frac{k-1}{2}\log(2q)-\frac k2\log k+O_h(k+\log k).
 \tag{CA28}
\]
Here the meaning of the signed $O_h$ term is a function bounded in
absolute value by a fixed multiple of $k+\log k$, as follows from the
preceding explicit formula.  This inequality yields
\[
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{k\to\infty}\frac{\log\kappa_{\rm old}}{k\log k}
 \ge\begin{cases}\tfrac12,&m=1,\\1,&m\ge2.\end{cases}}
 \tag{CA29}
\]
Indeed when $m=1$, $q=(k+1)^2$ gives $\log(2q)=2\log k+O(1)$.
When $m\ge2$ is fixed,
$q=[1+k(m-1)](k+1)^2$ gives $\log(2q)=3\log k+O_h(1)$.
Substitution in (CA28), division by $k\log k$, and passage to the
lower limit prove both cases.

This is a constraint on the actual relative moment operator, not on
freely chosen positive matrices.  It shows that the old convolution
reference cannot have a uniformly bounded condition number along these
original amplified sources.  In contrast, (CA13) proves the upper bound
$\log\kappa(D_*)=O_h(k+\log k)$ for the fixed reference, with the exact
identity comparison through (CA2).  Both source masses and both reference
maps remain explicit.  A condition-number lower bound does not fix the
orientation of the original window projections in its eigenvectors;
consequently (CA29) assigns no sign to the original signed four-volume
correction.
