\section{What has been reconstructed, and what has been decided}

This manuscript reconstructs the actual programme in the shared conversation
\href{https://chatgpt.com/share/6aa6bc6e-98cc-83eb-9343-e811c26d6631}{Branch
\textperiodcentered\ Compile Navier Stokes Literature}, together with the two
supplied continuation instructions. Its question is the counterfactual to RH
inside that programme: take a genuine off-critical zero of its original
arithmetic function, and carry its complete cohomological packet through the
constructed maps. The question is not answered by assigning arbitrary roots
to an auxiliary polynomial.

The recovered source does construct split support, an absolute base,
coefficient Frobenius, coefficient purity, arithmetic cohomology over that
base, its right adjoint, actual theta representatives, tensor and symmetric
pushforwards, conormal maps, exponential periods, and finite holonomy
comparisons. These constructions are used below. The source's final
auxiliary counterexample does not establish that RH is false, or that the
whole programme fails. This continuation also establishes neither an RH
contradiction nor an off-critical zeta zero. Its new results are exact
comparisons, explicit retained kernels, and quantitative restrictions on an
actual putative zero. Each is proved in the subsequent chapters.

\section{The original absolute base and the arithmetic scalar square}

For a commutative ring $R$, retain the disjoint union
\[
G(R)=\{\tau\}\sqcup\{r^\bullet:r\in R\},\qquad e=0_R^\bullet.
\]
Addition has identity $\tau$, with
$r^\bullet\oplus s^\bullet=(r+s)^\bullet$; multiplication has identity
$1_R^\bullet$, has absorbing element $\tau$, and satisfies
$r^\bullet s^\bullet=(rs)^\bullet$. The laws follow directly from the
ring laws when all inputs are supported; cases containing $\tau$ use
its stated identity or absorption law. In particular $e\ne\tau$,
$e\oplus e=e$, and $er^\bullet=e$ for every supported scalar.

Let $\mathbb B=\{0,1\}$ have addition OR and multiplication AND. The
amplitude and support observations are the unital semiring maps
\[
p_R:G(R)\longrightarrow R,\quad p_R(\tau)=0,\ p_R(r^\bullet)=r,
\qquad
\chi_R:G(R)\longrightarrow\mathbb B,\quad
\chi_R(\tau)=0,\ \chi_R(r^\bullet)=1.                 \tag{R.1}
\]
To verify additivity of $\chi_R$, two supported summands have a supported
sum even if their amplitudes cancel. Their OR is still one. The cases
with an absent input follow from the additive identity. Multiplicativity
is the corresponding support conjunction. The pair $(p_R,\chi_R)$ is
injective: support zero identifies $\tau$ uniquely, whereas support one
leaves the amplitude to identify $r^\bullet$ uniquely. Thus the zero
amplitude at supported zero retains an observable absent from the ordinary
ring quotient.

For a unital ring map $f:R\to S$, the map $G(f)$ fixes absence and sends
$r^\bullet$ to $f(r)^\bullet$. The operation rules prove it is a
unital semiring map, and $p_SG(f)=fp_R$ on both sorts of input. In
particular the actual scalar square is
\[
\begin{CD}
G(\mathbb Z) @>{G(\iota)}>> G(\mathbb C)\\
@V{p_{\mathbb Z}}VV @VV{p_{\mathbb C}}V\\
\mathbb Z @>{\iota}>> \mathbb C.
\end{CD}                                                   \tag{R.2}
\]
The lower-left arithmetic target is the full infinite ring $\mathbb Z$.
No finite characteristic or Boolean quotient replaces it in (R.2).

The original pointed-monoid blueprint base has elements $\{\tau,1\}$,
absorbing $\tau$, and the empty-sum relation $\varnothing\equiv\tau$.
It does not impose $1+1=1$. Its original structural map sends these
elements to absence and the unit in $G(R)$. This defines the chart's
structure over $\mathfrak b_\tau$. The support map in (R.1), whose
codomain does impose Boolean addition, is a further map, not that
structural map. The stated operations prove the two maps are compatible;
their different additive relations are not identified.

For a complex linear map $f:E\to F$ at an admitted support label
$\lambda$, its split lift sends $(\lambda,v)$ to $(\lambda,fv)$ and
external $\tau$ to external $\tau$. It is additive because $f$ is,
and commutes with supported scalar multiplication for the same reason.
If $fv=0$, its value is $(\lambda,0)$. This proves the support rule used
by every linear complex, quotient and kernel below. Between labels, the
same statement uses the explicitly specified linear transport; the PL
chapter calculates a transport that kills a nonzero relation class to
supported zero, without deleting its source label.

\section{The actual cohomological route of a hypothetical zero}

\[

\[
\mathcal T=(V,V,\mathscr B,0;\Theta,\Theta\mathcal F,0).
\]
The two-leg complex in degrees $0,1$ is
\[
\mathsf A_\tau(\mathcal T)=
[V\oplus V\xrightarrow{(\phi,\psi)\mapsto
\Theta(\phi-\widehat\psi)}\mathscr B].                 \tag{R.3}
\]
Its full $H^1$ is $Q$. The derived restriction comparison of the

\[
A_k^*G+GA_k-kG=-(R^*\mathscr B+\mathscr B^*R),
