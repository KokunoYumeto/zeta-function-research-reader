\section{The empty-packet endpoint of the exact source diagram}
\label{sec:EPE}
The original mixed coefficient attachment includes the empty packet
$h=1$, with $E_1=0$ and $\mathscr B_1=\Theta V$. Its section,
arithmetic-action and primitive maps on $E_1$ are the unique zero maps.
To include this endpoint in the named source functions of
(MCF4)--(MCF5) and (MCF26), define
\[
 F_1=\Theta\phi_*,\qquad 1(D)F_1=\Theta\phi_*,\qquad
 \mathcal MF_1=g.
 \tag{EPE1}
\]
These identities follow from the original theta seed equation; the
first is forced by the second because $1(D)$ is the identity.
For a nonempty full packet $H$, the defining equation
$H(D)F_H=\Theta\phi_*$ consequently gives
\[
 (H/1)(D)F_H=F_1.
 \tag{EPE2}
\]
For $H=1$ this is the identity on the same source function.
Every section, action and primitive compatibility with domain $E_1$
is the equality of the unique zero maps. Unit inverses and the
nonempty local jet and polynomial-remainder formulas are used only
at nonempty stages. Thus no unit inverse in the zero-vector-space
stage is needed. The source inclusion at this stage is exactly
$\Theta V\hookrightarrow\mathscr B_H$, not a new packet class.
This makes the original empty endpoint explicit without assuming
that the offcritical packet category has a nonempty member.

The historical MCF bytes remain preserved with the sealed edition
and source provenance. In the present edition, (EPE1)--(EPE2) are
also incorporated at the MCF, AG and CFA source definitions and
divisor identities themselves. Their endpoint convention was accepted
by the author of the coordinating source. Thus the current earlier
source diagrams already use the displayed function $F_1$ and the
distinct zero amplitude stage $E_1$.
