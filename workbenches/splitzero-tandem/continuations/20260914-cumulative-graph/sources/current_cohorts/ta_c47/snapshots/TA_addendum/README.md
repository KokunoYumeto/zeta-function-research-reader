# Actual theta sources and arithmetic metric extensions

This additive mathematical reader contains eight complete source bodies: current
Results R67–R74 and their final independent crosswalk; the full EW–AGT–AA proof
of the inherited constructions and the crossed-window refinement; the exact
original-metric compactness proof; TA1–24 and its full independent review; and
EM1–12 and its full independent review.

Read `Tau_Actual_Source_and_Metric_Addendum.pdf`. Edit `main.tex` and the complete
LaTeX bodies under `proofs/`. `sources/` retains the complete original source
text with machine locators changed to public source identifiers where needed.
`conversion/` retains full typed-source maps, ASTs and validation receipts.
The unchanged raw originals and exact inverses of private locator substitutions
are preserved in the accompanying local provenance workspace. Their hashes
are recorded here without publishing machine-specific paths.

The reader is an addition to the preserved cumulative programme. It does not
replace the complete 821-page predecessor, the frozen counterfactual source
reader, or the transcript audit. The successor inventory records their exact
source paths and which additional complete routes remain to assemble.

## Rebuild

With LuaLaTeX and the packages named in `main.tex` installed, run `python build.py`.
The script compiles the complete source twice and produces the named reader PDF.
No mathematical calculation or certificate is rerun by this typesetting build.

theta boundary maps, analytic domains and determinant signs remain attached to

and their stated continuing analytic work. The complete independent reviews
