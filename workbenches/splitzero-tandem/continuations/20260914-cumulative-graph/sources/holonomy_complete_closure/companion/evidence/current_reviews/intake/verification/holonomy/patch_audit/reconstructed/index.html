Result and scope

The original arithmetic source is recovered exactly by the complete family of twisted circle periodizations. The new calculation retains what happens when minimization over the original theta relations is performed in that family. There is a positive, explicitly calculated difference between the original quotient metric and the average of the fibre quotient metrics. It is the norm of a family of original relations, not an unspecified error.

If every phase-source Gram lies between (1−η)M(1-\eta)M and (1+η)M(1+\eta)M, where MM is their exact mean and 0≤η<10\leq\eta<1, the calculated comparison is

(1−η2)G≼G¯≼G,G−G¯=∫(C−Cθ)*Mθ(C−Cθ)dθ2π. (1-\eta^2)G\preceq \overline G\preceq G, \qquad G-\overline G =\int (C-C_\theta)^*M_\theta(C-C_\theta)\,\frac{d\theta}{2\pi}.
(H1)

Here CC and CθC_\theta are minimum-norm sections of the same original polynomial quotient. Every difference C−CθC-C_\theta has its actual theta primitive. The factor η2\eta^2 is sharp for the stated matrix hypotheses. This is an application of positive-matrix variational methods, not a priority claim for operator concavity.

The family is joined to finite cyclic covers, the new residue-constituent coefficient, and the original endpoint-volume criterion. All these constructions remain over the marked split base. No finite-field weight theorem is applied to a Hilbert circle quotient. No uniform arithmetic upper bound contradicting the inherited quartet threshold is proved in this note.
