# Finite-circle curvature and arithmetic control

The 19-page `Finite_Circle_Curvature_Control.pdf` contains four complete proof bodies:

| Pages | Proof body | Equation coverage |
|---|---|---|
| 2–4 | Exponential-period determinant | XD1–26 |
| 5–10 | Constituent period curvature | SC1–37, SC6a–b |
| 11–14 | Original Laplacian control | LC1–16, LC12a |
| 15–19 | Finite-circle curvature, control and commutator comparison | FC1–29, FC3a |

`Finite_Circle_Curvature_Control.tex` is the editable wrapper. `originals/` preserves
the four complete accepted bodies byte for byte. `proofs/` supplies their reader
editions: XD, SC and FC are byte-identical; LC1 has a reversible display line break,
recorded in `evidence/LC_LAYOUT.diff` and verified by its inverse.

`dependencies/` contains the complete supporting proof sources and retained review
and execution evidence. It includes the original periodized source, full PSA/PSD,
PC and RCX proofs, the complete original exponential, residue and SGA sources,
the corrected SGA edition, the Toda, arithmetic, tensor, boundary and metric
chapters, and all three public Connes proof notes. The dependency graph and exact
edition relationships are in `evidence/SOURCE_CLOSURE.md`; every dependency is
indexed in `dependencies/SOURCE_INDEX.json`. The latter distinguishes unchanged
mathematical sources from evidence copies whose machine locators were replaced.

The original coefficients, metrics, quotient maps, Fourier zero coordinate,
ordered operator products and theta boundaries are retained throughout. Common
calculations in the source and companion remain identified in the proof text.
Historical review and finite-check receipts retain their stated scope.

`evidence/current_reviews/` includes the complete final independent acceptance of
FC1–29 and FC3a, LC1–16 and LC12a, together with the separate parent commutator
proof review and its saved checker, execution receipts and substantive controls.
Its publication index retains the original and delivered hashes when machine
locators differ. This later acceptance supersedes the narrower earlier FC1–23
review scope recorded in the inherited dependency report.

All 19 pages were visually inspected. The final compilation has no warnings,
overflow, missing characters or unresolved references; all 112 explicit equation
tags occur in the PDF. The complete receipts are in `evidence/`. Artifact creation
did not rerun mathematical checkers or Lean.

To build with Python 3 and a LaTeX installation providing `pdflatex`:

```text
python build.py
```

The required TeX packages are named in the wrapper. `--compiler` selects the
compiler executable and `--build-dir` selects the auxiliary-output directory.
The build runs three LaTeX passes and writes a compilation receipt. A rebuild
may change PDF metadata bytes; the mathematical source pins are independent
of those generated bytes.

`MANIFEST.json` gives relative paths, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes for every
file in this delivered folder other than the manifest itself.
