\paragraph{The complete relation tail now supplies a fourth generator row in R65.}
Keep the original $T_t=A+tR-kI/2$, $t\in\mathbb C$, and all
primary coordinates. At fixed full $h,k$, (PGRT.6) gives the
actual positive form and its complete arithmetic coefficients
\[
\begin{gathered}
 T_N^{\rm rel}=\widehat G_N-\Omega_k=f_N^*f_N
   =\sum_{j=N}^{\infty}(a_j^{\rm rel})^{-1}
          (\alpha_j^{\rm rel})^*\alpha_j^{\rm rel},\\
 B_N^{\rm rel}=\Omega_k^{-1}T_N^{\rm rel}
       =\sum_i b_{N,i}P_{N,i},\qquad b_{N,i}\geq0,\\
 \mathsf S_N=\sum_i\sqrt{1+b_{N,i}}P_{N,i},\quad
 \mathsf S_N^{-1}=\sum_i(1+b_{N,i})^{-1/2}P_{N,i},\\
 \mathsf S_N^*\Omega_k\mathsf S_N=\widehat G_N.
\end{gathered}\tag{R65r1}
\]
The names $a_j^{\rm rel},d_j^{\rm rel},\alpha_j^{\rm rel}$
are exactly (ACM28), which supplies their full original moment
integrals and positive Gram construction. The form
$T_N^{\rm rel}$ retains this superscript and does not rename
the period generator $T_t$. The matrix $\Omega_k B_N^{\rm rel}$
is the positive Hermitian form $T_N^{\rm rel}$, proving
self-adjointness in the original $\Omega_k$ metric. Transport
of the complete eigenspaces of
$\Omega_k^{-1/2}T_N^{\rm rel}\Omega_k^{-1/2}$ proves the
displayed spectral decomposition, including repeated eigenspaces.
Multiplication of its projections proves the inverse and Gram
identities. Thus $\mathsf S_N:(E,\widehat G_N)\to(E,\Omega_k)$
is the specified isometry, with the displayed inverse and zero
kernel.

Put $X_G(t)=T_t+T_t^{\dagger_G}$ and
$\epsilon_G(t)=\|X_G(t)\|_G$, retaining both $t$ and $\bar t$.
The complete error on this fourth row is
\[
\begin{gathered}
 Z_N(t)=[\mathsf S_N,T_t]\mathsf S_N^{-1},\\
 \mathsf S_NX_{\widehat G_N}(t)\mathsf S_N^{-1}
      -X_{\Omega_k}(t)=Z_N(t)+Z_N(t)^{\dagger_{\Omega_k}},\\
 |\epsilon_{\widehat G_N}(t)-\epsilon_{\Omega_k}(t)|
 \leq\|Z_N(t)+Z_N(t)^{\dagger_{\Omega_k}}\|_{\Omega_k}
 \leq\frac{\|[B_N^{\rm rel},A]+t[B_N^{\rm rel},R]\|_{\Omega_k}}
                     {1+\min_i b_{N,i}}.
\end{gathered}\tag{R65r2}
\]
For the identity use
$T_t^{\dagger_{\widehat G_N}}
=\mathsf S_N^{-2}T_t^{\dagger_{\Omega_k}}\mathsf S_N^2$
and expand the two ordered terms. For the inequality, the
convergent integral
\[
 \int_0^\infty e^{-u\mathsf S_N}[B_N^{\rm rel},T_t]
                         e^{-u\mathsf S_N}\,du
   =[\mathsf S_N,T_t]
\]
has $(i,j)$ block
$\frac{b_{N,i}-b_{N,j}}{\sqrt{1+b_{N,i}}+\sqrt{1+b_{N,j}}}
P_{N,i}T_tP_{N,j}$, equal to the block on the right by
factoring the difference of squares. Its norm is bounded by
the commutator norm times
$\int_0^\infty e^{-2u\sqrt{1+\min b_{N,i}}}\,du
=1/(2\sqrt{1+\min b_{N,i}})$.
The inverse has norm $1/\sqrt{1+\min b_{N,i}}$.
Multiplication and addition of the adjoint give exactly the
factor in (R65r2). The first inequality is the reverse triangle
inequality under the specified isometry. This retains the
cancellation between the two original commutators.

For every fixed $r\geq0$ the finite-frame tail yields the
quantitative local uniform conclusion
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sup_{|t|\leq r}
 |\epsilon_{\widehat G_N}(t)-\epsilon_{\Omega_k}(t)|
 &\leq 2\|B_N^{\rm rel}\|_{\Omega_k}
                    (\|A\|_{\Omega_k}+r\|R\|_{\Omega_k})\\
 &\leq2(\|A\|_{\Omega_k}+r\|R\|_{\Omega_k})
 \sum_{j=N}^{\infty}
 \frac{\alpha_j^{\rm rel}\Omega_k^{-1}(\alpha_j^{\rm rel})^*}
      {a_j^{\rm rel}}\\
 &\longrightarrow0\qquad(N\to\infty;\ h,k,r\text{ fixed}).
\end{aligned}\tag{R65r3}
\]
Indeed $\|[B,A]\|\leq2\|B\|\|A\|$ and the analogous
inequality for $R$ prove the first line from (R65r2).
The largest eigenvalue of a positive matrix is at most its
trace. Here that trace is
$\operatorname{tr}(\Omega_k^{-1}T_N^{\rm rel})$, equal to the
displayed convergent series by (R65r1). It tends to zero by
the complete relation expansion, which proves the limit.
No statement of uniformity in $k$ is used.
