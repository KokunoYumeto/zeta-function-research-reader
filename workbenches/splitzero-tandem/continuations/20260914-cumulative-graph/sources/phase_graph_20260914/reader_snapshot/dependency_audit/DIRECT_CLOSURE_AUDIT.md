\[

\mathcal P_D\longrightarrow L^2(\mathbb R,dr;\mathcal K),

\[

\kappa_{a,D}=\sup_{v\ne0}
\frac{v^*(M_{a,+,D}+M_{a,-,D})v}{v^*M_Dv}.
\]

Since \(r=\log x_k\), these squared norms use exactly the weights \(x_k^{\pm2a}\) stated in HC14b. The identity coefficient map sends each HC polynomial to the identical polynomial in this H39 source; its domain and codomain are the same \(\mathcal P_D\) with the displayed original forms. No measure is divided by its mass.

For \(\mathcal C_D(s)=\int\Psi_D(r)^*\Psi_D(r+s)\,dr\), H40 is

\[
M_{D,\theta}=\sum_{b\in\mathbb Z}e^{ib\theta}\mathcal C_D(bL),

\]

Here the phase map is the exact HT5 map
\(\mathcal Z_{L,\theta}\psi(r)=\sum_j e^{ij\theta}\psi(r+jL)\), with target boundary condition \(\psi(r+L)=e^{-i\theta}\psi(r)\). For a coefficient vector \(v\), weighted Cauchy–Schwarz gives

\[

\le e^{-a|s|}

The positive and negative nonzero lattice indices are paired with their original conjugate phases. Summing their absolute bounds and using \(2\sqrt{AB}\le A+B\) gives

\[
