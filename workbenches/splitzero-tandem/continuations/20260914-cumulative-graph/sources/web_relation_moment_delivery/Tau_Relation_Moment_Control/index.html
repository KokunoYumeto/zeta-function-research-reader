1. Result and the current inherited theorem

This note continues the canonical endpoint-restriction construction. It does not choose a different positive metric or replace the arithmetic source by a comparison measure. Its new application is an upper bound for each original relation-block volume from two source-derived traces, together with a complete positive remainder and a source interpretation of the second trace as exterior area.

The general sharp determinant optimization with prescribed dimension, trace, and squared trace is classical; Grone, Johnson, Marques de Sá, and Wolkowicz (1984) study precisely that optimization. The proof below is included in full, using a scalar Hermite interpolation identity. No priority claim is made for the general inequality. The work here is its composition with the actual arithmetic representative, internal quotient, inverse restriction, exterior source maps, and consecutive-window theorem.

For one original block, the output is

ViVj≤(1+ai,j)(1+bi,j)q−1. \boxed{\frac{V_i}{V_j}\le(1+a_{i,j})(1+b_{i,j})^{q-1}.} \tag{1} (1)

Both ai,ja_{i,j} and bi,jb_{i,j} are calculated from the first two traces of a specified endomorphism. There is no assumption that the arithmetic action is normal, no assumption that a restriction eigenspace is invariant under it, and no independently supplied lower gap for the restriction.

5. A sharp two-trace upper bound, with a complete proof

Suppose q≥2q\ge2. Define

d=qt2−t12q−1,a=t1+(q−1)dq,b=t1−dq. d=\sqrt{\frac{qt_2-t_1^2}{q-1}},\qquad a=\frac{t_1+(q-1)d}{q},\qquad b=\frac{t_1-d}{q}. \tag{16} (16)

Both discriminants and signs come from the actual positive spectrum:

t12q≤t2≤t12,0≤b≤a, \frac{t_1^2}{q}\le t_2\le t_1^2, \qquad 0\le b\le a,

a+(q−1)b=t1,a2+(q−1)b2=t2. a+(q-1)b=t_1,\qquad a^2+(q-1)b^2=t_2. \tag{17} (17)

The spectral cap is proved, not supplied

For any eigenvalue xx of ZZ, Cauchy–Schwarz on the other q−1q-1 eigenvalues gives

t2≥x2+(t1−x)2q−1. t_2\ge x^2+\frac{(t_1-x)^2}{q-1}.

Solving this quadratic yields x≤ax\le a. Hence the same two traces also produce the actual restriction-gap bound

Qi,j≽11+aIEin the original metric Gi. \boxed{Q_{i,j}\succeq\frac1{1+a}I_E \quad\text{in the original metric }G_i.} \tag{18} (18)

This is a finite derived bound, not a uniform estimate in kk.

The optimal determinant bound from these two traces

The result is

logdet(I+Z)≤U2(Z):=log(1+a)+(q−1)log(1+b). \boxed{ \log\det(I+Z)\le U_2(Z):=\log(1+a)+(q-1)\log(1+b). } \tag{19} (19)

For a>ba>b, define the quadratic

Pa,b(x)=log(1+b)+x−b1+b+ca,b(x−b)2, P_{a,b}(x)=\log(1+b)+\frac{x-b}{1+b}+c_{a,b}(x-b)^2,

It matches log(1+x)\log(1+x) and its derivative at bb, and the value at aa. For 0≤x≤a0\le x\le a the exact remainder is

Pa,b(x)−log(1+x)=(a−x)(x−b)2∫1∞ds(s+x)(s+b)2(s+a)≥0. \boxed{ P_{a,b}(x)-\log(1+x) =(a-x)(x-b)^2 \int_1^\infty\frac{ds}{(s+x)(s+b)^2(s+a)}\ge0. } \tag{21} (21)

Proof of (21): use log(1+x)=∫1∞(1/s−1/(s+x))ds\log(1+x)=\int_1^\infty(1/s-1/(s+x))\,ds and the exact third divided difference of 1/(s+x)1/(s+x). Interpolation at the repeated point bb and at aa gives the displayed rational denominator. The integral converges as s−4s^{-4}; all factors have their shown positive signs. This also directly proves the Hermite remainder without an unspecified intermediate point.

Sum (21) over the actual eigenvalues. Since a quadratic trace depends only on q,t1,t2q,t_1,t_2, equations (17) give

TrPa,b(Z)=Pa,b(a)+(q−1)Pa,b(b)=U2(Z). \operatorname{Tr}P_{a,b}(Z)=P_{a,b}(a)+(q-1)P_{a,b}(b)=U_2(Z).

This proves (19) and (1).

If a=ba=b, equality in qt2≥t12qt_2\ge t_1^2 forces every eigenvalue to equal bb, so (19) is equality without the quotient in (20). If q=1q=1, the exact answer is log(1+t1)\log(1+t_1). If t1=0t_1=0, positivity gives Z=0Z=0 and the volume is zero. If q=0q=0, use the empty determinant, without forming (16).

For a>ba>b, the integrand in (21) is strictly positive except at x=a,bx=a,b. Equality in (19) therefore holds exactly when the spectrum consists of one aa and q−1q-1 copies of bb. These values have the original two traces by (17), so the bound is best possible given only those traces and the dimension. This does not assert that every such extremizer arises from an arithmetic packet.

The general optimization is classical. The source application uses precisely Z=(Kj−Ki)GiZ=(K_j-K_i)G_i, not an independently chosen Hermitian matrix.

7. The simplest bound exposes independent pair growth

For q≥2q\ge2 and t1>0t_1>0, (17) implies

𝒜2=(q−1)b(t1−q2b). \mathcal A_2=(q-1)b\left(t_1-\frac q2b\right).

𝒜2(q−1)t1≤b≤2𝒜2(q−1)t1,a≤t1. \frac{\mathcal A_2}{(q-1)t_1}\le b \le\frac{2\mathcal A_2}{(q-1)t_1},\qquad a\le t_1.

ViVj≤(1+t1)(1+2𝒜2(q−1)t1)q−1. \boxed{ \frac{V_i}{V_j} \le(1+t_1) \left(1+\frac{2\mathcal A_2}{(q-1)t_1}\right)^{q-1}. } \tag{25} (25)

The quantity 𝒜2\mathcal A_2 is exactly the sum of the original two-column exterior areas (13), including every cross-pairing. The first trace alone cannot distinguish nearly parallel old representative corrections from many independent ones; (13) does make that distinction quantitatively.

This is a statement about the rank of this specified comparison map, not a claim about uniqueness of the original scalar support or about the number of higher support fibres.

9. A finite certificate robust to moment enclosures
