\section{The exact joint-Schur dictionary and its signed arithmetic return}

Use the original quartet, $k=4l+1$ with $l\geq1$, and
$q=(k+1)^2[1+k(m-1)]$ throughout. In this section superscript
${\rm n}$ marks the remainder-coordinate matrices in the incoming
joint-Schur note, and superscript ${\rm j}$ marks GCR21--27's
divided-jet matrices. It does not change their underlying source.
Let $E$ be the coefficient-to-divided-Hermite-jet matrix for $\chi$,
and put
\[
 F=\operatorname{diag}_{(\lambda,r)}(r!),\qquad
 E^{\rm raw}=FE,\qquad
 U_f^{\rm raw}=FU_fF^{-1},\qquad
 M_f=E^{-1}U_fE=(E^{\rm raw})^{-1}U_f^{\rm raw}E^{\rm raw}.
 \tag{JOD1}
\]
Indeed the $r$th divided derivative is $P^{(r)}(\lambda)/r!$.
The $(r,s)$ entry of $U_f$ is $f^{(r-s)}(\lambda)/(r-s)!$
for $r\geq s$. Conjugation by $F$ makes it
$\binom rs f^{(r-s)}(\lambda)$, exactly the raw Leibniz matrix
in the note. Thus every primary nilpotent direction and every
derivative factorial is preserved by an explicitly invertible map.
In particular $\det M_f=\det U_f=\prod_\lambda f(\lambda)^{e_k}$.

Fix $N\geq q-1$ and $M=N+l$. The note's joint covariance in
the original remainder coordinates is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}K_M^{\rm n}&C_M^{\rm n}\\
 (C_M^{\rm n})^*&A_M^{\rm n}\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 G_M^{\rm n}=(K_M^{\rm n})^{-1},\qquad
 S_M^{\rm n}=K_M^{\rm n}-C_M^{\rm n}(A_M^{\rm n})^{-1}(C_M^{\rm n})^*.
 \tag{JOD2}
\]
Application of $\operatorname{diag}(E,I_l)$ to the observation
map gives the exact covariance congruence
\[
 K_{\chi,M}^{\rm j}=EK_M^{\rm n}E^*,\qquad
 K_{\chi,f;M}^{\rm j}=EC_M^{\rm n},\qquad
 K_{f,M}^{\rm j}=A_M^{\rm n},\qquad
 C_N^{\rm GCR}=ES_M^{\rm n}E^*.
 \tag{JOD3}
\]
Replacing $E$ by $FE$ gives the corresponding raw-jet
congruence. The additional nodes are simple, so their block has
no derivative factorial. The inverse congruence is
$(EK_NE^*)^{-1}=E^{-*}G_NE^{-1}$, in the displayed order.

Write $U_N^{\rm n}=(Jp_{N+1},\ldots,Jp_{N+l})$ and retain
$\Omega_N=\operatorname{diag}(\gamma_{N+1},\ldots,\gamma_{N+l})$.
Then GCR21 is exactly
\[
 U_N^{\rm j}=EU_N^{\rm n}\Omega_N^{-1/2},\qquad
 V_N^{\rm j}=EC_M^{\rm n}(A_M^{\rm n})^{-1/2},\qquad
 K_M^{\rm n}=K_N^{\rm n}
       +U_N^{\rm n}\Omega_N^{-1}(U_N^{\rm n})^*.
 \tag{JOD4}
\]
These equations follow term by term from the finite orthogonal
polynomial kernel sum, with its original monic norms retained.
For the note's two metrized endomorphisms set
\[
 X_N=\Omega_N^{-1}(U_N^{\rm n})^*G_N^{\rm n}U_N^{\rm n},\qquad
 Y_N=(A_M^{\rm n})^{-1}(C_M^{\rm n})^*G_M^{\rm n}C_M^{\rm n}.
\]
The positive Hermitian factors of GCR24 become
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A_N^{\rm GCR}
   &=\Omega_N^{1/2}(I_l+X_N)\Omega_N^{-1/2},\\
 B_N^{\rm GCR}
   &=(A_M^{\rm n})^{1/2}(I_l-Y_N)(A_M^{\rm n})^{-1/2}.
\end{aligned}\tag{JOD5}
\]
The first identity follows by inserting JOD3--4 into
$I+(U_N^{\rm j})^*(K_N^{\rm j})^{-1}U_N^{\rm j}$.
The second follows by inserting them into
$I-(V_N^{\rm j})^*(K_M^{\rm j})^{-1}V_N^{\rm j}$.
The full mixed part is retained: multiplication verifies
\[
 G_M^{\rm n}=G_N^{\rm n}-G_N^{\rm n}U_N^{\rm n}
 [\Omega_N+(U_N^{\rm n})^*G_N^{\rm n}U_N^{\rm n}]^{-1}
 (U_N^{\rm n})^*G_N^{\rm n}.
\]
Substitution in JOD5 yields precisely the positive mixed term
in GCR24. Neither $X_N$ nor $Y_N$ is silently treated as
Hermitian for the Euclidean metric: their exact metric maps
are respectively $\Omega_N^{1/2}$ and $(A_M^{\rm n})^{1/2}$.

To avoid the note's reused scalar $d_N$, call the positive
GCR23 determinant $\mathfrak d_N$. The exact identities are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak d_N
 &=\det(I_{2l}+J_lW_N^*(K_N^{\rm j})^{-1}W_N)\\
 &=\det(I_l+X_N)\det(I_l-Y_N)>0,\\
 \log\mathfrak d_N&=d_N^{\rm note}-\beta_N^{\rm note}
  =2\log|\det U_f|-\log D_{l,N}+\log R_N^{\rm GSR}.
\end{aligned}\tag{JOD6}
\]
The first two equalities are GCR23--24 and JOD5. The last is
GSR20; the note's $d_N^{\rm note}=\log\det(I+X_N)$ and
$\beta_N^{\rm note}=-\log\det(I-Y_N)$ give the middle equality.
The note's comparison mass is exactly the original one:
$C_k=c_\sigma^k/(4^lc_{k/4})
=(2\pi)^{k/2}/(\sqrt2\Gamma(k/2))=\beta_k^{\rm GCR}$.
Thus all three source and quotient presentations have identical
mass factors as well as the typed maps above.

Let $\epsilon=(1,1,-1,-1)$ at $(q-1,q,2q-1,2q)$. The exact
signed return is
\[
 \mathcal C_k^{\Gamma}:=\mathcal R_k^0-\mathcal R_k^\sigma
 =\sum_{a=0}^3\epsilon_a\log\mathfrak d_{N_a}
 =-\mathfrak T_k,\qquad
 \Delta_k^\Gamma=\mathcal C_k^\Gamma+\delta_k^\sigma.
 \tag{JOD7}
\]
This is the note's signed formula and GCR27 with identical
signs. The degree-independent full-unit term in JOD6 cancels
only after the four displayed signs have been inserted.

For the note's actual common $s=2+\sum_a\operatorname{Tr}X_{N_a}$
put $Z_N=I-(I+X_N)/s$. In its stated positive metric each
eigenvalue of $Z_N$ lies in $[0,1)$: the largest eigenvalue
of $X_N$ is at most its nonnegative trace, which is less than
$s-1$. The joint positive Schur complement similarly gives
$0\preceq Y_N\prec I$ in its stated metric. Put
$Q_N=-\log\det(I-Z_N)-\log\det(I-Y_N)$, so that
$\log\mathfrak d_N=l\log s-Q_N$. The note's finite trace
lower and upper sums $L_a,U_a$ therefore enclose $Q_{N_a}$.
Explicitly for a positive metrized endomorphism $T$ with
spectrum in $[0,1)$, let $b_p=\sum_{n=1}^p\operatorname{Tr}(T^n)/n$
and $a_p=\operatorname{Tr}(T^p)$. For a single eigenvalue $x$,
$r_{2p}(x)\leq x^pr_p(x)$, where
$r_p(x)=\sum_{n>p}x^n/n$. Summing gives
$r_{2p}(T)\leq a_pr_p(T)$. Since
$r_p(T)=b_{2p}-b_p+r_{2p}(T)$, any $a_p<1$ gives
$b_p\leq-\log\det(I-T)\leq b_p+(b_{2p}-b_p)/(1-a_p)$.
Such $p$ exists because the dimension is finite and every
eigenvalue is strictly below one; the upper remainder tends
to zero as $p\to\infty$. This applies separately in each
of the two metrics proved in JOD5.
It follows, with no commutation between blocks, that
\[
 L_2+L_3-U_0-U_1\leq\mathcal C_k^\Gamma
                 \leq U_2+U_3-L_0-L_1.
 \tag{JOD8}
\]

The accepted exact low endpoints of GSR3--6 yield a stronger
finite version of this same certificate. Define
\[
 A_k^{\rm low}=4\log|\det U_f|
    -\log D_{l,q-1}-\log D_{l,q}+\log\mathfrak r_\chi,
 \quad
 \mathfrak r_\chi=\frac{\int|f(c+iy)\chi(c+iy)|^2d\sigma(y)}
                       {\int|\chi(c+iy)|^2d\sigma(y)}.
 \tag{JOD9}
\]
Here $R_{q-1}^{\rm GSR}=1$ and $R_q^{\rm GSR}=\mathfrak r_\chi$;
hence JOD6 gives
$\log\mathfrak d_{q-1}+\log\mathfrak d_q=A_k^{\rm low}$.
Keeping that exact value in JOD7 gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \mathcal C_k^\Gamma=A_k^{\rm low}-2l\log s+Q_{N_2}+Q_{N_3},\\
 L_k^\sharp:=A_k^{\rm low}-2l\log s+L_2+L_3
 \leq\mathcal C_k^\Gamma
 \leq A_k^{\rm low}-2l\log s+U_2+U_3=:U_k^\sharp.
\end{gathered}\tag{JOD10}
\]
This interval is contained in JOD8 because
$Q_{N_0}+Q_{N_1}=2l\log s-A_k^{\rm low}$ lies between
$L_0+L_1$ and $U_0+U_1$. Its width is exactly
$(U_2-L_2)+(U_3-L_3)$, retaining only the two high trace
remainders. All original full-unit and low-relation factors
remain in JOD9; neither low endpoint is assigned growing rank.

For the actual asymmetric arithmetic return retain the accepted
original-degree quantities $D_k^-,D_k^+$ and TWA15a's evaluated
endpoints
\[
 \underline\delta_k^{\rm eval}=\max\{-qE_k^+,-D_k^-\},\qquad
 \overline\delta_k^{\rm eval}=\min\{qE_k^-,D_k^+\}.
\]
For either the note's JOD8 interval
or the sharper JOD10 interval $[L_\Gamma,U_\Gamma]$, the exact
original arithmetic, Gamma and HC residual enclosures are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal B_k^0+L_\Gamma+\underline\delta_k^{\rm eval}
 &\leq\mathcal B_k^{\rm ar}
 \leq\mathcal B_k^0+U_\Gamma+\overline\delta_k^{\rm eval},\\
 L_\Gamma+\underline\delta_k^{\rm eval}&\leq\Delta_k^\Gamma
                         \leq U_\Gamma+\overline\delta_k^{\rm eval},\\
 \max\{0,\mathcal B_k^0+L_\Gamma+\underline\delta_k^{\rm eval}
                         -4q\log(D_hk)\}
 &\leq\mathcal R_k
 \leq\mathcal B_k^0+U_\Gamma+\overline\delta_k^{\rm eval}-4q\log(D_hk).
\end{aligned}\tag{JOD11}
\]
Addition of the intervals in JOD7 proves the first two lines;
the original HC identity
$\mathcal R_k=\mathcal B_k^{\rm ar}-4q\log(D_hk)\geq0$
proves the third. In particular the explicit upper slack is
$\mathcal B_k^0+U_\Gamma+\overline\delta_k^{\rm eval}-4q\log(D_hk)$.
For $m\geq2$, the arithmetic allowance satisfies the proved
signed bounds $\liminf\delta_k^\sigma/(q\log k)\geq-(64m+245)$
and $\limsup\delta_k^\sigma/(q\log k)\leq128m+490$.
For $m=1$ the existing $5/7$ rate on the $kq$ scale remains.
The trace certificate is finite for every actual fixed input;
these statements do not assign it a uniform growing-family
rate or erase $\mathcal C_k^\Gamma$ from any return.
