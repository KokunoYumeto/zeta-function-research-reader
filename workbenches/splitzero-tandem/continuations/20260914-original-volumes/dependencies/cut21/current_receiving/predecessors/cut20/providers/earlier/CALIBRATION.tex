\subsection{Rigorous logarithm evaluation}

so that $r=2^e s$ and $1\le s<2$. Bound $s$ outward by consecutive
dyadic rationals of denominator $2^{128}$. For either endpoint use
$t=(s-1)/(s+1)$, so $0\le t\le1/3$. The first $96$ terms of
\[
2\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{t^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
\]
give the lower logarithm bound. The remaining positive tail is at most
\[
\frac{2t^{193}}{193(1-t^2)},
\]

uses exact rational arithmetic. This gives the saved finite bounds
