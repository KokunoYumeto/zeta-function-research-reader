\section{Exact source determinant deficit on restrictions and quotient metrics}

All coefficient spaces in this calculation are complex finite-dimensional
spaces, and a star denotes conjugate transpose in the stated coefficient
frames. A positive matrix is Hermitian positive definite. Determinants on
the zero-dimensional space are one, and its inverse is the unique empty
matrix. These conventions include zero boundary rank and zero kernel rank.
The maps below preserve the original coefficient vectors; their displayed
isometries calculate the comparison of two forms on those same vectors.

\subsection{The minimum section and the exact determinant factorization}

Let $D$ be a positive $n$ by $n$ matrix and let $A>0$ satisfy
$D\preceq A1_n$. Let
$L:\mathbb C^n\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb C^r$ be a fixed surjective
matrix, with $0\le r\le n$. Set
\[
 Q_D=(LD^{-1}L^*)^{-1},\qquad Q_1=(LL^*)^{-1},\qquad
 S_D=D^{-1}L^*Q_D,\qquad \mathsf E=A^{-1}D .
 \tag{QDC1}
\]
For every nonzero $z\in\mathbb C^r$, $L^*z\ne0$, whence
$z^*LD^{-1}L^*z>0$. Thus the displayed inverses exist.
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 LS_D=1_r,\qquad S_D^*DS_D=Q_D,\qquad
 z^*DS_D=0\quad(z\in\ker L).
 \tag{QDC2}
\]
Every lift of $y\in\mathbb C^r$ is uniquely of the form $S_Dy+z$
with $z\in\ker L$, and its exact norm is
\[
 (S_Dy+z)^*D(S_Dy+z)=y^*Q_Dy+z^*Dz.
 \tag{QDC3}
\]
Consequently $S_D$ is the unique minimum section of $L$ for the
form $D$, with the original target coefficient frame retained.

Define the Euclidean isometry and choose its orthogonal complement
by
\[
 W=L^*Q_1^{1/2}:\mathbb C^r\longrightarrow\mathbb C^n,\qquad
 K:\mathbb C^{n-r}\longrightarrow\ker L,\qquad
 W^*W=1_r,\quad K^*K=1_{n-r},\quad K^*W=0.
 \tag{QDC4}
\]
Such a $K$ is obtained by choosing any orthonormal basis of $\ker L$.
The matrix $[K,W]$ is unitary: its orthogonal summands have dimensions
$n-r$ and $r$. Also
$L=Q_1^{-1/2}W^*$ and $LW=Q_1^{-1/2}$.
In this full source frame put
\[
 F=K^*\mathsf EK,\qquad B=K^*\mathsf EW,\qquad
 C=W^*\mathsf EW,\qquad T=C-B^*F^{-1}B.
 \tag{QDC5}
\]
Positivity of $\mathsf E$ gives positivity of $F$. For $y\ne0$,
the vector $W y-KF^{-1}By$ is nonzero since its $W$ component is
$y$. Its $\mathsf E$ norm squared is $y^*Ty$, proving $T>0$.
The block triangular matrix
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}1_{n-r}&-F^{-1}B\\0&1_r\end{pmatrix}
\]
has determinant one. Congruence by this matrix transforms
$[K,W]^*\mathsf E[K,W]$ into $\operatorname{diag}(F,T)$.
This proves the exact determinant identity
\[
 \det\mathsf E=\det F\det T .
 \tag{QDC6}
\]
The lower right block of the inverse of the same block matrix is
$T^{-1}$, as follows either by multiplying the two triangular
factors and the diagonal inverse, or by solving
$Fz+By=0$ and $B^*z+Cy=v$. Thus
\[
 T=(W^*\mathsf E^{-1}W)^{-1},\qquad
 Q_D=AQ_1^{1/2}TQ_1^{1/2},\qquad
 S_DQ_1^{-1/2}=W-KF^{-1}B .
 \tag{QDC7}
\]
For the section equality the right side has the required $L$ image
$Q_1^{-1/2}$, and its $\mathsf E$ inner product with every vector
in $\ker L$ is zero by $F(-F^{-1}B)+B=0$. The uniqueness proved
in QDC3 gives the equality; scaling a source form by $A$ does not
alter its minimum section.

The inequality $0<\mathsf E\preceq1_n$ implies
$0<F\preceq1_{n-r}$ and $0<C\preceq1_r$. The matrix
$B^*F^{-1}B$ is positive semidefinite, so
$0<T\preceq C\preceq1_r$. All eigenvalues of $F$ and of $T$
therefore belong to $(0,1]$. Their determinants, being the products
of these eigenvalues, belong to $(0,1]$. QDC6--7 prove
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \det\mathsf E
 \ \le\ \frac{\det Q_D}{A^r\det Q_1}
 \ =\ \det T\ \le\ 1 .}
 \tag{QDC8}
\]
There is no assertion here that a lower bound on a full source
condition number persists after the quotient. QDC8 follows from
the complete determinant and the literal minimum-section map.

The full determinant deficit is the nonnegative scalar
\[
 d(D,A)=n\log A-\log\det D=-\log\det\mathsf E.
 \tag{QDC9}
\]
Its exact decomposition, including the retained kernel, is
\[
 \begin{split}
 d(D,A)&=d_{\ker L}+d_{\mathrm{quot}},\\
 d_{\ker L}&=-\log\det F\ge0,\\
 d_{\mathrm{quot}}
 &=r\log A+\log\det Q_1-\log\det Q_D
   =-\log\det T\ge0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{QDC10}
\]
In particular $d_{\mathrm{quot}}\le d(D,A)$.
For $r=0$, $F=\mathsf E$ in a unitary frame and $T$ is empty.
For $r=n$, $F$ is empty and $T$ is a unitary conjugate of
$\mathsf E$. The preceding formulas remain literal in both cases.

\subsection{Restrictions and original source frames}

Let $Y:\mathbb C^s\hookrightarrow\mathbb C^n$ be any fixed injective
matrix. Its Euclidean Gram $Y^*Y$ is positive. Define
$V=Y(Y^*Y)^{-1/2}$, so that $V^*V=1_s$. Completing $V$ to a
unitary matrix $[V,Z]$ and repeating the preceding triangular
calculation, now with $V^*\mathsf EV$ as the first block, gives
\[
 \det\mathsf E=\det(V^*\mathsf EV)\det
 \left(Z^*\mathsf EZ
       -Z^*\mathsf EV(V^*\mathsf EV)^{-1}V^*\mathsf EZ\right).
 \tag{QDC11}
\]
The second factor is the determinant of a positive contraction:
its positivity is the squared norm of the corresponding
orthogonal minimum vector, and it is at most $Z^*\mathsf EZ\preceq1$.
The first factor is also the determinant of a positive contraction.
Therefore
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \det\mathsf E\le
 \frac{\det(Y^*DY)}{A^s\det(Y^*Y)}
 =\det(V^*\mathsf EV)\le1 .}
 \tag{QDC12}
\]
This proves the restriction statement for its original domain
frame, including $s=0$ and $s=n$.

For use with the original polynomial source, let $H_0,H$ be
positive matrices on the same original coefficient space
$\mathcal V=\mathbb C^n$, with $H\preceq AH_0$. Retain a fixed
onto matrix $J:\mathcal V\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal E$ and set
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 D=H_0^{-1/2}HH_0^{-1/2},\qquad L=JH_0^{-1/2},\\
 G_0=(JH_0^{-1}J^*)^{-1},\quad
 G=(JH^{-1}J^*)^{-1},\quad
 R_H=H^{-1}J^*G.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{QDC13}
\]
The coordinate map $b=H_0^{1/2}a$ preserves both original
forms exactly:
\[
 a^*H_0a=b^*b,\qquad a^*Ha=b^*Db,\qquad Ja=Lb.
 \tag{QDC14}
\]
Thus $Q_D=G$, $Q_1=G_0$, and $R_H=H_0^{-1/2}S_D$.
The maps in QDC13--14 prove the claimed correspondence between
the original source and QDC1; no source vector, relation or
target coefficient is discarded. In these original frames,
QDC8 becomes
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 d(H,H_0;A)=n\log A-\log\frac{\det H}{\det H_0}\ge0,\\
 0\le
 r\log A-\log\frac{\det G}{\det G_0}
 \le d(H,H_0;A).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{QDC15}
\]
Here $r=\dim\mathcal E$. For an original injection
$Y_0:\mathbb C^s\hookrightarrow\mathcal V$, the map in QDC12
is $Y=H_0^{1/2}Y_0$. It proves
\[
 0\le s\log A
       -\log\frac{\det(Y_0^*HY_0)}{\det(Y_0^*H_0Y_0)}
 \le d(H,H_0;A).
 \tag{QDC16}
\]
If $Y_0$ spans exactly $\ker J$, its restriction deficit and
the quotient deficit in QDC15 sum exactly to $d(H,H_0;A)$.
Indeed $H_0^{1/2}Y_0(Y_0^*H_0Y_0)^{-1/2}$ is an orthonormal
basis of $\ker L$; substituting this $K$ into QDC10 proves
the statement with every original determinant retained.

\subsection{The four original polynomial quotients}

Retain the original packet, its integer multiplicity $m\ge1$,
and its parameters $\delta,\gamma$ from the full quartet.
Write
\[
 \ell_k=1+k(m-1),\quad q_k=(k+1)^2\ell_k,\quad c_k=k/2,\quad
 \chi_k(S)=\prod_{a,b=0}^k
 \bigl(S-c_k-(2a-k)\delta-i(2b-k)\gamma\bigr)^{\ell_k}.
 \tag{QDC17}
\]
The coefficient space is
$\mathcal E_k=\mathbb C[S]/(\chi_k)$, in its increasing
remainder frame of degrees zero through $q_k-1$.
The source $\mathcal P_N$ retains its increasing power frame
$1,S,\ldots,S^N$ and its literal value $S=c_k+iy$.
For $N\ge q_k-1$ its onto map is the unchanged monic remainder
\[
 J_{k,N}:\mathcal P_N\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal E_k,\qquad
 J_{k,N}P=[P],\qquad
 \ker J_{k,N}=\chi_k\mathcal P_{N-q_k}.
 \tag{QDC18}
\]
The last space is zero when $N=q_k-1$. The full-unit inclusion
$[P]\mapsto U^{\otimes k}[P(s_1+\cdots+s_k)]$ remains the
original subsequent map; QDC18 makes no replacement of $U$,
of its jets, or of the tensor nilpotents.

Let $w_h(y)=|v_h(1/2+iy)|^2/(2\pi)$ be the original
arithmetic source density, with the entire quotient $v_h=g/h$
and $g=2\xi$ from that packet. Put $\alpha=\pi/2$ and
$M_\alpha=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{\alpha y}w_h(y)\,dy>0$.
The two forms used in this calculation are exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 (H_{k,N})_{ab}
 &=\int_{\mathbb R}\overline{(c_k+iy)}^{\,a}(c_k+iy)^b
                    w_h^{*k}(y)\,dy,\\
 (H_{0,k,N})_{ab}
 &=\int_{\mathbb R}\overline{(c_k+iy)}^{\,a}(c_k+iy)^b
       \frac{|\Gamma(1/4+iy/2)|^2}{2\pi}\,dy ,
 \qquad 0\le a,b\le N .
 \end{split}
 \tag{QDC19}
\]
Thus $H_{0,k,N}=H_N^{\sigma,k}$ is the fixed one-factor Gamma
measure evaluated on the same shifted powers. Its total mass
is $\sqrt{2\pi}$. It is not the $k$-fold Gamma convolution.
The total mass of the first measure is the original $\mu_h^k$.
All moments and the strict positivity of these forms are
those of the original source calculation.

The full source upper comparison has the same scalar at every
degree:
\[
 H_{k,N}\preceq A_kH_{0,k,N},\qquad
 A_k=\frac{C_h}{c_\Gamma}\,k^{p+1}M_\alpha^{\,k-1},
 \quad p=8m-\frac92,\quad c_\Gamma=\sqrt2\,e^{-7/6}.
 \tag{QDC20}
\]
Here $C_h$ is the retained original tilt constant; none of these
constants are absorbed into the measures or coefficient frames.
For these matrices let $D_{k,N}$ be QDC13's exact relative
operator and let
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_{k,N}&=(J_{k,N}H_{k,N}^{-1}J_{k,N}^*)^{-1},\\
 G^\sigma_{k,N}
   &=(J_{k,N}H_{0,k,N}^{-1}J_{k,N}^*)^{-1},\\
 d_{k,N}&=(N+1)\log A_k-\log\det D_{k,N},\\
 b_{k,N}&=q_k\log A_k
                -\log\frac{\det G_{k,N}}{\det G^\sigma_{k,N}}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{QDC21}
\]
The exact map $L_{k,N}=J_{k,N}H_{0,k,N}^{-1/2}$ in
QDC13 proves, separately at each degree and without any
degree-dependent choice of quotient,
\[
 0\le b_{k,N}\le d_{k,N}.
 \tag{QDC22}
\]

For the four specified endpoints, retain their signs and
their source dimensions:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 (N_0,N_1,N_2,N_3)=(q_k-1,q_k,2q_k-1,2q_k),\\
 (\varepsilon_0,\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\varepsilon_3)
      =(1,1,-1,-1),\\
 (N_0+1,N_1+1,N_2+1,N_3+1)
      =(q_k,q_k+1,2q_k,2q_k+1).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{QDC23}
\]
Define their original quotient determinant sums
$F_{\rm ar,k}=\sum_i\varepsilon_i\log\det G_{k,N_i}$
and
$F_{\sigma,k}=\sum_i\varepsilon_i\log\det G^\sigma_{k,N_i}$.
Substituting the exact QDC21 equality, not replacing any
of its four deficits by a common unknown error, gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 F_{\rm ar,k}-F_{\sigma,k}
 &=-b_{k,q_k-1}-b_{k,q_k}
                  +b_{k,2q_k-1}+b_{k,2q_k},\\
 -d_{k,q_k-1}-d_{k,q_k}
 &\le F_{\rm ar,k}-F_{\sigma,k}
 \le d_{k,2q_k-1}+d_{k,2q_k}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{QDC24}
\]
The scalar $q_k\log A_k$ cancels because every quotient
has the same dimension $q_k$ and the stated signs sum to zero.
The four source dimensions differ and were retained in
$d_{k,N}$; their scalar factors were not canceled incorrectly.

Here is the exact passage from a source determinant rate to
this signed quantity. Define the literal source-rate remainder
and the literal upper-rate remainder by
\[
 e_{k,N}=\frac{\log\det D_{k,N}}{k(N+1)}-\log M_\alpha,
 \qquad
 \epsilon_k^A=\frac{(p+1)\log k+\log(C_h/c_\Gamma)-\log M_\alpha}{k}.
 \tag{QDC25}
\]
These are definitions, and their relation to the finite
deficit is the identity
\[
 \frac{d_{k,N}}{kq_k}
   =\frac{N+1}{q_k}(\epsilon_k^A-e_{k,N})\ge0.
 \tag{QDC26}
\]
In particular $\epsilon_k^A\to0$. The source determinant asymptotic proved in DMR26,
$\log\det D_{k,N_i}=(N_i+1)k\log M_\alpha+o(k(N_i+1))$
is precisely $e_{k,N_i}\to0$ at each of these four endpoints.
Its use in QDC26 is exact: the dimension multipliers in
QDC23 are at most $2+1/q_k$, so all four
$d_{k,N_i}/(kq_k)$ tend to zero. Substituting this result
into the two sides of QDC24 proves the requested propagation
\[
 \frac{F_{\rm ar,k}-F_{\sigma,k}}{kq_k}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{QDC27}
\]
The finite inequality QDC24 is independent of any asymptotic.
QDC25--27 specify exactly how the full determinant estimate,
proved in the source determinant calculation, enters the
quotient calculation. This independent calculation does not
replace that estimate by an assumption about quotient
eigenvalues, a fixed-degree limit, or four diagonal entries.

\subsection{The actual boundary and its retained kernel}

Fix the original onto observation
$\Lambda_k:\mathcal E_k\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal B_k$,
with its actual image as codomain, and its fixed kernel
inclusion $I_k:\mathcal K_k\hookrightarrow\mathcal E_k$.
This includes the period and monodromy observation with the
entire $U^{\otimes k}$ factor already present. Put
$r_k=\dim\mathcal B_k$ and $t_k=q_k-r_k$.
For either of the two quotient Grams $G$ in QDC21 define
\[
 H^K(G)=I_k^*GI_k,\quad
 Q^B(G)=(\Lambda_kG^{-1}\Lambda_k^*)^{-1},\quad
 S^B(G)=G^{-1}\Lambda_k^*Q^B(G).
 \tag{QDC28}
\]
The exact section identities QDC2--3 apply to $\Lambda_k$:
its section is $G$-orthogonal to $I_k$, and every vector
of $\mathcal E_k$ decomposes uniquely into a kernel vector
and this section's lift. In particular
$[I_k,S^B(G)]^*G[I_k,S^B(G)]
 =\operatorname{diag}(H^K(G),Q^B(G))$.

Choose once a coefficient right inverse $S_*$ of $\Lambda_k$,
independent of $N$ and of which source form is used.
The difference $S^B(G)-S_*$ has image in $\mathcal K_k$,
so is uniquely $I_kT_G$ for a matrix $T_G$. Therefore
\[
 \det[I_k,S^B(G)]=\det[I_k,S_*],\qquad
 \det H^K(G)\det Q^B(G)
        =|\det[I_k,S_*]|^2\det G.
 \tag{QDC29}
\]
This proves the exact frame cancellation in a ratio of the
two sources as well as in the four endpoint sum.

Minimizing $H_{k,N}\preceq A_kH_{0,k,N}$ on each identical
fibre of $J_{k,N}$ proves
$G_{k,N}\preceq A_kG^\sigma_{k,N}$ directly from QDC3.
Use this pair of forms in QDC15--16 with the fixed
$\Lambda_k$ and $I_k$. Define
\[
 \begin{split}
 b^K_{k,N}
 &=t_k\log A_k-
      \log\frac{\det H^K(G_{k,N})}
                    {\det H^K(G^\sigma_{k,N})},\\
 b^B_{k,N}
 &=r_k\log A_k-
      \log\frac{\det Q^B(G_{k,N})}
                    {\det Q^B(G^\sigma_{k,N})}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{QDC30}
\]
Both are nonnegative by the exact restriction and quotient
inequalities. QDC29 proves their exact sum, and QDC22 bounds it:
\[
 b^K_{k,N}+b^B_{k,N}=b_{k,N}\le d_{k,N}.
 \tag{QDC31}
\]
For either superscript $X=K$ or $X=B$, its arithmetic-minus-
reference four-endpoint determinant sum is consequently
\[
 \Delta F^X_k
   =-b^X_{k,q_k-1}-b^X_{k,q_k}
                   +b^X_{k,2q_k-1}+b^X_{k,2q_k},
 \qquad
 -d_{k,q_k-1}-d_{k,q_k}\le\Delta F^X_k
      \le d_{k,2q_k-1}+d_{k,2q_k}.
 \tag{QDC32}
\]
Its common scalar cancels with the same four signs, with
dimension $t_k$ or $r_k$, respectively. The original complete
sum satisfies the exact equality
$F_{\rm ar,k}-F_{\sigma,k}=\Delta F^K_k+\Delta F^B_k$.
By the original-source determinant limit DMR26 and the exact propagation QDC25--27,
each of $\Delta F^K_k/(kq_k)$ and
$\Delta F^B_k/(kq_k)$ tends to zero separately.
Neither the kernel nor a mixed block in its minimum section
has been deleted.

These identities control the rate relative to the specified
fixed one-factor Gamma source. They do not assign a sign to
QDC24 or QDC32, identify that reference with the original
$k$-fold Gamma endpoint, or prove an RH contradiction.
In particular, $o(kq_k)$ is the scale established by
QDC25--27; a smaller scale does not follow by renaming
the four deficits.
