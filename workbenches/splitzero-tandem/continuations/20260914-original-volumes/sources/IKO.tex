\section{An independent Gamma order on the complete original packet}
\label{sec:IKO}

\paragraph{Exploratory comparison status.}
This section preserves the tested independent Gamma family and its
complete comparison maps. The choice $K=q+1$ was selected to compare
orders of growth; the original tensor construction does not select
that choice here. Following the user's instruction against ad hoc
insertions, it is not substituted into the canonical source. The
canonical order remains $k$ with $q=[1+k(m-1)](k+1)^2$.
The exact translations, fibre maps, and arithmetic transition law
below state precisely how this auxiliary family relates to that
unchanged construction.

The original tensor order $k$ and the original dimension $q$ retain
BSL1. In particular $q=[1+k(m-1)](k+1)^2$, so $k/q\to0$ at
fixed $m$. We now introduce a second, independent order
\[
 K=4L+1,\qquad L\geq0,\qquad
 f_L(S)=\prod_{j=0}^{L-1}(S-c-t_j),\quad t_j=2j+\tfrac12,
 \quad \beta_K=\frac{(2\pi)^{K/2}}{\sqrt2\Gamma(K/2)}.
 \tag{IKO1}
\]
Every parameter and root of $\chi$ remains unchanged. Since $q$ is a
multiple of four, the exact choice
\[
 L=q/4,\qquad K=q+1
 \tag{IKO2}
\]
is available in this family. Thus the ratio $K/q$ tends to one without
identifying the original order $k$ with the original dimension $q$.

\subsection{The coordinate and source maps, with the whole mass}
The natural Gamma source of order $K$ lies on $U=K/2+iy$.
Its pullback to the original line $S=c+iy$ is the translation
\[
 U=S+(K-k)/2,\qquad
 \mathcal T_KP(U)=P(U-(K-k)/2).
 \tag{IKO3}
\]
This is a unital algebra isomorphism on polynomial rings, with inverse
given by the opposite translation; it preserves every degree and has
triangular coefficient matrix with diagonal one. It sends $\chi$ to
$\chi_K(U)=\chi(U-(K-k)/2)$ and induces an isomorphism
$\overline{\mathcal T}_K:E_\chi\to\mathbb C[U]/(\chi_K)$.
Every root is translated by $(K-k)/2$, so each divided Taylor jet at
its translated root is exactly the original divided Taylor jet.
The observed boundary map on that presentation is
$\Lambda_K=\Lambda\overline{\mathcal T}_K^{-1}$, whose kernel is
exactly $\overline{\mathcal T}_K(\ker\Lambda)$.

On the original line define the pulled-back measure
\[
 dm_{0,K}(y)=\beta_K|f_L(c+iy)|^2d\sigma(y).
 \tag{IKO4}
\]
Repeated use of $\Gamma(z+1)=z\Gamma(z)$ at
$z=1/4+iy/2$ gives
\[
 |\Gamma(K/4+iy/2)|^2
     =4^{-L}|\Gamma(1/4+iy/2)|^2
                    \prod_{j=0}^{L-1}(y^2+t_j^2).
\]
Consequently IKO4 is precisely the original order-$K$ Gamma density
after IKO3, with its mass $(2\pi)^{K/2}$. More explicitly, the exact
Gamma integral of the source gives
\[
 \int dm_{0,K}=(2\pi)^{K/2},\qquad
 \gamma_{K,j}=(2\pi)^{K/2}
            \frac{j!\Gamma(j+K/2)}{\Gamma(K/2)}
      =\beta_K\gamma_j\prod_{a=0}^{2L-1}(j+a+\tfrac12).
 \tag{IKO5}
\]
Here is a direct derivation of the parameter dependence. The Euler
double integral, with $u+v=w$ and $\log(u/v)=2x$, gives the density
characteristic function
\[
 \int e^{ity}\frac{|\Gamma(a+iy/2)|^2}{2\pi}\,dy
      =2\Gamma(2a)(2\cosh t)^{-2a},\qquad a>0.
\]
This is the same full calculation as CTR50: the Jacobian evaluates
the positive $w$ integral, and Fourier inversion is justified by the
integrable exponentially decaying derivatives of $(2\cosh x)^{-2a}$.
At $a=K/4$ multiply by $\beta_K4^L$; the constant becomes
$\beta_K2^{2L+1-K/2}\Gamma(K/2)=(2\pi)^{K/2}$.
It follows that the characteristic function of IKO4 is
$(2\pi)^{K/2}(\cosh t)^{-K/2}$, including its value at zero.
Its moment generating function for real $|t|<\pi/2$ is the same
formula with $\cos t$; analytic continuation is justified by the
Gamma exponential bound times its fixed polynomial $\Phi_L$.
Define the monic polynomials by
\[
 \sum_{j\geq0}p_{K,j}(y)\frac{z^j}{j!}
       =(1+z^2)^{-K/4}\exp(y\arctan z).
\]
For sufficiently small real $z,w$, integrate the product of these
two generating functions. The identity
$\cos(\arctan z+\arctan w)=(1-zw)/
\sqrt{(1+z^2)(1+w^2)}$ makes the result exactly
$(2\pi)^{K/2}(1-zw)^{-K/2}$.
Coefficient differentiation is justified by the integrable exponential
majorant. Its $z^iw^j$ coefficients are zero for $i\ne j$ and give
the squared norm $(2\pi)^{K/2}j!(K/2)_j$ when $i=j$.
The generating function shows leading coefficient one. Multiplication
by $i^j$ after $y=(S-c)/i$ gives monic polynomials on the original
line with the same norms, proving IKO5. For its last equality insert
$\gamma_j=\sqrt{2\pi}j!(1/2)_j$ and retain
$\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt\pi$ before cancellation.
For $L=0$ one has $K=1$,
$\beta_K=1$, and the original fixed measure $\sigma$.

\subsection{Exact multiplication on every remainder fibre}
For $N\geq q-1$, retain the same remainder map
$J_N:\mathcal P_N\to E_\chi$. Write $\mathsf E_\chi$ for the complete
ordered divided Taylor map from $E_\chi$ to its $q$ primary jets.
All original roots have nonzero imaginary displacement from $c$
because $k$ is odd and $(2b-k)\gamma\ne0$. The roots $c+t_j$ of
$f_L$ are distinct and real, so $f_L$ and $\chi$ have no common root.
Taylor multiplication by $f_L$ on the complete $\chi$ jets is therefore
invertible; call it $U_{f_L}$. Its determinant is exactly
\[
 \det U_{f_L}=\prod_{a,b=0}^{k}\prod_{j=0}^{L-1}
 \big((2a-k)\delta-t_j+i(2b-k)\gamma\big)^e\ne0.
 \tag{IKO6}
\]
Multiplication gives the bijection of specified affine spaces
\[
 \{P\in\mathcal P_N:J_NP=z\}\longrightarrow
 \{Q\in\mathcal P_{N+L}:\mathsf E_{f_L}Q=0,
                \ \mathsf E_\chi Q=U_{f_L}\mathsf E_\chi z\},
 \qquad P\longmapsto f_LP.
 \tag{IKO7}
\]
Indeed zero evaluation at every distinct $f_L$ root implies
divisibility by $f_L$, and division has degree at most $N$; its
$\chi$ jets are recovered using $U_{f_L}^{-1}$. This proves the
inverse on the stated fibre, not just injectivity on polynomials.
The full relation injection becomes
$Q\mapsto f_L\chi Q$ on the original domain $\mathcal P_{N-q}$.
On the product-jet image the observed map is exactly
\[
 (0,Y)\longmapsto\Lambda\mathsf E_\chi^{-1}U_{f_L}^{-1}Y.
 \tag{IKO8}
\]
Thus the original observation, unit, kernel, and all of its nilpotent
directions are carried through this independent order.

Here is its finite Gram formula. Let $p_j(S)$ be the original monic
Gamma polynomials in the $S$ coordinate with norms $\gamma_j$.
The kernel for any ordered list of divided evaluations $\mathcal E$
at degree $d$ is
$K_{\mathcal E,d}=\sum_{j=0}^d
\mathcal E(p_j)\mathcal E(p_j)^*/\gamma_j$.
At degree $N+L$ take the full product list $(\mathsf E_\chi,
\mathsf E_{f_L})$. It is onto because Hermite interpolation modulo
$\chi f_L$ has dimension $q+L\leq N+L+1$. Its kernel Gram is
positive definite. Its bottom block is $K_{f_L,N+L}$ and its Schur
complement is
\[
 C_{L,N}=K_{\chi,N+L}-K_{\chi,f_L;N+L}
 K_{f_L,N+L}^{-1}K_{f_L,\chi;N+L}>0.
\]
The minimum of the original squared norm for prescribed product jets
$(U_{f_L}\mathsf E_\chi z,0)$ is their quadratic form in the inverse
joint kernel. This follows by the same constrained minimum calculation
as BSL3; its upper-left inverse block is $C_{L,N}^{-1}$.
Multiplying by the exact source factor $\beta_K$ gives
\[
 G_N^{(K)}=\beta_K\mathsf E_\chi^*U_{f_L}^*
                       C_{L,N}^{-1}U_{f_L}\mathsf E_\chi.
 \tag{IKO9}
\]
For $L=0$, interpret the bottom block as empty; the formula is the
fixed Gamma quotient $G_N^\sigma$.

For completeness, this is also an exact determinant of size $2L$.
Let $A$ have columns \[\mathsf E_\chi(p_{N+j})/\sqrt{\gamma_{N+j}},\]
$1\leq j\leq L$, and let
\[B=K_{\chi,f_L;N+L}K_{f_L,N+L}^{-1/2}.\] The finite sum identity
$K_{\chi,N+L}=K_{\chi,N}+AA^*$ gives
\[
 d_{L,N}:=\frac{\det C_{L,N}}{\det K_{\chi,N}}
 =\det\left(I_{2L}+\operatorname{diag}(I_L,-I_L)
       [A,B]^*K_{\chi,N}^{-1}[A,B]\right)>0.
 \tag{IKO10}
\]
The identity $\det(I+AB)=\det(I+BA)$ follows by eliminating either
diagonal block in $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}I&A\\-B&I\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
This proves IKO10, including its mixed cross entries and its sign.
Taking determinants of IKO9 gives at each individual degree
\[
 \log\det G_N^{(K)}-\log\det G_N^\sigma
 =q\log\beta_K+2\log|\det U_{f_L}|-\log d_{L,N}.
 \tag{IKO11}
\]

\subsection{The exact four-endpoint return for arbitrary $L$}
For the original signs $+,+,-,-$ at $N=q-1,q,2q-1,2q$, define
$\mathcal B_k^{(K)}$ using $G_N^{(K)}$ and put
\[
 \mathfrak T_{k;L}=\mathcal B_k^{(K)}-\mathcal B_k^\sigma.
\]
The two degree-independent terms in IKO11 have signed coefficient
zero only after their complete values have been retained. Hence
\[
 \mathfrak T_{k;L}
   =-\log d_{L,q-1}-\log d_{L,q}
                  +\log d_{L,2q-1}+\log d_{L,2q}.
 \tag{IKO12}
\]
The complementary source/relation presentation follows from the
same monic frame used in BSL3. Define
\[
 \begin{split}
 D_{L,N}&=\prod_{j=0}^N\prod_{a=0}^{2L-1}(j+a+\tfrac12),\\
 P_{q,L}&=\log D_{L,q-1}+\log D_{L,q}
                    -\log D_{L,2q-1}-\log D_{L,2q},\\
 R_{\chi,L,r}&=\frac{\det\operatorname{Gram}_{\sigma}
                    [f_L\chi S^j]_{j<r}}
                  {\det\operatorname{Gram}_{\sigma}
                    [\chi S^j]_{j<r}}.
 \end{split}
\]
The source norm products IKO5 and the exact relation ranks $0,1,q,q+1$
then prove
\[
 \boxed{\mathfrak T_{k;L}
       =P_{q,L}-\log R_{\chi,L,1}
                    +\log R_{\chi,L,q}+\log R_{\chi,L,q+1}.}
 \tag{IKO13}
\]
All source and relation powers of $\beta_K$ are accounted for by
IKO11; their four quotient contributions cancel. Equations IKO12 and
IKO13 compute the same original finite return.

\subsection{Full tails when the independent order is comparable to $q$}
We prove that the actual $f_L$ is retained even when $L=q/4$.
Let $0\leq L\leq q/4$ and $r\in\{1,q,q+1\}$. Keep the full forms
$A_{\chi,L,r}=\operatorname{Gram}_\sigma[f_L\chi S^j]_{j<r}$
and $A_{0,L,r}=\operatorname{Gram}_\sigma[f_L(S)(S-c)^qS^j]_{j<r}$.
On the bulk $B$ of BSL6 their density ratio is exactly
$\exp\psi_\chi$, so the finite inequalities BSL8 hold with these
two forms and the same $b_-,b_+$, for every such $L$.

Here are full tail estimates on this larger degree domain. Put
$\Phi_L(y)=\prod_{j<L}(y^2+t_j^2)$. For real $z$,
\[
 \frac{\Phi_L(qz)}{\Phi_L(q)}\leq1\quad(|z|\leq1),\qquad
 \frac{\Phi_L(qz)}{\Phi_L(q)}\leq|z|^{2L}\quad(|z|\geq1),
 \quad\Phi_L(qz)\geq\Phi_L(q)\quad(1\leq z\leq2).
 \tag{IKO14}
\]
Each inequality follows factor by factor from
$(z^2+(t_j/q)^2)/(1+(t_j/q)^2)$; it does not replace this factor
by $z^2$ in any determinant.
For $Q(z)=P(c+iqz)$ with $\deg P\leq r-1$, the proved Legendre
coefficient inequality CTR40 gives
$|Q(z)|^2\leq r^28^{2q}\int_1^2|Q|^2$ on $|z|\leq\epsilon$,
and $|Q(z)|^2\leq r^2((131/32)|z|)^{2r-2}\int_1^2|Q|^2$
on $|z|\geq64$. The original density bounds are
$C_L\exp(-\pi|y|)\leq\sigma(y)\leq C_U\exp(-|y|)$,
with $C_U/C_L=(\cos1)^{-1/2}$, proved in BRD23--24.
Pairing each displacement root with its negative gives
$|\chi(c+iy)|^2\leq(y^2+k^2(d+g))^q$.
On $1\leq z\leq2$ the paired reverse bound gives
$|\chi(c+iqz)|^2\geq q^{2q}(3/4)^q$; on $|z|\leq\epsilon$
the upper bound is $q^{2q}(2\epsilon^2)^q$.
Together with IKO14 these prove, for each of the two forms,
\[
 H^I\preceq\kappa_{I,r}H^B
 \tag{IKO15}
\]
with the literal $\kappa_{I,r}$ of BSL9. The factor
$\Phi_L(q)$, the Jacobian $q$, and the factor $q^{2q}$ occur in
both numerator and bulk denominator before cancellation. The power
form gives a stronger inequality because its paired bounds are exact
powers of $z$.

For $|z|\geq64$, the paired upper bound is
$|\chi(c+iqz)|^2\leq q^{2q}|z|^{2q}\exp(q/(4\cdot64^2))$,
using $k\sqrt{d+g}/q\leq\epsilon/2\leq1/2$ and
$\log(1+x)\leq x$. Relative to the bulk lower factor, its added
exponent is at most
$q\log(4/3)+q/(4\cdot64^2)<q/2$.
The polynomial exponent in the far integral is
$D=2q+2L+2r-2\leq(9/2)q\leq5q$.
Using both tails and
\[
 \int_{64}^{\infty}z^D\exp(-qz)\,dz
 \leq\frac{64^D\exp(-64q)}{q-D/64},\qquad q-D/64\geq59q/64,
\]
gives a coefficient at most
\[
 \frac{128r^2}{59q\sqrt{\cos1}}
 \exp\left(-[64-2\pi-5\log64-2\log(131/32)-1/2]q\right).
\]
The bracket is greater than $20$: use $\pi<4$, $\log2<1$,
and $\log(131/32)<2$, the latter following from the positive
exponential series at $2$. Since $r\leq q+1\leq2q$, this proves
$H^F\preceq\kappa_FH^B$ with the same $\kappa_F$ as BSL9.
The power form has no added paired exponent and satisfies the same
bound. Thus every original tail value obeys $0\leq t_H\leq L_r$.

Define $\eta_{L,r}=\log\det A_{\chi,L,r}-\log\det A_{0,L,r}$.
We have proved throughout the stated macroscopic range
\[
 rb_- -L_r\leq\eta_{L,r}\leq rb_++L_r.
 \tag{IKO16}
\]
Let $\mathfrak T_{0;q,L}$ be IKO13 with the homogeneous relation
polynomial $(S-c)^q$ and with the same actual $f_L$. Its exact
difference from the original packet return is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathfrak T_{k;L}-\mathfrak T_{0;q,L}
   ={}& (\eta_{L,q}-\eta_{0,q})
       +(\eta_{L,q+1}-\eta_{0,q+1})\\
      &-(\eta_{L,1}-\eta_{0,1}),\\
 |\mathfrak T_{k;L}-\mathfrak T_{0;q,L}|
   \leq{}&(2q+2)(b_+-b_-)+2(L_q+L_{q+1}+L_1)
         =O(q/e).
 \end{split}
 \tag{IKO17}
\]
This is an exact transport and a proved error on the original packet.
In particular it is $o(q^2)$ when $L=q/4$, with all root locations
and the whole multiplying polynomial still in its finite formulas.

\subsection{The invariant arithmetic receiver and transition law}
The original arithmetic measure, quotient, and observation are fixed
throughout. Their already proved comparison with $\sigma$ is
$\mathcal B_k^{\rm ar}=\mathcal B_k^\sigma+\delta_k^\sigma$.
Substituting IKO12 proves the exact identity for every $L\geq0$
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal B_k^{\rm ar}
      =\mathcal B_k^{(K)}-\mathfrak T_{k;L}+\delta_k^\sigma.}
 \tag{IKO18}
\]
For two orders $K_a=4L_a+1$, $K_b=4L_b+1$ it also proves
\[
 \mathcal B_k^{(K_b)}-\mathcal B_k^{(K_a)}
       =\mathfrak T_{k;L_b}-\mathfrak T_{k;L_a}.
 \tag{IKO19}
\]
This transition satisfies the additive cocycle identity for three
orders by direct subtraction. When $L_b\geq L_a$, its underlying
source map is the literal multiplication by
$f_{L_b}/f_{L_a}=\prod_{j=L_a}^{L_b-1}(S-c-t_j)$, with the inverse
given by division on its zero-product-jet fibre, exactly as in IKO7.
The observation on that fibre is IKO8 with this quotient germ.
Applying BSL20 to each of these positive quotient metrics propagates
the same identity through the original kernel and boundary factors,
including the full frame determinant before its signed cancellation.

Thus IKO2 realizes the proposed comparable order in the complete
original construction. The baseline and return both change by the
explicit quantity IKO13, and their arithmetic combination in IKO18
is fixed. Their actual macroscopic coefficients are calculated with
the full $f_L$ field in the accompanying independent-order energy
proof; a comparison based only on matching powers does not specify
those coefficients.
