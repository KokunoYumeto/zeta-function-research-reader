The one-factor calculation gives the original finite-field sheaf's
exact decomposition, weight one, invariant-space vanishing and zero
finite-cover logarithm.  The ordered tensor calculation now carries
that result through every positive tensor order on the same parameter
line.  Keeping \(n=m+1\), the original phase has constant \(kc\),
and the character on an ordered monomial form is
\(\zeta^{\sum_i(b_i+1)}\), where \(0\leq b_i<m\) and
\(\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}\).  The complete sum of these characters
gives the exact ordinary complex invariant dimension
\[
 d_{0,k}=\frac1n\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
           \left(\sum_{a=1}^{n-1}\zeta^{ja}\right)^k
        =\frac{m^k+m(-1)^k}{m+1}.
 \tag{SPBT.1}\label{eq:spbt1}
\]
The inner sum is \(m\) for \(j=0\) and \(-1\) otherwise,
proving the equality.  It is zero at \(k=1\); for \(m\geq2\)
it is nonzero at every \(k\geq2\), and for \(m=1\) it is one
at even \(k\) and zero at odd \(k\).

       =\Pi_kU_k^{-1}\widehat Q_0.
 \tag{SPBT.2}\label{eq:spbt2}
\]
The diagonal power matrix in the exact tensor period formula
