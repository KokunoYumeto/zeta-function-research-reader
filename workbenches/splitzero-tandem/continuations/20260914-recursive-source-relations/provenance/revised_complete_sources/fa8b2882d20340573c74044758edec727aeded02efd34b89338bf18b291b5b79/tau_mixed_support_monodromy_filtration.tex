\section{The nilpotent filtration on every independent arithmetic face}
\label{sec:mixed-face-monodromy}

This construction applies Deligne's nilpotent-filtration linear algebra to the
actual arithmetic packets and to every independent coefficient face. It does
not assert an arithmetic purity estimate. Every formula below retains the
original arithmetic dilation, its scalar eigenvalue, and all its nilpotent
terms. The additional grading operator is recorded as an additional operator.

\subsection{Original packets and the filtration}

Let \(Z\) be a finite set of distinct zeros of the original function
\(g(s)=2\xi(s)\), with their full orders \(m_\rho\geq1\). Put
\[
 h_Z(x)=\prod_{\rho\in Z}(x-\rho)^{m_\rho},\qquad
 E_Z=\mathbb C[x]/(h_Z),\qquad
 E_\rho=e_\rho E_Z.
 \tag{MW1}
\]
Here \(e_\rho\) is the Chinese-remainder idempotent, and
\(u_\rho=e_\rho(x-\rho)\) is an element of this algebra, with local unit
\(e_\rho\). Write \(v_{\rho,r}=e_\rho(x-\rho)^r\), for
\(0\leq r<m_\rho\). These vectors are the original local jet basis.
The nilpotent operator \(N_\rho\) is multiplication by \(u_\rho\):
\[
 N_\rho v_{\rho,r}=v_{\rho,r+1}\quad(r<m_\rho-1),\qquad
 N_\rho v_{\rho,m_\rho-1}=0.
 \tag{MW2}
\]
For an integer \(j\), define the increasing filtration
\[
 M_jE_\rho
 =\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}
   \{v_{\rho,r}:m_\rho-1-2r\leq j\},\qquad
 M_jE_Z=\bigoplus_{\rho\in Z}M_jE_\rho .
 \tag{MW3}
\]
In particular a zero vector in \(M_jE_\rho\) remains the zero of its specified
coefficient face when this filtration is split-lifted.

\paragraph{Filtration calculation.}
Multiplication by \(u_\rho\) lowers the displayed index by exactly two.
Thus \(N_\rho M_j\subseteq M_{j-2}\). For \(k\geq0\), the only way
\(\operatorname{Gr}_k^M E_\rho\) is nonzero is
\[
 k=m_\rho-1-2r\geq0,\qquad 0\leq r<m_\rho .
\]
It is then one dimensional with basis the class of \(v_{\rho,r}\).
The map induced by \(N_\rho^k\) sends this basis vector to the class of
\(v_{\rho,r+k}\), whose index is \(-k\) and whose exponent
\(r+k=m_\rho-1-r\) lies between zero and \(m_\rho-1\).
Its coefficient is exactly one. Thus
\[
 N_\rho^k:\operatorname{Gr}_k^M E_\rho
       \xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\operatorname{Gr}_{-k}^M E_\rho .
 \tag{MW4}
\]
When the source graded space is zero, the target is zero by the same
index calculation. Direct sums prove the corresponding assertion for
\(N=\bigoplus_\rho N_\rho\) on \(E_Z\).
This is precisely the centered nilpotent filtration characterized in
Weil II, Proposition 1.6.1.

There is an intrinsic formula which also proves functoriality without
choosing a new jet basis:
\[
 M_jV=\sum_{i\geq0}
       \left(\ker N^{i+1}\cap
                    \operatorname{im}N^{\max(0,i-j)}\right),
 \qquad V=E_Z .
 \tag{MW5}
\]
To verify it on a block of length \(m\), both spaces in each intersection
are terminal spans of the original basis. Their intersection starts at
exponent \(\max(0,m-1-i,i-j)\). The sum over \(i\) starts at the smallest
such exponent, namely
\(\max(0,-j,\lceil(m-1-j)/2\rceil)\); an exponent at least \(m\) denotes the
zero space. When the extra term \(-j\) exceeds the other terms,
\(j<1-m\) and both spans are zero. Otherwise the starting exponents
agree with MW3. Kernels and images commute with the
finite direct-sum decomposition, proving MW5. A linear map commuting
with the nilpotent operators carries each kernel and each image into
the corresponding kernel and image. It therefore preserves MW5.

\subsection{Independent masks and the arithmetic action}

For \(n\geq1\), retain the independent carrier
\[
 D_{E_Z,n}=G(E_Z)^n,\qquad
 G(E_Z)=\{\tau\}\sqcup\{b^\bullet:b\in E_Z\}.
 \tag{MW6}
\]
For each \(A\subseteq I_n=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\), its face is
\(\{A\}\times E_Z^A\), with the empty face a single absent point.
The filtration on the entire carrier is the collection
\[
 \widetilde M_jD_{E_Z,n}
   =\{\boldsymbol\tau\}\sqcup
     \coprod_{\varnothing\ne A\subseteq I_n}
           \{A\}\times(M_jE_Z)^A .
 \tag{MW7}
\]
This notation specifies additive faces and their filtrations. No assertion
that each \(\widetilde M_j\) is a unital subsemiring is made.
Every present face has its own zero \((A,0)\) in every filtration step;
the filtration does not turn any of these zeros into absence.
The zero-insertion maps for \(A\subseteq B\) preserve MW7 because they
insert the original zero of \(M_jE_Z\).

The original arithmetic operator, for real \(a>0\), is multiplication by
the full jet of \(a^s=\exp(s\log a)\). On a local block it is
\[
 A_a|_{E_\rho}
 =a^\rho\sum_{r=0}^{m_\rho-1}
       \frac{(\log a)^r}{r!}N_\rho^r .
 \tag{MW8}
\]
It preserves MW3, and its induced action on every nonzero graded
piece of that block is exactly \(a^\rho\), since all terms with
\(r>0\) lower the filtration index. Its inverse is \(A_{a^{-1}}\).
Thus the split lift preserves every independent mask, including
the zero in each active slot.

For an odd prime power \(q\) with \(\gcd(q,n)=1\), retain the coefficient
Frobenius from the original negacyclic algebra:
\[
 \Psi_q(v\,t^i)
   =(-1)^{\lfloor qi/n\rfloor}v\,t^{qi\bmod n},
 \qquad B_n=E_Z[t]/(t^n+1).
 \tag{MW9}
\]
It sends \(A\) to \(qA\) and leaves the jet index unchanged.
Consequently it preserves the full filtered face diagram, with its
specified permutation of face labels and its original signs. It
commutes with \(N\) and \(A_a\). Define the combined operator, at its
full linear coefficient-space type, by
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 B_{\rho,n}=E_\rho[t]/(t^n+1),\qquad
 M_jB_{\rho,n}=\bigoplus_{i=0}^{n-1}(M_jE_\rho)t^i,\\
 T_{a,q}=A_a\Psi_q=\Psi_q A_a:B_n\longrightarrow B_n,\\
 T_{a,q}(v_{\rho,r}t^i)
 =(-1)^{\lfloor qi/n\rfloor}a^\rho
   \sum_{\ell=0}^{m_\rho-1-r}
      \frac{(\log a)^\ell}{\ell!}
      v_{\rho,r+\ell}t^{qi\bmod n}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW9a}
\]
Here \(A_a\) and \(N\) act coefficientwise, and \(B_n\) is the direct
sum of the \(B_{\rho,n}\). Applying MW8 to MW9 gives every coefficient
in MW9a; the two orders agree because the signed permutation acts only
on the coefficient-slot index. The split lift of \(T_{a,q}\) sends
the face labelled \(A\) to the face labelled \(qA\), with exactly
the displayed signs. Eigenspaces below are taken in the full linear
space \(B_{\rho,n}\); an individual moving face need not be invariant.

Since \(q\) is a unit modulo \(2n\), choose \(h\geq1\) with
\(q^h\equiv1\pmod {2n}\). Substitution sends \(t^i\) to
\(t^{q^hi}=t^i\), so \(\Psi_q^h=I\). The polynomial \(X^h-1\)
has distinct complex roots, hence \(\Psi_q\) is diagonalizable and
its eigenvalues \(\omega\) satisfy \(\omega^h=1\).
On each nonzero graded part of its \(\omega\)-eigenspace, the three
separate operators therefore have eigenvalues
\[
 \operatorname{Gr}^M(A_a):a^\rho,\qquad
 \operatorname{Gr}^M(\Psi_q):\omega,\qquad
 \operatorname{Gr}^M(T_{a,q}):\omega a^\rho,
 \qquad |\omega|=1 .
 \tag{MW10}
\]
The eigenspace decomposition respects the filtration. Its projection
onto the \(\omega\)-eigenspace is
\[
 P_\omega=h^{-1}\sum_{d=0}^{h-1}\omega^{-d}\Psi_q^d.
\]
On an eigenspace with eigenvalue \(\omega'\), this sum is one if
\(\omega'=\omega\) and zero otherwise, by the finite geometric-sum
identity and \((\omega'/\omega)^h=1\). Every summand preserves every
filtration step, so the projection does too. Thus the asserted
graded eigenspaces and the restrictions of
\(A_a\) are defined. MW10 computes the combined operator's modulus
as \(a^{\operatorname{Re}\rho}\); no bound
on \(\operatorname{Re}\rho\) has been inserted.

\subsection{Tensor products with every coefficient and sign retained}

The following proof supplies the tensor compatibility on these blocks,
including its constants. On a block of length \(m\), define auxiliary
operators
\[
 H v_r=(m-1-2r)v_r,\qquad
 L v_r=v_{r+1},\qquad
 E v_r=r(m-r)v_{r-1}.
 \tag{MW11}
\]
The formulas at the endpoints use \(v_{-1}=v_m=0\); \(L\) is precisely
the original nilpotent \(N_\rho\). Direct subtraction on each basis
vector gives
\[
 [H,L]=-2L,\qquad [H,E]=2E,\qquad [E,L]=H .
 \tag{MW12}
\]
For example the coefficient in the last commutator is
\((r+1)(m-r-1)-r(m-r)=m-1-2r\).
For this algebraic proof alone equip this basis with the auxiliary
positive Hermitian form having squared lengths
\[
 c_r=\frac{r!(m-1)!}{(m-1-r)!},\qquad c_0=1 .
 \tag{MW13}
\]
This is an explicitly specified additional form, not a replacement
for a source norm or an arithmetic positivity assertion.
The identity
\(c_{r+1}=(r+1)(m-r-1)c_r\) proves \(L^*=E\), and \(H^*=H\).

On any finite tensor product of blocks, retain the original tensor
basis and define \(L_{\rm tot},E_{\rm tot},H_{\rm tot}\) as the sums
of these operators acting in the individual factors. Operators on
different factors commute, so MW12 still holds. With the product
form from MW13, \(L_{\rm tot}^*=E_{\rm tot}\) and
\(H_{\rm tot}^*=H_{\rm tot}\).

Here is a direct proof of the needed graded isomorphisms.
Choose a nonzero vector \(v\) of maximal \(H_{\rm tot}\)-eigenvalue
\(d\). Then \(E_{\rm tot}v=0\), because \(E_{\rm tot}\) raises that
eigenvalue by two. Induction using MW12 gives
\[
 E_{\rm tot}L_{\rm tot}^r v
       =r(d-r+1)L_{\rm tot}^{r-1}v .
 \tag{MW14}
\]
The operator \(L_{\rm tot}\) is nilpotent. Let \(\ell\) be the
largest exponent for which \(L_{\rm tot}^\ell v\ne0\).
Applying MW14 at \(r=\ell+1\) gives
\((\ell+1)(d-\ell)L_{\rm tot}^\ell v=0\), hence \(d=\ell\).
Thus \(d\) is a nonnegative integer and the chain has weights
\(d,d-2,\ldots,-d\).
Its span is invariant under \(L_{\rm tot},E_{\rm tot},H_{\rm tot}\).
Its orthogonal complement is invariant too, because the first two
are adjoints and the third is self-adjoint. Induction on dimension
decomposes the entire tensor space into such chains.
On each chain \(L_{\rm tot}^k\) identifies weight \(k\) with weight
\(-k\) exactly as in MW4. Therefore the filtration by sums of
\(H_{\rm tot}\)-eigenspaces of index at most \(j\) is the centered
nilpotent filtration. In the original tensor coordinates it is
\[
 M_j\left(\bigotimes_{\nu=1}^k E_{\rho_\nu}\right)
   =\sum_{j_1+\cdots+j_k\leq j}
        M_{j_1}E_{\rho_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes M_{j_k}E_{\rho_k}.
 \tag{MW15}
\]
This also proves the precise relation to Weil II 1.6.9 on these
actual blocks. Tensoring labelled faces retains the ordered tuple
of their masks; MW15 operates on their amplitude fibres.

The original arithmetic tensor operator is, without dropping any term,
\[
 \bigotimes_{\nu=1}^k A_a|_{E_{\rho_\nu}}
  =a^{\rho_1+\cdots+\rho_k}
     \exp\!\left((\log a)\sum_{\nu=1}^k N_{\rho_\nu}\right).
 \tag{MW16}
\]
The exponential is finite and the equality follows by expanding
the product of commuting finite exponentials. On the \(M\)-graded
pieces its eigenvalue is \(a^{\rho_1+\cdots+\rho_k}\).
For identical packets its modulus is \(a^{k\operatorname{Re}\rho}\).
In particular the original arithmetic weight survives this complete
nilpotent-filtration construction.

For the coefficient spaces, take \(n_\nu\geq1\), a common odd prime
power \(q\) coprime to every \(n_\nu\), and
\(B_\nu=E_{\rho_\nu}[t_\nu]/(t_\nu^{n_\nu}+1)\).
Write \(N_\nu\) for the nilpotent in factor \(\nu\), extended by
identities to the other factors. Define
\(A_a^{\otimes}=\bigotimes_\nu A_a|_{B_\nu}\),
\(\Psi_q^{\otimes}=\bigotimes_\nu\Psi_q|_{B_\nu}\), and
\(T_{a,q}^{\otimes}=\bigotimes_\nu T_{a,q}|_{B_\nu}\). Then
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_{a,q}^{\otimes}
 &=A_a^{\otimes}\Psi_q^{\otimes}
  =a^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu}
       \exp\!\left((\log a)\sum_\nu N_\nu\right)
          \Psi_q^{\otimes},\\
 T_{a,q}^{\otimes}\left(\bigotimes_\nu
                         v_{\rho_\nu,r_\nu}t_\nu^{i_\nu}\right)
 &=a^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu}
   \left(\prod_\nu(-1)^{\lfloor qi_\nu/n_\nu\rfloor}\right)\\
 &\qquad{}\times
   \sum_{\substack{0\leq\ell_\nu\leq
                         m_{\rho_\nu}-1-r_\nu\;\text{for all }\nu}}
   \left(\prod_\nu\frac{(\log a)^{\ell_\nu}}{\ell_\nu!}\right)
   \bigotimes_\nu
    v_{\rho_\nu,r_\nu+\ell_\nu}
                      t_\nu^{qi_\nu\bmod n_\nu}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{MW16a}
\]
Expanding the factor formulas MW9a proves the second line, including
every sign and every factorial. The first line follows by grouping
the commuting operators on different tensor factors, and then using
MW16. On the tensor of coefficient eigenspaces with eigenvalues
\(\omega_\nu\), the three graded eigenvalues are respectively
\[
 A_a^{\otimes}:a^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu},\qquad
 \Psi_q^{\otimes}:\prod_\nu\omega_\nu,\qquad
 T_{a,q}^{\otimes}:\left(\prod_\nu\omega_\nu\right)
                                      a^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu}.
 \tag{MW16b}
\]
The positive nilpotent powers in MW16a decrease total degree by at
least two, which proves this graded formula from the complete
operator formula. Its combined modulus is
\(a^{\sum_\nu\operatorname{Re}\rho_\nu}\). The coefficient permutation
sends the ordered tuple \((A_\nu)_\nu\) to \((qA_\nu)_\nu\).

\subsection{The exact Tate-scaling comparison}

Deligne's local-monodromy operator has type \(N:V(1)\to V\).
For geometric Frobenius over \(\mathbb F_q\), the Tate line has
Frobenius eigenvalue \(q^{-1}\). After recording a generator of
that line, equivariance has the precise matrix form
\[
             FNF^{-1}=q^{-1}N .
 \tag{MW17}
\]
For the original arithmetic action MW8 one instead calculates
\(A_aN_\rho A_a^{-1}=N_\rho\). The exact relation between these
operator presentations can be constructed on the same jet space.
For real \(q>1\) define
\[
 \Gamma_{q,\rho}v_{\rho,r}
   =q^{(m_\rho-1)/2-r}v_{\rho,r},\qquad
 F_{q,\rho}=q^\rho\Gamma_{q,\rho}.
 \tag{MW18}
\]
Both are invertible and retain every original basis vector.
The quotient of successive diagonal entries is \(q^{-1}\), so
\[
 F_{q,\rho}N_\rho F_{q,\rho}^{-1}=q^{-1}N_\rho,\qquad
 A_q=F_{q,\rho}\Gamma_{q,\rho}^{-1}
                     \exp((\log q)N_\rho).
 \tag{MW19}
\]
The second equality is obtained by multiplying the first two
diagonal operators, giving exactly \(q^\rho I\), and then retaining
the complete exponential MW8. It proves reconstruction of the
original arithmetic action from the added operators.
Moreover, if \(U(z)=\exp(zN_\rho)\), then
\[
 F_{q,\rho}U(z)F_{q,\rho}^{-1}=U(q^{-1}z).
 \tag{MW20}
\]
Thus these explicit matrices define a complex representation of the
group generated by the additive one-parameter operators \(U(z)\)
and an invertible element acting on the parameter by \(z\mapsto
q^{-1}z\). This statement is at the displayed complex-linear
type; it does not assert that these matrices arise from an
arithmetic lisse sheaf.

On the graded piece of index \(j=m_\rho-1-2r\), the eigenvalue
of the added \(F_{q,\rho}\) is \(q^{\rho+j/2}\), with
\[
 2\log_q|q^{\rho+j/2}|=2\operatorname{Re}\rho+j .
 \tag{MW21}
\]
This gives exactly the index shift \(\beta+j\) in Weil II 1.8.4,
with the explicitly computed \(\beta=2\operatorname{Re}\rho\).
It does not determine \(\beta\). The two-sided boundary estimate in
Deligne's theorem comes from the pure lisse source, the trace and
Euler-product bound, and its tensor and dual arguments. MW18
constructs the matrix relation and MW21 computes every graded
modulus; neither computation supplies that geometric input.
All operators MW18 act coefficientwise on MW7, preserve its masks,
and commute with the coefficient permutation MW9.

For admissible odd prime powers \(q\) as in MW9, retaining the
separate coefficient operator also gives the exact reconstruction
and its tensor version
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_{q,q}|_{B_{\rho,n}}
   &=F_{q,\rho}\Gamma_{q,\rho}^{-1}
                         \exp((\log q)N_\rho)\Psi_q,\\
 T_{q,q}^{\otimes}
   &=F_q^{\otimes}(\Gamma_q^{\otimes})^{-1}
           \exp\!\left((\log q)\sum_\nu N_\nu\right)
                                      \Psi_q^{\otimes},\\
 F_q^{\otimes}&=\bigotimes_\nu F_{q,\rho_\nu},\qquad
 \Gamma_q^{\otimes}=\bigotimes_\nu\Gamma_{q,\rho_\nu}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{MW21a}
\]
The first two factors in the first line multiply to \(q^\rho I\);
in the tensor line they multiply to \(q^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu}I\).
This proves both identities in their displayed order. On total degree
\(j\), the added \(F_q^{\otimes}\) acts by
\(q^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu+j/2}\). Its combination with
\(\Psi_q^{\otimes}\) acts by
\((\prod_\nu\omega_\nu)q^{\sum_\nu\rho_\nu+j/2}\) on the indicated
coefficient eigenspaces. These are added-operator eigenvalues; the
unchanged arithmetic and combined eigenvalues remain MW16b.

For completeness, duality at the vector-space level also retains
its signs. On \(V^\vee\), use \(N^\vee=-N^{\mathsf T}\) and
\(M_jV^\vee=(M_{-j-1}V)^\perp\). The dual of a basis vector of
index \(d\) has index \(-d\), and \(N^\vee\) lowers that index
by two with coefficient \(-1\). Its iterated graded isomorphisms
have the nonzero coefficient \((-1)^k\).
The original contragredient arithmetic action is
\[
 (A_a^{-1})^{\mathsf T}
       =a^{-\rho}\exp(-(\log a)N^{\mathsf T}),
 \tag{MW22}
\]
so its graded eigenvalue is exactly \(a^{-\rho}\).
On the full coefficient dual put
\(\Psi_q^\vee=(\Psi_q^{-1})^{\mathsf T}\) and
\(T_{a,q}^\vee=(T_{a,q}^{-1})^{\mathsf T}\). Commutativity in MW9a
and transposition of the inverse product give
\[
 T_{a,q}^\vee=(A_a^{-1})^{\mathsf T}
                         (\Psi_q^{-1})^{\mathsf T}.
 \tag{MW22a}
\]
The dual of the \(\omega\)-coefficient eigenspace has
\(\Psi_q^\vee\)-eigenvalue \(\omega^{-1}\): evaluation gives
\((\Psi_q^\vee f)(v)=f(\Psi_q^{-1}v)=\omega^{-1}f(v)\).
Thus the three dual graded eigenvalues are \(a^{-\rho}\),
\(\omega^{-1}\), and \(\omega^{-1}a^{-\rho}\), respectively.
These formulas establish the tensor, dual and Tate-scaling
comparisons on every arithmetic amplitude fibre while retaining
the separate mixed-support labels.

\subsection{The actual length-two quotient retains its induced degrees}

Define \(Z_{\geq2}=\{\rho\in Z:m_\rho\geq2\}\). For each member
of this specified subset put \(m=m_\rho\), \(b=m-2\), and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 J_\rho=u_\rho^2E_\rho
       =\operatorname{span}\{v_{\rho,r}:2\leq r<m\},\qquad
 C_\rho=E_\rho/J_\rho,\qquad
 \pi_\rho:E_\rho\longrightarrow C_\rho,\\
 w_0=\pi_\rho(v_{\rho,0}),\qquad
 w_1=\pi_\rho(v_{\rho,1}),\qquad
 \phi_\rho:C_\rho\longrightarrow V_2=\mathbb C[u]/(u^2),
 \quad \phi_\rho(c_0w_0+c_1w_1)=c_0+c_1u.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW23}
\]
Here \(u=x-\rho\) is the same local coordinate: the map sends the
original local unit to \(1\) and the original first power to \(u\).
The basis multiplication satisfies
\(v_{\rho,r}v_{\rho,s}=v_{\rho,r+s}\) for \(r+s<m\), and zero
otherwise. Therefore \(J_\rho\) is an ideal, \(w_0\) is the quotient
unit, \(w_1^2=0\), and \(\phi_\rho\) is an algebra isomorphism with
inverse \(c_0+c_1u\mapsto c_0w_0+c_1w_1\).
For \(m=2\), the displayed span for \(J_\rho\) is zero. This
construction is indexed by \(Z_{\geq2}\); it does not assert that
this subset has a member. Raising an exponent preserves \(J_\rho\),
so \(N_\rho\) induces the operator \(\overline N\) on \(C_\rho\).

Give \(J_\rho\) the subspace filtration and \(C_\rho\) the quotient
filtration of MW3. The latter is exactly
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \overline M_jC_\rho&=\pi_\rho(M_jE_\rho)
 =\begin{cases}
 0&j<m-3,\\
 \mathbb Cw_1&m-3\leq j<m-1,\\
 C_\rho&j\geq m-1,
 \end{cases}\\
 \phi_\rho(\overline M_jC_\rho)&=M_{j-b}V_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{MW24}
\]
Indeed, all original powers of exponent at least two vanish under
\(\pi_\rho\); the two surviving powers have their original degrees
\(m-1\) and \(m-3\). Substituting \(b=m-2\) into the length-two
degrees \(1,-1\) proves the second line. For every \(j\) the kernel
of \(M_jE_\rho\to\overline M_jC_\rho\) is
\(J_\rho\cap M_jE_\rho\), and its image is the whole indicated
quotient step. Hence
\(0\to J_\rho\cap M_jE_\rho\to M_jE_\rho\to
\overline M_jC_\rho\to0\) is exact, proving strictness of both maps.
The quotient filtration is centered at \(b\): its only nonidentity
graded isomorphism is
\(\overline N:\operatorname{Gr}_{b+1}^{\overline M}C_\rho
\to\operatorname{Gr}_{b-1}^{\overline M}C_\rho\), sending the class
of \(w_0\) to that of \(w_1\) with coefficient one. For \(k=0\)
the centered-at-\(b\) map is the identity, and for \(k>1\) both
graded spaces are zero. If \(m>2\), this filtration is not centered
at zero: \(\operatorname{Gr}_{m-1}\) is nonzero whereas
\(\operatorname{Gr}_{-(m-1)}\) is zero. MW24 retains the quotient's
actual degrees in every case.

The ideal \(J_\rho\) is invariant under \(N_\rho\), every \(A_a\),
and the added diagonal operators \(\Gamma_{q,\rho},F_{q,\rho}\).
For \(N_\rho\) this follows by raising the exponent; for \(A_a\)
it follows term by term in MW8; for the diagonals each basis line
is invariant. Their induced quotient operators consequently satisfy
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \overline Nw_0=w_1,\quad \overline Nw_1=0,\qquad
 \overline A_aw_0=a^\rho(w_0+(\log a)w_1),\quad
 \overline A_aw_1=a^\rho w_1,\\
 \overline\Gamma_qw_r=q^{(m-1)/2-r}w_r,\qquad
 \overline F_qw_r=q^{\rho+(m-1)/2-r}w_r\quad(r=0,1),\\
 \phi_\rho\overline N=N_2\phi_\rho,\quad
 \phi_\rho\overline A_a=A_{a,2}\phi_\rho,\quad
 \phi_\rho\overline\Gamma_q=q^{b/2}\Gamma_{q,2}\phi_\rho,\quad
 \phi_\rho\overline F_q=q^{b/2}F_{q,2}\phi_\rho.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW25}
\]
The subscript \(2\) specifies the fixed length-two operators
\(N_2(1)=u\), \(N_2(u)=0\),
\(A_{a,2}=a^\rho(I+(\log a)N_2)\),
\(\Gamma_{q,2}(1)=q^{1/2}\), \(\Gamma_{q,2}(u)=q^{-1/2}u\),
and \(F_{q,2}=q^\rho\Gamma_{q,2}\). Every line of MW25 follows by
applying both sides to \(w_0,w_1\). In particular, the higher
terms of MW8 are killed by this specified quotient map; they have
not been discarded from the original packet.

An exact filtered operator isomorphism records both the degree and
diagonal scalar shifts. Let \(D_b=\mathbb C d_b\) have filtration
\((D_b)_j=0\) for \(j<b\) and \((D_b)_j=D_b\) for \(j\geq b\),
with \(N_{D_b}=0\), \(A_{a,D_b}=I\), and
\(\Gamma_{q,D_b}=F_{q,D_b}=q^{b/2}I\). Use sum filtrations,
tensor-sum nilpotents, and tensor-product invertible operators. Then
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \theta_\rho:C_\rho\xrightarrow{\sim}V_2\otimes D_b,
 \quad w_r\longmapsto u^r\otimes d_b\quad(r=0,1),\\
 \theta_\rho(\overline M_jC_\rho)
        =M_{j-b}V_2\otimes D_b,\qquad
 \overline A_q=\overline F_q\overline\Gamma_q^{-1}
                              (I+(\log q)\overline N).
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW26}
\]
The inverse sends \(u^r\otimes d_b\) to \(w_r\), so the displayed
filtration equality proves strictness in both directions. MW25
proves intertwining for \(A,\Gamma,F\), and its coefficient-one
nilpotent relation proves intertwining for \(N\). In the reconstruction,
\(\overline F_q\overline\Gamma_q^{-1}=q^\rho I\), so the
degree shift has changed no arithmetic scalar or logarithmic
coefficient. The original auxiliary \(H\) in MW11 descends to

For \(m>2\), the original auxiliary \(E\) does not descend:

Thus MW26 is the explicitly proved filtered isomorphism for

The kernel also has a specified shifted comparison. For \(m>2\), let
\(V_{m-2}=\mathbb C[u]/(u^{m-2})\) use the original powers, the
centered filtration of a length-\(m-2\) block, and the operators
MW8 and MW18 with the same \(\rho\). The linear map
\(\alpha_\rho:V_{m-2}\to J_\rho\),
\(\alpha_\rho(u^r)=v_{\rho,r+2}\), is bijective, with inverse given
by removing these specified two powers. It is a module comparison
for the displayed nilpotent and arithmetic operators. Its complete
filtration and diagonal relations are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \alpha_\rho(M_{j+2}V_{m-2})=J_\rho\cap M_jE_\rho,\qquad
 N_\rho\alpha_\rho=\alpha_\rho N_{m-2},\qquad
 A_a\alpha_\rho=\alpha_\rho A_{a,m-2},\\
 \Gamma_{q,\rho}\alpha_\rho
          =q^{-1}\alpha_\rho\Gamma_{q,m-2},\qquad
 F_{q,\rho}\alpha_\rho=q^{-1}\alpha_\rho F_{q,m-2}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW26a}
\]
The source degree of \(u^r\) is \(m-3-2r\); the image's degree is
\(m-1-2(r+2)=(m-3-2r)-2\), proving the first relation at every
step. Raising the exponent proves the nilpotent relation including
the last zero vector; expanding MW8 proves the arithmetic relation
with all its coefficients. The diagonal exponent on the image is
\((m-1)/2-(r+2)=((m-3)/2-r)-1\), which proves both remaining
relations. Consequently the kernel, quotient, and their exact
sequence all retain their actual induced shifts. For \(m=2\) the
kernel is zero as already specified.

The same quotient is available coefficientwise. Its map on each face is
\[
 (A,(x_i)_{i\in A})\longmapsto(A,(\pi_\rho x_i)_{i\in A}).
\]
Even a coefficient in \(J_\rho\)
becomes a present zero in the same slot. Surjectivity on each
filtered face follows by choosing, independently in each slot,
a preimage in \(M_jE_\rho\) from MW24. Zero insertion commutes with
this map because \(\pi_\rho(0)=0\). On the full coefficient space
it intertwines \(\Psi_q\) since the map is linear and sends
\((-1)^{\lfloor qi/n\rfloor}x\) to that same sign times
\(\pi_\rho(x)\); MW25 then proves intertwining for \(T_{a,q}\).
For a tensor of such quotient blocks the exact comparison is
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \bigotimes_\nu C_{\rho_\nu}
 \xrightarrow{\ \bigotimes_\nu\theta_{\rho_\nu}\ }
 \left(\bigotimes_\nu V_{2,\rho_\nu}\right)
                         \otimes\left(\bigotimes_\nu D_{m_{\rho_\nu}-2}\right),\\
 \text{total degree shift }\sum_\nu(m_{\rho_\nu}-2),\qquad
 \text{added }F\text{ and }\Gamma\text{ factor }
                      q^{\frac12\sum_\nu(m_{\rho_\nu}-2)}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{MW27}
\]
The tensor factors are rearranged by the explicit ordinary vector-space
map \(\bigotimes_\nu(u^{r_\nu}\otimes d_{b_\nu})\mapsto
(\bigotimes_\nu u^{r_\nu})\otimes(\bigotimes_\nu d_{b_\nu})\),
with coefficient one. Degrees add and diagonal eigenvalues multiply,
which proves both formulas and their inverse on every tensor basis
vector. The tensor quotient is strict: a source tensor basis vector
survives exactly when each exponent is zero or one, and the degree
of every surviving vector is unchanged. Thus each filtered target
step is the image of the same source step. Its kernel is spanned
by tensors with at least one exponent at least two, giving the
complete filtered kernel as their intersection with that step.
The unchanged arithmetic eigenvalues and the signed coefficient
products are precisely MW16a--MW16b, with the nilpotent powers
transported through these actual quotient maps. The strict
length-two subobject and quotient maps are developed with these
induced degrees in MRE34--MRE42 below.

\paragraph{Status of the geometric control.}
The construction proves MW1--MW27, including the lettered formulas,
on the original finite packets and the specified quotients.
It explicitly supplies the nilpotent filtration and its tensor
and dual compatibility, and it reconstructs the unchanged arithmetic
operator from a Tate-scaling operator. No theorem here identifies
the resulting complex representation with a pure lisse sheaf over
the absolute \(\tau\)-base. In particular it proves no numerical
restriction on \(2\operatorname{Re}\rho\). The relevant further
comparison is with the actual hypotheses and proof of Weil II
1.8.4--1.8.5 and the compact-support pushforward estimate 3.3.1;
the finite-poset section functor by itself has not supplied those
estimates.
