The actual final bootstrap in Weil II
The proof of the curve theorem in Weil II, 3.2.3–3.2.15, uses a more specific mechanism than tensor
amplificationalone. Alocalcalculationmakestheweightsofeveryrelevantnonconstantconstituent

integral weights are at most 1. The remaining constituents are geometrically constant on the
projective line, where their first cohomology vanishes. These two exclusions make a product-square
argument improve a universal error 𝑒 to 𝑒/2. The proof below retains the schemes, coeﬀicient
sheaves, boundary terms, maps, twists, and indices which make that improvement possible.
