# How much of Deligne's machinery is actually in use?

The use is substantive but incomplete and uneven. The marked-product continuation genuinely applies a Deligne purity theorem to a correctly dimensioned external family. It also supplies source maps and useful analytic calculations. It had not carried that family through the singular specialization and the mixed-extension controls that connect these results to the original arithmetic source. Our own earlier focus on the final tensor bound and finite coefficient purity left the same central work incomplete.

A percentage would be misleading: these are dependent constructions, and one missing comparison can prevent several already proved components from producing the desired arithmetic bound. The table records exact components and the scope actually checked.

## What the web continuation got right

The complete 708-line NOTE.tex and the supplied continuation were read. Its polynomial family splits over the original parameter ring, so the rank-d^k special fibre is an actual specialization, with every nilpotent retained. Its theta map contains the complete Taylor unit. Its cyclic annihilator defect away from the marked point is explicitly calculated. The four endpoint terms are combined before estimation, and the relation-valued section derivative is retained. These are useful mathematical advances and remain in the handoff.

The final paragraph identifies the eventual arithmetic target. That target is not an account of all the intermediate geometric machinery needed to reach it. The continuation prompt therefore starts at the singular boundary of the family and the associated mixed extensions.

## Corrections actually made or recorded

The source note's interpolation variable x, frequency variable y, and deformation variable u must remain distinct. In its displayed formulas (49), (50), (52) and (58), the accidental frequency occurrences du and c+iu should read dy and c+iy. Its formula (64) uses two endomorphisms: M_0^(-1)M_1 is positive self-adjoint; M_0^(-1)(M_1−M_0) is self-adjoint and can have either sign. The subsequent norm estimate survives that correction.

The two positive sums of projections in the signed calculation have equal trace 2q, but rank q+1. Their complete nonzero spectra are 1, 2 with multiplicity q−1, and 1. These spectra and the final (2q−1) log condition-number bound were already proved in AW7 and AW14 of the earlier audit's relation-window spectral-transport chapter. Our initial handoff incorrectly described that bound as new. The corrected handoff includes the complete AW proof and its stronger full-spectrum AW13 estimate. The precise correspondence on the identical original polynomial source is R_SP=U_AW, P_SP=W_AW and T_SP=C_AW, with interpolation x=t and frequency y=u_AW; substitution in the projector formulas and Q_(q−1)=0 prove the identities. AW's separate source isometry T has its own stated type. SP retains the inverse metric in the scalar-density map and adds the explicit overlap/common-positive-subspace refinement in SP8/SP10. No universal dominance between the overlap and full-spectrum refinements is asserted.

The MW filtration's intrinsic kernel/image formula was valid, but its integer-minimum proof omitted the term −j outside the active range. The exact minimum and the zero-span endpoint are now corrected. The relative-extension calculation also records why filtration preservation alone would not prove strictness, and constructs the induced filtrations that repair the actual short exact sequence.

These are bounded, source-grounded findings. This overview does not claim that every theorem in the entire repository or all prior sessions was reread. The accompanying full proofs and source pins specify what was checked.
