Full-jet interpolation kernels and the actual next relation layer

Integration supplement — 12 September 2026

This supplement reviews the supplied Coherent arithmetic interpolation and joint tensor boundary control package and derives an additional finite-stage bridge. The source’s canonical representative, original theta boundaries, full multiplicities, and support transport are retained. The new formulas below have written proofs and exact finite regression tests; they are not new Lean certificates.

The supplied ZIP has SHA-256 a753df8341d05b2fccb6487b1dabc0044153a6c1abcf35092504aadb95c51d26. All 29 entries of its manifest verified. Its note, handoff, and checker match the published Git blobs at PR #14, commit 3bff1b2bd87dfa2592f65c5ccc231f6ee9541dfc. The source checker was independently rerun: nineteen tests passed normally and under optimized Python, with identical result records; the intentionally false twentieth test failed in both modes. The comparison with PR #15 is limited precisely: its existing five-module receipt certifies its stated support-changing, relation-layer, finite-Krylov and rank-two interfaces. This supplement does not extend that receipt to the source’s theta analytics, all-degree orthogonal-polynomial construction, or the new tensor-kernel calculations.

1. Source results retained, rather than reconstructed differently

Write the source polynomial degree as NN, to distinguish it from a moment matrix. Fix tensor degree k≥1k\ge1, a nonempty packet polynomial hh of degree dd, and N≥k(d−1)N\ge k(d-1). The packet includes the complete orders of the selected actual zeros. Retain

g=2ξ,E=Eh⊗k,A=Ah(k),𝒥N:𝒫N(k)↠E. g=2\xi,\qquad E=E_h^{\otimes k},\qquad A=A_h^{(k)},\qquad \mathcal J_N:\mathcal P_N^{(k)}\twoheadrightarrow E. \tag{1}

The last map is reduction modulo the original ideal (h(t1),…,h(tk))(h(t_1),\ldots,h(t_k)), followed by multiplication by the original local unit υh⊗k\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}. Its kernel is ℐk,N\mathcal I_{k,N}. The source map is

𝒯(P)=P(D1,…,Dk)Fh⊗k,ℳ𝒯(P)=P(s1,…,sk)∏ig(si)h(si). \mathcal T(P)=P(D_1,\ldots,D_k)F_h^{\otimes k},\qquad \mathcal M\mathcal T(P)=P(s_1,\ldots,s_k)\prod_i\frac{g(s_i)}{h(s_i)}. \tag{2}

Let ℒN=𝒯ℐk,N\mathcal L_N=\mathcal T\mathcal I_{k,N}, PN=PℒNP_N=P_{\mathcal L_N}, and let σ:E↪Q⊗̂k\sigma:E\hookrightarrow Q^{\widehat\otimes k} be the source arithmetic inclusion. Its canonical representative and forms are

RN=(I−PN)sh⊗k,GN=RN*RN≻0,q(k)RN=σ,J(k)RN=I, R_N=(I-P_N)s_h^{\otimes k},\qquad G_N=R_N^*R_N\succ0, \qquad q^{(k)}R_N=\sigma,\quad J^{(k)}R_N=I, \tag{3} BN=D(k)RN−RNA,WN=A*GN+GNA−kGN. B_N=D^{(k)}R_N-R_NA,\qquad W_N=A^*G_N+G_NA-kG_N. \tag{4}

The supplied note proves BN(E)⊆ℒN+1B_N(E)\subseteq\mathcal L_{N+1} and, with ℰN=ℒN+1∩ℒN⊥\mathcal E_N=\mathcal L_{N+1}\cap\mathcal L_N^\perp,

YN=PℰNRN,CN=(I−PN)BN,WN=−(YN*CN+CN*YN). Y_N=P_{\mathcal E_N}R_N, \qquad C_N=(I-P_N)B_N, \qquad W_N=-(Y_N^*C_N+C_N^*Y_N). \tag{5}

These are source results (source note, §§2–4 and 7–9), not claimed as discoveries of this supplement. Their finite-dimensional derivations check: the constrained minimum is over precisely the original ideal, the derivative increases total degree by one, and orthogonal projection removes only the admitted relation space. The source’s exact sequence and packet naturality require full orders. Its total-degree statements require the threshold in (1). Its analytic function-space identities remain the stated analytic inputs, not consequences of a finite fixture.

The original coefficient square G(ℤ)→G(ℂ)G(\mathbb Z)\to G(\mathbb C) over ℤ→ℂ\mathbb Z\to\mathbb C, with projections pℤ,pℂp_\mathbb Z,p_\mathbb C, is unchanged. A vector in an admitted relation maps to the receiving fibre’s supported zero, never to external absence τ\tau.

2. Calculate the inverse interpolation metric by full-jet columns

Use exactly the source measure

dνh(t)=|g(1/2+it)h(1/2+it)|2dt2π. d\nu_h(t)=\left|\frac{g(1/2+it)}{h(1/2+it)}\right|^2\frac{dt}{2\pi}. \tag{6}

Let pj(h)(s)p_j^{(h)}(s) be its monic orthogonal polynomials, with the polynomial variable evaluated at s=1/2+its=1/2+it, and retain their positive squared norms

κj(h)=∫|pj(h)(1/2+it)|2dνh(t)>0. \kappa_j^{(h)}=\int|p_j^{(h)}(1/2+it)|^2d\nu_h(t)>0. \tag{7}

These are not the earlier theta-Krylov vectors uju_j, nor their norms 𝔥j\mathfrak h_j. In particular they depend on hh; they are not assigned norm one. Existence through any finite degree follows from the source measure’s positive-definite moment matrices. No limiting assertion is needed here.

Suppress hh in notation. For a multi-index α\alpha, define

pα=∏ipαi,κα=∏iκαi,aj=υh[pj]h∈Eh,aα=⨂iaαi∈E. p_\alpha=\prod_i p_{\alpha_i},\qquad \kappa_\alpha=\prod_i\kappa_{\alpha_i},\qquad a_j=\upsilon_h[p_j]_h\in E_h,\qquad a_\alpha=\bigotimes_i a_{\alpha_i}\in E. \tag{8}

Every aja_j is the full jet, including the local unit. Product orthogonality gives ⟨𝒯pα,𝒯pγ⟩=καδαγ\langle\mathcal T p_\alpha,\mathcal T p_\gamma\rangle=\kappa_\alpha\delta_{\alpha\gamma}. These polynomials form a basis of 𝒫N(k)\mathcal P_N^{(k)}, because the monic triangular basis change preserves total degree.

Set

KN:=GN−1=∑|α|≤Naαaα*κα. \boxed{K_N:=G_N^{-1} =\sum_{|\alpha|\le N}\frac{a_\alpha a_\alpha^*}{\kappa_\alpha}.} \tag{9}

Proof. In this basis the source moment matrix is diag(κα)\operatorname{diag}(\kappa_\alpha) and the full-jet matrix has columns aαa_\alpha. Substitute into the source identity GN−1=JNMN−1JN*G_N^{-1}=J_NM_N^{-1}J_N^*. A change of polynomial basis does not change that product. This proves (9) for the same canonical metric, not a newly selected metric.

Its representative has the explicit coefficient formula

RNu=∑|α|≤N𝒯(pα)aα*GNuκα. R_Nu=\sum_{|\alpha|\le N}\mathcal T(p_\alpha) \frac{a_\alpha^*G_Nu}{\kappa_\alpha}. \tag{10}

In particular RNKNR_NK_N is an actual map from finite antidual coordinates into the original test-function space. It satisfies J(k)RNKN=KNJ^{(k)}R_NK_N=K_N and q(k)RNKN=σKNq^{(k)}R_NK_N=\sigma K_N. The invertible coordinate change KNK_N is retained, not identified with the identity.

3. An explicit basis of the original next relation layer

Let ΣN+1={β∈ℕk:|β|=N+1}\Sigma_{N+1}=\{\beta\in\mathbb N^k:|\beta|=N+1\}. Define actual test functions

eβ=𝒯(pβ)−RNaβ,β∈ΣN+1. \boxed{e_\beta=\mathcal T(p_\beta)-R_Na_\beta, \qquad \beta\in\Sigma_{N+1}.} \tag{11}

They are in ℰN\mathcal E_N. First, their full jets vanish by (3), so their numerator polynomials lie in the original ideal at degree at most N+1N+1; thus eβ∈ℒN+1e_\beta\in\mathcal L_{N+1}. Second, 𝒯pβ\mathcal T p_\beta is orthogonal to all degree-at-most-NN polynomials and RNaβR_Na_\beta is orthogonal to ℒN\mathcal L_N. Hence eβ⊥ℒNe_\beta\perp\mathcal L_N.

Define

ℰN:ℂΣN+1→ℰN,c↦∑βcβeβ, \mathscr E_N:\mathbb C^{\Sigma_{N+1}}\longrightarrow\mathcal E_N, \qquad c\longmapsto\sum_\beta c_\beta e_\beta, \tag{12} UN=(aβ)β∈ΣN+1,DN=diag(κβ)β∈ΣN+1. U_N=(a_\beta)_{\beta\in\Sigma_{N+1}}, \qquad D_N=\operatorname{diag}(\kappa_\beta)_{\beta\in\Sigma_{N+1}}. \tag{13}

The map ℰN\mathscr E_N is an isomorphism. Independence follows by taking the degree-N+1N+1 polynomial part of (11). For spanning, subtract from a relation its degree-N+1N+1 combination of the eβe_\beta. The remainder is a relation of degree at most NN, hence belongs to ℒN\mathcal L_N. An element of ℰN\mathcal E_N has no such remaining component.

Its Gram matrix and pairing with the representative are exactly

HN:=ℰN*ℰN=DN+UN*GNUN,ℰN*RN=−UN*GN. \boxed{H_N:=\mathscr E_N^*\mathscr E_N =D_N+U_N^*G_NU_N,\qquad \mathscr E_N^*R_N=-U_N^*G_N.} \tag{14}

The cross terms vanish because the new-degree polynomials are orthogonal to the old polynomial space. In particular HN≻0H_N\succ0, and no original norm has been suppressed.

This identifies the existing quotient equivalence in coordinates:

ℂΣN+1→ℰNℰN→e↦[e]ℒN+1/ℒN. \mathbb C^{\Sigma_{N+1}}\xrightarrow{\mathscr E_N}\mathcal E_N \xrightarrow{e\mapsto[e]}\mathcal L_{N+1}/\mathcal L_N. \tag{15}

The inverse quotient-to-layer map is the source’s [z]↦(I−PN)z[z]\mapsto(I-P_N)z. For each nonzero coordinate vector, its class in this relative quotient is nonzero, and its image in the next admitted quotient is zero. At the split level this remains the next supported zero.

For an explicit theta primitive, form pβ−∑|α|≤Npαaα*GNaβ/καp_\beta-\sum_{|\alpha|\le N}p_\alpha a_\alpha^*G_Na_\beta/\kappa_\alpha. Its full jet is zero. Fixed-order monic division expresses it as ∑ih(ti)Qi,β\sum_i h(t_i)Q_{i,\beta}, with degQi,β≤N+1−d\deg Q_{i,\beta}\le N+1-d. In the ii-th cochain factor use the original ϕ*\phi_* in place of FhF_h, and multiply its primitive by (−1)i−1(-1)^{i-1}. The differential supplies the same sign, producing the displayed relation with positive sign. This supplies the actual primitive, not just a dimension count.

4. Both boundary maps now have explicit coordinates

Define the matrix FN:E→ℂΣN+1F_N:E\to\mathbb C^{\Sigma_{N+1}} by its rows

(FN)β=∑i:βi>0aβ−𝐞i*κβ−𝐞i. (F_N)_\beta=\sum_{i:\beta_i>0} \frac{a_{\beta-\mathbf e_i}^*}{\kappa_{\beta-\mathbf e_i}}. \tag{16}

Then

YN=−ℰNHN−1UN*GN,CN=ℰNFNGN. \boxed{Y_N=-\mathscr E_NH_N^{-1}U_N^*G_N, \qquad C_N=\mathscr E_NF_NG_N.} \tag{17}

The first equation is the actual orthogonal projection formula using (14). For the second, multiply the finite expansion (10) by s1+⋯+sks_1+\cdots+s_k. Monicity implies that its degree-N+1N+1 coefficient at pβp_\beta is exactly (FN)βGNu(F_N)_\beta G_Nu. The term RNAuR_NAu has degree at most NN. Subtract ℰNFNGNu\mathscr E_NF_NG_Nu from BNuB_Nu: the result has degree at most NN and zero full jets, hence is in ℒN\mathcal L_N. Projecting by I−PNI-P_N proves the equation.

Consequently the old quotient records

[BNu]ℬ(k)/ℒN=[ℰNFNGNu]ℬ(k)/ℒN. \boxed{[B_Nu]_{\mathscr B^{(k)}/\mathcal L_N} =[\mathscr E_NF_NG_Nu]_{\mathscr B^{(k)}/\mathcal L_N}.} \tag{18}

It becomes the supported zero under the next relation transition. The symbol ℬ(k)\mathscr B^{(k)} denotes the original product test-function space used by the source; no derivative on all of its L2L^2 completion is defined here.

5. The control form is a top-degree full-jet endpoint

Define

VN:=AKN+KNA*−kKN. \boxed{V_N:=AK_N+K_NA^*-kK_N.} \tag{19}

Substituting (17) into the source boundary identity (5), and using ℰN*ℰN=HN\mathscr E_N^*\mathscr E_N=H_N, gives

WN=GN(UNFN+FN*UN*)GN. \boxed{W_N=G_N\bigl(U_NF_N+F_N^*U_N^*\bigr)G_N.} \tag{20}

Meanwhile direct multiplication of (4) by KNK_N on both sides gives KNWNKN=VNK_NW_NK_N=V_N. Thus

VN=UNFN+FN*UN*=∑|α|=N∑i=1kaα+𝐞iaα*+aαaα+𝐞i*κα. \boxed{V_N=U_NF_N+F_N^*U_N^* =\sum_{|\alpha|=N}\sum_{i=1}^k \frac{a_{\alpha+\mathbf e_i}a_\alpha^* +a_\alpha a_{\alpha+\mathbf e_i}^*}{\kappa_\alpha}.} \tag{21}

This is a finite endpoint identity derived through the actual theta relation layer. It does not assume a small boundary, discard mixed terms, or replace the packet operator. It can also be obtained by finite Christoffel–Darboux telescoping. The standard scalar orthogonal-polynomial kernel and telescoping identities are recorded by NIST (n.d., §18.2(v)); the full-jet, original-layer identification (11)–(21) is proved here rather than imported from that scalar formula.

For this alternative telescoping proof, multiplication satisfies spj=pj+1+βjpj−(κj/κj−1)pj−1s p_j=p_{j+1}+\beta_jp_j-(\kappa_j/\kappa_{j-1})p_{j-1}, with Reβj=1/2\operatorname{Re}\beta_j=1/2. The imaginary part need not vanish for the packet-dependent measure. Only βj+βj¯=1\beta_j+\overline{\beta_j}=1 is needed. Interior adjacent terms cancel; (21) is the uncancelled total-degree boundary. The new tests include an asymmetric measure with a nonzero imaginary diagonal coefficient.

Regroup by α\alpha and put bα=∑iaα+𝐞ib_\alpha=\sum_i a_{\alpha+\mathbf e_i}. Equation (21) factors through the two sets of columns (aα)|α|=N(a_\alpha)_{|\alpha|=N} and (bα)|α|=N(b_\alpha)_{|\alpha|=N}. Therefore

rankWN=rankVN≤min{dk,2(N+k−1k−1)}. \boxed{\operatorname{rank}W_N=\operatorname{rank}V_N \le\min\left\{d^k,\;2\binom{N+k-1}{k-1}\right\}.} \tag{22}

This sharpens the source’s general next-layer bound 2(N+kk−1)2\binom{N+k}{k-1} for this product-polynomial construction. It does not change the actual dimension of ℰN\mathcal E_N, which remains (N+kk−1)\binom{N+k}{k-1}. The sharpening comes from the scalar summed derivative and regrouping its top-degree columns. For k=2,d=4,N=6k=2,d=4,N=6, the two structural bounds are 14 and 16 on a 16-dimensional packet; the exact regression tests cover this nonvacuous case. Neither rank bound is a small-eigenvalue estimate.

6. Estimate the same form without a second inversion

Since KNK_N is Hermitian positive definite,

−ϵGN≼WN≼ϵGN⇔−ϵKN≼VN≼ϵKN. \boxed{-\epsilon G_N\preceq W_N\preceq\epsilon G_N \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad -\epsilon K_N\preceq V_N\preceq\epsilon K_N.} \tag{23}

This is congruence by the specified invertible map KNK_N. More explicitly, for u=KNλu=K_N\lambda, u*GNu=λ*KNλu^*G_Nu=\lambda^*K_N\lambda and u*WNu=λ*VNλu^*W_Nu=\lambda^*V_N\lambda. This identifies the generalized Rayleigh quotients, including multiplicities of generalized eigenvalues. The original generator AA is not replaced.

The source’s same-metric reflection therefore still transfers an upper estimate to a lower estimate for precisely the original GNG_N. Under the natural weight-kk antidual action AD=kI−A*A^{\mathrm D}=kI-A^*, the defect of the antidual metric KNK_N is −VN-V_N, not VNV_N. The dual sign and weight are both retained.

The reflection map itself can be retained explicitly. If the source involution is j(u)=Cu¯j(u)=C\overline u, its antidual involution is j#(λ)=CTλ¯j^\#(\lambda)=C^T\overline\lambda. From CC¯=IC\overline C=I and C*GNC=GN¯C^*G_NC=\overline{G_N}, inversion gives (CT)*KNCT=KN¯(C^T)^*K_NC^T=\overline{K_N}. The control identity transports to (CT)*VNCT=−VN¯(C^T)^*V_NC^T=-\overline{V_N}. Thus the two-sided equivalence retains the actual involution, not only spectral symmetry.

Equation (23) avoids the second inversion GN=KN−1G_N=K_N^{-1} in the final inequality test. It does not eliminate the need to enclose moments, full-jet coefficients, or the conditioning of the first construction of KNK_N.

A deterministic finite certificate can therefore work directly with K̂,Â\widehat K,\widehat A and error bounds ∥KN−K̂∥≤ηK\|K_N-\widehat K\|\le\eta_K, ∥A−Â∥≤ηA\|A-\widehat A\|\le\eta_A, where K̂\widehat K is Hermitian. Put

V̂=ÂK̂+K̂Â*−kK̂,ηV=(2∥Â∥+k)ηK+2ηA(∥K̂∥+ηK). \widehat V=\widehat A\widehat K+\widehat K\widehat A^*-k\widehat K, \qquad \eta_V=(2\|\widehat A\|+k)\eta_K +2\eta_A(\|\widehat K\|+\eta_K). \tag{24}

Expanding both perturbations proves ∥VN−V̂∥≤ηV\|V_N-\widehat V\|\le\eta_V. Sufficient conditions, for ϵ≥0\epsilon\ge0, are

K̂−ηKI≻0,ϵK̂±V̂−(ϵηK+ηV)I≽0. \widehat K-\eta_KI\succ0,\qquad \epsilon\widehat K\pm\widehat V -(\epsilon\eta_K+\eta_V)I\succeq0. \tag{25}

No enclosure of the actual infinite arithmetic moments is asserted in this supplement.

7. The metric update and the univariate connection

The positive update from (9) and its inverse are

KN+1=KN+UNDN−1UN*, \boxed{K_{N+1}=K_N+U_ND_N^{-1}U_N^*,} \tag{26} GN+1=GN−GNUN(DN+UN*GNUN)−1UN*GN. \boxed{G_{N+1}=G_N-G_NU_N(D_N+U_N^*G_NU_N)^{-1}U_N^*G_N.} \tag{27}

Equation (27) follows either by direct multiplication with (26), or from RN+1=RN−YNR_{N+1}=R_N-Y_N and the orthogonal decomposition. The middle matrix is exactly the actual layer Gram matrix HNH_N in (14), not an unrelated auxiliary matrix. The lost Gram form is YN*YNY_N^*Y_N.

For k=1k=1 and N=n+dN=n+d, (11) recovers the earlier monic theta vector:

en+d+1=un+1,𝔥n+1=κn+d+1(h)+an+d+1*Gh,nan+d+1. \boxed{e_{n+d+1}=u_{n+1},\qquad \mathfrak h_{n+1}=\kappa_{n+d+1}^{(h)} +a_{n+d+1}^*G_{h,n}a_{n+d+1}.} \tag{28}

Indeed the numerator of (11) is monic of degree n+d+1n+d+1, divisible by monic hh, and its quotient has degree n+1n+1 and leading coefficient one. Applying 𝒯h\mathcal T_h gives fn+1f_{n+1} plus an element of LnL_n, orthogonal to LnL_n. This uniquely identifies un+1u_{n+1}. Thus the packet-dependent polynomial construction is connected to the packet-independent original theta norm by an exact identity; those two norm families were not conflated.

8. Degree recursion and the remaining estimate

For Aj=ajaj*/κjA_j=a_ja_j^*/\kappa_j, define finite matrix sums

Qk,m=∑|α|=m⨂iAαi,Qk,m=∑j=0mAj⊗Qk−1,m−j,Kk,N=∑m=0NQk,m. Q_{k,m}=\sum_{|\alpha|=m}\bigotimes_i A_{\alpha_i},\qquad Q_{k,m}=\sum_{j=0}^m A_j\otimes Q_{k-1,m-j},\qquad K_{k,N}=\sum_{m=0}^NQ_{k,m}. \tag{29}

These are finite degree convolutions; they do not assert convergence of an infinite generating series. They compute the product full-jet interpolation matrix using one-variable polynomial norms and jets, without inverting a total-degree moment matrix of dimension (N+kk)\binom{N+k}{k}. The target dimension remains dkd^k; no polynomial-time complexity or unconditional numerical stability is claimed.

In univariate packet inclusions, the source’s exact equality RH,nιhH=Rh,nR_{H,n}\iota_{hH}=R_{h,n} remains in force. The packet-dependent polynomial bases used here need not themselves commute under inclusion. No extension of this univariate equality to different joint total-degree budgets is presumed.

Finally, on Av=kρvAv=k\rho v, with v=v0⊗kv=v_0^{\otimes k}, and λ=GNv\lambda=G_Nv,

λ*VNλλ*KNλ=k(2Reρ−1). \frac{\lambda^*V_N\lambda}{\lambda^*K_N\lambda} =k(2\operatorname{Re}\rho-1). \tag{30}

The additional work is therefore the explicit layer basis, derivative-coordinate map, endpoint identity, and direct relative certificate for the same source-controlled object. It is not a proof of a uniform sublinear excess. No arithmetic block or nilpotent jet has been removed.

Verification and literature scope

The independent new checker contains eighteen exact test methods. They cover the kernel basis change, repeated jets, two-variable layers, actual derivative-class coordinates, Gram updates, the original univariate theta vector, endpoint factorization, a nonvacuous improved rank bound, antidual signs, generalized characteristic polynomials, nonzero off-line polynomial model diagnostics, nonreal recurrence diagonals, fixed-order theta primitives, and deterministic matrix perturbation certificates. Normal and optimized result records agree. Both intentional-failure modes return failure.

These are finite regression tests, not proofs of the general analytic inputs and not Lean execution. The general finite identities have the written proofs above. The source’s nineteen tests and this supplement’s eighteen tests are separate suites, not counts of novel theorems.

The matrix-kernel and Christoffel–Darboux methods have established antecedents. NIST’s general orthogonal-polynomial formulas supply the standard scalar comparison. Lasserre and Pauwels (2016) connect inverse moment matrices with orthogonal polynomials and the Christoffel function; only their abstract was examined here, so no theorem from that paper is used as a premise. No global priority claim is made for standard kernel or inverse-update identities.
