\section{An actual proper source label with a finite spectral kernel}
\label{sec:proper-label-spectral-kernel}

The full-source calculation (BK.18) applies to
$Q=\mathscr B/\Theta V$. It does not assert the same result for
$Q_W=\mathscr B/\Theta W$ at an arbitrary proper, $D$-invariant
source label $W\subset V$. The following construction proves the
distinction and its exact transition map on the original source
functions. It does not replace $g=2\xi$ by a model function and does
not assume an off-critical zero.

Retain $A=\mathbb C[t]$, $t=D=-x\partial_x$, and the original
source vector and its literal dilation
\[
 \phi_*(x)=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2},
 \qquad \psi(x)=\phi_*(2x).
 \tag{PL.1}
\]
Both functions belong to $V$: dilation preserves evenness, Schwartz
decay, and value zero at the origin, while
$\int_{\mathbb R}\psi(x)\,dx=\tfrac12\int_{\mathbb R}\phi_*(y)\,dy=0$.
The change of variable $y=2x$ in the original positive Mellin
integral gives
\[
 M_+\psi(s)=2^{-s}M_+\phi_*(s),\qquad
 H_\psi(s)=2^{-s},\qquad
 \mathcal M\Theta\psi(s)=g(s)2^{-s}.
 \tag{PL.2}
\]
The first equality holds in its initial half-plane of absolute
convergence; the entire continuation of $H$ proved in (BK.1)--(BK.3)
extends the second equality to the full plane. Here
$2^{-s}=\exp(-s\log2)$ uses the positive real logarithm of the
actual dilation constant $2$.

Define the original invariant source label
\[
 W=A\phi_*+A\psi\subset V.
 \tag{PL.3}
\]
This is a free rank-two $A$-module on the two displayed vectors.
Indeed an equality $P(D)\phi_*+Q(D)\psi=0$, for polynomials
$P,Q\in\mathbb C[t]$, implies by (BK.2) and (PL.2)
\[
 P(s)+Q(s)2^{-s}=0\quad(s\in\mathbb C).
\]
As the real variable $s$ tends to positive infinity,
$Q(s)2^{-s}$ tends to zero. Thus $P(s)$ tends to zero. A nonzero
complex polynomial cannot tend to zero along that ray: division
by its highest real power gives its nonzero leading coefficient.
Consequently $P=0$, and the nonvanishing exponential forces $Q=0$.
This also proves the exponential-polynomial independence used below.

Fix any $\rho\in\mathbb C$ and retain its exact nonzero scalar
$c_\rho=2^{-\rho}$. Set
\[
 \phi_\rho=\psi-c_\rho\phi_*\in W,
 \qquad H_{\phi_\rho}(s)=2^{-s}-c_\rho.
 \tag{PL.4}
\]
The last entire function vanishes at $s=\rho$. The already-proved
full-plane inverse (BK.4), including its endpoint and Fourier-reflected
cases, therefore gives the unique actual vector
\[
 \chi_\rho=S^V_\rho\phi_\rho\in V,
 \qquad (D-\rho)\chi_\rho=\phi_\rho,
 \qquad
 H_{\chi_\rho}(s)=\frac{2^{-s}-2^{-\rho}}{s-\rho}.
 \tag{PL.5}
\]
Its displayed quotient is entire with its retained value
$H_{\chi_\rho}(\rho)=-(\log2)2^{-\rho}\ne0$.
The vector is specified without an implicit choice: for
$\operatorname{Re}\rho>0$ its positive-half-line formula is
\[
 \chi_\rho(x)=x^{-\rho}\int_x^\infty
              \phi_\rho(y)y^{\rho-1}\,dy
          =-\int_0^1u^{\rho-1}\phi_\rho(xu)\,du,
 \tag{PL.6}
\]
extended evenly; for $\operatorname{Re}\rho\le0$ it is
$-\widehat{S_{1-\rho}\widehat{\phi_\rho}}$, with the same
positive-half-line integral for $S_{1-\rho}$. The proof in (BK.4)--(BK.7)
establishes all original source conditions for these formulas.

The vector $\chi_\rho$ is not in $W$. Suppose otherwise and write
$\chi_\rho=P(D)\phi_*+Q(D)\psi$. Multiplying the equality of their
$H$ transforms by $s-\rho$ gives the entire identity
\[
 \bigl((s-\rho)P(s)+2^{-\rho}\bigr)
   +\bigl((s-\rho)Q(s)-1\bigr)2^{-s}=0.
 \tag{PL.7}
\]
The proved exponential-polynomial independence makes both polynomial
coefficients identically zero. Evaluating the first at $s=\rho$
would give $2^{-\rho}=0$, a contradiction. This proves
$\chi_\rho\notin W$, so in particular $W\ne V$.

Now take the actual theta representative and its original supported
quotient class:
\[
 F_\rho=\Theta\chi_\rho\in\mathscr B,
 \qquad q_\rho=[F_\rho]_W\in Q_W=\mathscr B/\Theta W.
 \tag{PL.8}
\]
It is nonzero because $F_\rho=\Theta w$, $w\in W$, would imply
$\chi_\rho=w$ by injectivity of the original theta map. Nevertheless
\[
 (t-\rho)q_\rho=0,
 \qquad
 \mathcal MF_\rho(s)
       =g(s)\frac{2^{-s}-2^{-\rho}}{s-\rho}.
 \tag{PL.9}
\]
The first equality follows from
$(D-\rho)F_\rho=\Theta\phi_\rho\in\Theta W$; the second is the
original identity $\mathcal M\Theta=gH$ applied to $\chi_\rho$.
In fact $Aq_\rho\cong A/(t-\rho)$. For any polynomial $P$,
division by $t-\rho$ gives $P(t)q_\rho=P(\rho)q_\rho$.
Since $q_\rho\ne0$, this vanishes exactly when $P(\rho)=0$.

Let $O=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C,\rho}$, $z=s-\rho$, and retain
the actual proper-label comparison
\[
 M_W=O\otimes_AQ_W,\qquad
 b_W:M_W\longrightarrow O/(g),\qquad
 b_W(a\otimes[F]_W)=[a\mathcal MF],\qquad K_W=\ker b_W.
 \tag{PL.10}
\]
It is well-defined because $\mathcal M\Theta w=gH_w$ for
every $w\in W$; it is balanced because $\mathcal MDF=s\mathcal MF$.
It is onto by the original $F_0$ and its invertible transform $H_0$
in (BK.13): $b_W(aH_0^{-1}\otimes[F_0]_W)=[a]$.
The inclusion $Aq_\rho\hookrightarrow Q_W$ remains injective after
tensoring with the flat $A$-module $O$. Since
\[
 O\otimes_AAq_\rho\cong O/(z)\ne0,
\]
the element $k_\rho=1\otimes q_\rho$ is nonzero in $M_W$.
Its original image (PL.9) is divisible by $g$, giving
\[
 0\ne k_\rho\in K_W,\qquad zk_\rho=0.
 \tag{PL.11}
\]
Thus $K_W$ has a nonzero one-dimensional spectral submodule on an
actual proper source label containing the original arithmetic generator
$\phi_*$. Invariance of $W$ and inclusion of that generator do not
imply the polynomial-invertibility theorem for $K_W$.

We record the exact morphism that transports this class to the
proved full-source comparison. Inclusion of source labels gives
\[
 0\longrightarrow V/W
 \xrightarrow{\ [v]\mapsto[\Theta v]_W\ }
 Q_W\xrightarrow{\ [F]_W\mapsto[F]_V\ }Q
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{PL.12}
\]
The first arrow is injective by theta injectivity. The final map
is onto, and its kernel consists exactly of those representatives
in $\Theta V$, modulo $\Theta W$. These statements prove exactness
and identify its kernel with the original $V/W$, without changing
the source quotient. The arrows commute with $D$ because theta
does. Flatness therefore gives the actual balanced exact sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow O\otimes_A(V/W)\longrightarrow M_W
       \xrightarrow{\pi}M\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad M=O\otimes_AQ,
 \qquad b_W=b\pi.
 \tag{PL.13}
\]
Restricting $\pi$ to the indicated kernels gives
\[
 0\longrightarrow O\otimes_A(V/W)\longrightarrow K_W
       \xrightarrow{\pi}K\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{PL.14}
\]
Indeed the first term is already in $K_W$ since its Mellin values
are multiples of $g$. Every element of $K$ has a lift in $M_W$
by (PL.13), and every such lift is in $K_W$ because $b_W=b\pi$.
This proves surjectivity of the last arrow and identifies its kernel.
The explicit $k_\rho$ is the image of $1\otimes[\chi_\rho]$ in
the left term of (PL.14), and its image in $K$ is exactly zero.
Its finite spectral contribution is therefore removed by the
specified enlargement from $W$ to $V$. It does not contradict
the polynomial invertibility of the full-source $K$.

This difference is also detected by the finite derived observations.
Tensor the free resolution $[O\xrightarrow{z}O]$ with the proper-label
exact sequence $0\to K_W\to M_W\xrightarrow{b_W}O/(g)\to0$.
It produces the short exact sequence of complexes
\[
 0\to[K_W\xrightarrow{z}K_W]\to[M_W\xrightarrow{z}M_W]
       \to[O/(g)\xrightarrow{z}O/(g)]\to0.
 \tag{PL.15}
\]
The first complex has nonzero degree-minus-one cohomology, since
$k_\rho\in\ker(z:K_W\to K_W)\setminus\{0\}$. Equivalently the
degree-minus-one map on the last two complexes has the nonzero
kernel containing $k_\rho$. Thus $b_W\otimes_O^{\mathbf L}O/(z)$
is not a quasi-isomorphism. The finite derived comparison (DP.2)
is therefore a theorem for its stated full-source $Q$ and its
specified copies in the supported construction, not for arbitrary
proper source labels.

Every element above retains its original label. The source-label
transport carries $[\Theta\chi_\rho]_W$ to the supported zero in
the $V$ fibre; it does not send that element to the external absence
point $\tau$. Equations (PL.12)--(PL.15) prove the exact relation
between the proper-label finite spectral kernel and the full-source
meromorphic kernel, including the class that the transition kills.
Nothing in the construction asserts $g(\rho)=0$; it works at every
specified $\rho$. If $\rho$ is an actual off-critical zero, its
arithmetic block in $O/(g)$ remains the one computed in (DP.4),
and the proper-label class (PL.11) is the separately computed
kernel of the displayed source-label transition.
