\section{The closest unitary preserving the actual arithmetic observation}
\label{sec:vr-polar-repair}

The source isometry in AW9--AW10 has a unique closest unitary preserving the original arithmetic observation. This calculation constructs that unitary and proves its exact source-metric cost. It also computes the coefficient defect of the triangular relation-preserving repair and the positive triangular quotient block forced by the prescribed Gram--Schmidt phases. Every formula retains the original polynomial, arithmetic measure, Taylor unit, relation quotient, and absolute \(\tau\) base.

\subsection{Original objects and forms}

Fix \(g=2\xi\), its actual full packet \(h\), the full coefficient algebra \(E_h=\mathbb C[s]/(h)\), a positive integer \(k\), the exact monic cyclic polynomial \(\chi=\chi_{h,k}\) of degree \(q\ge1\), and \(t\in[0,1]\). Retain
\[
 H=\mathbb C[S]_{\le2q},\qquad E=\mathbb C[S]/(\chi),\qquad
 J=J_{2q}:H\longrightarrow E.
\]
Here \(J\) is literal remainder in the original increasing-power frame. The original arithmetic observation is
\[
 \mathcal O=\iota J,\qquad \iota=\sigma_h^{\otimes k}\eta,\qquad
 \eta[P]=\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}P(A_h^{(k)})1,\qquad
 \upsilon_h=j_h(g/h)\in E_h^\times.
\]
Both \(\eta\) and \(\iota\) are the original injections; thus \(\ker\mathcal O=\ker J\). Every Taylor coefficient of the unit remains present.

Write \(v_{2q}(u)=(1,S(u),\ldots,S(u)^{2q})\). Give \(H\) its actual form
\[
 \langle x,y\rangle_t=x^*M(t)y,\qquad
 M(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}v_{2q}(u)^*v_{2q}(u)m_t(u)\,du,\quad
 S=k/2+iu,
\]
\[
 m_t=(1-t)m_0+tm_1,\quad m_1=w_h^{*k},\quad
 w_h(u)=\frac{|(g/h)(1/2+iu)|^2}{2\pi},
\]
\[
 m_0(u)=\frac{(\sqrt{2\pi})^k}{c_{k/4}}
 \frac{|\Gamma(k/4+iu/2)|^2}{2\pi},\qquad
 c_a=2^{1-2a}\Gamma(2a).
\]
With \(\mu_h=\int_{\mathbb R}w_h(u)\,du\), the original masses are \(\mu_h^k\) and \((2\pi)^{k/2}\). Adjoints denoted star below use this form and the induced subspace forms. In the fixed coefficient frame,
\(Z^\star=M^{-1}Z^*M\). Define
\(\|Z\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2=\operatorname{Tr}(Z^\star Z)\).

Set
\[
 F=\chi\mathbb C[S]_{\le q}=\ker J,\quad K=F^\perp,\quad
 L=\mathbb C[S]_{\le q-1},\quad Q=Q_F,\quad \Pi=I-Q.
 \tag{VR1}
\]
Here \(Q_F\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(F\) in the actual form. Their dimensions are \(q+1,q,q\) respectively. Polynomial division proves \(H=L\oplus F\) algebraically: their intersection is zero by degree and their dimensions add to \(2q+1\). No orthogonality is assigned to that direct sum.

Let \(s:E\to L\) be the remainder section, \(R=R_{2q}:E\to K\) the original minimum section, and \(G=G_{2q}=R^*MR\). AT6 gives
\[
 JR=I_E,\qquad RJ=\Pi,\qquad R^*Mp=GJp\quad(p\in H).
 \tag{VR2}
\]
Indeed \(RJ\) and \(\Pi\) have image \(K\), kernel \(F\), and equal the identity on \(K\). For the last identity decompose \(p=RJp+Qp\) and use \(R(E)\perp F\). Thus \(R\) is an isometry from the original \(G\)-space \(E\) to \(K\). Write \(G_0=s^*Ms=G_{q-1}\); this subscript denotes the low section, not parameter value zero.

The prescribed AW isometry satisfies
\[
 T^\star T=TT^\star=I,\quad TL=K,\quad TL^\perp=F,\quad
 U=TWT^\star,
 \tag{VR3}
\]
with exactly the original window operators \(U,W\). Relative to \(H=K\oplus F\) set
\[
 T=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\ C&D\end{pmatrix},\quad
 A=\Pi T|_K,\ B=\Pi T|_F,\ C=QT|_K,\ D=QT|_F.
 \tag{VR4}
\]
Here \(C\) is the block \(K\to F\), not the derivative operator of AT/AW.

\subsection{Invertible blocks and strict leakage}

Multiplication of the two unitarity identities gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A^\star A+C^\star C&=I_K,&AA^\star+BB^\star&=I_K,\\
 D^\star D+B^\star B&=I_F,&DD^\star+CC^\star&=I_F,\\
 A^\star B+C^\star D&=0,&AC^\star+BD^\star&=0.
 \end{aligned} \tag{VR5}
\]
Both \(A,D\) are invertible. If \(Ax=0\) with \(x\in K\), then \(Tx\in F\), so
\(x\in K\cap T^{-1}F=F^\perp\cap L^\perp=(F+L)^\perp=0\).
If \(Dy=0\) with \(y\in F\), then \(Ty\in K\), so
\(y\in F\cap T^{-1}K=F\cap L=0\).
Both maps are square maps on their displayed spaces, proving invertibility.

Their singular values lie in \((0,1]\). Define \(|A|=(A^\star A)^{1/2}\) and \(\operatorname{polar}(A)=A|A|^{-1}\); both are defined because \(A\) is invertible. Taking traces in VR5 gives
\[
 \ell:=\|B\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
 =\|C\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
 =q-\operatorname{Tr}(A^\star A).
 \tag{VR6}
\]
In fact \(\ell>0\) on the actual source. For
\(\chi(S)=\sum_{j=0}^q a_jS^j\), define
\(\chi^\dagger(S)=\sum_{j=0}^q\overline{a_j}(k-S)^j\).
Then \(v=\chi\chi^\dagger\in F\) and
\[
 \langle1,v\rangle_t
 =\int_{\mathbb R}|\chi(k/2+iu)|^2m_t(u)\,du>0.
 \tag{VR7}
\]
On the unchanged line \(k-S=\overline S\), proving the equality. The moment bounds give finiteness; the nonzero polynomial \(\chi\) has only finitely many zeros on that line, and \(m_t\) is positive almost everywhere, giving strict positivity. Thus \(1\in L\) is not in \(K\), so \(K\ne L\). If \(\ell=0\) then \(B=0\), so \(T(F)=F\) by equal finite dimensions. But \(T^{-1}F=L^\perp\) by VR3; hence \(F=L^\perp\) and \(K=L\), a contradiction.

If \(s_1,\ldots,s_q\) are all singular values of \(A\), those of \(D\) are exactly
\[
 1,s_1,\ldots,s_q. \tag{VR8}
\]
For any \(\lambda>0\) the maps \(B^\star\) and \(\lambda^{-1}B\) identify the \(\lambda\)-eigenspaces of \(BB^\star\) and \(B^\star B\). Their nonzero spectra therefore coincide, including multiplicities; the respective dimensions add one zero eigenvalue to the latter. Subtracting from one in
\(AA^\star=I-BB^\star\) and \(D^\star D=I-B^\star B\), then taking positive square roots, proves VR8, including rank-deficient \(B\).

\subsection{Closest repairs}

The unique closest linear map to \(T\) under the exact constraint \(JX=J\) is
\[
 X=QT+\Pi=\begin{pmatrix}I_K&0\\C&D\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{VR9}
\]
Indeed \(JY=J\) is equivalent, by multiplication by \(R\), to \(\Pi Y=\Pi\). It fixes the upper blocks of \(Y\) to \(I_K,0\) and leaves its lower blocks free. The four block spaces are orthogonal in Hilbert--Schmidt inner product, so the unique minimizers of the remaining block errors are \(C,D\). Thus
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 d_{\rm lin}^2
 &:=\min_{JY=J}\|Y-T\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2\\
 &=\|I_K-A\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2+\|B\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
 =2q-2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}A .
 \end{aligned} \tag{VR10}
\]
The last equality expands the square and uses \(AA^\star+BB^\star=I_K\).

The complete class of exact-observation-preserving unitaries is
\[
 \{V:V^\star V=VV^\star=I,\ JV=J\}
 =\{I_K\oplus V_F:V_F\text{ unitary on }F\}. \tag{VR11}
\]
For \(JV=J\) implies \(V(F)\subset F\), hence \(V(F)=F\). Unitarity then preserves \(K\). For \(x\in K\), \(Vx-x\in K\) and \(J(Vx-x)=0\), hence \(Vx=x\). Conversely every displayed block map has \(JV=J\).

Since \(D\) is invertible, \(|D|=(D^\star D)^{1/2}\) is positive definite and \(V_D=D|D|^{-1}\) is unitary on \(F\). The unique closest member of VR11 is
\[
 V_{\rm obs}=\Pi+V_DQ=I_K\oplus V_D. \tag{VR12}
\]
Only the lower diagonal block varies in the distance, so one maximizes
\(\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(V_F^\star D)\). Write \(Z=V_F^\star V_D\), a unitary, and diagonalize \(|D|\) in an orthonormal basis with positive eigenvalues \(d_j\). The expression is \(\sum_jd_j\operatorname{Re}Z_{jj}\le\sum_jd_j\). Equality forces \(Z_{jj}=1\) for every \(j\). The unit length of each column then makes its other entries zero, so \(Z=I\). This proves attainment and uniqueness.

The exact minimum is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 d_{\rm unit}^2
 &:=\min_{\substack{V^\star V=I\\JV=J}}\|V-T\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2\\
 &=d_{\rm lin}^2+\|C\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
                    +\|V_D-D\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2\\
 &=d_{\rm lin}^2+2\operatorname{Tr}_F(I_F-|D|)\\
 &=2q-2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}A
       +2q-2\operatorname{Tr}_K|A|.
 \end{aligned} \tag{VR13}
\]
The second line adds orthogonal block errors beyond \(X\). For the third,
\(\|C\|^2=\operatorname{Tr}(I_F-|D|^2)\) and
\(\|V_D-D\|^2=\operatorname{Tr}((I_F-|D|)^2)\).
Adding these polynomials gives the stated expression; VR8 gives the last line. Consequently
\[
 \ell\le d_{\rm unit}^2-d_{\rm lin}^2
 =2\sum_{j=1}^q(1-s_j)\le2\ell,\qquad
 d_{\rm unit}^2\ge2\ell>0. \tag{VR14}
\]
Each inequality follows termwise from
\(1-s_j^2\le2(1-s_j)\le2(1-s_j^2)\), with
\(\ell=\sum_j(1-s_j^2)\), and from \(d_{\rm lin}^2\ge\ell\).

If only preservation of \(F\) is imposed on the unitary, its two diagonal blocks may vary independently. The same positive-weight trace argument proves that the unique closest one is
\[
 V_{\rm rel}=\operatorname{polar}(A)\oplus\operatorname{polar}(D),\qquad
 \|V_{\rm rel}-T\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
 =4(q-\operatorname{Tr}|A|). \tag{VR15}
\]
Its quotient action is the unitary corresponding to \(\operatorname{polar}(A)\), not necessarily the identity. The exact extra cost of the entire original observation is
\[
 d_{\rm unit}^2-\|V_{\rm rel}-T\|_{\mathrm{HS},t}^2
 =2(\operatorname{Tr}|A|-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}A)\ge0.
 \tag{VR16}
\]
The last inequality follows from the preceding trace maximization with the identity as a candidate unitary against \(A\).

\subsection{AW's specified phases and a determinant bound for the repair cost}

This calculation uses the prescribed Gram--Schmidt recursion. Write the positive definite original Grams as
\[
 G_0=L_0^*L_0,\qquad G=L_G^*L_G, \tag{VR17}
\]
with upper triangular Cholesky factors having positive real diagonals. These factors follow directly by the Gram--Schmidt recursion on the ordered remainder monomials: the change matrix is upper triangular with positive diagonal and its orthonormality equation gives VR17. The successive positive norms uniquely determine every entry, proving uniqueness as well.

The prescribed orthonormal frames of \(L\) and \(K\) are
\[
 p=sL_0^{-1}:\mathbb C^q\longrightarrow L,\qquad
 c=RL_G^{-1}:\mathbb C^q\longrightarrow K. \tag{VR18}
\]
The preceding triangular uniqueness verifies every Gram--Schmidt phase, and AW10 gives \(Tp=c\).

Let \(a=JTR:E\to E\). The matrix of the block \(A\) in the \(K\)-orthonormal frame \(c\) is
\[
 A_c=c^\star Tc=p^\star c
 =L_0^{-*}G L_G^{-1}
 =L_0^{-*}L_G^*. \tag{VR19}
\]
Here \(c^\star T=p^\star\) follows from \(T^\star c=p\), and \(s^*MR=G\) follows from VR2. Thus \(A_c\) is lower triangular with positive real diagonal entries
\[
 \lambda_j=(L_G)_{jj}/(L_0)_{jj}\in(0,1]\quad(1\le j\le q).
 \tag{VR20}
\]
Their upper bound follows because they are eigenvalues of the contraction \(A\). Hence \(\operatorname{Tr}A\) is positive real, and
\[
 \det a=\det A_c
 =\frac{\det L_G}{\det L_0}
 =\sqrt{\frac{\det G}{\det G_0}}>0. \tag{VR21}
\]
The square root is positive, forced by the actual AW phases. Since \(L_G\) identifies the original remainder frame with the \(c\)-frame, its full original-frame matrix is
\[
 a=L_G^{-1}A_cL_G=L_G^{-1}L_0^{-*}G. \tag{VR22}
\]

Define the actual two-endpoint quantity
\[
 \beta=\log\frac{\det G_0}{\det G}
       =\log\frac{V_{q-1}(t)}{V_{2q}(t)}. \tag{VR23}
\]
It is strictly positive. Indeed least-norm minimization gives \(G\le G_0\) and
\(G_0-G=s^*MQs\). Equality would imply \(Qs=0\), hence \(L=K\), contradicting VR7. Thus \(G_0^{-1/2}GG_0^{-1/2}\) has all eigenvalues in \((0,1]\) with at least one smaller than one; its determinant is strictly smaller than one.

The positive \(\lambda_j\) have product \(e^{-\beta/2}\), as do the positive singular values \(s_j\). The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives
\(\operatorname{Tr}A\ge q e^{-\beta/(2q)}\) and
\(\operatorname{Tr}|A|\ge q e^{-\beta/(2q)}\).
To recall a proof without an extra hypothesis, among nonnegative \(q\)-tuples \((z_1,\ldots,z_q)\) with a fixed sum the product has a maximum by compactness. Since the target product is positive, zero entries are excluded at the maximum. Replacing two unequal entries \(x,y\) by their common mean increases their product by \((x-y)^2/4\) while preserving the other factors and the sum. Thus every maximizing entry is equal, proving \(\prod_{j=1}^q z_j\le(q^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^q z_j)^q\). Applying this to the two actual positive lists proves
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 0<d_{\rm lin}^2
 &=2(q-\operatorname{Tr}A)
 \le2q(1-e^{-\beta/(2q)}),\\
 2\ell\le d_{\rm unit}^2
 &=4q-2\operatorname{Tr}A-2\operatorname{Tr}|A|
 \le4q(1-e^{-\beta/(2q)}).
 \end{aligned} \tag{VR24}
\]
These estimates retain the prescribed AW phase. An abstract unitary intertwiner with arbitrary phase on its zero eigenspace could change \(\operatorname{Tr}A\) while retaining its singular values; VR19 removes that ambiguity for the actual \(T\).

For \(q=1\), \(A\) is the positive scalar \(\sqrt{G/G_0}\), and the formulas reduce exactly to
\(d_{\rm lin}^2=2(1-\sqrt{G/G_0})\) and
\(d_{\rm unit}^2=4(1-\sqrt{G/G_0})\), retaining the original norms in AW19.

\subsection{Full coefficient action and the exact defect}

For \(f\in E\) let \(m_f:E\to E\) be multiplication by the full residue class \(f\), including all nilpotents. The original full-jet action obeys
\[
 \eta m_f=f(A_h^{(k)})\eta. \tag{VR25}
\]
Both sides on \([P]\) equal the full coefficient
\(\upsilon_h^{\otimes k} f(A_h^{(k)})P(A_h^{(k)})1\);
the Taylor unit commutes with these polynomial actions in the same commutative tensor coefficient algebra. The annihilating relation \(\chi\) makes the expression independent of representatives.

The relation-preserving triangular repair
\[
 Y_{\rm rel}=T-\Pi TQ=\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\C&D\end{pmatrix}
 \tag{VR26}
\]
is invertible because \(A\),\(D\) are. It has the exact quotient and arithmetic actions
\[
 JY_{\rm rel}=aJ,\qquad
 \mathcal OY_{\rm rel}=\iota aJ. \tag{VR27}
\]
Its value on \(R(E)\) is \(TR\), so its induced quotient map is \(a=JTR\); its value on \(F\) stays in \(F\). This proves the first identity on the direct sum \(R(E)\oplus F\), and the second follows by \(\iota\).

The coefficient-equivariance defect is exactly
\[
 \eta(am_f-m_fa)
 =\eta a m_f-f(A_h^{(k)})\eta a. \tag{VR28}
\]
It vanishes for every \(f\) if and only if \(a\) is multiplication by \(a(1)\): commutation gives \(a(f)=a(m_f1)=m_fa(1)=fa(1)\), and multiplication by a fixed element conversely commutes with every \(m_f\). Since \(E\) is generated by \([S]\), it is equivalent to the single matrix identity
\[
 a m_{[S]}-m_{[S]}a=0. \tag{VR29}
\]
Commutation with the generator implies commutation with its powers by induction and with every polynomial by linearity. Thus VR22 and the literal full companion multiplication matrix compute the entire defect, without replacing \(E\) by its reduced root algebra. No unsupported assertion that this actual commutator vanishes or is nonzero is made.

For a fully typed source formula define
\(\rho_f=Rm_fJ:H\to H\). Then
\[
 J[T,\rho_f]R=am_f-m_fa. \tag{VR30}
\]
This follows by substitution of \(JR=I\). Its identity element acts by \(\rho_1=\Pi\), so this extension by zero is not asserted to be a unital action on all \(H\). On the canonical image \(K\) it is precisely the unital original coefficient action.

The closest observation-preserving unitary satisfies
\[
 JV_{\rm obs}=J,\quad \mathcal OV_{\rm obs}=\mathcal O,\quad
 V_{\rm obs}R=R,\quad
 V_{\rm obs}\rho_f=\rho_fV_{\rm obs}=\rho_f\quad(f\in E).
 \tag{VR31}
\]
Its identity block on \(K\) proves the first three equalities; their substitution in \(Rm_fJ\) proves the last. It therefore preserves the full arithmetic coefficient action on the original canonical quotient, with every nilpotent and the full Taylor unit. There is no claim of commutation for an undefined degree-truncated source multiplication.

The contraction of \(a\) has the exact original \(G\)-energy
\[
 \|x\|_G^2-\|ax\|_G^2
 =\|C R x\|_t^2=\|QT R x\|_t^2\quad(x\in E). \tag{VR32}
\]
Indeed \(\Pi TRx=Rax\) and \(QTRx=CRx\) are orthogonal, \(T\) is an isometry, and \(R\) is the original \(G\)-isometry. Thus, in the original remainder frame,
\[
 G-a^*Ga=R^*T^*MQTR\succeq0,\qquad
 \ell=\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(G^{-1}(G-a^*Ga)\bigr).
 \tag{VR33}
\]
The first identity is the just-proved norm identity with \(Q^\star Q=Q\); the trace equality transports \(C^\star C\) by \(R\).

\subsection{No invertible relation-preserving exact intertwiner}

There is no invertible \(Z:H\to H\) with \(Z(F)\subset F\) and \(UZ=ZW\). For invertibility gives \(Z(F)=F\) and \(Z^{-1}(F)=F\). Since \(\operatorname{im}U=F\) from AW7--AW8,
\(ZW(H)=UZ(H)\subset F\), so \(W(H)\subset Z^{-1}(F)=F\).
But AW7 on the original polynomial flag gives \(\operatorname{im}W=L^\perp\), which has the same dimension \(q+1\) as \(F\). Hence \(F=L^\perp\) and \(K=L\), contradicting VR7. This proof does not assume \(Z\) is unitary.

Consequently exact window intertwining on this actual source cannot coexist with an invertible map preserving the original relation quotient. The explicit VR9, VR12 and VR26 maps instead compute each available correction, its full quotient action, its original-metric energy, and the exact coefficient defect.

At each fixed nonempty support label \(A_0\) all displayed linear maps lift by \((A_0,x)\mapsto(A_0,Zx)\), with external \(\tau\) sent to \(\tau\). Their zero outputs remain supported zero coefficients at \(A_0\). In particular \(\mathcal O V_{\rm obs}=\mathcal O\) preserves the supported arithmetic observation and does not identify a supported zero with the external basepoint.
