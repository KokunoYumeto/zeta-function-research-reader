\section{The single arithmetic carrier and its divisor--tensor gluing}
\label{sec:actual-arithmetic-gluing}

Put $U=\{s\in\mathbb C:0<\Re s<1,\ \Re s\ne1/2\}$.
The original objects are
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 V=\{\phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R):\phi(-x)=\phi(x),\
 \phi(0)=0,\ \int\phi=0\},\\
 \mathscr B=\{F\in C^\infty(\mathbb R_+):
 \sup_{x>0}x^b|D^jF(x)|<\infty\ (b\in\mathbb Z,j\ge0)\},\\
 D=-x\partial_x,
 \end{gathered}
\]
\[
 \Theta\phi(x)=2\sum_{n\ge1}\phi(nx),\qquad
 Q=\mathscr B/\Theta V,\quad q:\mathscr B\to Q,\qquad
 \mathcal MF(s)=\int_0^\infty F(x)x^s\frac{dx}{x}.
\]
The original theta and Euler inverse calculations (A1)--(A9) prove
that $\Theta$ is injective, commutes with $D$, and that every
nonzero polynomial $p(D)$ is injective on $\mathscr B$.  For roots of $p$
in the open strip they also prove
\[
 p(D)\mathscr B=\ker(j_p\mathcal M),\quad
 p(D)V=\ker(j_pH),\quad
 \mathcal M\Theta\phi=gH_\phi,\quad g=2\xi,
 \qquad H_{P(D)\phi_*}=P,
 \tag{AG1}
\]
where $\phi_*=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2}$.
The maps $j_p$ in this formula are the complete Taylor jets, including
every nilpotent coefficient through the indicated multiplicity.
The following construction takes place inside these original test
function spaces.  Their topologies are the displayed Schwartz
topologies and their inherited subspace topologies.

\subsection{A definition that does not presuppose a list of zeros}

Let $\mathcal P_U$ be the multiplicative set of monic polynomials all
of whose roots lie in $U$, including $1$.  Define the actual spaces
\[
 \begin{split}
 T_UQ&=\{u\in Q:p(D)u=0\text{ for some }p\in\mathcal P_U\},\\
 \mathscr B_U&=\{F\in\mathscr B:p(D)F\in\Theta V
                         \text{ for some }p\in\mathcal P_U\},\\
 C_U&=[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B_U]
              \subseteq[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B].
 \end{split}\tag{AG2}
\]
The complex has its original degrees zero and one.  Products of
annihilating polynomials show that both displayed sets are vector
spaces and $D$-modules.  For example, if $p(D)u=0$ and $r(D)v=0$,
then $(pr)(D)(au+bv)=0$; $D$ commutes with both polynomials.
The inverse image under $q$ of $T_UQ$ is exactly $\mathscr B_U$.
Consequently
\[
 H^0(C_U)=0,\qquad H^1(C_U)=T_UQ,
 \quad 0\to V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B_U
                         \xrightarrow{q}T_UQ\to0
 \tag{AG3}
\]
is the actual exact sequence, with the original arrows.

The same original source also contains the exact all-strip comparison.
Put $\Omega=\{0<\Re s<1\}$, $L=\{\Re s=1/2\}\cap\Omega$,
and define $T_XQ$ and $\mathscr B_X=q^{-1}(T_XQ)$ by (AG2)'s
annihilator condition with roots in $X$, for $X=L,\Omega$.
With $C_X=[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B_X]$ and
$C_+=[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B]$ the arrows are
\[
 C_U\hookrightarrow C_\Omega\hookrightarrow C_+,
 \qquad T_\Omega Q=T_UQ\oplus T_LQ.                    \tag{AG3a}
\]
Each cochain inclusion is identity on $V$ and the original inclusion
on the degree-one functions. For the asserted direct sum, factor any
annihilator as $p=p_Up_L$, retaining all multiplicities, and choose
$a p_U+b p_L=1$. Its two components are
$u_U=b(D)p_L(D)u$ and $u_L=a(D)p_U(D)u$. Multiplication proves
$p_U(D)u_U=p_L(D)u_L=0$, and their sum is $u$. A vector in both
submodules is zero by the same Bezout identity. This also proves
independence of the annihilator and Bezout choice.
The resulting exact cochain quotients are
\[
\begin{split}
 0&\to C_U\to C_\Omega\to T_LQ[-1]\to0,\\
 0&\to C_\Omega\to C_+\to(Q/T_\Omega Q)[-1]\to0.
\end{split}                                                   \tag{AG3b}
\]
Their degree-zero quotients are zero. In degree one, $q$ identifies
$\mathscr B_\Omega/\mathscr B_U$ with $T_\Omega Q/T_UQ$:
its kernel condition is exactly $qF\in T_UQ$. The identical proof
gives the second quotient. Thus the later tensor and packet diagrams
retain their actual offcritical subobjects and their critical-line
comparison quotient; they do not replace $C_+$ by $C_U$.
The proof of (AG4)--(AG8) uses only the open-strip range identity
(AG1), and therefore also gives the same full-unit packet maps for
packets in $\Omega$, with target $\mathscr B_\Omega$. Restriction
to packets in $U$ is the literal original inclusion in (AG3a).

Here is its precise finite-packet presentation.  For any $p\in
\mathcal P_U$, the map
\[
 Q[p(D)]\longrightarrow\ker(M_{j_pg}:E_p\to E_p),\quad
 [F]\longmapsto j_pH_\phi,
 \qquad p(D)F=\Theta\phi,
 \quad E_p=\mathbb C[s]/(p),
 \tag{AG4}
\]
is a $\mathbb C[s]$-linear isomorphism.  To check every arrow,
$\phi$ is unique by injectivity of $\Theta$.  Replacing $F$ by
$F+\Theta\psi$ replaces $\phi$ by $\phi+p(D)\psi$, whose
$p$-jet changes by zero by (AG1).  The target condition follows from
$j_p\mathcal M(p(D)F)=0$.  A zero source jet gives
$\phi=p(D)\psi$ by (AG1); injectivity of $p(D)$ then gives
$F=\Theta\psi$.  Conversely a target vector has a polynomial
representative $P$, and $j_p(gP)=0$ allows the unique inverse
$F=S_p^{\mathscr B}\Theta(P(D)\phi_*)$.  Changing $P$ by $pR$
changes this inverse by $\Theta(R(D)\phi_*)$.  These operations
are inverse and commute with $D$, again by its injectivity and (AG1).

At a root $\rho$ of $p$ of order $n$, use the literal coordinate
$z=s-\rho$.  If $g$ has order $m\ge0$ there, its full local jet
has the form $z^m u(z)$, $u(0)\ne0$, with the convention that it
is zero modulo $z^n$ when $m\ge n$.  The inverse of the unit
is the finite geometric expansion of its nilpotent part.  Thus
\[
 \ker(M_g:\mathbb C[z]/z^n\to\mathbb C[z]/z^n)
       =z^{\max(n-m,0)}\mathbb C[z]/z^n.
 \tag{AG5}
\]
This kernel is zero when $m=0$, and is killed by $z^m$ when
$m>0$.  Chinese remainders and (AG4) imply that every $u\in T_UQ$
is killed by the polynomial consisting of the actual roots of $g$
in its finite annihilator, with their full orders.  Adding the
finite orbits under conjugation and $s\mapsto1-s$ still gives an
annihilator whose roots lie in $U$.

Let $\mathcal D_U$ denote these finite invariant full-order packet
polynomials, with $1$ included.  Divisibility makes it a directed
set: the least common multiple is the polynomial on the union of
the two supports, retaining each full order once.  This directed
set is now a presentation of (AG2), rather than its definition.

\subsection{The exact divisor arrows and their arithmetic units}

For $h\in\mathcal D_U$, $h\ne1$, retain
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 E_h=\mathbb C[s]/h,\quad d_h=\deg h,\quad A_h=M_s,\\
 v_h=g/h,\quad \upsilon_h=j_hv_h,\quad
 \varepsilon_h=\upsilon_h^{-1},\\
 F_h=S_h^{\mathscr B}\Theta\phi_*,\quad
 \mathcal T_hP=P(D)F_h.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AG6}
\]
Thus $h(D)F_h=\Theta\phi_*$ and $\mathcal MF_h=v_h$.
The notation $F_h$ denotes this actual inverse function, not an
unconstructed functional calculus.  Complete root orders imply
$v_h(\rho)\ne0$ on the packet, proving the asserted unit property.
Write $r_h$ for the unique remainder representative of degree $<d_h$,
and define
\[
 s_h=\mathcal T_h r_h M_{\varepsilon_h},\qquad
 J_h=j_h\mathcal M,\qquad \sigma_h=qs_h.
 \tag{AG7}
\]
Then $J_hs_h=I$, $J_h\Theta=0$, and
\[
 h(D)s_hu=\Theta(r_h(\varepsilon_hu)(D)\phi_*),\quad
 D s_h-s_hA_h=\Theta\phi_*\ell_h,
 \qquad\ell_h(u)=[s^{d_h-1}]r_h(\varepsilon_hu).
 \tag{AG8}
\]
For the second identity, the difference
$s r_h(\varepsilon_hu)-r_h(\varepsilon_h A_hu)$ is divisible
by $h$, has degree at most $d_h$, and has quotient exactly
$\ell_h(u)$.  Applying $\mathcal T_h$ proves it.

At this same source vertex the distinction between the section and
its strict polynomial-linear realization has the following exact map.
Write $A_{\rm sp}=\mathbb C[s]$, let $s$ act on the original functions
by $D$, and put $e_h=r_h(\varepsilon_h)$. The section $s_h$ is not
$A_{\rm sp}$-linear for a nonempty packet: on $u=\upsilon_h$,
$s_hu=F_h$ and $h(D)F_h=\Theta\phi_*\ne0$, whereas $hu=0$.
Define instead the actual free complex and its cochain map
\[
\begin{gathered}
 P_h^{\rm free}=[A_{\rm sp}\xrightarrow{h}A_{\rm sp}],\qquad
 \kappa_h:P_h^{\rm free}\longrightarrow C_U,\\
 \kappa_h^0(P)=P(D)e_h(D)\phi_*,\qquad
 \kappa_h^1(P)=P(D)e_h(D)F_h .
\end{gathered}                                                   \tag{AG8a}
\]
The complex has degrees zero and one. Both components commute with
the polynomial action and
$\kappa_h^1(hP)=\Theta\kappa_h^0(P)$ by the defining equation
for $F_h$. For every $P\in A_{\rm sp}$ the exact comparison is
\[
 \kappa_h^1(P)-s_h([P]_h)
   =\Theta\left(
      \left(\frac{P e_h-r_h(\varepsilon_h[P]_h)}{h}\right)(D)
                 \phi_*\right).                              \tag{AG8b}
\]
The quotient is a polynomial because its numerator has zero full
$h$-remainder. Applying that numerator at $D$ to $F_h$ proves the
equation, including its full inverse unit. Hence the map on degree-one
cohomology is exactly $\sigma_h$, while the section itself retains
its nonzero boundary (AG8). At a stalk $O=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C,s_0}$,
the map $O\otimes_{A_{\rm sp}}\kappa_h$ is therefore defined;
$O\otimes_{A_{\rm sp}}s_h$ is not such an arrow. The stalk is
torsion-free over the PID $A_{\rm sp}$ and is flat, being a filtered
union of its finite generated free submodules. Thus balancing retains
this exact cohomology map. The full divisor homotopies and their
composition are (CFA.22a)--(CFA.22d), with the same $e_h$ and $F_h$.
The tensor contractions below remain complex-linear, as (AG8a)--(AG8b)
already require; their cohomology projection is polynomial-equivariant.

The image of $\sigma_h$ is exactly $Q[h(D)]$.  In fact (AG4)
identifies the latter with $E_h$, since $j_hg=0$.  Applying its
map to $\sigma_hu$ gives $\varepsilon_hu$ by the first identity
of (AG8), an invertible map of $E_h$.  Injectivity of $\sigma_h$
also follows from $J_hs_h=I$ and $J_h\Theta=0$.  Hence
\[
 \mathscr B_U=\Theta V+\bigcup_{h\in\mathcal D_U}s_hE_h,
 \qquad
 T_UQ=\varinjlim_{h\in\mathcal D_U}(E_h,\iota_{hH}),
 \tag{AG9}
\]
with the following explicit transition arrows.  The $h=1$ terms
mean $E_1=0$, $s_1=\sigma_1=\ell_1=0$, and $\Theta V$.
The named source function is $F_1=\Theta\phi_*$, since $1(D)$ is
the identity; hence $\mathcal M F_1=g$. This boundary function is not
the zero section on $E_1$. No inverse unit is used in the zero space.

For $h\mid H$, put $b=H/h$; $b$ and $h$ are coprime.  Let
\[
 \pi_{Hh}:E_H\twoheadrightarrow E_h,\quad[P]_H\mapsto[P]_h,
 \qquad
 \iota_{hH}:E_h\hookrightarrow E_H
 \tag{AG10}
\]
be respectively restriction of jets and insertion of zero jets at
the added centres.  Chinese remainder division proves
$\pi\iota=I$, $\iota\pi=M_{e_{hH}}$, where $e_{hH}$ is the
idempotent equal to one on the old local factors and zero on the
added ones.  Both maps commute with multiplication by $s$.
The map $\pi$ is a unital algebra map; $\iota$ preserves products
and is $\mathbb C[s]$-linear, with $\iota(1)=e_{hH}$.
Define the different, raw numerator map
\[
 \mu_b:E_h\to E_H,\qquad[P]_h\mapsto[bP]_H.
 \quad
 \mu_b=\iota M_{[b]_h},\qquad
 \iota=\mu_b M_{[b]_h^{-1}}.
 \tag{AG11}
\]
These identities follow on every old factor by multiplication by
the full unit $[b]_h$, and on every added factor by its full-order
zero.  They prove well-definedness and injectivity of $\mu_b$.

The analytic and unit identities are exactly
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 v_h=bv_H,\qquad b(D)F_H=F_h,\qquad
 \mathcal T_H(bP)=\mathcal T_hP,\\
 \pi\upsilon_H=[b]_h^{-1}\upsilon_h,
 \quad\pi\varepsilon_H=[b]_h\varepsilon_h,\qquad
 J_H\mathcal T_hP=\iota J_h\mathcal T_hP,\\
 s_H\iota=s_h,\quad\sigma_H\iota=\sigma_h,
 \quad\ell_H\iota=\ell_h.
 \end{gathered}\tag{AG12}
\]
The function identity follows by applying the injective $h(D)$
to $b(D)F_H$ and $F_h$.  The jet identity holds at the old centres
by equality of the entire functions, and at added centres because
$v_h$ vanishes there to their full orders.  For the section identity,
$b r_h(\varepsilon_hu)$ has degree $<d_H$ and its product with
$v_H$ has complete $H$-jet $\iota u$.  Since $\upsilon_H$
is a unit, this is precisely $r_H(\varepsilon_H\iota u)$.
The last identity follows either from monicity of $b$ and its
leading coefficient, or by subtracting (AG8) for these equal
sections.  These arguments also prove the composition laws
$\iota_{HK}\iota_{hH}=\iota_{hK}$ and
$\mu_{K/H}\mu_{H/h}=\mu_{K/h}$.
The unit and remainder calculations above concern nonempty stages.
For $h=1\mid H$, the source identity is still literally
$H(D)F_H=F_1=\Theta\phi_*$, including $H=1$. Every equation with
domain $E_1$ is the equality of zero maps; no old local factor or
unit inverse is invoked. This proves (AG12) at the directed endpoint
as well as at every nonempty divisor transition.

In particular the directed limit in (AG9) is explicitly
\[
 E_U:=\bigoplus_{\substack{\rho\in U\\g(\rho)=0}}
       \mathbb C[z_\rho]/z_\rho^{m_\rho},
 \quad s\cdot u_\rho=(\rho+z_\rho)u_\rho,
 \quad \sigma_U:E_U\xrightarrow{\sim}T_UQ.
 \tag{AG13}
\]
All vectors have finite support.  The direct sum is closed under
coordinatewise products, but has no prescribed global unit when
the set of roots is infinite.  No nilpotent coordinate is removed.
The map $s_U:E_U\to\mathscr B_U$ is defined by the coherent
$s_h$; the formula $\mathscr B_U=\Theta V\oplus s_UE_U$
is an equality of vector spaces proved by $J_hs_h=I$.
It is not asserted to be a $D$-equivariant splitting.

\subsection{One differential graded arithmetic object}

Define the algebraic differential graded tensor algebra
\[
 \mathfrak T_U=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}C_U^{\otimes k},\qquad
 C_U^{\otimes0}=\mathbb C,
 \quad(x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k)
       (y_1\otimes\cdots\otimes y_l)
       =x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k\otimes
        y_1\otimes\cdots\otimes y_l.
 \tag{AG14}
\]
On homogeneous tensors retain the literal differential
\[
 d(x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k)
 =\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{\sum_{j<i}|x_j|}
 x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes dx_i\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k.
 \tag{AG15}
\]
Every $|x_i|$ is its original cochain degree, zero or one.
The two orders of applying the differential at distinct positions
have opposite signs, and $d^2x_i=0$, proving $d^2=0$.
Separating this sum at the concatenation point proves
$d(xy)=(dx)y+(-1)^{|x|}x(dy)$.  Concatenation is associative
on pure tensors and hence by linearity.  Thus (AG14) is one actual
differential graded algebra embedded in the corresponding tensor
algebra of the original theta complex.  The operator
$D_{\rm tot}=\sum_iD_i$ commutes with $d$ and is a derivation
for concatenation.  It is zero on the length-zero term.

Every tensor uses finitely many entries, so (AG9) and a common
packet containing their supports prove
\[
 C_U^{\otimes k}
 =\varinjlim_{h\in\mathcal D_U}
 [V\to\Theta V+s_hE_h]^{\otimes k}.
 \tag{AG16}
\]
These are literal inclusions into the same tensor test-function
complex, rather than isomorphisms of unspecified carriers.

There is a direct proof of its complete cohomology.  For
$F=\Theta\phi+s_Uu$, define $tF=\phi$ and $pF=s_Uu$;
on degree zero put $p=0$.  Uniqueness of the decomposition makes
these linear maps well defined.  Then $dt+td=I-p$, with $t$
zero on degree zero, and $p$ is a cochain projection onto $s_UE_U$
in degree one.  On a length-$k$ tensor set
\[
 t_k=\sum_{i=1}^k p^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes t\otimes
                  I^{\otimes(k-i)},
 \tag{AG17}
\]
where the action of the degree-$-1$ map $t$ includes the sign
$(-1)^{\sum_{j<i}|x_j|}$ on each homogeneous input.
Computing $dt_k+t_kd$ cancels every term involving different
positions, by precisely these signs.  The remaining terms are
$\sum_i p^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes(I-p)\otimes
I^{\otimes(k-i)}=I-p^{\otimes k}$ by telescoping.
Consequently, in each length $k$,
\[
 H^j(C_U^{\otimes k})=
 \begin{cases}(T_UQ)^{\otimes k},&j=k,\\0,&j\ne k,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 H^\bullet(\mathfrak T_U)=
 \bigoplus_{k\ge0}(T_UQ)^{\otimes k}
 \tag{AG18}
\]
with concatenation and the degree $k$ retained.  A cocycle has
only finitely many length components, so cohomology commutes with
this direct sum directly.  The projection in (AG18) is the original
$q^{\otimes k}$ and is $D_{\rm tot}$-equivariant.  The contraction
$t_k$ is only the explicit complex-linear proof of its
quasi-isomorphism.  It introduces no equivariant section into
$\mathscr B$: such a section would send any $h$-torsion vector
to an element killed by the injective $h(D)$ and hence to zero.

\subsection{The finite original source and metric diagrams}

For a nonempty full packet $h$, $k\ge1$ and $M\ge k(d_h-1)$, put
\[
 \mathcal P_M^{(k)}=\mathbb C[s_1,\ldots,s_k]_{\le M},\quad
 I_{h,k}=(h(s_1),\ldots,h(s_k)),\quad
 E_h^{(k)}=E_h^{\otimes k},\quad
 U_{h,k}=\upsilon_h^{\otimes k},
\]
\[
 \mathcal T_h^{(k)}P=P(D_1,\ldots,D_k)F_h^{\otimes k},
 \quad J_{h,k,M}P=U_{h,k}[P]_{I_{h,k}}.
 \tag{AG19}
\]
Thus the following entire diagram has its given source, quotient,
and original cohomology arrow:
\[
 \begin{array}{ccccc}
 \mathcal P_M^{(k)}&\xrightarrow{\ \mathcal T_h^{(k)}\ }
   &\mathscr B_U^{\otimes k}&\xrightarrow{q^{\otimes k}}
   &(T_UQ)^{\otimes k}\\
 J_{h,k,M}\downarrow&&&&\uparrow\sigma_h^{\otimes k}\\
 E_h^{(k)}&\xrightarrow{\quad I\quad}&E_h^{(k)}
      &\xrightarrow{\quad I\quad}&E_h^{(k)}.
 \end{array}\tag{AG20}
\]
To prove commutativity, divide $P$ successively by the monic
$h(s_i)$; its remainder is represented by a linear combination
of separate-variable monomials of degree $<d_h$ in each variable.
The difference from the numerator of
$s_h^{\otimes k}(U_{h,k}[P])$ belongs to $I_{h,k}$.
Every relation $\sum_i h(s_i)P_i$ has the explicit primitive
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{i-1}P_i(\mathbf D)
 (F_h^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes\phi_*\otimes
                       F_h^{\otimes(k-i)}).
 \tag{AG21}
\]
The $i-1$ preceding factors have degree one; (AG15) supplies the
second sign $(-1)^{i-1}$.  Its differential is therefore exactly
$\mathcal T_h^{(k)}(\sum_i h(s_i)P_i)$.
Conversely its complete jet vanishes precisely when $P\in I_{h,k}$,
since $U_{h,k}$ is a unit.  This proves all the kernel assertions
used by the diagram.  Monic division lowers total degree, so at
degree $M$ the relation coefficients have degree at most $M-d_h$.

For $h\mid H$ and $b=H/h$, put $B_b(\mathbf s)=\prod_i b(s_i)$
and $N=M+k\deg b$.  The diagram of divisor arrows is exactly
\[
 \begin{array}{ccc}
 \mathcal P_M^{(k)}&\xrightarrow{\times B_b}&\mathcal P_N^{(k)}\\
 J_{h,k,M}\downarrow&&\downarrow J_{H,k,N}\\
 E_h^{(k)}&\xrightarrow{\iota_{hH}^{\otimes k}}&E_H^{(k)},
 \end{array}
 \qquad
 \mathcal T_H^{(k)}(B_bP)=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}P.
 \tag{AG22}
\]
This follows first on pure monomials from (AG12), and then on all
polynomials by linearity.  It sends the smaller relation space into
the larger one and hence agrees with the actual cochain inclusion.

All norms in this diagram are values of the original functions.
Mellin--Fourier Plancherel in $x=e^y$ gives exactly
\[
 \|\mathcal T_h^{(k)}P\|_{L^2(d^kx)}^2
 =\int_{\mathbb R^k}|P(1/2+it_1,\ldots,1/2+it_k)|^2
                \prod_{i=1}^k w_h(t_i)\,d\mathbf t,
 \qquad w_h(t)=\frac{|v_h(1/2+it)|^2}{2\pi}.
 \tag{AG23}
\]
The one-variable normalization is seen directly from
$F(x)\mapsto e^{y/2}F(e^y)$, which is unitary for $dx$ and $dy$,
followed by Fourier measure $dt/(2\pi)$.  Applying it in every
variable proves (AG23).  The entire multiplier $\prod_i v_h(s_i)$
is nonzero, so $\mathcal T_h^{(k)}$ is injective: a zero Mellin
product on an open set makes the polynomial zero there and hence
everywhere.  Thus the Gram matrix in (AG23) is positive definite.
The actual mass is $\mu_h^k$, $\mu_h=\int w_h>0$.
The numerator map in (AG22) is an isometry for these specified
source forms, because its actual functions are equal.  It does
not state that the bare coordinate insertion preserves Euclidean
metrics or every minimized quotient metric.

For completeness the exact minimized comparison is as follows.
Let $\mathcal L_{h,k,M}=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}
(I_{h,k}\cap\mathcal P_M^{(k)})$, with its inherited Hilbert
inner product, and let $P_{h,k,M}$ be its orthogonal projection.
Then
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 R_{h,k,M}=(I-P_{h,k,M})s_h^{\otimes k},\quad
 G_{h,k,M}=R_{h,k,M}^*R_{h,k,M},\\
 W_{h,k,M}=(A_h^{(k)})^*G_{h,k,M}
             +G_{h,k,M}A_h^{(k)}-kG_{h,k,M}.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AG24}
\]
Indeed every source lift of a fixed jet differs from
$s_h^{\otimes k}$ by exactly $\mathcal L_{h,k,M}$;
orthogonal projection is its unique minimum.  The section has
numerator of total degree at most $k(d_h-1)$, so it belongs to
the declared source.  Surjectivity follows from this section.
With $\iota_k=\iota_{hH}^{\otimes k}$ and the degrees in (AG22),
set
\[
 \mathcal F=\mathcal L_{H,k,N}\cap\mathcal L_{h,k,M}^{\perp},
 \qquad Z=P_{\mathcal F}R_{h,k,M}.
\]
The inclusion of relation spaces in (AG22) implies that the difference
of their projections is precisely $P_{\mathcal F}$.  Their common
section is $s_H^{\otimes k}\iota_k=s_h^{\otimes k}$ by (AG12).
Therefore
\[
 \begin{split}
 R_{H,k,N}\iota_k&=R_{h,k,M}-Z,\\
 \iota_k^*G_{H,k,N}\iota_k&=G_{h,k,M}-Z^*Z,\\
 \iota_k^*W_{H,k,N}\iota_k&=W_{h,k,M}
 -( (A_h^{(k)})^*Z^*Z+Z^*ZA_h^{(k)}-kZ^*Z).
 \end{split}\tag{AG25}
\]
Orthogonality proves the second line; $A_H^{(k)}\iota_k=
\iota_kA_h^{(k)}$ and expansion prove the third.  When $k=1$
and $M=n+d_h$, both relation spaces are
$\Theta\operatorname{span}\{\phi_*,D\phi_*,\ldots,D^n\phi_*\}$.
Then $Z=0$, so (AG25) is the exact one-leg metric and control
compression equality.  In general the displayed $Z^*Z$ is retained.

Tensor concatenation also retains its source norm exactly.  For
$P\in\mathcal P_M^{(k)}$ and $Q\in\mathcal P_L^{(l)}$, set
$P\boxtimes Q=P(s_1,\ldots,s_k)Q(s_{k+1},\ldots,s_{k+l})$.
Then
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 \mathcal T_h^{(k+l)}(P\boxtimes Q)
       =\mathcal T_h^{(k)}P\otimes\mathcal T_h^{(l)}Q,\\
 J_{h,k+l,M+L}(P\boxtimes Q)=J_{h,k,M}P\otimes J_{h,l,L}Q.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{AG26}
\]
Fubini gives the product of the two squared norms; polarization
gives the full tensor inner product.  Thus the map on the Hilbert
tensor of these finite polynomial sources is isometric onto its
specified product subspace of $\mathcal P_{M+L}^{(k+l)}$.
Different packets are first inserted into their least common
multiple by (AG22); equality of the actual functions makes the
result independent of this presentation.  Multiplication of the
factors $b(s_i)$ proves that (AG22) and (AG26) commute, including
their degree shifts.  The symmetric coefficient projection is the
literal average $\frac1{k!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_k}\sigma$.
At top cochain degree its realization is the signed cochain
average $\frac1{k!}\sum_\sigma\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)T_\sigma$,
since interchanging degree-one factors contributes the other
factor $\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)$.  Concatenation followed by
this specified projection supplies the symmetric product; no
isometry of that projection is presumed.

\subsection{The cyclic sources and every conormal depth in the same diagram}

Retain the original finite local algebras
\[
 B_{h,k,r}=\mathbb C[\mathbf s]/I_{h,k}^r,\quad
 A_{h,k,r}=B_{h,k,r}^{S_k},\quad
 U_{h,k,r}=[\prod_i v_h(s_i)]_{I_{h,k}^r},\qquad r\ge1.
 \tag{AG27}
\]
At an ordered root tuple $\boldsymbol\rho$, with multiplicities
$m_i$, their local presentation is
\[
 \mathbb C[z_1,\ldots,z_k]/(z_1^{m_1},\ldots,z_k^{m_k})^r.
\]
The basis consists exactly of $z^{\mathbf a}$ with
$\sum_i\lfloor a_i/m_i\rfloor\le r-1$: ideal membership is
equivalent to choosing $r$ generator factors whose exponents are
bounded by $\mathbf a$.  The highest surviving total degree is
$D_{\boldsymbol\rho}+(r-1)M_{\boldsymbol\rho}$, where
$D_{\boldsymbol\rho}=\sum_i(m_i-1)$ and
$M_{\boldsymbol\rho}=\max_i m_i$.  Put every one of the $r-1$
allowed blocks at an index attaining the maximum and retain each
remainder exponent $m_i-1$ to attain it.  Its multinomial coefficient
in the corresponding power of $\sum_i z_i$ is nonzero.  Thus
the exact sum nilpotency order is
$1+D_{\boldsymbol\rho}+(r-1)M_{\boldsymbol\rho}$.
The separate-variable Chinese remainder theorem proves the stated
local presentations: the local ideals are pairwise comaximal,
and their products, intersections, and $r$th powers agree.

Consequently, with $S=\sum_i s_i$,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \ell_{h,k,\lambda,r}
 &=\max_{\sum_i\rho_i=\lambda}
           \{1+D_{\boldsymbol\rho}+(r-1)M_{\boldsymbol\rho}\},\\
 \chi_{h,k,r}(T)&=\prod_\lambda(T-\lambda)^{\ell_{h,k,\lambda,r}},
 \quad C_{h,k,r}=\mathbb C[T]/\chi_{h,k,r},\\
 \alpha_{h,k,r}:C_{h,k,r}&\hookrightarrow A_{h,k,r},
                 \quad[P]\mapsto[P(S)],\qquad
 \eta_{h,k,r}=M_{U_{h,k,r}}\alpha_{h,k,r}.
 \end{split}\tag{AG28}
\]
Intersecting the local annihilator ideals takes the maximum at
each collided sum, proving that $\alpha$ is an injective unital
algebra map.  The full Taylor unit $U$ is invertible on every
local factor, so $\eta$ is an injective $\mathbb C[T]$-module
map.  It is not called a unital algebra map.
At $r=1$ its analytic source is the exact restriction
\[
 \mathcal V_{h,k}P=\mathcal T_h^{(k)}P(S),\quad
 J_h^{\otimes k}\mathcal V_{h,k}P=\eta_{h,k,1}[P],\quad
 \|\mathcal V_{h,k}P\|^2
 =\int_{\mathbb R}|P(k/2+iu)|^2 w_h^{*k}(u)\,du.
 \tag{AG29}
\]
The last equality is (AG23) with $u=\sum_i t_i$, eliminating
one coordinate with absolute Jacobian one, which is exactly the
definition of the convolution.  Higher $r$ in (AG27) are full
Taylor observations of this same analytic Mellin multiplier;
they are not new cohomology quotients killing the original
theta relations, since those relations need only vanish to
depth one.  The truncation maps record their further jets.

For fixed $h$, tensor regrouping has an exact all-depth form:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Delta_{k,l,r}:C_{h,k+l,r}&\hookrightarrow
   \prod_{a=0}^{r-1}(C_{h,k,a+1}\otimes C_{h,l,r-a}),\\
 [P(T)]&\longmapsto\bigl([P(T_1+T_2)]\bigr)_{a=0}^{r-1}.
 \end{split}\tag{AG30}
\]
Each component is well defined: a product of $r$ generators from
the two blocks has either at least $a+1$ first-block generators
or at least $r-a$ second-block generators.  Hence
$I_{h,k+l}^r\subseteq I_{h,k}^{a+1}+I_{h,l}^{r-a}$,
giving an algebra quotient to the corresponding two-block algebra.
The scalar map in (AG30) factors through its cyclic subalgebras.
On a pair of local sum factors the nilpotency order is the sum
of their orders minus one: expand $(z+w)^n$ in
$\mathbb C[z,w]/(z^A,w^B)$ and retain
$\binom{A+B-2}{A-1}z^{A-1}w^{B-1}\ne0$.
The maximal order at a total sum in all components of (AG30) is
therefore the maximum, over tuples and $a$, of
\[
 1+D_{\rm first}+D_{\rm second}
             +aM_{\rm first}+(r-1-a)M_{\rm second}.
\]
The maximum over $a$ is attained at an endpoint and equals
$1+D_{\rm total}+(r-1)\max(M_{\rm first},M_{\rm second})$.
This is exactly (AG28), proving that the kernel before taking
the quotient is $(\chi_{h,k+l,r})$ and thus proving injectivity.
For $r=1$ the product has one factor and gives the familiar
injection $C_{h,k+l,1}\hookrightarrow C_{h,k,1}\otimes C_{h,l,1}$.
In every component the full unit maps to
$U_{h,k,a+1}\otimes U_{h,l,r-a}$.  Accordingly
\[
 \Xi_a\eta_{h,k+l,r}
       =(\eta_{h,k,a+1}\otimes\eta_{h,l,r-a})\Delta_a,
 \tag{AG31}
\]
where $\Xi_a$ is the just-proved quotient of the full joint
algebras.  Grouping three blocks gives the components indexed by
$a+b+c=r-1$ and evaluation at $T_1+T_2+T_3$ in either order.
This proves associativity of these gluing maps with all depths
and all their nilpotent coefficients retained.

For divisor inclusion, the local old factors of $B_{H,k,r}$
are exactly those of $B_{h,k,r}$: the extra $b(s_i)$ are units
there, so the generated ideals and all their powers coincide.
Restriction $\pi_r$ and zero insertion $\iota_r$ therefore give
\[
 \pi_r\iota_r=I,\quad\iota_r\pi_r=M_{e_r},\quad
 \iota_r U_{h,k,r}=e_r B_b U_{H,k,r},\quad
 \pi_rU_{H,k,r}=(\pi_rB_b)^{-1}U_{h,k,r}.
 \tag{AG32}
\]
They commute with the depth truncations.  The $e_r$ are the
idempotents equal to one on tuples entirely in the old support
and zero on every other tuple; permutations preserve this set,
so these maps also preserve the invariant algebras.
The exact polynomial map through depth $r$ is
\[
 \mu_{b,r}:B_{h,k,r}\hookrightarrow B_{H,k,r},
       \quad[P]\mapsto[B_b^rP],\qquad
 \mu_{b,r}=\iota_r M_{[B_b^r]},
 \tag{AG33}
\]
where the multiplier on the right is taken in $B_{h,k,r}$.
To prove well-definedness on generators, for $\sum_i a_i=r$
one has $B_b^r\prod_i h(s_i)^{a_i}
=\prod_iH(s_i)^{a_i}\prod_i b(s_i)^{r-a_i}\in I_{H,k}^r$.
On an added tuple at least one $b(s_i)$ belongs to the corresponding
local generator ideal, so $B_b^r$ vanishes modulo its $r$th power.
On old tuples it is a unit.  These facts prove (AG33), injectivity,
and image $e_rB_{H,k,r}$.
The complete arithmetic identity is
\[
 U_{H,k,r}\mu_{b,r}[P]
       =\iota_r[ B_b^{r-1}U_{h,k,r}P],\qquad
 \mathcal T_H^{(k)}(B_b^rP)
       =\mathcal T_h^{(k)}(B_b^{r-1}P).
 \tag{AG34}
\]
It follows from (AG32) on old factors and from zero on added ones;
the analytic identity follows directly from $v_h=bv_H$.
The analytic equality in (AG34) is asserted for each specified
polynomial $P$ before passage to a finite quotient.  Its two
functions are not defined from $[P]$ alone; changing the polynomial
representative retains the corresponding actual source relation.
Under truncation from $r+1$ to $r$, the raw map satisfies
$\operatorname{red}\mu_{b,r+1}
=\mu_{b,r}M_{[B_b]}\operatorname{red}$.
Thus its extra multiplier is explicitly part of the depth diagram.
In particular the one-power physical map must not be declared to
descend at every depth: for $k=1$ and a proper inclusion,
$b h^r=Hh^{r-1}$ is not divisible by $H^r$ for $r>1$, since
$b$ and $h$ are coprime.  This tests the claim on the actual
packet polynomials themselves.

At depth one the physical divisor image of a cyclic vector is
the precise vector
\[
 \iota_1\eta_{h,k,1}[P]
       =e_1B_bU_{H,k,1}[P(S)]\quad\hbox{in }E_H^{\otimes k}.
 \tag{AG35}
\]
This is always the common-carrier arrow, including collisions of
old and added sum eigenvalues.  It belongs to the larger cyclic
module $\eta_{H,k,1}C_{H,k,1}$ exactly when
$e_1B_b[P(S)]\in\alpha_{H,k,1}C_{H,k,1}$, since $U_H$
is a unit.  No such membership is imposed by the gluing.
An exact common-source pullback, available without imposing it, is
\[
 \mathcal Z_{hH,k}=\{(x,y)\in C_{h,k,1}\oplus C_{H,k,1}:
        \iota_1\eta_{h,k,1}x=\eta_{H,k,1}y\}.
 \tag{AG36}
\]
Both projections are injective by injectivity of the displayed
maps; their images are exactly the cyclic vectors whose specified
physical jets agree.  This states the full agreement relation
inside (AG20), rather than substituting an unsupported scalar
transition.  The CRT restriction and insertion maps compose by their
explicit local-factor formulas.  They commute with the
block quotients in (AG31), because restriction or insertion of
old tuples and truncation of their local monomial ideals commute
coordinate by coordinate.

There is also a scalar restriction in the reverse direction at
every depth.  Every old tuple occurs in the larger packet with
unchanged orders, so $\chi_{h,k,r}\mid\chi_{H,k,r}$.  Let
$\pi_C:C_{H,k,r}\twoheadrightarrow C_{h,k,r}$ be polynomial
remainder.  Evaluation and (AG32) give the exact squares
\[
 \pi_r\alpha_{H,k,r}=\alpha_{h,k,r}\pi_C,\qquad
 M_{[B_b]_{I_{h,k}^r}}\pi_r\eta_{H,k,r}
          =\eta_{h,k,r}\pi_C.
 \tag{AG36a}
\]
Thus even this backwards arithmetic observation retains its full
extra multiplier.

The forward cyclic image admits complete complementary coordinates,
with no membership assumption in (AG35).  Here is the original
full-unit retraction, with its proof.  On each sum factor of
$A_{H,k,r}$, let $N=S-\lambda$, $\ell=\ell_{H,k,\lambda,r}$,
and choose a tuple and a highest surviving monomial $z^{\mathbf a^*}$
attaining degree $\ell-1$, as in the proof of (AG28).  On this
specific local factor let $\theta$ extract its coefficient.  For
$X=T-\lambda$, define
\[
 \Pi_\lambda^0(v)=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell-1}
       \theta(N^{\ell-1-j}v)X^j,\quad
 p_\lambda(X)=\Pi_\lambda^0(e_\lambda(S)U_{H,k,r}),\quad
 \Pi_\lambda=p_\lambda^{-1}\Pi_\lambda^0.
 \tag{AG36b}
\]
Here $e_\lambda$ is the complete scalar CRT idempotent and the
polynomials on the right are reduced modulo $X^\ell$.
Since $N^\ell=0$, coefficient comparison gives
$\Pi_\lambda^0(Nv)=X\Pi_\lambda^0(v)$.  Moreover
\[
 p_\lambda(0)=\left(\prod_i v_H(\rho_i)\right)
                   \frac{(\ell-1)!}{\prod_i a_i^*!}\ne0.
\]
In fact terms of positive total degree in the unit would exceed
the maximal surviving degree of the chosen local factor, while
the remaining multinomial coefficient is the one displayed.
This proves invertibility by the finite geometric series and
shows that $\Pi_\lambda\eta(P)=P$ on each local scalar factor.
Combining these maps by scalar CRT gives the exact
$\mathbb C[T]$-linear retraction $\Pi_H:A_{H,k,r}\to C_{H,k,r}$.
Put $K_H=\ker\Pi_H$ and $Q_H=I-\eta_H\Pi_H$.  Direct multiplication
gives the inverse module isomorphisms
\[
 A_{H,k,r}\longleftrightarrow C_{H,k,r}\oplus K_H,
 \qquad v\longmapsto(\Pi_Hv,Q_Hv),\quad
 (c,z)\longmapsto\eta_Hc+z.
 \tag{AG36c}
\]
They are the retained original coefficient changes; no
orthogonality for the arithmetic form is inferred.
The complete forward insertion is consequently the injection
\[
 \begin{split}
 (X_{hH},Y_{hH}):C_{h,k,r}&\hookrightarrow C_{H,k,r}\oplus K_H,\\
 X_{hH}&=\Pi_H\iota_r\eta_h,\qquad
 Y_{hH}=Q_H\iota_r\eta_h,\qquad
 \iota_r\eta_h=\eta_HX_{hH}+Y_{hH}.
 \end{split}\tag{AG36d}
\]
The last equality follows from (AG36c); if both coordinates vanish,
injectivity of $\iota_r\eta_h$ forces the input to vanish.
Its image is wholly cyclic exactly when $Y_{hH}=0$.  Under another
divisor insertion the whole map is transformed by (AG36c), so both
coordinates, including the cross maps from $K_H$, remain present.

For clarity the raw polynomial arrows also have their exact
compatibility with the distributed-depth quotients.  Write
$B_b=B_1B_2$ for its first and second blocks.  On their stated
finite quotients,
\[
 \Xi_a^H\mu_{b,r}^{(k+l)}
 =(\mu_{b,a+1}^{(k)}\otimes\mu_{b,r-a}^{(l)})
    M_{B_1^{r-a-1}B_2^a}\Xi_a^h.
 \tag{AG36e}
\]
Both sides multiply a polynomial representative by
$B_1^rB_2^r$ before the same block reduction; this proves equality
and retains every additional power.  The arithmetic identity
(AG34) supplies its corresponding actual source-function equality.

\subsection{Reflection and the retained split zero}

For an invariant packet use the original involutions
$P^\dagger(s)=\overline{P(1-\overline s)}$ and
$\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}F(x)=x^{-1}\overline{F(1/x)}$.
The latter is antiunitary for $dx$ by substitution $y=1/x$.
Its Mellin transform is $(\mathcal MF)^\dagger$ by the same
substitution, and $D\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}=
\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}(1-D)$ by differentiation.
Since $h^\dagger=(-1)^{d_h}h$ and $g^\dagger=g$,
$v_h^\dagger=(-1)^{d_h}v_h$.  The full jet argument and degree
bound used in (AG12) now give
$\mathfrak j_{\mathscr B}s_h=s_h\mathfrak j_h$.
The CRT maps commute with reflection, since old support and added
support are separately invariant.  The raw numerator map carries
the exact sign
\[
 (\mu_{b,r}P)^\dagger
       =(-1)^{kr(d_H-d_h)}\mu_{b,r}(P^\dagger),
 \tag{AG37}
\]
as follows from $B_b^\dagger=(-1)^{k(d_H-d_h)}B_b$.
Thus no sign has been removed from the original arithmetic unit.
These involutions, with reversal $S\mapsto k-\overline S$,
intertwine the divisor CRT, scalar-evaluation and depth-truncation
arrows just constructed.  In the complementary coordinates (AG36c)
their exact action is
$(c,z)\mapsto(\Pi_H\mathfrak j(\eta_Hc+z),
Q_H\mathfrak j(\eta_Hc+z))$.
No commutation of the chosen coefficient extractor with reflection
is assumed.
Before any parity consequence, the full-unit map has the exact law
$\mathfrak j_A\eta_{h,k,r}=(-1)^{kd_h}\eta_{h,k,r}\mathfrak j_C$,
because $U_{h,k,r}^\dagger=(-1)^{kd_h}U_{h,k,r}$.
For the present off-critical invariant packets $d_h$ is even:
$\rho\mapsto1-\overline\rho$ has no fixed centre in $U$ and
pairs equal full multiplicities.  The sign is therefore one here.

Finally apply the original split lift to every proved linear arrow.
For a vector space $X$ put
$G(X)=\{\tau\}\sqcup\{x^\bullet:x\in X\}$,
$e_X=0_X^\bullet$, with $\tau$ the additive identity and
$x^\bullet+y^\bullet=(x+y)^\bullet$.
For $L:X\to Y$, its specified lift is
\[
 G(L)(\tau)=\tau,\qquad G(L)(x^\bullet)=(Lx)^\bullet.
 \tag{AG38}
\]
Checking two supported inputs proves additivity and scalar
compatibility; a case involving $\tau$ follows from the given
identity and absorbing rules.  Thus composition and every
commutative diagram above lift exactly.  Products lift by
$x^\bullet y^\bullet=(xy)^\bullet$ and make $\tau$ absorbing;
the same check applies to concatenation and to the multiplicative
arrows of their proved types.  A retained support label is carried
by $(\lambda,x)\mapsto(\lambda,Lx)$ at each such arrow.
In particular the differential of (AG21) remains the represented
theta boundary $\mathcal T_h^{(k)}(\sum_i h(s_i)P_i)$; it is
the subsequent map $G(q^{\otimes k})$ that sends this boundary
to the supported zero $e$.  Every inserted zero jet remains a
supported zero.  External
absence is always $\tau$.  These lifts produce the split form of
the single actual object (AG14), with its divisor, tensor,
cohomology, full-jet and metric observation diagrams intact.
The conjugate-linear reflection uses its corresponding semilinear
lift, with $G(\mathfrak j)(c^\bullet x^\bullet)
=\overline c^{\,\bullet}G(\mathfrak j)(x^\bullet)$.
The square of the lifted differential is the lift of the zero
linear map: it sends a supported input to the supported zero
in the indicated degree, while fixing $\tau$.  This is the
original split-zero identity, not a replacement of $e$ by $\tau$.

For the independent mixed coefficient observation, (AG38) is applied
coordinatewise before any synchronization. Fix $n\ge1$ and a coefficient
mask $A\subseteq I_n$. The labelled additive face of an amplitude space
$X$ is $\{A\}\times X^A$, with absent entries outside $A$ and supported
zeros at every zero entry inside $A$. For $A\subseteq B$, zero insertion
$z_A^B:X^A\to X^B$ is identity on old coordinates and inserts $0_X$
on new coordinates. It sends $e_A$ to $e_B$. Every linear map commutes
with this arrow because it sends $0_X$ to $0_Y$; it is not a pointed
map preserving the wholly absent coefficient tuple when $A$ is empty
and $B$ is not. The coordinatewise lift $G(L)^n$ instead preserves
the coefficient mask exactly and has supported-zero fibre
$\{A\}\times(\ker L)^A$.

Independently retain $W\subseteq V$, $D W\subseteq W$, and the original
nonempty leg mask $S\subseteq\{+,-\}$. For $X=U,\Omega$ set
\[
\begin{gathered}
 C^X_{W,S;A}=
 \left[\left(\bigoplus_{+\in S}W\oplus
                  \bigoplus_{-\in S}\widehat W\right)^A
          \xrightarrow{(\phi_i,\psi_i)\mapsto
                  (\Theta\phi_i-\Theta\widehat\psi_i)}
                         \mathscr B_X^A\right],\\
 Q_{W,X}=\mathscr B_X/\Theta W,\qquad
 0\to(V/W)^A\xrightarrow{[v_i]\mapsto[\Theta v_i]_W}
 Q_{W,X}^A\to(T_XQ)^A\to0 .
\end{gathered}                                                   \tag{AG38a}
\]
The minus action is $1-D$ on $\widehat W$. Injectivity of $\Theta$
proves injectivity of the displayed first map, its image is the kernel
of the quotient modulo $\Theta V$, and that quotient is onto by
definition of $\mathscr B_X$. The joint-leg change
$u=\phi-\widehat\psi$, $v=(\phi+\widehat\psi)/2$ has inverse
$\phi=v+u/2$, $\psi=\widehat v-\widehat u/2$. Therefore
$H^0=W^A$ for the joint mask and $0$ for a single leg, while
$H^1=Q_{W,X}^A$ in every case. All source, coefficient and leg
inclusions commute with these formulas coordinate by coordinate.
In particular the proper-source kernel and the joint-leg diagonal
are different degrees and neither disappears in the full-source
arithmetic observation. The inclusions in (AG3a) induce these same
cochain inclusions and their first quotient $(T_LQ)^A$ at every
$W,S,A$, since the denominator $\Theta W$ is unchanged.

At $A=\varnothing$ the amplitude complex is zero but the label
$(W,S;\varnothing,0)$ remains; it is distinct from the global external
point $\tau_{\rm out}$. No coefficient-face colimit identifies those
labels. The full-unit section, (AG8)'s source boundary and the strict
map (AG8a) are applied on each active coordinate at their stated types.
For a proper source the residual Euler class is precisely
$([\phi_*\ell_h(u_i)])_i\in(V/W)^A$ under (AG38a). Its vanishing
means these source vectors lie in $W$. Dilation sends the label to
$R_aW$ and the corresponding residual source lies in $(V/R_aW)^A$,
as computed with its full primitive in (CFA.15)--(CFA.25).

The multiplication types at these vertices remain explicit. The
Cartesian semiring $\prod_iG(R_i)$ multiplies masks by intersection;
the original negacyclic convolution on $G(E_h)^n$ multiplies them by
sumset modulo $n$, including supported cancellations. For $n=2$ its
product is $(a,b)\star(c,d)=(ac+(-1)^\bullet bd,ad+bc)$, not the
Cartesian product. Its filling localization has target
$G(E_h[t]/(t^2+1))$ and supported-zero fibre $\{X_0,Y_0,Z_0\}$;
the synchronized section is additive and multiplicative but has unit
image $Z_1$, whereas the unsynchronized unit is $X_1$. These formulas
and their exact ideal branches are proved in (MFC.11)--(MFC.30).
The higher-lattice arrows retain their own threshold/localization
fibres from MFC; neither their join/meet operations nor their separate
ideal product is inserted as a multiplication of the theta source.
At the latter vertex the product is exactly the signed tensor
concatenation (AG14)--(AG15).
