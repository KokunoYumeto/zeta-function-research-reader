Equations (A5)--(A7), or equation (9) in the finite-cocycle chapter, prove
its range, injectivity, and continuity. The explicit one-factor inverse
used in that proof is
\[
 S_\rho F(x)=x^{-\rho}\int_x^\infty F(y)y^{\rho-1}\,dy,
 \qquad \mathcal MF(\rho)=0.
\]
It satisfies \((D-\rho)S_\rho F=F\). The zero moment writes its upper
integral as the negative lower integral; these two expressions give,
respectively, all decay bounds at infinity and zero. More explicitly,
for \(N>\operatorname{Re}\rho\) and \(N+\operatorname{Re}\rho>0\),
\[
 |S_\rho F(x)|\leq
 \frac{p_{N,0}(F)}{N-\operatorname{Re}\rho}x^{-N},\qquad
 |S_\rho F(x)|\leq
 \frac{p_{-N,0}(F)}{N+\operatorname{Re}\rho}x^N.
\]
Using the first bound for \(x\geq1\), the second for \(x\leq1\), and
\(DS_\rho F=\rho S_\rho F+F\) proves every defining derivative seminorm
bound. The homogeneous solution \(cx^{-\rho}\) belongs to \(\mathscr B\)
only when \(c=0\). Integration by parts gives
\(\mathcal MS_\rho F(s)=\mathcal MF(s)/(s-\rho)\), with the removable
value filled in. Iteration removes one of the prescribed zero factors
at each step and preserves every other vanishing order. This proves the
stated inverse on the complete jet kernel and injectivity of \(h(D)\),
without assuming an unproved operator range.

Define the original section exactly as in (A10):
\[
 R_{\mathrm{ref}}u
 =S_h^{\mathscr B}\Theta\bigl(R_Z(u)(D)\phi_*\bigr),\qquad
 J_ZF=j_h\mathcal MF,\qquad
 q:\mathscr B\longrightarrow Q=\mathscr B/\Theta V,
 \qquad \sigma=qR_{\mathrm{ref}}.
\]
The input to \(S_h^{\mathscr B}\) has Mellin transform \(gR_Z(u)\),
which has all required zero jets, so this definition has the stated
domain for every \(u\). The inverse formula gives
\[
 \mathcal MR_{\mathrm{ref}}u=vR_Z(u),\qquad
 J_ZR_{\mathrm{ref}}u=j_h(v)\varepsilon u=u.
\]
For \(\phi\in V\), the original Mellin identity
\(\mathcal M\Theta\phi=gH_\phi\), with \(H_\phi\) holomorphic at the
selected zeros, gives \(J_Z\Theta\phi=0\). Thus \(J_Z\) factors as
\(\overline J_Zq\), and
\[
 \overline J_Z\sigma=1_{E_h},\qquad
 h(D)\sigma(u)=q\Theta\bigl(R_Z(u)(D)\phi_*\bigr)=0.
\]
The displayed left inverse proves that
\(\sigma:E_h\hookrightarrow Q[h(D)]\) is injective. This is an actual
section inside the arithmetic cohomology, not a section inferred from
finite-dimensional quotient data alone.

\paragraph{All positive dilations act equivariantly after the original boundary.}
Set \(\ell(u)=[X^{d-1}]R_Z(u)\). Since multiplication by \(X\) commutes
with the unit \(\varepsilon\), the polynomial
\(XR_Z(u)-R_Z(Au)\) is divisible by \(h\). Its degree is at most \(d\);
its leading coefficient is \(\ell(u)\). Hence the exact polynomial
identity is
\[
 XR_Z(u)-R_Z(Au)=h(X)\ell(u).
\]
Applying the defining inverse, or multiplying by the injective
\(h(D)\), gives the original boundary coefficient (A11):
\[
 DR_{\mathrm{ref}}-R_{\mathrm{ref}}A=\Theta\phi_*\ell.
\]
For a single simple zero this coefficient is
\(\ell(u)=u/g'(\rho)=u/(2\xi'(\rho))\); its arithmetic unit and factor
of two remain present.

For \(a>0\) let \(\lambda=\log a\), \(T_r=e^{rA}\), and retain
\(U_aF(x)=F(x/a)\). Define
\[
 c_a(u)=\int_0^\lambda
 U_{e^{\lambda-r}}\phi_*\,\ell(T_ru)\,dr\in V.
\]
When \(\lambda<0\) this is the oriented integral
\(\int_0^\lambda=-\int_\lambda^0\). No orientation changes are hidden.
Fix any norm on the finite-dimensional \(E_h\), only for this convergence
estimate, with the induced operator norms for \(A\) and \(\ell\).
For the original Schwartz seminorms
\(q_{N,k}(\phi)=\sup_x(1+|x|)^N|\partial_x^k\phi(x)|\),
\[
 q_{N,k}(U_b\phi)\leq b^{-k}\max(1,b)^Nq_{N,k}(\phi).
\]
On the interval between 0 and \(\lambda\), this inequality and
\(\|T_r\|\leq e^{|r|\|A\|}\) give
\[
 q_{N,k}(c_a(u))\leq
 |\lambda|e^{(N+k+\|A\|)|\lambda|}
 q_{N,k}(\phi_*)\|\ell\|\|u\|.
\]
The integrand is continuous in every seminorm. Its Riemann sums converge
in the complete closed Schwartz subspace \(V\): uniform continuity on
the compact parameter interval makes them Cauchy in each seminorm,
and their limits retain evenness and both zero moments. Differentiating
dilation is legitimate in every seminorm, as the difference quotient's
integral remainder is bounded by finitely many seminorms of
\(D^2\phi\) on that compact interval. The same argument applies in
\(\mathscr B\), using \(p_{b,k}(U_aF)=a^bp_{b,k}(F)\).
Consequently
\[
 \frac{d}{dr}\bigl(U_{e^{\lambda-r}}R_{\mathrm{ref}}T_ru\bigr)
 =-U_{e^{\lambda-r}}
   (DR_{\mathrm{ref}}-R_{\mathrm{ref}}A)T_ru
 =-\Theta U_{e^{\lambda-r}}\phi_*\ell(T_ru).
\]
Integrating in the actual \(\mathscr B\) seminorms and using continuity
of \(\Theta\) proves, for every \(a>0\),
\[
 U_aR_{\mathrm{ref}}-R_{\mathrm{ref}}a^A=\Theta c_a,
 \qquad a^A=e^{(\log a)A}.
\]
This is precisely equation (15) of the finite-cocycle calculation.
The original relation space is invariant because
\(U_a\Theta=\Theta U_a\), so \(U_a\) induces \(\overline U_a\) on \(Q\).
Applying \(q\) to the identity gives the required full intertwining
\[
 \boxed{\overline U_a\sigma=\sigma a^A\quad\text{for every }a>0.}
\]
Inserting \(U_aR_{\mathrm{ref}}b^A\) into the defect for \(ab\)
and using injectivity of \(\Theta\) also gives the retained cocycle law
\(c_{ab}=U_a\circ c_b+c_a\circ b^A\).
This proves group equivariance of the section in \(Q\); it does not
assert strict equivariance of \(R_{\mathrm{ref}}\) in \(\mathscr B\).
Indeed the displayed boundary is exactly what connects those two maps.

\paragraph{The exact full-jet insertion and the source eigenline.}
The identity on germs gives the Chinese-remainder isomorphism
\[
 E_h\xrightarrow{\sim}\bigoplus_{\nu\in Z}A_\nu,
 \qquad A_\nu=\mathcal O_\nu/(g)
              =\mathcal O_\nu/((s-\nu)^{m_\nu}).
\]
At each centre the ideals generated by \(h\) and \(g\) both equal the
displayed power ideal, since their remaining factors are units.
For a fixed \(\rho\in Z\), let
\(\iota_\rho:A_\rho\hookrightarrow E_h\) be the inverse
Chinese-remainder map with all other blocks zero. An explicit formula,
retaining all Taylor coefficients, uses
\(H_\rho(X)=h(X)/(X-\rho)^{m_\rho}\): for a germ representative f,
\[
 \iota_\rho[f]=
 \left[H_\rho(X)\sum_{j=0}^{m_\rho-1}
 \frac{1}{j!}
 \left.\frac{d^j}{dX^j}\left(\frac{f(X)}{H_\rho(X)}\right)\right|_{X=\rho}
 (X-\rho)^j\right]_{h}.
\]
At \(\rho\) its jet is f; at every other selected centre the factor
\(H_\rho\) gives all required zero jets. A change of representative f
by a germ of order at least \(m_\rho\) leaves the displayed derivatives
unchanged. These facts prove that this is the exact well-defined
insertion, inverse to its block projection. In each block,
\[
 A|_\nu=\nu I+N_\nu,\qquad
 a^A|_\nu=a^\nu\sum_{j=0}^{m_\nu-1}
 \frac{(\log a)^j}{j!}N_\nu^j.
\]
Thus \(\iota_\rho\) intertwines the full block scaling; it does not
replace that block by its residue-value quotient.

Retain the preceding subsection's actual factorization
\(g(\rho+z)=u_\rho(z)z^m\), \(m=m_\rho\), and put
\[
 t_\rho:\mathbb C\longrightarrow A_\rho,
 \qquad t_\rho(c)=c[g']=cmu_\rho(0)z^{m-1}.
\]
Its domain has action \(c\mapsto a^\rho c\). The previous calculation
proves that \(t_\rho\) is an injective intertwiner, since
\(mu_\rho(0)\ne0\) and \(N_\rho z^{m-1}=0\). Therefore the complete
original source lift is
\[
 \boxed{L_\rho=\sigma\iota_\rho t_\rho:
 (\mathbb C,a^\rho)\hookrightarrow(Q,\overline U_a),
 \qquad w_\rho=L_\rho(1).}
\]
Its equivariance follows by composing the three proved
intertwiners. Its nonvanishing follows without any choice of a quotient
representative: \(\overline J_Zw_\rho=\iota_\rho[g']\ne0\).
For an explicit left inverse, let \(\operatorname{pr}_\rho\) denote the
block projection and set
\[
 \Lambda_\rho(q_0)=
 \frac{[z^{m-1}]\operatorname{pr}_\rho\overline J_Z(q_0)}
      {mu_\rho(0)}.
\]
Then \(\Lambda_\rho L_\rho=1_{\mathbb C}\). This linear left inverse
is used to certify injectivity; no equivariance of \(\Lambda_\rho\)
on the whole \(Q\) is asserted. In particular, for \(m>1\) it is not
residue evaluation, which would vanish on the socle. The construction
keeps precisely the nilpotent coefficient required for a nonzero lift.

\paragraph{The full tensor lift in the original top cohomology.}
For every integer \(r\geq1\), tensor over \(\mathbb C\) to obtain
\[
 L_\rho^{\otimes r}
 =\sigma^{\otimes r}\iota_\rho^{\otimes r}t_\rho^{\otimes r}:
 \mathbb C^{\otimes r}\hookrightarrow Q^{\otimes r},
 \qquad w_\rho^{\otimes r}=L_\rho^{\otimes r}(1^{\otimes r}).
\]
The map \(\Lambda_\rho^{\otimes r}\) is a left inverse, since the
tensor of the identities \(\Lambda_\rho L_\rho=1\) is the identity.
Thus this is a nonzero tensor in the source cohomology, not only in its
finite quotient. Composing the exact scaling intertwiners gives
\[
 \overline U_a^{\otimes r}w_\rho^{\otimes r}
 =a^{r\rho}w_\rho^{\otimes r}\qquad(a>0).
\]
The top-degree comparison in primary (47) identifies this tensor with
an actual class of
\(H^r\mathsf A_{\tau,r}(\mathcal T^{\boxtimes r})\).
Explicitly, put
\[
 b_\rho=R_{\mathrm{ref}}\iota_\rho[g']\in\mathscr B.
\]
Its representative in the original tensor complex is
\(b_\rho^{\otimes r}\in\mathscr B^{\otimes r}\). Top boundaries are
sums of tensors with a \(\Theta V\) factor. The map
\(J_Z^{\otimes r}\) kills every such tensor, whereas it sends this
representative to \((\iota_\rho[g'])^{\otimes r}\ne0\). This also
proves its nonzero top cohomology class directly in the original
complex.

The group action on this representative has the original source
boundary and tensor signs. Put
\(c=c_a(\iota_\rho[g'])\). Then
\(U_ab_\rho-a^\rho b_\rho=\Theta c\). In the joint two-leg complex,
\(d(c,0)=\Theta c\). With each \(\mathscr B\) factor in degree one,
form the degree \(r-1\) tensor
\[
 H_{a,r}=\sum_{j=1}^{r}(-1)^{j-1}
 (U_ab_\rho)^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes(c,0)
 \otimes(a^\rho b_\rho)^{\otimes(r-j)}.
\]
Only the degree-zero factor contributes to its differential, with
sign \((-1)^{j-1}\) from its preceding degree-one factors. It cancels
the displayed coefficient. The resulting sum telescopes, giving
\[
 dH_{a,r}=(U_ab_\rho)^{\otimes r}
             -(a^\rho b_\rho)^{\otimes r}.
\]
This is the cochain-level proof of the stated tensor eigencharacter
on the original complex, retaining its source boundary rather than
postulating a strict invariant representative.

Finally, the source character now has the exact original modulus
identity
\[
 \log\frac{|a^{r\rho}|^2}{a^r}
 =r(2\operatorname{Re}\rho-1)\log a\qquad(a>1)
\]
on the nonzero class \(w_\rho^{\otimes r}\) itself. The actual moment
line still has character \(a\); no norm or mass has been changed.
This identity supplies the source eigenline requested by the tensor
construction. It does not supply an upper estimate for its modulus.
At each original nonempty support mask, \(\sigma\), \(\iota_\rho\),
and \(t_\rho\) lift by keeping that mask and its labelled zero.
Their cohomological lifts commute with the identity transports among
the nonempty \(Q\)-fibres; external \(\tau\) remains external absence.
The joint tensor class above therefore belongs to the retained
supported arithmetic cohomology over the same absolute base.
