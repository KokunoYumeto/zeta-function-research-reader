1. Result and exact source boundary

The formalization session’s endpoint criterion selects an actual member of the canonical arithmetic family. This continuation proves one of its previously unproved analytic inputs. For the unchanged arithmetic measure, a fixed finite packet hh, and integers n≥k≥3n\ge k\ge3, there is a finite constant ChC_h, independent of nn and kk, such that

(ωh,k,2nωh,k,n)1/(2n)≤Chn. \boxed{\left(\frac{\omega_{h,k,2n}}{\omega_{h,k,n}}\right)^{1/(2n)} \le C_h n.} \tag{1} (1)

The norms are the original monic polynomial norms for mh,k=wh*km_{h,k}=w_h^{*k}; the total mass remains μhk\mu_h^k. No root-location hypothesis is used in this bound. The proof retains a compact-source mass, a lower convolution constant, and both exponential moments. These constants are defined explicitly below; they have not been numerically enclosed.

For the packet consisting exactly of a hypothetical nonreal off-line quartet, let

qk=[1+k(m−1)](k+1)2,Lh,k=2δ[1+k(m−1)](k+1)⌊(k+1)24⌋. q_k=[1+k(m-1)](k+1)^2, \quad L_{h,k}=2\delta[1+k(m-1)](k+1) \left\lfloor\frac{(k+1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor. \tag{2} (2)
