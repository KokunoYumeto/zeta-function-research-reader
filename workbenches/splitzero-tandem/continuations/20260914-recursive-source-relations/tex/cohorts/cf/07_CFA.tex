\section{The exact original cochain attachment on every independent coefficient face}
\label{CFStage:sec:coefficient-face-cochain-attachment}

This attachment supplies the cochain, source-section, balancing, and tensor
arrows for the independent coefficient faces of the original mixed carrier.
The coefficient mask and the original two-leg mask are separate indices.
The finite mixed semiring multiplication remains the original algebra
observation; the original source spaces acquire no additional multiplication.
All tensor products below are algebraic unless a topology is explicitly
specified.  We retain the original maps of (AG1)--(AG18), their analytic
comparison in (CAU.9)--(CAU.20), and their two-leg source attachment in
(CAU.34)--(CAU.37).

\subsection{Original spaces, independent faces, and their exact cochain maps}

Retain
\[
\begin{split}
 V&=\{\phi\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R):\phi(-x)=\phi(x),\quad
       \phi(0)=0,\quad\textstyle\int_{\mathbb R}\phi(x)\,dx=0\},\\
 \mathscr B&=\{F\in C^\infty(\mathbb R_{>0}):
       \sup_{x>0}x^b|D^jF(x)|<\infty\quad(b\in\mathbb Z,j\ge0)\},\\
 D&=-x\partial_x,\\
 \Theta\phi(x)&=2\sum_{n\ge1}\phi(nx),\qquad
 \mathcal MF(s)=\int_0^\infty F(x)x^s\frac{dx}{x},\\
 Q&=\mathscr B/\Theta V,\quad q:\mathscr B\to Q,\qquad
 U=\{s:0<\Re s<1,\ \Re s\ne\tfrac12\},\\
 T_UQ&=\{u\in Q:p(D)u=0\text{ for some }p\in\mathcal P_U\},\\
 \mathscr B_U&=q^{-1}(T_UQ),\qquad C_U=[V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B_U].
\end{split}                                                     \tag{CFA.1}
\]
Here $\mathcal P_U$ is the set of monic polynomials all of whose roots
lie in $U$, including $1$.  The last complex is in degrees zero and one.
Products of annihilating polynomials prove that $T_UQ$ and
$\mathscr B_U$ are vector spaces stable under $D$.  The original
theta map is injective.  Its image lies in $\mathscr B_U$, and the
definition of $q$ proves the actual sequence
\[
 0\longrightarrow V\xrightarrow{\Theta}\mathscr B_U
           \xrightarrow{q}T_UQ\longrightarrow0.                \tag{CFA.2}
\]
We use $A_{\rm sp}=\mathbb C[x]$ for spectral polynomials, with $x$
acting by $D$.  This is the original Euler polynomial algebra
$\mathbb C[t]$ under its explicit isomorphism $t\mapsto x$.
The cyclic coefficient variable $t$ in the separate finite algebra
$E_h[t]/(t^n+1)$ is never substituted for the spectral variable $x$.

Fix $n\ge1$, put $I_n=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$, and let $A\subseteq I_n$
be any coefficient mask, including the empty mask.  For a vector space
$M$ put $M[A]=\bigoplus_{i\in A}M$.  Its labelled carrier element
$(A,(m_i))$ means the tuple with $m_i^\bullet$ in active slot $i$
and external $\tau$ in each inactive slot.  Thus the zero of this
additive face group is $e_A$, supported exactly on $A$; the empty
face has the single wholly absent coefficient tuple.
For $A\subseteq B\subseteq I_n$ define
\[
 z_A^B:M[A]\longrightarrow M[B],\qquad
 (z_A^Bm)_i=
 \begin{cases}m_i,&i\in A,\\0_M,&i\in B\setminus A.
 \end{cases}                                                   \tag{CFA.3}
\]
This is linear.  For $A\subseteq B\subseteq C$, each coordinate of
$z_B^Cz_A^B$ is its original value if it lies in $A$ and is zero
otherwise, proving $z_B^Cz_A^B=z_A^C$.
On labelled carriers (CFA.3) sends $e_A$ to $e_B$.  In particular,
when $A$ is empty and $B$ is nonempty it sends the wholly absent
coefficient tuple to a supported zero tuple.  It is a support-changing
map of face groups, not a globally $\tau$-preserving pointed lift.

For any linear map $f:M\to N$, define $f[A]$ by applying $f$ in each
active coordinate.  On coordinates in $A$, both composites below are
$f(m_i)$; on coordinates in $B\setminus A$, both are zero because
$f(0_M)=0_N$.  Thus
\[
 f[B]z_A^B=z_A^Bf[A].                                         \tag{CFA.4}
\]
This proves naturality on every face, including the empty one.
The separate pointed coordinate map $\prod_{i\in I_n}G(f)$ preserves
the mask exactly: it sends $m_i^\bullet$ to $f(m_i)^\bullet$, even
when $f(m_i)=0$, and sends an absent coordinate to an absent coordinate.
Its inverse image of the supported zero on face $A$ is exactly
$(\ker f)[A]$.  These statements apply in the actual scalar categories
of $q$, the Mellin maps, the finite sections, the quotient inclusions,
and the analytic comparison maps below.

The face diagram has a terminal vertex $I_n$.  Its colimit as a diagram
of vector spaces is $M[I_n]=M^n$: every compatible family of maps is
uniquely its map from this terminal vertex.  Such a colimit identifies
each zero face with its full-face image.  The independent mixed carrier
is instead the labelled disjoint union of the faces.  Its simultaneous
amplitude and mask determine every coordinate, since they distinguish
supported zero from absence.  No colimit over the arrows (CFA.3) is
taken in this attachment.

The coefficient mask is independent of the original source-leg mask
$S\subseteq\{+,-\}$, with $S$ nonempty.  For a $D$-invariant original
subspace $W\subseteq V$, give the plus leg $W$ the action $D$ and
the minus leg $\widehat W$ the action $1-D$, where the Fourier kernel
is $e^{-2\pi ix\nu}$.  The original complex $C_{W,S}$ has central
term $\mathscr B$ in degree one, the indicated present legs in degree
zero, and differential
$d(\phi,\psi)=\Theta\phi-\Theta\widehat\psi$ with only present
terms included.  Define its coefficient face by
\[
 C_{W,S}[A]=\bigoplus_{i\in A}C_{W,S},\qquad
 C_U[A]=[V[A]\xrightarrow{\Theta[A]}\mathscr B_U[A]].           \tag{CFA.5}
\]
The differential acts independently on every active coordinate.
Hence it commutes with (CFA.3); the same coordinate check proves that
all source inclusions $W\subseteq W'$ and leg inclusions $S\subseteq S'$
commute with these face maps.  The source inclusion is inclusion on
each present degree-zero leg and the identity on the central term.
Its cochain identity is the equality of the indicated signed theta
terms before and after inclusion.

For the joint leg mask, set $u=\phi-\widehat\psi$ and
$v=(\phi+\widehat\psi)/2$.  Then
$\phi=v+u/2$ and $\psi=\widehat v-\widehat u/2$, and the differential
is $\Theta u$.  Injectivity of $\Theta$ gives a full diagonal copy of
$W$ in degree zero and $Q_W=\mathscr B/\Theta W$ in degree one.
For a plus-only leg injectivity of $\Theta$ gives zero in degree zero;
for a minus-only leg the Fourier isomorphism and the original minus
sign give the same conclusion.  In all cases the image is $\Theta W$.
Taking active coordinates proves
\[
 H^1(C_{W,S}[A])=Q_W[A],\qquad
 H^0(C_{W,S}[A])=
 \begin{cases}W[A],&S=\{+,-\},\\0,&|S|=1.
 \end{cases}                                                  \tag{CFA.6}
\]
For (CFA.5)'s arithmetic complex, taking coordinates in (CFA.2) gives
\[
 0\to V[A]\xrightarrow{\Theta[A]}\mathscr B_U[A]
       \xrightarrow{q[A]}(T_UQ)[A]\to0.                       \tag{CFA.7}
\]
Every vector in the last kernel has unique original source coordinates
under $\Theta$; this proves exactness directly.  The boundary relation
on face $A$ is therefore exactly $(\Theta V)[A]$.

For the independent split carrier, the coordinatewise lift of the
differential squares to $e_A$ on an element of face $A$, because the
ordinary differential squares to zero without changing the face.
Even outside the nonzero ordinary degrees, the split carrier of the
zero amplitude space is $G(0)^n=\{\tau,e\}^n$.  The supported cycle
condition is the equality of the differential with the supported-zero
map on that face; it is precisely the ordinary linear cycle condition.
The boundary coequalizer identifies exactly pairs whose difference is
an ordinary boundary within that same face.  Thus (CFA.6)--(CFA.7)
compute the supported cohomology without replacing the independent
face skeleton by one globally absent zero object.

\subsection{The complete original section and its Euler boundary}

Let $h(x)=\prod_{\rho\in Z}(x-\rho)^{m_\rho}$ be any nonempty actual
finite full-order packet with $Z\subset U$, where
$m_\rho=\operatorname{ord}_\rho g$ and
\[
 g(s)=2\xi(s)=s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),\qquad
 E_h=A_{\rm sp}/(h),\quad d_h=\deg h,
 \quad A_h=M_x:E_h\to E_h.                                  \tag{CFA.8}
\]
Every original nilpotent coefficient through order $m_\rho-1$ is
retained.  The statements on an empty packet use $E_1=0$ and zero
maps, without asking for an inverse unit in a zero vector space.
For a nonempty packet define the original maps of (AG6)--(AG8):
\[
\begin{gathered}
 v_h(s)=g(s)/h(s),\qquad \upsilon_h=j_hv_h\in E_h,
 \qquad\varepsilon_h=\upsilon_h^{-1},\\
 \phi_*=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2},\qquad
 H_{P(D)\phi_*}=P,\qquad F_h=S_h^{\mathscr B}\Theta\phi_*,\\
 h(D)F_h=\Theta\phi_*,\qquad \mathcal MF_h=v_h,\qquad
 P_h(u)=r_h(\varepsilon_hu),\qquad s_hu=P_h(u)(D)F_h,\\
 J_h=j_h\mathcal M,\qquad \sigma_h=q s_h.
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFA.9}
\]
Here $r_h:E_h\to\mathbb C[x]_{<d_h}$ is the unique monic remainder
representative and $j_h$ retains all the specified Taylor jets.
The original Euler range identity supplies $S_h^{\mathscr B}$ on
this input because $g$ has the required zeros.  Injectivity of $h(D)$
on $\mathscr B$ makes the inverse unique.  Full orders give
$v_h(\rho)\ne0$ at every retained centre, so $\varepsilon_h$ is the
inverse of the full unit jet, including its nilpotent coefficients.

Taking Mellin transforms gives
$\mathcal M(s_hu)=v_h P_h(u)$, so its $h$-jet is
$\upsilon_h\varepsilon_hu=u$.  Therefore $J_hs_h=1$ and $s_h$ is
injective.  Since $h(D)s_hu=\Theta(P_h(u)(D)\phi_*)$, every image
belongs to $\mathscr B_U$, with the specified original boundary.
Define
\[
 \ell_h(u)=[x^{d_h-1}]P_h(u),\qquad b_h(u)=\phi_*\ell_h(u).
 \quad
 Ds_h-s_hA_h=\Theta b_h.                                    \tag{CFA.10}
\]
To prove the last identity, the polynomial
$xP_h(u)-P_h(A_hu)$ has zero $h$-remainder and degree at most $d_h$.
Its leading coefficient is $\ell_h(u)$, because the second polynomial
has degree less than $d_h$.  Since $h$ is monic, this polynomial is
exactly $h\ell_h(u)$.  Apply it at $D$ to $F_h$ and use (CFA.9).
This proves the entire coefficient, sign, and source boundary.

Applying these maps in every active coordinate gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
 J_h[A]s_h[A]=1,\qquad q[A]s_h[A]=\sigma_h[A],\\
 D[A]s_h[A]-s_h[A]A_h[A]=\Theta[A]b_h[A],\qquad
 b_h[A](u_i)=(\phi_*\ell_h(u_i))_{i\in A}.
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFA.11}
\]
The proof is the coordinate identity (CFA.10), and (CFA.4) proves
naturality of every map here under every face insertion.  Regard
$E_h[A]$ as a complex concentrated in degree one.  Its map $s_h[A]$
to $C_U[A]$ is a cochain map, since the target differential out of
degree one is zero.  The map $b_h[A]$ has degree minus one, and
(CFA.11) says exactly that the Euler composites differ by the cochain
homotopy $db_h[A]+b_h[A]d=db_h[A]$.  After $q[A]$ the difference
vanishes, proving exact spectral equivariance of $\sigma_h[A]$.
Before the quotient the section is a complex-linear observation and
is not asserted to be a ring map or an $A_{\rm sp}$-linear map.

\subsection{Full-unit divisor transport on every face}

For full packet divisors $h\mid H$, write $b=H/h$; their added and
old zero sets are disjoint, so $b$ and $h$ are relatively prime.
Retain the restriction and arithmetic zero-extension maps
\[
 \pi_{Hh}:E_H\to E_h,\qquad
 \iota_{hH}:E_h\to E_H,
 \qquad\pi\iota=1,\quad\iota\pi=M_{e_{hH}},                  \tag{CFA.12}
\]
where $e_{hH}$ is one on old local factors and zero on the added
ones.  The polynomial Chinese remainder theorem gives these exact
maps: Bezout for $h,b$ constructs the complementary idempotents,
and multiplication by them inserts the prescribed old or new jets.
Thus $\pi$ is unital, whereas $\iota$ preserves products and sends
$1$ to $e_{hH}$.  Both are spectral-polynomial linear.
The raw numerator map is a different map:
\[
 \mu_b:[P]_h\longmapsto[bP]_H,\qquad
 \mu_b=\iota M_{[b]_h},\qquad
 \iota=\mu_bM_{[b]_h^{-1}}.                                  \tag{CFA.13}
\]
The value on each old factor is multiplication by its full unit
$[b]_h$, and the value on each added factor is zero to the full order.
These facts prove both equations and well-definedness.

The following are literal original function and polynomial identities:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 v_h=bv_H,\quad b(D)F_H=F_h,\quad
 \pi\varepsilon_H=[b]_h\varepsilon_h,\\
 r_H(\varepsilon_H\iota u)=bP_h(u),\qquad
 s_H\iota=s_h,\quad\sigma_H\iota=\sigma_h,\quad
 \ell_H\iota=\ell_h.
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFA.14}
\]
For the second function identity, applying $h(D)$ to both sides
gives $\Theta\phi_*$, and injectivity of $h(D)$ proves equality.
The inverse-unit equation follows by restricting
$v_h=bv_H$ and taking the inverse in the old finite quotient.
The polynomial $bP_h(u)$ has degree less than $\deg H$.
On old factors, $v_H bP_h(u)$ has jet $u$; on new factors it has
zero jet because $v_Hb=v_h$ vanishes to their full orders.
Thus its full jet is $\iota u$.  Multiplication by the invertible
$\upsilon_H$ proves that this is the required unique remainder
of $\varepsilon_H\iota u$.  Applying that polynomial at $D$ to
$F_H$ proves the section identity; applying $q$ proves the quotient
identity.  Monicity of $b$ identifies the top coefficient of
$bP_h(u)$ with that of $P_h(u)$, proving the identity for $\ell$.

On each coefficient face, (CFA.14) holds in every active coordinate.
Equation (CFA.4) then proves all divisor/face squares commute.
For a chain of full divisors, restriction and zero insertion compose
because their values agree at every local factor; the raw numerator
maps compose by multiplication of their literal polynomials.  None
of these equalities replaces $\mu_b$ by $\iota$ without its unit.
The independent split lift $G(\iota)^n$ preserves every coefficient
mask, and its finite mixed unit has image
$(e_{hH}^\bullet,\tau,\ldots,\tau)$, so it is not falsely declared
to preserve the larger packet's unit.

\subsection{The exact dilation homotopy on the original source}

Let $R_aF(x)=F(x/a)$ for $a>0$ on both original spaces.
Positive dilation preserves evenness and the value at zero, and it
multiplies the integral by $a$; hence it preserves $V$.
It preserves $\mathscr B$ by substituting in each Euler seminorm.
Direct substitution in the theta sum gives
$\Theta R_a=R_a\Theta$, and in the Mellin integral gives
$\mathcal MR_aF=a^s\mathcal MF$.
Write $L=\log a$ and $A_{h,t}=\exp(tA_h)$, so $A_{h,L}$ is
the actual finite multiplication by $j_h(a^s)$.  Define
\[
 B_{h,a}(u)=\int_0^L R_{e^{L-t}}\phi_*\,
                          \ell_h(A_{h,t}u)\,dt\quad\text{in }V.
                                                               \tag{CFA.15}
\]
This integral is taken with its indicated orientation also for
$L<0$.  It is an original Schwartz-space integral.  On the compact
interval between $0$ and $L$, the dilation parameters range in a
compact subset of $(0,\infty)$.  For a Schwartz seminorm
$p_{r,j}(\phi)=\sup_x|x^r\phi^{(j)}(x)|$, substitution gives
$p_{r,j}(R_c\phi)=c^{r-j}p_{r,j}(\phi)$.  The same identity with
the Euler derivatives of $\phi$ controls derivatives in $t$.
The finite-dimensional function $\ell_h(A_{h,t}u)$ is smooth.
Thus Riemann sums converge in every Schwartz seminorm, defining
the integral in the complete Schwartz space.  Each integrand has
value zero at zero and integral zero; these are continuous functionals
on that space, so the result belongs to $V$.

Differentiation in the same seminorms gives
\[
 \frac{d}{dt}\bigl(R_{e^{L-t}}s_hA_{h,t}u\bigr)
 =-R_{e^{L-t}}(Ds_h-s_hA_h)A_{h,t}u.
                                                               \tag{CFA.16}
\]
Indeed the derivative of $R_{e^{L-t}}$ is $-D R_{e^{L-t}}$, and
the derivative of $A_{h,t}$ is $A_hA_{h,t}$.  The two terms give
the displayed difference.  Integrate from $0$ to $L$ and use
(CFA.10) and $\Theta R_c=R_c\Theta$ to obtain exactly
\[
 R_as_h-s_hA_{h,L}=\Theta B_{h,a}.                             \tag{CFA.17}
\]
The commutation with the integral follows from the original continuous
theta map, or by its convergent original seminorm estimates on these
compact families.  This formula includes $a=1$, when both sides are
zero.  It is a source-level cochain homotopy, not an assertion that
$s_h$ intertwines dilation on its nose.  Coordinatewise application
and (CFA.4) give this same homotopy naturally on every face.
Applying $q$ gives the exact action relation
$\overline R_a\sigma_h=\sigma_hA_{h,L}$.
All multiplicities are retained: on the $\rho$ factor this action is
\[
 a^\rho\sum_{j=0}^{m_\rho-1}
       \frac{(\log a)^j}{j!}N_\rho^j,\qquad
 N_\rho=M_{x-\rho}.                                         \tag{CFA.18}
\]
It is the full Taylor expansion of $a^s$ modulo the actual factor.
The divisor identities (CFA.14), their commutation with $A_h$, and
the coordinate formula for the integral also give
$B_{H,a}\iota_{hH}=B_{h,a}$.  Thus the full source homotopies, not
only their quotients, have the exact divisor/face compatibility.

\subsection{A strict cochain map that can actually be balanced}

The section $s_h$ is not $A_{\rm sp}$-linear for any nonempty packet.
For example take $u=\upsilon_h$, so $P_h(u)=1$ and
$h(D)s_hu=\Theta\phi_*\ne0$, although $hu=0$ in $E_h$.
Here $\phi_*\ne0$ and injectivity of $\Theta$ prove the nonvanishing.
Thus $O\otimes_{A_{\rm sp}}s_h$ is not a defined tensor arrow.
There is a strict replacement with the same original cohomology map.
Put
\[
 e_h=r_h(\varepsilon_h),\qquad
 F'_h=e_h(D)F_h,\qquad \phi'_h=e_h(D)\phi_*,\qquad
 P_h^{\rm free}=[A_{\rm sp}\xrightarrow{h}A_{\rm sp}]
                                                               \tag{CFA.19}
\]
with degrees zero and one.  Define
\[
 \kappa_h:P_h^{\rm free}\longrightarrow C_U,\qquad
 \kappa_h^0(P)=P(D)\phi'_h,\quad
 \kappa_h^1(P)=P(D)F'_h.                                     \tag{CFA.20}
\]
Both maps are $A_{\rm sp}$-linear because the polynomial actions
commute.  Moreover $h(D)F'_h=\Theta\phi'_h$, so
$\kappa_h^1(hP)=\Theta\kappa_h^0(P)$, proving the cochain identity.
For every polynomial $P$, retain the exact polynomial quotient
\[
 R_h(P)=\frac{P e_h-r_h(\varepsilon_h[P]_h)}{h},\qquad
 \kappa_h^1(P)-s_h([P]_h)=\Theta\bigl(R_h(P)(D)\phi_*\bigr).
                                                               \tag{CFA.21}
\]
The numerator has zero $h$-remainder, so the quotient is a polynomial.
Applying the numerator at $D$ to $F_h$ and using (CFA.9) proves
the displayed equality.  The degree-one cohomology of the free
source is $E_h$, and (CFA.21) proves that its induced map is exactly
$\sigma_h$.  Thus no new spectral injection or replacement unit
has been substituted.

For every face, take the direct sum of (CFA.20).  It commutes with
zero insertion in both degrees by (CFA.4) and can be balanced at
every analytic stalk $O=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C,s_0}$ by the actual
map $x\mapsto s$:
\[
 [O[A]\xrightarrow{h(s)}O[A]]
       \xrightarrow{\ O\otimes\kappa_h[A]\ }
 [\,(O\otimes_{A_{\rm sp}}V)[A]
               \xrightarrow{\Theta[A]}(O\otimes_{A_{\rm sp}}\mathscr B_U)[A]].
                                                               \tag{CFA.22}
\]
The formula is balanced because (CFA.20) is polynomial-linear.
The stalk $O$ is torsion-free over the PID $A_{\rm sp}$ and hence
flat: it is the directed union of its finite generated torsion-free
submodules, each free over the PID; tensoring by free modules and
taking filtered colimits preserve exactness.  Thus its degree-one
map is the actual balanced version of $\sigma_h[A]$.
The finite-dimensional section $s_h[A]$ remains a complex-linear
observation with (CFA.11) and (CFA.17) as its full source homotopies.
This keeps both descriptions connected by (CFA.21), rather than
dropping the section when passing to the strict balanced map.

The divisor arrows between these strict free vertices must also be
specified.  For nonempty full divisors $h\mid H=bh$, put
$a_{hH}=r_h([b]_h^{-1})$ and define
\[
 L_{hH}:P_h^{\rm free}\longrightarrow P_H^{\rm free},\qquad
 L_{hH}^0(P)=a_{hH}P,\quad L_{hH}^1(P)=b a_{hH}P.
                                                               \tag{CFA.22a}
\]
Its cochain identity is $H a_{hH}=b a_{hH}h$.
On degree-one cohomology, the polynomial $ba_{hH}$ equals one
at the old factors and vanishes at the added factors, so this map
induces exactly $\iota_{hH}$.  The original sections commute strictly
by (CFA.14), whereas their free resolutions have the explicit homotopy
\[
\begin{gathered}
 w_{hH}=\frac{a_{hH}e_H-e_h}{h}\in\mathbb C[x],\qquad
 K_{hH}^1(P)=P(D)w_{hH}(D)\phi_*,\quad K_{hH}^0=0,\\
 \kappa_H L_{hH}-\kappa_h=dK_{hH}+K_{hH}d.
\end{gathered}                                                 \tag{CFA.22b}
\]
The polynomial exists because
$[e_H]_h=[b]_h\varepsilon_h$ and $[a_{hH}b]_h=1$, so its
numerator has zero $h$-remainder.  In degree zero the difference
is $P(D)(a_{hH}e_H-e_h)(D)\phi_*=K_{hH}^1(hP)$.
In degree one, using $b(D)F_H=F_h$, the difference is
$P(D)(a_{hH}e_H-e_h)(D)F_h
=\Theta(P(D)w_{hH}(D)\phi_*)$.  These are exactly the two
components of (CFA.22b), proving its sign and degree.

For $h\mid H\mid J$, put
$\eta=(a_{hH}a_{HJ}-a_{hJ})/h$.
Its numerator vanishes modulo $h$: both factors give the inverse
of $J/h$ there.  Thus $\eta$ is a polynomial, and the degree-minus-one
map $T^1:P_h^{{\rm free},1}\to P_J^{{\rm free},0}$ given by
multiplication by $\eta$ satisfies
\[
 L_{HJ}L_{hH}-L_{hJ}=dT+Td.                                  \tag{CFA.22c}
\]
In degree zero its right side is multiplication by $h\eta$,
and in degree one it is multiplication by $J\eta$; these are
precisely the differences of (CFA.22a)'s two multipliers.
Consequently free-resolution divisor composition is retained with
its exact homotopy, not asserted to be strict in these remainder
coordinates.  All multipliers here are polynomial-linear, and the
homotopies in (CFA.22b)--(CFA.22c) are $A_{\rm sp}$-linear.
Their direct sums commute
with every face insertion and can be balanced just as (CFA.20).
The attachment homotopies have the exact further composition identity
\[
 K_{HJ}L_{hH}+K_{hH}=K_{hJ}+\kappa_JT.                        \tag{CFA.22d}
\]
On a degree-one input its left multiplier on $\phi_*$ is
$b a_{hH}w_{HJ}+w_{hH}$, while the right multiplier is
$w_{hJ}+\eta e_J$.  Substituting their displayed polynomial
definitions makes both equal to
$(a_{hH}a_{HJ}e_J-e_h)/h$.  All other components are zero by
degree.  This proves compatibility of the attachment with the
composition homotopy itself, with its actual coefficients.
The empty finite packet remains its specified zero amplitude
observation; its independent split carrier still has every face
of $G(0)^n$.  The inverse-unit free vertices here are indexed by
nonempty packets.

\subsection{Every proper source retains its residual class}

For any proper $D$-invariant $W\subseteq V$ the original exact
sequence is
\[
 0\to V/W\xrightarrow{[\phi]\mapsto[\Theta\phi]_W}
       Q_W\longrightarrow Q\to0,\qquad Q_W=\mathscr B/\Theta W.
                                                               \tag{CFA.23}
\]
The first map is well-defined.  If $\Theta\phi\in\Theta W$,
injectivity of $\Theta$ gives $\phi\in W$, proving injectivity.
The quotient definitions identify its image with the last kernel
and prove surjectivity of the last arrow.  Taking active coordinates
retains the kernel $(V/W)[A]$.  For $W\subseteq W'\subseteq V$,
the quotient $V/W\to V/W'$ has kernel $W'/W$, and the quotient
$Q_W\to Q_{W'}$ has kernel identified with $W'/W$ by
$[\phi]\mapsto[\Theta\phi]_W$.  The coordinate maps commute with
all face arrows by their explicit zero-insertion formula.

Let $s_{h,W}(u)=[s_hu]_W$ as a complex-linear amplitude map to
$Q_W$.  Equation (CFA.10) has the exact residual form
\[
 D s_{h,W}-s_{h,W}A_h
   =\bigl(u\mapsto[\Theta\phi_*\ell_h(u)]_W\bigr),            \tag{CFA.24}
\]
which corresponds under the first map of (CFA.23) to
$[\phi_*\ell_h(u)]\in V/W$.  It is zero exactly when that
source correction lies in $W$.  Likewise (CFA.17) gives a precise
residual class $[B_{h,a}(u)]\in V/W$ when its two target functions
are compared in $Q_W$.  Dilation as an action on source-labelled
quotients actually goes from $Q_W$ to $Q_{R_aW}$, since
$R_a\Theta W=\Theta R_aW$; no invariance of $W$ under all dilations
is assumed.  In that target the exact comparison is
\[
 \overline R_a s_{h,W}(u)-s_{h,R_aW}(A_{h,L}u)
             =[\Theta B_{h,a}(u)]_{R_aW},                    \tag{CFA.25}
\]
whose residual source class lies in $V/R_aW$.
Applying the transition to the full source kills these residual
classes by (CFA.23).  Their coefficientwise versions lie in
$(V/W)[A]$ or $(V/R_aW)[A]$ with all labels retained.
The strict cochain map (CFA.20) has degree-zero source in $V$;
it is not silently retyped as a map to a proper source not containing
its source vectors.  Thus the full-source equivariance statements
do not erase any proper-label obstruction.

\subsection{Naturality to the independent mixed observation vertices}

Only the cochain transport of the original mixed observation is needed
here.  On the independent carrier $G(M)^n$ define the filling map
$\operatorname{syn}_M$ to keep the wholly absent tuple absent and
otherwise make every slot present with its original amplitude.
For any linear $f:M\to N$, one has
\[
 G(f)^n\operatorname{syn}_M
       =\operatorname{syn}_N G(f)^n.                          \tag{CFA.26}
\]
If the input is wholly absent, both sides are wholly absent.
Otherwise its mask remains nonempty after $G(f)^n$, even when all
amplitudes vanish.  Both sides then have all coordinates supported
with coefficients $f(m_i)$, taking $m_i=0$ at a formerly absent
slot.  This proves the equality with its exact empty and zero cases.
It applies to the original section, quotient, divisor, and analytic
comparison maps in their declared linear categories.

At the finite PS algebra $D_{E_h,n}$ this filling map is its proved
synchronization by $\mathsf E_{n,h}=(1^\bullet,e,\ldots,e)$.
Under the nonunital divisor map it has image
$(e_{hH}^\bullet,e,\ldots,e)$, which need not equal
$\mathsf E_{n,H}$.  Thus the synchronization square for this map
is justified by (CFA.26), not by applying a unital localization
universal property to a nonunital arrow.  Its synchronized section
has the actual image of the unit $\mathsf E_{n,h}$, not the original
mixed unit $(1^\bullet,\tau,\ldots,\tau)$.

The PS arithmetic retraction is taken after synchronization.  To verify
its coordinate formula, in $B_n=E_h[t]/(t^n+1)$ multiplication by
$t^j$ for $1\le j<n$ has no diagonal entry in the original basis
$1,t,\ldots,t^{n-1}$, because it shifts indices by the nonzero
class $j$ modulo $n$.  Multiplication by the constant coefficient
$b_0$ has $n$ diagonal entries $b_0$.  Hence
\[
 r_n\left(\sum b_it^i\right)=
 \frac1n\operatorname{Tr}_{B_n/E_h}(M_{\sum b_it^i})=b_0.
                                                               \tag{CFA.27}
\]
Its independent-carrier composite sends an empty mask to $\tau$
and every nonempty mask to $p(b_0)^\bullet$, including supported
zero when coefficient slot zero was absent.  Raw projection to slot
zero instead returns $\tau$ on that latter face.  These are distinct
arrows.  The PS constant section is carried to
$(f^\bullet,e,\ldots,e)$; (CFA.27) sends it back to $f^\bullet$.
Consequently the section and retraction remain exactly the original
arithmetic retract on all coefficient faces, with the factor $1/n$
and the changed masks retained.

For the original coefficient Frobenius, let $q_0$ be an odd prime
power with $\gcd(q_0,n)=1$.  Its integral signed permutation has
\[
 t^i\longmapsto(-1)^{\lfloor q_0i/n\rfloor}t^{q_0i\bmod n}.
                                                               \tag{CFA.28}
\]
It takes coefficient mask $A$ to $q_0A$ modulo $n$.
Applying any complex-linear map to a coordinate commutes with its
specified sign, and permuting positions commutes with applying the
same map in each position.  This proves exact compatibility of
(CFA.28) with the sections, source differentials, quotient maps,
boundary homotopies, and face transitions.  The original nilpotent
arithmetic action (CFA.18) instead acts within each face.

For completeness its compatibility with the finite product has a
different type from a semiring automorphism.  Write $\star$ for the
original PS negacyclic convolution, retaining every minus sign for
a wrap and its relation $t^n+1=0$.  For $a,c>0$ the coefficient
elements satisfy $j_h(a^s)j_h(c^s)=j_h((ac)^s)$.  Every convolution
summand therefore gives
\[
 (A_{h,\log a}b)\star(A_{h,\log c}d)
       =A_{h,\log(ac)}(b\star d).                            \tag{CFA.29}
\]
The scalar elements commute with every coefficient and with the wrap
sign, so summing proves the equality, including supported cancellations
and absent slots.  In particular the diagonal action with parameter
$a$ has target action with parameter $a^2$, not $a$.
The finite convolution is an algebra observation in degree zero.
The packet source in $C_U$ is in cohomological degree one; a degree-zero
chain map $C_U\otimes C_U\to C_U$ induces zero on its degree-two
cohomology because $H^2(C_U)=0$.  It therefore cannot realize the
nonzero finite packet product on those degree-one source classes.
The original DGA product of (AG14) is concatenation into tensor length
two and degree two.  The attachment keeps those two differently typed
products as their actual arrows.

\subsection{The full coherent and tensor comparison on all faces}

The original Mellin isomorphism, with its original inverse, is
\[
 \mathfrak m:T_UQ\xrightarrow{\sim}
 E_U:=\bigoplus_{\rho\in U:g(\rho)=0}\mathcal O_\rho/(g),
 \qquad[F]\mapsto([\mathcal MF]_\rho)_\rho.                  \tag{CFA.30}
\]
Its complete original-function proof is (CAU.9)--(CAU.13).
For clarity, its coordinate inverse on a full common packet $h$
sends $\alpha$ to the class of
\[
 S_h^{\mathscr B}\Theta\bigl(r_h(\varepsilon_h\alpha)(D)\phi_*\bigr),
 \qquad\mathcal M F_\alpha=(g/h)r_h(\varepsilon_h\alpha).
                                                               \tag{CFA.31}
\]
The Euler inverse exists because this theta input has all required
moment-zero jets.  Its Mellin identity gives exactly $\alpha$ at
each retained centre, using the complete inverse unit.  A different
polynomial representative differs by $hR$ and changes the source
by $\Theta(R(D)\phi_*)$.  If a torsion representative has zero
full local Mellin class, its equation $p\mathcal MF=gH_\phi$
forces every $p$-jet of $H_\phi$ to vanish.  The original range
identity gives $\phi=p(D)\psi$, and injectivity of $p(D)$ on
$\mathscr B$ gives $F=\Theta\psi$.  This proves both inverse
identities used here and does not assume the existence of an offcritical
zero.

On face $A$, apply these maps to each active coordinate.  Its finitely
many entries have altogether finite spectral support, so one common
full packet contains all of them.  Formula (CFA.14) proves that enlarging
this common packet changes none of the original functions in the
sections.  Thus (CFA.30) gives the exact face isomorphism
$(T_UQ)[A]\simeq E_U[A]$, naturally under all face maps by (CFA.4).
An active zero entry remains active at its supported zero.
At every analytic stalk with $s_0\in U$, balancing and the canonical original maps
$j$ and $\beta$ give
\[
 M[A]=N[A]\oplus K[A],\qquad
 j[A]:N[A]\hookrightarrow M[A],\quad
 \beta[A]j[A]=1,\qquad N=O/(g).                              \tag{CFA.32}
\]
The coordinate formulas for $j,\beta$ prove these identities; the
full-source kernel $K$ and the joint-leg diagonal of (CFA.6) remain
separate attached factors.  No field-vector property of $K$ is
asserted for a proper-source kernel from (CFA.23).

For a finite tensor word retain the tuple of coefficient masks
$(A_1,\ldots,A_k)$ and every original source-leg label.  Tensor the
already defined maps over $\mathbb C$, with the original differential
\[
 d(x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k)
 =\sum_{i=1}^k(-1)^{\sum_{j<i}|x_j|}
       x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes dx_i\otimes\cdots\otimes x_k.
                                                               \tag{CFA.33}
\]
The two orders of differentiating distinct positions have opposite
signs and cancel, while the same-position terms vanish because
$d^2x_i=0$.  Thus this is the original cochain tensor differential.
An equality of the already defined maps holds on each factor of a
pure tensor and therefore on their tensor product; pure tensors span,
so every face, divisor, quotient, and section square remains an exact
square.  A tensor of injective complex-linear sections is injective:
extend a basis of each image to a basis of its target, obtaining a
linear retraction in each factor, and tensor those retractions.

Tensor source homotopies have the exact signs as well.  If $f_i,g_i$
are degree-zero cochain maps and $H_i$ has degree minus one with
$f_i-g_i=dH_i+H_id$, then the tensor homotopy is
\[
 H=\sum_{i=1}^k
       g_1\otimes\cdots\otimes g_{i-1}\otimes H_i
            \otimes f_{i+1}\otimes\cdots\otimes f_k.         \tag{CFA.34}
\]
On a homogeneous input the $i$th summand includes the sign
$(-1)^{\sum_{j<i}|x_j|}$ forced by the degree of $H_i$.
Computing $dH+Hd$ cancels the different-position terms with the
same signs as (CFA.33).  The remaining terms are
$\sum_i g_1\otimes\cdots\otimes g_{i-1}\otimes(f_i-g_i)
\otimes f_{i+1}\otimes\cdots\otimes f_k$; the middle products
telescope to $\bigotimes_i f_i-\bigotimes_i g_i$.
This proves the full tensor transport of the Euler and dilation
homotopies, without asserting a strict section intertwining that
the original source does not have.

The finite tensor packet on a word $h_1,\ldots,h_k$ is exactly
\[
 \bigotimes_{i=1}^k E_{h_i}
 \simeq\frac{\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_k]}
              {(h_1(x_1),\ldots,h_k(x_k))}.                   \tag{CFA.35}
\]
Successive monic division gives a basis with exponent of $x_i$
strictly less than $\deg h_i$, and the tensor map takes the tensor
of the one-variable remainder bases to precisely that basis.
This proves the isomorphism and retains all local exponents
$0\le j_i<m_{\rho_i}$.  On each independent face of that word,
(CFA.31) acts in each original coefficient coordinate, so its unit
is the tensor of the literal full inverse unit jets.  No nilpotent
coordinate or coefficient mask is replaced by one common support bit.

\subsection{Finite-stage continuity and the attachment category}

Every fixed-packet section has a direct original seminorm bound.
Choose a finite basis $u_j$ of $E_h$, write $F_j=s_hu_j$, and let
$p$ be any original seminorm of $\mathscr B$.  Linearity gives
\[
 p\left(s_h\sum_j c_ju_j\right)
                \le\sum_j|c_j|p(F_j).                       \tag{CFA.36}
\]
Thus $s_h$ is continuous in its actual finite-dimensional topology.
Continuity of its Mellin inverse on the image can also be seen
directly.  For $\rho$ of real part $\sigma$, choose an integer
$N>|\sigma|$, put
$p_+(F)=\sup_{x>0}x^N|F(x)|$ and
$p_-(F)=\sup_{x>0}x^{-N}|F(x)|$.  Splitting the integral at one gives
\[
 \left|\frac{(\mathcal MF)^{(j)}(\rho)}{j!}\right|
 \le\frac{p_+(F)}{(N-\sigma)^{j+1}}
       +\frac{p_-(F)}{(N+\sigma)^{j+1}}.                     \tag{CFA.37}
\]
Indeed $\int_1^\infty x^{\sigma-N}(\log x)^j\,dx/x
=j!/(N-\sigma)^{j+1}$ by $x=e^y$, and
$\int_0^1x^{\sigma+N}|\log x|^j\,dx/x
=j!/(N+\sigma)^{j+1}$ by $x=e^{-y}$.
The same integrable bounds justify differentiation at the fixed
jet.  Every fixed finite Mellin observation is therefore continuous.
Together with $J_hs_h=1$ this proves that the finite section is a
topological embedding onto its image.  The quotient map $q$ is
continuous in the original quotient topology, so the induced section
$\sigma_h$ is continuous; its inverse $J_h$ descends continuously
through $q$ since $J_h\Theta=0$.

Finite zero insertions and fixed-face maps are finite linear maps.
For the labelled disjoint-union topology, addition has output mask
$A\cup B$, and negacyclic multiplication has output mask $A+B$
in $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$.  A nonempty supported sum remains
supported even when its amplitude cancels, so these output masks
are fixed on a pair of input faces.  On that pair the amplitude
formulas are respectively linear and polynomial finite-dimensional
maps, proving continuity there; the disjoint-union topology proves
continuity on the whole finite mixed carrier.  These are fixed-packet,
fixed-$n$ facts.  Formula (CFA.36) does not give a uniform bound as
the packet or $n$ varies, and it does not identify the topology of an
infinite section $s_U$ with a direct-limit topology.  No such additional
continuity or estimate is asserted by this attachment.

Finally, the attachment is one specified extension of the represented
object.  Its generating indices are $(W,S,h,n,A)$ with independent
$S$ and $A$, together with the actual full-source and empty-packet
vertices.  Its values are the face complexes, strict free complexes,
finite observation modules, and the original mixed carriers above.
For the cochain part use the graded complex-linear path category:
degree-zero generators are the displayed cochain maps, and the
specified homotopies are degree-minus-one linear generators.
The target Hom complexes have their actual differential
\[
 \partial f=d_{\rm target}f-(-1)^{|f|}f d_{\rm source}.
                                                               \tag{CFA.38}
\]
Direct expansion using $d^2=0$ gives $\partial^2f=0$, and expansion
of both sides gives
$\partial(fg)=(\partial f)g+(-1)^{|f|}f(\partial g)$.
In particular a cochain map has $\partial f=0$, while a homotopy
has $\partial H=dH+Hd$.  Impose the proved coordinate and linear
composition relations, including $f-g=\partial H$ with the displayed
generator $H$.  This does not identify the two unmodified cochain
paths $f$ and $g$.  Sending each generator to its actual graded map
and each linear combination of paths to the same linear combination
of composites is well-defined because precisely these identities
were proved above.  The differential formula proves compatibility
with the graded structure.

Include all tensor words with (CFA.33) and their Koszul permutations.
