# Declaration sources and editorial response

Checked on **17 September 2026**. This is an additive attribution and exposition record for the research programme. It does not replace the mathematical archive, restart completed calculations, certify the programme, or assert endorsement by either declaration's authors. Human readability and accurate provenance are commitments of this programme. The companion explains its mathematics, credits the human sources it uses, and records the scope and limits of the source audit.

## Scope and authentication

The source question is attribution and editorial policy: what concerns do the two declarations state, who wrote them, and how should this project respond in its public reading material? This is not a verification of a mathematical theorem or a novelty search. Coefficients, gradings, variance and completions therefore do not apply to these policy sources. A declaration supplies no missing mathematical implication.

Both official declaration websites were read. Complete creator names and deposited ordering were checked against the Zenodo-deposited DataCite DOI records, and are retained without an `and others` truncation in [DECLARATIONS.bib](DECLARATIONS.bib). The website's named list and the deposited creator list agree in membership for each declaration. Website signatories or endorsers outside the deposited creator lists have not been promoted to authors.

Direct Zenodo landing-page/API requests returned timeout or HTTP 504 errors during this check. The official Leiden PDF link also timed out. Therefore the record authenticates the declaration text through its official website and the bibliography through the DOI registrant's deposited metadata; it does **not** claim a successful direct Zenodo API/PDF read. No PDF page number is asserted. Section and paragraph locators below refer to the web text. Website sections are stable descriptive locators; paragraph and recommendation numbers supplied below are editorial counts, not numbering printed by the declarations. No complete declaration text has been copied into this output.

- **Precise locations:** the fourteen prose paragraphs between the title/date and named list, counted in reading order. Source synopsis: the declaration places insight and human mathematical transmission ahead of problem-solving benchmarks. It criticizes rushed announcements that omit careful writing, new ideas and prior work, and emphasizes the community's role in developing ideas. [Primary text](https://mathandai.org/)

## Editorial practice

The following are **project editorial decisions**, formulated for this repository. They are not quotations, additional rules attributed to the declarations, or claims that the declarations prescribe this exact file structure. The locators explain the connection. They do not confer approval on the work.

| Concern and source locator | Concrete addition to the reader and repository | Evidence needed before marking it done |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical understanding and teaching: D1 paragraphs 3–8; D2 preamble values 1 and 5 | Provide a short route from the motivating question to the original objects, a notation dictionary, a worked finite example, and a diagram whose arrows are defined. Explain what each construction contributes before giving its technical details. | The explanatory text names its audience and prerequisites, supplies all definitions needed for its examples, and points to complete argument locations. A third-party human reader's assessment must be reported as such only if actually obtained. |
| Point-of-use human credit: D1 paragraph 9; D2 individual recommendations 2 and 6 | Give each imported idea or theorem an explicit human-source citation at the first substantive use. Preserve a machine-readable bibliography and a claim-to-source table recording the exact page/theorem, edition and role. | The cited text was located, the borrowed statement was extracted, and hypotheses and notation were compared with this project's use. An eponym or search hit alone does not complete the entry. |
| Honest reconstruction: D2 individual recommendation 6 | Search older web sessions, local papers, translations, notes and formal dependencies. Mark the source of an idea separately from later exposition of it. Keep unresolved attributions visible, with search scope and specific missing evidence. | Corpus inventory, occurrence list, source-resolution status and dated reconstruction record. A bounded audit cannot claim to identify every unobservable contribution to model training. |
| Reuse and novelty: D1 paragraphs 7–9; D2 preamble values 2 and 4 | Label a step as direct reuse, notation translation, an established corollary, a new proof of a known statement, an unverified candidate contribution, or an open question. Explain the exact difference from prior work before suggesting novelty. | A source comparison with the same hypotheses and conclusion strength, plus a recorded literature-search boundary for any novelty claim. No source-free claims of precedence. |
| Tool disclosure: D2 individual recommendation 1 | Add a dated disclosure of the tools actually used, their role in discovery, drafting, computation, translation and checking, and available versions. Identify scripts and formal proofs at their exact scope. | Recorded tool activity, source revisions and check receipts. An AI review is identified as an AI review; compilation, numerical evidence and a formal theorem each receive their own scope. |
| Human responsibility: D2 individual recommendations 3–5 | Distinguish authorized public human attribution from automated assistance. State who, if anyone, has accepted responsibility for which public claims. Retain rights and licences for third-party material. | An explicit public attribution instruction or verified public credit record; no inference from filesystem paths, accounts or a model's prose. Do not manufacture a human sign-off. This documentation does not impose a new gate on ongoing mathematical work. |
| Uncertainty and scrutiny: D2 preamble threats 1 and 4; organizational recommendations 3 and 5 | Place the current established result, open target, unresolved defects and review status near the beginning. Give each checked claim a stable revision and evidence locator. Provide a correction route that propagates into the human reader. | Exact check type, checker identity or role when known, revision, scope and outcome. GitHub publication, a DOI, model agreement or source retention alone is not peer review. |
| Public reading route: D1 paragraphs 8–10; D2 individual recommendation 2 | Add a clearly titled human introduction to GitHub and the Zenodo reading route, with links to the short exposition, full proof body, source bibliography, provenance archive and current status. Keep historical source collections intact. | Working links, public-safe metadata, matched edition identifiers and a readable PDF preview where the edition contains a pertinent PDF. These are additions, not replacements for the detailed corpus. |

## Deligne-specific implementation rule

The declaration establishes that Pierre Deligne participates in the September statement. It does **not** establish which of his mathematical results this programme uses. Each occurrence of his name, an eponymous theorem, a weight or mixed-structure argument attributed to him, and any associated coauthor must be traced to the actual mathematical publication and the precise result used. The relevant source may have multiple human authors; credit must follow that source. If a new construction is motivated by his work but does not satisfy a cited theorem's hypotheses, explain the mathematical comparison and the limitation explicitly rather than borrowing the authority of his name. His participation in either declaration is not an endorsement of this programme.
