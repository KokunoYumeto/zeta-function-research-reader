# Split-zero structures and the zeta-function programme

This research programme studies whether a construction that retains both arithmetic coefficients and their support can lead to cohomological control of the Riemann zeta function. Its analytic starting point uses the theta and dilation construction of Ralf Meyer, following Alain Connes. Its geometric ambition is informed by Pierre Deligne's work on weights. The relationship to those human sources is made explicit in the accompanying exposition.

The mathematical development remains open. The accepted edition explained here is the 726-page *Original Mixed Cohomology: Periods, Kernels and Arithmetic Control*. It establishes the identities and estimates stated at its individual proof locations; it does not prove the Riemann hypothesis or produce an off-critical-line zero. Its current calculation studies a hypothetical zero quartet with its complete multiplicities.

1. **[The mathematical exposition](RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.pdf)** explains what the programme asks, defines its objects, proves the elementary comparison maps, and leads to the actual remaining calculation. The [LaTeX source](RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.tex) and its `sources/` directory are included.
2. **[Human sources and their roles](HUMAN_SOURCES.md)** identifies what is inherited from the literature, what is a project calculation, and which source comparisons have actually been checked. The bibliography is available as [BibTeX](REFERENCES.bib).
3. **[The full accepted proof edition](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22803503)** provides the 726-page reader (file 82), complete editable public sources (file 83), and the 51-page new-proof supplement (file 84). The exposition uses R726 for that successor and retains R14 page references to the preserved 692-page predecessor, which remains available as files 79–81.
4. **[The attribution map](ATTRIBUTION_MAP.md)** connects uses to human sources; **[the unresolved register](UNRESOLVED_ATTRIBUTION.md)** identifies remaining source checks. **[Audit coverage](COVERAGE.md)** explains the inspected corpus and the limits of the source reconstruction. **[Authorship and tools](AUTHORSHIP_AND_TOOLS.md)** states the roles of people, AI assistance, proof review, and computational checks.

For the mathematical route, begin with the distinction between an absent coefficient and a present zero. Continue through the theta quotient, complete zero jets, their tensor and cyclic maps, the period observation, and the source and quotient metrics. The final chapters explain why the known total determinant growth does not yet determine its two correlated contributions, and give the precise matrix expression still being studied.

## Why this companion exists

Human readability and accurate provenance are commitments of this programme. The companion explains its mathematics, credits the human sources it uses, and records the scope and limits of the source audit. The [Leiden Declaration](https://leidendeclaration.ai/) and [September Math and AI declaration](https://mathandai.org/) provide context for the discussion of intelligibility and attribution in [the source note](DECLARATIONS.md).

Earlier calculations, source editions, and mathematical continuation instructions are retained. This companion adds a reading route and citation layer; its proof references remain tied to the stated edition even while later mathematical work continues.
