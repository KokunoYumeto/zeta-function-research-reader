# Exposé authors and the Deligne source chain

The programme's comparison with weights cites Pierre Deligne's actual results with their geometric hypotheses. The underlying literature has several distinct authorship roles. [The reader](../RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.pdf) gives the exact local-jet comparison; this note records source ancestry without claiming an arithmetic application of every geometric theorem.

| Inspected source trail | Human credit and precise role | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Weil II 3.2.15 → SGA 4½, *dualité*, 1.3 and 2.1 | Pierre Deligne's duality complements | Citation trail read in Weil II; complete source-to-project application remains separately scoped |
| Weil II 3.3.2–3.3.3 → SGA 7 XXI §5, especially 5.2.2 | Exposé XXI is by Nicholas M. Katz; §5 is the appendix *Théorèmes d'intégralité* by Pierre Deligne | Both roles appear on the [author-hosted scan's first page](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/niveaucoho.pdf); §5 begins on printed p. 383. A citation to the appendix must credit Deligne, while retaining Katz's role for the containing exposé |
| Weil II 6.2.11 → SGA 4½, *Théorèmes de finitude* | Deligne's finiteness results, with an appendix by Luc Illusie | Source pointer recovered; this does not assert that the programme satisfies generic acyclicity hypotheses |
| Historical trace-formula reading → SGA 5 III | Alexander Grothendieck's seminar work; rédaction by Luc Illusie; Jean-Louis Verdier's pairing contribution | Illusie's [first-person historical account](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~luc.illusie/Chronology-SGA5-1.pdf), especially pp. 1–4, identifies these roles. The [author's errata](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~luc.illusie/ErrSGA5III.pdf) must accompany any result-level check |
| SGA 4½ source collection | Arcata lectures by Deligne written up by Jean-François Boutot; Illusie's finiteness appendix; Verdier's derived-category text | These distinctions are stated in the historical account's opening footnote and retained in the bibliography |

The recovered one-variable reference to Michel Raynaud near SGA 5 III 6.8.5 remains incompletely identified. A plausible paper is not substituted for that missing citation. The source audit also preserves the correction that the Roman locator `(I.8.11)` in the relevant Weil II discussion points back to *Weil I*.

These names are not an endorsement list. Exposé authors, contributors and editors are credited for their actual texts. The source hierarchy is not a proof that a proposed new cohomology theory has the properties of étale cohomology.
