# Checked corrections to historical bibliography records

The [163 transcribed bibliography records](SELECTED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json) preserve what nine historical mathematical documents actually cite. Their inclusion does not authenticate every entry or establish a current dependency. The corrections below travel alongside the original records.

1. **A conflated Grothendieck entry.** In the source with SHA-256 `bfdc970b8541c397253d523976bfdf37e35625893bc0ef91887da02d7d4bd1e0`, line 679, key `Grothendieck_SGA4` combines *Récoltes et Semailles*, “Sect. IV,” Springer and 1977. These belong to different bibliographic objects. [Gallimard's announcement preserved by IHES](https://www.ihes.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/CP_Recoltes_Semailles.pdf) identifies the two-volume 2022 edition of *Récoltes et semailles*. SGA 4 comprises Springer LNM 269 and 270 (1972) and 305 (1973), under Artin, Grothendieck and Verdier. [SGA 4½](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0091516) is Deligne's *Cohomologie étale*, LNM 569 (1977), with the stated collaborators. The historical use at line 378 invokes an unidentified comparison theorem to infer equality of operators. Its exact source and applicability remain unresolved; no candidate silently replaces it.
2. **Finite path-integral ancestry.** The historical geometry manuscript `split_support_geometry_arithmetic_curve_v11.tex`, attribution ledger lines 8686–8782 and use lines 7052–7061, cites Freed–Hopkins–Lurie–Teleman. Their [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.0731v2), §5.2, Lemma 5.17, credits Samuel Eilenberg and Saunders Mac Lane's quadratic-map result, *On the groups H(Π,n), II* (1954), Theorem 26.1. This is a recovered recursive citation. It does not certify the historical manuscript's full claimed application or promote this branch into a dependency of the current zeta estimate.
3. **Semiring bibliography.** The Chajda–Eigenthaler–Länger article is *Miskolc Mathematical Notes* 21(1) (2020), 113–125, DOI [10.18514/MMN.2020.2819](https://doi.org/10.18514/MMN.2020.2819). Golan's semiring-fractions chapter is chapter 11, pp. 129–134. The [tau audit](../tau/TAU_GENERAL_SOURCE_METADATA.md) records the original erroneous locators and the exact version checks.
4. **Volume editors versus chapter authors.** *Hochschild homology, trace map and ζ-cycles* is by Alain Connes and Caterina Consani. The containing volume's additional editors must not be made coauthors of that chapter. Its historical use cites Proposition 3.3, Lemma 4.1(iii), Proposition 4.2, Corollary 4.3 and Remark 5.5; this recovered reading report is not a new independent verification of every application.
5. **Physics source versions.** The arXiv records authenticate Connes–Rovelli `gr-qc/9406019` v1; Jens Mund `hep-th/0101227` v2; Ted Jacobson `gr-qc/9504004` v2; and Eling–Guedens–Jacobson `gr-qc/0602001` v1. Jacobson's withdrawal flag applies to v1, not the current v2. A 2026 regenerated HTML date on the Connes–Rovelli text is not an arXiv revision date. These are metadata checks and historical roles, not new physics claims or current analytic dependencies.
