## Jonathan S. Golan

**Authenticated citation:** Jonathan S. Golan, *Semirings and their Applications*. Kluwer Academic Publishers, Dordrecht, 1999. DOI [10.1007/978-94-015-9333-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-015-9333-5). Hardcover ISBN 978-0-7923-5786-5; electronic ISBN 978-94-015-9333-5.

The [current Springer publisher record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-015-9333-5) identifies the author, 1999 copyright and first edition. Its later electronic publication date is 18 April 2013. That electronic date must not replace 1999 in the citation. The [publisher-hosted front matter, PDF page 3](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bfm:978-94-015-9333-5/1) explicitly confirms the original Kluwer publication in 1999. The current Springer imprint is therefore compatible with, rather than a correction of, the historical Kluwer imprint.

The contents identify Chapter 13, additively regular semirings, pp. 143–147; Chapter 14, semimodules, pp. 149–161; and Chapter 17, free, projective and injective semimodules, pp. 191–202. These are verified chapter locators, not certification of an uninspected theorem. Use Golan as a standard background citation unless the exact internal statement is separately read and recorded.

**Status:** primary bibliographic metadata and original imprint authenticated; no book-body theorem extraction or project application audited here.

## Richard P. Stanley and Garrett Birkhoff

**Authenticated historical citation:** Richard P. Stanley, *Enumerative Combinatorics*, Volume 1. Wadsworth & Brooks/Cole, 1986, Theorem 3.4.1. The [author's book page](https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/) confirms the 1986 first publication and a Cambridge second printing in April 1997. The second printing is not the later second edition. The [Springer archive record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4615-9763-6) and its [front matter, PDF pages 3–4](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bfm:978-1-4615-9763-6/1) confirm the original title, volume, author and imprint. The archive DOI is [10.1007/978-1-4615-9763-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-9763-6).

**Theorem and edition authentication:** Stanley's [author-hosted second-edition manuscript](https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf), expressly dated **15 July 2011**, has Theorem 3.4.1 on manuscript p. 290, with proof continuing on p. 291. It represents a finite distributive lattice as the lattice of order ideals of its poset of nonzero join-irreducibles. The map is

\[
L\longrightarrow J(P),\qquad t\longmapsto\{s\in P:s\leq t\},
\]

with inverse given by joining the members of an ideal. The author’s edition-concordance on manuscript p. 659 explicitly matches first-edition Theorem 3.4.1 with second-edition Theorem 3.4.1. This verifies the number through an author-supplied concordance; it is not a claim that the 1986 theorem page was directly inspected. Historical notes on p. 391 attribute this theorem to Birkhoff, reference [3.11], Theorem 17.3; reference [11] on p. 395 identifies the 1933 paper below. Project use must establish finiteness and distributivity of the actual support lattice before applying this theorem. The theorem alone does not classify module fibres or their transport maps.

**Historically specific citation supplied by Stanley:** Garrett Birkhoff, “On the combination of subalgebras,” *Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society* **29** (1933), no. 4, 441–464. DOI [10.1017/S0305004100011464](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100011464). The [Cambridge publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mathematical-proceedings-of-the-cambridge-philosophical-society/article/abs/on-the-combination-of-subalgebras/848B24E326A568DCECF2294F626AC977) authenticates author, title, volume, issue, pages and issue date 30 October 1933. The site's 24 October 2008 online date is not the original publication year. **Theorem 17.3 attribution is checked in Stanley; Birkhoff's original theorem text was not read.**

**Related historical lead, not substituted silently:** Garrett Birkhoff, “Rings of sets,” *Duke Mathematical Journal* **3** (1937), no. 3, 443–454, DOI [10.1215/S0012-7094-37-00334-X](https://doi.org/10.1215/S0012-7094-37-00334-X). The direct [Project Euclid target](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/duke-mathematical-journal/volume-3/issue-3/Rings-of-sets/10.1215/S0012-7094-37-00334-X.short) returned no substantive content in this run. This is a recognizable bibliographic lead, but no primary-text authentication is claimed here. For the historical attribution of the specific finite theorem used by Stanley, the verified author-supplied chain points to **1933, Theorem 17.3**. Do not replace that exact chain with a blanket assertion that 1937 is the earliest source.

**Status:** 1986 edition metadata and author concordance authenticated; theorem statement and proof read in the dated 2011 author manuscript; original Birkhoff theorem text not accessed; applicability to the project's support object not checked in this bibliography subtask.

## S. M. Yusuf

**Bibliographically corroborated citation:** S. M. Yusuf, “Inverse semimodules,” *Journal of Natural Sciences and Mathematics* **6** (1966), 111–117.

The primary publisher page for the later paper by M. K. Sen, A. K. Bhuniya and S. K. Maity, [“Inverse semimodules,” DOI 10.1142/S1793557121500224](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1793557121500224), gives this citation in both its abstract and reference 8. Its authors attribute introduction of inverse semimodules to Yusuf. This authenticates what those authors cite and credit. It does **not** amount to direct authentication of Yusuf's complete 1966 article or any theorem therein.

No accessible original article or primary journal archive entry was located during this bounded search. No DOI, issue number, expansion of the author's initials, or precise theorem number is supplied without verification. The exposition may credit Yusuf using the attribution explicitly supplied by Sen–Bhuniya–Maity and cite both sources, while stating that the original text was not inspected. It must not present an invented quotation or theorem statement as a reading of Yusuf.

**Status:** later-author bibliographic and historical corroboration; original-source authentication and theorem extraction unresolved. This is consistent with the retained local `PRIMARY_SOURCES.md` boundary.
