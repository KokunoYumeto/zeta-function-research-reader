## Exact comparison with the Płonka operation

This paragraph records a translation proved from C10A's displayed operations. It is not an inference from two similar names.

Fix the original commutative ring \(R\), the original semiring \(S=G(R)=\{\tau_0\}\sqcup\{[r]:r\in R\}\), its structural zero \(\tau_0\), and its supported zero \(e=[0]\). For an \(S\)-semimodule \(M\), put \(L=eM\), with \(l\vee k=l+k\), and \(M_l=\{m:em=l\}\). C10A's support identities, fibre-module theorem, and transport proposition give \(R\)-module fibres and coherent linear maps \(T_{l,k}(m)=m+k\) for \(l\leq k\). No scalar, zero, or support point is identified in this translation.

Use the signature consisting of binary addition and the unary supported scalar operations \([r]\), with no nullary operation. Every \(M_l\) is an algebra for this signature, and every \(T_{l,k}\) preserves its operations. Its Płonka sum is the tagged union \(P=\bigsqcup_l M_l\). Define \(\Phi:P\to M\) by \(\Phi(l,m)=m\). It is bijective because the fibres partition \(M\). If \(m\in M_l\), \(n\in M_k\), and \(v=l\vee k\), then

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi((l,m)+_P(k,n))
 &=T_{l,v}(m)+_{M_v}T_{k,v}(n)\\
 &=(m+v)+(n+v)\\
 &=(m+n)+v\\
 &=m+n,
\end{aligned}
\]

because \(v+v=v\), \(e(m+n)=v\), and \(x+ex=x\). For a supported scalar, \(\Phi([r]_P(l,m))=rm=[r]\Phi(l,m)\). Thus \(\Phi\) is an isomorphism of the specified reducts. For a semimodule map \(f:M\to N\), \(ef(m)=f(em)\); the induced fibrewise map sends \((l,m)\) to \((f(l),f(m))\), commutes with \(\Phi\), and respects transports because \(f(m+k)=f(m)+f(k)\). This proves naturality at the stated morphism level.

To recover the full semimodule, add the specified global constant \((0_M,0_M)\) and the unary operation \(\tau_0(l,m)=(0_M,0_M)\). Their images under \(\Phi\) are exactly \(0_M\) and the original structural-zero scalar action. Hence \(\Phi\) extends to an isomorphism of the original semimodules. These extra operations are supplied explicitly by the project construction; they are not omitted or claimed as automatic consequences of the nullary-free adjunction. This supplies the exact bridge to the cited Płonka structure while retaining the distinction between supported and structural zero.

## Audit boundary and remaining source work

This pass audited the two identified retained project chapters and the support-diagram lineage they cite, plus the directly relevant references behind selected named results. It did not audit every historical web session, every proof of either the 692-page predecessor or the 726-page successor, every theorem referenced within every classical book, or priority in the world literature. In particular: original Yusuf and Sen–Bhuniya–Maity full texts; original Clifford full text; original Horn–Kimura Theorem 5.3; the Płonka–Romanowska 1992 chapter; and page-level Johnstone/Mac Lane–Moerdijk applications remain unverified at that level. Those access boundaries are provenance facts, not reasons to omit their credit.

The files distributed here are attribution analysis and bibliographic metadata. Original third-party papers remain at their cited source locations.
