## Critical calculations and exact receiving bounds

The second-derivative numerator bound is
`2^6 {2F0(F0+1)+2F0^2} A_abs exp(2pi gamma)`.
It is at most the stated `2^8 F0(F0+1) A_abs exp(2pi gamma)`.
Differentiating `t^(-v)` on `|t|>=1/2` adds exactly the terms
`4vF0+4v(v+1)` inside incoming `B2`. Thus no enlarged replacement constant is needed.

For `Q_D=I+W^2`, `det Q_D=1`, `||Q_D||<=kappa_s`, and
`||exterior^r (Q_D^(-4))^(oplus c)|| <= kappa_s^(4(cd-r))`.
This complementary-rank estimate is what eliminates the spurious rank times `log D` loss. The same identity for the **whole** original `A` gives
`I_r=c J+F_(cd-r)`. It never assumes `g` is zero-free on the unit disk.

For the native correction, `d=N-v+1`, `t=N-q+1`:

- The relation matrix has `t` independent columns `T_A(chi_a S^j)`.
- The full lower ideal matrix has `d-Q` independent columns `chi_- S'^j`.
- Their annihilators have dimensions `q-v` and `Q`, hence quotient dimension `m` at every cutoff.
- The native dual map is exactly `B=A^T`, and the two quotient forms are the original `D` and `Dhat` in the same fixed `x` coordinates.
- In the orthonormal minimum-lift frames the map is `Dhat^(1/2) D^(-1/2)`; its squared Gram is exactly `D^(-1/2) Dhat D^(-1/2)` from TAC9.

Consequently `-2I_(D,s,m,1)<=epsilon_(a,N,s)<=2F_(D,s,m,1)`. Writing `Fi=F_(Di,s,m,1)`, `Ii=I_(Di,s,m,1)`, the original signed return satisfies
