# Circle heat in the original arithmetic quotient metrics

The earlier circle heat construction now has a quantified map through the full original tensor source, its complete primary quotient and every fixed restriction and quotient of its attained metric. The proof also puts Deligne’s nilpotent filtration into those original metrics and bounds the errors of the actual induced graded maps, retaining all cross blocks.

Read the [complete proof](HEAT_TO_ORIGINAL_QUOTIENT_METRICS.pdf), [LaTeX](HEAT_TO_ORIGINAL_QUOTIENT_METRICS.tex), [two dated results](NEW_RESULTS_2026-09-19.md), and [mathematical continuation](CONTINUATION.md). Exact earlier circle source, full D032, finite diagnostics and all three cumulative LaTeX successors are included.

The heat depth is finite and explicit in the actual original Gram constant. No unproved uniform bound on that constant is used. In the simple-quartet case the nilpotent filtration has one grade holding the full arithmetic metric; the observation angles and separate absolute allocations remain to be calculated. Human sources are cited at the steps that use them.
