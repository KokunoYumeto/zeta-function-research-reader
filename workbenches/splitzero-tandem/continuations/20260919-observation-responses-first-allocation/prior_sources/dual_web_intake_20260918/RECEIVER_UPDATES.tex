\section{Propagation into the earlier receiving equations}
\label{sec:DRU}

This section uses the unchanged domain PCB0, source orders $s=1,a$,
$q=(a+1)^2$, $Q=(a-7)^2$, $m=q-Q-v$, and the original complete
coefficient spaces and attained quotient metrics.  It records the
current receiving equations beside the earlier formulas.  In particular,
historical statements that a term had only an $o(aq)$ estimate are
strengthened by the estimates below; their original proofs remain valid.

\subsection{The conductor and covariance receiver}
The original WCF26 and TAC6 square, including its ordinary transpose,
identifies the transported covariance with the lower-root covariance
and its actual endpoint error.  The determinant phase and the two
complementary exterior maps are proved in LT1--28 and XIM1--23.
Their full-rank estimates imply
\[
 \mathcal R e^C=O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q),\qquad
 \Xi_{a,s}=O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q),\qquad
 F_{L_a}^{(s)}=O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q),
 \tag{DRU1}
\]
where the last estimate is the unchanged LRP10--13 bound.  Each symbol
is the actual term in the exact identity
\[
 \mathcal B_a^{(s)}=
 \mathcal C^-_{a,s}+\mathcal R e^C+
 F_{L_a}^{(s)}+\Xi_{a,s}+\mathcal T_{a,s}.
 \tag{DRU2}
\]
The directed finite intervals remain those in the transport proof;
DRU1 records their asymptotic sizes and does not choose their signs.
Substitution of the independently proved LRS21--22 in DRU2 gives
\[
 \mathcal T_{a,s}
 =16C_\partial aq-128q\log a+O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q),
 \qquad s=1,a.
 \tag{DRU3}
\]
Thus the lower-root $+128q\log a$ term and the tail's negative term
are related by the original covariance equality.  No diagonal
replacement of an attained norm is used in this substitution.

\subsection{Every original prefix and the correlated tail}
For the FTR11 operations, keep their exact coefficients:
\[
 \operatorname{pre}_Rh=2h(q+R)-h(q)-h(q-1),\qquad
 \operatorname{tail}_Rh=h(2q-1)+h(2q)-2h(q+R).
 \tag{DRU4}
\]
They sum to the same complete four-endpoint return.  Applying these
linear operations to the endpoint covariance identity underlying DRU2,
and using the directed LRS25 intervals,
proves, uniformly for every integer $0\le R<q$,
\[
 \mathcal T_{a,s}^{\le R}=\mathfrak P_{a,s}(R)+O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q),
 \qquad
 \mathscr T_{a,s,R}=16C_\partial aq-128q\log a
                   -\mathfrak P_{a,s}(R)+O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q).
 \tag{DRU5}
\]
Here $\mathfrak P$ is the complete LRS23 expression, including its
last single-row term.  The old growing-prefix statement is thereby
refined: at $R=\lfloor a^{3/2}/\log a\rfloor$ its leading size is
$8a^{5/2}$, while the full $\mathfrak P$ is retained whenever
$O(q)$ precision is used.  A claim that this prefix itself is
negligible at scale $q\log a$ would contradict this evaluated size.
Its occurrence in the complete action is controlled by the exact
partition in DRU4--5.

The FTR9--10 native tail is exactly
\[
 \tau_{\mathrm{total}}
 =\log\frac{\det\Omega_{q-1}\det\Omega_q}{(\det\Omega_R)^2}
 =\mathscr T_{a,s,R}.
 \tag{DRU6}
\]
For every original pair of row cutoffs $0\le p\le r\le q$, the
complete Gram update gives
\[
 \log\det(I+Y^*\Omega_p^{-1}Y)
 =16aq\int_{p/q}^{r/q}\mathfrak f(t)\,dt
       +O_{\mathrm{actual}}(q\log q),\qquad
 Y=[x_{p+1},\ldots,x_r].
 \tag{DRU7}
\]
The error is uniform in both cutoffs by PCB16--17.  Indeed the
determinant equals the sum of the same attained rows, whose full
absolute discrepancy is $O(q\log q)$; the decreasing integral
comparison costs $O(a\log q)$, and replacing $\Delta$ by $16a$
costs $O(q)$.  Thus the old inverse and all cross pairings remain
in the evaluated determinant.  DRU5 gives the finer correlated
total with its $q\log a$ coefficient; DRU7 supplies uniform interval
control with the displayed, larger allowance.

\subsection{The allocations, complements and proper source}
The exact receiving maps in FTR10 and ATA34 continue to read
\[
 S_K+S_R=\mathcal T,\qquad
 4S_K=\mathcal L_e+V_b+V_{\mathrm{rem}}+V_s+V_A.
 \tag{DRU8}
\]
These equalities apply to the same labelled row occurrences as well.
For a set of $d\le q$ occurrences, PCB18 therefore gives a common
finite budget of size
\[
 O_{\mathrm{actual}}\!\left(
 ad\{1+\log(q/d)\}+q\log q\right)
 \tag{DRU9}
\]
for each nonnegative single-copy allocation and four times that
budget for each original four-copy CPQ complement and signed
profile bound.  Empty sets give zero exactly; a possible row-zero
atom is handled by PCB13.  The proof is the unchanged extension of
the actual restriction and quotient frames: Schur factorization
splits the full increasing determinant into nonnegative increments,
so each quotient increment is bounded by the full increment.
This is the exact map transporting the row control into DRU8.
The sum in DRU8 is now evaluated by DRU3 or DRU5.  Its two summands
still retain their separate original subspaces; no individual
asymptotic coefficient follows from their sum.

At the independent proper degree $k$, retain its own source order
$s_0=1,k$, metric, centre and quotient frames, and its independent
finite guards $k\ge257$ and
$k\sqrt{\delta^2+\gamma^2}\le2^{-33}q_k$, together with its original
quartet domain.  They hold eventually for the fixed quartet and are
not inferred from the target degree's guards.  ATA35--39 proves
for the actual displaced-cutoff correction
\[
 -E_Q^-\le\delta V_Q\le E_Q^+,\qquad
 E_Q^-=O_{\mathrm{actual}}(k^2\log k),\qquad
 E_Q^+=4(8k+24)(16k-48)\mathfrak f(1)
            +O_{\mathrm{actual}}(k\log q_k).
 \tag{DRU10}
\]
The two high intervals each have length $2(8k+24)$; the factor
four counts these rows in a single copy.  The proper source's own
standard-window return remains in PRD's original degree-$k$
Schur minimum.  DRU10 controls its displacement through precisely
that map and does not assign a value to the standard-window return.

\subsection{The complete arithmetic action}
Substituting DRU2 and the exact prefix--tail partition into WCF27
recovers the original TAC identity, without selecting any endpoint
extremizers independently:
\[
 J_a=\mathcal B_a^{(s)}+\delta_{a,s}^{\mathrm{ar}}
                          +W_a-P_{a,-}-P_{a,+}.
 \tag{DRU11}
\]
The evaluated original window LRS30 and order-one source LRS22 give
\[
 J_a-\delta_{a,1}^{\mathrm{ar}}
       =C_Bq^2+8q\log a+O_{\mathrm{actual},h}(q).
 \tag{DRU12}
\]
For the other source order the exact identity
$\delta_{a,a}^{\mathrm{ar}}-\delta_{a,1}^{\mathrm{ar}}
=\mathcal B_a^{(1)}-\mathcal B_a^{(a)}$ cancels its source change.
Here $h$ remains the fixed original quartet polynomial of LRS30.
The still unevaluated term is the same signed arithmetic correction
in DRU11--12.  The exterior lower benchmark is a statement about
$J_a$ itself; DRU12 therefore provides a precise receiving equation
for the next signed calculation, with no arithmetic sign inserted.
