\begin{abstract}
The real-time mixed-boundary theorem separates four genuine factor escapes
from one excluded-chart escape. This note completes the corresponding global
action test on the actual two-chart factor variety. Its six-coordinate
constraint-and-product Jacobian has determinant exactly twice the square of
the original resultant. This gives a polynomial formula for every lifted
linear target field, with no denominator on the normalized factor variety.
A direct discriminant-degree argument proves that completing the missing
sign does not enlarge the family of complete lifts: the same one-dimensional
weight family is exhaustive. Thus chart repair and global action completeness
remain distinct even after all eight original signed states have been restored.
\end{abstract}

\section{Original factors and a full Jacobian identity}
Retain the factor coefficients from the published FC44 construction and the
FRF proof \cite{FRF,Signed}, but use all six coefficients as coordinates:
\[
 L=aU+bV,\qquad Q=cU^3+dU^2V+eUV^2+fV^3.
\]
Define the actual incidence and resultant polynomials
\[
 H=ad+bc,\qquad R=-cb^3+dab^2-ea^2b+fa^3,
\]
and the actual four free product coefficients
\[
 \Pi=(ac,ae+bd,af+be,bf)=(A,B,C,D).
\]
The complete two-chart factor space and multiplication map are
\[
 \mathfrak X=\{H=1,R=1\}\subset\C^6,\qquad
 \pi=\Pi|_{\mathfrak X}:\mathfrak X\longrightarrow\C^4.
 \tag{FRFX1}
\]
Both signs over every simple projective root are retained. In particular
both $a=0,b=\pm i$ components belong to this space.

\begin{lemma}[Full constraint-and-product determinant]\label{lem:jac}
In the exact row order $(H,R,A,B,C,D)$ and column order $(a,b,c,d,e,f)$,
let
\[
 \mathcal J=\frac{\partial(H,R,A,B,C,D)}{\partial(a,b,c,d,e,f)}.
\]
Then the identity on the entire six-coordinate affine space is
\[
 \boxed{\det\mathcal J=2R^2.}\tag{FRFX2}
\]
Consequently $\mathfrak X$ is smooth and $d\pi$ is invertible at every
point, including both restored sign components.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On $a\ne0$ introduce coordinates $(a,r,A,H,B,C)$ by
\[
 b=-ar,\quad c=A/a,\quad d=(H+Ar)/a,
\]
\[
 e=(B+Hr+Ar^2)/a,\qquad f=(C+Br+Hr^2+Ar^3)/a.
 \tag{FRFX3}
\]
The determinant of this change to $(a,b,c,d,e,f)$ is $-a^{-3}$: its
successive diagonal factors after the first coordinate are
$-a,a^{-1},a^{-1},a^{-1},a^{-1}$.
Set $V=4Ar^3+3Hr^2+2Br+C$. In these coordinates
\[
 D=-Ar^4-Hr^3-Br^2-Cr,\qquad R=a^2V.
\]
With $(A,H,B,C)$ fixed, the determinant of $(R,D)$ in $(a,r)$ is
$-2aV^2$. Reordering the full outputs from $(H,R,A,B,C,D)$ to
$(R,D,A,H,B,C)$ is an even permutation, so their full determinant is
also $-2aV^2$. Dividing by $-a^{-3}$ gives $2a^4V^2=2R^2$.
Both sides of FRFX2 are polynomials, hence the identity extends to $a=0$.
On $R=1$ the full determinant equals two. The first two differentials
are independent, and their common kernel maps isomorphically through
the last four rows, proving both geometric assertions.
\end{proof}

For a constant target matrix $M\in\operatorname{Mat}_4(\C)$ define the
six-coordinate polynomial vector field
\[
 \boxed{\widetilde{\mathcal X}_M
 =\tfrac12\adj(\mathcal J)
       \begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\M\Pi\end{pmatrix}\bigg|_{\mathfrak X}.}
 \tag{FRFX4}
\]
Multiplication by $\mathcal J$ gives $(0,0,R^2M\Pi)^T$ on the ambient
space. Thus the field is tangent to both original constraints, and on
$\mathfrak X$ it obeys
\[
 d\pi\,\widetilde{\mathcal X}_M=M\pi.
 \tag{FRFX5}
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:jac} proves uniqueness. The formula is polynomial in the
original factor coefficients; no inverse function on an unproved chart
or deleted zero direction is used.

If $\iota_\pm:\C^4\to\mathfrak X$ are the two original factor charts,
then on each chart domain the exact return is
\[
 d\iota_\pm\,\mathcal X_M^\pm
       =\widetilde{\mathcal X}_M\circ\iota_\pm,
\quad \mathcal X_M^\pm=(DP_\pm)^{-1}MP_\pm.
 \tag{FRFX6}
\]
This follows by applying the invertible $d\pi$ to both sides. In
particular the four genuine escaping trajectories of FRF-B are trajectories
of FRFX4 on the completed factor space itself.

\section{The discriminant alone determines every linear complete action}
Let
\[
 F_Y(u)=Au^4+u^3+Bu^2+Cu+D,\qquad \Delta(Y)=\operatorname{Disc}_uF_Y.
\]
The original normalized resultant gives two factor signs over each simple
projective root. Thus
\[
 \#\pi^{-1}(Y)=2\,\#\{\text{simple projective roots of }F_Y\},
 \qquad \#\pi^{-1}(Y)=8\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Delta(Y)\ne0.
 \tag{FRFX7}
\]
These statements include $A=0$ and both infinity signs. The root-incidence
parameterization FRF10 and the nonzero gradient at $(0,0,-3,2)$ show that
$\Delta$ is irreducible and reduced; their complete proof is retained in
\cite[Theorem FRF-C]{FRF}.

\begin{theorem}[Completed-space classification, FRF-X]
For every constant complex matrix $M$, the following are equivalent:
$\widetilde{\mathcal X}_M$ is complete for all real times and all initial
points of $\mathfrak X$; it is complex-time complete; and
\[
 \boxed{M=\tau\diag(1,-1,-2,-3),\qquad\tau\in\C.}\tag{FRFX8}
\]
Consequently adjoining the missing factor sign does not enlarge the
complete-action family. This is a classification of constant linear target
actions and their unique lifts; it does not classify arbitrary nonlinear
target actions or all automorphisms of $\mathfrak X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A complete real flow on $\mathfrak X$ has its inverse at negative time
and satisfies $\pi\varphi_t=e^{tM}\pi$. It therefore preserves the
cardinality of every full factor fibre. Equation FRFX7 makes the
discriminant invariant. Irreducibility, reducedness and the total degree
bound give
\[
 (MY)\cdot\nabla\Delta=\lambda\Delta,\qquad\lambda\in\C.
 \tag{FRFX9}
\]
Unlike the positive chart, full-factor fibre parity no longer singles
out $A=0$. We must recover its invariance rather than assume it.

Write $\Delta=\Delta_0+A\Delta_1+O(A^2)$. The exact polynomials are
\[
 \Delta_0=B^2C^2-4C^3-4B^3D-27D^2+18BCD,
\]
\[
 \Delta_1=144BD^2-6C^2D-80B^2CD+18BC^3+16B^4D-4B^3C^2.
 \tag{FRFX10}
\]
At $A=0$, the first component of $MY$ is some linear form
$uB+vC+wD$. Its contribution to FRFX9 is $(uB+vC+wD)\Delta_1$.
Every other term has total degree at most four in $B,C,D$, as does
$\lambda\Delta_0$. The homogeneous degree-six part is therefore
\[
 (uB+vC+wD)(16B^4D-4B^3C^2)=0.
\]
Since the second factor is a nonzero polynomial, $u=v=w=0$.
Thus $A=0$ is automatically invariant for every linear infinitesimal
symmetry of this discriminant, even though it was not read off from
full-factor fibre parity.

For completeness the remaining coefficient comparison is short.
The degree-two term $-27D^2$ in $\Delta_0$ forces the off-diagonal
$B,C$ entries of the $D$ component to vanish and gives
$\lambda=2m_{DD}$. The degree-three terms force the other off-diagonal
entries at $A=0$ to vanish, with diagonal entries
$(\beta,2\beta,3\beta)$ on $(B,C,D)$ and $\lambda=6\beta$.
Write the remaining field as
\[
 \alpha A\partial_A+(\beta B+pA)\partial_B+
 (2\beta C+qA)\partial_C+(3\beta D+rA)\partial_D.
\]
The coefficient of $A$ in FRFX9 is
\[
 (\alpha+\beta)\Delta_1+p\partial_B\Delta_0+
 q\partial_C\Delta_0+r\partial_D\Delta_0=0.
\]
Its degree-five part forces $\alpha=-\beta$; its degree-one part
forces $r=0$; its degree-two part then forces $p=q=0$. This is FRFX8.

Conversely for $M=\tau\diag(1,-1,-2,-3)$, the complete flow on the
original six coefficients is explicitly
\[
 (a,b,c,d,e,f)\longmapsto
 (e^{\tau t}a,b,c,e^{-\tau t}d,e^{-2\tau t}e,e^{-3\tau t}f),
 \qquad t\in\C.\tag{FRFX11}
\]
It preserves both $H=1$ and $R=1$ term by term and induces $e^{tM}$
on $\Pi$. Uniqueness in FRFX5 proves that it is the flow of FRFX4.
This proves complex-time completeness; the remaining implication to
real-time completeness is immediate.
\end{proof}

For the literal original quartet, $M=KRK^{-1}$ lies outside FRFX8 by
its spectrum. The original translation matrix also lies outside it,
since it is nonzero and nilpotent. Thus both remain incomplete after
restoring every signed state, as opposed to an incompleteness caused
solely by deleting one affine-chart component.

\section{Metric and conductor return}
The completed factor space is not identified with the original linear
conductor source. Its multiplication map feeds the same coefficient
target as before, followed by the actual $\Psi=YK^{-1}$. Equations
FRFX5--6 specify the exact derivatives and chart comparisons. On either
chart the original fixed positive Gamma Gram is still FRF45, and the
actual conductor identity is still $T_A\Phi=\Psi$. The four
quarter-power escapes retain their unbounded factor coefficient $a$;
they persist in every ambient factor-coordinate norm and in the
original chart-to-conductor Gamma norm with the constants in FRF35.
The additional infinity state has a finite factor-coordinate limit,
while its positive-chart conductor-coordinate image diverges with
FRF36. The two statements use their stated different coordinate maps;
no single global isometry between these spaces is being asserted.

The full Jacobian in FRFX2 is nonzero at every actual factor point.
The failures are incomplete trajectories toward the resultant boundary
at infinity, not finite zeros of the original linear conductor's
determinant. Its full quotient and all period moments remain unchanged.

discriminant tangency system, the explicit weight action, and the
six-coordinate lifted-field identity. Its execution receipt is recorded

complete-flow argument.
