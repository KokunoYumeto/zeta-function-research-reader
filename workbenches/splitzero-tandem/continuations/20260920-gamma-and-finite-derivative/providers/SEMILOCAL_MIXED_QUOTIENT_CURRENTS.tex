\section{Original objects, complete relations and finite-prime constants}
This is an independent receiving calculation from SD1--24 of
\cite{SD}, which implements the dual semilocal transfers of Alain
Connes, Caterina Consani and Henri Moscovici \cite{CCM}. We prove the
quotient statements below, rather than identifying two independently
minimized positive metrics. All scalar products are conjugate linear
in their first argument.

Keep $\HH=L^2((0,\infty),dx)$, $D=-x\partial_x$, and
\[
 \M f(s)=\int_0^\infty f(x)x^{s-1}\,dx,
 \qquad Uf(t)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}\M f(1/2+it).                 \tag{MQ1}
\]
The substitution $x=e^y$ identifies $U$ with the unitary Fourier
transform of $e^{y/2}f(e^y)$, with positive exponential. Retain the
original strong source $\B$: $f$ is smooth and
$\sup_{x>0}x^a|D^jf(x)|<\infty$ for every real $a$ and integer
$j\ge0$. Retain its theta map
$\Theta\phi(x)=2\sum_{n\ge1}\phi(nx)$ on the original even Schwartz
space with $\phi(0)=\int_{\mathbb R}\phi=0$. No factor two is removed.

Let $h(s)=\prod_\rho(s-\rho)^{m_\rho}$ be the given finite packet
of nontrivial zeros, each with its complete order, $g=2\xi$, and
$\M F_h=g/h$ for the original $F_h\in\B$. Put
\[
 w_h(t)=\frac{|(g/h)(1/2+it)|^2}{2\pi},\quad
 \mu_h=\int_{\mathbb R}w_h(t)\,dt,\quad
 m_k=w_h^{*k},\quad S=k/2+iu.                              \tag{MQ2}
\]
In particular the mass of $m_k$ is $\mu_h^k$, not one. No measure
in this note replaces $m_k$. For the integer $k\ge1$ and polynomial
space $\mathcal P_N=\{P:\deg P\le N\}$ in its ordered original
basis $1,S,\ldots,S^N$, define
\[
 V_NP=P(D_\Sigma)F_h^{\otimes k},\qquad
 D_\Sigma=\sum_{j=1}^kD_j,\qquad
 (H_N)_{ij}=\int_{\mathbb R}\overline{S^i}S^j m_k(u)\,du.
                                                               \tag{MQ3}
\]
The original exponential decay gives all these moments and places
these source vectors in every domain of $D_\Sigma$. Tensor
Plancherel proves $V_N^*V_N=H_N$. The nonzero analytic Mellin
function in MQ2 is nonzero almost everywhere on the line;
convolution retains nonzero density on an interval. A nonzero
polynomial has only finitely many zeros there. Consequently
$H_N>0$ and $V_N$ is injective.

Here are the complete arithmetic relations, including collisions of
tensor sums. In divided jets the original physical unit is
$\upsilon_h=j_h(g/h)$; each value $(g/h)(\rho)$ is nonzero because
the order divided out is complete. In the factor at $\rho$, write
$s=\rho+\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon^{m_\rho}=0$. At a tuple
$\boldsymbol\rho=(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_k)$ the nilpotent part of
$S$ is $\sum_j\varepsilon_j$. Its highest nonzero power has degree
$\sum_j(m_{\rho_j}-1)$: the coefficient of
$\prod_j\varepsilon_j^{m_{\rho_j}-1}$ is the nonzero multinomial
coefficient, and all higher powers vanish. Thus, with no simple-zero
assumption,
\[
 \chi(S)=\prod_\sigma(S-\sigma)^{\nu_\sigma},\qquad
 \nu_\sigma=\max_{\sum_j\rho_j=\sigma}
       \left(1+\sum_j(m_{\rho_j}-1)\right).                    \tag{MQ4}
\]
Indeed Chinese remainders split the tensor algebra into exactly
these local factors; an annihilator must have the indicated order
at each tuple, and the least common multiple uses the maximum at
coincident sums. Multiplication by the tensor unit is invertible and
does not alter the annihilator. Hence, putting $d=\deg\chi$,
\[
 C=\C[S]/(\chi),\quad J_N:\mathcal P_N\longrightarrow C,
 \quad \eta:C\hookrightarrow(\C[s]/(h))^{\otimes k},\quad
 J^{(k)}V_N=\eta J_N,                                       \tag{MQ5}
\]
where $\eta[P]=P(\sum s_j)\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}$ in complete
physical divided jets. For $N\ge d-1$, $J_N$ is onto and the full
relation space is
\[
 \DD_N=\ker J_N=\chi\mathcal P_{N-d};                         \tag{MQ6}
\]
it is zero when $N=d-1$. Polynomial division proves this exact
description; it is not a Hilbert kernel of $V_N$.
For the original simple-quartet current receiver of \cite{PD} we
retain precisely its domain $k\equiv1\pmod4$, $k\ge77$,
$q=(k+1)^2$, $d=q$ and $N\ge q-1$. MQ4 also records the full
multiplicities for the general finite-packet construction, without
enlarging that receiver's admitted domain.
On that domain $C$ is the original $E_k$ in its cyclic polynomial
frame; the physical injection $\eta$ is retained separately below.

For the fixed finite prime set $\mathcal P$ define
\[
 V_pf(x)=\sqrt p\,f(px),\quad r_p=p^{-1/2},\quad
 E=\prod_{p\in\mathcal P}(I-r_pV_p)^{-1},\quad
 T=\prod_{p\in\mathcal P}(I-r_pV_p^*)=(E^*)^{-1}.             \tag{MQ7}
\]
The adjoint is $V_p^*f(x)=p^{-1/2}f(x/p)$. Geometric series
converge in operator norm, and all these dilation factors commute.
On the line in MQ1 their multipliers are
\[
 e(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})^{-1},\qquad
 b(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{s-1}),\qquad \overline{b(s)}e(s)=1.
                                                               \tag{MQ8}
\]
Change of variables proves these signs. Put
$\TT=T^{\otimes k}$, $\EE=E^{\otimes k}$ and
\[
 b_-=\prod_p(1-p^{-1/2}),\quad b_+=\prod_p(1+p^{-1/2}),\quad
 \alpha_T=b_-^{2k},\ \beta_T=b_+^{2k},\quad
 \alpha_E=b_+^{-2k},\ \beta_E=b_-^{-2k}.                       \tag{MQ9}
\]
Pointwise bounds on MQ8 and tensor Plancherel give
\[
 \TT^*\EE=\EE^*\TT=I,\quad
 H_N^T=V_N^*\TT^*\TT V_N,\quad H_N^E=V_N^*\EE^*\EE V_N,
 \quad \alpha_LH_N\preceq H_N^L\preceq\beta_LH_N\ (L=T,E).
                                                               \tag{MQ10}
\]
All factors commute with multiplication by $S$ in the Mellin model,
so they preserve every domain of $D_\Sigma$ and commute there.
These are finite-prime bounds; no infinite-prime assertion follows.
In particular the positive source Grams have the explicit original
integral expressions
\[
 (H_N^L)_{ij}=\int_{\mathbb R}\overline{(k/2+iu)^i}
          (k/2+iu)^j w_L^{*k}(u)\,du,\quad
 w_T(t)=|b(1/2+it)|^2w_h(t),\quad
 w_E(t)=|e(1/2+it)|^2w_h(t).                              \tag{MQ10a}
\]
This follows by tensor Plancherel and integration over
$u=t_1+\cdots+t_k$. Their degree-zero entries are the actual
$(\int w_L)^k$; neither mass is replaced by $\mu_h^k$ or by one.
For the cross Gram the cancellation
$\overline{b(1/2+it_j)}e(1/2+it_j)=1$ occurs separately for
every $j$, before convolution, giving exactly $H_N$.

\section{Unique opposite-relation orthogonality and exact quotient Gram}
Fix an admitted $N$ and abbreviate $H=H_N$, $J=J_N$. Set
\[
 G=(JH^{-1}J^*)^{-1},\qquad R=H^{-1}J^*G.                    \tag{MQ11}
\]
Full row rank of $J$ and positivity of $H$ prove invertibility.
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 JR=I_C,\quad R^*HR=G,\quad R^*H Z=0
       \quad(\im Z=\DD_N).                                 \tag{MQ12}
\]
Conversely, each $c$ with $Jc=u$ has the unique expression
$c=Ru+d_0$, $d_0\in\DD_N$, with
$c^*Hc=u^*Gu+d_0^*Hd_0$. This proves both the original minimum
and its uniqueness.

Define injective source maps $F_T=\TT V_N$, $F_E=\EE V_N$,
and the paired quotient lifts
\[
 L_T=F_TR,\qquad L_E=F_ER.                                  \tag{MQ13}
\]
The full opposite relation spaces are $F_E\DD_N$ and
$F_T\DD_N$, not selected columns of them. MQ10--12 prove
\[
 \langle L_Tu,F_Ed_0\rangle=0,
 \quad \langle L_Eu,F_Td_0\rangle=0\quad(d_0\in\DD_N),
 \qquad L_T^*L_E=L_E^*L_T=G.                                \tag{MQ14}
\]
In fact $L_Tu$ is the unique element of its entire source fibre
$\{F_Tc:Jc=u\}$ orthogonal to $F_E\DD_N$. If $F_Tc$ has this
property, $c^*Hd_0=0$ for every $d_0$, so MQ12 and positivity
force $c=Ru$. The same proof interchanges $T,E$. Thus the pairing
is nondegenerate on the two mixed complements. It does not descend
from arbitrary unchosen representatives: the displayed complements
are the precise maps that make it a quotient pairing.

On $\im F_T$ and $\im F_E$, respectively, the projectors
\[
 \Pi_T=F_TRJ F_T^{-1},\qquad \Pi_E=F_ERJ F_E^{-1}             \tag{MQ15}
\]
have ranges $\im L_T,\im L_E$ and kernels $F_T\DD_N,F_E\DD_N$.
Every inverse here is on the indicated image. The identity
$(RJ)^*H=HRJ$, obtained from MQ11, proves
$\langle\Pi_T f_T,f_E\rangle=\langle f_T,\Pi_E f_E\rangle$.
These mutually adjoint mixed projectors need not be the same-side
orthogonal projectors. Their positive same-side quotient Grams are
\[
 C_T=R^*H^TR,\quad C_E=R^*H^ER,\qquad
 \alpha_L G\preceq C_L\preceq\beta_LG.                      \tag{MQ16}
\]

\section{The unchanged action, observations, kernel and complex currents}
Keep the original action $A=M_S$ on $C$, original observation
$\Lambda:C\to\C^r$, original fixed class $x$, and $K=\ker\Lambda$.
Let $P$ be the $G$-orthogonal projection onto exactly $K$ and put

\[
 A_L=L_LAL_L^{-1},\quad \Lambda_L=\Lambda L_L^{-1},\quad
 P_L^\times=L_LPL_L^{-1},\quad K_L=L_LK\qquad(L=T,E).
                                                               \tag{MQ17}
\]
All domains are $\im L_L$. MQ14 proves, for every $u,v$,
$\langle L_TPu,L_E(I-P)v\rangle=0$ and the reverse relation.
It also proves the exact full-phase transport
\[
 \begin{split}
 z&=a^*GA b_0
  =\langle P_T^\times L_Tx,A_E(I-P_E^\times)L_Ex\rangle,\\
 w&=b_0^*GA a
  =\langle(I-P_T^\times)L_Tx,A_EP_E^\times L_Ex\rangle.
 \end{split}                                                  \tag{MQ18}
\]
Interchanging $T,E$ leaves the same right sides. Neither quantity
has been replaced by its modulus or real part. In particular their

When $\Lambda$ has full row rank, put

 (L_TR_{\rm obs})^*(L_ER_{\rm obs})=Q.                      \tag{MQ19}

For a rank-deficient presentation use its actual image as codomain;

\section{Degree-raising source action: the complete relation correction}
Let $i_N:\mathcal P_N\to\mathcal P_{N+1}$ be coefficient inclusion
and $X_N:\mathcal P_N\to\mathcal P_{N+1}$ multiplication by the
original $S$. Then
\[
 J_{N+1}i_N=J_N,\quad J_{N+1}X_N=AJ_N,\quad
 H_N=i_N^*H_{N+1}i_N,\quad D_\Sigma V_N=V_{N+1}X_N.         \tag{MQ20}
\]
Define, with no cutoff identification,
\[
 C_N^{\rm rel}=X_NR_N-i_NR_NA,\qquad
 \Gamma_N=R_N^*i_N^*H_{N+1}C_N^{\rm rel}.                  \tag{MQ21}
\]
Its range is in $\DD_{N+1}$ by MQ20. But $i_NR_N$ is only
orthogonal to $i_N\DD_N$, not to this larger relation space.
There is an exact rank-one expression for this entire correction.
Let $\ell_N$ select the coefficient of $S^N$ in $\mathcal P_N$,
and let $\zeta_N\in\mathcal P_{N+1}$ be the coefficient vector
of the monic polynomial $S^{N+1-d}\chi(S)$. Polynomial division
and its leading coefficient give
\[
 C_N^{\rm rel}-\zeta_N\ell_NR_N\in
       \operatorname{Hom}(C,i_N\DD_N),\qquad
 \Gamma_N=(R_N^*i_N^*H_{N+1}\zeta_N)(\ell_NR_N),\quad
 \operatorname{rank}\Gamma_N\le1.                          \tag{MQ21a}
\]
Indeed the difference is a relation of degree at most $N$, and
MQ12 annihilates its pairing with $R_N$. This applies also at
$N=d-1$, when $\DD_N=0$ but $\DD_{N+1}$ need not vanish.
Using commutation with $D_\Sigma$ from MQ10 gives the exact formula
\[
 \langle\TT V_NR_Nu,D_\Sigma\EE V_NR_Nv\rangle
       =u^*(G_NA+\Gamma_N)v.                               \tag{MQ22}
\]
Indeed replace the second vector by $\EE V_{N+1}X_NR_Nv$,
cancel $\TT^*\EE$, and insert MQ21. It is invalid to replace
the raw differential action in MQ22 by $A_E$ of MQ17 without
subtracting this explicitly known relation pairing.

There is an exact next-cutoff description and a proved finite bound.
Put $Y_N=i_NR_N-R_{N+1}$. Its range lies in $\DD_{N+1}$ and
orthogonality of $R_{N+1}$ gives
\[
 G_N-G_{N+1}=Y_N^*H_{N+1}Y_N\succeq0,\qquad
 \Gamma_N=Y_N^*H_{N+1}C_N^{\rm rel},                       \tag{MQ23}
\]
\[
 |u^*\Gamma_Nv|^2\le
   [u^*(G_N-G_{N+1})u]
   [v^*(C_N^{\rm rel})^*H_{N+1}C_N^{\rm rel}v].             \tag{MQ24}
\]
Expand $i_NR_N=R_{N+1}+Y_N$ to prove the first identity;
the mixed terms vanish. The second follows because
$R_{N+1}^*H_{N+1}C_N^{\rm rel}=0$. Cauchy--Schwarz in the
positive $H_{N+1}$ metric proves MQ24. All its matrices are
explicit functions of the original moments through degree $2N+2$;
their values are not evaluated here. Using $R_{N+1}$ in the first
slot of MQ22 instead produces $G_{N+1}A$, not $G_NA$.

The retained real part $\Re S=k/2$ yields an additional exact
identity. In MQ3, $\overline S+S=k$ gives
\[
 i_N^*H_{N+1}X_N+X_N^*H_{N+1}i_N=kH_N,
 \quad
 \boxed{\Gamma_N+\Gamma_N^*=kG_N-G_NA-A^*G_N.}              \tag{MQ25}
\]
The second follows by multiplying the first by $R_N^*,R_N$ and
using MQ21--22. This is the coefficient form of the source identity
$D_\Sigma+D_\Sigma^*=kI$ on these vectors, with its original
scalar center intact.

For the two fixed classes of MQ18 write
\[
 z_{\rm raw}=z+r_z,\quad w_{\rm raw}=w+r_w,\qquad
 r_z=a^*\Gamma_Nb_0,\quad r_w=b_0^*\Gamma_Na.                \tag{MQ26}
\]
MQ25 and $a^*G_Nb_0=0$ prove
\[
 z_{\rm raw}+\overline{w_{\rm raw}}=0,\qquad
 \boxed{z+\overline w=-a^*(\Gamma_N+\Gamma_N^*)b_0.}         \tag{MQ27}
\]
Thus the source phase relation does not pass to the quotient by
dropping the degree-raised relation. The entire product correction is
\[
 \overline zw=\overline{z_{\rm raw}}w_{\rm raw}
     -\overline{r_z}w_{\rm raw}
     -\overline{z_{\rm raw}}r_w+\overline{r_z}r_w.            \tag{MQ28}
\]
MQ24 bounds each correction with its actual native finite-cutoff
energies if those are evaluated; it supplies no unproved phase sign.

\section{Separate positive minima and their full cross defect}
The same polynomial fibre has two distinct positive-metric minima:
\[
 G_L=(J(H^L)^{-1}J^*)^{-1},\quad
 R_L=(H^L)^{-1}J^*G_L,\quad \Delta_L=R_L-R\quad(L=T,E).
                                                               \tag{MQ29}
\]
Both $\Delta_L$ have range in $\DD_N$. MQ12 therefore proves
\[
 R_T^*HR_E=G+\Xi,\qquad
 \boxed{\Xi=\Delta_T^*H\Delta_E.}                           \tag{MQ30}
\]
In general this cross defect is a complex, not necessarily Hermitian,
matrix. There is an explicit formula involving every relation.
Take $Z$ whose columns are the coefficient vectors of
$\chi,S\chi,\ldots,S^{N-d}\chi$ and put $K_L=Z^*H^LZ$.
For $N=d-1$ all expressions involving $Z$ below mean zero.
Otherwise $Z$ is injective, $K_L>0$, and the normal equations
$Z^*H^L(R+\Delta_L)=0$ give
\[
 \Delta_L=-ZK_L^{-1}Z^*H^LR,\qquad
 \Xi=R^*H^TZK_T^{-1}(Z^*HZ)K_E^{-1}Z^*H^ER.                \tag{MQ31}
\]
This is invariant under an invertible change of the full relation
basis and retains its actual dimension $N+1-d$.

The same normal equations prove the exact positive Pythagorean
defects
\[
 C_L-G_L=\Delta_L^*H^L\Delta_L\succeq0,
 \qquad \alpha_LG\preceq G_L\preceq\beta_LG.                \tag{MQ32}
\]
For the first, write $R=R_L-\Delta_L$ and eliminate the cross
terms. For the second, minimize MQ10 on the same entire fibre.
Consequently Cauchy--Schwarz, MQ9 and MQ30 give the explicit
finite-prime bounds
\[
 \begin{split}
 |u^*\Xi v|&\le (b_+/b_-)^k
  \sqrt{u^*(C_T-G_T)u}\sqrt{v^*(C_E-G_E)v},\\
 |u^*\Xi v|&\le(\kappa_{\mathcal P}-1)\|u\|_G\|v\|_G,
 \qquad \kappa_{\mathcal P}=(b_+/b_-)^{2k}.
 \end{split}                                                \tag{MQ33}
\]
Indeed $\Delta_L^*H\Delta_L\preceq
\alpha_L^{-1}(C_L-G_L)$, which proves the first inequality;
$C_L-G_L\preceq(\beta_L-\alpha_L)G$ proves the second, since
$\beta_L/\alpha_L=\kappa_{\mathcal P}$ for both sides. These
bounds are proved, but are not asserted small for a growing prime
set, growing tensor degree or growing cutoff.

If the original $a,b_0$ and $A$ are retained while only the lifts
are separately minimized, the resulting currents are exactly
\[
 z_{\rm sep}=z+a^*\Xi A b_0,\qquad
 w_{\rm sep}=w+b_0^*\Xi A a.                               \tag{MQ34}
\]
If one additionally uses the respective $G_T,G_E$ projections
$P_T,P_E$ onto the same $K$, put $e_T=(P_T-P)x$,
$e_E=(P_E-P)x$. The separate-side currents then satisfy
\[
 \begin{split}
 z_{\rm sep}'-z={}&e_T^*GA b_0-a^*GAe_E-e_T^*GAe_E
       +(a+e_T)^*\Xi A(b_0-e_E),\\
 w_{\rm sep}'-w={}&b_0^*GAe_E-e_T^*GAa-e_T^*GAe_E
       +(b_0-e_T)^*\Xi A(a+e_E).
 \end{split}                                                 \tag{MQ35}
\]
These follow by multiplying the full expressions
$(a+e_T)^*(G+\Xi)A(b_0-e_E)$ and
$(b_0-e_T)^*(G+\Xi)A(a+e_E)$; no cross term is discarded.
For either convention write the two displayed differences as
$\delta_z,\delta_w$. Then
\[
 \overline{z_{\rm sep}}w_{\rm sep}-\overline zw
   =\overline{\delta_z}w+\overline z\delta_w
     +\overline{\delta_z}\delta_w,                          \tag{MQ36}
\]
with primes in both places for MQ35. This retains unknown phases.

There is also a complete source-action correction for any two
right inverses $R_1,R_2$ of $J_N$, including MQ29. Define
\[
 B_{12}=R_1^*H_NR_2,\quad
 \Gamma_{12}=R_1^*i_N^*H_{N+1}(X_NR_2-i_NR_2A).
                                                               \tag{MQ37}
\]
Precisely the proof of MQ22 gives
$\langle\TT V_NR_1u,D_\Sigma\EE V_NR_2v\rangle
=u^*(B_{12}A+\Gamma_{12})v$.
Thus for separate minima $B_{TE}=G+\Xi$ and $\Gamma_{TE}$ is
an additional, explicitly retained correction. No minimum at cutoff
$N+1$ is substituted without its own class Gram.

\section{Physical divided jets, the full unit, and the local domain of E}
The finite formula $T_pf=f-p^{-1}f(\cdot/p)$ preserves each
strong seminorm and the original theta test space. Absolute
convergence gives $T\Theta=\Theta T$, with the original factor
two. Thus $T$ is the strong theta-chain map of \cite{SD}.
We make no assertion that $E\B\subset\B$, nor that Hilbert
invertibility supplies a strong-source inverse.

There is nevertheless an exact, narrower local-jet map for $E$.
Let $\mathcal N_{\mathcal P}$ be the multiplicative semigroup
generated by these primes. For $f\in\B$ and every $\sigma>0$,
Tonelli and a change of variables give
\[
 \int_0^\infty\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_{\mathcal P}}
 |f(nx)|x^{\sigma-1}\,dx
 =\prod_{p\in\mathcal P}(1-p^{-\sigma})^{-1}
       \int_0^\infty|f(y)|y^{\sigma-1}\,dy<\infty.           \tag{MQ38}
\]
The series also converges in $L^2(dx)$ since
$\sum_n n^{-1/2}<\infty$, and hence represents the same Hilbert
operator $E$ of MQ7. On each compact subset of $\Re s>0$, powers
of $|\log x|$ are bounded by a fixed multiple of
$x^\delta+x^{-\delta}$ for sufficiently small $\delta>0$.
Apply MQ38 at the two surrounding positive real parts to dominate
all Mellin derivatives. It follows by differentiation under the
integral that
\[
 \M(Ef)(s)=e(s)\M f(s)\quad(\Re s>0)                      \tag{MQ39}
\]
is a holomorphic equality, with all finite derivatives there. This
proves actual Mellin jets of the transformed original inputs,
not a global strong theta-chain map. It applies to every original
polynomial source, and in $k$ variables to $\Re s_j>0$ by the
same absolutely convergent product integrals. Every native packet
point lies in this domain.

For completeness all finite-order jet maps and inverse coefficients
are explicit. Let $n_A=\prod_{p\in A}p$, $n_\varnothing=1$.
At a packet point $\rho$ put
\[
 \begin{split}
 \beta_{\rho,j}&=\frac1{j!}\sum_{A\subset\mathcal P}
       (-1)^{|A|}n_A^{\rho-1}(\log n_A)^j,\\
 \zeta_{\rho,j}&=\frac1{j!}\sum_{A\subset\mathcal P}
       (-1)^{|A|}n_A^{-\rho}(-\log n_A)^j.
 \end{split}                                                 \tag{MQ40}
\]
Use $0^0=1$ in the degree-zero empty term. The lower Toeplitz
blocks with coefficients $\beta_{\rho,j}$ form $B_h=j_h(b)$;
those with coefficients $\zeta_{\rho,j}$ form $C_h=j_h(c)$,
where $c(s)=\prod_p(1-p^{-s})=e(s)^{-1}$. Their diagonals are
nonzero since $0<\Re\rho<1$. For either coefficient sequence
$d_j$, its inverse sequence is exactly
\[
 t_0=d_0^{-1},\qquad
 t_j=-d_0^{-1}\sum_{l=1}^jd_lt_{j-l}\quad(1\le j<m_\rho).
                                                               \tag{MQ41}
\]
Multiplication of the finite Taylor series proves this formula to
the complete order. Write $E_h=C_h^{-1}=j_h(e)$ and
$\mathbb B=B_h^{\otimes k}$, $\mathbb E=E_h^{\otimes k}$.
MQ39 and the strong $T$ map give the literal injections
\[
 \eta_T=\mathbb B\eta,\qquad \eta_E=\mathbb E\eta,
 \quad j^{(k)}F_T=\eta_TJ_N,\quad j^{(k)}F_E=\eta_EJ_N.
                                                               \tag{MQ42}
\]
The last $j^{(k)}$ is the actual holomorphic Mellin-jet evaluation
on the domain just proved, not an asserted $E$ map on the global
theta quotient. Since $\mathbb B,\mathbb E$ are invertible, each
cyclic image has exactly the original relation ideal $(\chi)$.
Neither tensor multiplier is replaced by a function only of $S$;
they commute with the total action but keep every factor and the
full unit $\upsilon_h^{\otimes k}$.

For an explicit coordinate expression let
$\eta^\ell=(\eta^*\eta)^{-1}\eta^*$, a left inverse on the
original cyclic image, and define the displayed extensions
\[
 Z_T=\eta^\ell\mathbb B^{-1},\qquad
 Z_E=\eta^\ell\mathbb E^{-1}.                               \tag{MQ43}
\]
Their restrictions to $X_T=\im\eta_T$ and $X_E=\im\eta_E$
are the unique inverses of the respective injections. For actual
physical vectors $u_T\in X_T$, $v_E\in X_E$, the mixed pairing is
\[
 \langle L_TZ_Tu_T,L_EZ_Ev_E\rangle
