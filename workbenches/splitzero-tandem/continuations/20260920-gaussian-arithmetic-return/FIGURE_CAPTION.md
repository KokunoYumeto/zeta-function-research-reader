# The Gaussian action in the original quotient

The top panel displays the actual admitted source \(\mathcal P_{\le N}\) in \(L^2(\mu_k)\), its least-norm quotient representatives \(\mathcal V_N\), and the original relation space \(Q_k\mathcal P_{<d}\), where \(d=N+1-q\). The two blocks have a coupling of rank at most one. The full relation polynomial and the original source metric remain. The Gamma-to-arithmetic characteristic-value comparison has error \(O_h(k+\log q)=o(q)\). Complete proofs: NG3–6 and NG18–20.

The middle panel evaluates the exact limiting densities at ratio \(s=1\), in the plotted coordinate \(z=y/q\). The full source density \(h_0(z/2)\) has mass two; the relation density \(|z|\rho_1(z^2)\) has mass one; their retained difference \(d\nu_1/dz\) is a positive probability density. The origin has an integrable divergence. Endpoint decimals and curves are numerical illustrations, not interval certificates. NG10 and NG20 give the complete formulas; GM17 proves the sign directly.

The lower panel retains the original four degree cutoffs and their signs. Here \(I_s=-H_s'(1/1024)\), not the modified Bessel function \(I_0\) that occurs inside the analytic Gaussian transform. For the original observation this conclusion uses \(m_0=1\) and the proved five-orbit domain \(|u|\ge R_*\) of MRI1d and NG24. GM21–25 prove the endpoint bound \(m(1)>10249/10000\); NG27 then gives \(2(I_0-I_1)<-657061/960000\). Neither a finite-degree threshold nor the unresolved logarithmic allocations follow from the picture.
