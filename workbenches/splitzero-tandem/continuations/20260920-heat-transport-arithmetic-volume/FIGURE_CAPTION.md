\[
|\operatorname{tr}e^{-sM^{\dagger_{G_{N+1}}}M}-\operatorname{tr}e^{-sM^{\dagger_{G_N}}M}|
\le(1-e^{-v})e^{-v/(e^v-1)}\le v/e,\qquad s>0.
\]

The middle expression is continuously zero at (v=0). No dimension factor occurs. The blue shaded area is a permissible range for the absolute heat change, not a confidence region. Full proof: `DEGREE_VOLUME_HEAT_RETURN.tex`, VH1–31; sharp general-matrix equality examples: VH30. The construction uses the programme's original source–boundary determinant calculation, whose full provenance is cited there. The heat-test context is Alain Connes, [Heat Expansion and Zeta](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13082v1), equation `ft`, and Alain Connes and Walter D. van Suijlekom, [Quadratic Forms, Real Zeros and Echoes of the Spectral Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23257v1), section `sect:sa`.
