## DA-3. Proposed sharper arithmetic-defect bound

Use the same G throughout and set
\[
v=b_L,\quad u=b_{L+1},\quad \omega=\omega_L>0,
\quad \alpha=v^*Gv,\quad\delta=u^*Gu,
\quad \epsilon=\frac{\sqrt{\alpha\delta}}{\omega}.
\]
Write X^(dagger_G)=G^(-1)X*G. The correction Q=A-M=(i/omega)uv*G satisfies
\[
Q^{\dagger_G}=-\frac{i}{\omega}vu^*G,
\qquad \|Q\|_G=\epsilon.
\]
Since A=A^(dagger_G), M=A-Q and M_S=cI+iM, the actual defect is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C
&=M_S+M_S^{\dagger_G}-kI\\
&=i(M-M^{\dagger_G})\\
&=-i(Q-Q^{\dagger_G})\\
&=\frac{uv^*+vu^*}{\omega}G.
\end{aligned}
\tag{DA.1}
\]
Thus C is G-selfadjoint; equivalently GC is an ordinary Hermitian matrix. C itself need not be Hermitian in the displayed Euclidean coordinates.

If alpha delta>0, the parity identity v*Gu=0 makes
\[
e=\frac{v}{\sqrt\alpha},\qquad
h=\frac{u}{\sqrt\delta}
\]
G-orthonormal. Direct multiplication gives
\[
\mathcal Ce=\epsilon h,\qquad
\mathcal Ch=\epsilon e,\qquad
\mathcal Cx=0\quad
(x\perp_G\operatorname{span}\{e,h\}).
\tag{DA.2}
\]
Consequently (e+h)/sqrt(2) and (e-h)/sqrt(2) are eigenvectors for epsilon and -epsilon, respectively. These are the two nonzero eigenvalues, each simple, and the other q-2 eigenvalues are zero. In particular ||C||_G=epsilon. This is the exact rank-two arithmetic defect in the retained quotient metric.

If alpha delta=0, positivity of G makes at least one of u,v zero. Then Q=C=0 and epsilon=0. All q eigenvalues of C are zero. This separate statement also handles q=1 without mentioning a negative zero-eigenvalue multiplicity or dividing by a zero norm.

For any original eigenpair M_Sx=kappa x with x nonzero, including at a repeated root,
\[
\frac{x^*G\mathcal Cx}{x^*Gx}
=2\operatorname{Re}\kappa-k.
\tag{DA.3}
\]
The G-selfadjoint Rayleigh bound now gives
\[
\boxed{|2\operatorname{Re}\kappa-k|\le\epsilon},
\qquad
\boxed{|\operatorname{Re}\kappa-c|\le\epsilon/2}.
\tag{DA.4}
\]
Equivalently, for alpha_0=(kappa-c)/i, one has |Im alpha_0|<=epsilon/2. This improves NS10's strip estimate by a factor of two, while NS10's distinct distance-to-spec(A) estimate remains valid and need not be deleted. No diagonalizability of M or M_S is used.
