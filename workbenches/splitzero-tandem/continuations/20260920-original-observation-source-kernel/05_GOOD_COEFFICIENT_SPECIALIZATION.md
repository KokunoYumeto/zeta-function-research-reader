# Good coefficient specialization of the sixteen cofactor observations

19 September 2026. This concerns the explicitly defined graded biform/quadric modules, not an identification with the polynomial sum-eigenvalue module or with geometric Frobenius cohomology.

## 1. A fixed coefficient ring

Let O be the subring of the original complex coefficient field generated by the finitely many entries of H,H^{-1}, the original primitive fifth root zeta, and 1/5. Include the coefficients of Q and the five Qa. Localize at the following finitely many original nonzero coefficients: chosen leading coefficients of the five Qa in one fixed monomial order, and one nonzero coefficient from each of two distinct character components of Q. The latter exist on the original five-orbit locus. All these choices are made at the fixed period, independently of tensor degree.

Over any algebraically closed residue field of this ring, zeta has exact order five, H is invertible, Q is an invertible linear transport of the smooth Segre quadric, and the two retained character coefficients show that its five translates are pairwise nonassociate. Thus every fibre satisfies the purely algebraic hypotheses proved in note 02. This ring does not invert the growing sum-grid Vandermonde.

## 2. Formation of the original graded images commutes with specialization

Let S_O=O[x1,x2,x3,x4], P=product_{a=0}^4 Qa, A=product_{a=1}^4 Qa, R_O=S_O/(Q). Invariants have the original monomial character decomposition; the factors 1-zeta^j are units because their product is five. The character-zero part is therefore exactly spanned by its original monomials.

The fixed monic leading terms give free O-modules in each degree for R_O and for

    B_O=S_O^G/(P S_O^G).

For B_O, polynomial division by P remains inside the character-zero monomials because P and its leading monomial have character zero. The original restriction B_O -> R_O is injective: over the fraction field this is the prime-factor argument, and the source has no O-torsion. Over every algebraically closed residue field it remains injective by the same argument.

A matrix from a finite free module whose reduction has full column rank at a point has an invertible maximal minor on a neighbourhood of that point. There it splits by the literal inverse of that minor, and its cokernel is free. Applying this at every point proves that R_O/B_O is locally free degree by degree and that this exact sequence commutes with every residue-field base change.

Multiplication by A gives an injection

    (R_O)_{d-8} -> (B_O)_d.

It remains injective on every fibre because A is a nonzero element of the fibre domain S/(Q); its membership in B is the original orbit-sum identity. The same maximal-minor argument proves that

    (J_O)_d=(B_O)_d / A(R_O)_{d-8}

is locally free and that its formation commutes with specialization. The analogous argument treats (C_O)_d=(R_O)_d/A(R_O)_{d-8}. In particular the geometric fibre of J_O is the original invariant image modulo the full conductor, not a replacement obtained by discarding an unexamined tensor kernel.

## 3. The recovery map is fibrewise injective and has no new base-change kernel

Form over O the sixteen polynomial maps

    X_full,d: (J_O)_d -> direct_sum_{i,r=1}^4 (R_O/B_O)_{d+4r},
    [f] |-> ([xi^(4r) f])_{i,r}.

The proof of note 02 works over every algebraically closed field of characteristic different from five. The generic linear form used for its saturation proof can be chosen over that algebraic closure; injectivity descends along a field extension because tensoring a vector space is faithful. Thus every geometric fibre of X_full,d is injective.

Its source and target are locally free of finite rank. The same nonzero-minor proof now gives a locally split injection with locally free cokernel. Consequently

    ker(X_full,d tensor_O K)=0

for every residue field K of O, with the actual source and target images just verified. Equivalently, the exact sequence with that cokernel has zero Tor_1 from residue-field base change. This does not require an unspecified flatness assumption: the flatness follows from the displayed fibre ranks and the local inverse minors.

Degree by degree, the finite locally free cokernel is projective over the affine coefficient ring, so the sequence also splits as an O-module sequence. This does not assert a single bounded graded module inverse, a period-uniform inverse minor, or a Hermitian isometry. No numerical split has been computed here.

## 4. Low jets and the separate cyclic interpolation map

The result above applies to the full graded invariant source. It therefore restricts to any actual low-jet subspace preserved by the specified cofactor products. To identify a reduced low-jet image with reduction of its characteristic-zero presentation, retain its actual finite jet matrix and its rank conditions; that separate comparison is not implied by the graded statement. Hasse coefficients obey the full product formula without factorial division. Ordinary derivative coordinates also require their original factorial denominators to be units.

There is likewise a separate original map from polynomials in the sum variable S to the labelled grid values. In the simple fibre it is the explicit evaluation matrix

    V_k[(a,b),j]=lambda_ab^j, 0<=j<q.

Repeated values after reduction give a nonzero kernel of this matrix. Nothing in the sixteen cofactor maps changes V_k or removes that kernel. The new recovery is injectivity on the displayed biform invariant module; any proposed composite starting from the cyclic polynomial module retains the actual kernel of V_k (and its full multiplicity analogue).

Finally the coefficient maps commute with coefficient Frobenius in their literal base-change form Frob(Xv)=X^(p)Frob(v). This relates the map to its Frobenius pullback. It is not an identification of the arithmetic operator exp((log Q)A) with Frobenius on a lisse sheaf, and it does not construct a nonzero same-class map into the pure middle image. Those additional comparison equations remain part of the programme.
