# The original arithmetic spectral determinant

The Split-Zero programme represents finite zero-divisor classes by polynomials in a theta-source Hilbert space. Its quotient metric is the least source norm in each actual remainder class. This edition calculates the determinant of multiplication on that quotient, with that metric unchanged.

[Complete proofs and parent calculations](COMPLETE_PROOFS.tex) · [Principal proof, SD1–28](ORIGINAL_SPECTRAL_DETERMINANT.tex) · [Independent derivation](INDEPENDENT_MATHEMATICAL_CHECK.tex) · [Results and exact uses](RESULT_INDEX.json) · [Validation](VALIDATION.md)

The determinant contains the complete root-polynomial term and a positive term formed from both original boundary vectors. This gives an exact return to the canonical action, with the boundary-energy correction retained. The exact determinant at zero also forces small singular values in the original metric. It does not make the companion's algebraic eigenvalues small or identify a singular value with a zeta zero.

The result applies to the full original quartet/divisor, fixed multiplicity, admissible tensor degrees and the entire stated cutoff window. The eventual bounds do not give an effective finite-degree threshold. Individual projected-current signs and separate kernel/observation allocations remain uncalculated by this result; it does not settle RH.
