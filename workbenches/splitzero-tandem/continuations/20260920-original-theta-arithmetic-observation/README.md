# Original theta closure and arithmetic observation

This edition applies three specific results from the human literature to the Split-Zero cohomology programme. The programme keeps the theta source, its quotient by theta relations, and finite observations of that quotient in their original coordinates. The aim here is to calculate their topology and arithmetic metrics without replacing the original source by a model weight.

[Complete derivation in LaTeX](LITERATURE_RECEIVERS_COMPLETE.tex) · [Machine-readable results and proof locators](RESULT_INDEX.json) · [Exact source manifest](MANIFEST.json).

- **Exact Gamma kernel and joint observation bound — SZ-20260920-007.** The finite evaluation kernel is calculated exactly, then transported through the actual arithmetic coefficient and observation maps. The bound applies jointly to the two consecutive arithmetic boundary classes and the terminal class. Their complete complex three-by-three Gram, positive remainder, kernel complement and all current cross terms are retained. [Complete proof](ORIGINAL_GAMMA_NATIVE_OBSERVED_GRAM.tex). The human input is [Christian Berg and Ryszard Szwarc, *The smallest eigenvalue of Hankel matrices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.4506v1), Section 3; the measure's mass is retained in the receiving proof.
- **The original full theta image is closed — SZ-20260920-008.** The original weighted derivative seminorms are identified explicitly with Meyer's topology. The full theta map is a closed embedding, so the formerly unevaluated closure kernel in E5 is zero. Its two-leg source generator is D plus (1−D). The exact formula for restricted source closures remains; no density or Hilbert-closedness claim is substituted. [Complete proof](ORIGINAL_THETA_CLOSED_RANGE.tex). Human source: [Ralf Meyer, *A spectral interpretation for the zeros of the Riemann zeta function*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412277v3), Theorem 3.3.
- **The actual arithmetic Jacobi commutant — SZ-20260920-015.** One coefficient sequence generated by the original arithmetic Laplace transform reconstructs every finite Gram entry. The finite compression retains an explicit rank-two boundary defect. The original observation metric is then recovered by its exact attained-minimum formula, including the physical Taylor unit and all complex coordinates. [Complete proof](NATIVE_COMMUTANT_INDEPENDENT_PROOF.tex). Human input: [František Štampach and Pavel Šťovíček, *Spectral representation of some weighted Hankel matrices and orthogonal polynomials from the Askey scheme*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05820v1), the Jacobi-commutant theorem.

These are exact topological and finite-metric results. They do not evaluate the remaining signed cross-product asymptotic and do not prove or disprove RH. The complete preceding source providers are included at their stated scope, and their human citations remain.
