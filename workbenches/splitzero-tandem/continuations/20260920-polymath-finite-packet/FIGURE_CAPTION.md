# The finite packet in both original coordinates

The left rectangle is the actual certified region $1/10<\Re s<9/10$, $507/4<\Im s<513/4$. The right is its exact image under $z=-2i(s-1/2)$: $507/2<\Re z<513/2$, $-4/5<\Im z<4/5$. Each panel uses equal lengths on its two coordinate axes, but the two panels have their own plotting scale. The arrows show the same counterclockwise orientation. The blue segment and dashed outer circle are an actual vertical-mesh segment and its error disk: radius $1/16$ in $s$, radius $1/8$ in $z$.

The green open segment marks the possible location of the unique simple zero, not a numerical root estimate. Exactly one zero, with full multiplicity, is certified inside the rectangle for every $t\in[1023/4096,1025/4096]$. Reflection $s\mapsto1-\bar s$, proved from the original integral, then forces its real part to be $1/2$. The count uses 128 segments, the exact source cutoff $N=K=4$, a positive margin on every boundary segment and winding number one. It also yields $|\rho'(t)|<1102$. The theorem does not assert a zero at every point of the green segment or an extension to $t=0$.

The source factors $g_t=16H_t$, $\partial_s^j=(-2i)^j\partial_z^j$, and $|dz|=2|ds|$ remain visible. Complete proof locators: PMH1–4 for the original source and finite stopping estimate; PMH5–9 for jets, exterior terms and traces; PMH10 for the actual packet; PMH11 and the independent derivation for the repaired source remainder.
