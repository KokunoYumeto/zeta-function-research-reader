# Complete-Weil determinants at five actual poles

The plotted ordinate is the base-ten logarithm of the strictly positive lower endpoint of each stored leading-principal determinant enclosure, for the original Toeplitz coefficient matrix (T_d(2+i\tau)), (0\le d\le8). The five curves use (\tau=0,14,21,100,1000). Matrix size is (d+1). The data are exactly the 45 determinant balls in `ARITHMETIC_BALL_CERTIFICATES.json`; rounding is used only for drawing positions.

The proof of their interpretation as complete-Weil matrices is MP8–15 in `MOVABLE_POLE_WEIL_DERIVATION.tex`. WP24–27 in `WEIL_POSITIVITY_AND_ARITHMETIC_CERTIFICATES.tex` gives the full-function coefficient algorithm and the Sylvester-criterion proof. Neither the numerical computation nor the analytic interpretation truncates the zero set. The small low-pole determinants remain strictly positive under 768-bit enclosure arithmetic. Determinants depend on the specified coefficient basis, so the curves make no claim about relative native-metric conditioning or an asymptotic spectral density.

The finite result is positive definiteness through degree eight at these five poles. MP16–18 is the separate unrestricted-degree criterion; the plot provides no inference of RH.
