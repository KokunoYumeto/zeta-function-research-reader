\subsection{The resulting operator and the excluded arithmetic neighborhood}

For every finite $D\ge0$ and original $s\in\{1,a_{\rm amp}\}$,
put $\rho_j=\sqrt{j!/(s/2)_j}$ and retain the original Gamma

AUH23 symbol. Then the extended original conductor has order zero
throughout $I_z$, and its exact matrix and inverse are
\[
\begin{gathered}
 B_{ij}^\Xi=\boldsymbol1_{j\ge i}g_{j-i}^\Xi\rho_j/\rho_i,
 \qquad \det B^\Xi=(\mu_0^\Xi)^{D+1}\ne0,\\
 h_0^\Xi=(\mu_0^\Xi)^{-1},\qquad
 h_n^\Xi=-(\mu_0^\Xi)^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n g_j^\Xi h_{n-j}^\Xi,
