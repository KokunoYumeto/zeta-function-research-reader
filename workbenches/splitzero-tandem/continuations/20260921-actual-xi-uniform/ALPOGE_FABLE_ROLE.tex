\section{Alp\"oge--Fable provenance and the exact role of the counterexample}
\label{sec:alpoge-fable-role}

The originating Jacobian counterexample is the one announced by
\textbf{Levent Alp\"oge} on 20 July 2026. His original announcement
credits Akhil for asking the question and Fable for the work leading
to the explicit polynomial \cite{AlpogeAnnouncement}. The canonical
ES source identifies these as Akhil Mathew and Claude Fable~5
\cite{ES488}. Terence Tao subsequently gives the polynomial and
its geometric explanation \cite{Tao}. The counterexample and its
discovery are not results of this Split-Zero calculation.

The mathematical input is precise: the three-dimensional polynomial
has constant Jacobian determinant $-2$, while three distinct points
share one image. It supplies an explicit mechanism by which local
invertibility coexists with global failure of injectivity. The ES
reader then constructs the four-dimensional marked-factor extension
used here, with four labelled inputs $q_{\mathrm M},q_{\mathrm N},
q_{\mathrm T},q_{\mathrm E}$ \cite{ES488}, theorem
\texttt{explicit-four-time-Jacobian-collision}. Those four inputs
belong to that extension; they are not an extra preimage silently
added to Alp\"oge's three-dimensional fibre. Their full coordinates
are retained in FC15a and FC31 of the accompanying cumulative proof.
