The analytic inputs to this note are the proved full-zero-sum
inequalities WP14 and WP23, and the certified five-pole matrices
WP26, in \cite{WP}. The receiving maps and their two distinct
original metrics are RW1--20 \cite{RW}; their exact native
matrices are NH1--42 \cite{NH}. We use those results at the
stated poles, retain every zero multiplicity in the Weil form,
and calculate the entire source and target coercivity on the
four-dimensional quotient of the full eight-state space.
No assertion about untested degrees or poles is used.

\section{The full signed space, its exact kernel and both minimum lifts}
Keep the four marked roots $r_j$ of the literal quartet polynomial
$h_\rho(r)=Ar^4+r^3+Br^2+Cr+D$, where
\[
 A=-\tfrac12,\quad B=\delta^2-\gamma^2-\tfrac34,\quad
 C=\gamma^2-\delta^2+\tfrac14,\quad
 D=-\tfrac1{32}-\tfrac{\gamma^2-\delta^2}{4}
                         -\tfrac{(\delta^2+\gamma^2)^2}{2},
 \quad0<\delta<\tfrac12,\quad\gamma>2.
\]
At each root retain both signs of $a_j^2h_\rho'(r_j)=1$,
put $d_j=h_\rho'(r_j)$, and use the actual mean and difference
columns of the inverse fibre:
\[
 e_j=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-r_j\\3id_j\\
 (B-17r_j+Ar_j^2)d_j-2Ad_j^2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 o_j=a_j\begin{pmatrix}1\\-id_j\\Ad_j^2+2r_jd_j\\
 7ir_j^2d_j-13id_j^2\end{pmatrix},\quad E=(e_j),\ O=(o_j).
 \tag{NW1}
\]
These are SE1, proved from the original ES polynomial
\cite{ES,SE}. The complete determinant proved in SE7 is
\[
 \det O=1792\chi\delta^2\gamma^2
 (2\delta^2\gamma^2-\delta^2+\gamma^2)\ne0,
 \qquad \chi=A^2\det(r_j^n)_{n,j}\prod_ja_j\in\{1,-1\}.
\]
Thus the inverse in every formula below is the explicit
$O^{-1}=\operatorname{adj}(O)/\det O$; no sign partner is
discarded.

Let $f_{j,+},f_{j,-}$ be the eight separate basis states.
Set $\iota_+c_j=(f_{j,+}+f_{j,-})/2$ and
$\iota_-c_j=(f_{j,+}-f_{j,-})/2$. Every state is uniquely
$v=\iota_+\alpha+\iota_-\beta$; define its complete evaluation
$z=E\alpha+O\beta$. The original source and target states
are, respectively,
\[
 (X\alpha,\Phi z)\in U\oplus U,\qquad
 (Y\alpha,\Psi z)\in W\oplus W,
 \quad T_AX=Y,\quad T_A\Phi=\Psi.
 \tag{NW2}
\]
All four maps are the existing exact conductor maps of
FC26--35, with the original periods and moments. Put
$G_X=X^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_U X$,
$G_Y=Y^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_W Y$,
$G_U=\Phi^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_U\Phi$ and
$G=\Psi^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_W\Psi$.
Each direct sum in NW2 has its original orthogonal direct-sum
norm, so the exact squared norms are
\[
 \|v\|_{8,U}^2=\alpha^*G_X\alpha+z^*G_Uz,
 \qquad
 \|v\|_{8,W}^2=\alpha^*G_Y\alpha+z^*Gz.
 \tag{NW3}
\]
No summand uses the norm from the other side of the conductor.

For the rational receiver retain
$\omega=2+i\tau$, $\tau\in\{0,14,21,100,1000\}$,
$h=3$, $d=h/2=3/2$, $c_*=a_{\rm amp}/2-4$ and
$\kappa=c_*-1/2$. Let $R_\omega$ be the complete coefficient
matrix of
$N_j(s)=\sqrt3(s+\bar\omega-1)^j(\omega-s)^{3-j}$,
$0\le j\le3$, and let $C_\kappa$ act by
$p(S')\mapsto p(s+\kappa)$. Its entries are
$(C_\kappa)_{r\ell}=\binom\ell r\kappa^{\ell-r}$ for
$r\le\ell$; its inverse is $C_{-\kappa}$. Define the exact
rational coefficient column and receiver by
\[
 d_v=B_\omega z,\qquad B_\omega=R_\omega^{-1}C_\kappa\Psi,
 \qquad
 F_v(s)=\sum_{j=0}^3(d_v)_j\frac{\sqrt3}{\omega-s}
                     \left(\frac{s+\bar\omega-1}{\omega-s}\right)^j.
 \tag{NW4}
\]
RW3 gives every coefficient of $R_\omega$ and its inverse;
$\det R_\omega=3^8\ne0$. Thus $B_\omega$ is an actual
isomorphism. The full Weil form is exactly
\[
 \mathbb W_\omega(v,v)=\W(F_v,F_v)=d_v^*T_3(\omega)d_v.
 \tag{NW5}
\]
This is the full zero sum of WP3 and RW12, including all
nontrivial zero multiplicities, not a four-zero truncation.

At all five poles $T_3(\omega)$ is strictly positive by
WP26, and at $\tau=0$ WP23 also supplies an analytic positive
lower bound. Therefore the full kernel in the eight-state
space is precisely
\[
 \mathcal N=\{\iota_+\alpha-\iota_-O^{-1}E\alpha:
                              \alpha\in\C^4\}.
 \tag{NW6}
\]
Its four explicit independent generators, in the original
eight marked basis states, are
\[
 n_j=\frac{f_{j,+}+f_{j,-}}2
       -\frac12\sum_{\ell=1}^4(O^{-1}E)_{\ell j}
                                      (f_{\ell,+}-f_{\ell,-}).
 \tag{NW7}
\]
Independence follows from their even components $c_j$.
Their evaluation is zero, so they are annihilated by NW5.
Conversely positivity forces $d_v=0$, and the invertible
$B_\omega,O$ then give exactly NW6. This proves both
inclusions and the exact nullity four.

For every coefficient column $d$ there is a unique vector
orthogonal to $\mathcal N$ in either of the two original
norms and having that column:
\[
 \ell_\omega(d)=\iota_-O^{-1}B_\omega^{-1}d.
 \tag{NW8}
\]
To prove both minimization assertions rather than just a
section identity, every vector with this column is
$\ell_\omega(d)+\iota_+\alpha-\iota_-O^{-1}E\alpha$.
In NW3 its evaluation is the fixed $B_\omega^{-1}d$,
and the remaining squared norm is $\alpha^*G_X\alpha$
or $\alpha^*G_Y\alpha$, strictly minimized at $\alpha=0$.
The cross term vanishes by NW2, because the first component
of the section and the second component of the null vector
are both zero. Consequently the exact quotient norms are
\[
 \|[v]\|_{Q,W}^2=d_v^*H_\omega d_v=z^*Gz,
 \qquad
 \|[v]\|_{Q,U}^2=d_v^*H_{U,\omega}d_v=z^*G_Uz.
 \tag{NW9}
\]
The actual target polynomial of NW8 is
$C_{-\kappa}R_\omega d$; its actual source is
$T_A^{-1}C_{-\kappa}R_\omega d$, with FC24 coefficients
evaluated below. Thus both minimum lifts are fully typed
through the original conductor.

\section{Evaluated original target and source constants}
Retain $b=s/2>0$, $M=(2\pi)^{s/2}$ and the original
measure
\[
 dm_s(y)=\frac{M2^{s/2}}{4\pi\Gamma(s/2)}
                 |\Gamma(s/4+iy/2)|^2dy.
 \tag{NW10}
\]
Its moments are $M$, $Mb$, $M(3b^2+2b)$ and
$M(15b^3+30b^2+16b)$ in degrees $0,2,4,6$, with
odd moments zero \cite{WCF,NH}. For the target, all diagonal
entries of $H_\omega$ are
$3\int((3/2)^2+(y-\tau)^2)^3dm_s(y)$. Expanding with
these unchanged moments gives the following exact trace:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 t_W(\tau):=\tr H_\omega=12M\big[&\tau^6+(15b+27/4)\tau^4
 +(45b^2+141b/2+243/16)\tau^2\\
 &+15b^3+201b^2/4+715b/16+729/64\big]>0.
\end{aligned}\tag{NW11}
\]
Its exact determinant is
\[
 D_W:=\det H_\omega=12\cdot3^{16}M^4b^3(b+1)^2(b+2)>0.
 \tag{NW12}
\]
No test-pole height is omitted from NW11; the height cancels
from NW12 by the exact coefficient determinant proved in
RW11 and NH10.

Here is an equally explicit source trace, including every
original inverse coefficient. Let $v$ be the actual order
of the original conductor and $\mu_n=E_A^{(n)}(0)$,
$\mu_v\ne0$. Retain
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \nu_{v+j}=\sum_{r=0}^j\binom{v+j}{v+r}(-4)^{j-r}\mu_{v+r},
 \quad0\le j\le3,\\
 g_0=(-i)^v\mu_v/v!,\quad
 g_1=(-i)^{v+1}\nu_{v+1}/(v+1)!,\\
 g_2=(-i)^{v+2}\nu_{v+2}/(v+2)!-(v/3)g_0,\quad
 g_3=(-i)^{v+3}\nu_{v+3}/(v+3)!-((v+1)/3)g_1,\\
 \xi_0=g_0^{-1},\quad \xi_1=-g_1g_0^{-2},\quad
 \xi_2=g_1^2g_0^{-3}-g_2g_0^{-2},\quad
 \xi_3=-g_1^3g_0^{-4}+2g_1g_2g_0^{-3}-g_3g_0^{-2},\\
 k_j=(b)_{v+j}/(v+j)!,\quad j=0,1,2,3.
\end{gathered}\tag{NW13}
\]
These are the finite FC24 inverse coefficients, including
the original center separation $4$ and the original
$w(t)=-i\arctan t$ phase. Their derivation and multiplication
check are NH26--32. Write the full matrix
\[
 F=\begin{pmatrix}
 \xi_0&\xi_1&b\xi_0+2\xi_2&(3b+2)\xi_1+6\xi_3\\
 0&\xi_0&2\xi_1&(3b+2)\xi_0+6\xi_2\\
 0&0&2\xi_0&6\xi_1\\0&0&0&6\xi_0
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{NW14}
\]
In the original minimum quotient orthonormal frame
$[\phi_v],\ldots,[\phi_{v+3}]$ at center $c_0=c_*+4$,
the inverse images of $1,y,y^2,y^3$, where
$y=(S'-c_*)/i$, have matrix
$\sqrt M\diag(\sqrt{k_j})F$. The actual polynomials are
specified by
$\phi_n(S)=p_{n,s}((S-c_0)/i)/\sqrt{Mn!(b)_n}$ and
$p_{n+1,s}=yp_{n,s}-n(n-1+b)p_{n-1,s}$,
$p_{0,s}=1,p_{1,s}=y$. These representatives contain no
component in the removed kernel $\mathcal P_{v-1}$, so
their Gram $K_U=MF^*\diag(k_j)F$ is the original minimum
quotient Gram. Its entries and all leading minors are
evaluated in NH33--34.

For complete trace evaluation define the following explicit
real symmetric polynomial matrix, with $d=3/2$:
\[
 Z(\tau)=4\begin{pmatrix}
 (d^2+\tau^2)^3&-3\tau(d^2+\tau^2)^2&-(d^2-3\tau^2)(d^2+\tau^2)&\tau(d^2-\tau^2)\\
 -3\tau(d^2+\tau^2)^2&(d^2+\tau^2)(5d^2+9\tau^2)&-\tau(7d^2+9\tau^2)&-(d^2-3\tau^2)\\
 -(d^2-3\tau^2)(d^2+\tau^2)&-\tau(7d^2+9\tau^2)&5d^2+9\tau^2&-3\tau\\
 \tau(d^2-\tau^2)&-(d^2-3\tau^2)&-3\tau&1
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{NW15}
\]
Every entry comes from $Z=Q(\tau)Q(\tau)^*$, where the
column $j$ of $Q$ is the full coefficient vector of
$(d+i(y-\tau))^j(d-i(y-\tau))^{3-j}$. NH6 gives all
sixteen entries of $Q$; expanding their pairwise products
proves NW15 directly. The exact source trace and determinant
are therefore the finite original-moment expressions
\[
 \boxed{\quad t_U(\tau)=3M\sum_{r=0}^3k_r
               \sum_{p,q=0}^3F_{rp}\overline{F_{rq}}Z_{pq}(\tau),
 \qquad
 D_U=144\cdot3^{16}M^4k_0k_1k_2k_3|\xi_0|^8.\quad}
 \tag{NW16}
\]
All symbols on the right are evaluated in NW13--15;
there is no unspecified matrix norm. Indeed
$H_{U,\omega}=3Q^*K_UQ$, so its trace is the first
expression by multiplication. Its determinant is the
squared modulus of the exact triangular source coefficient
matrix, times $|\det(\sqrt3Q)|^2=3^{16}$, giving the
second expression. Both are strictly positive. Formula
NW16 retains every cross term and every inverse moment;
it does not replace $K_U$ by its diagonal.

\section{Five explicit unconditional native coercivity bounds}
The provider's analytic full-form inequality WP23 is
\[
 T_3(2)\succeq a_*I,\qquad
 a_*=\frac1{2\cdot200^4\cdot30^{12}}>0.
 \tag{NW17}
\]
Its proof includes the unverified high zeros by the signed
tail estimate WP20--22, and depends on the stated theorems
of Platt--Trudgian and Hasanalizade--Shen--Wong through
WP4--5. It is unconditional. The five exact-ball matrices
in WP26 give stronger numerical coefficient bounds that
can be extracted without rerunning the zeta calculation.
The following are strict outward rational enclosures of
the provider's stored real balls:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 \tau&c_0<q_\tau&\det T_3(2+i\tau)>D_\tau&
       a_\tau=27D_\tau/(4q_\tau)^3\\\hline
 0&7/50&468/10^{17}&3159/4390400000000000\\
 14&38/25&967/10^8&26109/22475571200\\
 21&173/100&1299/10^5&175365/165686944\\
 100&251/100&9&3796875/15813251\\
 1000&127/25&556&58640625/32774128
\end{array}
\tag{NW18}
\]
All first four principal minors in each stored matrix were
proved positive by WP26, hence the four eigenvalues are
positive. Their sum is $4c_0<4q_\tau=:S_\tau$. If
$\lambda$ is the smallest, the product of the other three
is at most $(S_\tau/3)^3$ by the arithmetic--geometric
mean inequality. Thus
\[
 a_\tau I\preceq T_3(2+i\tau)\preceq S_\tau I.
 \tag{NW19}
\]
The strict inequalities from the balls imply these weak
matrix inequalities with the displayed exact rational
constants. At zero, both $a_*$ and $a_0$ are available;
$a_0>a_*$ is also an exact rational comparison.

Every positive four-by-four Hermitian matrix $H$ satisfies
\[
 H\preceq(\tr H)I,\qquad
 H\succeq\frac{27\det H}{(\tr H)^3}I.
 \tag{NW20}
\]
The first follows by summing its positive eigenvalues;
the second follows by the same product-of-three argument
just used. Apply NW20 separately to the actual $H_\omega$
and $H_{U,\omega}$, with their explicitly evaluated
traces and determinants. Combining with NW5, NW9 and
NW19 proves the complete two-sided quotient estimates:
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \frac{a_\tau}{t_W(\tau)}\|[v]\|_{Q,W}^2
 &\le\mathbb W_{2+i\tau}(v,v)
 \le\frac{S_\tau t_W(\tau)^3}{27D_W}\|[v]\|_{Q,W}^2,\\
 \frac{a_\tau}{t_U(\tau)}\|[v]\|_{Q,U}^2
 &\le\mathbb W_{2+i\tau}(v,v)
 \le\frac{S_\tau t_U(\tau)^3}{27D_U}\|[v]\|_{Q,U}^2,
 \qquad\tau\in\{0,14,21,100,1000\}.
\end{aligned}}\tag{NW21}
\]
Every constant on the right and left is the finite expression
NW11--16 and NW18. Thus these are quantitative inequalities
in each original native norm, with no coefficient norm left
unconverted. At $\tau=0$ the entirely analytic lower
constants $a_*/t_W(0)$ and $a_*/t_U(0)$ follow from
NW17 by exactly the same proof, independently of the
matrix ball computation.

For completeness, a sharper direct native expression is
also exact at each certified pole:
\[
 \mathbb W_\omega(v,v)\ge
 \frac{\det T_3(\omega)}{\tr(\operatorname{adj}(T_3(\omega))H_J)}
                         \|[v]\|_{Q,J}^2,
 \qquad J=W\text{ or }U.
 \tag{NW22}
\]
The denominator is strictly positive. To prove it, let
$A=H_J^{1/2}T_3^{-1}H_J^{1/2}>0$. Then
$\lambda_{\max}(A)\le\tr A$
$=\tr(T_3^{-1}H_J)$; inversion gives NW22.
Its numerator and denominator are finite determinant and
cofactor expressions in the actual certified Toeplitz
entries and the full original matrices, so NW22 can be
enclosed directly without introducing a spectral norm
parameter. NW21 already supplies explicit rational and
original-moment bounds without requiring this refinement.

\section{The cubic Gaussian counterpart in both original norms}
The provider also proves the complete Gaussian inequality
WP14. To retain its exact meaning, put
\[
 \mathcal A(s)=\tfrac12s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2),\quad
 \phi_*(x)=(4\pi^2x^4-6\pi x^2)e^{-\pi x^2},\quad
 D_x=-x\,d/dx.
 \tag{NW23}
\]
For $r(s)=\sum_{j=0}^3r_js^j$, its actual test function
$r(D_x)\phi_*$ has Mellin transform $\mathcal A(s)r(s)$
on $\Re s>-2$, by WP1--2 including their boundary-term
proof. WP14 gives, for this full cubic source space,
\[
 \W(\mathcal A r,\mathcal A r)\ge e^{-300}\sum_j|r_j|^2.
 \tag{NW24}
\]
For our full eight-state evaluation, its exact polynomial
is $r(s)=(\Psi z)(s+\kappa)$ and its source is therefore
$[\Psi z](D_x+\kappa)\phi_*$. This retains the original
center translation and every entry of $\Psi$, as in WP15--16.

The coefficient conversion from $r(s)$ to its value on
$s=1/2+iy$ is exactly
\[
 D_G=\begin{pmatrix}
 1&1/2&1/4&1/8\\0&i&i&3i/4\\0&0&-1&-3/2\\0&0&0&-i
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 Z_G=D_GD_G^*=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 85/64&-27i/32&-7/16&i/8\\
 27i/32&41/16&-17i/8&-3/4\\
 -7/16&17i/8&13/4&-3i/2\\
 -i/8&-3/4&3i/2&1
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{NW25}
\]
The original target monomial Gram in $y$ is
$K=(m_{p+q})_{0\le p,q\le3}$ with the moments NW10;
the source one is $K_U=MF^*\diag(k_j)F$ from NW14.
The Gaussian coefficient Grams in the two original
arithmetic norms are exactly $J_W=D_G^*KD_G$ and
$J_U=D_G^*K_UD_G$. Their evaluated traces are
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 t_{G,W}&=M(85/64+339b/16+141b^2/4+15b^3),\\
 t_{G,U}&=M\sum_{r=0}^3k_r\sum_{p,q=0}^3
                         F_{rp}\overline{F_{rq}}(Z_G)_{pq}.
\end{aligned}}\tag{NW26}
\]
For the first identity, integrate
$\sum_{j=0}^3|1/2+iy|^{2j}$ using the full moments NW10;
for the second, multiply the explicitly displayed matrices.
Both traces are strictly positive, and no Gaussian
$L^2$ norm has been substituted for the original conductor
norm. The two original centers have been transported
through the exact $s=S'-\kappa$ dictionary.

The Gaussian full eight-state form has exactly the same
four-dimensional kernel NW6: its coefficient receiver
$C_\kappa\Psi$ is invertible and NW24 is strictly positive.
For a Gaussian coefficient column $r$, the actual
orthogonal minimum lift in either original norm is
\[
 \ell_G(r)=\iota_-O^{-1}\Psi^{-1}C_{-\kappa}r.
 \tag{NW27}
\]
Its target is the polynomial $r(S'-\kappa)$ and its
source is $T_A^{-1}r(S'-\kappa)$, with inverse coefficients
NW13--14. The same minimization argument as NW8 proves
that its quotient squared norms are $r^*J_Wr$ and
$r^*J_Ur$. Combining NW24 with the trace upper bounds
therefore gives the two unconditional original-native
Gaussian inequalities on every full signed state:
\[
\boxed{\quad
 \W(\mathcal A r,\mathcal A r)
 \ge\frac{e^{-300}}{t_{G,W}}\|[v]\|_{Q,W}^2,
 \qquad
 \W(\mathcal A r,\mathcal A r)
 \ge\frac{e^{-300}}{t_{G,U}}\|[v]\|_{Q,U}^2.
 \quad}\tag{NW28}
\]
These apply to the same original quotient spaces as NW21,
through the displayed Gaussian coefficient receiver; the
rational and Gaussian coefficient columns are not
identified with one another.
