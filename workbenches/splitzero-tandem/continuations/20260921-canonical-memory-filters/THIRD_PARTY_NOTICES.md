# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited proof container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). `REPRODUCE.py` extracts the exact retained bytes using the offsets and hashes in `SOURCE_LEDGER.json`.

Its GNU Free Documentation License applies separately from the surrounding files. The unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) accompanies it, with the [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING). No additional restriction is imposed on that source. The complete Stacks chapter requires its upstream dependencies to build. Retention is not a claim of exhaustive reading. Earlier uses of [cochain homotopies](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3057) and [quasi-isomorphisms](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3160) retain their precise citations.

The finite Feshbach–Schur derivations cite **Geneviève Dusson, Israel Michael Sigal and Benjamin Stamm**, [original arXiv:2105.02058v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02058v1). This edition does not republish their full paper; it retains the programme's complete finite derivations, exact primary-source links and actual source-use coverage.

`HUMAN_EQUATION_SOURCES.tex` preserves original NIST DLMF equation TeX. Source records and the reader retain formula locators and chapter-author credits, including T. M. Apostol; R. A. Askey and R. Roy; T. H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek, R. F. Swarttouw and W. P. Reinhardt. Earlier and new programme derivations retain their distinct source identities.

All 19 owner-sealed files remain unchanged. This notice and the licence copy are additive publication companions.
