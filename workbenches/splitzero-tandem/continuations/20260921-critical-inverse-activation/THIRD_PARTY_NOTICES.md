# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited source container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). `REPRODUCE.py` extracts its exact bytes; `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` records the extraction offsets, lengths and hashes.

The chapter is an original human source, not a programme-authored derivation. Its GNU Free Documentation License applies independently of the surrounding files. The unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) accompanies it, with the [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING). No additional restriction is imposed on that source. Building the complete Stacks chapter requires its upstream dependencies. Retention does not claim exhaustive reading.

The earlier complex definitions use the chapter's [cochain homotopies](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3057) and [quasi-isomorphisms](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3160). Their complete programme sources and citations remain in the retained proof container.

`HUMAN_EQUATION_SOURCES.tex` preserves original NIST DLMF equation TeX. The reader and source records retain formula locations and chapter authors, including T. M. Apostol; R. A. Askey and R. Roy; T. H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek, R. F. Swarttouw and W. P. Reinhardt. The mathematical reading/use records specify the actual source coverage and receiving calculations. Earlier and new derivations retain their distinct source identities and credits.

All 19 owner-sealed files remain unchanged. This notice and licence copy are additive publication companions.
