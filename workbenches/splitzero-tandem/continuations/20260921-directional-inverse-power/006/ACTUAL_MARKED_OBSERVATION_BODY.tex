\section{The four original upper corners in the actual observation}
This continuation uses the actual invariant observation
$\Lambda_k:E_k\to B_k$, rather than selecting a lift into $B_k$.
Its original period and all its unit factors are those in FC13.
Retain $j=k-8$, $k\equiv1\pmod4$, $k\ge9$ and the two complete
grid polynomials in CA1, with $k$ and $j$ respectively. Define
$J_+:\C^4\to E_k$ and $J_-:\C^4\to E_j$ by the original
four corner cardinal classes in the order
$(\mathrm M,\mathrm N,\mathrm T,\mathrm E)$.
Their roots are $k\rho_l$ and $j\rho_l$. Write $p_l^+,p_l^-$
for their unique representatives of degrees less than $(k+1)^2$
and $(j+1)^2$. Every denominator is its original
$\chi_k'(k\rho_l)$ or $\chi_j'(j\rho_l)$.

Let $C:E_k\to E_j$ be the original conductor quotient map and
$\bar C:B_k\to E_j$ the map with $\bar C\Lambda_k=C$.
This is an actual factorization: the conductor-multiple invariant
polynomials give $\ker\Lambda_k\subset\ker C$, as proved in
\cite[OB3]{Observed}. The formula
$\bar C(\Lambda_kx)=Cx$ is independent of the choice of lift
by that kernel inclusion. Put
\[
 D_\partial=\diag(a_{88},a_{80},a_{08},a_{00}),\qquad
 O_+=\Lambda_kJ_+,\qquad
 CJ_+=J_-D_\partial,\quad
 \bar C O_+=J_-D_\partial. \tag{MO1}
\]
To prove the third identity, evaluate the finite-shift conductor
at every lower grid root. A shifted lower root reaches an upper
corner only when the lower root is that same corner and the
shift is the matching one among $(8,8),(8,0),(0,8),(0,0)$.
The values are exactly the corresponding original coefficients,
so equality of classes follows by interpolation. This proves
all four columns, including their actual phases.

Before reduction, the entire residual is retained:
\[
 T_Ap_l^+(S')=d_l p_l^-(S')+\chi_j(S')Z_l(S'),\qquad
 Z_l(S')=\frac{\sum_{u,w=0}^8a_{uw}
 p_l^+(S'+\beta_{8,u,w})-d_l p_l^-(S')}{\chi_j(S')},
 \tag{MO2}
\]
where $d_l=(D_\partial)_{ll}$.
The numerator vanishes at every distinct lower grid root by the
preceding evaluation proof, so $Z_l$ is a polynomial, not a
meromorphic substitution. Its degree is at most
$(k+1)^2-(j+1)^2-v-1=16k-49-v$; a negative bound means the
zero polynomial. MO2 gives every coefficient by exact finite
division of its displayed numerator. Thus MO1 has not silently
deleted a polynomial relation from the original WCF norm.

On the explicit original large-period domain of
\cite[OC50--74]{CornerNonzero}, every $d_l$ is nonzero. In the
original notation that domain is $\gamma\ge1000\delta$ and
$|\varpi|\ge R_{\rm corner}^{\rm explicit}$, where all constants
are given in OC53, OC64 and OC67--71; the exact lower bound is
$|d_l|\ge(m_{\rm corner}/2)^4$. This is an already evaluated
period-domain result, with the unit phase retained, rather than
an assumed coefficient sign. MO1 then proves that $O_+$ is
injective: if $O_+c=0$, injectivity of $J_-$ and invertibility
of $D_\partial$ give $c=0$. At every other original period,
the exact partial-support treatment AO1--23 below applies and
does not identify $d_l=0$ with $\Lambda_ke_l=0$.

Let $A_k$ and $A_j$ be the original multiplication operators
on $E_k,E_j$. They act on these corners by
\[
 A_kJ_+=J_+ \mathsf B_k,\quad A_jJ_-=J_-\mathsf B_j,\qquad
 \mathsf B_k=\diag(k\rho_l),\quad \mathsf B_j=\diag(j\rho_l).
 \tag{MO3}
\]
These are identities of the actual quotient operators by the
cardinal formula CA6. On the four-dimensional image of $O_+$,
the actual observed action is
$\Theta_+=O_+\mathsf B_kO_+^{-1}$: directly
$\Lambda_kA_kJ_+=\Theta_+\Lambda_kJ_+$.
No action on the full one-step quotient $B_k$ is inferred.
Its conductor defect is completely explicit:
\[
 (\bar C\Theta_+-A_j\bar C)O_+
       =8J_-D_\partial\diag(\rho_l),\qquad
 \bar C\Theta_+=\frac{k}{j}A_j\bar C
                 \quad\text{on }\operatorname{im}O_+.
 \tag{MO4}
\]
This follows from MO1--3 and the fact that the two displayed
diagonal matrices commute. The factor $k/j$ is retained;
neither original arithmetic action has been replaced by the other.

\section{The exact original minimum correction on these four corners}
Fix $N\ge(k+1)^2-1$ and the original polynomial Gram $H_N$
of $m_k(y)dy=w_h^{*k}(y)dy$ at $S=k/2+iy$, with
$w_h(y)=|(2\xi/h)(1/2+iy)|^2/(2\pi)$ and the original
quartet factor $h(S)=\prod_l(S-\rho_l)$.
Let $J_N:\mathcal P_N\to E_k$ be reduction and retain
\[
 G_N=(J_NH_N^{-1}J_N^*)^{-1},\quad
 Q_N=(\Lambda_kG_N^{-1}\Lambda_k^*)^{-1},\quad
 T_N=(\bar C Q_N^{-1}\bar C^*)^{-1},\quad
 R_N=Q_N^{-1}\bar C^*T_N. \tag{MO5}
\]
These are the original attained metrics of \cite[EQ1--5]{EightMinimum}.
Their inverses exist: the reductions and observation are onto their
defined images, and $C$ is onto because its triangular polynomial
map drops degree by $v\le80$ while the grid-degree difference is
$16k-48\ge96$. The actual density is positive almost everywhere;
its isolated zero set cannot annihilate a nonzero polynomial, so
the finite Grams are positive. The moments and original total mass
in $H_N$ are not replaced by a Gamma surrogate.

The difference between the original observation and its minimum
lift through the conductor is the exact matrix
\[
 \Delta_N=O_+-R_NJ_-D_\partial,\qquad
 \bar C\Delta_N=0. \tag{MO6}
\]
Since $R_N^*Q_N=T_N\bar C$, the two summands are orthogonal
in the original $Q_N$ metric. Thus the complete four-corner
observed Gram is
\[
 H_+=O_+^*Q_NO_+
 =D_\partial^*J_-^*T_NJ_-D_\partial+
                   \Delta_N^*Q_N\Delta_N. \tag{MO7}
\]
No assertion is made that the second term vanishes.
There is a separate exact correction from the original source
quotient: put $M_N=G_N^{-1}\Lambda_k^*Q_N$ and
$Z_N=(I-M_N\Lambda_k)J_+$. Then
$\Lambda_kM_N=I$, $M_N^*G_NM_N=Q_N$,
$\Lambda_kZ_N=0$, and direct orthogonal decomposition gives
\[
 J_+^*G_NJ_+=H_++Z_N^*G_NZ_N. \tag{MO8}
\]
Equations MO7 and MO8 retain the two different kernels and both
original positive residual energies. They also show precisely
which norms accompany an original observed trajectory.

\section{The literal Fable marking and its arithmetic generator}

$\bar C(F_BPF_B^{-1})=(F_-PF_-^{-1})\bar C$.

\[
 \widehat B_k=K\diag(k\rho_l)K^{-1},\qquad

 \qquad \widehat G_N=K^{-*}H_+K^{-1}>0.
 \tag{MO10}

This formula gives every coefficient through the displayed FC15a

The unique polynomial lift of MO10 is

\section{Every point of the original common fibre along its actual orbit}
Let $h_0=(0,0,-1,0)^T=2iKe_{\mathrm M}$ and
$\lambda=k\rho_\mathrm M$.
MO10 proves $\widehat B_k h_0=\lambda h_0$.
Set $b(t)=e^{\lambda t/2}$; this entire function never vanishes.
The complete original fibre over $e^{\lambda t}h_0$ consists of
the following seven trajectories:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 q_\infty(t)&=(0,0,ib(t)^2/2,0)^T,\\
 q_{0,\epsilon}(t)&=
 (\epsilon i/b(t),-\epsilon b(t),-3ib(t)^2,
                       13\epsilon b(t)^3)^T,
                         &&\epsilon\in\{1,-1\},\\
 q_{\eta,\epsilon}(t)&=
 \bigl(\epsilon/(\sqrt2 b(t)),
  -(\eta+i\epsilon\sqrt2)b(t),
  (2\eta\epsilon\sqrt2+6i)b(t)^2,
  (-34\eta-19i\epsilon\sqrt2)b(t)^3\bigr)^T,
                         &&\eta,\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}.
 \end{aligned} \tag{MO11}
\]
GF7 gives them from the roots $0,\pm b(t)$ with derivatives
$-b(t)^2,2b(t)^2,2b(t)^2$, and GF8 gives the infinity point.
Substituting in the full original $P$ proves
$P(q(t))=e^{\lambda t}h_0$. Conversely that cubic has precisely
these three simple finite roots and the one chart infinity root,
so GF's full fibre classification proves completeness of the list.
The original four named points occur at $t=0$ as
$q_\infty,q_{0,+},q_{+,+},q_{-,+}$ in order M,N,T,E.

Differentiating their exact image equality and using $DP^{-1}$
shows that every trajectory solves $q'=X_{\widehat B_k}(q)$.
All seven are entire functions of complex time, so none escapes
at a finite complex time on this particular original orbit.
After the target period $2\pi i/\lambda$, $b$ changes sign:
$q_\infty$ returns, $q_{0,\epsilon}$ is exchanged with
$q_{0,-\epsilon}$, and $q_{\eta,\epsilon}$ with
$q_{-\eta,-\epsilon}$. This computes the original fibre's
continuation without merging any two of its marked states.

\section{An exact marked arithmetic orbit with five escaping branches}
The same original generator nevertheless has an explicit finite
boundary crossing. Put
\[
 u_*=(0,1,0,0)^T,\qquad
 u(t)=e^{t\widehat B_k}u_*
     =K\diag(e^{k\rho_l t})K^{-1}u_*.
 \tag{MO12}
\]
This is a complete formula in the fixed original period. Direct
multiplication of FC15a and its inverse gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \nu_A:=A'(0)&=\frac{k}{154}
       [-68\gamma+i(25\sqrt2\gamma-136\delta)]\ne0,\\
 \nu_D:=D'(0)&=\frac{k}{77}
       [-884\delta+(1631\sqrt2+442i)\gamma]\ne0.
 \end{aligned} \tag{MO13}
\]
Nonvanishing follows from the nonzero real part of the first
and the positive imaginary part of the second. These identities
are checked exactly over $\mathbb Q(i,\sqrt2)$ by
\texttt{derive\_marked\_arithmetic\_generator.py}.
At $u_*$ the quartic is $h_*(x)=x^2(x+1)$.
The full degree-four discriminant polynomial, retaining its
fixed cubic coefficient, has gradient $(0,0,0,-4)$ there.
This follows by differentiation of its resultant determinant
(and is reproduced by \texttt{verify\_marked\_fibre\_flow.py}).
Thus the discriminant along MO12 has derivative $-4\nu_D\ne0$.

There is a completely specified punctured time disk containing
only eight-state fibres. For an explicit radius form the finite
polynomial
\[
 \mathcal D(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4)=
 \operatorname{Disc}_x\bigl(A(z)x^4+x^3+B(z)x^2+C(z)x+D(z)\bigr)
 =\sum_\mu c_\mu z^\mu,
 \quad (A(z),B(z),C(z),D(z))^T
       =K\diag(z_l)K^{-1}u_*.
 \tag{MO14}
\]
This specifies every coefficient by the finite resultant and the
literal original matrix. Write $A(z)=\sum_l\alpha_lz_l$ and
$\theta_\mu=k\sum_l\mu_l\rho_l$, and retain the finite sums
\[
 M_D=\sum_\mu|c_\mu|\,|\theta_\mu|^2e^{|\theta_\mu|},\quad
 M_A=\sum_l|\alpha_l|\,|k\rho_l|^2e^{|k\rho_l|},\quad
 r_0=\min\{1,4|\nu_D|/M_D,|\nu_A|/M_A\}>0.
 \tag{MO15}
\]
Both sums are nonzero since the respective first derivatives
are nonzero. Taylor's integral remainder for each exponential
gives errors at most $M_D|t|^2/2$ and $M_A|t|^2/2$ for
$|t|\le1$. Their constant terms vanish. It follows that,
for $0<|t|\le r_0$, the discriminant and $A(t)$ have moduli
at least $2|\nu_D||t|$ and $|\nu_A||t|/2$ respectively.
GF16 therefore gives exactly eight inverse states throughout
this punctured disk. The radius does not require a numerical
search for zeros or a discarded higher Taylor term.

Four states select the two roots colliding at zero. Substitute
$t=s^2$ and $x=sv$ in the full $h_{u(t)}(x)$ and divide by
$s^2$. The analytic extension at zero is $v^2+\nu_D$,
whose two roots $v_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-\nu_D}$ are simple.
Analytic implicit inversion gives
\[
 r_\pm(t)=v_\pm t^{1/2}+O(t),\quad
 h_{u(t)}'(r_\pm(t))=2v_\pm t^{1/2}+O(t),\quad
 t^{1/4}q_{\pm,\epsilon}(t)\longrightarrow\kappa_{\pm,\epsilon}e_a,
 \quad\kappa_{\pm,\epsilon}^2=(2v_\pm)^{-1}.
 \tag{MO16}
\]
Both values of each square root are retained on a chosen time
sector. The final limit follows from all four coordinates of
GF7: $a\sim\kappa t^{-1/4}$, while $y,z,w\to0$.

The projective root at infinity is simple at $t=0$ and supplies

The positive infinity state is finite. The remaining root

\[
 (0,1,7i/2,11)^T,\qquad

                       \quad\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}. \tag{MO18}

Substitution in $P$ verifies their common image $u_*$, and

The fibre count eight on the punctured disk and three at its
