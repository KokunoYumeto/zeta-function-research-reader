\subsection{A complete finite map from the original moments to the two jets}

\[

       \frac{\prod_{c\ne a}(X-\rho_c)}
            {\prod_{c\ne a}(\rho_a-\rho_c)}

 \tag{ARR1}
\]

coefficient column is exactly $(V_\rho^{\mathsf T})^{-1}K^{-1}e_1$.

no real part of the original complex polynomial is discarded.
For the unchanged real translation $x=P+\kappa$ define
\[

 \quad0\le q\le3,\qquad
 \eta'_q=(v+q)\eta_{q-1}\quad(q\ge1),\quad\eta_0=\mu_v.
 \tag{ARR2}
\]
The derivative formula follows from
$r\binom{v+q}{r}=(v+q)\binom{v+q-1}{r-1}$.
The map from $(\mu_v,\ldots,\mu_{v+3})$ to $\eta$ is triangular
with diagonal one. Its full inverse is
$\mu_{v+q}=\sum_{r=0}^q\binom{v+q}{r}(-x)^r\eta_{q-r}$:
coefficient multiplication gives the factor $(x-x)^r$ for each
off-diagonal entry. Thus both directions retain all four moments.
The original triangular matrix $B_{v,*}$ now gives
\[

\]

\]
