\section{The rational test receiver with the full original Gamma metric}
The received continuation \cite[Equations (33)--(36)]{ResidueIntake}
proposes a rational-function receiver of the signed object. Here its
entire coefficient matrix, inverse, original metric, graph kernel and
spectral comparison are calculated. No positivity claim from that
intake is used in this calculation. Its full-Weil form below is the
actual zero sum, including all multiplicities.

Retain the original $a_{\rm amp}$, $s\in\{1,a_{\rm amp}\}$,
$v$, $c_*=a_{\rm amp}/2-4$, $\kappa=c_*-1/2$,
the original measure $dm_s$ in FC28, and the exact maps
$\Phi,\Psi$ in FC32. For the test pole set
\[
 \omega=\sigma+i\tau,\quad\sigma>1,\quad
 h=2\sigma-1>0,\quad b_\omega=\bar\omega-1,\quad
 d=\sigma-\tfrac12=h/2.
 \tag{RW1}
\]
The positive square root $\sqrt h$ is retained. The symbol $s$ in
$dm_s$ is the original Gamma parameter; the complex variable of
the rational functions will be $z$ throughout this section.
Define, for $0\le j\le n$,
\[
 N_j^{(n)}(z)=\sqrt h\,(z+b_\omega)^j(\omega-z)^{n-j},\qquad
 \varphi_j^\omega(z)=\frac{N_j^{(n)}(z)}{(\omega-z)^{n+1}}
 =\frac{\sqrt h}{\omega-z}
           \left(\frac{z+b_\omega}{\omega-z}\right)^j.
 \tag{RW2}
\]
In particular this defines the same rational function for every
$n\ge j$; it does not change the denominator convention of a
fixed finite numerator space.

Let $R_\omega^{(n)}$ take a column $d_0,\ldots,d_n$ to the
coefficients of $\sum_jd_jN_j^{(n)}$ in $(1,z,\ldots,z^n)$.
Every entry and every inverse entry is the following finite sum:
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (R_\omega^{(n)})_{\ell j}
  &=\sqrt h\sum_{r=0}^j
    \binom jr b_\omega^{j-r}
    \binom{n-j}{\ell-r}\omega^{n-j-\ell+r}(-1)^{\ell-r},\\
 ((R_\omega^{(n)})^{-1})_{j\ell}
  &=h^{-n-1/2}\sum_{r=0}^{\ell}
     \binom\ell r\omega^r(-b_\omega)^{\ell-r}
                         \binom{n-\ell}{j-r}.
 \end{aligned}\tag{RW3}
\]
In the first line only indices with $0\le\ell-r\le n-j$
are included, so there is no negative power hidden behind a
vanishing binomial coefficient. Binomial coefficients outside
their nonnegative range in the second line are zero.
Expansion of RW2 proves the first formula. For the inverse put
$u=(z+b_\omega)/(\omega-z)$, so
$z=(\omega u-b_\omega)/(1+u)$ and
$\omega-z=h/(1+u)$. For an arbitrary numerator $p$, the polynomial
\[
 h^{-n-1/2}(1+u)^n
                   p\left(\frac{\omega u-b_\omega}{1+u}\right)
\]
has coefficient column $d$. Expanding each monomial of $p$
gives exactly RW3. Substitution back proves both inverse identities.

The determinant, with the original coefficient order, is
\[
 \det R_\omega^{(n)}=h^{(n+1)^2/2},\qquad
 R_\omega^{(3)}=\sqrt h
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \omega^3&b_\omega\omega^2&b_\omega^2\omega&b_\omega^3\\
 -3\omega^2&\omega^2-2b_\omega\omega&2b_\omega\omega-b_\omega^2&3b_\omega^2\\
 3\omega&b_\omega-2\omega&\omega-2b_\omega&3b_\omega\\
 -1&1&-1&1
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{RW4}
\]
For a direct determinant proof use $t=\omega-z$. The $j$th
polynomial is $\sqrt h(h-t)^jt^{n-j}$. Reversing the column
order makes its coefficient matrix triangular with diagonal
$\sqrt h h^j$. Reversal contributes
$(-1)^{n(n+1)/2}$, and changing $t$ back to $z$ contributes the
same sign, because the substitution matrix has diagonal $(-1)^\ell$.
Their product is positive and the remaining power is
$(n+1)/2+n(n+1)/2=(n+1)^2/2$. This proves RW4 without dropping
the square-root factor or changing a coefficient basis.

\section{Exact conductor return and the native Toeplitz matrix}
On $\mathcal P_n$ let
\[
 (C_\kappa)_{r\ell}=\binom\ell r\kappa^{\ell-r}\quad(r\le\ell),
 \qquad C_\kappa^{-1}=C_{-\kappa},\quad\det C_\kappa=1.
 \tag{RW5}
\]
This is precisely $p(S')\mapsto p(z+\kappa)$, by the binomial
formula; composition of translations proves the inverse.
For the four-dimensional receiver take $n=3$ and write $R=R_\omega^{(3)}$.
With the original monomial realization of $\Psi$, define
\[
 B_\omega=R^{-1}C_\kappa\Psi,\quad
 B_\omega^{-1}=\Psi^{-1}C_{-\kappa}R,\qquad
 B_{\omega,U}=R^{-1}C_\kappa T_A\Phi=B_\omega.
 \tag{RW6}
\]
Thus an original target state $p=\Psi q$ has the exact rational
image $p(z+\kappa)/(\omega-z)^4=\sum_j(B_\omega q)_j\varphi_j^\omega(z)$.
Equation RW6 is the original conductor commutation, including
all the moments in $\Psi$ and $T_A$; it is not an identification
of $P$ with $T_A$.

Let $\mathcal H$ be the original target monomial Gram in FC56a,
and $G=\Psi^*\mathcal H\Psi$. In rational coefficient coordinates
the original physical target norm has the exact positive Gram
\[
 H_\omega=B_\omega^{-*}GB_\omega^{-1}
       =R^*C_{-\kappa}^*\mathcal HC_{-\kappa}R.
 \tag{RW7}
\]
To evaluate it, put $S'=c_*+iy$ in the unchanged original
measure. Since $S'-\kappa=1/2+iy$, the actual numerator
polynomial is $N_j^{(3)}(1/2+iy)$. Its two factors are
$d+i(y-\tau)$ and $d-i(y-\tau)$, which are conjugate.
Consequently all sixteen entries are
\[
 (H_\omega)_{ij}=g_{j-i},\qquad
 g_r=h\int_{\R}(d+i(y-\tau))^{3+r}
                    (d-i(y-\tau))^{3-r}\,dm_s(y),
 \quad -3\le r\le3,\quad g_{-r}=\bar g_r.
 \tag{RW8}
\]
Thus the original metric itself is Toeplitz in this basis.
This is a proved identity, not a unitary-metric substitution.
The original center moved through the stated translation;
the measure, its total mass and every correlation remain.

Here is a complete finite evaluation of RW8. Write
$\mu_j(\tau)=\int(y-\tau)^jdm_s(y)$ and retain
\[
 \mu_j(\tau)=\sum_{\ell=0}^j\binom j\ell(-\tau)^{j-\ell}m_\ell,
 \quad m_0=M_s=(2\pi)^{s/2},\quad m_2=M_s\beta,
 \quad m_4=M_s(3\beta^2+2\beta),\quad
 m_6=M_s(15\beta^3+30\beta^2+16\beta),\quad\beta=s/2,
 \tag{RW9}
\]
where all odd $m_\ell$ vanish. These are exactly the proved
original moments of FC56a, with their cited DLMF provenance
\cite{WCF}. In terms of these full shifted moments,
\[
 g_r=h\sum_{p=0}^{3+r}\sum_{q=0}^{3-r}
 \binom{3+r}p\binom{3-r}q d^{6-p-q}i^p(-i)^q\mu_{p+q}(\tau).
 \tag{RW10}
\]
Every sum is finite and includes all odd shifted moments.
It is obtained by expanding the two factors in RW8, so it
specifies every entry without a quadrature or asymptotic error.
For example $g_0=h(d^6m_0+3d^4\mu_2+3d^2\mu_4+\mu_6)$.

The full determinant is also explicit:
\[
 \det H_\omega
 =h^{16}\prod_{j=0}^3\bigl[M_s j!(s/2)_j\bigr]
 =12h^{16}M_s^4\beta^3(\beta+1)^2(\beta+2)>0.
 \tag{RW11}
\]
Indeed RW7, RW4 and $\det C_{-\kappa}=1$ give
$\det H_\omega=h^{16}\det\mathcal H$; the exact monomial
Gram determinant is the product of the original monic
orthogonal squared norms in FC28--29. This proves independence
of $\tau$ for the determinant, not bounded conditioning as
$|\tau|$ grows. Each entry in RW8 still retains its full height
dependence.

\section{The same exact comparison for the full eight-state object}
Define the actual full-Weil form of the rational functions by
\[
 (T_3(\omega))_{ij}
 =\sum_{\rho}m_\rho\overline{\varphi_i^\omega(1-\bar\rho)}
                                     \varphi_j^\omega(\rho).
 \tag{RW12}
\]
Every sum includes the complete nontrivial zero multiset.
For fixed $\omega,i,j$ each summand is $O(|\rho|^{-2})$:
both pole factors have size comparable to $|\Im\rho|$, and
the finite powers of their ratio are bounded for large height.
For completeness the convergence needed here also follows
directly from the classical theta integral used in the residue
calculation below. Its Gaussian bound gives, for $|z|\le R$,
$|\xi(z)|\le\exp(CR\log(R+2))$ with a fixed constant $C$:
bound the power of $x\ge1$ by $x^{\lceil(R+1)/2\rceil}$,
integrate against $e^{-\pi x}$ and use $k!\le k^k$.
Jensen's formula centered at $\xi(0)=1/2$, on a circle between
radii $2R$ and $3R$ avoiding zeros, then gives
$N(R)\log2\le C'(R+1)\log(R+2)$ for the zero count with
multiplicity in $|z|\le R$. Splitting into annuli
$2^j\le|\rho|<2^{j+1}$ proves
$\sum_\rho m_\rho|\rho|^{-2}<\infty$, because
$\sum_j(j+1)2^{-j}<\infty$. This is the same absolute
convergence underlying the classical canonical product
\cite[25.2.12]{DLMFXi}. Reflection of the zero multiset
proves it Hermitian by changing $\rho$ to $1-\bar\rho$.
The exact identity
$\overline{U_\omega(1-\bar\rho)}=U_\omega(\rho)^{-1}$,
where $U_\omega(z)=(z+b_\omega)/(\omega-z)$, also proves
\[
 (T_3(\omega))_{ij}=c_{j-i},\qquad
 c_r=h\sum_\rho m_\rho
       \frac{(\rho+b_\omega)^{r-1}}{(\omega-\rho)^{r+1}},
 \quad c_{-r}=\bar c_r.
 \tag{RW13}
\]
Neither denominator vanishes in the open critical strip, since
$\sigma>1$. This verifies the exact form needed for the
receiving calculation; finite arithmetic evaluation and tail
bounds of $c_r$ belong to the separately retained full-Weil
calculation, not to a claim that these sums have been numerically
evaluated here.

Retain both original label and evaluation coordinates of SE/TC.
Let $E,O$ be their full mean and difference matrices, and let
$G_Y=Y^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_{\Gamma,W}Y>0$.
The complete original target direct-sum Gram and Weil form are
\[
 \mathbb H=
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\E&O\end{pmatrix}^{\!*}
 \begin{pmatrix}G_Y&0\\0&G\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\E&O\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \mathbb W=[E\ O]^*B_\omega^*T_3(\omega)B_\omega[E\ O].
 \tag{RW14}
\]
The original $O$ is invertible by SE5 on its stated quartet
domain. The exact simultaneous change and inverse are
\[
 S_\omega=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-O^{-1}E&O^{-1}B_\omega^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 S_\omega^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\B_\omega E&B_\omega O\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{RW15}
\]
Multiplying either way proves the inverse; multiplying by the
two actual receiving matrices proves simultaneously
\[
 S_\omega^*\mathbb W S_\omega=\diag(0_4,T_3(\omega)),\qquad
 S_\omega^*\mathbb H S_\omega=\diag(G_Y,H_\omega).
 \tag{RW16}
\]
Thus no positive physical norm has been discarded to obtain
the Weil congruence. In the full original native metric the
generalized eigenvalues are four zeros together with those
of $H_\omega^{-1/2}T_3(\omega)H_\omega^{-1/2}$.
To prove this, apply the positive square root of each diagonal
block in RW16 to the equation $\mathbb Wv=\lambda\mathbb Hv$.
Both transformations are invertible; the resulting equation is
the direct sum of a zero Hermitian matrix and the displayed
Hermitian matrix. If $T_3$ itself has a kernel, the total
nullity is $4+\dim\ker T_3$, and the complete original kernel is
\[
 \left\{\left(\alpha,-O^{-1}E\alpha+
          O^{-1}B_\omega^{-1}d\right):\ d\in\ker T_3\right\}.
 \tag{RW17}
\]
In particular the four-dimensional evaluation graph is retained
exactly, without claiming that it exhausts every possible Weil
kernel. Positive and negative inertia are those of the actual
four-dimensional $T_3$. This statement is an exact consequence
of two constructed forms, not a supposition about their signs.

For every $d$ the canonical lift with zero retained-label
coordinate is the explicit original state
$(0,O^{-1}B_\omega^{-1}d)$. Its physical target polynomial is
$C_{-\kappa}Rd$, and its original conductor source is
\[
 T_A^{-1}C_{-\kappa}Rd,
 \tag{RW18}
\]
where the full original inverse FC24 supplies every coefficient
and its source norm. Hence any evaluated test vector is carried
back through the actual conductor, rather than only through a
formal Hermitian congruence. For degrees $n>3$, RW2--5 use the
actual $\mathcal P_n$ conductor restriction in FC21 and FC24;
the original eight-state space itself stays eight-dimensional.

The actual original source direct-sum norm is retained as well.
Let $G_X=X^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_{\Gamma,U}X$ and
$G_U=\Phi^*\langle\ ,\ \rangle_{\Gamma,U}\Phi$, where the
bracket is the original quotient norm. Its full Gram and
transported rational coefficient Gram are
\[
 \mathbb H_U=
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\E&O\end{pmatrix}^{\!*}
 \begin{pmatrix}G_X&0\\0&G_U\end{pmatrix}
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\E&O\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 H_{U,\omega}=B_\omega^{-*}G_UB_\omega^{-1}.
 \tag{RW19}
\]
The source state represented by $(\alpha,\beta)$ is
$(X\alpha,\Phi(E\alpha+O\beta))$. Applying $T_A$ on
each component gives exactly the target state
$(Y\alpha,\Psi(E\alpha+O\beta))$, because
$T_AX=Y$ and $T_A\Phi=\Psi$.
Consequently the same explicit matrix RW15 proves
\[
 S_\omega^*\mathbb H_U S_\omega=\diag(G_X,H_{U,\omega}),
 \qquad
 S_\omega^*\mathbb W S_\omega=\diag(0_4,T_3).
 \tag{RW20}
\]
No mixed source--target direct-sum metric is being substituted.
The actual source generalized eigenvalues are four zeros and
those of $H_{U,\omega}^{-1/2}T_3H_{U,\omega}^{-1/2}$, by
the same invertible square-root argument used for RW16.
Its kernel is still exactly RW17. The complete entries and
all source height constants of $H_{U,\omega}$ are evaluated
in NH26 onward using the original FC24 inverse and quotient
Gram. Thus the two original sides of the conductor have
separate fully specified metrics and the same exact Weil receiver.
