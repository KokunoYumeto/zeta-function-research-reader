# SZ-20260921-007: the original conductor detects growing inverse-power spaces

For the original conductor with J nonzero coefficients and moment order v, its unchanged lower-root value map is injective on span([y^-1],...,[y^-s]) whenever Js-v <= (k-7)^2. Thus every nonzero class in this space survives the complete original observation. This strengthens the single late-heat-class detection in LS13-17.

The proof evaluates the numerator degree as Js-v-n0, where n0 is the first nonzero inverse-power coefficient. It reconstructs every coefficient by interpolation at the original lower roots and principal-part extraction at an original conductor pole. All powers of i and all root differences remain.

IK9-10 give the explicit left inverse; IK11 bounds it. IK12-13 carry that bound through the actual attained observation metric, yielding an observation-energy fraction at least exp[-O(q log q)] uniformly over the allowed growing space and all four cutoffs. This finite lower bound may tend to zero; no limiting fraction, kq allocation coefficient or individual current sign is assigned.

Thirty-one exact checks include incorrect-phase and insufficient-row negative controls. The interpolation source is Nico M.
