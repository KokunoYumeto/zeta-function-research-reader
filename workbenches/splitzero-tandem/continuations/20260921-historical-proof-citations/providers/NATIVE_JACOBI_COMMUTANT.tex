\section{The exact arithmetic operator and its first column}
Retain the real monic Gamma polynomials $p_n$ and their full norms:
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}p_n(y)\frac{t^n}{n!}
 =(1+t^2)^{-1/4}e^{y\arctan t},\quad
 \gamma_n=M_\sigma n!(1/2)_n,\quad
 \varphi_n=p_n/\sqrt{\gamma_n},\quad
 r_n=\sqrt{M_\sigma}\varphi_n.
\]
Thus $\varphi_0=M_\sigma^{-1/2}$ and $r_0=1$; the functions
$r_n$ are not declared orthonormal in the original measure.
The complete beta-integral/generating-function proof of these norms
is included in the companion original-Gamma proof. They are also
the Meixner--Pollaczek family of the cited paper, with
$r_n(y)=\sqrt{n!/(1/2)_n}P_n^{(1/4)}(y/2;\pi/2)$.
This is an explicit polynomial identity, retaining the factor $y/2$.

Define the original density ratio
\[
 R_k(y)=m_k(y)/\rho_\sigma(y).
\]
It is the literal ratio, positive and bounded above by $A_k$ by
\eqref{eq:envelope}. Multiplication by $R_k$ is consequently a bounded
positive self-adjoint operator on $L^2(\sigma)$. Let
\[
 U_\sigma:\ell^2(\N_0)\longrightarrow L^2(\sigma),\quad
 U_\sigma e_n=\varphi_n,
 \qquad \mathcal C=U_\sigma^*M_{R_k}U_\sigma.
\]
The polynomials form a complete orthonormal basis here. For an explicit
density argument, if $f\in L^2(\sigma)$ is orthogonal to every
polynomial, Cauchy--Schwarz and the exponential moments of $\sigma$
show that $\int f(y)e^{zy}d\sigma(y)$ is holomorphic in a strip
about the imaginary axis. Every derivative at zero vanishes, so the
transform vanishes in that strip by analytic uniqueness. On the
imaginary axis it is the Fourier transform of the finite measure
$f\,d\sigma$, hence that measure and $f$ vanish. This proves
surjectivity of $U_\sigma$ without changing $d\sigma$.

The entries and bounds of the actual operator are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:C}
 \mathcal C_{ij}=\int_\R\varphi_i(y)\varphi_j(y)m_k(y)dy,
 \qquad 0\le\mathcal C\le A_k I,
 \qquad \mathcal C_{00}=\mu_h^k/M_\sigma.
\end{equation}
Define the real symmetric Jacobi matrix $\mathcal J$ by
\[
 \mathcal J_{n,n}=0,\quad
 \mathcal J_{n,n+1}=\mathcal J_{n+1,n}=b_n,
 \qquad b_n=\sqrt{(n+1)(n+1/2)}.
\]
Its polynomial recurrence is exactly multiplication by $y$ in the
$\varphi_n$ basis. Applying that recurrence under the integral in
\eqref{eq:C} proves entrywise $\mathcal J\mathcal C=\mathcal C\mathcal J$.
Every product here uses only finitely many adjacent entries. No
interchange of an uncontrolled infinite series is needed.

Put $\alpha_j=\mathcal C_{j0}$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:first}
 \boxed{\mathcal C_{mn}=
 \sum_{j=|m-n|}^{m+n}\alpha_j\,[r_j(\mathcal J)]_{mn}.}
\end{equation}
To prove the formula fully, the recurrence and $r_0=1$ give
$r_n(\mathcal J)e_0=e_n$ by induction. The polynomial $r_mr_n$
expands in the $r_j$ basis, with coefficient
\[
 d_{mnj}=\frac1{M_\sigma}\int r_m r_n r_j\,d\sigma
 =[r_j(\mathcal J)]_{mn}.
\]
Orthogonality makes this coefficient zero for $j>m+n$, $j+m<n$
or $j+n<m$. Finally
\[
 \mathcal C_{mn}=\frac1{M_\sigma}\int r_mr_n m_kdy
 =\sum_j d_{mnj}\frac1{M_\sigma}\int r_jm_kdy
 =\sum_j d_{mnj}\alpha_j,
\]
which proves \eqref{eq:first}. This is the commutant formula of
\v{S}tampach--\v{S}\v{t}ov\'{i}\v{c}ek with every actual mass
factor visible. Since $m_k$ is even, $\alpha_j=0$ for odd $j$,
and $\mathcal C_{mn}=0$ for odd $m+n$.

The first column is itself generated by the original one-factor
arithmetic input
\[
 M_h(z)=\int_\R e^{zy}w_h(y)dy.
\]
For $t$ in a sufficiently small complex neighbourhood of zero,
the exponential tail in CAI17 and Fubini give
\[
 \int e^{y\arctan t}m_k(y)dy=M_h(\arctan t)^k.
\]
Differentiation of the Gamma generating function under this integral
therefore gives the exact formula
\begin{equation}\label{eq:alpha}
 \boxed{\alpha_j=
 \frac{j!}{M_\sigma\sqrt{j!(1/2)_j}}
 [t^j]\bigl((1+t^2)^{-1/4}M_h(\arctan t)^k\bigr).}
\end{equation}
In particular $\alpha_0=\mu_h^k/M_\sigma$, as in \eqref{eq:C}.
This formula keeps the actual $k$th convolution, rather than
replacing it by a Gamma convolution or a finite Euler product.

\section{Finite boundary defect and exact original quotient}
Let $P_N$ be projection to $e_0,\ldots,e_N$ and put
$C_N=P_N\mathcal C P_N$, $J_N=P_N\mathcal J P_N$, regarded as
$(N+1)$-square matrices. Write
$c_N=(\mathcal C_{0,N+1},\ldots,\mathcal C_{N,N+1})^T$.
Then the finite identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:boundary}
 \boxed{J_NC_N-C_NJ_N=b_N(c_Ne_N^*-e_Nc_N^*).}
\end{equation}
Indeed insert $I=P_N+(I-P_N)$ between the two factors of the full
commutation identity. The only Jacobi entry crossing the cutoff is
$b_N$, so the two missing products are respectively
$b_Ne_Nc_N^*$ and $b_Nc_Ne_N^*$. Rearrangement gives
\eqref{eq:boundary}, including its sign. Each right-hand term has
rank at most one. Formula \eqref{eq:first} computes $C_N$ from
$\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_{2N}$, and computes $c_N$ from
$\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_{2N+1}$.
Here $r_j(\mathcal J)$ denotes the polynomial in the full Jacobi
matrix, then compressed. It is not silently replaced by $r_j(J_N)$,
which would discard paths leaving and returning to the cutoff.

For an original polynomial $F(S)=\sum_{j=0}^N a_jS^j$ use
\[
 T_{rj}=\binom jr c^{j-r}i^r\ (r\le j),\qquad T_{rj}=0\ (r>j).
\]
It sends $a$ to the real-variable monomial coefficients of the same
function $F(c+iy)$. Let $B$ have in column $n$ the monomial
coefficients of $\varphi_n$. Its diagonal entries are
$1/\sqrt{\gamma_n}$, so it is invertible. Thus the exact coefficient
map from $S$-powers to the orthonormal Gamma frame is
\[
 D_N=B^{-1}T,\qquad F(c+iy)=\sum_{n=0}^N(D_Na)_n\varphi_n(y).
\]
If $H_{S,N}^{\rm ar}$ is the full original arithmetic source Gram,
then integration proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sourcegram}
 H_{S,N}^{\rm ar}=D_N^*C_ND_N.
\end{equation}
No scalar mass is cancelled: it occurs in both $B$ and $C_N$ with
the exact powers fixed above.

Let $\mathcal A_S=\Lambda\mathcal J_{S,N}$ be the actual polynomial
to observation map, where $\mathcal J_{S,N}$ is the original class
map, with its full physical Taylor unit in the selected jet frame.
This notation is distinct from the real Jacobi matrix $J_N$.
Use the original observation image as codomain, so the map is onto.
Set $\mathcal E_N=\mathcal A_SD_N^{-1}$. The actual observation
metric and its attained lift are exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quotient}
 \boxed{Q_{B,N}=(\mathcal E_N C_N^{-1}\mathcal E_N^*)^{-1},
 \qquad a_{\min}=D_N^{-1}C_N^{-1}\mathcal E_N^*Q_{B,N}u.}
\end{equation}
Positive density makes $C_N$ positive definite. Substituting the lift
gives $\mathcal A_Sa_{\min}=u$. Any other lift differs by a vector
whose Gamma-frame coefficients are in $\ker\mathcal E_N$; its
cross pairing with $C_N^{-1}\mathcal E_N^*Q_{B,N}u$ is zero.
Its energy is therefore the displayed minimum plus the positive
energy of the difference. This proves \eqref{eq:quotient} as the
same original minimum, not an auxiliary metric.

For the unchanged boundary classes $a_1=[p_N^{\rm ar}]_\chi$,
$a_2=[p_{N+1}^{\rm ar}]_\chi$ and terminal class $v$, the actual

\[
 B_i=(\Lambda a_i)^*(\mathcal E_N C_N^{-1}\mathcal E_N^*)^{-1}
              \Lambda v,\qquad
 \Phi_{B,N}=\frac{2\Re(\overline{B_1}B_2)}{\omega_{N,k}^{\rm ar}}.
\]
Equations \eqref{eq:first}, \eqref{eq:alpha}, \eqref{eq:boundary}
and \eqref{eq:quotient} give a fully specified finite arithmetic
evaluation route and its cutoff correction. Real parity of $C_N$

No asymptotic estimate or RH conclusion follows merely from this
finite representation. In particular the positive norm calculation
