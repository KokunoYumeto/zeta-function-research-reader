# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited proof container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). Its exact bytes are identified by the offsets, lengths and hashes in `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` for `RETAINED_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCES.tex`.

Its GNU Free Documentation License applies separately from the surrounding files. The unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) accompanies it, with the [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING). No additional restriction is imposed on that source. The complete Stacks chapter requires its upstream dependencies to build. Retention is not a claim of exhaustive reading. Earlier uses of [cochain homotopies](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3057) and [quasi-isomorphisms](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3160) retain their precise citations.

The positive integer condensation calculation cites **Christian Krattenthaler**, [Advanced Determinant Calculus, arXiv:math/9902004v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9902004v3), Section 2.3 (`sec:cond`, `prop:cond`, `eq:cond`). The source-reading ledger records the exact original-author TeX and corrected arXiv identity. The programme's complete finite derivation is included; the full original paper is not redistributed in this edition.

The retained original NIST DLMF equation sources are inside `RETAINED_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCES.tex`, including `sealed017/HUMAN_EQUATION_SOURCES.tex`, recoverable by its recorded offsets and hash. Meixner–Pollaczek references retain **T. H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek and R. F. Swarttouw**, with **W. P. Reinhardt**, and formula locators 18.22.8 and 18.23.7. Gamma bounds retain **R. A. Askey and R. Roy**. Earlier source records and human credits remain unchanged, including the finite Schur derivations' citations to **Geneviève Dusson, Israel Michael Sigal and Benjamin Stamm**, [arXiv:2105.02058v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02058v1). Their full paper is not redistributed.

All 19 owner-sealed files remain unchanged. This notice and the licence copy are additive publication companions. A programme derivation is not represented as an original theorem of its cited human sources.
