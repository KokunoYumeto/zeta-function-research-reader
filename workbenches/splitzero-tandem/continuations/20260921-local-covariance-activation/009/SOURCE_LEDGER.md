# Exact proof sources and reading coverage

The new proofs are complete in the main LaTeX and the three individual source files. This edition uses earlier sources at their recorded proof status, including the EIQ equilibrium and the original full arithmetic-to-Gamma form estimate. It does not claim a new independent proof of RH.

- DCR1-27: complete new root derivation. Independent DCV1-16 and FQR1-17 derivations checked coordinate transport, every quotient fibre, source degrees, finite complementary costs and signs; their private working notes remain preserved.
- CGR1-29 and CGS1-22: complete independent derivations, fully read by the root. Their complete proofs are included.

Included as ACTUAL_KERNEL_FILTRATION_AND_GRAPH.tex; complete reading recorded.

Classical human sources are preserved at use: J.L.W.V.Jensen, *Sur un nouvel et important théorème de la théorie des fonctions* (1899), [doi:10.1007/BF02417878](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02417878); R.A.Askey and R.Roy, [DLMF Chapter5](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5); T.H.Koornwinder, R.Wong, R.Koekoek, R.F.Swarttouw and W.P.Reinhardt, [DLMF18.22.8](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.22.E8) and [18.23.7](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.23.E7). Original DLMF TeX encodings read for the source bounds are retained in the programme research corpus. The finite Jensen, polynomial interpolation, minimum and determinant arguments needed here are reproduced in full in their included proofs.

Every new source is a programme derivation. It is not presented as an original human-authored paper or a transcription of one.
