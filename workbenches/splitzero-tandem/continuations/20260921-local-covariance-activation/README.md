# Local branches, conductor covariance and source activation

Three separate Split-Zero papers extend the study of finite spectral classes through the original theta source, conductor maps and arithmetic quotient metric. They belong to the [complete zeta-function research programme](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader); this folder describes these additions, not a replacement project.

- [008: Two local branches and the exact conductor quotient](008/REAL_PAIR_LOCAL_OBJECT.pdf), 18 pages. The two vanishing spectral branches are coupled in a finite module. The paper constructs that module and its exact maps, then calculates the moving quotient and both disappearing projection angles in the original Gamma metric. It keeps the distinction between the specified geometric family and arithmetic zeta zeros. [LaTeX](008/REAL_PAIR_LOCAL_OBJECT.tex), [complete cumulative source](008/FABLE_TO_ORIGINAL_CONDUCTOR.tex), [human and programme sources](008/SOURCE_USE_LEDGER.json).
- [009: The actual conductor covariance](009/COMPLETE_CONDUCTOR_COVARIANCE_READER.pdf), 22 pages. The paper calculates the conductor-divisor contribution to the original arithmetic-kernel determinant. It retains the full invariant image, all root multiplicities and the exact four-projection-angle remainder. [LaTeX](009/COMPLETE_CONDUCTOR_COVARIANCE_READER.tex), [complete preceding proofs](009/PREVIOUS_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCE.tex), [source ledger](009/SOURCE_LEDGER.md).
- [010: Original source activation and native currents](010/COMPLETE_ACTIVATION_AND_NATIVE_READER.pdf), 40 pages. The paper computes the leading activation law for the added tensor source, bounds the smaller original-kernel loss uniformly, and gives exact one- and two-column projected-current formulas. It reduces the four-angle remainder to two cutoffs and gives a lower-root Cauchy calculation. The smaller kernel coefficient and individual native current signs remain unevaluated. [LaTeX](010/COMPLETE_ACTIVATION_AND_NATIVE_READER.tex), [results and uses](010/RESULTS_20260921_010.md), [human-source and verification ledger](010/VALIDATION_AND_SOURCE_LEDGER.json).

[The machine-readable result index](RESULT_INDEX.json) states what each calculation is for, what it achieves and what remains. [Exact proof links](PROOF_LINKS.json) point to source lines. [Public antecedents](PUBLIC_ANTECEDENTS.json) record full-byte verification of the imported programme links.
