\[

\]

\subsection{8. The exact kernel determinant on every observation-rank stratum}\label{the-exact-kernel-determinant-on-every-observation-rank-stratum}

\tag{PC32}

\tag{PC33}
\] The rank is bounded by the two dimensions of C. For r=0 the exact loss is zero. When Z=0 this is the earlier RF66 angle identity.
