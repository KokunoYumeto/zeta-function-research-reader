# Polynomial sectors at arithmetic collisions and the exact observation complex

This derivation receives equations (55)–(61) and (79)–(81) of the supplied moving-family continuation. It proves the extension at every primary multiplicity and repairs the section used in its chain homotopy. The earlier quotient on the resolvent locus is [RF65–67, sealed 016](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/d44d94aa43d0a1934455ddac008368583f43a2c1/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260921-canonical-memory-filters/RATIONAL_FILTER_PROOFS.md). The proofs below are finite algebra and metric calculations, written in full. They introduce no new analytic estimate at a pole of the arithmetic resolvent.

## 1. The complete polynomial frame

For an integer t≥0, write \(\mathcal P_{<t}\) for complex polynomials of degree below t, including the zero space when t=0. Let Q be any monic polynomial of degree q, retaining all root multiplicities. Identify \(E=\mathbb C[y]/(Q)\) with its unique representatives in \(\mathcal P_{<q}\). Let M denote multiplication by y followed by remainder modulo Q. For a monic D of degree \(1\le d\le q\), define
\[
\pi_D x=\operatorname{quo}_D\operatorname{rem}_Q(Dx),\qquad
\iota:\mathcal P_{<q-d}\hookrightarrow E.
\tag{PC1}
\]
Here quo is the entire quotient in monic Euclidean division; no term is discarded before taking the indicated remainder. Both maps use the original coefficient bases. Since deg(Dx)<q for x in the low polynomial space, \(\pi_D\iota=I\). Consequently
\[
E=V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}\oplus\iota\mathcal P_{<q-d},\qquad
V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}=\ker\pi_D,
\quad\dim V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}=d.
\tag{PC2}
\]
Define \(F_D:\mathbb C^d\to E\) by
\[
F_De_j=y^{q-d+j}-\iota\pi_D(y^{q-d+j}),\quad 0\le j<d.
\tag{PC3}
\]
Its last d coefficient rows form the identity matrix, so it is injective and its image is precisely PC2's kernel. Monic division consists only of coefficient additions and multiplications. Thus the entries of F_D are polynomial in the coefficients of D and Q, with no inverse resultant. This proves an everywhere defined frame, even on the discriminant of Q.

Let \(\jmath:\mathcal P_{<d}\hookrightarrow E\) denote the numerator inclusion, and let the d-by-d coefficient matrix B_D have columns
\[
B_De_j=\operatorname{rem}_D\operatorname{rem}_Q(Dy^{q-d+j}).
\]
Subtracting D times the quotient in PC3 proves the typed identity
\[
D(M)F_D=\jmath B_D.
\tag{PC4}
\]
The codomain basis \([\jmath,D\iota]\) is triangular with determinant one. The domain basis \([F_D,\iota]\) has determinant \((-1)^{d(q-d)}\), because its high block is the identity and the displayed order moves d columns across q−d columns. In these bases D(M) is diag(B_D,I). Therefore
\[
\det B_D=(-1)^{d(q-d)}\det D(M)
=(-1)^{d(q-d)}\operatorname{Res}(Q,D).
\tag{PC5}
\]
For completeness, the last equality has the convention \(\operatorname{Res}(Q,D)=\prod_{Q(\rho)=0}D(\rho)\), roots counted with their full multiplicities. On each primary block \(\mathbb C[y]/((y-\rho)^m)\), the basis \(1,y-\rho,\ldots,(y-\rho)^{m-1}\) makes D(M) triangular with diagonal D(ρ). The Chinese remainder isomorphism follows from Bezout for the pairwise coprime primary factors. Multiplying these determinants gives exactly PC5; repeated roots require no limiting argument.

If the resultant is nonzero, \(U_D=D(M)^{-1}\jmath\) is the original rational numerator map, and PC4 yields
\[
U_D=F_DB_D^{-1},\qquad \operatorname{im}U_D=V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}.
\tag{PC6}
\]

## 2. The full primary defect and an explicit lift

Let \(g=\gcd(Q,D)\) be monic, \(e=\deg g\), \(Q=gQ_1\), and \(D=gD_1\). The coprime polynomials Q_1,D_1 have Bezout coefficients; choose \(T\) with \(TD_1\equiv1\pmod{Q_1}\) if Q_1 has positive degree. Then
\[
0\longrightarrow (Q/g)\mathcal P_{<e}
\longrightarrow V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}
\xrightarrow{D(M)}\jmath\bigl(g\mathcal P_{<d-e}\bigr)
\longrightarrow0
\tag{PC7}
\]
is exact. Indeed, \(Q\mid Dx\) is equivalent to \(Q_1\mid x\), giving the left term, with exactly e independent columns. The image ideal of multiplication by D in E is the ideal generated by g: Bezout gives inclusion in both directions. A representative of degree below d belongs to this ideal exactly when it is divisible by g, because g divides Q. This proves the image in PC7. More explicitly, the lift of gp, deg p<d−e, is
\[
x=\operatorname{rem}_{Q_1}(Tp),\qquad
\operatorname{rem}_Q(Dx)=gp.
\tag{PC8}
\]
It belongs to V_D by PC1. If Q_1 is constant then necessarily e=q=d and the target is zero, so no T is needed. PC7 proves \(\operatorname{rank}B_D=d-e\), and identifies the entire missing multiplication image.

For example, if \(D=(y-\rho)^d\) and \((y-\rho)^m\) is the full primary factor of Q, then e=min(d,m). For d≤m, the entire continued sector is
\[
V_D^{\mathrm{flat}}=(Q/D)\mathcal P_{<d}=\ker D(M).
\tag{PC9}
\]
For d>m, PC7 retains both the m-dimensional primary kernel and the nonzero image \((y-\rho)^m\mathcal P_{<d-m}\). In the single moving-pole family \(D_z=(y-z)^d\), PC5 gives
\[
\det B_{D_z}=(-1)^d Q(z)^d.
\tag{PC10}
\]
Thus at a root ρ of multiplicity m the determinant has order md although its rank defect is min(d,m). These are two separately evaluated features of the same exact map.

## 3. Original metrics and the resultant factor

Fix an original cutoff and activation. Let G be the full attained positive metric on E. Let \(\Lambda:E\to B\) be the original surjection, with full attained target metric
\[
Q_B=(\Lambda G^{-1}\Lambda^*)^{-1},\qquad
L=G^{-1}\Lambda^*Q_B,\quad \Lambda L=I.
\tag{PC11}
\]
All adjoints marked * are coefficient conjugate transposes. The associated Hilbert-space adjoints use these stated metrics. In particular Λ is a coisometry and its norm is one. Define
\[
H=F_D^*GF_D>0,\qquad A=\Lambda F_D,\qquad J=A^*Q_BA\succeq0.
\tag{PC12}
\]
On the resolvent locus PC6 gives, exactly,
\[
H_D^E=B_D^{-*}HB_D^{-1},\qquad
H_D^B=B_D^{-*}JB_D^{-1}.
\tag{PC13}
\]
If A has full column rank, taking determinants gives
\[
\log\det H_D^E=\log\det H-2\log|\operatorname{Res}(Q,D)|,
\quad
\log\det H_D^B=\log\det J-2\log|\operatorname{Res}(Q,D)|.
\tag{PC14}
\]
The full source/observation ratio is det J/det H. It extends continuously across the resultant divisor wherever G and Q_B remain positive and A retains rank d. The positive matrix \(H^{-1/2}JH^{-1/2}\) has the same spectrum as the original relative Gram matrix by PC13: the coefficient map B_D^{-1} is an isometry between the respective pulled-back source forms. These spectra are the exact observed fractions. No bound uniform across a zero observed fraction follows just from PC14.

For fixed D and Q at the four original cutoffs, the resultant term is the same and its signed return is zero. If D itself differs between cutoffs, its four resultant terms must instead be retained. If A drops rank, det J is zero and a finite logarithmic ratio cannot be asserted. The maps and the retained intersection below still exist.

## 4. Corrected chain section in the observable case

Suppose first that \(\operatorname{rank}A=d\), and put V=im F_D and W=ΛV. Define
\[
h=F_DJ^{-1}A^*Q_B:B\to E.
\tag{PC15}
\]
Then \(h\Lambda F_D=F_D\), so hΛ is a projection onto V. Also Λh is the Q_B-orthogonal projection onto W: it is idempotent, has image W, and is selfadjoint in Q_B directly from its formula. Let \(\rho:B\to B/W\) be the quotient map in any fixed coordinates. Equip both quotients with their full minimum metrics and define their unique minimum sections
\[
L_\pi=G^{-1}\pi_D^*(\pi_DG^{-1}\pi_D^*)^{-1},\qquad
L_\rho=Q_B^{-1}\rho^*(\rho Q_B^{-1}\rho^*)^{-1}.
\tag{PC16}
\]
Empty quotients use their unique zero maps. Set
\[
\bar\Lambda\pi_D=\rho\Lambda,\qquad
j_E=(I-h\Lambda)L_\pi,\qquad j_B=L_\rho.
\tag{PC17}
\]
The induced map \(\bar\Lambda\) is well defined since ρΛ annihilates V. We have \(\pi_Dj_E=I\), \(\rho j_B=I\), and
\[
\Lambda j_E=(I-\Lambda h)\Lambda L_\pi
=L_\rho\rho\Lambda L_\pi=j_B\bar\Lambda.
\tag{PC18}
\]
For each x, \(x-L_\pi\pi_Dx\) belongs to V and is fixed by hΛ. Hence
\[
j_E\pi_D=I-h\Lambda,\qquad j_B\rho=I-\Lambda h.
\tag{PC19}
\]
Writing the two-term differential as d=Λ from degree zero to degree one, these are precisely \(I-jp=dh+hd\). Further \(ph=0\), \(hj=0\), and h²=0 by its degree. This is a strong deformation retract of the original complex onto its quotient by the acyclic subcomplex \([V\to W]\).

The uncorrected minimum section L_π alone generally fails PC18–19. Its projection is the G-orthogonal projection onto V-perpendicular, whereas hΛ need not be orthogonal. PC17 is the required exact adjustment, and is also valid at an arithmetic collision with full observation rank.

Let \(Q_\pi=(\pi_DG^{-1}\pi_D^*)^{-1}\). Because L_π has image orthogonal to V and hΛL_π has image in V, the adjusted source metric is
\[
j_E^*Gj_E=Q_\pi+(h\Lambda L_\pi)^*G(h\Lambda L_\pi).
\tag{PC20}
\]
In the original quotient metric its determinant change lies between zero and
\[
\min(d,q-d)\log(1+\|h\|^2).
\tag{PC21}
\]
To prove this, factor PC20 by the stated Q_π isometry. The correction is R*R, rank at most min(d,q−d) and norm at most ||h||² since L_π is an isometry and Λ a coisometry. The logarithm is the sum of log(1+s_j(R)²). If the moving-family comparison gives \(J\succeq e^{-2E_{k,d,N}}H\), then the singular values of \(\Lambda|_V\) are at least e^{-E}; h is their inverse on W and zero on its orthogonal complement. Thus
\[
\|h\|\le e^{E_{k,d,N}},\qquad
0\le\log\frac{\det(j_E^*Gj_E)}{\det Q_\pi}
\le\min(d,q-d)\log(1+e^{2E_{k,d,N}}).
\tag{PC22}
\]
In particular, for d=O(k), q of the retained quadratic order, and \(E=O(k\log^2(q+2)+(k+Jd)\log(q+Jd+2))\) with fixed conductor size J, summing all four errors is O(k² log²q)=o(kq). This proves that repairing the chain section preserves the leading programme allocation in that range; it does not assign the initial allocation a value.

## 5. Exact reduction when observation rank is lost

There is still a complete reduction when \(Z=V\cap K\ne0\), K=kerΛ. Split V=Z⊕V_0 orthogonally in G and write W=ΛV. The restriction \(\Lambda|_{V_0}:V_0\to W\) is bijective. Let h be its inverse after the Q_B-orthogonal projection onto W. Equivalently, put
\[
T=Q_B^{1/2}AH^{-1/2},\qquad
h=F_DH^{-1/2}T^+Q_B^{1/2},
\tag{PC23}
\]
where \(T^+\) is the Moore–Penrose inverse: in an orthonormal singular expansion \(T=\sum_{j=1}^r\sigma_j u_jv_j^*\), it is \(\sum_{j=1}^r\sigma_j^{-1}v_ju_j^*\). This formula proves, without suppressing a zero singular value, that hΛ is the identity on V_0 and zero on Z, and Λh is the orthogonal projection onto W. If r=0 take h=0.

Keep L_π,L_ρ as in PC16 and define
\[
j_0=(I-h\Lambda)L_\pi,\quad
z(x)=(I-h\Lambda)(I-L_\pi\pi_D)x\in Z.
\tag{PC24}
\]
The reduced complex, projection, and section are
\[
[Z\oplus\mathcal P_{<q-d}\xrightarrow{(z,c)\mapsto\bar\Lambda c} B/W],
\]
\[
p_E x=(z(x),\pi_Dx),\quad p_B=\rho,
\quad j_E(z,c)=z+j_0c,\quad j_B=L_\rho.
\tag{PC25}
\]
To check z(x) belongs to Z, the inner factor lies in V, and I−hΛ is its projection onto Z along V_0. Next π_Dj_0=I, z(j_0c)=0 because \((I-h\Lambda)h=0\), and z(z)=z. Thus pj=I. A direct subtraction gives
\[
j_Ep_E=I-h\Lambda,\qquad j_Bp_B=I-\Lambda h.
\tag{PC26}
\]
The chain equality follows as in PC18, since Λz=0. This proves the deformation retract with the newly retained Z in degree zero. It also proves the exact native-kernel sequence
\[
0\to Z\to K\xrightarrow{\pi_D}\ker\bar\Lambda\to0.
\tag{PC27}
\]
For surjectivity, choose x over c in ker barΛ. Then Λx belongs to W, so choose v in V with Λv=Λx; x−v belongs to K and projects to c. The kernel is exactly K∩V.

The metric on PC25's source is the orthogonal direct sum of the inherited metric on Z and
\[
Q_\pi+(h\Lambda L_\pi)^*G(h\Lambda L_\pi).
\tag{PC28}
\]
Indeed, Z is orthogonal to L_π and to V_0, hence to j_0. The same determinant bound PC21 holds with min(r,q−d), and \(\|h\|=1/\sigma_r(T)\) when r>0. There is no finite bound uniform through a changing positive rank unless this last positive singular value is bounded away from zero. The exact formulas still retain every dimension and metric on each rank stratum.

## 6. Two explicit observation behaviours at the same arithmetic collision

Take the auxiliary finite algebra \(Q=y^2+1\), \(D=y-z\) and coefficient metric G=I. Direct division gives
\[
F_D=(z,1)^{\mathsf T},\quad B_D=-(1+z^2),\quad
\pi_D(a,b)=a-zb.
\tag{PC29}
\]
For \(\Lambda(a,b)=b\), Q_B=1, the observed fraction is \((1+|z|^2)^{-1}\) and h(t)=(zt,t). It remains defined at z=i, where F_D is a nonzero i-eigenvector of M. The rational vector has a pole but both the continued sector and its observed fraction remain finite and nonzero.

For the different observation \(\Lambda(a,b)=a-ib\), Q_B=1/2 and
\[
\frac{J}{H}=\frac{|z-i|^2}{2(1+|z|^2)},\qquad
h(t)=\frac{(z,1)^{\mathsf T}t}{z-i}\quad(z\ne i).
\tag{PC30}
\]
At z=i, Z=V=K has dimension one, W=0, and h=0. Since π_D=Λ there, barΛ is the identity on the one-dimensional quotient. PC25 is exactly \([Z\oplus\mathbb C\to\mathbb C]\) with differential (z,c)↦c, retaining the original one-dimensional kernel. These examples illustrate the proved maps; neither is represented as a numerical arithmetic-zero packet.

## 7. Receiving the actual proper-source comparison

For an independent proper-source pair retain its original matrices \(Q_N^p\) and \(H_N^p=A_N-B_N^*C_N^{-1}B_N\), with the full target minimum in C_N. On explicitly fixed coefficient spaces of the same rank, set \(\delta_{D,N}=\log\det H_{D,N}^E-\log\det H_{D,N}^B\). Direct cancellation, without identifying either proper metric with the reference, gives
\[
\mathcal R\log\frac{\det H_N^p}{\det Q_N^p}
=\mathcal R\left[\log\frac{\det H_N^p}{\det H_{D,N}^B}
-\log\frac{\det Q_N^p}{\det H_{D,N}^E}\right]
-\mathcal R\delta_{D,N}.
\tag{PC31}
\]
Here \(\mathcal R\) has the original cutoffs q−1,q,2q−1,2q and signs +,+,−,−. The moving-family norm estimate controls the last term with the original angle and all cross terms. PC14 proves cancellation of its common resultant off the boundary; PC23–28 specifies the changed kernel when observation rank drops. PC31 leaves the two native comparisons as their actual unevaluated determinants. No arithmetic sign or initial kernel coefficient is inferred from this identity.

## 8. The exact kernel determinant on every observation-rank stratum

The boundary also has a determinant receiver, after retaining the common kernel Z explicitly. Give K/Z its full attained metric, and let \(\widetilde\pi:K/Z\to\ker\bar\Lambda\) be the isomorphism proved in PC27. The target inherits its metric from the entire quotient E/V. If r=rank A, then
\[
\det(\widetilde\pi^\dagger\widetilde\pi)
=\det{}^+(H^{-1/2}JH^{-1/2}).
\tag{PC32}
\]
Here det-positive is the product of exactly the r positive eigenvalues, with the empty product equal to one. Its zero eigenvalues have not been discarded from the complex: their Z appears in PC25 and PC27.

To prove PC32, work in the Hilbert space \(Z^\perp\), using the isometric minimum sections of the quotient by Z. The two subspaces are \(K_0=K\cap Z^\perp\) and \(V_0=V\cap Z^\perp\), with zero intersection. Choose orthonormal frames X,Y for them and put \(C=X^\dagger Y\), using the Hilbert adjoint of these isometries. Orthogonal projection onto \(V^\perp\) restricts to \(K_0\) with Gram \(I-CC^\dagger\). Projection onto \(K^\perp\) restricts to \(V_0\) with Gram \(I-C^\dagger C\). The nonzero eigenvalues of \(CC^\dagger\) and \(C^\dagger C\) agree, as follows by the maps C and \(C^\dagger\) on each positive eigenspace. Therefore their two identity-minus determinants agree. The latter determinant is exactly the product of the positive eigenvalues in PC32, since on \(V=Z\oplus V_0\) the observation is zero on Z and has this Gram on \(V_0\). This proves the equation with both full minima retained.

In particular, if \(\sigma_r(T)^2\ge\ell>0\), its logarithmic loss obeys
\[
0\le-\log\det(\widetilde\pi^\dagger\widetilde\pi)
\le\min(\dim K-\dim Z,r)\log(1/\ell).
\tag{PC33}
\]
The rank is bounded by the two dimensions of C. For r=0 the exact loss is zero. When Z=0 this is the earlier RF66 angle identity. At rank loss it is its exact continuation on K/Z, together with the retained inherited metric on Z. This gives the strongest finite determinant transfer at the boundary without an unproved positive-angle margin.

Finally the corrected section j_0 on ker barΛ maps isomorphically into K_0. Indeed Λj_0=0 by PC18 and j_0 is perpendicular to Z by PC28. It is the inverse minimum representative of \(\widetilde\pi\). Consequently
\[
\log\det\bigl((j_0|_{\ker\bar\Lambda})^\dagger
(j_0|_{\ker\bar\Lambda})\bigr)
=-\log\det{}^+(H^{-1/2}JH^{-1/2}).
\tag{PC34}
\]
Domain and target metrics are respectively the inherited metric from E/V and the inherited metric on K_0. Thus, in the full-rank moving family, the chain-section correction on the actual kernel has error at most \(2\min(\dim K,d)E_{k,d,N}\), including growing \(d=\lfloor q/\log^2(q+2)\rfloor\). Its four-cutoff return is o(kq) by the complete activated bound. This larger-degree assertion follows from the restricted determinant identity, rather than applying the global source bound PC22 beyond its stated d=O(k) range.
