# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited source container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). `REPRODUCE.py` extracts its exact bytes as `extracted_sources/STACKS_HOMOLOGY_original.tex`, with the byte offset and SHA-256 recorded in `SOURCE_LEDGER.json`.

The chapter is a separate original human source, not a programme-authored derivation. Its GNU Free Documentation License applies independently of the surrounding files. The unaltered [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) accompanies the source; [the pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING) remains available. No additional restriction is imposed on that source. A complete build of the Stacks chapter uses the upstream repository's dependencies.

The preceding complex definitions use the chapter's [cochain homotopies](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3057) and [quasi-isomorphisms](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex#L3160). Retention of a complete source does not claim exhaustive reading.

H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek, R. F. Swarttouw and W. P. Reinhardt. The original joint-minimum proof and its citations remain complete in `JOINT_MINIMUM_COMPLETE.tex`. Earlier programme derivations and new derivations have distinct source identities; their mathematics is not attributed to an undifferentiated agent.
