This paper retains the source metric, conductor observation, complete roots, Gamma mass, physical coordinate, finite period guards, complex phases and four cutoffs with signs +,+,-,-.

- [AP14–18: positive observation-layer determinants](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/9104d852c6bc5b79e6fc4588c403128a40b7fda3/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260922-arithmetic-probe-mass/PROBE_PROOFS.md#L138) decomposes the original kernel determinant into positive successive quotients. The coupled inverse-energy bound improves the filtered multiplier from 5/4 to 9/8. - [PEN9–14: canonical projective sections and physical action](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/9104d852c6bc5b79e6fc4588c403128a40b7fda3/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260922-arithmetic-probe-mass/PENCIL_PROOFS.md#L167) retains inclusion by v, multiplication by u and physical action (k/2)v+iu.
