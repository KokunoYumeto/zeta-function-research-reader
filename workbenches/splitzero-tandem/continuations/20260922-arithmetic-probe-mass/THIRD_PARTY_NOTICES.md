# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited proof container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). Its exact bytes are identified by `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` offsets and hashes in `RETAINED_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCES.tex`. Its GNU Free Documentation License applies separately; the unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) and [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING) accompany it. No additional restriction is imposed. Its upstream dependencies are needed to build the whole chapter; retention does not claim exhaustive reading.

Human provenance includes **Qiang Zhang, Ion Victor Gosea and Athanasios C. Antoulas**, [Factorization of the Loewner matrix pencil and its consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09674v1), Section 2/Lemma 2.1; **Geneviève Dusson, Israel Michael Sigal and Benjamin Stamm**, [The Feshbach–Schur map and perturbation theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02058v1); and **Dave Platt and Tim Trudgian**, [The Riemann hypothesis is true up to 3·10¹²](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09765). **Thomas Berger and Stephan Trenn**, [The quasi-Kronecker form for matrix pencils](https://doi.org/10.1137/110826278), Corollary 4.13, retain the incoming historical attribution: their original-author TeX was not read for this edition, and PEN2 gives the finite argument directly.

Original DLMF equation credits retain **R. A. Askey and R. Roy** and **T. H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek and R. F. Swarttouw**, with **W. P. Reinhardt**. The classical Kantorovich constant and complex polarization are not presented as newly discovered identities; this edition proves the finite formulas it uses. Exact source versions, reading coverage, historical incoming claims and programme uses are distinguished in [SOURCE_READING_USE.md](SOURCE_READING_USE.md). Earlier human attributions remain intact. No complete original paper is newly redistributed, and a programme derivation is not presented as an original theorem of its cited sources.

This notice and the licence copy are additive companions. All owner-sealed files retain their exact bytes.
