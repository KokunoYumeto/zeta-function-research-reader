# Human sources and separate licences

The inherited proof container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). Its exact bytes are identified by `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` offsets and hashes in `RETAINED_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCES.tex`. Its GNU Free Documentation License applies separately; the unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) and [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING) accompany it. No additional restriction is imposed. Building that complete chapter requires its upstream dependencies; retention is not a claim of exhaustive reading.

**Geneviève Dusson, Israel Michael Sigal and Benjamin Stamm**, [The Feshbach–Schur map and perturbation theory, arXiv:2105.02058v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02058v1), are credited at the exact construction used in RM1–18. **P. G. Lejeune-Dirichlet**'s progression theorem is cited through **Ralf Stephan**'s explicitly credited [English translation, arXiv:0808.1408v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1408v2). The original-author and translated TeX archives are retained locally, with exact identities and reading coverage in [SOURCE_READING_USE.md](SOURCE_READING_USE.md); no newly redistributed complete original paper is claimed.

Retained DLMF equation sources credit **R. A. Askey and R. Roy** for Gamma formulas, and **T. H. Koornwinder, R. Wong, R. Koekoek and R. F. Swarttouw**, with **W. P. Reinhardt**, for orthogonal-polynomial formulas. Historical and inherited sources retain their existing credits, including **J. L. W. V. Jensen**, **Christian Krattenthaler**, **Ming Gu** and **Stanley C. Eisenstat**. The source-use ledger distinguishes actual source reading and use from contextual attribution. Programme derivations are not represented as original theorems of their cited sources.

This notice and the licence copy are additive companions; the sealed mathematical and source-reading files remain unchanged.
