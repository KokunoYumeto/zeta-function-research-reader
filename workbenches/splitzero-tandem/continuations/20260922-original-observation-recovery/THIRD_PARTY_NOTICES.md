The inherited proof container retains the unchanged **Homological Algebra** chapter by **The Stacks Project Authors**, version [a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/homology.tex). Its exact bytes are identified by `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` offsets and hashes in `RETAINED_COMPLETE_PROOF_SOURCES.tex`. Its GNU Free Documentation License applies separately; the unchanged [licence copy](STACKS_COPYING.txt) and [pinned upstream licence](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project/blob/a04446e57ec1fbc252a871afcec7752fb2807b14/COPYING) accompany it. No additional restriction is imposed. Its upstream dependencies are needed to build the whole chapter; retention does not claim exhaustive reading.
