# Split-Zero cohomology: full support, prime observations and heat endpoints

These papers develop the Split-Zero zeta programme's actual coefficient objects and observation maps. The coefficient construction distinguishes a zero on a specified support from an absent support. The papers carry those coefficients through cohomology, prime actions, spectral jets, arithmetic theta sources, quotient metrics and heat evolution. This edition retains the complete arguments and their human-source citations; it is not a proof or disproof of RH.

## Separate readers and complete sources

- [208-page reconstruction](graph-pulse/FULL_RECONSTRUCTION.pdf) · [complete self-contained LaTeX](graph-pulse/FULL_RECONSTRUCTION.tex). This cumulative paper develops full-prime and full-support cohomology, exact mixed observations, complete graph metrics and prime-pulse maps. The new GC, PO and PCO sections construct the graph completion, prescribe every retained Taylor coordinate using finitely many prime pulses, and calculate the cost and full complementary Weil contribution of that prescription. The global arithmetic sign question is not decided by finite interpolation.
- [103-page full-lattice Connes–Consani component](cc-totality/FULL_LATTICE_CC_READER.pdf) · [master LaTeX and relative inputs](cc-totality/FULL_LATTICE_CC_READER.tex). This paper develops full-lattice arithmetic sites, tropical prime spectra, absolute-curve character families, sheaf fibre products, bounded sections and divisor multiplication. Its twistor and prime-receiver constructions retain all support labels, nilpotent tensor ladders and the original metric. The named classical sources and the programme's new constructions remain separately attributed.
- [40-page heat and endpoint addition](heat-endpoints/SUPPORTED_ZERO_HEAT_ENDPOINTS.pdf) · [complete self-contained LaTeX](heat-endpoints/SUPPORTED_ZERO_HEAT_ENDPOINTS.tex). This addition calculates inverse-monoid and semilattice heat maps, the original endpoint cluster-energy expansion, holonomy-to-Weil maps and an exact totalized-potential defect. The nonzero nilpotent holonomy has a zero Weil image and a nonzero residue observation; those are explicitly different maps. The calculated value −3 of one extended potential is not presented as a negative complete Weil test.

## Human sources, provenance and verification

The reconstruction cites Alain Connes, André Weil, Pierre Deligne, Ralf Meyer, Connes–Consani and the other authors at their actual points of use. The [reconstruction source ledger](graph-pulse/prior_results/05_SOURCE_READING_LEDGER.json), [Connes–Consani source account](cc-totality/README_READER.md#human-sources-and-originating-programme-sources), and [heat source ledger](heat-endpoints/SOURCE_READING_LEDGER.json) state versions, reading coverage and programme applications. Original author material, supplied translations and local derivations are identified separately. References to prior programme mathematics link to the corresponding public proofs, not merely a project name.

In particular the heat calculations use Brad Rodgers and Terence Tao's [original de Bruijn–Newman conventions and results](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5), and Dave Platt and Tim Trudgian's [specified threshold bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09765v1). The full-lattice component uses the original Connes–Consani sources listed in its reader. This publication does not claim new proofs of those literature results or exhaustive reading of their papers.
