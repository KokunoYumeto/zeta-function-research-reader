[Read the 20-page PDF](https://zenodo.org/records/22906270/files/152-splitzero-mixed-weil-and-boundary.pdf?download=1) · [complete LaTeX](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/3c78cfbd9e194d35117b33277c421c6564ce3dbf/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-full-prime-support/033/MIXED_PRIME_ENERGY_AND_BOUNDARY.tex) · [human sources](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/3c78cfbd9e194d35117b33277c421c6564ce3dbf/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-full-prime-support/033/HUMAN_SOURCES.md).

Apply the actual degree-p operator to admissible mixed tests and evaluate their full Weil form, including the Archimedean contribution. The entire distinct-prime three-pulse family has Q(g) > (5981/52500)N > N/10 for all four signs; the signed joint trace remains negative at every r >= 2; the original companion has an injective annulus-distribution map with explicit nonzero boundary. The negative partial/mixed sign is not a full-Weil counterexample. Use the complete FWP evaluation together with PME and FWJ, and retain the exact distributional target of SBT. Unfinished: General full Weil positivity, canonical global interior-cohomology identification and an RH decision.
