## What the new calculations establish

1. **Mixed support produces a nonzero arithmetic extension.** Keeping every adelic boundary face gives `0 → K(s) → B(s) → C² → 0`, with no equivariant section. At a prime, its connecting classes are `(p^{-s}−1)e_p` and `(p^s−1)e_infinity`. Their actual differential has determinant `−p^s(1−p^{-s})²`. The named finite-place line gives the Euler factor and its exact logarithmic derivative. **Proof: ME1–19.**
2. **The added open prime has nonzero relative cohomology.** The full sheaf calculation gives `Ri!F=[A → B_X]`. Applied to the universal arithmetic coupling it gives principal parts, and over `Spec Z` its degree-one relative group is `Q/Z = ⊕_p Q_p/Z_p`. Its actual residue filtration has one `F_p` layer at every depth; that produces the exact `log p` weight in the prime-power expression. The coefficient category and its maps to semiring realizations are proved explicitly. **Proof: RP1–38.**
3. **The prime degree fixes the half-power and a positive local pairing.** Multiplication by `p` on `Q_p/Z_p` has exactly `p` preimages. Its pullback has norm `sqrt(p)`. The explicit dilation on `L²(Q_p)` has correlations `p^{−|n|/2}`. Thus the original prime term is exactly `W_p(g*g*) = (log p)(||E_p g||² − ||g||²)`, with the original Haar measure, every prime power, and both signs retained. **Proof: PD1–19.**
4. **There is a proved local weight-one identity.** On that computed relative space the actual unweighted operator obeys `T* T = T T* = pI`, and its Hilbert spectrum is precisely `|z|=sqrt(p)`. This supplies a local operator for the purity calculation; it does not prove Deligne purity of the full interior cohomology. **Proof: PD20–21.**
5. **Every integer, including zero, remains in the action.** All nonzero integers act on the common relative group and finite-adelic space. Their integral-vector Gram matrix is `gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)`; its classical Smith determinant is proved and cited. Integer zero acts into a boundary distribution, distinctly from `tau`. The two operators and their transpose maps are written explicitly. **Proof: IA1–9.**

The remaining target is the full cohomological image and its dual pairing, in the role played by `im(H_c^i → H^i)` in Deligne II (3.3.6). The prime energies above still occur as differences. Neither full Weil positivity nor RH is asserted.
