# Full-prime cohomology, complete support and the mixed Weil calculation

These three complete papers develop the Split-Zero programme from its full arithmetic support, then calculate how its actual prime-degree operators act on mixed-support tests. Each paper retains its own proofs, original coordinates, source attributions, figures and verification records. They are not a proof or disproof of RH.

## Start with the geometry

[031: Full prime geometry and the relative arithmetic pairing — 75-page PDF](031/02_REBUILT_MATHEMATICS.pdf) · [complete LaTeX](031/01_REBUILT_MATHEMATICS.tex) · [results and precise equation families](031/04_RESULT_INDEX.json).

The starting semiring has the supported integer zero e and a distinct added zero tau. Its full prime space, including the prime (e), produces relative principal parts. Over Spec Z the calculated relative group is Q/Z, with every p-primary part retained. Multiplication by p has exactly p preimages; the derived local operator has norm factor sqrt(p), the full prime-power energy and a local complex weight-one identity. The paper also retains all adelic faces, their nonsplit extension, every integer action and the original metric comparison. The prime chain itself is credited prior work, not a new discovery in this edition. The interior compact-support-to-ordinary cohomology image, global purity and full Weil positivity remain unproved.

## Recover all support, including its mixed part

[032: Recovered full-support programme — 50-page PDF](032/RECOVERED_PROGRAMME.pdf) · [complete LaTeX](032/RECOVERED_PROGRAMME.tex) · [221 exact equation locators](032/PROOF_INDEX.json).

Individual support branches lose mixed terms. This paper constructs the full additive support jet, all extensions of the earlier ST4 action, supported cohomology and joint observations recovering those terms. It proves the obstruction to treating the original nonmonomial prime matrix as a free-semimodule automorphism, and supplies the corresponding fibre-groupoid equivalences. Its weighted place functional is explicitly defined; it is not asserted to be a forced analytic trace. Negative evaluations at every even order in this constructed channel do not establish a counterexample to Weil's criterion.

## Apply the actual prime operator and include the Archimedean contribution

[033: Full Weil positivity and the surviving mixed-support trace — 20-page PDF](033/MIXED_PRIME_ENERGY_AND_BOUNDARY.pdf) · [complete LaTeX](033/MIXED_PRIME_ENERGY_AND_BOUNDARY.tex) · [86 exact equation locators](033/PROOF_INDEX.json).

PME1–29 applies 031's actual degree-p operator to 032's mixed support. FWP1–24 then calculates the complete Weil form for the same compact three-pulse family, proving Q(g) > (5981/52500)N > N/10 for all distinct-prime pairs and all four signs, with N and the test defined in the proof. This excludes that entire test family as full-Weil counterexamples. FWJ1–8 retains the common Archimedean contribution: the four individual values are positive while their signed mixed trace is negative at every order r >= 2. SBT1–23 injects the original companion object into a concrete annulus distribution space and computes its nonzero finite boundary, retaining G_N, the full support lattice L and the observation kernel. It does not identify this channel with canonical global interior cohomology.

## How these results change the reading of earlier work

The scalar branch observations are not the full additive support calculation; use 032 FSJ and JMD for the mixed information and its exact recovery. The failure of a free-semimodule automorphism is accompanied by the explicit fibre-groupoid maps in 032 CR. Even tensor order alone does not imply positivity of a signed support trace. Most importantly, the negative mixed-prime calculation must be read together with 033's positive full-Weil evaluation, including its complete Archimedean contribution. No negative partial term is being promoted into a counterexample to RH. Earlier sealed papers remain unchanged and should be read at their own stated scopes; the separately published correction to 027 RD15 remains in force.
