# Original Heat Monodromy and Supported Trace Responses

The15-page reader gives complete new proofs of regular monodromy on the original heat-collision algebra, the first and next responses of every nilpotent coefficient layer, the logarithmic connection residue, and synchronized projections retaining the entire support lattice. It applies the latter construction to the positive eight-state collision subalgebra, evaluating its multiplication defect and spectrum map.

The original heat coordinate, time, analytic unit, coefficients and reflected trace are retained. The standalone LaTeX is `ORIGINAL_HEAT_MONODROMY_AND_TRACE_RESPONSES.tex`. The three component sources supply exact proof locators HD1-44, HF1-16 and HL1-15. `RETAINED_HEAT_COLLISION_PROOFS.tex` contains the complete predecessor proof source, also publicly linked in the introduction. The new diagram is reproducible TikZ in the flag source.

The Python checks require SymPy and validate finite auxiliary identities. They do not certify a zeta zero or replace the analytic proofs. `SOURCE_READING_AND_USE.json` records human sources, versions, actual reading coverage and the use of the supplied mathematical text. The unavailable incoming archive has not been represented as read.

The completed15-page supplement is distinct from the sealed60-page edition. The programme's whole cumulative working manuscript includes all of these results. The global arithmetic Weil-sign question remains unfinished.
