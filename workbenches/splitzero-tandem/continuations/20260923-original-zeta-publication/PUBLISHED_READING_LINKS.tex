collection}\label{original-zeta-reconstruction-in-the-split-zero-collection}

\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22911829}{Current cumulative Zenodo

The project studies zeta zeros through coefficient geometry, cohomology,
arithmetic spectral actions and quantitative metric estimates. The full
collection retains its theta-source, Gamma--Toda, conductor, prime,
trace, formalization and connected geometric, heat, fluid, complexity
and thermal investigations, with each result's actual scope. The
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22911829/files/154-splitzero-full-support-graph-pulses.pdf?download=1}{208-page
cohomology paper} remains the preview. This edition does not replace
that whole-project account with its newest calculation.

For the original Gaussian kernel, all integer samples recover every
original coefficient through an explicit inverse and a convergent
interpolation kernel. The discrete map carries the supported-zero
action, every lower label, independent graph mass and heat evolution.
Circle periodization instead forgets the integer coefficient profile and
retains the mass. Moving the samples by half an integer preserves
uniqueness but destroys a uniform inverse bound; a finite alternating
source proves that precise failure. No new metric is substituted for the
original arithmetic seminorm.
