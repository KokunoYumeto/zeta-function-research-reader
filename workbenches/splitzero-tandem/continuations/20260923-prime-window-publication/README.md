## Human source and exact coordinate comparison

The compact density, Fourier product and zero multiplicities are classical. The point-of-use attribution cites Juan Arias de Reyna, [*An infinitely differentiable function with compact support: Definition and properties*, arXiv:1702.05442v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.05442v1), the author's English translation of his 1982 paper. Theorem 1 and equations (1), (4), (9) give the source construction. In the retained programme coordinates, the density is b_r(v) = r^(-1) phi(v/r), and its entire transform is G_r(z) = phi-hat(rz/(2 pi i)), with the original r = 1/64. These equalities, their Jacobian, the Fourier sign, index shift and resulting zero multiplicities are proved beside UP's construction.

Arias de Reyna credits Jessen and Wintner's 1935 construction; that historical attribution is identified as coming through his paper, not as a new reading of their original. The author TeX was read through the main argument and bibliography. The [citation-revision record](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/84edd11df011c3e161a57dad9f083276a3c0bb95/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/branches/tau-zero-prime-positivity/CITATION_REVISION.json) records the publication-copy changes against the unchanged owner snapshot. Complete original proofs and displayed equations remain. The source author's archive stays private; the publication supplies citations and programme derivations rather than copying the archive.
