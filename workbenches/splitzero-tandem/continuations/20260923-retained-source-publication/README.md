The cohomology paper remains the preview. The continuation retains ten separate papers (208, 385, 37, 21, 10, 34, 7, 24, 403 and 16 pages), complete programme LaTeX, human citations, figures, checks and 16,477 pinned proof locations. All 100 base files, including 98 separate PDFs, and all twelve preceding continuation downloads remain unchanged. [Reading guide and verified file-publication receipt](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-publication/README.md).

Each paper retains its actual proved scope. The collection does not claim an RH decision.

## What the two added papers calculate

The 403-page collection retains 54 complete derivations, with the original zeta function and every completion factor still explicit. Its resonant test detects each nontrivial zero at time 1/32. The paired-source extension computes the exact translated Weil matrix and retains the small-time exponential even where its time derivatives vanish. The resulting global bound is not proved. [Complete source and proof guide](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/bd02776240eef3bc3596e7d3929fbdaa885bfa37/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/branches/tau-zero-prime-positivity/PUBLICATION_COMPANION.md).

The 16-page paper evaluates a two-dimensional arithmetic Weil form, proves its exact prime-boundary map, and calculates supported-zero multiplication, its metric defect and the local cohomology at the vanishing time of that defect. Full integer-prime module support remains; coefficient heat time and theta time are not identified. Result IDs are SZ-20260923-149, -150 and -151. An independent derivation corrected one general-ring explanatory clause by using the meet-prime support label; it did not change a theorem, formula, metric, certificate or result ID.
