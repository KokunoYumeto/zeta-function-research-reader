\section{Split-Zero cohomology and the zeta-function research
programme}\label{split-zero-cohomology-and-the-zeta-function-research-programme}

\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22911829}{The base collection} and
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22920247}{the current continuation
collection} together retain the Split-Zero cohomology and zeta research
programme: arithmetic geometry, cohomology, theta sources, analytic
estimates, spectral and heat calculations, formalization, and the
connected investigations. The cohomology paper remains the preview. The
continuation retains ten separate papers (208, 385, 37, 21, 10, 34, 7,
24, 403 and 16 pages), complete programme LaTeX, human citations,
figures, checks and 16,477 pinned proof locations. All 100 base files,
including 98 separate PDFs, and all twelve preceding continuation
downloads remain unchanged.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-publication/README.md}{Reading
guide and verified file-publication receipt}.

The programme investigates original zeta and arithmetic theta sources,
cohomology and supported-zero coefficients, original quotient metrics
and conductor estimates, spectral and geometric comparisons, heat and
prime trace calculations, and formalization. The original arithmetic
function, Gamma and endpoint factors, trivial zeros, poles,
multiplicities and support labels remain explicit in the reconstructed
receiving maps. Each paper retains its actual proved scope. The
collection does not claim an RH decision.

\subsection{What the two added papers
calculate}\label{what-the-two-added-papers-calculate}

The 403-page collection retains 54 complete derivations, with the
original zeta function and every completion factor still explicit. Its
resonant test detects each nontrivial zero at time 1/32. The
paired-source extension computes the exact translated Weil matrix and
retains the small-time exponential even where its time derivatives
vanish. The resulting global bound is not proved.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/bd02776240eef3bc3596e7d3929fbdaa885bfa37/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/branches/tau-zero-prime-positivity/PUBLICATION_COMPANION.md}{Complete
source and proof guide}.

The 16-page paper evaluates a two-dimensional arithmetic Weil form,
proves its exact prime-boundary map, and calculates supported-zero
multiplication, its metric defect and the local cohomology at the
vanishing time of that defect. Full integer-prime module support
remains; coefficient heat time and theta time are not identified. Result
IDs are SZ-20260923-149, -150 and -151. An independent derivation
corrected one general-ring explanatory clause by using the meet-prime
support label; it did not change a theorem, formula, metric, certificate
or result ID.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/bd02776240eef3bc3596e7d3929fbdaa885bfa37/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-readers/two-source-supported-zero/PUBLICATION_READING.md}{Complete
sources, exact correction and reading scope}.

All earlier separate PDFs and complete source archives remain available.
