\section{Split-Zero cohomology and the zeta-function research
programme}\label{split-zero-cohomology-and-the-zeta-function-research-programme}

\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22911829}{The base collection} and
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22913062}{the linked continuation
collection} together retain the Split-Zero cohomology and zeta research
programme: arithmetic geometry, cohomology, theta sources, analytic
estimates, spectral and heat calculations, formalization, and the
connected investigations. The cohomology paper remains the preview. The
continuation supplies separate 385/37/21/10/34/7-page original-zeta and
arithmetic-boundary papers alongside the 208-page cohomology entry
point, with complete programme LaTeX, human citations, figures, checks
and 13,743 pinned proof locations. All 100 base files, including 98
separate PDFs, remain unchanged.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-publication/README.md}{Reading
guide and verified publication receipt}.

The source archive and mathematical PDFs bind source commit
\texttt{acd7016b8232a56f31ae3e69fb800571e8789080}. The 385-page reader
has 52 complete main proofs. The separate 34-page source correction
explicitly fixes the classical theta-origin, transported
multiplication/unit and embedded-fibre account; original-zeta factors
and exact local maps are retained. The other papers keep their own
scope. The seven-page arithmetic-pairing successor proves
50\textless K\textless64 for one specified test at t=1/32 while its two
linear prime-boundary moments remain negative; this does not establish
whole-space positivity or decide RH.
