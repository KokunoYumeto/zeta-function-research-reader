\section{Split-Zero cohomology and the zeta-function research
programme}\label{split-zero-cohomology-and-the-zeta-function-research-programme}

\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22911829}{The base collection} and
\href{https://zenodo.org/records/22913468}{the current continuation
collection} together retain the Split-Zero cohomology and zeta research
programme: arithmetic geometry, cohomology, theta sources, analytic
estimates, spectral and heat calculations, formalization, and the
connected investigations. The cohomology paper remains the preview. The
continuation retains eight separate papers (208, 385, 37, 21, 10, 34, 7
and 24 pages), complete programme LaTeX, human citations, figures,
checks and 15,498 pinned proof locations. All 100 base files, including
98 separate PDFs, and all nine preceding continuation downloads remain
unchanged.
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-publication/README.md}{Reading
guide and verified file-publication receipt}.

The programme investigates original zeta and arithmetic theta sources,
cohomology and supported-zero coefficients, original quotient metrics
and conductor estimates, spectral and geometric comparisons, heat and
prime trace calculations, and formalization. Each retained paper states
its actual proved scope. The original arithmetic function, Gamma and
endpoint factors, trivial zeros, poles, multiplicities and support
labels remain explicit in the reconstructed receiving maps. Neither this
collection nor its newest addition asserts an RH decision.

SZ-20260923-147 and -148. It reconstructs the actual radial adelic
source together with its complete prime boundary, and gives a finite
exact test for the full boundary of rational mixed profiles. It retains
the original seminorm, both endpoint characters, compact-character
receiver and label corrections. These are source and boundary theorems,
not all-test positivity. The separate seven-page paper proves
50\textless K\textless64 for one specified original test; its negative
linear boundary coordinates are retained.
