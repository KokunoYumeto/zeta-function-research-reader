# Original-zeta and full-support companion readers

These separate papers extend the [Split-Zero cohomology and zeta-function research programme](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader). They retain the original arithmetic functions, heat sources, support labels, exceptional local modules, endpoint terms, and explicit maps. They accompany, rather than replace, the cohomology, quotient-volume, geometric and spectral papers and the [385-page supported-zero collection](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/branches/tau-zero-prime-positivity/TAU_ZERO_PRIME_POSITIVITY.pdf). No RH decision is claimed.
