The four delivery files are the PDF, its expanded TeX, the reproducible source ZIP and this note. The ZIP supplies the figure required when compiling the expanded TeX.

CDV constructs every rational-prime translate of the original Connes–Consani coefficient sheaf, with all Haar factors, mixed-prime cohomology, Fourier scalars and the original zeta Mellin factors. ULC constructs its actual localized coefficient semimodule on Spec G_L(Z) for an arbitrary bounded distributive lattice, and proves the exact comparison with the different inverse-image coefficient sheaf. Its amplitude group reflection is i_*H_k; its support labels remain an explicit retract of the original semimodule.

The source, projective image, common-label quotient, prime action and original amplitudes are all specified. No action on an unprovided spectral quotient and no Weil positivity conclusion is asserted.
