# Programme source closure and human provenance

This public package retains the complete programme proofs, predecessor proofs, expanded LaTeX, figures, figure builder, check scripts and source-use records. Human primary archives in the received local package are omitted from this separate public source closure; their file identities and original-host links are recorded in PRIMARY_SOURCE_RETENTION.json. No programme proof or cited human attribution is removed. References in the unchanged received reading notes to bundled original author archives describe the local delivery, not this public package. The reproduction builder reads the retained programme sources.

The principal human source is Alain Connes, Caterina Consani and Matilde Marcolli, [The Weil proof and the geometry of the adeles class space, arXiv:math/0703392v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703392v1). Its author filename Yuri70v2.tex does not change the arXiv version. Brad Rodgers and Terence Tao's [version5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5) and the earlier programme foundations retain their point-of-use citations. Exact reading coverage is recorded, not presumed complete.

The owner's delivery record binds all34 pages, the source fragments, the independently reviewed scopes and the reproduced build. It is not a claim of a new full proof audit by the publisher. No full Weil positivity or RH conclusion is asserted.
