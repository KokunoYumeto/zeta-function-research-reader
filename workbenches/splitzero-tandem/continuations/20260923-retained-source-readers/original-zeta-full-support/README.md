# Original zeta, full support and the arithmetic trace

The [37-page reader](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-readers/original-zeta-full-support/ORIGINAL_ZETA_RECONSTRUCTION.pdf) and [complete compiling LaTeX](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-readers/original-zeta-full-support/ORIGINAL_ZETA_RECONSTRUCTION.tex) retain seven complete proofs, their two figures, reproducible figure scripts, exact finite checks and input data. This is the original-zeta reconstruction, not a replacement of the original function by a completed object.

The full support lattice and its prime stalks come first. The calculation then retains the entire Gamma/pi/endpoint multiplier, the original trivial zeros and pole, shifted fibres and jets, every heat derivative term and signed trace correction. A faithful labelled heat source retains its marked mass; its prime-boundary moments give explicit theta values and return the original zeta function. The final proof constructs both sectorial continuations of the unfiltered endpoint. The calculated negative uncompensated divisor form is accompanied by its explicit multiplier correction; it is not a proof that RH is false.

Compile ORIGINAL_ZETA_RECONSTRUCTION.tex with LuaLaTeX twice in this directory. The full individual proofs are in the seven input TeX files. The native Schur and witness proofs are retained under supporting_proofs/. Input hashes and exact-arithmetic receipts state what was calculated and what earlier quadrature input was retained. OWNER_CLOSURE.json binds the owner's sealed PDF, seven proofs, independently scoped reviews and build report. Publication adopts that recorded mathematical and all-page visual review; it is not a new independent audit of all37 pages.

Human sources are cited at point of use in the TeX: [Brad Rodgers and Terence Tao, version5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5), [Ricardo Pérez-Marco, version2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04445v2), the Gamma formulas of [R. A. Askey and R. Roy](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5), and [N. M. Temme's error-function convention](https://dlmf.nist.gov/7.2.E2). Bounded source reading and original source identities are recorded in the manuscript; a citation is not a claim of a complete reading. Primary literature archives and private correspondence are not redistributed in this package.
