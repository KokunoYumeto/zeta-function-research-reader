\section{Original zeta, full support and the arithmetic
trace}\label{original-zeta-full-support-and-the-arithmetic-trace}

The
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-readers/original-zeta-full-support/ORIGINAL_ZETA_RECONSTRUCTION.pdf}{37-page
reader} and
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-retained-source-readers/original-zeta-full-support/ORIGINAL_ZETA_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{complete
compiling LaTeX} retain seven complete proofs, their two figures,
reproducible figure scripts, exact finite checks and input data. This is
the original-zeta reconstruction, not a replacement of the original
function by a completed object.

The full support lattice and its prime stalks come first. The
calculation then retains the entire Gamma/pi/endpoint multiplier, the
original trivial zeros and pole, shifted fibres and jets, every heat
derivative term and signed trace correction. A faithful labelled heat
source retains its marked mass; its prime-boundary moments give explicit
theta values and return the original zeta function. The final proof
constructs both sectorial continuations of the unfiltered endpoint. The
calculated negative uncompensated divisor form is accompanied by its
explicit multiplier correction; it is not a proof that RH is false.

Compile ORIGINAL\_ZETA\_RECONSTRUCTION.tex with LuaLaTeX twice in this
directory. The full individual proofs are in the seven input TeX files.
The native Schur and witness proofs are retained under
supporting\_proofs/. Input hashes and exact-arithmetic receipts state
what was calculated and what earlier quadrature input was retained.
OWNER\_CLOSURE.json binds the owner's sealed PDF, seven proofs,
independently scoped reviews and build report. Publication adopts that
recorded mathematical and all-page visual review; it is not a new
independent audit of all37 pages.

Human sources are cited at point of use in the TeX:
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5}{Brad Rodgers and Terence Tao,
version5}, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04445v2}{Ricardo
Pérez-Marco, version2}, the Gamma formulas of
\href{https://dlmf.nist.gov/5}{R. A. Askey and R. Roy}, and
\href{https://dlmf.nist.gov/7.2.E2}{N. M. Temme's error-function
convention}. Bounded source reading and original source identities are
recorded in the manuscript; a citation is not a claim of a complete
reading. Primary literature archives and private correspondence are not
redistributed in this package.
