\section{Supported zero on an evaluated arithmetic source
plane}\label{supported-zero-on-an-evaluated-arithmetic-source-plane}

The \href{NOTE.pdf}{16-page paper} starts with the original theta test
and evaluates its two-dimensional Weil form. It proves an exact map to
the arithmetic prime boundary, calculates the action of supported zero
in the unchanged metric, and determines the local cohomology where its
square-zero metric defect vanishes. The integer-prime support is
retained. Theta time and coefficient heat time are separate parameters.

The \href{NOTE.tex}{complete master}, both included proof sources,
reproducible figures, interval certificates and three independent
derivations are included. Each Markdown derivation also has a TeX
companion. \href{PUBLIC_RESULT_INDEX.json}{The public result index}
links results SZ-20260923-149, -150 and -151 to their exact proof
locations. \href{SOURCE_READING.json}{Human-source reading and
attribution} records versions and reading scope.

The preserved nineteen-file source edition is unchanged. This
publication changes only the general-ring explanatory clause after TS30
and its rebuilt PDF among those nineteen files; \href{CORRECTION.md}{the
correction and exact file identities} are included. The publisher
checked source hashes, full dependencies and rendered pages. Independent
derivations checked TS11--29 and TS30--32a and supplied the corrected
support-label argument. The owner's interval and mathematical review
records are adopted at their stated scope; the publisher did not rerun
the numerical certificates. The full proof does not establish positivity
for all Weil tests or decide RH.
