\section{Prime holonomy on the original compact tests and full support}
\label{sec:pfh}

We now carry the endpoint fixed-locus calculation through the original
compact tests. This uses the complete Weil form of HW24--31, with
the two global moments, all prime powers and the Archimedean term
retained. The underlying Connes--Consani source conventions are cited
there and in HH28--30. The two-prime joint spectral receiver is also
proved in the earlier programme source
\href{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/5a89872598df0902b7c1393cf8e4692ca3010a95/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260923-cohomology-heat-residue/graph-pulse/FULL_RECONSTRUCTION.tex}{Two-prime torus receiver, TP1--6}.
The exact fixed-locus and compact-test maps needed here have complete
proofs below.

Let
\[
 \mathcal V=\left\{F\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R):
    \int F(t)e^{t/2}\,dt=\int F(t)e^{-t/2}\,dt=0\right\},
 \qquad \mathcal L F(s)=\int F(t)e^{(s-1/2)t}\,dt.
 \tag{PFH1}
\]
For real $b$ define $\tau_bF(t)=F(t-b)$. Direct change of variables
gives
\[
 \mathcal L(\tau_bF)(s)=e^{b(s-1/2)}\mathcal LF(s),\quad
 \tau_b\mathcal V=\mathcal V,\quad
 Q(\tau_bF,\tau_bG)=Q(F,G).
 \tag{PFH2}
\]
For the second equality the two zero moments are multiplied by
$e^{b/2}$ and $e^{-b/2}$, respectively. For the third equality,
every correlation
$\int F(t)\overline{G(t-a)}\,dt$ is unchanged by translating
both arguments. The complete finite-prime terms and every term of
the Archimedean distribution in HW29 are expressions in precisely
these correlations; the endpoint terms still vanish by PFH1.
This proves invariance of the full analytic form, not merely one
prime contribution. Equivalently, on its spectral side the factors
at a reflected pair cancel exactly:
\[
 \overline{e^{b((1-\bar\rho)-1/2)}}
                    e^{b(\rho-1/2)}=1.
\]
This identity does not require $\Re\rho=1/2$. It therefore
asserts an isometry of the actual form, whose positivity is still
the arithmetic question, rather than assuming a positive inner product.

The same transport acts on the original even Schwartz source through
the explicit map
\[
 (\mathcal EF)(x)=|x|^{-1/2}F(\log|x|)\quad(x\ne0),
       \qquad(\mathcal EF)(0)=0.
 \tag{PFH3}
\]
The support of $F$ is compact, so $\mathcal EF$ vanishes in a
neighbourhood of zero and outside a compact set; it is an even
smooth Schwartz function. Its full integral is twice the positive
moment in PFH1, hence zero. Change of variables proves
\[
 \mathcal E\tau_bF(x)=e^{-b/2}(\mathcal EF)(e^{-b}x),\qquad
 \mathcal M(\Theta\mathcal EF)(s)=2\zeta(s)\mathcal LF(s),
       \quad\Re s>1,
 \tag{PFH4}
\]
where $\mathcal M\phi(s)=\int_0^\infty\phi(x)x^{s-1}dx$ and
$\Theta\phi(x)=2\sum_{n\ge1}\phi(nx)$. Absolute convergence in
$\Re s>1$ justifies the second equality term by term. The factor
two and the half density are retained. Dilation commutes with the
full theta sum by the same termwise identity.

For a prime $p$, set $b=-\log p$. The actual source operator is
$\phi(x)\mapsto p^{1/2}\phi(px)$ and its Mellin factor is
$p^{1/2-s}$. At an actual zero $\rho$ its scalar value is
$p^{1/2-\rho}$. On the entire length-$m_\rho$ jet the operator is
\[
 T_p|_{A_\rho}
   =p^{1/2-\rho}
      \sum_{k=0}^{m_\rho-1}
                \frac{(-\log p)^k M_v^k}{k!},
       \qquad A_\rho=\mathbb C[v]/(v^{m_\rho}).
 \tag{PFH5}
\]
This is exactly HH30 with $L=-\log p$ at a critical zero, and
the same Taylor calculation at any other actual zero. It is the
opposite orientation to positive $L$; no phase sign is suppressed.

There is now an exact conclusion for the full supported common
fixed locus. For any nonempty finite packet of actual completed-zeta
zeros let $E=\prod_\rho A_\rho$, with its entire zero-support
lattice $L$ attached as in HH39. Then
\[
 \boxed{\ker(T_2-I)\cap\ker(T_3-I)=0,\qquad
    \operatorname{Fix}(\widehat T_2)\cap
    \operatorname{Fix}(\widehat T_3)
         =\{(0,\lambda):\lambda\in L\}.}
 \tag{PFH6}
\]
To prove it, if both scalar characters were one at a zero $\rho$,
then $(\rho-1/2)\log2$ and $(\rho-1/2)\log3$ would belong to
$2\pi i\mathbb Z$. A nonzero $\rho-1/2$ would imply a rational
relation between $\log2$ and $\log3$, hence an equality of nonzero
integer powers of $2$ and $3$, impossible by unique factorization.
Thus $\rho=1/2$ would be required. But the original theta summands
in HH1 are positive for $u\ge0$ because $2\pi n^2e^{4u}-3>0$.
Consequently $H_0(0)>0$ and $\xi_{\mathrm R}(1/2)\ne0$.
For each actual primary factor at least one of $T_2-I,T_3-I$
has a nonzero constant coefficient. Its full nilpotent inverse is
the terminating expression HH41 with the corresponding scalar and
parameter. The common fixed amplitude is therefore zero on every
factor. Both operators fix every zero label, proving PFH6.

This fixed-locus result is unconditional and includes noncritical
zeros if any exist. It does not erase their locations from the full
action: the two scalar characters jointly distinguish $\rho$, by
the same irrationality calculation applied to $\rho-\sigma$.
Moreover their nilpotent action retains every jet. Once the primary
scalar $\lambda_p=p^{1/2-\rho}$ is specified, the exact finite
logarithm recovers
\[
 M_v=-\frac1{\log p}\sum_{j=1}^{m_\rho-1}
           \frac{(-1)^{j+1}}j
           (\lambda_p^{-1}T_p-I)^j.
 \tag{PFH7}
\]
The exponential and logarithmic formal identities hold modulo
$v^{m_\rho}$, so their series terminate and are inverse there.
Thus the common fixed support is insensitive to the modulus, while
the joint original action and its jet logarithm retain the exact
arithmetic data. PFH2--5 give its maps back to the compact tests and
the complete Weil form. This identifies what must be retained when
using a supported fixed point in a purity or positivity calculation.
