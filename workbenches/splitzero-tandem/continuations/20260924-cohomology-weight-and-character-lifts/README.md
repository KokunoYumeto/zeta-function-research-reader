# Split-Zero cohomology: geometric weight control, positive quotients and original-character lifts

This edition integrates three collaborating tasks in the wider **Split-Zero cohomology and zeta-function research programme**. That programme studies the original Riemann zeta function through support-preserving coefficient constructions, arithmetic cohomology, theta/Mellin sources, spectral actions and their exact comparison maps. Its earlier analytic estimates, formalized interfaces, heat-flow calculations, complexity investigations and research self-audit remain in the [whole-project guide](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/main/CURRENT_RESEARCH.md), [base archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22678085) and [continuation collection](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22913061).

The human investigator supplied the construction, mathematical agenda and corrections; the collaborating AI tasks developed and checked the calculations recorded here. The support notation remains **τ〈Z1; no Z2〉**. Addition on that primitive datum was retracted and is not a premise. Arithmetic operations below take place in the separately constructed coefficient and arithmetic objects, with the maps between them retained.

**This is a research edition, not a proof or disproof of RH.** It includes complete cumulative arguments and their corrections, rather than replacing them with these summaries. Classical theorems are credited to their human sources; programme proof labels are not substitutes for those sources.

## How the results fit together

The geometric task supplies actual source, boundary and duality maps on which a weight argument can act. The positive-quotient task calculates what is preserved and what is removed when the transfer is made adjoint with respect to a positive form. The character-lifting task calculates which original characters and extension classes survive those source and quotient passages. These are comparisons of specified objects, not identification of every boundary class with a failure of purity.

Five distinctions are mathematically decisive in the combined edition:

1. **The global shifted-source lift does not annihilate the local boundary quotient.** LNC4 and LNC9 calculate the surviving local map. RGR's total residue/Gysin class can remain nonzero even on the critical line. RGR11 constructs the separate receiver of the adjoint defect. The later ADC/DCA/ACD cross identifies the primal obstruction as \(\beta_r(Q)\), with source \(Q/Z_r\), and calculates its cover and continuous-dual comparisons. The current-side quotient vanishes independently of that defect; it is not a proof that the original arithmetic obstruction vanishes.
2. **The positive quotient retains an exact reconstruction term.** With the complete original zero-value space, multiplicities and transfer operators of PTQ0, PTQ1 calculates the closed defect range as the off-line value sector. PSC5 retains the identity

   \[
   W_L(F,G)=A(0)+A(1)+\mathcal A_\infty(v)-P_{\rm hist}(v)-W_O(F,G).
   \]

   All five terms are defined in [PSC5](geometric-positive-quotient/POSITIVE_QUOTIENT_SOURCE_AND_ARITHMETIC.md#psc5-the-original-arithmetic-formula-and-the-exact-discarded-contribution). In particular, the discarded original Weil term \(W_O\) has been calculated, not proved zero. A positive compression is therefore not a proof of positivity of the original form. PSC3 gives the exact full-jet compatibility object and its topological closedness criterion.
3. **The geometric positive receiver has an exact source and kernel.** GDC identifies the strong-dual closure of the original defect image and constructs the restricted Fréchet source row whose transpose is the separated costalk row. It retains the raw degree-one quotient alongside the separated one, the full coefficient kernel in the comparison triangle, the higher jets and both labelled endpoint lines. All coefficient scales \(r,t>1\) have the same unclosed image and explicitly isomorphic receivers, with bounded inverses and a cocycle law. Only recovered positive integers are asserted to give geometric cover degrees. Quotienting out the calculated defect does not prove that it was absent from the original source.
4. **A lift into a quotient and a lift into the full original target have different calculated images.** The character-lifting notes retain the restriction kernel, pushout, full germ, sign action and continuous contraction that explain this difference. They do not treat an annihilator-restricted failure as failure of ordinary surjectivity.
5. **The complete category of continuous positive receivers is now calculated, rather than assumed.** On the original Fréchet full-jet quotient \(Q\), CFP proves that every jointly continuous positive Hermitian form satisfying \(B(T_nF,G)=B(F,nT_{1/n}G)\) for all recovered positive integers has the unique expression

   \[
   B(F,G)=\sum_{\Re\rho=1/2}m_\rho c_\rho F(\rho)\overline{G(\rho)},
   \qquad 0\le c_\rho\le C(1+|\Im\rho|)^d.
   \]

   The converse holds too. Multiplicities are retained; simple zeros and density of finite primal jets are not assumed. CPS completes the quotient by the common radical in the topology generated by these forms to a rapid weighted sequence space. For the actual specialization source \(R=Q/N_O\), where \(N_O\) consists of classes whose values vanish at every actual off-line zero, it proves \(\mathscr P(R)=\{0\}\): every continuous compatible positive form is zero. This is **not** the assertion \(R=0\). The positive-seminorm topology is not silently identified with the original Fréchet topology, and the full original Weil complement remains. See [AST](geometric-positive-quotient/ADJOINT_DEFECT_SOURCE_TOPOLOGY_COMPARISON.md), [CFP](geometric-positive-quotient/CONTINUOUS_POSITIVE_TRANSFER_FORMS.md) and [CPS](geometric-positive-quotient/POSITIVE_SOURCE_COMPLETION_AND_SPECIALIZATION.md).

These results make the programme's geometric and analytic interfaces more explicit. They do **not** yet derive Deligne's required numerical weight separation for the full arithmetic object, remove the original complementary Weil contribution, prove RH, or prove a new P-versus-NP implication. The originating GCT task's name is not a claim that this batch settles geometric complexity theory.

## Human sources and reading provenance

The proofs cite the sources actually used, including Pierre Deligne's [*La conjecture de Weil. II*](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1980__52__137_0/), particularly §§3.4 and 3.6; Connes–Consani's [*Schemes over F1 and zeta functions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.2024v3), §5, and [*The Absolute Twistor Line and the Geometry of the compactified Spec Z*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00299v1); Ralf Meyer's [*A spectral interpretation for the zeros of the Riemann zeta function*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412277); and Ryan Reich's [notes on Beilinson's gluing construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.1686v4). The latest publication pass also restores explicit credits for the classical zero count, Hahn–Banach extension, Hilbert and measure forms of Riesz representation, Fréchet open mapping, Bochner's positive Fourier-measure theorem and the Guinand–Weil formula at the places where they are used. Exact inspected source locations are in [BUILD_AND_REVIEW.md](BUILD_AND_REVIEW.md) and [RESULTS.json](RESULTS.json).

Reading records distinguish original author TeX from local transcriptions and indirect witnesses. In particular, the Deligne French record is a transcription, not Deligne-authored TeX; the older Guinand and Weil articles are credited through the inspected Connes witness. Source locations and inspected ranges do not imply that every cited paper was read in full. See the [Deligne reading ledger](gct-weight-control/DELIGNE_FULL_READING_LOG.md) and [nearby-cycle source-use record](gct-weight-control/NEARBY_CYCLES_SOURCE_READING_PUBLIC.json).

Original human-author archives are **not** redistributed in this edition merely because they are accessible from arXiv. The complete programme LaTeX, proof notes and reproducible figure sources are included. Private correspondence and verbatim user transcripts are excluded; their public mathematical definitions and attributed arguments remain in the proofs.
