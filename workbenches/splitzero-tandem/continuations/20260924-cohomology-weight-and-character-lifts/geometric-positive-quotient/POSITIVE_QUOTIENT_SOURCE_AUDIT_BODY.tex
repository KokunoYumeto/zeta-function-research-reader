\subsection{PSC1--PSC2: complete projector and exact source
domains}\label{psc1psc2-complete-projector-and-exact-source-domains}

bounded and nonnegative, and vanishes exactly at \(\Re\rho=1/2\). Thus
the kernel is \(H_{\rm line}\). Every finitely supported vector in
\(H_{\rm off}\) is in the range of \(B_a\), by division by finitely many
strictly positive coefficients; this proves the range closure without a
lower bound on all off-line coefficients.

The norm-convergent exponential series has the stated diagonal
coefficients. For every \(x\in H\), the squared difference in PSC1.4 is
dominated by the original summable sequence \(m_\rho|x_\rho|^2\), giving
strong convergence on the complete divisor. This proves neither
operator-norm convergence nor a decay gap, and neither is used. The zero
set of \(b_a\), and hence the projector, is independent of the chosen
\(a>1\).

The two coordinate subsets are invariant under conjugation and \(\#\);
consequently \(J\) preserves both. On the line \(\rho^\#=\rho\), so the
original Weil form restricts to the positive value inner product. The
coefficients \(a^{\overline\rho}\) and \(a^{1-\rho}\) agree there,
proving the adjoint relation with the original degree factor \(a\).
Geometric covering interpretations are correctly restricted to recovered
positive integers.

The jet and value ideals in PSC2 are closed by the specified continuous
evaluations. Their quotients and the kernel in PSC2.3 are Fréchet, so
that exact sequence is strict. Factoring the continuous map

\subsection{PSC3: algebraic reconstruction and its exact topology
criterion}\label{psc3-algebraic-reconstruction-and-its-exact-topology-criterion}

The intersection \(J_L\cap J_O=\mathcal I\) proves injectivity of
\(\Delta\). The difference map is well defined and onto. For a
compatible pair with \(f-g=l+o\), the formula \[
F=f-l=g+o
\] gives both requested observations. Changing representatives or the
decomposition changes \(F\) only by the intersection ideal, by the
already proved injectivity. This proves the algebraic fibre-product
description and its actual inverse. The complementary exact sequence
PSC3.4 follows because the kernel of \(Q_O^{\rm jet}\to C_{\rm rec}\) is
exactly \((J_L+J_O)/J_O\). Its preimage in \(J_L\) has kernel
\(\mathcal I\). The empty-subset cases are consistent with all these
formulas.

The topology is governed precisely by \[
\Delta^{-1}:\Delta(\mathcal Q)\longrightarrow\mathcal Q
\text{ is continuous}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
J_L+J_O\text{ is closed in }\mathcal B.
\tag{PSCA1}
\] For the forward direction, the image becomes topologically isomorphic
to the complete Fréchet space \(\mathcal Q\), hence is a complete
subspace of a Hausdorff locally convex space and therefore closed. Its
preimage under the continuous map \(f\mapsto([f]_{J_L},0)\) is exactly
\(J_L+J_O\). Conversely, closedness of that sum makes \(C_{\rm rec}\)
Hausdorff Fréchet; \(\ker d\) is then a closed Fréchet subspace of the
product, and the continuous bijection \(\Delta:\mathcal Q\to\ker d\) has
continuous inverse by the open mapping theorem.

The map \(d\) is itself open for the specified quotient topology, even
before closedness of the sum is established. If \(\pi=q_L\times q_O\),
then \[
d\pi=q_{\rm sum}\circ(f,g\mapsto f-g).
\] For an open subset \(U\) of the product quotient, \(\pi^{-1}U\) is
open; its image under the open difference map and then the quotient map
is \(d(U)\), hence open.

The more precise closure calculation in PSC3.3a is also correct. Let
\(d_{\rm sep}\) be the difference map into
\(\mathcal B/\overline{J_L+J_O}\). Continuity gives
\(\overline{\Delta(\mathcal Q)}\subset\ker d_{\rm sep}\). For the
reverse inclusion choose \(l_j+o_j\to f-g\), possible with a sequence
because \(\mathcal B\) is Fréchet. Then \(F_j=f-l_j\) has first
observation exactly \([f]_{J_L}\) and second observation tending to
\([g]_{J_O}\), because \[
F_j-g-o_j=(f-g)-(l_j+o_j)\longrightarrow0.
\] This proves the reverse inclusion and retains the exact topological
defect. No closedness or subset interpolation is assumed. PSC's
\(C_{\rm rec}\) denotes this reconstruction quotient; it is not
identified with any earlier coefficient-record algebra.
