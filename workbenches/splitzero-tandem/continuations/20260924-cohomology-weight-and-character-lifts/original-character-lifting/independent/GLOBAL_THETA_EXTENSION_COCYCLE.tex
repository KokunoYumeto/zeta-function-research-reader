\subsection{The exact all-primary section and its original source defect}

Use the original adelic antecedent and test
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC1}
 \begin{gathered}
 \eta(x)=(\pi^2x^4-3\pi x^2/2)e^{-\pi x^2},\quad

 \frac{\pi^2\Gamma((s+4)/2)}{2\pi^{(s+4)/2}}
 -\frac{3\pi\Gamma((s+2)/2)}{4\pi^{(s+2)/2}}\right].
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The value and additive integral of $\eta$ are both zero.
Thus $b\xi_h=0$: its infinity boundary factor is the zero
pair, while every finite reference factor is $(1,1)$.
For every original nontrivial zero $\rho$ of multiplicity
$m_\rho$ retain, for $1\le j\le m_\rho$,
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC2}
 \begin{gathered}

    \int_x^\infty y^{\rho-1}(\log(y/x))^{j-1}h(y)\,dy,\qquad
 k_{\rho,0}=h,\\
 Mk_{\rho,j}(s)=\frac{M(s)}{(s-\rho)^j},\qquad
 (D-\rho)k_{\rho,j}=k_{\rho,j-1},\qquad D=-x\partial_x.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
GDT13 proves membership in the entire original $\mathscr B$ by
the two-ended integral and all vanishing Mellin moments. Its
quotient classes $u_{\rho,j}=[k_{\rho,j}]$ form an actual
length-$m_\rho$ Jordan chain; $u_{\rho,0}=0$. Jets at all the
original zeros prove that these chains for distinct $\rho$
are independent. The whole algebraic primary subspace and
the explicit section therefore exist:
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC3}
 U=\bigoplus_{\rho}\operatorname{span}
       \{u_{\rho,1},\ldots,u_{\rho,m_\rho}\}\subset H,\qquad
 s:U\to\mathscr B,\quad s(u_{\rho,j})=k_{\rho,j},\quad qs=1_U.
\end{equation}
Only finite sums occur in $U$. Every original zero and every
original multiplicity is included; no height cutoff is used.
No claim that $U$ exhausts the closed quotient is made.

The original dilation is $R_a f(x)=f(x/a)$ for $a>0$.
It descends to $\bar R_a$ on $H$, and its generator descends
to $\bar D$. The actual infinitesimal extension is
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC4}
 (Ds-s\bar D)u_{\rho,j}=
          \begin{cases}h,&j=1,\\0,&j>1.\end{cases}
\end{equation}
This follows immediately from both original equations in GEC2,
including $k_{\rho,0}=h$ and $u_{\rho,0}=0$. The boundary
amplitude is the same actual $h$ for every primary eigenline,
not a discarded numerical zero.

There is a full finite-action formula. For any real $t$ put
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC5}
 \begin{split}
 C_{\rho,j}(t)
  &=\int_0^t e^{\rho(t-v)}\frac{(t-v)^{j-1}}{(j-1)!}
                      R_{e^v}h\,dv,\\
 R_{e^t}k_{\rho,j}
  &=e^{\rho t}\sum_{\ell=0}^{j-1}\frac{t^\ell}{\ell!}
                   k_{\rho,j-\ell}+C_{\rho,j}(t).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The integral is oriented as written also for $t<0$.
To prove it let $X_j(t)=R_{e^t}k_{\rho,j}$ and
$X_0(t)=R_{e^t}h$. Strong differentiability of original
dilation gives $X_j'=\rho X_j+X_{j-1}$. Multiplication
by $e^{-\rho t}$ and the fundamental theorem of calculus
give its Volterra integral with initial value $k_{\rho,j}$.
Iterating exactly $j$ times gives the displayed finite
polynomial and the last integral, with its factorial.
Equivalently differentiation of GEC5 gives that recursion
and the same initial value. Applying continuous linear
functionals and scalar uniqueness proves the equality in
the Hausdorff original test space. Thus no formal exponential
series of $D$ on arbitrary test functions is assumed.

Every defect in GEC5 has an original Schwartz antecedent in

\subsection{Every tensor direction and its complete correction}

For every $r\ge1$ retain $s_r=s^{\otimes r}$ on $U^{\otimes r}$,
with original target $\mathscr B^{\otimes r}$. On an arbitrary
original pure primary tensor $v=\bigotimes_{i=1}^r u_{\rho_i,j_i}$,
put
$P_i(t)=e^{\rho_i t}\sum_{\ell=0}^{j_i-1}
t^\ell k_{\rho_i,j_i-\ell}/\ell!$ and
$C_i(t)=C_{\rho_i,j_i}(t)$. Expanding the entire finite product
gives the exact tensor correction
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC8}
 \begin{split}
 \Delta_t^{[r]}v
 &:=(R_{e^t}^{\otimes r}s_r-s_r\bar R_{e^t}^{\otimes r})v\\
 &=\sum_{\varnothing\ne S\subset[r]}
          \bigotimes_{i=1}^r
          \begin{cases}C_i(t),&i\in S,\\P_i(t),&i\notin S.
          \end{cases}
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Every cross contribution is present. By GEC6 its range lies in
$N_r^V=\sum_i\mathscr B^{\otimes(i-1)}\otimes V
\otimes\mathscr B^{\otimes(r-i)}$, hence in the exact
closed-image tensor kernel of GFT9--12 and GBC16.
Its generator defect is the complete original expression
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC9}
 (D^{[r]}s_r-s_r\bar D^{[r]})v
  =\sum_{i=1}^r\mathbf1_{j_i=1}\,
    k_{\rho_1,j_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes h
                       \otimes\cdots\otimes k_{\rho_r,j_r}.
\end{equation}
Here $h$ occupies the displayed original position $i$.
This follows from the tensor Leibniz sum for the action and
GEC4, with no absorbed factor in $D^{[r]}=\sum_iD_i$.
The analogue of GEC7 follows by the same multiplication of
the actual tensor actions. These equations hold for the
whole algebraic primary tensor, by linearity.

There is no equivariant section of an actual nonzero primary
eigenline into the original test space. Indeed an intertwining
lift $f$ of $u_{\rho,1}$ would satisfy $Df=\rho f$.
In each compact-unit coordinate its only solutions are
$f(x,u)=x^{-\rho}c(u)$, by the first-order differential equation.
No nonzero such function has every required power decay at
both zero and infinity. Hence $f=0$, contrary to its nonzero
quotient class. This proves that the cocycle extension on that
actual eigenline cannot be removed by any equivariant section.

The tensor statement is equally global. The coefficient map
$\mathscr B^{\otimes r}\to\{\hbox{functions on }C^r\}$ taking
an original pure tensor to its product in separate variables
is injective. To verify this, restrict to the finite-dimensional
factor spans in any finite tensor presentation. Point evaluations
separate each such span and therefore span its dual; products
of these evaluations separate the corresponding finite tensor
space. Thus a zero product function has zero tensor amplitude.
Every such tensor function decays to every power along a common
positive dilation of all coordinates, in both directions: this
follows termwise from the original weighted seminorms and the
finite tensor presentation. An eigenvector of the diagonal
generator with eigenvalue $\lambda$ would, along that orbit,
be a fixed nonzero value times the single power $a^{-\lambda}$.
That cannot obey both collections of decay bounds. Therefore
\begin{equation}\tag{GEC10}
 \ker(D^{[r]}-\lambda)=0\quad\hbox{on }\mathscr B^{\otimes r}
                      \quad\hbox{for every }\lambda\in\mathbb C.
\end{equation}
On $U^{\otimes r}$, by contrast,
$\bigotimes_i u_{\rho_i,1}$ is an actual nonzero eigenvector
of eigenvalue $\sum_i\rho_i$. No equivariant section of that
line into the original tensor test space exists. Thus the
extension measured by GEC8--9 is nontrivial at every tensor
order. This is not a statement that a tensor eigenvalue is
off the critical weight.
