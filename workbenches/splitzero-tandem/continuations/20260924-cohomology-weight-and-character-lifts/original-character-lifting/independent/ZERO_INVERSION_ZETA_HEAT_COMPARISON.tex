\section{The unchanged Rodgers--Tao heat family through the full lattice map}
\label{sec:zero-inversion-zeta-heat}

\paragraph{Human source and exact reading coverage.}
Brad Rodgers and Terence Tao,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914v5}{\emph{The De Bruijn--Newman
constant is non-negative}}, arXiv:1801.05914v5, 3 July 2021,
is the author source used here. The user-supplied intact source archive
is retained separately. Root read the original
\nolinkurl{fmp-template.tex}, lines 1--133, including the source equations
\texttt{hoz}, \texttt{sas}, \texttt{phidef}, \texttt{htdef}, and
Theorem \texttt{main}. This is a bounded reading of the definitions
and theorem statement, not a claim to have audited the entire
Rodgers--Tao proof. Their theorem is cited as their result.
The threshold characterization is attributed in their introduction
to de Bruijn and Newman; the backwards heat evolution is attributed
there to Csordas, Smith and Varga. The connecting maps below are
proved here using the full-lattice construction in ZIL1--25.

Keep every constant and the original time and complex coordinates:
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR1}
\begin{gathered}
 \xi(s)=\frac{s(s-1)}2\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),
 \qquad H_0(z)=\frac18\xi\left(\frac12+\frac{iz}{2}\right),\\
 \Phi(u)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
 (2\pi^2n^4e^{9u}-3\pi n^2e^{5u})e^{-\pi n^2e^{4u}},\\
 H_t(z)=\int_0^\infty e^{tu^2}\Phi(u)\cos(zu)\,du,
 \qquad \partial_tH_t=-\partial_z^2H_t .
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
These are exactly the cited author definitions, not a replacement
kernel. The source's threshold and its main theorem state
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR2}
 \{t\in\mathbb R: \text{every zero of }H_t\text{ is real}\}
    =[\Lambda,\infty),\qquad \Lambda\ge0.
\end{equation}
Since the source identifies RH with real zeros of $H_0$, substitution
of $t=0$ into the displayed set gives RH if and only if $0\ge\Lambda$.
Combining this with the cited Rodgers--Tao theorem gives the exact
logical consequences
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR3}
 \mathrm{RH}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Lambda=0,
 \qquad
 \neg\mathrm{RH}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Lambda>0.
\end{equation}
Neither statement replaces the inequality in their theorem by a
strict one. The invertibility of a supported zero is not used in
the proof of (ZHR3).

Now use the same arbitrary nontrivial bounded distributive lattice
$L$ as before, the tropical coefficient extension
$K_L=K\otimes_{\mathbb B}L$, and the proved injection
$\iota_L(\lambda)=1_K\otimes\lambda$. The actual lifted family is
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR4}
 \widehat H(t,z)=(H_t(z),1_L)\in G_L(\mathbb C),\qquad
 \mathcal H_L(t,z)=\Phi_{\mathbb C}(\widehat H(t,z))
                 =(H_t(z),1_{K_L}).
\end{equation}
The map $\Phi_{\mathbb C}$ is the injective unital coefficient
map of ZIL12. The family $\widehat H$ is a family of points; it
is not asserted to be a zero-preserving homomorphism from the
ring of functions into the supported semiring. In particular
the ordinary zero value of $H_t$ is lifted to $e$, not $\tau$.
Direct equality of the two coordinates proves
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR5}
\begin{gathered}
 H_t(z)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \widehat H(t,z)=e
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       \mathcal H_L(t,z)=(0,1_{K_L}),\\
 \ell_\mu(\widehat H(t,z))=\mu,\qquad
 d_\mu\operatorname{pr}_2\mathcal H_L(t,z)=d_\mu,
 \quad d_\mu=\iota_L(\mu),\quad\mu\in L.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The last line follows from ZIL4 and ZIL14 and holds for every
$t,z$, including all zeros and all nonzeros. At $\mu=1_L$ the
localized value is always the unit. Thus $e$ really is invertible
in this localization, while the entire distinguishing amplitude
coordinate has been identified away. The unlocalized joint map
continues to detect the exact zero locus by the first line.

The actual counterfactual zero set, in both original coordinates,
is therefore
\begin{equation}\tag{ZHR6}
\begin{gathered}
 \mathcal E_L=\{z\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb R:
              \mathcal H_L(0,z)=(0,1_{K_L})\},\\
 s=\frac12+\frac{iz}{2},\qquad
 z=-2i(s-\tfrac12).
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
Writing $z=x+iy$ gives
$s=(1-y)/2+ix/2$. Thus $z\notin\mathbb R$ is equivalent to
$\operatorname{Re}s\ne1/2$. Equation (ZHR1), including its
nonzero factor $1/8$, identifies the zeros with those of $\xi$.
The source's RH equivalence identifies these with the nontrivial
zeta-zero question. Consequently (ZHR3) is also equivalent to
$\mathcal E_L=\varnothing$ for RH and
$\mathcal E_L\ne\varnothing$ for its negation. This gives the exact
connecting map; it does not assert that either emptiness or
nonemptiness has been proved here. The localized family in (ZHR5)
is constant on the entire ambient domain, so its inverse image of
the localized unit cannot be substituted for $\mathcal E_L$.

There is a further precise distinction between time levels.
For each $t<0$, (ZHR2) and $\Lambda\ge0$ give $t<\Lambda$,
so $H_t$ has a nonreal zero. At $t=0$ the same argument gives
no strict inequality: the endpoint $\Lambda=0$ is allowed.
The localization formula (ZHR5) is identical at every one of
these time levels and therefore does not supply that missing
strict comparison. This statement concerns the actual author
heat family, with no replacement of its kernel or coordinates.

The algebraic zero-inversion theorem and the analytic theorem
are therefore simultaneously valid. Their proved connecting
maps do not imply $\Lambda>0$, and this calculation is neither
a proof nor a disproof of RH.
