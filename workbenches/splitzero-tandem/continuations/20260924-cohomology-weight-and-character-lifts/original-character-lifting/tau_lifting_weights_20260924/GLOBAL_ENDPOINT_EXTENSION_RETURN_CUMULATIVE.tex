\subsection{GEX7. Entire Ext groups in every algebraic
degree}\label{gex7.-entire-ext-groups-in-every-algebraic-degree}

Let \(\mathscr R\) be the specified unital operator algebra, with
\(P(L)\) central. It may be just \(\mathbb C[L]\). It may also contain
the actual dilation operators and a mirror satisfying GEX5.2--GEX5.3;
\(L\) need not be central in that larger algebra, but \(P(L)\) is. The
inverse \(K_Q\) is \(\mathscr R\)-linear because every generator
commutes with \(P(L_Q)\), hence with its inverse.

For all \(n\ge0\), \[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathscr R}^n(Q,B)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathscr R}^n(B,Q)=0.
}
\tag{GEX7.1}
\] This is about entire Ext groups in the stated category, strengthening
AC3's earlier vanishing of one specific two-extension class.

Take a free resolution \(P_\bullet\to Q\). Central multiplication by
\(P(L)\) is a chain map lifting the automorphism \(P(L_Q)\). For any
\(\mathscr R\)-linear cochain \(h:P_j\to B\), \[
h(P(L)x)=P(L_B)h(x)=0.
\tag{GEX7.2}
\] Thus its precomposition action on the entire Hom complex is zero. On
Ext that same action is the functorially induced action of the
automorphism \(P(L_Q)\); its inverse is induced by \(K_Q\). Therefore it
is both zero and invertible, so each Ext group is zero.

For the reverse order, take a free resolution \(P_\bullet\to B\). Its
central multiplication map lifts zero on \(B\), and is null-homotopic.
To construct the homotopy, in degree zero lift its image, whose
augmentation is zero, into the next boundary using freeness.
Inductively, subtract the previously constructed \(h_{j-1}d_j\) from the
degree-\(j\) central map. Its differential is zero by the preceding
identity, so exactness and freeness give a lift \(h_j\) through the next
boundary. Thus \[
P(L)_{P_j}=d_{j+1}h_j+h_{j-1}d_j
\] in every degree. On \(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathscr R}(P_\bullet,Q)\),
precomposition is therefore zero on cohomology. By centrality it equals
postcomposition by the automorphism \(P(L_Q)\), whose inverse is
postcomposition by \(K_Q\). Again the cohomology vanishes. Degree zero
is also directly GEX4.5.

No decomposition of the whole \(Q\) into a direct sum of finite packets
appears in this proof, and no passage through an unproved infinite
spectral limit is used.

\subsection{GEX8. Strict topological Yoneda
extensions}\label{gex8.-strict-topological-yoneda-extensions}

The all-degree conclusion also holds for strict Yoneda extensions in the
category of GEX6, with specified continuous operators whose relations
make \(P(L)\) central. These are finite strings of strict short exact
sequences; the assertion is not about an unspecified derived functor in
a larger analytic category.

The finite pushouts and pullbacks needed here stay in the category. A
pushout of the closed embedding \(\iota:B\to E\) along continuous
\(g:B\to B'\) is \[
(E\oplus B')/\{(\iota b,-gb):b\in B\}.
\tag{GEX8.1}
\] Put \(N_g=\{(\iota b,-gb):b\in B\}\), let
\(q:E\oplus B'\to P=(E\oplus B')/N_g\) be the quotient, and let
\(p:E\to Q\) be the original strict quotient. The subspace \(N_g\) is
closed: in closed \(\iota B\oplus B'\), it is the graph of the
continuous map \(-g\iota^{-1}\). Therefore \(P\) is Hausdorff locally
convex, with its quotient topology, and \(q\) is open by the saturation
calculation in GEX6.

The induced \(\bar p:P\to Q\) has composite \(\bar p q(e,b')=p(e)\). It
is onto and open: the composite \(p\,\mathrm{pr}_E\) is open, so for
every open \(O\subset P\), \(\bar p(O)=p\,\mathrm{pr}_E(q^{-1}O)\) is
open. Its kernel is closed because \(Q\) is Hausdorff. Its preimage in
\(E\oplus B'\) is the saturated subspace \(K=\iota B\oplus B'\). The
restriction \(q|_K\) is open onto that kernel with its subspace
topology: for open \(O\subset E\oplus B'\), \[
 q(O\cap K)=q(O)\cap q(K),
\tag{GEX8.1a}
\] since \(K\) is saturated. The quotient of \(K\) by \(N_g\) is
topologically \(B'\): the map \[
 K\to B',\qquad(\iota b,b')\mapsto gb+b'
\tag{GEX8.1b}
\] is the open projection after the continuous invertible triangular
change \((b,b')\mapsto(b,gb+b')\), has kernel \(N_g\), and has
continuous section \(b'\mapsto(0,b')\). Thus \(B'\to P\) is exactly a
closed topological embedding and \(P\to Q\) is exactly an open quotient.
No algebraic pushout topology has been presumed to be strict.

For a continuous map \(h:Q'\to Q\), the pullback \[
 P_h=\{(e,q')\in E\oplus Q':p(e)=h(q')\}
\] is a closed subspace, since it is the kernel of the continuous map
\((e,q')\mapsto p(e)-h(q')\) into Hausdorff \(Q\). Its projection
\(p':P_h\to Q'\) is onto and open. For any product-neighbourhood
\(V_E\times V_{Q'}\), the exact image is \[
 p'\big((V_E\times V_{Q'})\cap P_h\big)
 =V_{Q'}\cap h^{-1}(p(V_E)),
\tag{GEX8.1c}
\] which is open because \(p\) is open and \(h\) continuous. Its kernel
is the copy \(b\mapsto(\iota b,0)\), with precisely the original
subspace topology. These prove that the pullback is strict too.

Identities, finite direct sums and compositions of closed embeddings or
open quotients preserve their stated properties. Together with these
pushout and pullback calculations, they give the exact structure used
for the finite Yoneda strings below. Every compatible continuous
operator preserves the relevant graph or fibre product and descends or
restricts continuously. No completed tensor product, completion of a
quotient, or open mapping theorem is used.

For every such \(n\)-extension class, \[
P(L_B)_*[\mathfrak e]=P(L_Q)^*[\mathfrak e].
\tag{GEX8.2}
\] Here is the exact Yoneda comparison. Central multiplication on every
middle object is a morphism of the extension to itself with endpoint
maps \(P(L_B)\) and \(P(L_Q)\). Push the left end out along \(P(L_B)\),
and pull the right end back along \(P(L_Q)\). At the first object the
induced comparison is \[
[e,b]\longmapsto P(L_E)e+\iota b,
\] which kills the pushout relation because
\(P(L_E)\iota=\iota P(L_B)\). At the last object it is \[
e\longmapsto(P(L_E)e,\pi e),
\] which lies in the pullback by \(\pi P(L_E)=P(L_Q)\pi\). Intermediate
maps are central multiplication. For \(n=1\) the combined formula is
\([e,b]\mapsto(P(L_E)e+\iota b,\pi e)\). They are continuous, commute
with the differentials, and induce identity maps on the endpoints. This
is the Yoneda equivalence proving GEX8.2.

The left endpoint action is zero because \(P(L_B)=0\): the first strict
short factor \(0\to B\to E_{n-1}\to K_1\to0\), pushed out by the zero
map \(B\to B\), becomes \(0\to B\to K_1\oplus B\to K_1\to0\), with its
explicit continuous section \(k\mapsto(k,0)\). Its Yoneda splice is
therefore zero. The right endpoint action is invertible because pullback
by the continuous automorphism \(P(L_Q)\) is inverted by pullback by
\(K_Q\). Thus every class in the stated order vanishes.

For the opposite order, the same naturality identity equates pushout by
the target automorphism \(P(L_Q)\) with pullback by the zero source map
\(P(L_B)\). The latter splits the last strict short factor: its pullback
middle object is the product of that factor's kernel with \(B\), with
the section \(b\mapsto(0,b)\). The pushout action is invertible, with
inverse induced by \(K_Q\), so every reverse class vanishes too. Degree
zero follows from GEX4.5. Thus GEX7.1 also holds for these explicitly
specified topological Yoneda groups, without an assumed topological
projective resolution.

No completeness assumption on an arbitrary middle object was used. If a
narrower category of topological representations is selected, its exact
structure and closure under these finite constructions must first be
verified.
