## Why the entire radial factor matters

\[

(\mathcal C\xrightarrow{i\partial_y}\mathcal C)

The symbol \(\mathcal B[-1]\) here means \(\mathcal B\) in degree one. This precisely identifies the boundary term lost by removing the original factor. The raw degree-one Frobenius includes \(\mu^{-1}\); the original degree-one action does not.
