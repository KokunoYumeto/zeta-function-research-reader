# Split-Zero cohomology and original-zeta research: three-task continuation

This edition brings together the subsequent completed arguments from **Split Zero to GCT bridge**, **Based on split zero cohomology**, and **Explore the identity–absorber square**. The wider programme investigates whether the investigator's support construction can carry arithmetic cohomology and the geometric weight control relevant to the Riemann hypothesis. Its source symbol is retained as **τ〈Z1; no Z2〉**: presence without integer parity. The withdrawn self-addition rule is not used. Arithmetic, analytic and tensor operations in these papers take place in explicitly constructed receiving spaces, not on that primitive support.

The programme takes motivation and actual constructions from [Connes–Consani's arithmetic sheaf](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.2024v3), [Meyer's spectral interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0412277v3), [Connes's trace-formula work](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9811068v1), and [Deligne's *Weil II*, especially §3.6](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1980__52__137_0/). The papers distinguish those human results from their own subsequent calculations. The original Riemann zeta function, its poles and trivial zeros, Gamma factors, full zero multiplicities, source topology and endpoint terms are retained. **This edition does not prove RH, arithmetic purity or a complexity-theory conclusion.**

The investigator's construction order is also retained: the whole absorption history precedes its global quotient and retrospective arithmetic reconstruction. The parityless supporting datum is not identified with the recovered counting unit `1`, nor is a fixed underlying point identified with its stalk's unit. The [original definitions and retraction](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/blob/36a82ecca98addb8a98b48204e349737856c7223/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260924-cohomology-weight-and-character-lifts/original-character-lifting/foundations/user_definitions_20260924/SOURCE_OPERATIONS_AND_PROOFS.md) remain the receiving calculations' prerequisites.

These are complete new proof continuations, not replacements for the [preceding cumulative edition and definitions](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/zeta-function-research-reader/tree/36a82ecca98addb8a98b48204e349737856c7223/workbenches/splitzero-tandem/continuations/20260924-cohomology-weight-and-character-lifts). The wider repository also retains the earlier structural constructions, analytic estimates, formalization, connected investigations, literature records and research self-audit.

## What changed and why it matters

### 1. Full-source synthesis and tensor comparison

[Read the complete PDF](gct/CONTINUATION.pdf) · [Markdown](gct/CONTINUATION.md) · [LaTeX](gct/CONTINUATION.tex)

The GCT-bridge task worked on the source/topology question underlying its geometric comparison: can the entire original spectral quotient and its completed tensor powers be reconstructed from actual full zero jets, rather than a finite selected list? GSP proves Gaussian finite-rank synthesis in the original quotient topology with all multiplicities; CTS uses it to prove injectivity of the completed tensor source map and determine the common radical of the positive product-transfer forms. FST then writes the fixed-base tensor square with its actual first specialization and later boundary map. The single geometric cover factor and the external product factor remain distinct. Neither a dense image nor a later-degree boundary is substituted for the missing arithmetic weight statement.

### 2. Positive source measures and the original divisor

[Read the complete PDF](identity/CONTINUATION.pdf) · [Markdown](identity/CONTINUATION.md) · [LaTeX](identity/CONTINUATION.tex)

The identity–absorber task asked which positive forms genuinely arise on the original analytic source and which descend through its actual zeta relation. SPF/SMC classify the specified continuous positive transfer forms by tempered positive measures and calculate the descent/closure maps. OZD/OZR construct an original-zeta divisor receiver and globally convergent tests, retaining the pole, trivial zeros and full functional-equation multiplier. The symmetric nonnegative divisor contribution survives the cancellation of the reflected odd contribution. This calculates what those positivity and reflection operations preserve; it does not infer that every zero lies on the critical line.

### 3. Arithmetic returns, harmonic comparison and closed support

[Read the complete PDF](counterfactual/CONTINUATION.pdf) · [Markdown](counterfactual/CONTINUATION.md) · [LaTeX](counterfactual/CONTINUATION.tex)

The cohomology task made the product, diagonal-return and supported maps explicit, then returned the Hilbert observations to that same arithmetic source. FGR retains the full Gram inverse where individual same-side vectors need not become asymptotically orthogonal; the fixed-finite-set harmonic trace limit is calculated without assuming an infinite-window interchange. HSW constructs a convergent whole-divisor harmonic distribution and an explicit original summation-image test. It recovers the off-critical divisor from the meromorphic harmonic density and expresses its unsigned defect as a positive sum of norms of canonical original-primary representatives. OPD gives the exact all-dilation forcing and Green identities, including the nonzero source cross term. HCS calculates the closed-support and restriction maps on the full surviving kernel, retaining the intermediate cyclic component. These calculations expose and carry the remaining arithmetic class rather than removing it by a Hilbert closure.
