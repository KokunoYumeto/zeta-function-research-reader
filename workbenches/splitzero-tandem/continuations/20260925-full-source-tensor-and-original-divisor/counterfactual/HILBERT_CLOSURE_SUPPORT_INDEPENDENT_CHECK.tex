generator. For any of these complexes, \[
\mathscr H^n(M)=M^n\oplus M^{n-1},\qquad
\partial^n(u,v)=(d_Mu,d_Mv-(-1)^nq u).
\tag{CHC7.1}
\] The quotient in CHC5.3 has Hom complex
\texttt{tilde\ A\ -\/-(+q)→tilde\ A} in degrees one and two; the

\tag{CHC7.2}
\] All other groups vanish. The degree-two splitting on \texttt{K×} is
specified by the original equivariant plus-chart extension of the

\texttt{chi\ E\textquotesingle{}} and its unchanged degree-one

Write \texttt{delta\_q:ker\ q\textbar{}tilde\ S→Y/qY} for the original
ORE connecting class, and let \texttt{p:Y/qY→T\_K/qT\_K} be the actual

([t],\eta,e)\longmapsto
\left([t]-\frac12p\delta_q(\eta),
-\frac12\delta_q(\eta),e\right).
\tag{CHC7.3}
\] Proof: extend \texttt{eta} by the plus-chart cochain \texttt{u},
choose \texttt{alpha} with \texttt{Sigma\textquotesingle{}alpha=eta},
and put \texttt{q\ alpha=pi\_A\textquotesingle{}y\_alpha}. In the target

identity, and endpoint/extra classes map by identity. Changing
\texttt{alpha} changes \texttt{y\_alpha} by \texttt{qY}, so CHC7.3 is

In degree one the map is \texttt{t↦(t,0)}, in degrees \texttt{−1,0} it

\texttt{chi\ H\textquotesingle{}/q→chi\ E\textquotesingle{}/q}, an

Here is the complete cochain proof of the degree-two splitting and its

\] extend a Schwartz functional only on the plus chart, and extend each

A degree-two hybrid \texttt{K} cocycle is
