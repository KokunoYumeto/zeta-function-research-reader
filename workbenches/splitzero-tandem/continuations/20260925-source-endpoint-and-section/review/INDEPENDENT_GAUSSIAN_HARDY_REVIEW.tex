The source is the original Fréchet space of entire functions rapidly decreasing in every closed vertical strip, modulo its ideal of vanishing through every actual nontrivial-zeta zero order. With

\[
\rho=\sigma+i\gamma,\quad \rho^\#=1-\bar\rho,
\quad H=\ell^2(\mathscr Z,m),\quad
p_N(x)^2=\sum_\rho m_\rho(1+|\gamma|)^{2N}|x_\rho|^2,

For the actual source multiplier \(g_t(s)=e^{ts^2}\), the exact integral remainder gives

\[
|e^{t\rho^2}-1|
\le\int_0^t |\rho|^2e^{u\sigma^2}\,du
\le te(1+\gamma^2),\qquad 0<t\le1.
\]

Thus \(p_N((G_t-I)x)\le te\,p_{N+2}(x)\). The original strip version is

\[
q_{A,N}((g_t-1)F)
\le te^{A^2}(A^2+1)q_{A,N+2}(F).
\]

Taking infima over the actual invariant closed subspace proves the quotient-seminorm estimate without needing a continuous section. The directed seminorm family gives the quotient topology. It follows that the source approximation is uniform on bounded sets in the given quotient topology, not merely at finitely many zeros.

The half-Mellin factor is retained: \(\Theta a=\frac12\int a(u)u^sdu/u\), with inverse coefficient \(1/\pi\). \subsection{GHR2. Reflected operators, energies, and every multiplicity}\label{ghr2.-reflected-operators-energies-and-every-multiplicity}

Put

\[
\Delta_r(\sigma)=r^\sigma-r^{1-\sigma},\quad
d_\rho=e^{-i\gamma\log r}\Delta_r(\sigma),\quad
B=D_r^*JG_t,\quad A=B^*B.
\]

Inner products are linear in the first argument. On a reflected off-line pair, equal multiplicities give the exact matrix in the orthonormal coordinate basis

\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}
0&\bar d_\rho g_t(\rho^\#)\\
-\bar d_\rho g_t(\rho)&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Multiplying this matrix verifies, without dropping a phase or changing a receiver,

\[
A_\rho=\Delta_r(\sigma)^2e^{2t(\sigma^2-\gamma^2)},
\quad BB^*=JAJ,
\quad B^2=-M_c,
\]

\[
c_\rho=\bar d_\rho^2g_t(\rho)g_t(\rho^\#)
=\Delta_r(\sigma)^2e^{t(\sigma^2+(1-\sigma)^2-2\gamma^2)}
e^{2i\gamma(\log r+t)}.
\]

It also verifies \(B^*JB=-G_t^*P_rJG_t\). Reindexing by the original multiplicity-preserving reflection gives precisely the source energy GDE2.4. Its radical is the stated value kernel \(N_O\), not the full zero-jet ideal. Finite actual isolators give the asserted dense images; no surjectivity or equality of source and Hilbert topologies follows from density.

The ordinary orthonormal basis is \(u_\rho=e_\rho/\sqrt{m_\rho}\). Consequently

\[
\operatorname{Tr}_H A=\sum_\rho a_\rho,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tr}_\zeta A=\operatorname{Tr}_H(\mathsf M A)
=\sum_\rho m_\rho a_\rho.
\]

The original zero count gives a polynomial bound on each multiplicity and Gaussian summability with every fixed polynomial height weight. Hence all stated products with powers of \(\mathsf M\) have bounded trace-class extensions. For weighted cyclicity, \(X=\mathsf M^{1/2}B\) is Hilbert--Schmidt; the equal traces of \(X^*X\) and \(XX^*\) prove the actual assertion. This does not commute a general unbounded operator through a trace.

Each off-line pair contributes

\[
m_\rho\Delta_r(\sigma)^2e^{-2t\gamma^2}
\bigl(e^{2t\sigma^2}+e^{2t(1-\sigma)^2}\bigr)>0.
\]

Off-diagonal and antisymmetric traces can vanish while this term remains. The reflection form, positive form, and square \(B^2\) have the different Gaussian placements stated in GDE8.2; those placements were checked explicitly.

\subsection{GHR3. Determinants, transfer degree, and limiting regimes}\label{ghr3.-determinants-transfer-degree-and-limiting-regimes}

Absolute convergence of \(\sum a_\rho\) and \(\sum m_\rho a_\rho\) proves local-uniform convergence of the two entire products

\[
\prod_\rho(1+za_\rho),\qquad
\prod_\rho(1+za_\rho)^{m_\rho}.
\]

The second has the counted-carrier realization in GDE4, with \(m_\rho\) scalar copies. That construction records divisor multiplicity; it does not replace the original nilpotent jet module by a scalar module. The third product \(\prod(1+zm_\rho a_\rho)\) is different, as correctly stated. The logarithmic derivatives, local logarithm expansions, and strong resolvent limit to \(P_O\) all follow on exactly the stated domains.

The nonsymmetric pair determinant is \(1+z^2c_\rho\); the positive pair determinant is

\[
1+z(a_\rho+a_{\rho^\#})+z^2|c_\rho|^2.
\]

Thus cancellation of the first derivative of the first determinant does not cancel the positive linear coefficient of the second.

For positive recovered integers \(n\ge1\), the raw operators retain \(T_nU_n=nI\). The covariance \(BT_n=U_n^*B\) gives

\[
T_n^*AT_n=B^*U_nU_n^*B.
\]

Its source trace coefficient is \(n^{2\sigma}a_\rho\); after the valid cyclic operation the target coefficient is \(n^{2(1-\sigma)}a_{\rho^\#}\). The reflected Gaussian cannot be replaced by the unreflected one. Both cross terms from \(T_rU_r=rI\) are retained in

\[
P_r=T_rT_r^*+U_r^*U_r-2rI.
\]

For a reflected orbit with \(\delta=\sigma-1/2>0\), direct expansion gives GDE7.6 with the full prefactor

\[
2m_\rho n\Delta_r(\sigma)^2e^{-2t\gamma^2+t/2+2t\delta^2}
\bigl[\cosh(2\delta(t+\log n))-\cosh(2t\delta)\bigr].
\]

It is positive for \(n>1\) at an actual such orbit. No existence of that orbit is assumed.

The unregularized operators are bounded. Strong convergence and the stronger common-domain rates follow from GHR1. Norm convergence is equivalent to decay of \(\Delta_r(\sigma)^2\) outside finite sets, since each regularized operator is compact and the reverse implication follows by finite-coordinate/tail splitting.

For the traces, use the exact nonnegative comparison

\[
e^{-2t}a_\rho=\Delta_r(\sigma)^2
e^{-2t(1-\sigma^2+\gamma^2)}.
\]

It increases as \(t\downarrow0\); monotone convergence and the retained factor \(e^{2t}\) give either the full finite sum or \(+\infty\). The determinant limits follow from the stated logarithmic upper/lower bounds. These are whole-divisor arguments, not finite numerical tests. The differentiated, parameter-comparison, and first-order normality-defect formulas also check with the full factors in GDE9--11.

GDE8 retains original \(\zeta\), both equivalent complete functional-equation factors, the full \(F_0\), its endpoint/trivial-point comparisons, and every product derivative. Positive nonholomorphic energy is not inserted without proof as a holomorphic test in the original prime explicit formula. The growing values at the trivial points are not summed without a convergence assertion.

\subsection{GHR4. Hardy source uniqueness and exact regularized boundary}\label{ghr4.-hardy-source-uniqueness-and-exact-regularized-boundary}

The current Hardy receiver has a particularly important source statement: injectivity is proved on the full continuous prequotient dual, before restricting to the original zero quotient.

The tests

\[
F_{t,j}(s)=e^{ts^2}\left(\phi_j(s)+\frac{8F_0(s)}s\right)
\]

are entire because \(8F_0(0)=1\) cancels exactly the residue of \(\phi_j\). Their polynomial-in-\(j\) strip-seminorm bounds give a compact-open holomorphic receiver of the strong dual. If all received coefficients vanish, the scalar entire function

\[
L(w)=\Lambda\left(e^{ts^2}
\frac{e^{(1-s)w}-8e^wF_0(s)}s\right)
\]

has growth at most \(C\exp(C(1+|w|^2))\) and zeros at every \(\log n\). Jensen's formula about a nonzero value would allow only \(O(R^2)\) zeros in a disk of radius \(R\), whereas the displayed zeros supply exponentially many. Hence \(L=0\).

The exact identity \((\partial_w-1)L=-\Lambda(e^{ts^2}e^{(1-s)w})\) removes the endpoint correction only after differentiation. The inverse half-Mellin formula then represents \(e^{us^2}F\), \(u>t\), as a source-valued integral of those exponentials. Its coefficient \(1/2\), inverse coefficient \(1/\pi\), and all strip estimates were checked. Holomorphic continuation in \(\Re u>0\), followed by the proved Gaussian source limit, yields \(\Lambda(F)=0\) for every original source function. No assumption that \(\Lambda\) annihilates the zero ideal enters this uniqueness argument.

For the actual left value sector \(H_-\), the functional \(\lambda_x(F)=\langle\beta_rF,x\rangle\) is conjugate-linear in \(x\) and linear in \(F\). It annihilates \(N_O\). Its individual kernel is not asserted equal to \(N_O\); the restricted family's joint kernel tests precisely the right off-line values. The dense source image proves injectivity in \(x\).

The two conjugate-linear operations give the exact linear Hardy map

\[
\mathcal L_{r,t}x
=-\sum_{\lambda\in\mathscr Z_+}
m_\lambda d_r(\lambda)\overline{e^{t\lambda^2}}
x_{\lambda^\#}g_\lambda.
\]

Its minus sign, Gaussian conjugation, and reflected input are necessary. The bound

\[
\Delta_r(\sigma)^2\|g_\lambda\|^2
\le \frac{(r+1)^2(\log r)^2}{4}
\]

uses the actual factor \(\Delta_r(\sigma)\), which cancels the possible near-line growth in \(\|g_\lambda\|^2\le(2\sigma-1)^{-1}\). No uniform lower bound on \(\sigma-1/2\) is assumed. Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz proves absolute vector convergence for every \(x\in H_-\). On the orthonormal input basis, squared output norms sum to the exact \(C_{r,t}\), with one multiplicity factor. The sum of output norms is also finite, proving the stronger trace-class assertion between the two distinct Hilbert spaces.

Injectivity follows from the full-dual theorem. The image lies in \(\mathcal N^\perp\) because the original identity is

\[
\langle h_k,g_s\rangle=(1-k^{1-s})\zeta(s)/s,
\]

not a completed-zeta replacement. Higher source jets remain upstream in \(Q\) and in the specified value-kernel. The theorem does not embed them as separate coordinates of \(H_-\).

\subsection{GHR5. Receiving-map domain, cover defect, and all scales}\label{ghr5.-receiving-map-domain-cover-defect-and-all-scales}

The exact factorization is

\[

on the explicitly proved domain \(\sum m_\lambda|y_\lambda|\|g_\lambda\|<\infty\). The estimate in GHR4 proves \(B^*(H_-)\) lies in that domain. Neither boundedness of \(C_+\) on every \(H_+\) nor equality with the GDE norm is inferred. The double Gram series is absolutely convergent and retains every cross term.

For the original raw cover,

\[
W_n^*W_n=nI,\qquad W_nW_n^*=nP_n,
\qquad W_n^*\mathcal L=\mathcal LT_n.
\]

These equations give, with \(K=\mathcal L^*\mathcal L\),

\[
nK-T_n^*KT_n=n\mathcal L^*(I-P_n)\mathcal L.

The right side is retained. Iteration gives exactly the finite identity in ABH6.1/AHR7.1. Its positive remainder has trace

=\sum_{\lambda\in\mathscr Z_+}
\|\mathcal Lu_{\lambda^\#}\|^2

Every exponential factor is at most one and tends to zero separately; the dominating sum is the previously proved \(C_{r,t}\). The exact adjoint domain is \(\operatorname{ran}(I-S^*)=\operatorname{dom}(I-S)^{-*}=\mathcal D_{\delta_1}\). The positive receiver does not silently acquire this stronger regularity. No completeness assertion for the family of zero kernels in \(\mathcal N^\perp\) is used or proved.

\subsection{GHR6. Additional proved underclaim: all Schatten exponents}\label{ghr6.-additional-proved-underclaim-all-schatten-exponents}

GDE proves trace-class membership, which is sufficient for its determinants. Its actual coefficients give a modest stronger operator statement.

For every fixed \(r>1,t>0,p>0\), and every fixed real \(k\ge0\), the bounded extensions of \(\mathsf M^kB_{r,t}\) and \(\mathsf M^kA_{r,t}\) belong to the Schatten class \(S_p\). This statement also holds for their counted-carrier lifts, with the original multiplicities repeated.

Proof: since reflection commutes with \(\mathsf M\),

\[
(\mathsf M^kB)^*(\mathsf M^kB)=\mathsf M^{2k}A.
\]

Therefore the exact singular values, counted by distinct coordinates, are

\[
m_\rho^k|\Delta_r(\sigma)|e^{t(\sigma^2-\gamma^2)}.
\]

The sum of their \(p\)-th powers is bounded by

\[
(r-1)^pe^{pt}
\sum_\rho m_\rho^{kp}e^{-pt\gamma^2}<\infty.
\]

The pointwise polynomial multiplicity bound proved in GDE1 and the original zero count prove convergence: on each unit height interval, both the number of distinct zeros and the displayed power of the multiplicity grow at most polynomially, while the Gaussian decays faster than every polynomial. The same calculation for \(\mathsf M^kA\) uses singular values \(m_\rho^ka_\rho\). Repeating a coordinate \(m_\rho\) times in the counted carrier inserts one further polynomially bounded multiplicity factor and gives the same conclusion. This remains valid for \(0<p<1\), where Schatten membership is defined by singular-value \(p\)-summability, without invoking a norm triangle inequality.

This concerns the regularized receivers only. It neither supplies inverse continuity on their images nor a bound for the unregularized trace. It proves no additional arithmetic cancellation. The result follows from inspected coefficients rather than a new literature theorem.
