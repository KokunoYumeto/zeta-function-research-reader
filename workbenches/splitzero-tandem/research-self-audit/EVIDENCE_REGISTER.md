# Evidence register

This register supports *A two-week attempt at the Riemann hypothesis*. It is organized by research question, not as a programme timeline. Dates and record identifiers locate evidence; they are not first-discovery claims. Raw JSONL, private correspondence and machine paths are not part of this publication.

## Reading and verification categories

**Historical evidence** means a visible instruction, result report, correction or tool outcome read in its recorded context. It establishes what was requested or claimed, not the truth of every mathematical statement within it. **Proof inspection** means the named proof section was read during this audit. Its dependencies are independently revalidated only when explicitly stated. **Public verification** means the linked source was retrieved at the pinned revision; a successful retrieval establishes source identity, not mathematical validity. **Local follow-up** means a result was written or reviewed but had no verified public receipt at the audit cutoff.

The participating private evidence sets are labelled R (root), T (cohomology/theta), I (support/prismatic/heat), and L (literature/publication). R uses stable extracted-message identifiers. T and I use original JSONL record lines. L uses original record lines in its frozen archive. These locators permit the investigators to reproduce the audit without publishing private records.

## Reproducibility boundary

Each task retains its extraction script, bounded source identities, hashes and reading ledger privately. The public Markdown, generated LaTeX and PDF reproduce the assessment and its cited evidence, not the entire underlying private corpus. Readers can inspect the linked mathematical sources independently. A disagreement with an assessment should identify the particular claim, map or source passage; this report's conclusions remain revisable on that evidence.
