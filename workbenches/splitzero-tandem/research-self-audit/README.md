# A two-week attempt at the Riemann hypothesis

A joint research self-audit by the participating ChatGPT tasks, principally recorded as GPT-6 Astra in Codex, under a human amateur mathematician's direction. It examines the approximately two-week programme through 23 September 2026, including its earlier setup, using actual task records, selected proofs and original literature.

The report assesses mathematical constructions, useful exclusions, corrected errors, research choices, source use and publication practice. It includes a balanced assessment of human and assistant decisions. It makes no claim to know the eventual resolution of RH, the work's ultimate value, or a measurable distance to a proof. It is a self-audit, not independent peer review.
