\begin{abstract}
This edition incorporates the supplied terminal adjacent-norm calculation,
corrects the domain of the divided primary-image statement, and proves
a further estimate on every integer degree in the original window.
For each fixed complete hypothetical packet,
$\min_{q-1\le N\le2q}\log\epsilon_{h,k,N}=q\psi(1)+O_h(k+\log q)$,
where $\psi(1)>0$. An explicit interval estimate covers the previously
uncontrolled small-ratio degrees. The original two receiver columns
also give one exterior vector whose six orthogonal components retain
the complete upper-source, joint-kernel and observable energies.
All units, source masses, adjacent factors and cross pairings remain.
The proofs, exact source/image maps and independently executed
integer interval certificate are included. These results exclude a
small canonical allowance in this degree window; they do not evaluate
the signed order-$q$ arithmetic return or establish RH.
\end{abstract}

the product of the distinct real sign-changing roots of a real monic
orthogonal polynomial. If its degree is smaller than the polynomial's
degree, their product has a constant nonzero sign off finitely many
points, contradicting orthogonality in a positive almost-everywhere
measure. Thus all the degree is accounted for by real simple roots.
Existence and uniqueness of the monic minimizers follow from positive
finite moment Grams.

\section{A uniform single-norm estimate, not a difference of free energies}

\subsection{Proof of the lower estimate}
Use the arcsine probability measure $\nu$ on $[A,B]=[u^2,v^2]$ and
$\mathrm{cap}=(B-A)/4$. Its logarithmic potential satisfies
\[
 \int\log|z-x|d\nu(x)\ge\log\mathrm{cap}\quad(z\in\C),
 \qquad \int\log(d\nu/dx)d\nu=-\log(\pi\mathrm{cap}).
\]
For completeness, write $x=(A+B)/2+2\mathrm{cap}\cos\theta$ and
$z=(A+B)/2+\mathrm{cap}(w+w^{-1})$, $|w|\ge1$.
Factoring $z-x=\mathrm{cap}(w-e^{i\theta})(w-e^{-i\theta})/w$
and integrating the elementary circle logarithm gives
$\log\mathrm{cap}+\log|w|$. The entropy follows from
$\pi^{-1}\int_0^\pi\log\sin\theta\,d\theta=-\log2$;
this last identity follows by doubling the angle in the same integral.
Thus $\int\log|P|d\nu\ge j\log\mathrm{cap}$ for every monic $P$,
including complex roots. Integrating the EIQ Euler equality against
$\nu$ gives $\int Vd\nu-2\log\mathrm{cap}=\ell$, because $\mu$
is supported on the interval. Jensen's inequality on that interval now
gives the lower bound in TCN17, with the factor $r_-$ and the full
arcsine entropy retained. All logarithmic singularities at polynomial
zeros are integrable.

\subsection{Proof of the upper estimate}
Let $x_i$ be the $(i-1/2)/j$ quantile of the actual equilibrium
measure and take $P_j(x)=\prod_{i=1}^j(x-x_i)$. The empirical
CDF differs from the equilibrium CDF by at most $1/j$.
For $\delta_j=1/j$, the function
$f_x(t)=\log\max(|x-t|,\delta_j)$ on $[A,B]$ has variation at
most $2\log(1+j(B-A))$. Integration by parts against the two CDFs
therefore bounds the quadrature error by this variation divided by $j$.
Moreover bounded density gives
\[
 \int(\log\max(|x-t|,\delta_j)-\log|x-t|)d\mu(t)
 \le2M_*\delta_j,
\]
uniformly for every $x>0$. Since the logarithm of the actual polynomial
is bounded above by the clipped sum, these two bounds prove
\[
 \log|P_j(x)|\le jU_\mu(x)+2M_*+2\log(1+jW_+)\quad(x>0).
\]
This includes the entire small-coordinate region. Set
$\zeta=V-2U_\mu-\ell\ge0$. For $x=t^2\ge\max(1,B_+)$,
\[
 \zeta(t^2)\ge b_-t-2(a_++2)\log t-C_*.
\]
The elementary $\log t\le\sqrt t$ for $t\ge1$ and TCN16 give
$\zeta(t^2)\ge b_-t/2$ for $t\ge T_*$. Hence
$\int_0^\infty e^{-j\zeta(x)}dx\le J_*$ for every $j\ge1$.
Squaring the polynomial bound and using $r\le r_+$ proves TCN17.
