# Split-Zero arithmetic estimates: 16 September continuation

The programme constructs finite, full-multiplicity packets from zeros of the Riemann xi function and represents them in the original theta-source quotient. The current analytic question is whether a representative's canonical norm gives an upper allowance smaller than the same packet's exterior spectral defect. The source mass, relation space, Taylor unit, mixed pairings and original metric are part of that question—not adjustable parameters.

This update follows the [15 September programme reconstruction](PROGRAMME_RECONSTRUCTION_20260915.md). Its old source-order reservation is now completed. Its broader mathematical history remains useful, but it is no longer the current work assignment.

## Results and what they change

| Calculation | Motivation and result | Scope retained |
|---|---|---|
| Gamma source-order transport and equilibrium refinement | Carry estimates from the original order-one observation to the tensor-order observation through the same coefficient and quotient fibres. Complete proofs now give directed allocation-difference bounds and a sharper common-loss comparison. | Fixed-period simple-quartet domain, both parity blocks and exterior tails, original finite guards. Absolute allocations are not assigned by a comparison interval. |
| Complete arithmetic action | Determine which terms can really improve the proposed bound. Exact determinant telescoping gives `J_action=B_ar+W_ar-P_minus-P_plus`; the apparent allocation and its correlated budget cancel together. The original convolution has a degree-linear recurrence bound. | Full arithmetic mass, complete zero orders, total-degree output face, both penalties and their exact `4q-1` combined coefficient count. |
| Uniform exponential contraction | Bound every original adjacent volume ratio, rather than infer a minimum from an average. A two-band Jensen argument retains the actual relation polynomial and density and gives exponentially small penalties. | Explicit root-radius and finite smallness guards; a separate argument covers every fixed proportional degree window. No growing-window uniformity is claimed. |
| Sharp canonical-band rate and penalty tail | Refine the coarse bound using the existing equilibrium and paired-root comparison. The minimum has logarithmic rate `q a_1`, with `a_1` between `0.06651895202027` and `0.06651895202028`; a geometric sum bounds the penalty and its final-row strip. | Alternative explicit finite norm constant, complete small-rank patch, both adjacent factors. No exact finite minimizer or final-row dominance is asserted. |
| Shared-flag cancellation and threshold profile | Expose the same cancellation inside the original connecting/invariant flags. The complete finite argument bounds the error of a signed threshold profile by `32 q_j log 2`. | All eight original flag subspaces and their full preceding minimum metrics remain. The profile is not yet evaluated by this reduction. |

The important consequence is specific: within every fixed proportional degree window, the canonical allowance considered here is exponentially larger than the polynomial exterior benchmark. Selecting another degree in the same window, or improving an allocation that cancels from the complete action, does not fix this particular upper-bound strategy. This is not a disproof of RH, a counterexample to RH, or a no-go theorem for the whole construction.

The reference equilibrium profile points toward a different calculation: estimates with a growing degree-to-packet ratio, retaining the actual root polynomial when its radius becomes comparable with the inner equilibrium scale. That arithmetic extension has not been proved here.
