# Contribution opportunities

## Full four-dimensional detector

Section 5 specifies the massless scalar equation, in/up normalization, HHI state and peak equation. Compute the radial spectral density with flux checks and truncation/error estimates. Compare its peak with the exactly soluble conformal channel; do not assume the coefficient is universal across detector models.

## Moving detector and finite switching

Section 11 fixes the trajectory, proper clock, two-time kernel and compact switching. Integrate the response with a justified distributional treatment and test the Schwarzian expansion against it. Do not assume that an instantaneous temperature determines a nonstationary response.

## Field and gravitational entropy

The surface Dirac system has a complete spectrum, an entropy series, a zeta transform and a radius minimum tied to the optical potential. The gravitational entropy has a conical-action construction. A stronger microscopic identification must calculate renormalization and G, not choose the proper-distance cutoff to force coefficient agreement.

## Actual backreaction

The sigma-model target sphere agrees with the laboratory shell only at the stated reference calibration. Construct a coupled action and derive any stronger identification. Preserve the independent probe result if the coupled candidate fails.

## Phase-sensitive and anomaly data

The round untwisted Dirac spectrum has eta=0. Choose explicit gauge/chiral/bulk-boundary data, calculate the determinant phase and reduced state, and retain information lost by entropy alone. The old successive-inflow programme remains an intended investigation, not an already established consequence of the horizon calculation.
